This is a record of our family of four's incredible journey through Bali and Komodo Island. We spent three days in Bali, then headed to Komodo for two days, making it a total five-day adventure, where we joined a two-day, one-night liveaboard tour.

## Wonderful Experiences in Bali

I highly recommend the private driver Xiao Wu in Bali. During our three days in Bali, Xiao Wu accompanied and took care of us throughout. Xiao Wu is Betty Pinky's husband and speaks fluent Chinese, so we started discussing the itinerary with him while still in Taiwan. He arranged accommodations and activities based on our needs—whether it was mountain-and-sea excursions or sampling local snacks, he effortlessly guided us to experience Bali's charm in the most relaxed way.

On our last day in Bali, since we were heading to Komodo Island, we chose to stay near the airport. When we planned to simply take the hotel's taxi to the airport, Xiao Wu worried the driver might mistakenly take us to the international terminal, so he got up early to remind the driver and ensure everything went smoothly. However, a little hiccup still happened (I was too relaxed and gave the wrong amount of money 😭), but thankfully with Xiao Wu's help, he and his wife worked hard to resolve the issue, and we eventually got our money back. In an unfamiliar place, having them around was incredibly reassuring. When we returned to Bali after our Komodo trip, Xiao Wu even came to the airport to return the money to us and escorted us all the way from the domestic terminal to the security checkpoint of the international terminal before he felt at ease leaving.

## Komodo Island Liveaboard Adventure

Sunset view from the liveaboard (courtesy of Tyty Chow):

https://youtu.be/rVM2inxq8G4

Our two-day, one-night liveaboard trip to Komodo Island was an unforgettable experience. The comfort of the boat exceeded our expectations; the sea was calm, and the gentle breeze made it very pleasant (though this depends on the weather at the time). The meals on board were simple but delicious, with the grilled fish being especially memorable.

![科摩多島船宿及美景（Tyty Chow提供）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a615a173436b8ee9cd7ee4f0d691c0ea.jpg)

Komodo Island liveaboard and scenery (courtesy of Tyty Chow)

One of the biggest advantages of joining a liveaboard is the chance to watch the sunrise at Padar Island without the back-and-forth hassle of a speedboat tour. Speedboat trips usually can't arrange sunrise viewing, but a liveaboard allows us to witness Padar's magnificent dawn. Additionally, the evening sunset and starry night sky left us lingering in awe.

![左：帕達爾日出/中：有成群蝙蝠飛過，可惜太暗沒拍出/右：蘭科洞穴游泳（Tyty Chow提供）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_501ebd9a73e18b60967656df34adeb8f.jpg)

Left: Sunrise at Padar / Center: A flock of bats flew by, but it was too dark to capture / Right: Swimming at Rangko Cave (Photo by Tyty Chow)

However, the two-day, one-night itinerary felt a bit too short, especially when we encountered a light afternoon rain that made the sea slightly choppy, slowing the boat and reducing our schedule. Based on this experience, I recommend that anyone planning a Komodo trip opt for a three-day, two-night or longer itinerary, especially if you're interested in diving or snorkeling. A two-day trip is indeed a bit rushed to fully experience all the highlights of Komodo Island.
