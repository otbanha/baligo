# Complete Guide to Kuber ATV in Ubud: Ride Through Colonial-Era Tunnels and Into Waterfalls on Bali's Top Adventure

If you only lounge on the beach in Bali, you're missing half the fun. Looking for a different thrill, **Kuber Bali Adventure** is one of the most talked-about ATV experiences in Ubud. The four-wheel buggy ride takes you from rice paddies into the jungle, through tunnels, and straight into the spray of a waterfall. On Trip.com, Kuber's park tickets have been booked by over 3K travelers, earning an overall rating of 4.5/5, rated as Outstanding.

## What is Kuber Bali Adventure?

Kuber's base is in Payangan, north of Ubud—about a 20-minute drive from Ubud town center and roughly an hour from Kuta, Legian, and Seminyak. This location keeps you away from the crowds, with the entire route retaining a rustic countryside feel.

## Route Highlights: Four Landscapes in One Ride

**1. Rice Paddies and Villages**
The ride starts along lush green rice paddy paths and through rural villages, where you can catch glimpses of local life—a gentle warm-up to the adventure.

**2. Jungle and Rivers**
Next, you dive into muddy jungle trails, crossing rivers, climbing slopes, and navigating ravines. This is where the off-road fun really kicks in.

**3. Dutch Colonial-Era Tunnels**
This is Kuber's most famous stretch. Legend has it these tunnels were hideouts during the colonial period. Riding inside, the tropical heat instantly gives way to cool air, with water dripping from the rocky walls—an incredibly atmospheric experience.

**4. Waterfall Sprint**
The final stretch takes you directly under a natural waterfall, where the spray hits you—the most exhilarating finish to the entire ride.

## How the Tour Works?

- **Check-in and Safety Briefing**: Arrive about 30 minutes early to check in, attend the safety briefing, and get familiar with the vehicle in the practice area.
- **Gear Collection**: Helmets, rain boots, and other equipment are provided on-site.
- **Departure**: The most common ride lasts about 1.5 hours.
- **End of Ride**: Showers, towels, changing rooms, and lockers are available. Clean up and enjoy a meal.

## Age Requirements

The venue accepts participants aged 6 to 65, suitable for both beginners and experienced riders. Age restrictions may vary for single riders and tandem rides depending on the package—please check the details before booking.

## What to Wear and Prepare

This is a full-on mud and water adventure—the dirtier, the better. Here’s what to bring:

- A set of **clothes you don’t mind getting dirty**, plus a **change of clean clothes** and a **towel** to change into after the ride
- Sunscreen
- A waterproof phone pouch, or simply leave valuables in the on-site lockers
- Some small change to buy drinks on-site

## Quick Tips

- If you have a spinal injury, this type of off-road activity is not recommended.
- Listen carefully to the instructor's briefing before departure, wear your gear properly, and follow the guide's pace to have a fun and safe ride.
- If you want to capture videos, bring an action camera. On-site filming services are available, but travelers note that during peak season you may have to queue, so it's best to ask as soon as you arrive if you're interested.
- There may be a short wait for your scheduled time slot. It's best to schedule this activity in the morning when your itinerary is more flexible, so it won't disrupt your later plans.
