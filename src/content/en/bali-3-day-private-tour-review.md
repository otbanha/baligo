### How to Plan Your Day-by-Day Itinerary?

**Day 1** Depart for a guided tour of the Gates of Heaven at Lempuyang Temple and Tirta Gangga Water Garden, then enjoy lunch. In the afternoon, head to Goa Raja Waterfall. Optional add-ons include a jungle swing and coffee plantation (Satria Agrowisata in Gianyar Regency), ending the day at the Tegalalang Rice Terraces in Ubud.

**Day 2** Take a ride for a guided sunrise jeep tour of Mount Batur, followed by a visit to the Batur Natural Hot Springs and the Ulun Danu Batur Temple. After lunch, head to Tibumana Waterfall.

**Day 3** Depart by car for a guided tour of three artisan villages—Mas, Batubulan, and Celuk (showcasing Bali's gold and silver jewelry, batik, and wood carving crafts). Then visit Kanto Lampo Waterfall and the Ubud Monkey Forest. After lunch, head to Uluwatu Temple to enjoy a Kecak dance open-air theater performance before returning.
