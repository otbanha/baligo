Nusa Lembongan (also known as Lembongan Island) and its neighboring islands—Ceningan Island (Nusa Ceningan) and Nusa Penida—attract a more niche crowd of travelers with their crystal-clear turquoise waters, laid-back coastal village atmosphere, and unique island adventure experiences. However, these three islands off the coast of Bali have now become must-visit destinations for any vacation. Whether you choose a day trip to Lembongan and Ceningan or a two-day deep dive into Nusa Penida, these islands will add endless color to your Bali getaway.

Nusa Lembongan is an island located southeast of Bali, reachable by a 30-minute boat ride from Sanur Harbor. Covering just 8 square kilometers, its main economic activities are tourism and seaweed farming.

Upon arriving at Nusa Lembongan, you'll feel a completely different, relaxing atmosphere compared to the busy beaches of Kuta, Seminyak, or Canggu. Here, you can choose to actively explore the island (island hopping) and join activities, or simply unwind by your accommodation's pool or at a restaurant. It's a great way to take a short break and recharge, allowing you to fully relax before returning to daily life.

## Nusa Lembongan's Captivating Attractions

Nusa Lembongan covers about 6.15 square kilometers and takes just 30 minutes to reach by speedboat. For travelers short on time, it's an ideal choice—you can explore the entire island in a day, whereas Nusa Penida requires at least two days to cover the main sights on its east and west coasts. As the most developed of the three islands, Nusa Lembongan boasts a variety of resorts, restaurants, and beach clubs, making it easy to relax and soak up the sun on the sand.

Upon arriving at Nusa Lembongan, visitors are typically dropped off at the island's main areas—Jungut Batu Beach or Mushroom Bay—both of which offer plenty of accommodations and dining options. If you plan to stay overnight, booking ahead is a must, so you can drop your bags and start exploring right away.

## Beach Exploration

Heading south by bike, you'll discover some intriguing beaches like Sandy Bay, Devil's Tear, and Dream Beach. Sandy Bay is the best spot for sunset views, so save it for last.

![Sandy Bay有個beach club](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_60e81c23058acdc6be657d2a801fb230.jpg)

Sandy Bay has a beach club

Devil's Tear, known in Chinese as 'The Devil's Tear,' is a rocky cliff that meets the ocean. Standing here, you can take in sweeping panoramic sea views from above. When the timing is right, big waves crash against the rocks, sending up sprays of water—a perfect Instagram photo spot. However, be careful not to stand too close, as sudden large waves can be incredibly powerful. There have been several tragic incidents where people were swept into the sea, so stay safe!

https://youtube.com/shorts/exdiGPLkVjw?si=sbLmSwieLvWyJ2uL

Dream Beach is a popular spot outside the main area of the island, though it can get a bit crowded. A morning visit makes for a more pleasant experience. Stroll over to the rocky area, where you'll find interesting photo spots like rock formations or small caves. There's also a cliff jumping point here, but it's best to check with a local guide to ensure it's safe for diving.

## Mangrove Tour

Heading north by bike, you'll reach the serene mangrove area of Nusa Lembongan. It's generally less crowded than the southern part, making it a perfect spot for those who enjoy peace and quiet.

https://youtube.com/shorts/yerwXithouw?si=nDV7gK4eyEVeKb16

Once you arrive on Nusa Lembongan, you'll find several ways to explore this enchanting mangrove forest. You can rent a kayak and paddle leisurely through the calm waterways, soaking in the tranquility surrounded by mangroves while listening to the natural symphony of birdsong and sea breeze. For a deeper understanding of this ecosystem, consider joining a small-group guided boat tour.

Typically, when visitors arrive on Nusa Lembongan, locals will enthusiastically recommend various mangrove boat tours. It's best to opt for a small private boat, which usually accommodates 3-4 people with an experienced boatman. A reasonable price is around 100,000 Indonesian Rupiah per person after negotiation. During the half-hour journey, you can get up close to the stunning views of the pristine mangroves, spot various water birds, and even see crabs scurrying among the roots.

A special tip: aim to visit during **low tide**, as the mangrove roots are fully exposed above the water, revealing the most captivating scenery. The boatman can also navigate through shallower waters, allowing you to observe this unique ecosystem up close.

## A Leisurely Day

Spending a leisurely afternoon at a beachfront restaurant in the Jungut Batu or Mushroom Bay area is the most comfortable way to go. There are plenty of reasonably priced seaside eateries here. Since these are also the main arrival and departure points, with the harbor located in both spots, you can observe the ebb and flow of people throughout the day.

![悠閒的一天](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a1f56eb2c4193e81b53d33fffa1b1f7c.jpg)

At sunset, Jungut Batu is a great place to watch the sun go down. If you've spent the entire afternoon here, transitioning from the bustling activity to complete tranquility is a simple yet pure delight.

For a different perspective on the sunset, take a short hike to Panorama Point and admire the beautiful view from above.

## Surfing

If you can't resist the call of the waves, there are three popular surf spots near Jungut Batu Beach: **Playgrounds**, **Lacerations**, and **Shipwrecks**. You can find guidance and rental services at the surf center on Jungut Batu Beach.

## Best Choices for Diving and Snorkeling

For adventurers who love the underwater world, Lembongan Island and its neighboring islands, Ceningan and Nusa Penida, offer abundant diving and snorkeling spots. Mangrove Point is the closest snorkeling site to the shore of Lembongan, perfect for travelers who want a quick glimpse of the underwater beauty. Around Ceningan and Nusa Penida, there are also several snorkeling and diving locations, such as the famous Manta Point, where you can swim alongside graceful giant manta rays—an unforgettable experience.

https://youtube.com/shorts/a4mL69nASLY?si=UciZXsalBlU3fOjo

A half-day snorkeling trip on Lembongan Island, including hotel pickup, snorkeling gear, boat fees, and three dive spots, costs around 150,000 Indonesian Rupiah (USD$10) per person.

## Water Activities and Adventures

Beyond diving and snorkeling, Lembongan Island offers a variety of water activities to suit all interests. You can try waterskiing, stand-up paddleboarding (SUP), banana boat rides, or even the unique experience of seawalking. Additionally, you can join a boat tour around the island to admire its stunning scenery from different angles, enjoying close contact with the water and the charm of the ocean.

## Getting Around

The best way to explore Lembongan and Ceningan Islands is by bicycle or renting a scooter. Like Nusa Penida, these islands do not have ride-hailing services such as Gojek or Grab. If you prefer not to ride, another unique local transport option is the modified pickup truck shuttle service.

![當地的 pick-up truck](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_38697b346c428fcbc1d2bff17b0e777d.jpg)

Local pickup truck

Transportation costs on Lembongan Island:

- Scooter rental: 75,000–100,000 IDR per day (150–200 TWD / $5–$7 USD)
- Pickup truck: 50,000 IDR per trip (100 TWD / $3 USD)
- Private car hire: Limited options and more expensive, around 800,000 IDR for 8 hours (1,600 TWD / $51 USD)

Renting a scooter is not only budget-friendly but also gives you the freedom to explore every corner of the island at your own pace. For those who aren't comfortable riding or are traveling with more luggage, the truck shuttle service offers a fun local experience.

**Friendly reminder**: When renting a scooter, make sure to check its condition and keep the receipt. If you opt for the truck shuttle, it's best to confirm the price and destination with the driver in advance to avoid any miscommunication.

## The Future of Nusa Lembongan

Nusa Lembongan is set to become Bali's most digitally advanced tourist destination. In 2024, the local government and business sector launched the "Digital Island Nusa Lembongan" initiative, aiming to enhance the travel experience and boost the local economy through a digital payment system. The plan covers transportation, dining, accommodation, and shops, fully supporting cashless payments. Additionally, Bank Indonesia will provide financial education to integrate digital technology into daily life. This project is expected to improve the quality of tourism on Nusa Lembongan, increase visitor stay duration, and drive growth in the Klungkung Regency's original local revenue (PAD).

In the future, Nusa Lembongan will become Bali's most digitally connected tourist destination. Through the "Digital Island Nusa Lembongan" initiative, the island will gradually implement a fully digital payment system—from booking ferry tickets and island transportation to attraction entry, hotels, restaurants, and shops—making it easier for travelers to plan and organize their trips. Furthermore, the strengthening of digital infrastructure will address current signal dead zones, ensuring stable network connectivity across all areas. By driving these changes, Nusa Lembongan will become a world-class travel destination, extending visitor stays and integrating the local community into the digital economy, spurring economic growth and improving quality of life across the region.

## Nusa Lembongan Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. **Is Nusa Lembongan worth visiting?**

Absolutely! Nusa Lembongan is beloved for its serene atmosphere, offering a quieter escape compared to Bali, with plenty of attractions to explore. If you add nearby Nusa Ceningan and Nusa Penida, you can easily fill a week-long itinerary. The island boasts thrilling ocean activities like swimming with manta rays and cliff jumping, along with numerous beautiful beaches to enjoy.

### 2. **What is Nusa Lembongan famous for?**

Nusa Lembongan is known for its Yellow Bridge, seaweed farms, white sand beaches, crystal-clear waters, stunning sunsets, picturesque views, excellent cafes, and its proximity to Nusa Ceningan and Nusa Penida. These highlights draw travelers from around the world.

### 3. **Which is better: Nusa Penida or Nusa Lembongan?**

Both islands are worth visiting! But if you have to choose just one, Nusa Lembongan is more developed with better facilities and more activities. If time allows, we recommend visiting both to experience their unique charms.

### 4. **How long is the ferry from Bali to Nusa Lembongan?**

The ferry ride is short, taking approximately 30 to 45 minutes from Bali to Nusa Lembongan.

### 5. **How many days should you stay on Nusa Lembongan?**

You can explore the main attractions of Nusa Lembongan in a single day, but if you want to take your time and soak in the island's beauty, a 3-day stay is recommended. If you also plan to visit Nusa Ceningan and Nusa Penida, allow around 4 days. Spending at least one night on the island is essential to truly experience its stunning sunsets and laid-back island vibe.

### 6. **Is Nusa Lembongan suitable for a family trip?**

Nusa Lembongan offers plenty of family-friendly activities, such as snorkeling, playing on the beach, and watching the sunset, making it a great choice for traveling with kids. The island's facilities are also relatively well-developed, ideal for family vacations.

### 7. **Is Nusa Lembongan suitable for a digital nomad lifestyle?**

While the internet connection on Nusa Lembongan is weaker than in Bali, digital infrastructure has been improving in recent years. Some areas now have stable internet, making it suitable for short-term work or a digital nomad lifestyle.

### 8. **What are the must-see attractions on Nusa Lembongan?**

In addition to the Yellow Bridge and beautiful beaches, Devil's Tears is a famous natural attraction on Nusa Lembongan. Waves crashing against the cliffs create spectacular sprays of mist, making it a truly worthwhile sight.

### 9. **How do you get from Bali to Nusa Lembongan?**

You can take a ferry from Sanur Harbor in Bali to reach Nusa Lembongan. Multiple ferries run daily, making transportation very convenient.

### 10. **What is the best time to visit Nusa Lembongan?**

The dry season (April to October) is the best time to visit Nusa Lembongan. During this period, the weather is sunny, the seawater is crystal clear, and it's perfect for outdoor activities like snorkeling and island hopping.

## Tips

1. **Book transportation and accommodation in advance**: As the number of tourists on Nusa Lembongan increases year by year, it's advisable to book transportation and accommodation ahead of time to avoid last-minute hassles.
2. **Bring cash**: Although there are a few ATMs on the island, they sometimes run out of cash. Carrying enough Indonesian rupiah will ensure a smooth trip.
3. **Protect yourself from the sun**: The sun on the island is intense. Be sure to apply sunscreen and reapply regularly to avoid sunburn.
4. **Follow environmental rules**: Please take care of the marine environment, do not litter, and adhere to local eco-friendly guidelines to help preserve this beautiful tropical paradise.
