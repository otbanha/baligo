# Electronic payments are everywhere in Indonesia and Bali! How to pay? A complete guide to QRIS and GoPay e-wallets

Still carrying a thick stack of Indonesian rupiah every day when traveling to Bali?

Now in Indonesia, especially Bali, more and more restaurants, cafes, spas, attractions, shops, and even street vendors are using electronic payments. Some places will even tell you directly:

**"Cashless Only"**

Meaning: **Only cashless electronic payments are accepted—no cash!**

If it's your first time in Bali, you might easily run into the awkward situation of "having money but not being able to pay."

And the most important keyword for electronic payments in Indonesia is:

## 🇮🇩 QRIS

Pair it with an e-wallet like **GoPay**, and traveling in Bali now means you can significantly reduce the need to carry large amounts of cash.

Here's a complete guide for you:
**What is QRIS? How do foreign tourists use it? How to register for GoPay? And how can travelers from Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore, and Malaysia make payments?**

## 🇮🇩 What is QRIS? Why Are QR Codes Everywhere in Bali?

QRIS stands for **Quick Response Code Indonesian Standard**—think of it as Indonesia's unified QR Code payment standard.

In Indonesia, you'll find QRIS codes at restaurants, cafes, convenience stores, spas, small shops, tourist attractions, and even street stalls.

As long as your payment app supports QRIS, you can simply scan the merchant's QR code, enter the amount, and complete the payment.

Bank Indonesia states that QRIS is now widely used across all provinces and cities in Indonesia, including micro-enterprises and tourist destinations.

So in Bali, you'll see:

**Scan QRIS / QRIS / QRIS Payment**

Basically, it's telling you:

📱 **"You can pay by scanning a QR Code!"**

***

## 💡 So, how exactly can foreign tourists use QRIS?

This is where most first-time visitors to Bali get confused.

Because:

**"QRIS is accepted" ≠ "Any country's credit card can directly scan QRIS."**

Indonesia has now launched **QRIS Cross-Border**, allowing travelers from select partner countries to use their own payment apps to scan QRIS in Indonesia.

Bank Indonesia has partnered with:

🇹🇭 Thailand
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇯🇵 Japan
🇰🇷 South Korea
🇨🇳 China

Wait for the market to establish QR cross-border payment cooperation.

In other words, travelers from different countries don't use it in exactly the same way.

***

## 🇸🇬 Travelers from Singapore: Can Use QRIS Cross-Border Directly

If you're traveling from Singapore to Bali, using QRIS is relatively convenient.

Currently, Bank Indonesia lists the following participating payment service providers from Singapore:

- OCBC
- UOB
- DBS

Travelers from Singapore can use supported payment apps to scan Indonesian merchants' QRIS codes directly.

When making a payment, enter the amount in Indonesian Rupiah. The app will convert it to your local currency based on the system exchange rate. After confirming, enter your PIN to complete the transaction.

***

## 🇲🇾 Travelers from Malaysia: DuitNow QR and QRIS Are Now Interoperable

Travelers from Malaysia also have it very convenient.

Indonesia's QRIS and Malaysia's **DuitNow QR** have established a cross-border payment connection.

PayNet also explains that when Malaysian travelers visit Indonesia, they can directly use supported mobile banking apps to scan QRIS for payment.

Currently, Bank Indonesia lists related Malaysian payment service providers including CIMB, Hong Leong Bank, Maybank, Public Bank, Touch 'n Go eWallet, BigPay, and others.

So if you're already used to using mobile payments in Malaysia, you don't necessarily need to prepare a lot of extra cash when traveling to Bali.

***

## 🇹🇼 What about travelers from Taiwan and 🇭🇰 Hong Kong?

This is something you need to pay close attention to.

Currently, Taiwan and Hong Kong are not included in Bank Indonesia's list of partner markets for QRIS Cross-Border.

Therefore, **general banking apps or local payment apps from Taiwan and Hong Kong cannot simply scan and pay a QRIS code just because they see one.**

However, this doesn't mean travelers from Taiwan and Hong Kong can't use QRIS at all.

For now, the most practical approach is:

## 📱 GoPay + Visa/Mastercard

GoPay now supports linking a Visa or Mastercard credit/debit card within the GoPay app, allowing you to use that card as the payment source for QRIS transactions.

In other words:

**Travelers from Taiwan and Hong Kong → GoPay → Link Visa/Mastercard → Scan QRIS**

This is a workaround worth exploring.

However, there is a very important limitation here:

## ⚠️ GoPay Credit/Debit Card QRIS Payments Are Not Unlimited

According to GoPay's official guidelines, when using a credit or debit card to pay via QRIS:

- Supports Visa and Mastercard
- Must be done within the **GoPay App**
- The traveler scans the merchant's QRIS code themselves
- Currently, card-based QRIS transactions must be **under Rp250,000**
- Merchants scanning the QR code on your phone in reverse is not supported for this card payment method
- Some transactions may require entering a CVV or OTP.

So for higher-value expenses like hotels, spas, or restaurants, **do not rely solely on 'GoPay linked to a credit card' as your only payment method.**

It's best to be prepared with:

💳 Physical credit card
💰 A small amount of cash
📱 GoPay / other digital payments

A combination of all three.
