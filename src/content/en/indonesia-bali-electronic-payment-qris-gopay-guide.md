# Electronic payments are everywhere in Indonesia and Bali! How to pay? A complete guide to QRIS and GoPay e-wallets

Still carrying a thick stack of Indonesian rupiah every day when traveling to Bali?

Now in Indonesia, especially Bali, more and more restaurants, cafes, spas, tourist attractions, shops, and even street vendors are adopting electronic payments. Some places will even tell you directly:

**'Cashless Only'**

Meaning: **Only cashless electronic payments are accepted, no cash!**

If it's your first time in Bali, you can easily run into the awkward situation of 'having money but being unable to pay.'

And the most important keyword for electronic payments in Indonesia is:

## 🇮🇩 QRIS

With the addition of e-wallets like **GoPay**, traveling in Bali now allows you to significantly reduce the need to carry large amounts of cash.

This article has it all covered for you:
**What is QRIS? How can foreign tourists use it? How to register for GoPay? How can travelers from Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore, and Malaysia make payments?**

## 🇮🇩 What is QRIS? Why Are QR Codes Everywhere in Bali?

QRIS stands for **Quick Response Code Indonesian Standard**, which can be simply understood as Indonesia's unified QR Code payment standard.

In Indonesia, you can find QRIS QR codes at restaurants, cafes, convenience stores, spas, small shops, tourist attractions, and even street stalls.

As long as your payment app supports QRIS, you can scan the merchant's QR code, enter the amount, and complete the payment.

Bank Indonesia states that QRIS is widely used across all provinces and cities in Indonesia, including micro-enterprises and tourist destinations.

So in Bali, you'll see:

**Scan QRIS / QRIS / QRIS Payment**

Basically, it's telling you:

📱 **"You can pay by scanning a QR Code!"**

***

## 💡 So, how exactly can foreign tourists use QRIS?

This is the part that confuses most first-time visitors to Bali.

Because:

**"QRIS is accepted" ≠ "Any country's credit card can directly scan QRIS."**

Indonesia has now launched **QRIS Cross-Border**, allowing travelers from select partner countries to use their own payment apps to scan and pay via QRIS in Indonesia.

Currently, Bank Indonesia has partnered with:

🇹🇭 Thailand
🇲🇾 Malaysia
🇸🇬 Singapore
🇯🇵 Japan
🇰🇷 South Korea
🇨🇳 China

QR cross-border payment cooperation is being established in the market.

In other words, travelers of different nationalities do not use it in exactly the same way.

***

## 🇸🇬 Travelers from Singapore: Can Use QRIS Cross-Border Directly

If you're traveling from Singapore to Bali, using QRIS is relatively convenient.

Currently, Bank Indonesia lists the following participating payment service providers from Singapore:

- OCBC
- UOB
- DBS

Travelers from Singapore can use supported payment apps to scan Indonesian merchants' QRIS codes directly.

When making a payment, enter the amount in Indonesian Rupiah. The app will convert it to your local currency based on the system rate. After confirming, enter your PIN to complete the transaction.

***

## 🇲🇾 Travelers from Malaysia: DuitNow QR and QRIS Are Now Interoperable

Travelers from Malaysia also have it very convenient.

Indonesia's QRIS and Malaysia's **DuitNow QR** have established a cross-border payment connection.

PayNet also explains that when Malaysian travelers visit Indonesia, they can directly use supported mobile banking apps to scan QRIS for payment.

Currently, Bank Indonesia lists relevant Malaysian payment service providers including CIMB, Hong Leong Bank, Maybank, Public Bank, Touch 'n Go eWallet, BigPay, and others.

So if you're already used to using mobile payments in Malaysia, you don't necessarily need to prepare a lot of extra cash when traveling to Bali.

***

## 🇹🇼 What about travelers from Taiwan and 🇭🇰 Hong Kong?

This is something you need to pay special attention to.

Currently, Taiwan and Hong Kong are not included in the QRIS Cross-Border cooperation market list designated by Bank Indonesia.

Therefore, **general banking apps or local payment apps from Taiwan or Hong Kong cannot simply scan and pay via QRIS just because they see the QR code.**

However, this does not mean that travelers from Taiwan and Hong Kong cannot use QRIS at all.

For now, the most practical approach is:

## 📱 GoPay + Visa/Mastercard

GoPay now supports linking Visa or Mastercard credit/debit cards within the GoPay app, allowing you to use those cards as the payment source for QRIS transactions.

In other words:

**Travelers from Taiwan and Hong Kong → GoPay → Link Visa/Mastercard → Scan QRIS**

is a viable option worth exploring.

However, there is one very important limitation here:

## ⚠️ GoPay Credit/Debit Card Payments via QRIS Are Not Unlimited

According to GoPay's official guidelines, when using a credit or debit card to pay via QRIS:

- Supports Visa and Mastercard
- Must be done within the **GoPay App**
- The traveler scans the merchant's QRIS code themselves
- Currently, card payments via QRIS must be **under Rp250,000**
- Merchants scanning the QR Code on your phone is not supported for this card payment method
- Some transactions may require entering a CVV or OTP.

So for higher-value expenses like hotels, spas, or restaurants, **do not rely solely on 'GoPay linked to a credit card' as your only payment method.**

It's best to prepare:

💳 Physical credit card
💰 A small amount of cash
📱 GoPay / other e-wallets

A combination of all three.

### ❓ Can AlipayHK scan QRIS for payment?

AlipayHK currently **cannot** make direct payments at merchants in Bali (Indonesia).

You can **transfer money from AlipayHK to GoPay**, then use GoPay's QRIS to make purchases.

Only **Alipay Mainland (China version)** supports Indonesia's QRIS payment system.

## 📲 How to Pay with QRIS at a Store?

It's actually very simple.

Once you see the QRIS code:

### Step 1

Open the GoPay app.

### Step 2

Tap on QRIS.

### Step 3

Scan the store's QRIS code.

### Step 4

Enter the amount to pay.

### Step 5

Confirm the payment method.

If using a linked Visa or Mastercard, select that credit or debit card.

### Step 6

Complete the OTP/CVV/PIN verification as prompted by the app.

Once done, the payment is successful.

## ⚠️ Traveling to Bali: Don't Rely Solely on QRIS for Payments

Although QRIS is becoming more widespread, I still wouldn't recommend traveling to Bali without carrying any cash.

The reason is simple:

Some places might:

❌ Not have QRIS
❌ Have a temporarily broken QRIS system
❌ Have poor internet connection
❌ Only accept cash
❌ Only accept physical credit cards
❌ Have a QRIS transaction limit that exceeds your current payment tool's cap

Cash is still very handy, especially at small vendors, in remote areas, at traditional markets, or for special events.

So, a more ideal approach is:

### 📱 Digital Payments

Use at restaurants, cafes, spas, shops, and some attractions.

### 💳 Credit Cards

Hotels, department stores, larger restaurants, etc.

### 💵 Cash

For tips, small vendors, traditional markets, transportation, and when electronic payments aren't accepted.
