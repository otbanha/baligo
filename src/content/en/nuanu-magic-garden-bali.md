Located near Tanah Lot Temple, **Magic Garden** is a natural park that opened in November 2024, perfect for families and kids. This 3,600-square-meter attraction offers rich plant life and interactive experiences that are both educational and fun.

### Highlights of Magic Garden:

- Nature and Ecology: Key features include an **Orchid Garden** and **Butterfly Garden**, where kids can observe butterflies up close and learn about their role in maintaining ecological balance. The park also has a **Plant Museum** displaying over 400 rare plant species and 500 orchid varieties, helping children discover nature and conservation while having fun.

- Interactive and Educational Experiences: Magic Garden is more than just a viewing space—it offers hands-on activities like ecological exploration and plant knowledge sessions, allowing kids to learn through play.

![Magic Garden 的特色：](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f0a2c4abe2cc59a6ae87a92225e9f4b9.jpg)

- Sustainability Concept: The park promotes eco-friendliness and sustainability, preserving 70% green space and planting over 15,000 trees, showcasing a harmonious coexistence between people and nature.
- Affordable Tickets: Admission is just IDR 150,000 (about USD$10), making it a great choice for family outings.

![Magic Garden 的特色：](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a64d7e55ca50092678f3a0030d627d61.jpg)

### Who It's For:

- Families with children
- Nature lovers
- Eco-educators

### Nearby Attractions:

After visiting Magic Garden, you can also head to the nearby **Luna Beach Club** or **The Dome multimedia exhibition** to create more unforgettable memories for the whole family!

Magic Garden is one of the highlights of Nuanu Creative City, blending nature, education, and entertainment—perfect for visitors looking to explore ecological beauty and learn about sustainability. 🌿
