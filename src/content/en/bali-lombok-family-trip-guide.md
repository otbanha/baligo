### Bali Transportation Tips:

We originally planned to hire a private driver for our entire trip in Bali, especially thinking it would be more convenient with two kids—but reality had other plans! On the first day, we were shocked by the chaotic traffic on the island—gridlock was everywhere! 🤯 If we had stuck with a driver, all our time would have been wasted in traffic jams.

So, we quickly changed our strategy and decided to rent a scooter as our main mode of transport. For our family of four, we specifically rented a larger scooter, with two adults and two kids riding together. The kids easily fell asleep on the scooter, so we sandwiched them between us for safety. The road conditions on the island were indeed challenging, but renting a scooter gave us much more flexibility—especially when moving between different attractions, it was so much easier and more convenient! 🚴‍♀️🚴‍♂️

If we needed to go to a farther destination, no worries! We used ride-hailing services like GRAB or GO-JEK, which are well-established in Bali and super convenient—just call and they come right away.

Bali's most iconic seaside temple, combining stunning ocean views with majestic architecture, truly opened our eyes. The kids had a great time here, especially drawn to this direct encounter with nature.

## August 9 (Fri) Tirta Empul Temple + Alas Harum Bali (Rice Terraces & Luwak Coffee) + Lunch at Cretya

Our first stop today was the famous **Tirta Empul Temple**. This temple is renowned for its holy spring water, which local devotees believe has the power to purify the mind and body. Though we are not Hindu, experiencing this unique cultural ritual felt truly special. The kids were also fascinated by the mystical atmosphere and stunning architecture.

Next, we visited **Alas Harum Bali**, known not only for its breathtaking rice terraces but also for the chance to taste local **Luwak Coffee**. The terrace views were stunning, and we snapped plenty of photos. The kids were curious about the terraces, while we enjoyed a fragrant cup of Luwak coffee. There were also fun activities like the sky swing, making for a relaxing and joyful time.

For lunch, we chose to dine at **Cretya**, which offers beautiful rice terrace views and a rich menu. Not only was the food delicious, but the restaurant's ambiance also allowed us to soak in the peaceful surroundings—a true feast for both the eyes and the taste buds.

## August 10 (Sat) Kanto Lampo Waterfall + Ubud Palace + Ubud Traditional Market

Today we visited the **Kanto Lampo Waterfall**, hidden in the jungle. The water cascades gently down the rocks, creating a beautiful natural landscape. The kids had a blast playing in the water, and the natural surroundings let us soak in Bali's ecological charm. The waterfall has a moderate flow, allowing visitors to get close and feel its refreshing coolness.

https://youtube.com/shorts/w-gzis-G6OI

Afterward, we toured the **Ubud Palace**, located in the heart of Ubud. This ancient palace features intricate carvings and traditional Balinese architecture, making it a great place to learn about local culture. Though the palace isn't large, its history and architectural beauty left a strong impression on us.

Next, we headed to the famous **Ubud Traditional Market**. While the variety of goods is vast, the prices aren't cheap, and the sellers generally come across as indifferent, making bargaining a challenge. Tip: If you're just looking for souvenirs, this might not be the best spot. However, on our way back to **Puri Padma Hotel**, we discovered a small market where prices were much more reasonable, and we picked up some affordable, quality handicrafts. 🎁

## August 20 (Tuesday) Coffee Shops, Traditional Markets, and Batuidung Hill Views

Today's itinerary was all about relaxation. We started at a stylish, artsy coffee shop called **Konyu Coffee & Space**, whose unique decor and serene atmosphere instantly won us over. The brunch here was delicious, making it a perfect spot to enjoy a coffee break in a cozy corner during our travels. Whether you're a coffee lover or a brunch enthusiast, this place is well worth a visit.

Next, we headed to two local traditional markets: **Sindu Traditional Market** and **Cakranegara Traditional Market**. These markets are very authentic, with hardly any tourists, and they're filled with the daily life of Lombok's residents. The prices here are very affordable, making them ideal for buying local fruits, vegetables, or handmade crafts. We discovered many interesting little items and enjoyed the fun of bargaining with locals, which felt genuine and close to everyday life.

In the afternoon, we took on the challenge of reaching the legendary **Batuidung Hill**. The journey was tough, requiring a winding mountain road to get there, but once we stood at the top, the view made all the effort worthwhile. This hidden hill offers breathtaking scenery with an excellent vantage point overlooking the vast natural landscape. The tranquility and beauty here provided a perfect ending to our day's adventure.
