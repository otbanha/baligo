# Bali Wins 6 Awards at the 2026 World Travel Awards, from Boutique Resorts and Water Parks to Wedding Destinations

The results for the 2026 World Travel Awards (WTA) Asia region have been announced, with Indonesia securing a total of 8 awards, 6 of which belong to Bali. In an official statement, Bali Governor Wayan Koster noted that these 6 awards present an opportunity to enhance the quality of tourism services and international competitiveness, while continuing to protect the natural environment and Bali's cultural heritage.

## Full List of Winners

All 6 awards are in the Asia category, covering a comprehensive range of travel experiences:

- Mandapa, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve (Ubud): Asia's Best Boutique Resort
- Anantara Uluwatu Bali Resort: Asia's Best Family Villa Resort
- Ulaman Eco Luxury Resort: Asia's Best Green Resort
- Sofitel Bali Nusa Dua Beach Resort: Asia's Best Resort
- Waterbom Bali: Asia's Best Water Park
- Bali: Asia's Best Wedding Destination

Two other Asia-region awards for Indonesia went to Bunaken Oasis Dive Resort & Spa in North Sulawesi and Bawah Reserve in the Riau Islands.

## Other Recognition and Policy Direction

Ubud earned recognition from *Travel + Leisure*'s World Best Awards in 2022, while Bali ranked first in the Popular Destinations category of Tripadvisor's 2026 Travelers' Choice Awards. Combined with this latest WTA win, international accolades have been steadily accumulating.

Koster explained that Bali's tourism policy is guided by the vision of "Nangun Sat Kerthi Loka Bali," emphasizing culture, environmental conservation, and resident well-being. It is rooted in the philosophies of Tri Hita Karana and Sad Kerthi, with institutional support from Regional Regulation No. 5 of 2020 (Cultural Tourism Standards) and Governor Regulation No. 28 (Tourism Governance). The focus is on quality and sustainability rather than tourist numbers, with fees collected from foreign visitors allocated to cultural and environmental preservation.

> Editor's Note: Bali won Asia's Best Wedding Destination consecutively from 2019 to 2023, a total of five years. In 2024, Cebu took the title, ending Bali's five-year streak. Bali reclaimed the award in 2025 and won again in 2026, marking its seventh win in eight years.
