## Family Activities

The best activities for families with kids in Ubud:

### Alas Harum (👉🏼 Map: [Popular Spots Around Ubud](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1jrOaKVOZ4jpcB_kprr67w3ltg3WPbf4&usp=sharing))

![Alas Harum （地圖：烏布周邊熱門景點）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_bc22b17fd8a9f30fd3626ee79eab7984.jpg)

Suitable age: 1 year and up

Near Ubud’s famous Tegalalang Rice Terraces, there’s a fantastic family-friendly spot called the 'Alas Harum' complex. It’s about a 20-minute drive from central Ubud and is best known for its stunning rice terrace views.

This place offers not only stunning terraced rice fields but also family-friendly outdoor activities like flying foxes, swings, and the latest sky biking. Kids can enjoy thrilling adventures here, while parents can relax in the peaceful surroundings, making quality family time together.

Alas Harum is more than just an adventure park—it's rich in cultural heritage. The area around the park is dotted with temples, statues, and other historical sites that showcase Bali's long history and culture. For families, Alas Harum is an ideal spot to explore Ubud, combining breathtaking scenery, exciting activities, and a wealth of cultural experiences.

### Ubud Art Market (👉🏼 Map: [Ubud Center](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1jrOaKVOZ4jpcB_kprr67w3ltg3WPbf4&usp=sharing))

![烏布藝術市場 （地圖：烏布中心）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_026ba58f9cd09d22c78e23def9b1c5c2.jpg)

Suitable for ages: 0 and up

At Ubud Market, kids can immerse themselves in a world of vibrant colors. It's filled with a dazzling array of handicrafts, from intricate carvings to delicate textiles, all showcasing Bali's unique cultural charm. Colorful fabrics are woven into various patterns and prints, each radiating the skill and rich history of local artisans.

This market is a true cultural treasure trove. Children can observe, smell, and even experience Bali's unique art and craftsmanship firsthand. They can admire the finesse of ceramic art, feel the intricacy of wood carving, and learn how locals preserve and pass on Bali's cultural traditions through weaving and handicrafts.

At this market, kids can not only pick up unique souvenirs but also appreciate the beauty of culture and the value of traditional art through interactions with local artisans. Such experiences will spark their curiosity about the world and enrich their journey with deeper learning.

### Bali's New Monkey Forest: Kemenuh Monkey River (👉🏼 Map: [Top Attractions Around Ubud](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1jrOaKVOZ4jpcB_kprr67w3ltg3WPbf4&usp=sharing))

Just a 20-minute drive from Ubud, Kemenuh Monkey River is a natural paradise that immerses you in Bali's pristine beauty! The crystal-clear Petanu River winds through the forest, while man-made waterfalls add a refreshing, cool atmosphere.

Here, you can observe long-tailed macaques, rare white monkeys, and flying foxes, all while relaxing in traditional Balinese pavilions, listening to the flowing water and soaking in nature's tranquility.

Now open to visitors! Hours: Daily 9 AM to 6 PM (last entry at 5 PM). Special rates: Adults 80,000 IDR, Children 40,000 IDR

Escape the crowds and experience a unique Melukat water purification blessing ceremony, feeling the spirituality and natural beauty of Bali!

Friendly tip: If you want to take photos with monkeys in Bali, choose this spot—the monkeys here are gentle and won't attack. At Ubud's Monkey Forest, the monkeys tend to snatch things and are more aggressive.

## Bali Reptile Park｜Snakes Around Necks, Giant Lizards in Sight—A Reptilian Wonderland for the Brave

**📍 Address:** Adjacent to Bali Bird Park, Singapadu, Gianyar

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-05/1780206204799-_2026-05-31_12.42.57.png)

Right next to Bali Bird Park, Bali Reptile Park is another fascinating world that both frightens and enthralls kids. The park houses Bali's most extensive collection of reptiles, including various pythons, cobras, monitor lizards (close relatives of Komodo dragons), colorful geckos, and tortoises. Just approaching the display tanks is enough to make the faint-hearted's pulse race.

The most popular experience here is having the staff help you **drape a python over your shoulders and neck for a photo** — the coolness of the snake's body and the strength of its muscles transmit through your skin, creating an indescribably unique sensation and the most thrilling, laughter-filled challenge for kids. After bravely completing it, you're rewarded with that proud, triumphant look on your child's face.

A guide accompanies you throughout, explaining each animal's habits and conservation status in detail, turning this 'courageous journey' into a vivid natural education lesson.

### Kid-Friendly River Tubing

https://youtube.com/shorts/Z_vDb7AbMKg

Suitable age: 4 years and up

While rafting is one of Ubud's most famous activities, it carries certain risks, with safety affected by river conditions. For a safer water experience, cave tubing or river tubing (as shown in the video above) is a relatively safer, more family-friendly option.

It's worth noting that many caves in the Ubud area date back to Japan's brief occupation of Bali. During World War II, Japanese forces carried out construction projects on the island, including building bunkers, caves, and tunnels for defensive purposes. These caves and tunnels were later repurposed or renovated by local residents or government agencies. Some were transformed into tourist attractions, while others were used for irrigation. These caves bear witness to a historical era and have now become part of Bali's history and culture.

Whether it's rafting or river tubing, both water activities in Ubud offer unforgettable experiences for adventurers. They let you feel the thrill of challenge in the river while enjoying the tranquility and vitality bestowed by nature. These activities allow you to interact with nature and history, adding rich experiences and lasting memories to your journey.

## Tips for Family Travel in Ubud

![烏布家庭親子旅遊的注意事項](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a2a659ccd7ce835a19aafcb770b98d37.png)

Here are some things to keep in mind when traveling to Ubud with kids:

- **Book a family-friendly hotel or guesthouse** Ubud offers many hotels and guesthouses, with plenty designed specifically for families. These often provide amenities like babysitting services, kids' clubs, and swimming pools.
- **Consider hiring a private driver** Hiring a private driver is a great way to explore Ubud and its surroundings. It allows you to travel at your own pace and stop for photos and sightseeing along the way.
- **Protect against the sun** Bali's sun is intense, so bring sun protection like hats, sunglasses, and UV-protective clothing, and apply sunscreen frequently to prevent your kids from getting sunburned.
- **Watch food and water hygiene** Children have weaker immune systems and are more prone to illness, so never let them drink water of uncertain cleanliness, and avoid food from street vendors.
- **Be prepared for weather changes** Bali's weather can be unpredictable—sunny one moment and rainy the next—so it's best to carry rain gear and pack light jackets or layers for your kids.

These are some basic tips to keep in mind. Hope you and your kids have a fun and safe time in Ubud, Bali!
