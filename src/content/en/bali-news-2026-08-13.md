# Bali Travel Hot Topics + News Roundup (August 13, 2026)

Here are 5 pieces of news from Bali and its surrounding islands, focusing on traveler safety, inter-island transportation, changes at attractions, and tourism trends.

## 🚨 Ferry Fire in Lombok Strait, Rescue Underway

The medium-to-large ferry KMP Putri Yasmin caught fire in the Lombok Strait. Bali search and rescue teams have deployed helicopters to assist, and authorities are continuing passenger evacuation and follow-up operations. An update from Tribun indicates that 173 people were evacuated that day, highlighting the significant impact of this incident on sea travel between Bali and Lombok.

Traveler advice: If you plan to take a ferry to Lombok, Nusa Penida, or other islands tomorrow, be sure to check the latest ferry schedules and port announcements—don't just rely on your original booking time.

## 🗑️ Gili Trawangan Faces Garbage Pressure, Gili Meno Still Has Water Shortages

In the Gili Islands, Gili Trawangan continues to struggle with garbage issues, while Gili Meno faces ongoing water shortages. The environmental and infrastructure pressures on these two popular islands are once again in the spotlight. For travelers who often arrange island-hopping trips from Bali, this is important supplementary information.

Traveler advice: If your itinerary includes the Gili Islands, it's wise to confirm the water supply conditions at your accommodation in advance and choose operators with strong environmental and waste management practices.

## 🛥️ Nusa Penida Day Trips May Adjust Return Times, Travelers Need to Recheck Ferry Schedules

Nusa Penida is considering adjusting the last ferry departure time for travelers returning to Sanur or Klungkung, potentially moving the latest crossing to 3 PM. While still in the proposal stage, this would already impact how day-trippers plan their itineraries.

Traveler Tip: If you're used to doing Nusa Penida as a round trip in one day, you'll need to pay closer attention to return ferry schedules going forward. You might want to consider turning your island excursion into an overnight trip to avoid rushing for the boat.

## 🏗️ Sira Village Opening Sparks Controversy, Turtle Island Economic Zone Back in the Spotlight

According to Bali.live, Sira Village in Serangan has entered its soft opening phase, but since the Kura Kura Bali economic zone itself remains under review and embroiled in controversy, the launch of this new outlet has stirred up considerable debate. Local councils and some parties have raised questions about the development process, regulations, and land use, keeping this new attraction a hot topic.

Traveler Tip: If you're planning a visit to Sira Village soon to check out the new mall and sea views, be sure to confirm its operating status and nearby traffic conditions beforehand to avoid unexpected closures or construction detours.

## 📈 Bali's Tourism and Construction Sectors Boosted by Summer Holidays, Business Confidence Rises

The Bali branch of Indonesia's central bank reports that, driven by summer holiday tourism, Bali's tourism and construction-related businesses saw a significant uptick in June 2026, with foreign visitor arrivals also growing compared to the previous quarter. This indicates that despite facing various safety and environmental issues, Bali's overall tourism momentum remains strong.

Traveler's tip: During the peak summer and year-end holiday seasons, popular hotels, private car services, and island excursions should be booked well in advance, especially in sought-after areas like Sanur, Canggu, Ubud, and Nusa Penida.
