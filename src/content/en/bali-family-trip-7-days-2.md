Thank you to the group admin and all members for your selfless feedback! This summer's Bali trip made our whole family happy and delighted, especially for the kids—it was a beautiful memory filled with adventure and laughter.

**Bottom line first**: For a 7-day, 6-night family trip, we recommend staying at **The Dusun Villa in Seminyak** (near Jalan Kayu Aya Street). It was our first time bringing the kids to experience a villa, and we were highly satisfied. It's perfect for families who want kids to play in a spacious private space while adults can also relax.

**Travel dates: June 28 - July 4**

## Accommodation

We chose two fantastic villas this time. It was our first time staying in a villa as a family, and the kids found the experience novel and exciting.

We stayed at The Dusun Villa, which boasts an excellent location near Jalan Kayu Aya Street

### **Ubud Accommodation**

We stayed at Tanah Gajah, a Resort by Hadiprana

Here you can book a Kecak Dance performance without jostling through the crowds. The stay also includes a very generous afternoon tea and breakfast. The resort has a relaxed atmosphere, and the staff are warm and friendly, especially towards children. The entire resort is set amidst rice paddies and lotus ponds, with breathtaking scenery that makes you feel like you're in a hidden paradise.

Jimbaran (Photo by Ya-yun Chen)

- After arriving in Bali, we exchanged currency and headed straight to Jimbaran to enjoy a sunset seafood feast at Sunshine88 Restaurant. ## **Day 4:**

![包車一日遊（Ya-yun Chen提供）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f2a109172a9a3e812e741f773c5dcd9c.jpg)

## Tips

- **Pre-trip tipping culture insights:**
- - Daily driver tip: 50,000–100,000 IDR
    - Luggage: 10,000/50,000 IDR (approx. USD$0.65/$1.60)
    - Housekeeping/restaurant: 10,000/20,000 IDR (approx. USD$0.65/$1.30)
    - Massage: Give a 10% tip in advance
    - "KEEP THE CHANGE": No need for exact change; locals often can't break bills under 10,000 IDR.

https://youtu.be/B_oT905RkQw

- **Villa Accommodation Tip:** If you have kids who love playing in the water, I highly recommend staying at a villa. You can ask your private driver to arrange renting a large pool float from local vendors, or book one through Klook—the kids will have a blast.

![小提醒](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a1dc699ccad5ec44b57b56c8328810f8.jpg)

> Klook [Large Pool Float Rental Service](https://affiliate.klook.com/redirect?aid=116349&aff_adid=775806&k_site=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.klook.com%2Factivity%2F4837-pool-float-rental-bali%2F)

- **Transportation Note:** July and August are peak season, and traffic often gets heavy after 11:30 AM. I suggest limiting your daily itinerary to 2–3 attractions to keep things enjoyable and not rushed.
- **Nusa Penida Experience:** The roads are bumpy, but the kids treated it like a bumper car ride and found it especially fun. Since our children are all in elementary school, we skipped the snorkeling trip, but even just the west coast day tour was well worth it.

Bali’s local culture and charm are truly captivating—whether it’s the temples, water activities, or natural scenery, each has its unique allure. This trip deepened our understanding and love for Bali, and we can’t wait to visit again!
