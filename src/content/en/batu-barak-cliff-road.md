Bali, a paradise of mystery and romance, hides many stunning secrets beyond its famous beaches and rice terraces. Located in Uluwatu, **Batu Barak** (also known as **Tanah Barak**) is one of the most popular spots to emerge in recent years. This place, called the 'Blue Road' or 'Cliffside Road,' captivates countless travelers with its majestic limestone cliffs, white sandy beaches, and crystal-clear waters. Today, let's explore this dreamy destination and feel its charm!

***

### **Where is Batu Barak/Tanah Barak?**

Batu Barak Beach is located in the Uluwatu area of southern Bali, specifically in Kutuh Village, adjacent to the well-known Pandawa Beach and [Green Bowl Beach](/en/blog/green-bowl-beach-bali/). This man-made road is hidden among limestone cliffs, offering a unique natural landscape and serving as a hidden gem at the southern tip of Bali.

***

### **Why is it called 'Blue Road' or 'Cliffside Road'?**

This coastal road is called the 'Blue Road' because the limestone cliffs contrast beautifully with the deep blue sky, creating a stunning scene. Especially on a sunny Bali day, the vivid blue of the sky and the earthy tones of the cliffs create a striking contrast, making it feel like a blue path to heaven. Since the road is flanked by steep cliffs, it's also known as 'Cliffside Road.'

***

### **Highlights of Batu Barak/Tanah Barak**

1. **White Sandy Beach and Unique Rock Formations**:
   The beach here is fine and white, feeling like walking on clouds. Scattered natural rocks, like sculptures from nature, add unique visual appeal to the shoreline.
2. **Clear Waters and Majestic Cliffs**:
   The water is so clear you can see the bottom, showing a mesmerizing blue-green hue that contrasts sharply with the towering limestone cliffs, making you feel like you're in a painting.
3. **Peace and Privacy**:
   Since it's not yet overrun by tourists, the beach is exceptionally quiet. You can enjoy your own private time here, away from the crowds.
4. **Close to Pandawa Beach**:
   Batu Barak Beach is next to Pandawa Beach, so you can easily walk between them and experience two different beach vibes in one trip.

![Batu Barak/Tanah Barak 的亮點](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b1c0bf1ebb9e5f22f466bcb222140ba6.jpg)

### **How to Get to Batu Barak/Tanah Barak?**

1. **From Denpasar**:
   Take the Bali Mandara Highway toward Nusa Dua, then follow signs to Pandawa Beach, and finally arrive at Batu Barak Beach.
2. **From Kuta**:
   The drive takes about 45 minutes, with scenic views along the way—ideal for self-driving or hiring a car.
3. **From GWK Cultural Park**:
   Just a 20-minute drive, very convenient.

**Note**: Due to new local traffic regulations, private vehicles must park at the Lelangon parking lot, then walk or take a shuttle to the beach. The shuttle service makes the experience safer and more comfortable. Entry requires a ticket, IDR 25,000 per person (about USD$1).

### **Travel Tips & Notes**

1. **Transportation**:
   It's recommended to hire a car or use a taxi/ride-hailing service to avoid parking difficulties.
   If driving, park at nearby Pandawa Beach and take a shuttle or walk to Batu Barak Beach.
2. **What to Bring**:
   Since there are few vendors on the beach, bring your own food and drinking water.
   Wear comfortable shoes for walking on rocky areas.
   Pack sunscreen, a hat, and plenty of water.
3. **Safety Tips**:
   The stairs down to the beach are steep—watch your step.
   There are no lifeguards, so be extra cautious during water activities.
4. **Best Time to Visit**:
   Weekday mornings are less crowded, perfect for those seeking tranquility.
   Sunset views are especially stunning and a must for photography enthusiasts.
5. **About the Shuttle**:
   Currently, the shuttle at Batu Barak (Tanah Barak) doesn't have a formal fixed schedule—it operates on a continuous loop.
   Shuttles typically start around 7:00 AM
   and end around 6:00 PM
   During busy times, they run back and forth frequently.
