# 1. Trip.com Hidden Search Tricks: Three Features to Uncover Stealth Low Fares

## 1. Enable Price Alerts

Many people open the site and repeat the same search every day.

But you really don't have to.

For example:

Taipei ➜ Bali

Travel dates:

2026/9/1 – 2026/10/30

Once you've entered the details, just click the price alert bell icon.

Once the price drops, the app will immediately send you a push notification.

Here are the price thresholds I usually set:

- Direct flights: under USD$387
- Connecting flights: under USD$258

Sometimes airlines suddenly release tickets, and you can really snag an unexpected deal.

## 2. Make the Most of the Low-Price Calendar

This is one of my most recommended features. Many people habitually choose to depart on Friday and return on Sunday.

But airlines know exactly what travelers are thinking, so those dates are usually the most expensive.

When you actually search, you'll often find:

| Date | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Depart Friday | USD$597 |
| Depart Saturday | USD$574 |
| Depart Tuesday | USD$435 |

Just three days apart, yet the price difference is over five thousand dollars.

If your schedule allows, try to avoid weekends and popular holidays.

# 2. Direct Flights vs. Budget Transfers: How to Choose in 2026?

Many first-time visitors to Bali often ask:

"Is a direct flight worth it?"

The answer really depends on your time and budget.

| Flight Strategy | Representative Airlines | Pros | Cons | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Premium Direct | EVA Air, China Airlines, Starlux | Arrive in about 5–5.5 hours, meals & luggage included | Higher cost | Families with kids, elderly travelers |

# 3. After scoring cheap flights, don't overlook these three hidden costs

## 1. Budget airline baggage fees can cost more than the ticket itself

Many people were thrilled to snag super cheap flights.

But on the return trip, they ended up buying:

- Coffee beans
- Shrimp crackers
- Snacks
- Wood carvings
- Essential oils

Their luggage was instantly overweight.

Buying extra baggage allowance at the airport is usually much more expensive than adding it in advance.

It's recommended to confirm your return baggage needs right after booking your ticket.

## 2. Don't Cut Your Layover Too Short

My personal advice:

Leave at least 2 hours or more.

Especially at:

- Kuala Lumpur (KUL)
- Singapore (SIN)

These airports are huge and crowded during peak season.

If your first flight is delayed, it can easily cause problems down the line.
