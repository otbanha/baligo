This Bali trip was originally planned to celebrate my girlfriend's birthday, and also to take a brief escape from my busy expat work in Vietnam, giving myself a breather. Lately, both of us have been overwhelmed by work stress. When we spotted a promotion from VietJet Air, we decisively booked round-trip tickets from Ho Chi Minh City to Bali, seizing this chance to unwind a little. This time, we chose tickets with 20 kg of checked luggage and also added emergency exit row seats. All in, it came to less than USD$200 per person for the airfare—incredible value for money!

(Friendly reminder: At the time of posting, VietJet Air still had plenty of zero-fare ticket deals, so interested folks should keep an eye out!)

On departure day, we arrived at the airport smoothly and completed check-in. VietJet Air's emergency exit row seats are truly worth recommending—the legroom is so spacious you can fully stretch your legs, and the seat reclines, making the entire flight experience noticeably more comfortable! However, there was a small hiccup along the way: the passenger behind me was a tall Western man whose knees kept pressing against my seatback due to his cramped space, which was a bit uncomfortable. But aside from that little issue, the overall flight was enjoyable.

(VietJet Air pro tip: Choose row 11 seats, and you'll have plenty of room to stretch your legs without bumping into the person in front!)

## **September 4: Welcome flower garlands and a first taste of spa treatments**

As soon as we stepped out of Bali's airport, our private driver Dwik warmly greeted us with welcome flower garlands and thoughtfully handed us cold water, instantly refreshing us! One reason we chose Dwik was that he drives a 2024 Toyota new SUV—spacious and quiet inside, making it especially relaxing for a quick nap on the go. Dwik speaks fluent American English; when we asked, we learned he used to work on cruise ships between the US and Mexico, so it's no wonder his service mindset is so strong.

Afterward, we headed to a nearby money exchange point to convert some local currency—the rate there was much better than at the airport. Then, on Dwik's recommendation, we visited **Calma Spa** for a two-hour spa treatment, and through Dwik, we even got a better price than the menu rate. This spa experience was truly relaxing, leaving us completely soothed.

## **September 5: A Spectacular Nusa Penida Day Trip and Snorkeling Experience**

![9月5日：壯觀的Nusa Penida一日遊與浮潛體驗](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_732ed238988d475c0faa6b704e8e9383.jpg)

Today's highlight was a day trip to **Nusa Penida**. We set off early, and our private driver even arranged for his girlfriend, Indah, to join us as an assistant guide. She accompanied us throughout, helping pick good seats on the boat, taking photos, and looking after our belongings. Once on Nusa Penida, we visited the island's stunning spots, such as **Broken Beach** and **Kelingking Beach**, where the crystal-clear turquoise waters sparkled like sapphires, leaving us in awe.

The snorkeling that followed was an unforgettable experience. We visited three snorkeling spots and marveled at the diverse marine life. Although we didn't spot manta rays at Manta Bay and got a slight sting from a small jellyfish, this unique experience became another cherished memory of the trip. Even more surprising, we encountered a pod of dolphins leaping from the water near our boat—saving us the planned trip to North Bali to see them. It was an unexpected gift.

This Nusa Penida trip, from the island's stunning landscapes to the underwater wonders, truly made us feel the natural beauty of Bali.

> Editor's note: Siobak: A Balinese specialty from Singaraja

![9月6日：火山日出與豬肉料理之旅](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_5c5fd5848222adbc7cbc5b7ebac95bdf.jpg)

Siobak is an authentic dish from Singaraja, made from pork. Its name traces back to Chinese culture (siu yuk or roasted pork), blending local and Chinese flavors seamlessly. Since Hinduism is widely practiced here, beef is considered sacred, making pork a popular alternative.

The meat is seasoned with spices, boiled at low heat to remove any gamey taste, then mixed with cloves, shallots, garlic, and other aromatics, resulting in a unique flavor profile.

You can find this dish in Singaraja or at select restaurants in Denpasar. If you ever visit Bali, be sure to try Siobak's distinctive taste!

## **September 9: A Surprise Birthday Celebration for My Girlfriend**

Early in the morning, the staff at Ubud Element prepared a birthday cake and softly sang the birthday song, surprising us with an incredibly warm moment in the Bali dawn. My girlfriend smiled at the staff and guests, feeling the genuine warmth and sincerity of the moment. This small birthday blessing became an unforgettable memory that added so much meaning to our journey.

After breakfast, we took a short rest and then went to see the **Tegalalang Rice Terraces**. The terraces were beautifully arranged like a painting. Despite the crowds and the rule against bringing water inside, the whole setting was still a perfect spot for taking stunning photos.

Next, we headed to Bali's famous sea temple, **Tanah Lot**. Perched on a rocky islet offshore, this sacred temple can only be approached during low tide. We joined the crowd and drew closer, with a gentle sea breeze brushing past us and waves softly lapping against the rocks, sending up splashes of water. The majestic and holy sight left us breathless. Despite the many visitors, the temple's mystical aura still filled us with awe. We stepped up to the shrine, lightly touched some holy water, and prayed for peace and blessings.

In the evening, we arranged a special birthday dinner for her at **Cuca Restaurant**. Located in Jimbaran, the restaurant was lit by flickering candlelight with a lovely night view. We enjoyed creative tapas-style dishes. The staff thoughtfully prepared a small birthday gift, making the atmosphere both romantic and warm. We specifically ordered a set meal called "Nicholas Tse's Favorite" from the menu, thinking it would be cool—but well, let's just keep it a mystery (laughs). It was one of those experiences you know! This romantic dinner was the perfect end to my girlfriend's birthday.

## **September 10: GWK Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park & Kecak Fire Dance Tour**

![（張家偉提供）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_1033d331528078fc690724949582df98.jpg)

(Provided by Zhang Jiawei)

As our trip neared its end, the surprises kept coming. We headed to the **GWK (Garuda Wisnu Kencana) Cultural Park** in the morning. Famous not only for its giant Garuda statue, the park also offers a rich array of traditional Balinese dance performances. Almost every time slot features a different dance show, showcasing the beauty of Balinese culture. We watched two or three performances in a row, and before each one, we used ChatGPT to translate and read the descriptions, which helped us better understand the mythological stories behind the dances, adding a lot to the enjoyment.

For lunch, we chose **Waroeng De Dusun**, recommended by our driver. This restaurant serves delicious and affordable Indonesian-style Chinese cuisine—no regrets here, with light and satisfying flavors.

In the afternoon, we drove to **Uluwatu Temple**. Watch out for the monkeys along the way! These monkeys are famously clever and sneaky, known for snatching (or swiftly grabbing) sunglasses, hats, and even hand sanitizer from tourists. I saw one monkey unlock a visitor's bag and munch on its contents, only for staff to trade food to get it back. So, it's best to leave valuables in the car to avoid trouble.

![（張家偉提供）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_430945d058d78bbef4733cf6ac83c202.jpg)

(Provided by Zhang Jiawei)

As dusk fell, we found seats to watch the legendary **Kecak Fire Dance**. This performance blends Balinese mythology with traditional dance on a semicircular tiered stage, with the audience surrounded by fire for a visually stunning effect. There are no musical instruments—just dozens of dancers chanting a deep, rhythmic 'kecak-kecak' beat, creating a captivating backdrop that immerses you completely. Near the end, a spectator proposed to their partner amidst the flames, blending this surprise with the classic show into a delightful highlight of the trip.

## **【Important PS】Arriving in Bali: From Smooth Customs to Luggage Woes**

I have to mention that after landing in Bali, everything seemed to go smoothly at first. We had already applied for our visas online and paid the tourist tax, so the customs process was surprisingly quick and efficient. Apart from a slight delay of 3 to 5 minutes at the new health declaration checkpoint, the whole procedure was straightforward and reassuring. However, the first hiccup of our trip quietly unfolded at the baggage carousel.

At that moment, like other travelers, we anxiously waited for our luggage. Nearly an hour later, we finally saw our suitcases slowly emerge from the conveyor belt. When I spotted mine, I quickly grabbed it; everything looked fine on the surface, so I headed straight out of the airport. But upon arriving at the hotel and opening the suitcase, I discovered the zipper had been pried open and the TSA lock was broken. Some small change and a gold trinket (not real gold, thankfully) were missing. Fortunately, my important documents and credit cards were still there, which was a relief, but I definitely learned a lesson: always keep valuables on you!

### **Seeking Help and Kindness: Driver Dwik's Warm Support**

After discovering the tampered luggage, I immediately contacted the Taiwanese representative office in Surabaya, the airport ground handling company, and Vietjet Air's customer service. All three kept passing the buck, leaving us exhausted. The representative office suggested we talk to the ground crew, who then shifted the blame to the airline's customer service, and after going in circles, we got no real help.

That's when our private driver, Dwik, showed incredible warmth and kindness. Upon hearing about the situation, he felt deeply sorry, even blaming himself for not taking better care of our luggage. Dwik proactively noted the time of the incident and prepared to file a police report. Although we ultimately decided not to report it due to the time involved, his sincerity and sense of responsibility were truly heartwarming. He even stayed up all night, not only discussing the matter with fellow drivers but also contacting the travel company to see if similar incidents had occurred, making us feel supported and cared for in an unfamiliar place.

(To this day, I am still deeply grateful for Dwik's tremendous help and kindness.)
