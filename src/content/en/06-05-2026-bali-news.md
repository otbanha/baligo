## 📄 New Travel Routes and Safety Guidelines

![raw image](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_6727b9f6f57b7ae2c320641b63c0569c.jpg)

- **2026 Travel Trends: Slow Travel and the Rise of the East**: This year, travelers are increasingly steering clear of crowded areas, opting for more culturally immersive itineraries. The popularity of eastern destinations like **Amed (black sand beaches)**, **Sidemen (often called the early Ubud)**, and **Penglipuran Village** has surged, making them ideal for those seeking tranquility and natural landscapes.

- **Water Taxi Progress Update**: The highly anticipated water taxi route connecting the airport, Canggu, and Uluwatu is now scheduled to **begin construction in August 2026**, with work continuing through 2027. The project aims to bypass severe road congestion by using sea routes to significantly cut travel time.
- **Health and Safety Double Alert**:
    - **Food Safety**: Officials have once again warned about the risk of food and drink tampering. Never leave your beverage unattended or accept drinks from strangers.
    - **Vaccination Advice**: If you plan to stay in rural areas for more than a month, consult a doctor about whether you need a **Japanese encephalitis** vaccine.

## 🗞️ Enforcement Crackdown and Waste Issues

- **Operation Dharma Dewata Concludes**: Bali's Immigration Office announced that a large-scale patrol operation from April 15 to May 4 resulted in the detention of **62 foreign nationals**. The operation aimed to maintain tourism order, with cases including illegal overstays and activities inconsistent with visa conditions (such as unauthorized online services).
- **Hotels Unite Against Waste**: Facing pressure from the impending closure of the Suwung landfill, several top-tier resorts have launched a joint initiative to actively assist with waste sorting and processing in popular tourist areas, reinforcing Bali's image as a sustainable travel destination.
