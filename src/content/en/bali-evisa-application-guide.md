## What is an Electronic Visa?

**Bottom Line**: Indonesia's electronic visa (eVoA) must be applied for on the **official Directorate General of Immigration website** (the only official URL—many fake scam sites exist online). The fee is approximately **IDR 500,000 (USD$16)**, valid for 30 days and extendable once up to 60 days. General tourists must select the **【B1 - Tourism】** category, apply within 3 days before departure, and pay by card using Visa, Mastercard, or JCB (Amex not accepted).

An electronic visa (eVoA/eVisa) is a visa applied for online via the official Indonesian immigration website before departure. The application fee is the same as the on-arrival visa at the airport: IDR 500,000 (about USD$16), valid for 30 days and extendable once for a maximum stay of 60 days. However, card payments incur an additional processing fee.

## Steps to Apply for an Electronic Visa

1. [Fill out the form on the website](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/) (<-- this is the only official link; many fake scam sites exist online)
2. Prepare a passport valid for at least six months
3. For general tourists traveling to Indonesia or Bali, please select 【B1 - Tourism (Visa On Arrival 30 Days)】—do not choose incorrectly!
4. Fill in travel details and upload your passport (photo page) and a photo (a phone photo is fine, as long as it clearly shows your face; a plain background is best)
5. Have a credit card ready for payment (online payment requires Visa, Mastercard, or JCB. Up to five people can apply and pay with one card. Amex is not accepted! With the surcharge, the final amount is IDR 519,500)

The electronic visa only needs to be applied for within 3 days before departure. It is valid for 90 days from the date of issue. For any questions about the visa application, check out this [FAQ on entering Indonesia/Bali: eVisa/All Indonesia Common Questions](/en/blog/bali-evisa-customs-health-declaration/).

## What Are the Advantages of an Electronic Visa?

Applying for an electronic visa usually takes just a few minutes. The main advantage of getting it in advance is skipping the queue for on-arrival visas after landing. Starting in 2024, you can also use the nearly queue-free electronic gates.
