## 🌊 High Wave Warning for Bali Waters! Waves Up to 4 Meters—Check Ferry Schedules Before Heading to Nusa Penida

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) issued a marine warning on October 24, forecasting **waves of 2.5 to 4 meters in parts of Bali waters from October 24 to 27**.

The warning covers the Badung Strait (Selat Badung), southern Bali waters, the southern Bali Strait, and the southern Lombok Strait. The Badung Strait is a key route connecting Sanur and Nusa Penida, and changing sea conditions may affect speedboat and other maritime traffic.

Meteorological authorities warn that strong winds and high waves could increase navigation risks, urging boat operators to check wind speeds, wave heights, and the latest marine conditions before departure.

### What Should Travelers Do?

If you've booked tickets to Nusa Penida, Lembongan, or other offshore islands, it's best to reconfirm ferry schedules before heading out.

- **Don't just rely on sunny skies:** Bright sunshine doesn't mean calm seas.
- **Check with your boat operator:** Ask about delays, cancellations, or schedule changes before departure.
- **Leave flexibility in your itinerary:** If you need to catch a flight that day, make sure to allow plenty of time between island excursions and your return flight.
- **Follow crew instructions:** If the ferry company cancels trips due to sea conditions, don't push to go out just to stick to your schedule.

This alert doesn't mean all ferries have stopped, but island transportation over the next few days is definitely worth keeping an eye on.

## 🏝️ Kelingking Beach Launches Automated Ticketing! Nusa Penida Tourism Services Get an Upgrade

Kelingking Beach on Nusa Penida unveiled new permanent tourism fee collection facilities on October 23.

Developed in partnership between the Klungkung Regency government and PT Ctrip Internasional Indonesia, the new setup replaces the previous tent-based service area and adds three multilingual automated ticket machines.

Beyond the ticketing and tourist information center, the collaboration also includes pavilion facilities at Kelingking Beach and other attractions, along with water rescue equipment.

Kelingking Beach is one of Nusa Penida's most iconic spots, famous for its cliffs, turquoise waters, and unique coastal terrain, drawing crowds of visitors each year for photos.

### Have Ticket Prices Gone Up?

The facility launch announcement did not introduce any new ticket prices, so the arrival of automated machines shouldn't be taken as a price hike.

Travelers should still confirm the applicable ticket policies at the destination and whether additional fees for Nusa Penida are required.

Additionally, while automated ticket machines help improve purchasing and information services, they do not mean that attractions have fully resolved issues with queues, traffic, or crowd management. Special caution is needed on the trails and cliff areas of Kelingking Beach.

Kelingking Beach tickets are cashless; payments can be made by credit card or QRIS.

## 🏨 Hotels and Restaurants in Bali Risk Warnings for Not Using Local Products; Severe Cases May Face Business License Review

Bali Governor Wayan Koster stated on October 23 that the provincial government will intensify inspections of hotels, restaurants, and related businesses regarding their use of locally sourced products. Violators may receive first, second, and third warnings in sequence, followed by a possible review of their business licenses.

This policy is based on Bali Governor Regulation No. 99 (2018), which promotes and mandates the use of local agricultural, fishery, and industrial products.

Local products cited by the provincial government include rice, eggs, fruits, vegetables, meat, beverages, and salt. The aim is to create more opportunities for local farmers, small and medium enterprises, and food suppliers through the purchasing power of the tourism industry.

Bali's economy is heavily dependent on tourism, so the government hopes that hotels and restaurants will not only attract tourist spending but also ensure that more tourism revenue stays within the local community.

### Will This Affect Travelers' Accommodation and Dining?

In the short term, this policy primarily targets procurement and operational management for businesses, without announcing new regulations for travelers or requiring hotels to fully use local products.

For travelers, it may become easier to find local agricultural products, food, and beverages at some accommodations and restaurants in the future, but the actual situation will depend on each business's procurement arrangements.

This policy also reflects a new direction for Bali's tourism development: beyond increasing visitor numbers, the government is equally focused on ensuring that local residents and small-scale producers can share in the tourism revenue.

## 🇨🇭 Switzerland and Bali Discuss Deeper Tourism Cooperation! Waste and Wastewater Management Also on the Agenda

Bali Governor Wayan Koster met with Swiss Ambassador to Indonesia Konstantin Obolensky on October 23 to discuss cooperation opportunities in tourism, sustainable development, and investment.

The meeting coincided with the 75th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Indonesia and Switzerland. Beyond tourism exchanges, the discussions also covered collaboration in innovative industries, waste management, and wastewater treatment.

The Bali Governor expressed hope that international cooperation could boost local economic development while simultaneously improving tourism and environmental management.

Switzerland, in turn, expressed interest in Bali's sustainable development and related investment opportunities.

### Why This News Matters

Bali has long faced challenges with waste management, wastewater treatment, and tourism development pressures. These issues are not just administrative matters for local government—they are closely tied to beach environments, ocean quality, and the overall travel experience.

Currently, both sides are still in the stage of discussing potential collaboration, and no specific new construction plans, investment amounts, or completion timelines have been announced. Therefore, this meeting should not be interpreted as a sign that related facilities are about to undergo a comprehensive upgrade.

However, if wastewater treatment and waste management cooperation can be realized in the future, it will hold practical significance for maintaining Bali's long-term tourism quality.

## 🌙 Indonesia Rises to Second Place in the 2026 Global Muslim Travel Index, Spotlight on Halal-Friendly Tourism

Indonesia ranked second globally in the 2026 Global Muslim Travel Index (GMTI), climbing three spots from fifth place in 2025, marking its best performance since joining the index.

Indonesia's Minister of Tourism, Widiyanti Putri Wardhana, announced this news on October 22, stating that the improved international recognition helps enhance Indonesia's appeal across different tourism markets.

The global Muslim travel market encompasses travelers who prioritize halal dining, prayer facilities, and services that align with religious needs. For a destination, beyond the attractions themselves, the availability of dining options, accommodation services, and the accessibility of travel information can also influence travelers' decisions.

### What Does This Mean for Bali?

This ranking applies to Indonesia as a whole, not to Bali individually achieving second place. However, as a key international tourism destination in Indonesia, Bali can also benefit from the overall enhancement of the country's tourism brand and service quality.

For travelers from markets with larger Muslim populations, such as Malaysia and Singapore, halal dining and related service information remain important considerations when planning a trip.

It's important to note that Indonesia's overall ranking improvement does not mean every restaurant or hotel in Bali offers halal services. Travelers should still verify the actual arrangements of individual businesses before booking accommodations, ordering meals, or reserving travel activities.
