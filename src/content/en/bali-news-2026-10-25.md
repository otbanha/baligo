## 🌊 Southern Bali Waters Could See Waves Up to 3 Meters! Check Sea Conditions Before Heading to Offshore Islands or Water Activities

Bali's meteorological agency has issued a forecast indicating that on October 25, wave heights in southern Bali waters could reach up to 3 meters, while northern waters are expected to be calmer, with waves ranging from 0.5 to 1.5 meters.

The changing wave and wind conditions in southern waters may affect some ferries, speedboats, and water activities. For travelers planning to visit Nusa Penida, Lembongan Island, or take part in water sports along the southern coast, it's still important to check sea conditions before setting out.

The forecast also shows temperatures in Bali ranging from 20 to 34°C on that day, with some areas possibly experiencing cloudy or hazy conditions.

### What Travelers Should Keep in Mind

- Before heading to Nusa Penida or Lembongan Island, confirm with the boat company whether ferry services are running normally.
- When planning surfing, snorkeling, or other water activities, follow on-site safety instructions.
- If you have an international flight that day, it's best to allow plenty of buffer time for any island excursions.

Current forecasts do not mean all ferries will be canceled, nor that all beaches on the island face the same risks. Sea conditions can vary significantly between different coasts, so travelers should rely on real-time information at their destination.

## 🛂 Deported from Bali for Promoting Villas on a Visa on Arrival! Immigration Reminds: Tourist Visas Are Not Work Permits

Indonesian immigration recently deported a 46-year-old Czech man after determining that he engaged in villa sales and promotional activities while holding a Visa on Arrival (VoA), which violated the terms of his visa.

According to the case details released, immigration officials found during their investigation that the individual allegedly promoted villas through Facebook and posted sale information and contact numbers outside properties. The immigration office deemed these activities beyond the scope permitted by his visa, leading to deportation and a recommendation to place him on the entry blacklist.

This incident serves as a reminder to foreign travelers that Indonesia's tourist visa does not automatically grant the right to work or conduct business activities. Even without receiving a salary, promotional, commercial collaboration, or service activities may not be considered compliant with tourist visa regulations.

### Which Travelers Need to Be Extra Cautious?

If you're simply vacationing in Bali, filming personal travel videos, or sharing travel experiences, you shouldn't interpret this case as meaning general travel filming is illegal.

However, if your activities involve hotel promotion, commercial collaborations, property sales, operating travel services, or other commercial activities, you should first confirm whether your visa permits such activities and whether additional work or residence permits are required.

This is especially important for long-term Bali residents, content creators who frequently engage in commercial collaborations, and real estate professionals, who should ensure their activities comply with Indonesian immigration regulations.

## 🌳 New Attractions Beyond North Bali! Mas Art Village Near Ubud Draws Travelers with Wood Carving Culture

Located in Gianyar Regency, the Mas Art Village is one of Bali's renowned traditional wood carving hubs. The area is home to wood carving studios, art galleries, and traditional craft culture, allowing visitors to explore the unique characteristics of Balinese wood carving art through the works and the artisans' creative processes.

Recent travel coverage has reintroduced this village, drawing more travelers' attention to cultural tourism options centered on traditional crafts around Ubud, beyond the usual temples, rice terraces, and cafes.

Mas is not a newly opened attraction but a village with a long-standing craft tradition. If you've visited Ubud multiple times or want to plan a trip that differs from typical Instagram-worthy spots, consider pairing it with nearby art museums, craft shops, or other cultural sites.

### Things to Confirm Before You Go

- Whether there are studios or galleries open for visits.
- Whether you can watch live carving demonstrations and if reservations are required.
- When purchasing wood carvings, check the size, material, packaging, and international shipping options.

If you're only stopping by briefly, it's best to choose studios with clear opening hours rather than treating the entire village as a single ticketed attraction.

## 🏢 International Energy Conference to Be Held in Sanur! Enhanced Security Around the Venue from October 26 to 28

Bali police are deploying security measures for the 51st ASCOPE (ASEAN Council on Petroleum) Conference. This international energy industry event is scheduled for **October 26 to 28** at The Meru Sanur Bali Beach Convention Center in Sanur.

The conference is expected to draw participants from Southeast Asia's energy industry, with police deploying hundreds of personnel to ensure security at the venue and related events.

This type of conference is a professional industry event, not a tourist attraction open to general travelers. However, for visitors staying in Sanur or planning to head near the venue, it's still worth noting the traffic and security arrangements during the event.

### What does this mean for travelers?

There is currently no information indicating a full road closure in Sanur, so there's no need to cancel your accommodation or travel plans because of the conference.

If you plan to visit Sanur Beach, nearby hotels, or the conference center area from October 26 to 28, it's advisable to allow extra travel time and follow on-site traffic controls and security checks.

If you need to head to the airport, it's also a good idea to leave early to avoid delays due to road conditions or temporary restrictions.

## 📊 Tourism remains Bali's economic backbone! Central bank forecasts 5.4% to 5.9% economic growth this year

In a recent economic outlook, Bank Indonesia's Bali branch noted that the island's economy remains heavily reliant on tourism. Estimates suggest tourism contributes about 66% to Bali's economy, with 2026 economic growth projected between 5.4% and 5.9%.

These figures reflect the importance of tourist spending on accommodation, dining, transportation, retail, and other service industries. The number of international and domestic Indonesian visitors is also seen as a key indicator of local economic performance.

However, such a high reliance on tourism also means Bali is vulnerable to changes in traveler spending habits, international flight routes, accommodation supply, and travel costs.

### What does this mean for travelers?

This doesn't necessarily mean hotel prices or dining costs will rise, nor that every tourism area will benefit equally.

For travelers, the more important takeaway is understanding that Bali is grappling with the long-term challenge of balancing tourism growth with quality of life. Moving forward, it's worth keeping an eye on developments in public transportation, waste management, water supply infrastructure, and whether tourism revenue can more broadly benefit local communities.
