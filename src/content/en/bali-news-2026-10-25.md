## 🌊 Southern Bali Waters Could See Waves Up to 3 Meters! Stay Alert for Island Trips and Water Activities

Bali's meteorological agency's latest forecast indicates that on October 25, wave heights in southern Bali waters could reach 3 meters, while northern waters remain relatively calm with predicted waves of 0.5 to 1.5 meters.

Changes in sea conditions and winds in southern waters may affect some ferries, speedboats, and water activities. For travelers planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or participating in water activities along the southern coast, it's important to check sea conditions before departure.

The weather forecast also shows that temperatures in Bali on that day will range from 20 to 34°C, with some areas possibly experiencing cloudy or hazy conditions.

### What Should Travelers Watch Out For?

- Check with ferry companies before heading to Nusa Penida or Nusa Lembongan to confirm whether boat schedules are normal.
- Follow on-site safety instructions when planning surfing, snorkeling, or other water activities.
- If you have an international flight that day, allow plenty of buffer time for island excursions.

Current forecasts do not mean all ferries will be canceled, nor that all beaches on the island face the same risks. Sea conditions can vary significantly between different coastlines, so always check real-time information for your destination.

## 🛂 Deported from Bali for Promoting Villas on a Visa on Arrival! Immigration Reminds: Tourist Visas Are Not Work Permits

Indonesian immigration authorities recently deported a 46-year-old Czech man after determining that he used a Visa on Arrival (VoA) to engage in villa sales and promotional activities that did not align with the visa's purpose.

According to the case details released, immigration officials found during their investigation that the individual allegedly promoted villas through Facebook and posted sale information and contact numbers on the properties. The immigration office deemed these activities outside the scope permitted by his visa, leading to deportation and a recommendation to place him on the immigration blacklist.

This incident serves as a reminder to foreign travelers that Indonesia's tourist visa does not automatically grant the right to work or engage in commercial activities. Even without receiving a salary, not all promotional, business collaboration, or service activities are considered compliant with tourist visa regulations.

### Which Travelers Need to Be Especially Cautious?

If you're simply vacationing in Bali, shooting personal travel videos, or sharing travel experiences, you shouldn't interpret this case as meaning all general travel filming is illegal.

However, if your activities involve promoting hotels, business collaborations, selling real estate, operating travel services, or any other commercial activities, you should first confirm whether your visa allows it and whether additional work or residence permits are required.

This is especially important for long-term Bali residents, content creators who frequently take on commercial partnerships, and real estate professionals—they should ensure their activities comply with Indonesian immigration regulations.

## 🌳 New Attractions Beyond North Bali! Mas Art Village Near Ubud Draws Visitors with Woodcarving Culture

Located in Gianyar Regency, the Mas Art Village is one of Bali's renowned traditional wood carving hubs. The area is home to wood carving studios, art galleries, and traditional craft culture, allowing visitors to explore the distinctive features of Balinese wood carving art through the works and the artisans' creative processes.

Recent travel features have once again spotlighted this village, drawing more travelers' attention to the cultural tourism options around Ubud that go beyond temples, rice terraces, and cafes, offering a focus on traditional crafts.

Mas is not a newly opened attraction but a village with a long-standing craft tradition. If you've visited Ubud multiple times or are looking to plan a trip that differs from typical Instagram-worthy spots, consider pairing it with nearby art museums, craft shops, or other cultural sites.

### Things to check before you go

- Whether there are studios or galleries open for visits.
- Whether you can watch live carving demonstrations and if reservations are required.
- When purchasing wood carvings, confirm the size, material, packaging, and international shipping options.

If you're only stopping by briefly, it's best to pick studios with clear opening hours rather than treating the entire village as a single ticketed attraction.

## 🏢 International Energy Conference to Be Held in Sanur! Enhanced Security Around Venue from October 26 to 28

Bali police are deploying security measures for the 51st ASEAN Council on Petroleum (ASCOPE) Conference. This international energy industry meeting is scheduled to take place from **October 26 to 28** at The Meru Sanur Bali Beach Convention Center in Sanur.

The conference is expected to draw representatives from Southeast Asia's energy industry, and police will deploy hundreds of personnel to ensure security at the venue and related events.

This type of conference is a professional industry event, not a tourist attraction open to general travelers. However, for those staying in Sanur or planning to visit the area near the venue, it's still worth noting the traffic and security arrangements during the event.

### What does this mean for travelers?

There is currently no information indicating a full road closure in Sanur, so there's no need to cancel your accommodation or travel plans because of the conference.

If you plan to visit Sanur Beach, nearby hotels, or the conference center area between October 26 and 28, it's advisable to allow extra travel time and follow on-site traffic controls and security checks.

If you need to head to the airport, it's also a good idea to leave early to avoid delays due to road conditions or temporary restrictions.

## 📊 Bali's tourism remains an economic pillar! Central bank forecasts 5.4% to 5.9% economic growth this year

According to a recent economic outlook from Bank Indonesia's Bali branch, the island's economy remains heavily reliant on tourism. Estimates suggest that tourism contributes about 66% to Bali's economy, with a projected economic growth rate of 5.4% to 5.9% in 2026.

These figures highlight the importance of tourism spending on accommodation, dining, transportation, retail, and other service industries. The number of international and domestic visitors to Bali is also seen as a key indicator of local economic performance.

However, such a high reliance on tourism also means Bali is vulnerable to shifts in traveler spending habits, international flight routes, accommodation supply, and changes in travel costs.

### What Does This Mean for Travelers?

This doesn't necessarily mean hotel prices or dining costs will rise, nor that all tourism operators will benefit equally.

For travelers, the more important takeaway is understanding how Bali is grappling with the long-term challenge of balancing tourism growth with quality of life. Moving forward, it's worth keeping an eye on developments in public transportation, waste management, water supply infrastructure, and whether tourism revenue can more broadly benefit local communities.
