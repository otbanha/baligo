## 🌊 High Wave Warning for Bali Waters! Waves Up to 4 Meters—Check Ferry Schedules to Nusa Penida

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) issued a marine warning on October 24, forecasting **wave heights of 2.5 to 4 meters in parts of Bali waters from October 24 to 27**.

The warning covers the Badung Strait (Selat Badung), southern Bali waters, the southern Bali Strait, and the southern Lombok Strait. The Badung Strait is a key route between Sanur and Nusa Penida, and changing sea conditions may affect speedboat and other maritime traffic.

Meteorological authorities warn that strong winds and high waves could increase navigation risks, urging ferry operators to check wind speed, wave height, and the latest marine conditions before departure.

### What Should Travelers Do?

If you've booked tickets to Nusa Penida, Lembongan Island, or other offshore islands, it's wise to reconfirm ferry schedules before heading out.

- **Don't just check the weather:** Sunny skies don't guarantee calm seas.
- **Check with the ferry operator:** Ask about delays, cancellations, or schedule changes before departure.
- **Build in schedule flexibility:** If you need to catch a flight that day, allow ample time between your island trip and return flight.
- **Follow crew instructions:** If the ferry company cancels trips due to sea conditions, don’t push to go out just to keep your schedule.

This alert doesn’t mean all ferries have stopped, but island transportation over the next few days does deserve extra attention.

## 🏝️ Kelingking Beach Launches Automated Ticketing! Nusa Penida Tourism Services Get an Upgrade

Kelingking Beach on Nusa Penida launched a new permanent tourism fee collection facility on October 23.

Developed by the Klungkung Regency government in partnership with PT Ctrip Internasional Indonesia, this facility replaces the previous tent-based setup and adds three multilingual automated ticket machines.

In addition to the ticketing and tourist information center, the collaboration also includes pavilions at Kelingking Beach and other attractions, as well as water rescue equipment.

Kelingking Beach is one of Nusa Penida’s most iconic spots, famous for its cliffs, turquoise waters, and unique coastal terrain, drawing crowds of visitors for photos every year.

### Have Ticket Prices Gone Up?

The facility launch announcement did not introduce new ticket prices, so the automated machines should not be interpreted as a price hike.

Travelers should still check the applicable fee regulations at the site and whether additional tourism fees for Nusa Penida are required.

Additionally, while automated ticket machines help improve ticketing and information services, they do not mean the attraction has fully resolved issues with queues, traffic, or crowd management. Special caution is needed on the trails and cliff areas of Kelingking Beach.

Entry tickets to Kelingking Beach are cashless; payments can be made via credit card or QRIS.

## 🏨 Bali Hotels and Restaurants Risk Warnings for Not Using Local Products – Severe Cases May Lead to License Review

Bali Governor Wayan Koster stated on October 23 that the provincial government will intensify inspections of hotels, restaurants, and related businesses regarding their use of locally sourced products. Violators may receive first, second, and third warnings in sequence, after which a review of their business licenses may be considered.

This policy is based on Bali Governor Regulation No. 99 (2018), which covers the promotion and use of local agricultural, fishery, and industrial products.

Local products mentioned by the provincial government include rice, eggs, fruits, vegetables, meat, beverages, and salt. The aim is to create more opportunities for local farmers, small and medium enterprises, and food suppliers through the purchasing needs of the tourism industry.

Bali's economy is heavily dependent on tourism, so the government hopes that hotels and restaurants will not only attract tourist spending but also ensure more tourism revenue stays within the local community.

### Will This Affect Travelers' Accommodation and Dining?

In the short term, this policy primarily targets procurement and operational management for businesses, without announcing new regulations for travelers or requiring hotels to fully use local products.

For travelers, it may become easier to find local agricultural products, food, and beverages at some accommodations and restaurants in the future, but the actual situation will depend on each business's procurement arrangements.

This policy also reflects a new direction for Bali's tourism development: beyond increasing visitor numbers, the government is equally focused on how to share tourism revenue with local residents and small-scale producers.

## 🇨🇭 Switzerland and Bali Discuss Deepening Tourism Cooperation! Waste and Wastewater Treatment Also on the Agenda

Bali Governor Wayan Koster met with Swiss Ambassador to Indonesia Konstantin Obolensky on October 23 to discuss cooperation opportunities in tourism, sustainable development, and investment.

The meeting coincided with the 75th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Indonesia and Switzerland. In addition to tourism exchanges, the discussions covered collaboration in innovative industries, waste treatment, and wastewater management.

The Bali Governor expressed hope that international cooperation could boost local economic development while simultaneously improving tourism and environmental management.

Switzerland, for its part, expressed interest in Bali's sustainable development and related investment opportunities.

### Why This News Matters

Bali has long faced challenges with waste management, wastewater treatment, and tourism development pressures. These issues are not just administrative matters for local government—they are closely tied to beach environments, ocean quality, and the overall travel experience.

The two sides are still in the discussion phase regarding potential collaboration, and no specific new construction plans, investment amounts, or completion timelines have been announced. Therefore, this meeting should not be interpreted as a sign that related facilities are about to undergo comprehensive improvements.

However, if wastewater treatment and waste management cooperation can be realized in the future, it would hold practical significance for Bali in maintaining its long-term tourism quality.

## 🌙 Indonesia Rises to Second Place in the 2026 Global Muslim Travel Index, Spotlight on Halal-Friendly Tourism

Indonesia ranked second globally in the 2026 Global Muslim Travel Index (GMTI), climbing three spots from fifth place in 2025—its best performance since joining the index.

Indonesia's Minister of Tourism, Widiyanti Putri Wardhana, announced the news on October 22, stating that the improved international recognition helps enhance Indonesia's appeal across diverse tourism markets.

The global Muslim travel market encompasses travelers who prioritize halal dining, prayer facilities, and services that align with religious needs. For a destination, beyond the attractions themselves, factors like dining options, accommodation services, and the accessibility of travel information can also influence travelers' decisions.

### What Does This Mean for Bali?

This ranking applies to Indonesia as a whole, not to Bali individually achieving second place. However, as a key international tourism destination in Indonesia, Bali stands to benefit from the nationwide enhancement of tourism branding and service quality.

For travelers from markets with significant Muslim populations, such as Malaysia and Singapore, halal dining and related service information remain important considerations when planning a trip.

It's worth noting that Indonesia's overall ranking improvement does not mean every restaurant or hotel in Bali offers halal services. Travelers should still confirm the actual arrangements with individual providers before booking accommodations, ordering meals, or reserving tour activities.
