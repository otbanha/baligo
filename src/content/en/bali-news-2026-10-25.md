## 🌊 High Wave Warning Issued for Bali Waters! Waves Up to 4 Meters—Check Ferry Schedules Before Heading to Nusa Penida

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) issued a marine warning on October 24, predicting that from October 24 to 27, wave heights in parts of Bali's surrounding waters could reach 2.5 to 4 meters.

The warning covers the Badung Strait (Selat Badung), southern Bali waters, the southern Bali Strait, and the southern Lombok Strait. The Badung Strait is a key route connecting Sanur and Nusa Penida, and changing sea conditions may affect speedboats and other maritime traffic.

Meteorological authorities remind that strong winds and high waves could increase navigation risks. Ferry companies should check wind speed, wave height, and the latest marine information before departure.

### What Should Travelers Do?

If you've already booked tickets to Nusa Penida, Lembongan Island, or other offshore islands, it's recommended to reconfirm whether ferry schedules are running as usual before heading out.

- **Don't just check if it's sunny:** Plenty of sunshine doesn't mean calm seas.
- **Check ferry company announcements:** Ask about delays, cancellations, or schedule changes before departure.
- **Keep your itinerary flexible:** If you need to fly on the same day, allow plenty of time between island trips and your return flight.
- **Follow crew instructions:** If the ferry company cancels trips due to sea conditions, don't force a departure just to stick to your schedule.

This alert doesn't mean all ferries have stopped running, but island transportation over the next few days does deserve extra attention.

## 🏝️ Kelingking Beach Launches Automated Ticketing! Nusa Penida Tourism Services Upgraded

Kelingking Beach on Nusa Penida has unveiled new permanent tourism fee collection facilities as of October 23.

Developed by the Klungkung Regency government in partnership with PT Ctrip Internasional Indonesia, this facility replaces the previous tent-based service area and adds three multilingual automated ticket vending machines.

Beyond ticketing and tourist information services, the collaboration also includes pavilion facilities at Kelingking Beach and other attractions, along with water tourism rescue equipment.

Kelingking Beach is one of Nusa Penida's most iconic spots, famed for its cliffs, turquoise waters, and unique coastal terrain, drawing crowds of visitors for photos every year.

### Have ticket prices gone up?

The current facility launch announcement doesn't introduce new ticket prices, so the activation of automated vending machines shouldn't be interpreted as a price hike.

Travelers should still check the applicable ticket policies at the site and whether additional fees for Nusa Penida sightseeing are required.

Additionally, while automated ticket machines help improve ticketing and information services, they do not mean that attractions have fully resolved issues with queues, traffic, or crowd management. Special caution is still needed on the trails and cliff areas of Kelingking Beach.

Tickets for Kelingking Beach are cashless; payments can be made via credit card or QRIS.

## 🏨 Hotels and Restaurants in Bali Risk Warnings for Not Using Local Products; Severe Cases May Face Business License Review

On October 23, Bali Governor Wayan Koster stated that the provincial government will intensify inspections of hotels, restaurants, and related businesses regarding their use of local Balinese products. Violators may receive first, second, and third warnings in sequence, after which a review of their business licenses may be considered.

This policy is based on Bali Governor Regulation No. 99 (2018), which covers the promotion and use of local agricultural, fishery, and industrial products.

Local products mentioned by the provincial government include rice, eggs, fruits, vegetables, meat, beverages, and salt. The aim is to leverage the purchasing power of the tourism industry to create more opportunities for local farmers, small and medium enterprises, and food suppliers.

Bali's economy is heavily dependent on tourism, so the government hopes that hotels and restaurants will not only attract tourist spending but also ensure that more tourism revenue stays within the local community.

### Will This Affect Travelers' Accommodation and Dining?

In the short term, this policy primarily targets procurement and operational management for businesses, without announcing new regulations for travelers' spending, nor does it mandate that hotels must fully use local products.

For travelers, it may become easier to spot local agricultural products, food, and beverages at some accommodations and restaurants in the future, but the actual situation will depend on each business's procurement arrangements.

This policy also reflects a new direction for Bali's tourism development: beyond increasing visitor numbers, the government is equally focused on how to share tourism revenue with local residents and small-scale producers.

## 🇨🇭 Switzerland and Bali Discuss Deepening Tourism Cooperation! Waste and Wastewater Management Also on the Agenda

Bali Governor Wayan Koster met with Swiss Ambassador to Indonesia Konstantin Obolensky on October 23 to discuss cooperation opportunities in tourism, sustainable development, and investment.

The meeting coincided with the 75th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Indonesia and Switzerland. In addition to tourism exchanges, the two sides also discussed collaboration in innovative industries, waste management, and wastewater treatment.

The Bali Governor expressed hope that international cooperation could boost local economic development while simultaneously improving tourism and environmental management.

Switzerland, for its part, expressed interest in Bali's sustainable development and related investment opportunities.

### Why This News Matters

Bali has long faced challenges with waste management, wastewater treatment, and tourism development pressures. These issues are not just administrative matters for local government—they are also closely tied to beach environments, ocean quality, and the overall travel experience.

The two sides are still in the discussion phase regarding potential cooperation directions, with no specific new construction plans, investment amounts, or completion timelines announced yet. Therefore, this meeting should not be interpreted as an imminent comprehensive upgrade of related facilities.

However, if wastewater treatment and waste management cooperation can be realized in the future, it will have practical significance for maintaining Bali's long-term tourism quality.

## 🌙 Indonesia Rises to Second Place in the 2026 Global Muslim Travel Index, Spotlight on Halal-Friendly Tourism

Indonesia ranked second globally in the 2026 Global Muslim Travel Index (GMTI), climbing three spots from fifth place in 2025, marking its best performance since participating in this index.

Indonesian Minister of Tourism Widiyanti Putri Wardhana announced this news on October 22, stating that the improved international recognition helps enhance Indonesia's tourism appeal across different markets.

The global Muslim travel market encompasses travelers who prioritize halal dining, prayer facilities, and religiously compliant needs. For a destination, beyond the attractions themselves, factors like dining options, accommodation services, and the accessibility of travel information can also influence travelers' decisions.

### What Does This Mean for Bali?

This ranking applies to Indonesia as a whole, not specifically to Bali achieving second place. However, as a key international tourism destination in Indonesia, Bali can also benefit from the overall enhancement of the country's tourism brand and service quality.

For travelers from markets with large Muslim populations, such as Malaysia and Singapore, halal dining and related service information remain important considerations when planning trips.

It's worth noting that Indonesia's overall ranking improvement does not mean every restaurant or hotel in Bali offers halal services. Travelers should still verify the actual arrangements of individual operators before booking rooms, ordering meals, or arranging travel activities.
