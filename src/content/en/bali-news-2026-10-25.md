## 🌊 High Wave Warning for Bali Waters! Waves Up to 4 Meters—Check Ferry Schedules to Nusa Penida

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) issued a marine warning on October 24, forecasting **wave heights of 2.5 to 4 meters in parts of Bali waters from October 24 to 27**.

The warning covers the Badung Strait (Selat Badung), southern Bali waters, the southern Bali Strait, and the southern Lombok Strait. The Badung Strait is a key route between Sanur and Nusa Penida, where changing sea conditions may affect speedboats and other maritime traffic.

Meteorological authorities warn that strong winds and high waves could increase navigation risks, advising ferry operators to check wind speed, wave height, and the latest marine conditions before departure.

### What Should Travelers Do?

If you've booked tickets to Nusa Penida, Lembongan, or other offshore islands, it's best to reconfirm ferry schedules before heading out.

- **Don't just check the weather:** Sunny skies don't guarantee calm seas.
- **Check with the ferry operator:** Ask about delays, cancellations, or schedule changes before departure.
- **Leave flexibility in your itinerary:** If you need to catch a flight on the same day, allow ample time between island excursions and your return flight.
- **Follow crew instructions:** If the ferry company cancels trips due to sea conditions, don't force a departure just to stick to your schedule.

This alert doesn't mean all ferry services have been suspended, but island transportation over the next few days does warrant extra attention.

## 🏝️ Kelingking Beach Launches Automated Ticketing! Nusa Penida's Tourism Services Get an Upgrade

Located on Nusa Penida, Kelingking Beach unveiled a new permanent tourism fee collection facility on October 23.

Developed in collaboration between the Klungkung Regency government and PT Ctrip Internasional Indonesia, this facility replaces the previous tent-based setup with a more permanent service space, adding three multilingual automated ticket machines.

Beyond the ticketing and tourist information center, the partnership also includes pavilion facilities at Kelingking Beach and other attractions, along with water tourism rescue service equipment.

Kelingking Beach is one of Nusa Penida's most iconic spots, famed for its cliffs, turquoise waters, and unique coastal terrain, drawing crowds of visitors each year for photos.

### Have Ticket Prices Gone Up?

The current announcement about the facility launch doesn't include any new ticket prices, so the introduction of automated ticket machines shouldn't be interpreted as a price hike.

Travelers should still confirm the applicable ticket policies at the site and whether additional fees for Nusa Penida sightseeing are required.

Additionally, while automated ticket machines help improve purchasing and information services, they don't mean the attraction has fully resolved queue, traffic, or crowd management issues. Special caution is needed on the trails and cliff areas of Kelingking Beach.

Kelingking Beach tickets are cashless—payments can be made via credit card or QRIS.

## 🏨 Bali Hotels and Restaurants Risk Warnings for Not Using Local Products—Severe Cases May Lead to License Review

Bali Governor Wayan Koster stated on October 23 that the provincial government will intensify inspections of hotels, restaurants, and related businesses regarding their use of local Balinese products. Violators may receive first, second, and third warnings in sequence, followed by possible license reviews.

This policy is based on Bali Governor Regulation No. 99 (2018), which promotes and mandates the use of local agricultural, fishery, and industrial products.

Local products cited by the provincial government include rice, eggs, fruits, vegetables, meat, beverages, and salt. The aim is to channel tourism industry procurement demand to benefit local farmers, small and medium enterprises, and food suppliers.

Bali's economy heavily relies on tourism, so the government hopes hotels and restaurants not only attract tourist spending but also retain more tourism revenue within the local economy.

### Will this affect travelers' accommodations and dining?

In the short term, this policy primarily targets business procurement and operational management, without announcing new regulations for travelers or requiring hotels to fully use local products.

For travelers, it may become easier to find local agricultural products, food, and beverages at some accommodations and restaurants in the future, but the actual situation will still depend on the procurement arrangements of individual businesses.

This policy also reflects a new direction for Bali's tourism development: beyond increasing visitor numbers, the government is equally focused on how to share tourism revenue with local residents and small-scale producers.

## 🇨🇭 Switzerland and Bali Discuss Deepening Tourism Cooperation! Waste and Wastewater Treatment Also on the Agenda

Bali Governor Wayan Koster met with Swiss Ambassador to Indonesia Konstantin Obolensky on October 23 to discuss cooperation opportunities in tourism, sustainable development, and investment.

The meeting coincided with the 75th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Indonesia and Switzerland. In addition to tourism exchanges, the two sides also discussed collaboration in innovative industries, waste treatment, and wastewater management.

The Governor of Bali expressed hope that international cooperation could boost local economic development while simultaneously improving tourism and environmental management.

Switzerland, for its part, expressed interest in Bali's sustainable development and related investment opportunities.

### Why This News Matters

Bali has long faced challenges with waste management, wastewater treatment, and tourism development pressures. These issues are not just administrative matters for local government—they are closely tied to beach environments, ocean quality, and the overall travel experience.

The two sides are still in the discussion phase regarding potential collaboration, and no specific new construction plans, investment amounts, or completion timelines have been announced yet. Therefore, this meeting should not be interpreted as a sign that related facilities are about to undergo a comprehensive upgrade.

However, if wastewater treatment and waste management cooperation can be realized in the future, it would hold practical significance for maintaining Bali's long-term tourism quality.

## 🌙 Indonesia Rises to Second Place in the 2026 Global Muslim Travel Index, Spotlight on Halal-Friendly Tourism

Indonesia ranked second globally in the 2026 Global Muslim Travel Index (GMTI), climbing three spots from fifth place in 2025—its best performance since joining the index. Indonesia's Minister of Tourism, Widiyanti Putri Wardhana, announced the news on October 22, stating that the improved international recognition helps enhance Indonesia's appeal across diverse tourism markets.

The global Muslim travel market encompasses travelers who prioritize halal dining, prayer facilities, and services aligned with religious needs. For a destination, beyond the attractions themselves, factors like dining options, accommodation services, and the accessibility of travel information can also influence travelers' decisions.

### What Does This Mean for Bali?

This ranking applies to Indonesia as a whole, not to Bali individually achieving second place. However, as one of Indonesia's key international tourism destinations, Bali stands to benefit from the overall enhancement of the country's tourism brand and service quality.

For travelers from markets with significant Muslim populations, such as Malaysia and Singapore, halal dining and related service information remain important considerations when planning a trip.

It's worth noting that Indonesia's overall ranking improvement does not mean every restaurant or hotel in Bali offers halal services. Travelers should still verify the actual arrangements of individual establishments before booking accommodations, ordering meals, or reserving tour activities.
