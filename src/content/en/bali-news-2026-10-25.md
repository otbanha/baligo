## 🌊 Southern Bali Waters See Waves Up to 3 Meters! Exercise Caution for Island Trips and Water Activities

Bali's meteorological agency has forecast that on October 25, wave heights in southern Bali waters could reach 3 meters, while northern waters remain relatively calm with predicted waves of 0.5 to 1.5 meters.

Changes in sea conditions and winds in southern waters may affect some ferries, speedboats, and water activities. For travelers planning to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join water sports along the southern coast, it's important to check sea conditions before departure.

The weather forecast also shows temperatures in Bali ranging from 20 to 34°C that day, with some areas possibly experiencing cloudy or hazy conditions.

### What Travelers Should Note:

- Before heading to Nusa Penida or Nusa Lembongan, confirm with the boat company whether services are running normally.
- When planning surfing, snorkeling, or other water activities, follow on-site safety instructions.
- If you have an international flight that day, allow ample buffer time for island excursions.

Current forecasts do not mean all ferries will be canceled, nor that all beaches on the island face the same risks. Sea conditions can vary significantly between different coasts, so it's best to check real-time information for your destination.

## 🛂 Deported from Bali for Promoting Villas on a Visa on Arrival! Immigration Reminds: Tourist Visas Are Not Work Permits

Indonesian immigration recently deported a 46-year-old Czech man after determining that he had engaged in villa sales and promotional activities inconsistent with the purpose of his Visa on Arrival (VoA).

According to the case details released, immigration officials found during their investigation that the individual had allegedly promoted villas through Facebook and posted sale information and contact numbers on the properties. The immigration office deemed these activities outside the scope permitted by his visa, leading to deportation and a recommendation to place him on the immigration blacklist.

This incident serves as a reminder to foreign travelers that Indonesia's tourist visa does not automatically grant the right to work or engage in business activities. Even without receiving a salary, promotional, commercial partnership, or service activities may not necessarily comply with tourist visa regulations.

### Which Travelers Need to Be Especially Cautious?

If you're simply visiting Bali for a vacation, shooting personal travel videos, or sharing travel experiences, you shouldn't interpret this case as meaning general travel filming is illegal.

However, if your activities involve promoting hotels, commercial partnerships, selling real estate, operating travel services, or other commercial activities, you should first confirm whether your visa permits such activities and whether additional work or residence permits are required.

This is especially important for long-term Bali residents, content creators who frequently take on commercial collaborations, and real estate professionals—they should ensure their activities comply with Indonesian immigration regulations.

## 🌳 New Attractions Beyond North Bali! Mas Art Village Near Ubud Draws Visitors with Woodcarving Culture

Located in Gianyar Regency, the Mas Art Village is one of Bali's renowned traditional wood carving communities. The area is home to wood carving studios, art galleries, and traditional craft culture, allowing visitors to learn about the distinctive features of Balinese wood carving art through the works and the artisans' creative processes.

Recent travel coverage has once again highlighted this village, drawing more travelers' attention to the fact that beyond Ubud's temples, rice terraces, and cafes, there are also cultural tourism options centered on traditional crafts.

Mas is not a newly opened attraction but a village with a long-standing craft tradition. If you've visited Ubud multiple times or want to plan an itinerary that differs from typical Instagram-worthy spots, consider pairing it with nearby art museums, craft shops, or other cultural sites.

### Things to Confirm Before You Go

- Whether there are studios or galleries open for visits.
- Whether you can watch live carving demonstrations and if reservations are required.
- When purchasing wood carvings, check the size, material, packaging, and international shipping options.

If you're only stopping by briefly, it's best to choose studios with clear opening hours rather than treating the entire village as a single ticketed attraction.

## 🏢 International Energy Conference to Be Held in Sanur! Enhanced Security Around the Venue from October 26 to 28

Bali police are deploying security measures for the 51st ASCOPE (ASEAN Council on Petroleum) Conference. This international energy industry meeting is scheduled to take place from **October 26 to 28** at The Meru Sanur Bali Beach Convention Center in Sanur.

The conference is expected to draw representatives from Southeast Asia's energy industry, and authorities will deploy hundreds of personnel to ensure security at the venue and related events.

This type of conference is a professional industry event, not a tourist attraction that general travelers can freely attend. However, for those staying in Sanur or planning to visit the surrounding areas, it's still worth noting the traffic and security arrangements during the event.

### What does this mean for travelers?

There is currently no information indicating a full road closure in Sanur, so there's no need to cancel your accommodation or travel plans because of the conference.

If you plan to visit Sanur Beach, nearby hotels, or the conference center area between October 26 and 28, it's advisable to allow extra travel time and follow on-site traffic controls and security checks.

If you need to head to the airport, it's also wise to leave early to avoid delays due to road conditions or temporary restrictions.

## 📊 Bali's tourism remains an economic pillar! Central bank forecasts economic growth of 5.4% to 5.9% this year

According to a recent economic outlook from Bank Indonesia's Bali branch, the island's economy remains heavily dependent on tourism. Estimates suggest that tourism contributes about 66% to Bali's economy, with the 2026 economic growth rate projected between 5.4% and 5.9%.

These figures highlight the importance of tourism spending on accommodation, dining, transportation, retail, and other service industries. The number of international and domestic Indonesian visitors is also seen as a key indicator of local economic performance.

However, such a high reliance on tourism also means Bali is vulnerable to changes in traveler spending habits, international flight routes, accommodation supply, and travel costs.

### What Does This Mean for Travelers?

This doesn't necessarily mean hotel prices or dining costs will rise, nor that every tourism area will benefit equally.

For travelers, the more important takeaway is understanding how Bali is grappling with the long-term challenge of balancing tourism growth with quality of life. Moving forward, it's worth keeping an eye on public transportation, waste management, water supply infrastructure, and whether tourism revenue can more broadly benefit local communities.
