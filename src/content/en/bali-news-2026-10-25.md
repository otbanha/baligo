## 🌊 Southern Bali Waters Could See Waves Up to 3 Meters! Check Sea Conditions Before Heading to Islands or Engaging in Water Activities

Bali's meteorological agency has forecast that on October 25, wave heights in southern Bali waters could reach 3 meters, while northern waters remain relatively calm with predicted waves of 0.5 to 1.5 meters.

Changes in wave and wind conditions in southern waters may affect some ferries, speedboats, and water activities. For travelers planning to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or participate in water sports along the southern coast, it's still important to confirm sea conditions before departure.

The weather forecast also shows temperatures in Bali ranging from 20 to 34°C that day, with some areas possibly experiencing cloudy or hazy conditions.

### What Should Travelers Pay Attention To?

- Check with ferry companies to confirm schedules before heading to Nusa Penida or Nusa Lembongan.
- Follow on-site safety instructions when planning surfing, snorkeling, or other water activities.
- If you have an international flight that day, allow plenty of buffer time for island excursions.

Current forecasts do not mean all ferries will be canceled, nor that all beaches on the island face the same risks. Sea conditions can vary significantly between different coasts, so it's best to rely on real-time information for your destination.

## 🛂 Deported from Bali for Promoting Villas on a Visa on Arrival! Immigration Reminds: Tourist Visas Are Not Work Permits

Indonesian immigration recently deported a 46-year-old Czech man after determining that he used a Visa on Arrival (VoA) to engage in villa sales and promotion activities that did not align with the visa's purpose.

According to the case details, immigration officials found during their investigation that the individual allegedly promoted villas via Facebook and posted sales information and contact numbers on the properties. The immigration office deemed these activities outside the scope of his visa and proceeded with deportation, also recommending he be placed on a blacklist.

This incident serves as a reminder to foreign travelers that Indonesia's tourist visa does not automatically grant the right to work or conduct business activities. Even without receiving a salary, promotional, commercial partnership, or service activities may not be considered compliant with tourist visa regulations.

### Which Travelers Need to Be Especially Cautious?

If you're simply vacationing in Bali, filming personal travel videos, or sharing travel experiences, you shouldn't interpret this case as meaning all travel filming is illegal.

However, if your activities involve promoting hotels, commercial partnerships, selling real estate, operating travel services, or any other commercial nature, you should first confirm whether your visa allows it and whether additional work or residence permits are required.

This is especially important for long-term Bali residents, content creators who frequently take on commercial collaborations, and real estate professionals—they should ensure their activities comply with Indonesian immigration regulations.

## 🌳 New Attractions Beyond North Bali! Mas Art Village Near Ubud Draws Visitors with Woodcarving Culture

Located in Gianyar Regency, the Mas Art Village is one of Bali's renowned traditional wood carving hubs. The area is home to wood carving studios, art galleries, and traditional craft culture, allowing visitors to explore the distinctive features of Balinese wood carving art through the works and the artisans' creative processes.

Recent travel features have once again spotlighted this village, drawing more travelers' attention to the cultural tourism options around Ubud—beyond temples, rice terraces, and cafes—that center on traditional crafts.

Mas is not a newly opened attraction but a village with a long-standing craft tradition. If you've visited Ubud multiple times or are looking to plan an itinerary that differs from typical Instagram-worthy spots, consider pairing it with nearby art museums, craft shops, or other cultural sites.

### Things to check before you go

- Whether there are studios or galleries open for visits.
- Whether you can watch live carving demonstrations and if reservations are required.
- When purchasing wood carvings, confirm the size, material, packaging, and international shipping options.

If you're only stopping by briefly, it's best to pick studios with clear opening hours rather than treating the entire village as a single ticketed attraction.

## 🏢 International Energy Conference to Be Held in Sanur! Enhanced Security Around Venue from October 26 to 28

Bali Police are deploying security measures for the 51st ASEAN Council on Petroleum (ASCOPE) Conference. This international energy industry meeting is scheduled to take place from **October 26 to 28** at The Meru Sanur Bali Beach Convention Center in Sanur.

The conference is expected to draw representatives from Southeast Asia's energy industry, and police will deploy hundreds of personnel to ensure security at the venue and related events.

This type of conference is a professional industry event, not a tourist program that general travelers can freely attend. However, for travelers staying in Sanur or planning to visit the surrounding areas, it's still worth noting the traffic and security arrangements during the event.

### What impact does this have on travelers?

Currently, there is no information indicating a full road closure in Sanur, so there's no need to cancel your accommodation or travel plans because of the conference.

If you plan to visit Sanur Beach, nearby hotels, or the convention center area between October 26 and 28, it's advisable to allow extra travel time and follow on-site traffic controls and security checks.

If you need to head to the airport, it's also wise to leave early to avoid delays due to road conditions or temporary restrictions.

## 📊 Bali's tourism remains an economic pillar! Central bank forecasts economic growth of 5.4% to 5.9% this year

According to a recent economic outlook from Bank Indonesia's Bali branch, the island's economy remains heavily reliant on tourism. Estimates indicate that tourism contributes about 66% to Bali's economy, with projected economic growth for 2026 ranging between 5.4% and 5.9%.

These figures highlight the importance of tourism spending on accommodation, dining, transportation, retail, and other service industries. The arrival of international and domestic Indonesian tourists is also seen as a key indicator of local economic performance.

However, a high reliance on tourism also means Bali is vulnerable to changes in traveler spending habits, international flight routes, accommodation supply, and travel costs.

### What Does This Mean for Travelers?

This doesn't necessarily mean hotel prices or dining costs will rise, nor does it guarantee that all tourism areas will benefit equally.

For travelers, the more important takeaway is understanding that Bali is grappling with the long-term challenge of balancing tourism growth with quality of life. Moving forward, it's worth keeping an eye on developments in public transportation, waste management, water supply infrastructure, and whether tourism revenue can more broadly benefit local communities.
