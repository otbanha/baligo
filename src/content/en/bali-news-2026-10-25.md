## 🌊 High Wave Warning Issued for Bali Waters! Waves Up to 4 Meters—Check Ferry Schedules Before Heading to Nusa Penida

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) issued a marine warning on October 24, forecasting that **from October 24 to 27, wave heights in parts of Bali's surrounding waters could reach 2.5 to 4 meters**.

The warning covers the Badung Strait (Selat Badung), southern Bali waters, the southern Bali Strait, and the southern Lombok Strait. The Badung Strait is a key route for ferries between Sanur and Nusa Penida, and changing sea conditions may affect speedboat and other maritime traffic.

Meteorological authorities warn that strong winds and high waves could increase navigation risks. Ferry operators should check wind speed, wave height, and the latest marine conditions before departure.

### What Should Travelers Do?

If you've already booked tickets to Nusa Penida, Lembongan Island, or other offshore islands, it's recommended to reconfirm ferry schedules before heading out.

- **Don't just check if it's sunny:** Clear skies don't guarantee calm seas.
- **Check ferry company announcements:** Ask about delays, cancellations, or schedule changes before departure.
- **Keep Your Schedule Flexible:** If you need to catch a flight that day, allow plenty of time between your island excursion and your return flight.
- **Follow Crew Instructions:** If the ferry company cancels trips due to sea conditions, don't insist on going out just to stick to your itinerary.

This alert doesn't mean all ferry services have been suspended, but it's worth paying extra attention to island transportation in the coming days.

## 🏝️ Kelingking Beach Launches Automated Ticketing! Nusa Penida Tourism Services Get an Upgrade

Kelingking Beach on Nusa Penida has introduced new permanent tourism fee collection facilities as of October 23.

Developed in partnership between the Klungkung Regency government and PT Ctrip Internasional Indonesia, this facility replaces the previous tent-based service area and adds three multilingual automated ticket machines.

In addition to the ticketing and tourist information center, the collaboration also includes pavilion facilities at Kelingking Beach and other attractions, as well as equipment for water tourism rescue services.

Kelingking Beach is one of Nusa Penida's most iconic spots, famous for its cliffs, turquoise waters, and unique coastal terrain, drawing large crowds of visitors for photos each year.

### Have Ticket Prices Gone Up?

The announcement about the new facilities did not include any new ticket prices, so the launch of automated ticket machines should not be interpreted as a price increase.

Travelers should still check the applicable entrance fee regulations at the site and whether additional tourism fees for Nusa Penida are required.

Additionally, while automated ticket machines help improve ticketing and information services, they don't mean the attraction has fully resolved issues with queues, traffic, or crowd management. Special caution is needed on the trails and cliff areas of Kelingking Beach.

Kelingking Beach now operates a cashless payment system for entrance fees, accepting credit cards or QRIS payments.

## 🏨 Bali Hotels and Restaurants Risk Warnings for Not Using Local Products—Severe Cases May Face License Review

Bali Governor Wayan Koster stated on October 23 that the provincial government will intensify inspections of hotels, restaurants, and related businesses regarding their use of local Balinese products. Violators may receive first, second, and third warnings in sequence, after which a review of their business licenses could follow.

This policy is based on Bali Governor Regulation No. 99 (2018), which covers the promotion and use of local agricultural, fishery, and industrial products.

Local products cited by the provincial government include rice, eggs, fruits, vegetables, meat, beverages, and salt. The aim is to leverage the purchasing power of the tourism industry to create more opportunities for local farmers, small and medium enterprises, and food suppliers.

Bali's economy is heavily dependent on tourism, so the government hopes that hotels and restaurants will not only attract tourist spending but also ensure more tourism revenue stays within the local community.

### Will This Affect Travelers' Accommodation and Dining?

In the short term, this policy primarily targets the procurement and operational management of businesses, without announcing new consumer spending regulations, nor does it mean hotels must fully use local products.

For travelers, it may become easier to spot local agricultural products, food, and beverages at some accommodations and restaurants in the future, but the actual situation will depend on each business's procurement arrangements.

This policy also reflects a new direction for Bali's tourism development: beyond increasing visitor numbers, the government is equally focused on how to share tourism revenue with local residents and small-scale producers.

## 🇨🇭 Switzerland and Bali Discuss Deepening Tourism Cooperation! Waste and Wastewater Management Also on the Agenda

Bali Governor Wayan Koster met with Swiss Ambassador to Indonesia Konstantin Obolensky on October 23 to discuss cooperation opportunities in tourism, sustainable development, and investment.

The meeting coincided with the 75th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Indonesia and Switzerland. In addition to tourism exchanges, the two sides also discussed collaboration in innovative industries, waste management, and wastewater treatment.

The Governor of Bali expressed hope that international cooperation could boost local economic development while simultaneously improving tourism and environmental management.

Switzerland, for its part, expressed interest in Bali's sustainable development and related investment opportunities.

### Why This News Matters

Bali has long faced challenges with waste disposal, wastewater management, and tourism development pressures. These issues are not just administrative matters for the local government—they are also closely tied to beach environments, ocean quality, and the overall travel experience.

The two sides are still in the stage of discussing collaboration directions, with no specific new construction plans, investment amounts, or completion timelines announced yet. Therefore, this meeting should not be interpreted as a sign that related facilities will undergo comprehensive improvements.

However, if wastewater treatment and waste management cooperation can be realized in the future, it will have practical significance for maintaining Bali's long-term tourism quality.

## 🌙 Indonesia Rises to Second Place in the 2026 Global Muslim Travel Index, Spotlight on Halal-Friendly Tourism

Indonesia ranked second globally in the 2026 Global Muslim Travel Index (GMTI), climbing three spots from fifth place in 2025, marking its best performance since joining the index.

Indonesian Minister of Tourism Widiyanti Putri Wardhana announced this on October 22, stating that the improved international recognition helps enhance Indonesia's tourism appeal across different markets.

The global Muslim travel market encompasses travelers who prioritize halal dining, prayer facilities, and religiously compliant needs. For destinations, beyond attractions themselves, factors like dining options, accommodation services, and the accessibility of travel information can also influence travelers' decisions.

### What Does This Mean for Bali?

This ranking applies to Indonesia as a whole, not specifically to Bali achieving second place alone. However, as a key international tourism destination in Indonesia, Bali can also benefit from the nationwide improvement in tourism branding and service quality.

For travelers from markets with large Muslim populations, such as Malaysia and Singapore, halal dining and related service information remain important considerations when planning trips.

It's worth noting that Indonesia's overall ranking improvement does not mean every restaurant or hotel in Bali offers halal services. Travelers should still confirm the actual arrangements of individual operators before booking rooms, ordering meals, or arranging travel activities.
