## 🌊 Southern Bali Waters See Waves Up to 3 Meters! Check Sea Conditions Before Heading to Islands or Water Activities

Bali's meteorological agency has forecasted that waves in southern Bali waters could reach up to 3 meters on October 25, while northern waters remain relatively calm with waves predicted between 0.5 and 1.5 meters.

Changes in wave and wind conditions in southern waters may affect some ferries, speedboats, and water activities. For travelers planning to visit Nusa Penida, Lembongan Island, or join water activities along the southern coast, it's still important to confirm sea conditions before departure.

The forecast also shows temperatures in Bali ranging from 20 to 34°C on that day, with some areas possibly experiencing cloudy or hazy conditions.

### What Travelers Should Note:

- Check with ferry companies before heading to Nusa Penida or Lembongan Island to confirm whether services are running normally.
- Follow on-site safety instructions when planning surfing, snorkeling, or other water activities.
- If you have an international flight that day, allow plenty of buffer time for island excursions.

Current forecasts do not mean all ferries will be canceled, nor do they indicate the same risk across all beaches on the island. Sea conditions can vary significantly between different coasts, so real-time information at your destination should be your guide.

## 🛂 Deported from Bali for Promoting Villas on a Visa on Arrival! Immigration Reminds: Tourist Visas Are Not Work Permits

Indonesian immigration recently deported a 46-year-old Czech man, ruling that he engaged in villa sales and promotion activities on a Visa on Arrival (VoA) that were inconsistent with the visa's purpose.

According to the case details released, immigration officials found during their investigation that the individual was suspected of promoting villas via Facebook and posting sale information and contact numbers outside properties. The immigration office deemed these activities beyond the scope of his visa and took deportation action, also recommending he be placed on a blacklist for entry.

This incident serves as a reminder to foreign travelers that Indonesia's tourist visa does not automatically grant the right to work or conduct business activities. Even without receiving a salary, not all promotional, commercial collaboration, or service activities are considered compliant with tourist visa regulations.

### Which travelers need to pay special attention?

If you're simply vacationing in Bali, filming personal travel videos, or sharing travel experiences, you shouldn't interpret this case as meaning general travel filming is illegal.

However, if your activities involve hotel promotion, commercial collaborations, property sales, operating travel services, or other commercial activities, you should first confirm whether your visa permits them and whether additional work or residence permits are required.

This is especially important for long-term Bali residents, content creators who frequently take on commercial collaborations, and real estate professionals, who should ensure their activities comply with Indonesian immigration regulations.

## 🌳 New Attractions Beyond North Bali! Mas Art Village Near Ubud Draws Travelers with Wood Carving Culture

Located in Gianyar Regency, the Mas Art Village is one of Bali's renowned traditional wood carving hubs. The area is home to wood carving studios, art galleries, and traditional craft culture, allowing visitors to explore the unique characteristics of Balinese wood carving art through the works and the artisans' creative processes.

Recent travel reports have reintroduced this village, drawing more travelers' attention to cultural tourism options centered on traditional crafts around Ubud, beyond the usual temples, rice terraces, and cafes.

Mas is not a newly opened attraction but a village with a long-standing craft tradition. If you've visited Ubud multiple times or want to plan a trip that differs from typical Instagram-worthy spots, consider pairing it with nearby art museums, craft shops, or other cultural sites.

### Things to Confirm Before You Go

- Whether there are studios or galleries open for visits.
- Whether you can watch live carving demonstrations and if reservations are needed.
- When purchasing wood carvings, confirm the size, material, packaging, and international shipping options.

If you're only stopping by briefly, it's best to choose studios with clear opening hours rather than treating the entire village as a single ticketed attraction.

## 🏢 International Energy Conference to Be Held in Sanur! Enhanced Security Around Venue from October 26 to 28

Bali police are deploying security measures for the 51st ASEAN Council on Petroleum (ASCOPE) Conference. This international energy industry event is scheduled for **October 26 to 28** at The Meru Sanur Bali Beach Convention Center in Sanur.

The conference is expected to draw participants from Southeast Asia's energy industry, with police deploying hundreds of personnel to ensure security at the venue and related events.

This type of conference is a professional industry event, not a tourist attraction open to the general public. However, for travelers staying in Sanur or planning to visit the area around the venue, it's worth noting the traffic and security arrangements during the event.

### What does this mean for travelers?

There is currently no information suggesting a full road closure in Sanur, so there's no need to cancel accommodations or travel plans because of the conference.

If you plan to visit Sanur Beach, nearby hotels, or the conference center area from October 26 to 28, allow extra travel time and follow on-site traffic controls and security checks.

If you need to head to the airport, it's also wise to leave early to avoid delays due to road conditions or temporary restrictions.

## 📊 Bali's tourism remains an economic pillar! Central bank forecasts 5.4% to 5.9% economic growth this year

In a recent economic outlook, Bank Indonesia's Bali branch noted that Bali's economy remains heavily reliant on tourism. Estimates suggest tourism contributes about 66% to Bali's economy, with the 2026 economic growth rate projected between 5.4% and 5.9%.

These figures reflect the importance of tourism spending on accommodation, dining, transportation, retail, and other service industries. The arrival of international and domestic Indonesian tourists is also seen as a key indicator of local economic performance.

However, the high reliance on tourism also means Bali is vulnerable to changes in tourist spending habits, international flight routes, accommodation supply, and travel costs.

### What Does This Mean for Travelers?

This doesn't necessarily mean hotel prices or dining costs will rise, nor that every business in tourist areas will benefit equally.

For travelers, the more important takeaway is understanding that Bali is grappling with the long-term challenge of balancing tourism growth with quality of life. Moving forward, it's worth keeping an eye on developments in public transportation, waste management, water supply infrastructure, and whether tourism revenue can more broadly benefit local communities.
