If your impression of Bali ATV rides is still stuck in mud, rice paddies, and jungles, this route will completely refresh your imagination. Located in Ubud/Sukawati, **Jambe Asri ATV**’s biggest selling point is—**the only ATV experience in Bali that crosses a railway bridge**. Not just viewing the bridge from afar, but actually riding a four-wheeled buggy, rumbling across the iconic iron bridge, feeling your adrenaline spike in the high, bumpy, and narrow bridge deck.

Now you can also choose a more complete one-day combo: **morning Ayung River rafting, afternoon ATV off-roading, including lunch and hotel transfer**. Spend the entire day immersed in Ubud’s jungle, river valleys, rice paddies, and mud, from morning till night, maxing out your Bali itinerary.

## ATV Route Challenges Are Richer Than You Think

This isn't just a ride—it's a jungle adventure:

- **River Start**: Begin by exploring the river; the atmosphere in Sukawati Forest is absolutely top-notch.
- **Iron Bridge Challenge**: The only ATV experience in all of Bali that crosses an iron bridge, delivering a direct adrenaline rush at high altitude.
- **Rice Field Romance**: Pause amid lush green rice paddies, adding a touch of romance to the scorching trail.
- **Narrow Passages**: Rows of ATVs like 'battle machines'—teamwork and focus are key to navigating through.
- **Wet Zone Acceleration**: Warm up before entering the wet zone, then floor it here.
- **Muddy Mayhem**: If you're not dirty, you're not having fun! That's the reason to head to Sukawati.
- **Hidden Waterfall**: Take a break from the ATV and enjoy a moment of cool refreshment under the spray of a waterfall.
- **Stone Pathways and Canyons**: From rivers to narrow gorges, the trail's landscape shifts constantly, full of surprises at every turn.
