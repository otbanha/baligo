When traveling to Bali, many people pick up woven bags, unique clothing, or handmade crafts as souvenirs. But have you ever thought that a pair of seemingly ordinary flip-flops could actually be made from discarded tires?

Indosole, based in Bali, turned this idea into a real business.

The brand uses recycled tires for the soles, pairing them with different materials and minimalist designs to create sandals and flip-flops perfect for everyday wear. It doesn't rely on vibrant Balinese patterns to catch your eye but starts from a very practical question: Can discarded tires still find a new purpose?

If you love eco-friendly design, an outdoor aesthetic, or want to discover a different side of Bali's local brands, Indosole is a name worth adding to your shopping list.

## Brand Story｜A Broken Flip-Flop Sparks a New Idea

Indosole's story began in 2004.

Founder Kyle Parsons was traveling in Bali when his flip-flop suddenly broke while walking along the streets of Seminyak. At a local shop, he spotted sandals with tire soles and unexpectedly discovered that what was once considered waste could become durable everyday items.

This discovery got him thinking: Since tires are naturally wear-resistant, why not repurpose them to create truly comfortable shoes?

In 2009, Indosole officially launched its brand products. The early production process was straightforward: recycled tires were cut into soles, combined with other upper materials, and refined in collaboration with local Indonesian artisans to gradually improve product quality and manufacturing methods.

However, the brand also realized that an eco-friendly concept alone wasn't enough. If a pair of shoes isn't comfortable or attractive, consumers won't keep buying them. So Indosole later invested more time in researching materials and production techniques, aiming to strike a balance between recycling, comfort, and design.

This is what makes Indosole so intriguing: it doesn't ask you to settle for a pair of shoes just for the sake of the environment. Instead, it hopes you'll fall in love with the shoes first, and then discover the eco-friendly story behind them.

## Eco-Friendly Design｜Can Waste Tires Really Be Made into Soles?

Tires are highly durable, but disposing of waste tires has long been an environmental issue. Without proper recycling and treatment, they can occupy space for years and cause other environmental problems.

Indosole chose to reprocess waste tires into soles for flip-flops and sandals, giving new life to materials that might otherwise be discarded.

The brand later developed a recycled tire sole technology called SETT, and in 2018 launched the ESSNTLS collection featuring this innovation.

According to the brand's formula, these soles consist of 40% recycled tires, 30% natural rubber, and 30% new materials. This means the product isn't made entirely from waste tires, but combines recycled materials with others to create a wearable sole.

The brand also continues to experiment with other materials that might otherwise be discarded, giving resources more opportunities for reuse.

## Is Indosole a Bali Brand?

Indosole's brand story began in Bali, with the island's lifestyle, Indonesian craftsmanship, and recycled design as key features.

In its early days, the brand worked with Indonesian artisans, starting with hand-cutting tires for soles and gradually improving products and production methods. Later, Indosole expanded into different product lines, evolving from a simple recycled tire sandal into a more complete casual footwear brand.

The brand has also earned B Corporation certification, which focuses on a company's social and environmental performance, not just its products.

So, Indosole's appeal isn't just about 'making slippers from waste tires'—it's about combining recycled materials, product design, and corporate responsibility.

If your impression of Bali is still tied to woven bags, wood carvings, and handicrafts, Indosole offers a different perspective on local brands: eco-consciousness doesn't have to feel like a slogan—it can be a pair of sandals you wear every day.

## What You Bring Home Isn't Always a Traditional Souvenir

Bali is home to many local brands worth discovering, and Indosole's story began with a broken pair of flip-flops, eventually evolving into a brand that repurposes recycled tires into footwear designs.

Its products may not suit everyone, but if you appreciate minimalist casual style and enjoy buying items with a story behind them, Indosole is definitely worth a look.

Next time you travel to Bali, beyond rattan bags, sarongs, and handicrafts, consider checking out design brands that weave eco-consciousness into everyday life.
