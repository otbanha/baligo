Nusa Lembongan and Nusa Ceningan are two hidden gem islands near Bali, offering captivating ocean views and a laid-back atmosphere. Beyond being popular diving destinations, their restaurants and bars provide a rich array of dining choices. Here are several recommended dining and bar spots, plus beach clubs ideal for total relaxation—especially suited for those seeking to release stress and enjoy great food and scenery.

## Must-Visit Restaurants and Bars on Nusa Lembongan

### 1. Thai Pantry (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![1.Thai Pantry（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_3396bcd7fb375be2e42c3163938c0f3b.jpg)

If you love Thai cuisine and delicious cocktails, this place is an absolute must-visit. The Thai dishes here are incredibly authentic, and the cocktails are outstanding! The atmosphere is also very relaxed, making it a perfect choice for a gathering with friends or a romantic dinner.

### 2. Kayu Lembongan (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![2.Kayu Lembongan（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a0e3265965d5557c3a12710fbb60a9b5.png)

Kayu is a restaurant known for its delicious food and famous Frozen Margaritas. The menu offers a wide variety of choices, from local specialties to international dishes, making it especially great for unwinding and enjoying dinner after a full day of exploration.

### 3. Cloudland (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![3.Cloudland（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b723e761ad9f105a43748468984140cd.jpg)

This is an ideal spot to watch the sunset, offering affordable cocktails. Sitting here at dusk, taking in the sunset and feeling the sea breeze, is truly a feast for both the eyes and the taste buds.

### 4. Tigerlillys Beach Shack (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![4.Tigerlillys Beach Shack（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_02abe71b39d40cf449e9cfe8bef6df43.jpg)

Tigerlillys Beach Shack is a family-friendly restaurant right on the beach, serving delicious breakfasts during the day and boasting a lively sunset atmosphere in the evening. It's a fantastic place to watch the sunset, especially recommended with an Aperol Spritz or an ice-cold beer, soaking in the colorful skyline and sea breeze. On weekends, they have Happy Hour, live music, and movie nights, ensuring a relaxed and enjoyable evening.

### 5. The Deck (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![5.The Deck（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e8986e02267a3ab18d94277f8ced41a0.jpg)

Located right next to Thai Pantry, The Deck is another great spot for dining and enjoying cocktails. It offers a variety of creative dishes and drinks, perfect for both gatherings and dates.

### 6. Curry Traders (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![6.Curry Traders（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_baadfa00828e8fadc5638879b70a7c99.jpg)

If you're looking to savor a spice feast, Curry Traders is an absolute must-visit. This restaurant blends Indian, Sri Lankan, and Southeast Asian flavors, with the aroma hitting you right from the open kitchen at the entrance. Signature dishes like butter chicken, Sri Lankan-style vegetarian lentil curry, and Bengali-style shrimp curry with pineapple are unmissable, and when paired with expertly crafted cocktails, they elevate the dining experience to pure bliss.

### 7. Hai Bar & Grill (🗺️ [Official Website & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![7.Hai Bar & Grill（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_59e2488ca558f8067df0b4f824052ee3.jpg)

🍽️ 2024 Travelers' Choice Winner, the best beachfront restaurant on Nusa Lembongan

Located on the famous Mushroom Bay Beach in Nusa Lembongan, Hai Bar & Grill offers the ultimate seaside dining experience. The restaurant's open design lets in the warm tropical breeze while you take in the stunning water views of the bay.

### 8. Cat Island Lembongan (🗺️ [Official Website & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

https://www.instagram.com/reel/C94T2VvSev7/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==

If you're a cat lover, this unique feline paradise—Cat Island Lembongan—will steal your heart. Home to 45 healthy and adorable cats, you can pet and play with them to your heart's content. This peaceful cat haven sits by the coast near Yellow Bridge, offering stunning ocean views. Admission is 120,000 Indonesian Rupiah (about USD$8), which includes one drink, and the ticket is valid for a month, so you can come back anytime to visit these fluffy friends. The staff are incredibly friendly, and their genuine love for the cats is unmistakable. A visit takes about 1-2 hours, making it a heartwarming and fun spot on Nusa Lembongan.

### 9. The Pontoon at Lago (🗺️ [Official Website & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DQNlEq3iLc0/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==

Lago is a unique floating bar perched over the ocean on Nusa Lembongan, powered entirely by solar energy. The vibe here is relaxed and cheerful, with surfers often showing off their skills at the nearby Playgrounds break. You can lounge on bean bags by the water or hop onto floating platforms, sipping ice-cold beer, classic margaritas, and espresso martinis while enjoying hand-cut fries and spicy chicken burgers—making it the perfect spot for a lazy afternoon.

### 10.The Coconut Hut (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![10.The Coconut Hut（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7f6d154219950b848b4ab6efe45116bf.jpg)

The Coconut Hut blends a restaurant, bar, and mini-golf into one unique entertainment experience. Here, you can sip a Piña Colada while taking on a mini-golf challenge, with hot dogs, pasta, and other bites accompanied by upbeat music. At night, the golf course lit up with twinkling lights, paired with a few cocktails, makes it a perfect spot for both fun and relaxation.

### 11.Nusa Bodega (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![11.Nusa Bodega（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f90877c21bf4e189ba83b709de8963d5.jpg)

If you're a fan of Spanish-style tapas and wine, Nusa Bodega is a must-visit. This cozy bar focuses on serving a variety of Spanish tapas, paired with a carefully selected range of quality wines, letting you soak up the rich flavors of Spain away from the island's beachy vibe. Whether you're spending an evening with friends or enjoying a romantic dinner, Nusa Bodega is an excellent choice.

### 12.Ginger & Jamu (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![12.Ginger & Jamu（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e197c5eb1b1194d670f82451daa1517a.png)

Ginger & Jamu is a restaurant in the heart of Jungutbatu Beach on Nusa Lembongan, focused on health-conscious, locally sourced whole foods. Every dish is packed with fiber and nutrients, offering options from morning smoothie bowls and hearty veggie breakfasts to burritos, sandwiches, and colorful salad bowls. My personal favorite is the hearty falafel bowl—its texture and flavor are incredibly satisfying.

The restaurant features a spacious outdoor seating area overlooking the ocean, with tables and bean bag loungers, perfect for a leisurely meal with friends. Located right next to Ohana's Beach Club, Ginger & Jamu is the ideal spot for lunch before heading over for cocktails under the sun.

### 13.La Bianca (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![13.La Bianca（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-03/1774877991302-la_bianca.jpg)

La Bianca is one of the most enchanting restaurants on Nusa Lembongan. By day, you can take in 180-degree ocean views, while at night, this romantic spot is bathed in the warm glow of hanging lights and flickering candles, creating a cozy and inviting atmosphere.

This European-style restaurant features outdoor seating, a wood-fired pizza oven, and a large grill, serving freshly caught fish, seafood skewers, and delicious meat dishes. The tapas selection is plentiful, and the tempting desserts round off your dining experience perfectly. While La Bianca is one of the most expensive restaurants on the island, it still offers great value for money—whether you're celebrating a special occasion or enjoying a high-quality dinner, it's not to be missed.

### 14. Soka Warung (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![14.Soka Warung（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_faac031e3a73966604c5bbe2056d243c.png)

Soka Warung is a highly-rated, affordable little eatery near Mushroom Beach on Nusa Lembongan. Their Indonesian fried rice, banana pancakes, and corn fritters are hearty and delicious, with many visitors calling it the best Indonesian food they've ever had! The chicken fried noodles and chicken noodle soup have a particularly homey feel, and the Balinese chicken curry is also highly recommended. The restaurant's location is excellent—you can enjoy dinner while watching the sunset, creating a warm and relaxing atmosphere for an unforgettable meal.

### 15. Warung Tumpang Sari Lembongan (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![15.Warung Tumpang Sari Lembongan（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_ec50abc0a61fade2fbdbb8ac34f5ba97.png)

Warung Tumpang Sari is a cozy family-run restaurant known for its delicious home-style cooking and affordable prices. The quality of the food is amazing! We recommend trying their tuna katsu curry, tender and juicy tuna pepes, and gado-gado with rich peanut sauce—the flavors are outstanding. Banana pancakes make the perfect sweet ending. Be patient, as everything is made to order, ensuring freshness.

### 16. Tropical Juice Corner & Foods (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![16.Tropical Juice Corner & Foods（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a33335de6b9d6901039892784695731e.png)

A hidden food gem known for its fresh, vibrant tropical smoothie bowls, perfect for brunch, lunch, or dinner. We stumbled upon it and it quickly became our favorite dining spot on the island. Besides the delicious smoothie bowls, the Balinese-style fish dishes and generous seafood platters are impressive—every dish bursts with fresh flavors at wallet-friendly prices. While breakfast options are limited, lunch and dinner menus are rich, and the coffee is wonderfully aromatic. The owner's warm hospitality adds to the charm, making it a must-visit for travelers craving authentic Balinese cuisine.

### 17. Cookies Coffee Shop and Warung (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![17.Cookies Coffee Shop and Warung（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_573c79acf22a1fc909679946ce5dd274.png)

Cookies Coffee Shop and Warung offers spacious seating and is a lovely, friendly little spot on Nusa Lembongan. Many visitors dine here daily and rave about the menu. Breakfast is especially outstanding, with smoothie bowls and coffee being must-have daily treats at very affordable prices. The restaurant also serves delicious local dishes like chicken satay, squid ink rice, chicken avocado salad, and garlic bread. Notably, the WiFi is fast, making it perfect for remote work—a great blend of work and dining. Whether you're looking to experience local cuisine or find a comfortable workspace, this is an ideal choice.

### 18. D Tari Warung (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![18.D Tari Warung（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_1d03d25fd3501c11676b2d609e976121.png)

This is a popular local eatery run by a warm and welcoming couple, renowned for its delicious Indonesian cuisine and heartfelt service. The signature dish is a whole freshly grilled red snapper, offered at an incredibly competitive price (IDR 60,000, about USD$4), hailed as the best grilled fish in Bali. Other highlights include egg noodles, juicy hand-pressed beef burgers, chicken satay, grilled prawns, spring rolls, fried chicken, and fried bananas that customers have called "the best I've ever had." They also serve the largest and freshest coconuts on the island. Known for affordable prices, generous portions, fresh ingredients, authentic flavors, and friendly service, many visitors say it's the best meal they've had in Bali. Some guests even visited daily during a six-day trip, sometimes twice a day. The food is so good that regulars often order the same dishes, having found their favorites and sticking with them. D Tari Warung is more than just a restaurant—it's a perfect place to experience local food culture and warm hospitality, making it a must-visit spot on Nusa Lembongan.

### 19. Warung Makan Kevin (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![19.Warung Makan Kevin（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f9d9ca291af745bdaef4685abc6b5044.png)

Warung Makan Kevin is a popular local eatery on Nusa Lembongan, run by a friendly and welcoming family. This restaurant is famous for its authentic Indonesian cuisine, especially the signature dishes: banana leaf-wrapped grilled fish (Ikan Pepes) and chicken noodle soup (Soto Ayam), each showcasing the chef's exceptional cooking skills. Their fried rice is generously portioned, loaded with fresh seafood, and has become many travelers' favorite Indonesian fried rice. The grilled seafood selection is equally impressive, particularly the grilled squid, which has earned high praise from numerous diners.

Although dishes take time to prepare—especially during peak hours when you might wait over an hour—it's absolutely worth it. The dining atmosphere is warm and cozy, the staff are friendly and courteous, and the most delightful surprise is that such high-quality food comes at very reasonable prices. Note that this restaurant only accepts cash, no credit cards. Many visitors to Nusa Lembongan return again and again, making it one of the island's most beloved dining spots.

### 20. Karana Pool Bar & Dining

https://youtube.com/shorts/_-PGilx6H_s

**Karana Pool Bar & Dining** boasts a commanding hilltop position with unbeatable ocean views. The restaurant faces the vast, rolling sea and features an infinity pool overlooking the water, making it a top spot for capturing the stunning moment when the sun dips below the horizon.

The menu offers a diverse range of international cuisine and refreshing cocktails, all in a relaxed, resort-like atmosphere. Perched high above, the views are completely unobstructed—whether you're lounging on a poolside bean bag or seated in the dining area, you can enjoy your meal while taking in the breathtaking seascape. It's widely recognized as one of the best places on the island to watch the sunset and feel the rhythm of the waves.

## Recommended Restaurants & Bars on Ceningan Island

### 1. Seabreeze Ceningan (🗺️ [Official Site & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

This restaurant is famous for its stunning views, but service can be slow, so be sure to set aside plenty of time to soak in the scenery and enjoy your meal. Sitting here gazing out at the crystal-clear turquoise waters is truly a feast for the eyes.

Nestled beside the stunning Blue Lagoon, KLYF Club offers breathtaking views along with delicious food and cocktails. The atmosphere here is incredibly resort-like, making it a perfect spot to enjoy a moment of tranquility and great cuisine.

### 3. The Dungki Bendega Resto (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

The Dungki Bendega Resto is a restaurant crafted by experienced chef I Kadek Astawa, who brings 15 years of culinary expertise from five-star hotels. Using fresh, local, and healthy ingredients, he serves up incredibly flavorful dishes. Recommended dishes: crispy chicken burger, pork ribs, and beef rendang.

### 4. MyBeach Ceningan (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

MyBeach Ceningan is a great spot for the whole family to unwind, especially for a leisurely lunch or a drink in the sun. It features a pool overlooking the sea, and kids can play on a swing set right in the ocean. The food is reasonably priced, with the seafood curry being a memorable recommendation. Their chicken satay and signature fish and chips are also popular with visitors, perfect for sharing with the family.

The restaurant has a relaxed and cheerful vibe, with efficient and friendly service, making it a great choice any time of day or night. Run by a warm family, MyBeach Ceningan offers guests delicious meals and captivating sea views—highly recommended for a visit!

### 5. Mangrove Restaurant (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![5.Mangrove Restaurant（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_77cc621dd9cad7736bee41ac682162db.jpg)

Mangrove Restaurant is a quiet and cozy little eatery known for its diverse and delicious cuisine. The head chef, Bu Illuh, is not only talented but also especially attentive to guests with dietary restrictions, carefully explaining menu options so everyone can dine with peace of mind. The menu is extensive, ranging from authentic Indonesian dishes to burgers, curries, and even Ukrainian dumplings and pasta, catering to all tastes.

The portions at Mangrove Restaurant are generous, and the prices are reasonable. In addition to great views and food, the restaurant also offers hookah and board games, making it a relaxing spot for friends, couples, and families. Even if you're not staying at the dive resort, it's well worth a visit when you're on Nusa Ceningan!

## Beach Club Recommendations

### 1. Ohana's Beach Club (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

🏝️ **The Perfect Blend of Luxury and Laid-Back Island Living**

![1.Ohana's Beach Club（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0818393dd76712be34aed51bb82f68d6.jpg)

Ohana's Beach Club brings a trendy vibe to Nusa Lembongan. It masterfully combines high-end luxury with the island's relaxed atmosphere, making it the ultimate spot to unwind. Savor fresh seafood feasts, healthy salads, and juicy steaks—all served right beside bright white sun loungers, with cool pools and beach umbrellas nearby. Arrive early to snag the best sunny spots, and at night, head to the upper deck for a romantic dinner under the stars.

### 2. Sandy Bay Beach Club (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

🌅 **A Must-Visit Beach Club on Nusa Lembongan**

![2.Sandy Bay Beach Club（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_378a10dfdb8a9a70542a68c5402ec708.jpg)

Sandy Bay Beach Club is simply irresistible—many travelers come to Nusa Lembongan just for it. Nestled in a stunning bay, it features an open-air lounge area with soft sand underfoot and the sound of waves all around. It's the perfect place to enjoy delicious seafood and tropical cocktails. Start with the 'snack platter,' then move on to fresh tiger prawns or traditional Indonesian beef rendang. Whether you go for a Bloody Mary or a Margarita, both are highly recommended! Don't miss the breathtaking sunset views.

### 3. Suku Beach Club (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

🍹 **A Slice of Paradise on Nusa Ceningan**

![3.Suku Beach Club（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2acb0c969ad35b6675da2c2663d93b7f.jpg)

Suku Beach Club sits on Nusa Ceningan, overlooking pristine white sands and a famous surf break. This tiered pool lounge features a restaurant and bar, letting you while away the day by the water with a cool coconut in hand or light bites on a comfy sunbed. Don't miss their signature cocktails—especially the spicy Aga, a tropical twist on a classic margarita made with tequila, Cointreau, watermelon, and agave syrup.

### 4.Le Pirate Beach Club (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

🌴 **A Minimalist Boutique Beach Club on Ceningan**

![4.Le Pirate Beach Club（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f0da17ee287571e58427e613849d63c3.jpg)

Le Pirate sits right on the water's edge on Ceningan, with a laid-back, tropical vibe. Its design is simple yet stylish—perfect for showing up in your swimsuit with salty, sandy hair, leaning against the bamboo bar for a tropical cocktail, or lounging on striped sunbeds overlooking the turquoise lagoon. If you can't bear to leave, you can even stay in one of their beachfront bungalows for a taste of simple island living.

### 5.The Sand Ceningan (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

🌊 **A Lazy Beach Club on Ceningan**

![5.The Sand Ceningan（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_24e0a3c88eee644eb713888ef74488a1.jpg)

The Sand is a simple beach bar on the shores of Ceningan, serving up strong cocktails and easygoing beach grub—perfect for sun-seekers. It boasts a prime viewing spot and an Instagram-worthy swing suspended over the water, ideal for snapping photos. Bring your bikini and get ready for a blissful day here!

### 6.Agung Beach Club (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

🌅 **A Local Favorite for the Best Sunset Views**

![6.Agung Beach Club（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7ce7cea258b5dcde446e41199097ad3f.jpg)

Agung Beach Club is a laid-back beach club on Nusa Lembongan, beloved by locals and expats alike. Smaller and quieter, it has its own unique charm. You can enjoy meals on floating huts by the shore, relax in wooden beach bungalows, or swing on the sandy beach swings. It's especially recommended at dusk, when it faces the sunset with a distant view of Mount Agung—an ideal spot to watch the day end.
