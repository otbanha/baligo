Beyond being renowned diving destinations, their restaurants and bars offer a rich array of dining choices. Here are a few standout dining and bar spots, plus beach clubs ideal for total relaxation—especially suited for those seeking to release stress while indulging in delicious food and beautiful scenery.

## Must-Visit Restaurants and Bars on Nusa Lembongan

### 1. Thai Pantry (🗺️ [Official Site & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![1.Thai Pantry（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_3396bcd7fb375be2e42c3163938c0f3b.jpg)

If you love Thai cuisine and delicious cocktails, this spot is an absolute must. The restaurant serves authentic Thai dishes, and the cocktails are outstanding! The atmosphere is wonderfully relaxed, making it a perfect choice for a gathering with friends or a romantic dinner.

### 2. Kayu Lembongan (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![2.Kayu Lembongan（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a0e3265965d5557c3a12710fbb60a9b5.png)

Kayu is a restaurant known for its delicious food and famous Frozen Margaritas. The menu offers a wide variety of choices, from local specialties to international dishes, making it a perfect spot to unwind and enjoy dinner after a full day of exploration.

### 3. Cloudland (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![3.Cloudland（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b723e761ad9f105a43748468984140cd.jpg)

This is an ideal spot to watch the sunset, offering affordable cocktails. Sitting here at dusk, watching the sun go down while feeling the sea breeze, is a true feast for both the eyes and the taste buds.

### 4. Tigerlillys Beach Shack (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![4.Tigerlillys Beach Shack（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_02abe71b39d40cf449e9cfe8bef6df43.jpg)

Tigerlillys Beach Shack is a family-friendly restaurant right on the sand, serving delicious breakfasts during the day and buzzing with sunset vibes in the evening. It's a fantastic place to watch the sunset, especially with an Aperol Spritz or an ice-cold beer in hand, taking in the colorful sky and ocean breeze. On weekends, they also have Happy Hour, live music, and movie nights for a relaxed and fun evening.

### 5. The Deck (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![5.The Deck（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e8986e02267a3ab18d94277f8ced41a0.jpg)

Located right next to Thai Pantry, The Deck is another great spot for dining and cocktails. It offers a variety of creative dishes and drinks, making it perfect for both gatherings and romantic dates.

### 6. Curry Traders (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![6.Curry Traders（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_baadfa00828e8fadc5638879b70a7c99.jpg)

If you're craving a spice-filled feast, Curry Traders is an absolute must-visit. This restaurant blends Indian, Sri Lankan, and Southeast Asian flavors, with rich aromas wafting from the open kitchen at the entrance. Signature dishes like butter chicken, Sri Lankan-style vegan dal, and Bengali-style shrimp curry with pineapple are not to be missed, and when paired with their expertly crafted cocktails, the dining experience becomes even more delightful.

### 7.Hai Bar & Grill (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![7.Hai Bar & Grill（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_59e2488ca558f8067df0b4f824052ee3.jpg)

🍽️ 2024 Travelers' Choice winner, the best beachfront restaurant on Lembongan Island

Located on the famous Mushroom Bay Beach in Lembongan, Hai Bar & Grill offers an ultimate seaside dining experience. The open-air design lets in warm tropical breezes while you take in the stunning water views of the bay.

### 8.Cat Island Lembongan (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

https://www.instagram.com/reel/C94T2VvSev7/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==

If you're a cat lover, this unique feline paradise—Cat Island Lembongan—will absolutely steal your heart. Home to 45 healthy and adorable cats, you can pet and play with them to your heart's content. This peaceful cat haven sits by the coast near Yellow Bridge, offering stunning sea views. Admission is 120,000 Indonesian Rupiah (about USD$8), including one drink, and the ticket is valid for a month, so you can come back anytime to visit these furry friends. The staff are incredibly friendly, and their genuine love for the cats shines through. A visit takes about 1–2 hours, making it a heartwarming and fun spot on Lembongan Island.

### 9.The Pontoon at Lago (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DQNlEq3iLc0/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==

Lago is a unique floating bar set on the ocean off Lembongan Island, powered entirely by solar energy. The vibe here is relaxed and lively, with surfers often showing off their skills at the nearby Playgrounds break. You can lounge on bean bags by the water or hop onto floating gear, sipping ice-cold beer, classic margaritas, or espresso martinis while enjoying hand-cut fries and spicy chicken burgers. It's the perfect spot for a lazy afternoon.

### 10.The Coconut Hut (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![10.The Coconut Hut（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7f6d154219950b848b4ab6efe45116bf.jpg)

The Coconut Hut combines a restaurant, bar, and mini-golf concept, offering visitors a unique entertainment experience. Here, you can sip a Pina Colada while taking on a mini-golf challenge, with hot dogs, pasta, and other dishes accompanied by upbeat music. At night, the golf course lit up with twinkling lights, paired with a few cocktails, makes it the perfect spot for both fun and relaxation.

### 11. Nusa Bodega (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![11.Nusa Bodega（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f90877c21bf4e189ba83b709de8963d5.jpg)

If you love Spanish-style tapas and wine, Nusa Bodega is a must-visit. This small bar focuses on serving a variety of Spanish tapas, paired with a curated selection of fine wines, letting you soak in the rich flavors of Spain while stepping away from the island's beachy vibe. Whether you're spending an evening with friends or enjoying a romantic dinner, Nusa Bodega is an excellent choice.

### 12. Ginger & Jamu (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![12.Ginger & Jamu（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e197c5eb1b1194d670f82451daa1517a.png)

Ginger & Jamu is a restaurant in the heart of Jungutbatu Beach on Lembongan, dedicated to health-conscious, locally sourced whole foods. Every dish here is packed with fiber and nutrients, offering everything from morning smoothie bowls and hearty veggie breakfasts to burritos, sandwiches, and colorful salad bowls. My personal favorite is the hearty falafel bowl—its texture and flavor are truly satisfying.

The restaurant features a spacious outdoor seating area overlooking the ocean, with tables and beanbag loungers, perfect for a relaxed meal with friends. Located right next to Ohana's Beach Club, Ginger & Jamu is the ideal spot for lunch before heading over for cocktails in the sun.

### 13. La Bianca (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![13.La Bianca（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-03/1774877991302-la_bianca.jpg)

La Bianca is one of the most enchanting restaurants on Lembongan Island. By day, you can enjoy a 180-degree ocean view, while at night, this romantic spot is bathed in the warm glow of hanging lights and flickering candles.

This European-style restaurant offers outdoor seating, complete with a wood-fired pizza oven and a large grill, serving freshly caught fish, seafood skewers, and delicious meat dishes. The appetizer selection is generous, and the tempting desserts provide a perfect finish to your meal. Although La Bianca is one of the priciest restaurants on the island, it still offers great value—whether you're celebrating a special occasion or enjoying a high-quality dinner, it's not to be missed.

### 14. Soka Warung (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![14.Soka Warung（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_faac031e3a73966604c5bbe2056d243c.png)

Soka Warung is a highly-rated, affordable little eatery near Mushroom Beach on Nusa Lembongan. Their Indonesian fried rice, banana pancakes, and corn fritters are hearty and delicious, with many visitors claiming it's the best Indonesian food they've ever had! The chicken fried noodles and chicken noodle soup have a particularly homey flavor, and the Balinese curry chicken is also highly recommended. The restaurant's location is excellent—you can enjoy dinner while watching the sunset, creating a warm and relaxing atmosphere for an unforgettable dining experience.

### 15. Warung Tumpang Sari Lembongan (🗺️ [Official Website & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![15.Warung Tumpang Sari Lembongan（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_ec50abc0a61fade2fbdbb8ac34f5ba97.png)

Warung Tumpang Sari is a cozy family-run restaurant known for its delicious home-style cooking and affordable prices. The quality of the food is amazing! We recommend trying their tuna katsu curry, tender and juicy tuna pepes, and gado-gado with rich peanut sauce—the flavors are outstanding. The banana pancake is the perfect sweet ending. Please be patient, as everything is made to order to ensure freshness.

### 16. Tropical Juice Corner & Foods (🗺️ [Official Website & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![16.Tropical Juice Corner & Foods（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a33335de6b9d6901039892784695731e.png)

A hidden food gem known for its fresh, vibrant tropical smoothie bowls, perfect for brunch, lunch, or dinner. We stumbled upon this place and it quickly became our favorite spot on the island. Besides the delicious smoothie bowls, the Balinese-style fresh fish dishes and hearty seafood platter are also impressive—every dish is bursting with fresh flavors and is reasonably priced. While breakfast options are limited, the lunch and dinner menus are abundant, and the coffee is wonderfully aromatic. The owner's warm hospitality adds to the restaurant's charm, making it a must-visit for travelers who love authentic Balinese cuisine.

### 17. Cookies Coffee Shop and Warung (🗺️ [Official Website & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![17.Cookies Coffee Shop and Warung（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_573c79acf22a1fc909679946ce5dd274.png)

Cookies Coffee Shop and Warung offers a spacious dining area and is a cute, friendly little spot on Nusa Lembongan. Many visitors come here daily and rave about the menu. The breakfast is especially outstanding, with smoothie bowls and coffee being must-order items every day, all at very affordable prices. The restaurant also serves delicious local dishes like chicken satay, squid ink rice, chicken avocado salad, and garlic bread. Notably, the WiFi here is fast, making it convenient for remote work—a perfect blend of work and dining. Whether you want to experience local cuisine or find a comfortable workspace, this is an ideal choice.

### 18. D Tari Warung (🗺️ [Official Website & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![18.D Tari Warung（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_1d03d25fd3501c11676b2d609e976121.png)

This is a popular local eatery run by a warm, welcoming couple, known for its delicious Indonesian dishes and friendly service. The restaurant's signature dish is a whole grilled red snapper, offered at a competitive price (60,000 IDR, about USD$4), hailed as the best grilled fish in Bali. Other highlights include egg fried noodles, juicy handmade beef burgers, chicken satay, grilled prawns, spring rolls, fried chicken, and banana fritters that customers call 'the best they've ever had.' They also serve the largest and freshest coconuts on the island. Known for its affordable prices, generous portions, fresh ingredients, authentic flavors, and warm service, many visitors say it's the best meal they've had in Bali. Some guests even visit daily during their six-day trip, sometimes twice a day. The food is so good that guests often order the same dishes repeatedly, having found their favorites and not wanting to try anything else. D Tari Warung is more than just a restaurant—it's a perfect place to experience local food culture and hospitality, and a must-visit food destination on Nusa Lembongan.

### 19. Warung Makan Kevin (🗺️ [Official Website & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![19.Warung Makan Kevin（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f9d9ca291af745bdaef4685abc6b5044.png)

Warung Makan Kevin is a beloved local eatery on Nusa Lembongan, run by a warm and friendly family. Known for its authentic Indonesian cuisine, the restaurant's signature dishes include Ikan Pepes (grilled fish wrapped in banana leaves) and Soto Ayam (chicken noodle soup), each showcasing the chef's exceptional culinary skills. Their fried rice is generously portioned, loaded with fresh seafood, and is considered by many visitors to be the best Indonesian fried rice on the island. The grilled seafood selection is equally impressive, especially the grilled squid, which earns high praise from diners.

Although meals take a while to prepare—especially during peak hours when you might wait over an hour—it's absolutely worth it. The dining atmosphere is cozy and welcoming, the staff are friendly and courteous, and the most delightful surprise is that such high-quality food comes at very reasonable prices. Note that this restaurant only accepts cash, no credit cards. Many visitors to Nusa Lembongan return again and again, making it one of the island's most popular food spots.

### 20. Karana Pool Bar & Dining

https://youtube.com/shorts/_-PGilx6H_s

**Karana Pool Bar & Dining** boasts a commanding hilltop location with unbeatable views overlooking the ocean. The restaurant faces the vast, rolling sea and features an infinity pool that stretches toward the horizon, making it a top-tier spot for capturing the sunset as it dips into the water.

The menu offers a diverse range of international dishes and refreshing cocktails, all in a relaxed, resort-style atmosphere. Perched high above the coast with unobstructed views, whether you're lounging on a poolside bean bag or seated in the dining area, you can enjoy your meal while taking in the stunning seascape. It's widely regarded as one of the best places on the island to watch the sunset and feel the rhythm of the waves.

## Recommended Restaurants and Bars on Ceningan Island

### 1. Seabreeze Ceningan (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![1.Seabreeze Ceningan（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2ae7e8d96e43f10c5bb8025c3d593323.jpg)

This restaurant is known for its stunning views, but service can be slow, so be sure to set aside plenty of time to soak in the scenery and savor your meal. Sitting here gazing at the crystal-clear turquoise waters is truly a feast for the eyes.

### 2. KLYF Club (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![2.KLYF Club（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_8389c6bab22f04c8039d0d1098b2929c.jpg)

Perched beside the magnificent Blue Lagoon, KLYF Club offers breathtaking views along with delicious food and cocktails. The vibe here is pure resort-style bliss—a perfect spot to unwind and enjoy a moment of tranquility and great flavors.

### 3. The Dungki Bendega Resto (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![3.The Dungki Bendega Resto（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_1d5d5314ed93c7d3a9623b60df79fee9.jpg)

The Dungki Bendega Resto is the creation of seasoned chef I Kadek Astawa, who brings 15 years of five-star hotel culinary experience to the table. Using fresh, locally sourced ingredients, he crafts incredibly flavorful dishes. Must-tries include the crispy chicken burger, pork ribs, and beef rendang.

### 4.MyBeach Ceningan (🗺️ [Official Site & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![4.MyBeach Ceningan（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7861fe084f1ec3a41f2d7054cde0597c.jpg)

MyBeach Ceningan is a perfect spot for the whole family to unwind, especially for a leisurely lunch or a casual drink in the sun. It features a pool overlooking the sea, and kids can play on a swing set in the ocean. The food is reasonably priced, with the seafood curry being a standout that leaves a lasting impression. The chicken satay and signature fish and chips are also popular among visitors, making it a great choice for a family meal.

The restaurant has a relaxed and cheerful vibe, with efficient and friendly service, suitable for any time of day or night. MyBeach Ceningan is a family-run restaurant that offers delicious meals and stunning sea views—highly recommended for a visit!

### 5.Mangrove Restaurant (🗺️ [Official Site & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

![5.Mangrove Restaurant（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_77cc621dd9cad7736bee41ac682162db.jpg)

Mangrove Restaurant is a quiet and cozy little eatery known for its diverse and delicious cuisine. The head chef, Bu Illuh, is not only talented but also especially attentive to guests with dietary restrictions, carefully explaining menu options to ensure everyone can dine with peace of mind. The menu is extensive, ranging from authentic Indonesian dishes to burgers, curries, and even Ukrainian dumplings and pasta, catering to all tastes.

Mangrove Restaurant serves generous portions at reasonable prices. Besides great food and views, it also offers hookah and board games, making it ideal for friends, couples, and families to enjoy a relaxing time together. Even if you're not staying at the dive resort, it's well worth a visit when you're on Nusa Ceningan!

## Beach Club Recommendations

### 1.Ohana's Beach Club (🗺️ [Official Site & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

🏝️ **The Perfect Blend of Luxury and Laid-Back Island Living**

![1.Ohana's Beach Club（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0818393dd76712be34aed51bb82f68d6.jpg)

Ohana's Beach Club brings a trendy vibe to Nusa Lembongan. It seamlessly combines high-end luxury with the island's relaxed atmosphere, making it the ultimate spot to unwind. Enjoy fresh seafood feasts, healthy salads, and juicy steaks, all served right by bright white sun loungers, surrounded by a refreshing pool and beach umbrellas. Arrive early to snag the best sunny spots, and at night, head to the upper deck for a romantic dinner under the stars.

### 2. Sandy Bay Beach Club (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

🌅 **A Must-Visit Beach Club on Nusa Lembongan**

![2.Sandy Bay Beach Club（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_378a10dfdb8a9a70542a68c5402ec708.jpg)

Sandy Bay Beach Club is simply irresistible—many travelers come to Nusa Lembongan just for this spot. Perched on a stunning bay, it features an open-air lounge area with soft sand underfoot and the sound of waves all around. It's the perfect place to savor fresh seafood and tropical cocktails. Start with the 'snack platter,' then move on to juicy tiger prawns or traditional Indonesian beef rendang. Whether you go for a Bloody Mary or a Margarita, both are highly recommended! Don't miss the beautiful sunset views.

### 3. Suku Beach Club (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

🍹 **A Slice of Paradise on Nusa Ceningan**

![3.Suku Beach Club（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2acb0c969ad35b6675da2c2663d93b7f.jpg)

Suku Beach Club sits on Nusa Ceningan, overlooking pristine white sands and a famous surf break. Its tiered pool lounge area offers a restaurant and bar, where you can while away the day by the pool with a cool coconut in hand, or enjoy light bites on a comfy sunbed. The signature cocktails are a must-try, especially the spicy Aga cocktail—a tropical twist on the classic Margarita made with tequila, Cointreau, watermelon, and agave syrup.

### 4. Le Pirate Beach Club (🗺️ [Official Website & Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

🌴 **A Chic, Minimalist Beach Club on Nusa Ceningan**

![4.Le Pirate Beach Club（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f0da17ee287571e58427e613849d63c3.jpg)

Le Pirate sits right on the water's edge on Nusa Ceningan, offering a relaxed, tropical vibe. The design is simple yet stylish—perfect for showing up in your swimsuit with salt-crusted hair and sandy feet. Lean against the bamboo bar for a tropical cocktail, or lounge on a striped sunbed overlooking the turquoise lagoon. If you can't bear to leave, you can even stay overnight in one of their beachfront bungalows for a taste of simple island living.

### 5.The Sand Ceningan (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

🌊 **A Lazy Beach Club on Ceningan Island**

![5.The Sand Ceningan（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_24e0a3c88eee644eb713888ef74488a1.jpg)

The Sand is a simple beach bar on Ceningan Island, serving strong cocktails and easygoing beach bites—perfect for soaking up the sun. With a prime viewing spot and an Instagram-worthy swing suspended over the water, it's a dream for photos. Grab your bikini and get ready for a blissful day here!

### 6.Agung Beach Club (🗺️ [Official Site & Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1lWr3PTNRvmh44nr_WsTJ-PsnZLtSC30&usp=sharing))

🌅 **A Local Favorite for Sunset Views**

![6.Agung Beach Club（ 官網&地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7ce7cea258b5dcde446e41199097ad3f.jpg)

Agung Beach Club is a laid-back beach club on Lembongan Island, beloved by locals and expats alike. Quieter and more intimate, it has its own unique charm. You can dine on floating huts by the shore, relax in wooden beach cabanas, or swing on a beach swing. It's especially magical at dusk, facing the sunset with a distant view of Mount Agung—an ideal spot to watch the day end.
