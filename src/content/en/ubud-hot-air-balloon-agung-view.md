## **Bali Ubud Hot Air Balloon Adventure: A Dreamy Aerial Date for a Family of Three**

**Bottom line first**: The Ubud hot air balloon experience offers stunning aerial views of **Mount Agong and lush green rice terraces**. We recommend departing at dawn to catch the first light. It's perfect for families with kids aged 5 and up, offering a romantic and breathtaking aerial itinerary. Be sure to read the operator's safety guidelines carefully and book popular time slots in advance.

"Mom, are we really going to fly?" Our 5-year-old son clutched my shirt, his eyes sparkling with excitement. My husband adjusted his camera with a smile: "Don't worry, Dad will protect you." This was our family of three's most special adventure in Bali—our first Ubud hot air balloon experience.

## **Bali's Only Family Aerial Adventure**

As the **only** tethered hot air balloon on the island, this 23-meter-high rainbow giant is especially suitable for families. When the staff saw us with our child, they thoughtfully brought out a small step stool: "Kids can step on this first!" The wicker basket was roomier than expected; the three of us fit perfectly (total weight limit is 150 kg, so no problem for families with kids).

## **The Magical Golden Hour at Dusk**

We chose the highly recommended **5:30 PM sunset flight**. When the flame roared to life with a "whoosh," our little one gasped and buried himself in his dad's arms, but the next second, the rising sensation had him giggling with delight. From 50 meters up, Ubud's rice paddies looked like they were dusted with gold leaf under the setting sun, while Mount Agong appeared and disappeared in the twilight. My husband wrapped one arm around me and used the other to shield our son as he peeked over the edge. Seeing Bali from this angle—it's truly something else!

## **Thoughtful Services Tailored for Families**

- **Kid-Friendly**: The staff took special care to explain how hot air balloons work to the children, and after the flight, they awarded a 'Little Pilot Certificate'—our little one proudly held it up for over a dozen photos.
- **Family Photo Session**: The accompanying photographer captured the moment we embraced in the air as a family.
- **Delicious Dinner**: The Singaporean chef’s specially prepared kids’ meal had our picky eater enjoying every bite, while my husband and I savored a romantic candlelit dinner infused with local flavors.

## **Warm Tips**

- The evening flight is the most popular, so it's best to **check in 30 minutes early**—otherwise, you might end up waiting until dark like we almost did (luckily, the staff prioritized families with kids).
- The basket edge reaches about chest height on a child, so **you'll need to hold your child tightly the whole time** (my husband never let go).
- The burner makes a loud noise during the flight, so **sensitive kids might want to bring earplugs** (our little one was startled at first but quickly forgot the fear once captivated by the view).

Those brief 10 minutes not only showed us the grandeur of Bali but also became the brightest highlight of our family trip. If romance is for two, then happiness is floating together as three in the sky above Ubud.
