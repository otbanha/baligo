## 🎪 GWK Penjor Festival Kicks Off! Free Entry to Lotus Pond Event, Running Until November 8

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1793864089186-15490.jpg)

The GWK Cultural Park in southern Bali officially launched the fourth Penjor Festival 2026 on November 5. This year's event is held at Lotus Pond, blending traditional Balinese culture, art, music, and local cuisine, all set against the festive atmosphere of the upcoming Galungan celebration.

The biggest highlight this year: **The Lotus Pond festival area is free to enter—no need to purchase a full ticket for GWK Cultural Park.**

However, free entry is limited to the festival area at Lotus Pond. If you want to visit the giant GWK statue, Festival Park, or other attractions within the park, you'll still need to buy a regular ticket.

This year's event includes:

- Traditional Penjor decoration displays
- Mini Penjor Competition
- Penjor-making workshops
- Balinese script and Lontar palm leaf writing workshop
- Batik workshop
- Traditional dance and music performances
- Local food and handicraft market

The event runs until November 8.

### Is it worth visiting?

If you're staying in Jimbaran, Uluwatu, or Nusa Dua soon, consider adding the Penjor Festival to your itinerary.

For travelers who want to learn about Balinese culture without spending time touring the entire GWK complex, the free Lotus Pond activity area is a worthwhile option.

Note that only the festival area is free, not the entire GWK Cultural Park.

## 🚋 Latest Progress on Bali's Tram Project! Government Allocates Around IDR 2 Trillion, Construction Expected to Take Two Years

Bali has long struggled with traffic congestion, especially along the route from Ngurah Rai International Airport to Canggu, where traffic volumes are extremely heavy.

On November 5, Bobby Rasyidin, President Director of Indonesia's state railway company PT Kereta Api Indonesia (KAI), announced that the government has allocated approximately IDR 2 trillion for Bali's rail transit project.

The current plan is divided into two phases:

**Phase 1: Airport to Legian**

Starting from Ngurah Rai International Airport, connecting to the Legian area.

**Phase 2: Legian to Canggu**

Extending the route to Canggu, linking the main tourist areas of South Bali.

KAI stated that the overall construction is expected to take about two years.

Previously, the Bali Provincial Government, Badung Regency Government, and KAI signed a cooperation agreement in September to advance the battery-powered tram system.

The planned route is approximately 12 kilometers long. In addition to serving tourists, it aims to improve transportation links between hotels, accommodations, attractions, residents, and local communities.

### When can you ride it?

The project is still in the planning and construction preparation phase and is not yet in operation.

Therefore, travelers still need to rely on private car hires, taxis, Gojek, Grab, and existing public transportation to get around.

If the electric tram is successfully built, it could add a new option for travel from the airport to Canggu. However, actual stations, ticket prices, and the opening date have yet to be announced.

For travel to Bali, this is a transportation development worth keeping an eye on in the long term, but it won't directly change travelers' transportation arrangements in the short term.

## 📲 Bali BMKG Considers Launching WhatsApp Weather Alerts! Future Notifications for Weather and Earthquakes Possible

Bali's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BBMKG Region III) stated on November 5 that it is studying the possibility of issuing weather and earthquake early warnings to the public and travelers via WhatsApp Blast.

Currently, BMKG's early warnings are primarily sent via SMS Blast, with assistance from Indonesia's Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs for delivery.

As for WhatsApp Blast, further coordination is still needed with BMKG headquarters and WhatsApp's parent company Meta, so it has not yet been officially launched.

This proposal was put forward during the BBMKG Region III Public Service Consultation Forum. The meeting also discussed the limitations of currently sharing weather information via WhatsApp groups, including group member caps and message reach.

BMKG also uses digital platforms like Telegram and Instagram to publish weather, climate, and earthquake information.

### How Does This Help Travelers to Bali?

If WhatsApp Blast is officially launched in the future, travelers will have a more direct way to receive weather and earthquake alerts.

Especially as Bali gradually enters the rainy season, timely access to weather information is crucial for those planning trips to waterfalls, mountains, beaches, or nearby islands.

However, this feature is still under discussion and is not yet a formal service available for registration.

For travelers heading to Bali in the near future, it's still recommended to keep an eye on BMKG's official Instagram and weather bulletins.

## 🌊 Waves Up to 2.5 Meters Off Southern Bali! Heavy Rain Alerts Continue in Badung, Tabanan, and Buleleng

Bali's meteorological agency issued a weather advisory on November 5, warning of moderate to heavy rainfall in parts of the island and higher wave risks in southern waters.

The advisory is in effect from 8:00 AM on November 5 to 8:00 AM on November 6 (Bali local time).

Key rainfall warning areas include:

- **Badung Regency:** Moderate to heavy rainfall
- **Tabanan Regency:** Moderate to heavy rainfall
- **Buleleng Regency:** Moderate to heavy rainfall

Additionally, waves in southern Bali waters could reach up to 2.5 meters.

The agency noted that ferry travel may be risky when wind speeds hit 21 knots and waves reach 2.5 meters; for fishing boats, even lower wind and wave conditions could pose safety concerns.

### What should travelers watch out for?

If you're planning to take a speedboat, ferry, or join marine activities on the morning of November 6, it's advisable to check the latest sea conditions and company announcements before heading out.

If you plan outdoor activities in Ubud, mountain waterfalls, or Tabanan, be mindful of rainfall and road conditions.

This warning is valid until 8:00 AM on November 6, but it does not guarantee that heavy rain or high waves will persist afterward. Travelers should refer to the latest updates on the day.
