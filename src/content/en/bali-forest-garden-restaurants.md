If you're weary of Bali's cookie-cutter beach clubs and the congested city streets, and crave a dining space where you can slow down and be gently embraced by nature and greenery, this list is one to save.

This guide features **5 forest and garden restaurants spread across different areas**—from vibrant daytime oases to enchanting evening eateries, each hiding its own surprises.

## Overview of the 5 Restaurants

| Restaurant | Area | Opening Hours | Highlights | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 1. FERRO Grill Restaurant & Bar｜A Green Oasis Grill in Ubud's Alleys

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdeM7dgoX2D/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

**Area:** Ubud, located on the quiet alley of Jl. Bisma **Opening Hours:** 12:00–00:00

FERRO Grill Restaurant & Bar is a refined space that perfectly blends gourmet grilling with a bar atmosphere. Surrounded by Ubud's iconic tropical greenery, it offers an immersive escape from the hustle and bustle.

Whether enjoying a leisurely lunch surrounded by nature during the day or savoring flavorful steaks and signature bar drinks under the evening lights, it showcases a unique mountain dining charm. With high-quality cuisine, professional service, and a decor that blends modernity with nature, it has become a must-visit highlight on Ubud's culinary map.

**Best for:** Travelers looking to enjoy a green lunch in Ubud or a signature cocktail in the evening.

## 2. The Hidden Space Ubud Cafe｜A Secret Base Between Rice Fields and Forests

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYvlHKWPMQQ/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

**Area:** Ubud (Singakerta) **Opening Hours:** 09:00–18:00 (Closed on Sundays)

True to its name, The Hidden Space Ubud Cafe is tucked away between lush green rice fields and tropical forests. This handcrafted wooden building features outdoor terraces and balconies with direct views of gardens, rice paddies, and distant forests.

It offers single-origin specialty coffee, thoughtfully curated dishes with vegetarian options, high-speed Wi-Fi, power outlets, and a cozy little library. Free from commercial hustle, it’s a serene space filled with pure coffee aroma, birdsong, and deep breaths—a secret base for digital nomads and those seeking tranquility.

**Best for:** Digital nomads, vegetarians, and travelers looking for a quiet corner.

## 3. Woods Pererenan｜West Coast Slow Living Brunch in a Wooden Building

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXLzIevgHH6/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

**Area:** Canggu **Hours:** 08:00–23:00

Woods Pererenan takes natural aesthetics and wooden architecture to the extreme. Surrounded by beautiful natural landscapes, the restaurant extensively uses warm wooden elements, creating an atmosphere that blends rustic coziness with modern elegance.

It emphasizes community connection and sustainability, serving dishes and brunch made from the freshest local ingredients. Whether sipping specialty coffee in the morning sunlight or dining amidst lush greenery in the afternoon, you can fully unwind and immerse yourself in the slow-paced lifestyle unique to Bali's west coast.

**Best for:** Travelers looking to start their day with brunch or coffee.

## 4. Samm's Farm｜A Tropical Oasis with an Indoor Organic Farm

https://www.instagram.com/reel/Ddpw6a8Ah6c/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

**Area:** Canggu **Hours:** 08:30–22:00

Samm's Farm is a refreshing immersive oasis that perfectly blends tropical living aesthetics, vintage charm, and Scandinavian minimalist elegance.

The highlight is the stunning **indoor organic farm**, which surrounds diners with lush greenery, offering an incredibly soothing and restorative experience. The semi-outdoor bar is an ideal spot to enjoy coffee while feeling the tropical breeze, perfectly combining nature with refined dining.

**Best for:** Travelers staying in Canggu who want to relax and dine in a green setting.

## 5. Bread Yard｜A Garden Bakery Cafe in the Heart of Seminyak

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DL6rGFgSkbm/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

**Area:** Seminyak, on Jl. Kayu Aya **Hours:** 07:00–21:30

Bread Yard sits in the lively core of Seminyak, yet cleverly brings in a lush garden-style decor, instantly enveloping you in a fresh, resort-like vibe as you step inside.

Known for its handcrafted, crispy baked goods—like the signature pistachio croissant—and hearty brunch platters, it’s a perfect spot for those who love a leisurely brunch, travel with pets, or seek a photogenic corner to soak up the sun and greenery.

**Best for:** Travelers staying in Seminyak who want a convenient, green-filled brunch option.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which cafe offers rice field views while dining?

The Hidden Space Ubud Cafe’s outdoor terrace and balcony provide direct views of rice paddies, gardens, and distant forest scenery. Note that it’s open from 09:00 to 18:00 and closed on Sundays.

### Where can I dine with my pet?

Bread Yard in Seminyak is pet-friendly and also features a garden-style photo corner.

### Need a place to work or go online? Any recommendations?

The Hidden Space Ubud Cafe offers high-speed Wi-Fi and power outlets, making it a great choice for digital nomads.

### Looking for brunch options?

Woods Pererenan and Bread Yard are both famous for brunch—the former focuses on local ingredients, while the latter is known for its artisan baked goods.

### Where to go for dinner and drinks in the evening?

FERRO Grill Restaurant & Bar is open until midnight, where you can enjoy grilled dishes and signature cocktails.
