Tired of the same old Bali beach clubs and the crowded city traffic? If you're looking for a dining space that lets your mind and body slow down, gently embraced by greenery and nature, this list is worth saving.

This selection features **5 forest-themed and garden restaurants across different areas**, from vibrant daytime oases to charming eateries that stay enchanting after dark—each hiding its own surprises.

## Overview of the 5 Restaurants

| Restaurant | Area | Opening Hours | Highlights | Best For |

## 1. FERRO Grill Restaurant & Bar｜A Green Grill Restaurant in Ubud's Alleys

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdeM7dgoX2D/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

**Area:** Ubud, located on the quiet alley of Jl. Bisma **Opening Hours:** 12:00–00:00

FERRO Grill Restaurant & Bar is a refined space that perfectly blends gourmet grilling with a bar atmosphere. Surrounded by Ubud's iconic tropical greenery, it offers an immersive escape from the hustle and bustle.

Whether enjoying a leisurely lunch surrounded by nature during the day or savoring flavorful steaks and signature bar drinks under the evening lights, it showcases a unique mountain dining charm. With high-quality cuisine, professional service, and a decor blending modernity with nature, it has become a must-visit highlight on Ubud's culinary map.

**Best for:** Travelers who want to enjoy a green lunch in Ubud or sip a signature cocktail in the evening.

## 2. The Hidden Space Ubud Cafe｜A Secret Base Between Rice Fields and Forests

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYvlHKWPMQQ/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

**Area:** Ubud, located in Singakerta **Opening Hours:** 09:00–18:00 (Closed on Sundays)

True to its name, The Hidden Space Ubud Cafe is tucked away between lush green rice fields and tropical forests. This handcrafted wooden building features outdoor terraces and balconies overlooking gardens, rice paddies, and distant forest views.

It offers single-origin specialty coffee, thoughtfully prepared dishes with vegetarian options, high-speed Wi-Fi, power outlets, and a cozy little library. Free from commercial hustle and bustle, it’s a serene space filled with pure coffee aroma, birdsong, and deep breaths—a secret base for digital nomads and those seeking tranquility.

**Best for:** Digital nomads, vegetarians, and travelers looking for a quiet corner.

## 3. Woods Pererenan｜West Coast Slow Living Brunch in a Wooden Building

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXLzIevgHH6/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

**Area:** Canggu **Hours:** 08:00–23:00

Woods Pererenan takes natural aesthetics and wooden architecture to the extreme. Surrounded by beautiful natural landscapes, the restaurant makes extensive use of warm wood elements, creating an atmosphere that blends rustic coziness with modern elegance.

It emphasizes community connection and sustainability, serving dishes and brunch made from the freshest, highest-quality local ingredients. Whether enjoying a specialty coffee in the morning sunlight or dining amid lush greenery in the afternoon, you can fully unwind and immerse yourself in the slow-paced lifestyle unique to Bali's west coast.

**Best for:** Travelers looking to start their day with brunch or coffee.

## 4. Samm's Farm｜A Tropical Oasis with an Indoor Organic Farm

https://www.instagram.com/reel/Ddpw6a8Ah6c/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

**Area:** Canggu **Hours:** 08:30–22:00

Samm's Farm is a refreshing immersive oasis that perfectly blends tropical living aesthetics, vintage charm, and Scandinavian simplicity.

The highlight is the stunning **indoor organic farm**, which surrounds diners with lush greenery, offering an incredibly soothing and restorative experience. The semi-outdoor bar is an ideal spot to enjoy coffee while feeling the tropical breeze, perfectly combining nature with fine dining.

**Best for:** Travelers staying in Canggu who want to relax and dine in a green setting.

## 5. Bread Yard｜A Garden Bakery Cafe in the Heart of Seminyak

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DL6rGFgSkbm/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

**Area:** Seminyak, on Jl. Kayu Aya **Hours:** 07:00–21:30

Bread Yard sits in the lively core of Seminyak, yet cleverly incorporates lush garden-style decor, instantly enveloping you in a fresh, resort-like vibe as you step inside.

Renowned for its artisanal, flaky baked goods (like the signature pistachio croissant) and hearty, delicious brunch platters, it's a perfect spot for travelers who love leisurely brunches, bring their pets, or seek photogenic corners to enjoy a relaxing time amidst sunlight and greenery.

**Best for:** Those staying in Seminyak who want a convenient yet verdant brunch option.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which cafe offers rice field views while dining?

The Hidden Space Ubud Cafe's outdoor terrace and balcony offer direct views of rice paddies, gardens, and distant forest scenery. Note that it's open from 09:00–18:00 and closed on Sundays.

### Where can I dine with my pet?

Bread Yard in Seminyak is pet-friendly and also features a garden-style photo corner.

### Need to work or go online? Any recommendations?

The Hidden Space Ubud Cafe offers high-speed Wi-Fi and power outlets, making it a great choice for digital nomads.

### Looking for brunch options?

Woods Pererenan and Bread Yard are both famous for brunch—the former focuses on local ingredients, while the latter excels in handmade baked goods.

### Where to go for dinner and drinks in the evening?

FERRO Grill Restaurant & Bar is open until midnight, where you can enjoy grilled dishes and specialty cocktails at night.
