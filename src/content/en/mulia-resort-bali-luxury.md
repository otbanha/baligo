Nestled in Nusa Dua, Mulia Resort is the ultimate choice for a luxurious getaway. This five-star resort, which opened in 2012, features 424 rooms and is just a 25-minute drive from Bali's Ngurah Rai International Airport, offering convenient access. Check-in begins at 3 PM, and check-out is at noon. Perfect for families, children aged 2 to 11 can stay free of charge, making it an ideal destination for a family trip. The facilities and services here promise an unforgettable vacation in Bali.

### **Art Collection and Spatial Design at the Resort**

Mulia Resort isn't just known for its luxurious amenities; its unique charm lies in seamlessly integrating contemporary global artworks into every corner, transforming the entire resort into an open-air art space. Featuring pieces by artists from China, India, and beyond, these works are scattered across guest rooms, restaurants, the lobby, pathways, and pool areas, crafting a vacation experience rich in cultural taste and visual delight.

### **Serene Artistic Companionship in Guest Rooms**

Stepping into a guest room, your eyes meet authorized paintings by Nanjing artist Yu Qiping. Yu specializes in traditional Chinese literati painting, interpreting classical aesthetics with a restrained contemporary flair. His subjects often immerse themselves in quiet contemplation, surrounded by lotus flowers, still waters, or birds soaring across the sky—these artworks don't vie for attention but quietly accompany you as you unwind, infusing the private space with tranquility and depth. Amid Bali's vibrant vacation atmosphere, this serenity perfectly embodies the essence of true luxury: a calm that comes from within.

### **Contemporary Creative Dialogue in the Lobby**

The Mulia Villa lobby showcases a massive work by Shanghai artist Pianzi. Born in Yunnan and based in Shanghai, Pianzi creates with a humorous and symbolic perspective, using her signature cat motifs and layered symbols to build scenes brimming with imagination and order. Another piece transforms a traditional abacus into a structure of human faces, turning 'calculation' into a metaphor for the relationship between the individual and the collective—a subtle commentary on commerce, order, and societal rhythms, gently prompting guests to ponder the systems behind everyday life.

### **Narratives of Power at Table8 Restaurant**

At the resort's Table 8 Chinese restaurant, works by Guangzhou artist Huang Zhongyang reinterpret historical themes through a cross-cultural lens. His piece on Empress Dowager Cixi uses the imagery of mirrors to explore the literal and metaphorical meanings of 'reflection,' presenting power with subtle, delicate strokes—not declaring, but questioning. The subtle interplay of poses between Cixi and a monkey silently probes the structures of power. In this space where tradition meets precision cooking, art leaves ample room for thought beyond taste.

### **A Tribute to Labor and Service on the Pathway**

Along the resort's pathway, Indian contemporary artist Valay Shende crafts human figures from metal discs and clock parts, exploring themes of labor, collaboration, and collective identity. Inspired by Mumbai's famous dabbawala system, the body made of watch faces symbolizes time and reliability, while the stomach, shaped like a lunchbox, points to nourishment and need. This sculpture subtly captures the essence of hotel service: not a performance, but a steady, consistent fulfillment.

### **Blessings of the Water Goddess at the Pool Area**

In the courtyard pool, ocean pool, and oasis pool areas, three female statues draw inspiration from Bali's water goddess Dewi Danu, symbolizing water, fertility, and the cycle of life. The lotus in the courtyard pool represents devotion and purity; the water bowl in the ocean pool signifies purification; and the rice and water in the oasis pool embody nourishment and renewal. These custom pieces become key landmarks in the space, seamlessly connecting architecture, landscape, and local beliefs, allowing every moment by the pool to be enriched with cultural and spiritual depth.

The Mulia Resort's curation philosophy emphasizes experience and meaning over mere display. Art need not be understood all at once but unfolds gradually during your stay. Amid contemporary luxury's constant pursuit of expression and scale, Mulia chooses a more restrained path—where art doesn't assert its presence but naturally blends into the space. This is true luxury: born from clear choices and values that stand the test of time.
