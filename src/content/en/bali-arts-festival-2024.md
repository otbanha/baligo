The Bali Arts Festival is about to begin in Bali. As one of the island's most significant cultural events, it brings together artists, musicians, and dancers from around the world to showcase their work. During this period, Bali becomes a haven for the arts. You don't have to worry about tickets because all performances at the Bali Arts Festival are free. You'll witness not only Bali's rich performing arts but also cultural highlights from other Indonesian islands. If you're an art and culture enthusiast, this is an event you absolutely can't miss.

## What is the Bali Arts Festival?

The Bali Arts Festival has been held annually since 1979. Though only four decades old, it plays a vital role in promoting Indonesian culture. During this month-long event, you can enjoy spectacular live performances every day, including art, costumes, and handicrafts. There are also dance performances, shadow puppet shows, and music competitions. Hundreds of local and international artists take the festival stage, and no matter your age, you're sure to find performances you'll love during this period.

## When is the 2024 Bali Arts Festival?

The Bali Arts Festival is held once a year. This cultural extravaganza lasts about a month, typically starting on the second Saturday of June and ending in mid-July. However, the exact dates vary slightly each year. In 2024, it will run from **June 15 to July 13**.

## Theme of the Bali Arts Festival

![峇里島藝術節的主題](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_c32e212b2a773b031367bb0fbbad6089.jpg)

The theme of the 2024 Bali Arts Festival is 'Jana Kerthi Pramaguna Wikrama,' which means 'Elevating Human Dignity and Excellence.' The festival's themes are usually based on one of these life-sustaining forces. As a result, the performances and exhibitions may carry a philosophical foundation, offering rich intellectual depth and artistic expression throughout the event.

## Where to Celebrate the Bali Arts Festival?

The Bali Arts Festival is held at the Werdhi Budaya Art Centre ([Werdhi Budaya Art Centre](https://maps.app.goo.gl/bwFAjeWSsyVvMvK57)), a sprawling complex with a distinct local artistic style. On regular days, it may not draw many tourists as a sightseeing spot. But come mid-June, when the Bali Arts Festival kicks off, it becomes the busiest hub. Dance competitions, musical performances, and art exhibitions attract visitors from around the globe. There are also open-air parades. The entire art center feels like a garden, filled with Southeast Asian-style buildings rich in local character and set in a beautiful environment. During the festival, stages and seating areas are set up. Once the performance schedule is finalized, you can pick your favorite shows and plan your time to watch and participate.

### Opening Parade

The Bali Arts Festival kicks off with a grand and uniquely exotic cultural parade. The procession starts at Puputan Badung Square and winds through one of Bali's main roads. In the 2024 parade, hundreds of artists took part, including talents from around the world and local artists from Bali's eight regencies. You'll see traditional Indonesian costumes and instruments, along with representatives and floats from other Indonesian islands. To fully soak in the festive atmosphere, try watching up close. The roadside is a great spot, but it can get hot, so be sure to prepare with sun protection in advance.

### Open-Air Theater

The performances at the open-air theater are not to be missed. Bali's signature dance is known as Balinese dance. Influenced by religious beliefs, it often involves depictions of deities. Based on these themes, you'll see a variety of traditional Balinese dances, impromptu performances by Balinese children, and collaborative shows blending vocals, music, and dance.

### Art Exhibitions

If you're looking for something beyond traditional cultural performances, this event is for you. The exhibition also features a range of stalls for food, floral art, fashion, and handicrafts. You can browse these diverse booths, sample traditional Balinese dishes, admire outstanding local crafts, or pick up some traditional Balinese clothing you like. If you find something you love, buying it as a souvenir for friends is a great idea.

## How to Get to the Bali Arts Festival Venues?

Denpasar, the largest city in Bali, serves as the island's public transport hub. You can reach your destination by chartering a car, taking a taxi, or using a ride-hailing service. Alternatively, renting a scooter is a convenient and nimble way to get around. Denpasar's layout is uniquely woven with villages and historic Hindu temples, so you'll enjoy glimpses of ancient temples and lively villages along the way.

## Tips for Attending the Bali Arts Festival

![參加峇里島藝術節的貼士](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_eb580db9253ea62dc0130897433dc0e1.jpg)

### Weather and Clothing

Bali sits on the equator, with a tropical rainforest climate that's hot and humid. June and July are two of the most comfortable months, with average temperatures ranging from 22 to 27°C (72 to 81°F). Pack light, breathable clothing. While it's not the rainy season, it's wise to bring an umbrella just in case.

### Sun Protection

Although temperatures in June and July are relatively comfortable, sun protection is still essential. During the Bali Arts Festival, there are some outdoor parades, so you can bring a sun-protective jacket, sunglasses, and sunscreen to protect your skin. In the heat, you may lose a lot of fluids, so carrying a water bottle is a smart choice.

### Safeguarding Personal Belongings and Children

During the month of the Bali Arts Festival, Bali becomes very crowded. You must always keep an eye on your valuables. Especially when carrying cash, it's best to keep it in your bag or store it in the hotel safe. Just a small amount of cash on hand is enough. If you want to bring children to experience Indonesia's exotic atmosphere, make sure they never leave your sight.

### Learning the Local Language

Bali's official languages are Indonesian and English. In most cases, English is sufficient. But just in case, you can learn some simple everyday Indonesian phrases. You can say "Terima kasih!" in Indonesian, which means "Thank you."

### Booking Accommodation and Tickets

The Bali Arts Festival is one of Indonesia's most popular festivals, attracting visitors from around the world. Therefore, during this month, Bali's population density will be high. It's a good idea to confirm your accommodation and tickets in advance. You can book a hotel near the festival venue to save time and transportation costs.
