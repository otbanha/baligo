The Bali Arts Festival is about to begin in Bali. As one of the island's most significant cultural events, it brings together artists, musicians, and dancers from around the world to showcase their work. During this period, Bali becomes a haven for art. You don't need to worry about tickets because all performances at the Bali Arts Festival are free. You'll witness not only Bali's rich performing arts but also cultural highlights from other Indonesian islands. If you're an art and culture enthusiast, this is an event you absolutely cannot miss.

## What is the Bali Arts Festival?

The Bali Arts Festival has been held annually since 1979. Despite being only four decades old, it plays a vital role in promoting Indonesian culture. Over the month-long event, you can enjoy spectacular live performances every day, featuring art, costumes, and handicrafts. There are also dance performances, shadow puppet shows, and music competitions. Hundreds of local and international artists take the festival stage, and no matter your age, you'll find performances to enjoy during this period.

## When is the 2024 Bali Arts Festival?

The Bali Arts Festival is held once a year. This cultural feast lasts about a month, typically starting on the second Saturday of June and ending in mid-July. However, the exact dates vary slightly each year. In 2024, it will run from **June 15 to July 13**.

## Theme of the Bali Arts Festival

![峇里島藝術節的主題](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_c32e212b2a773b031367bb0fbbad6089.jpg)

The theme of the 2024 Bali Arts Festival is 'Jana Kerthi Pramaguna Wikrama,' which means 'Elevating Human Dignity and Excellence.' The festival's themes are often based on one of these life-sustaining forces. As a result, the performances and exhibitions may carry philosophical undertones, offering a rich tapestry of thought and artistic expression throughout the event.

## Where is the Bali Arts Festival celebrated?

The Bali Arts Festival will be held at the Werdhi Budaya Art Centre ([Werdhi Budaya Art Centre](https://maps.app.goo.gl/bwFAjeWSsyVvMvK57)), a sprawling complex of buildings with a distinct local artistic style. On ordinary days, as a tourist attraction, it might not draw many visitors. But come mid-June, when the Bali Arts Festival kicks off, it becomes the busiest spot. Dance competitions, music performances, and art exhibitions will attract travelers from around the world. There will also be some open-air parades. The entire art center feels like a garden, filled with Southeast Asian-style buildings that reflect local character, all set in a beautiful environment. During the festival, stages and audience seating will be set up. Once the performance schedule is finalized, you can pick shows that catch your interest and plan your time to watch and participate.

## What kind of performances can you enjoy?

### Opening Parade

The Bali Arts Festival kicks off with a grand and uniquely exotic cultural parade. It starts at Puputan Badung Square and winds through one of Bali's main roads. In the 2024 parade, hundreds of artists took part, including performers from around the world and local artists from Bali's eight regencies. You'll see traditional Indonesian costumes and instruments, along with representatives and floats from other Indonesian islands. To fully soak in the festive vibe, try to watch up close. The sides of the road are a good spot, but the weather can be hot, so it's wise to prep with sun protection.

### Open-Air Theater

Don't miss the performances at the open-air theater. Bali's signature dance is known as Balinese dance, influenced by religious beliefs that involve depictions of deities. Based on this, you'll see a variety of traditional Balinese dances, impromptu performances by Balinese children, and collaborative shows blending vocals, music, and dance.

### Art Exhibition

If you're looking for something beyond traditional cultural performances, this event is for you. The exhibition will also feature a range of stalls for food, floral arrangements, fashion, and handicrafts. You can browse these diverse booths, sample traditional Balinese food, admire outstanding local crafts, or pick up some traditional Balinese attire you like. If you find something you love, buying it as a souvenir for friends is a great idea.

## How to Get to the Bali Arts Festival Venues?

Denpasar, Bali's largest city, serves as the island's public transport hub. You can reach your destination by private car, taxi, or ride-hailing service. Alternatively, renting a scooter offers a lighter, more flexible way to get around. ## Tips for Attending the Bali Arts Festival

![參加峇里島藝術節的貼士](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_eb580db9253ea62dc0130897433dc0e1.jpg)

### Weather and Clothing

Bali sits on the equator with a tropical rainforest climate—hot and humid. June and July are two of the most comfortable months, with average temperatures between 22°C and 27°C (72°F to 81°F). Pack light, breathable clothing. While it's not the rainy season, bringing an umbrella is wise just in case.

### Sun Protection

Though June and July are relatively mild, sun protection is still essential. The festival features outdoor parades, so bring a sun-protective shirt, sunglasses, and sunscreen to shield your skin. You'll likely lose plenty of fluids in the heat, so carrying a water bottle is a smart move.

### Safeguarding Your Belongings and Children

During the month of the Bali Arts Festival, Bali becomes extremely crowded. You must always keep an eye on your valuables. Especially when carrying cash, it's best to keep it in your bag or opt to store it in the hotel safe. Just a small amount of cash on hand is enough. If you want to bring your children along to experience Indonesia's exotic atmosphere, make sure they never leave your sight.

### Learning the Local Language

The official languages in Bali are Indonesian and English. English is sufficient in most cases. But just in case, you can learn a few simple everyday Indonesian phrases. You can say "Terima kasih!" in Indonesian, which means "Thank you."

### Booking Accommodation and Tickets

The Bali Arts Festival is one of Indonesia's most popular festivals, attracting visitors from around the world. Therefore, during this month-long period, Bali's population density will be very high. It's a good idea to confirm your accommodation and tickets in advance. You can book a hotel near the festival venue to save time and transportation costs.
