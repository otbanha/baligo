The fifth Bali Digital Festival (Bali Digifest 2026) officially kicked off on November 12 at Prime Plaza Hotel in Sanur and will run through November 14.

- Digital economy and creative industry forums

- Business startup competitions
- AI Innovation and Digital Content Competition

## 3. North Bali Boosts Agricultural and Fishery Processing! Bank Indonesia Aims to Reduce Southern Economic Concentration

Bali's economic development has long been concentrated in the southern tourist areas, with Denpasar, Badung Regency, and surrounding regions hosting a large number of hotels, restaurants, and tourism-related industries.

On November 12, Bank Indonesia's Bali branch proposed strengthening the agricultural and fishery processing industry in Buleleng Regency, North Bali, to boost local economic value and employment opportunities.

Currently, products with development potential in the area include:

- Coffee
- Cloves
- Grapes
- Tuna
- Milkfish

Bank Indonesia believes that further processing local agricultural and fishery products, rather than selling them as raw materials, will help increase product added value and drive local industry development.

This recommendation aligns with Bali's goal of reducing the economic concentration in the south.

According to Bank Indonesia, the southern Sarbagia region (Denpasar, Badung, and Gianyar) contributes approximately 58% of Bali's Gross Regional Domestic Product.

This development trend is worth noting because Bali's economy is not solely reliant on tourism. If the agricultural and fisheries sectors in the north can continue to grow, there is potential to create more local jobs and diversify the economy.

## 4. Denpasar Renovates 1,571 Meters of Irrigation Canals Involving 9 Subak Traditional Farming Organizations

The Denpasar city government is advancing a project to construct, restore, and maintain irrigation canals spanning approximately 1,571 meters, involving nine Subak traditional irrigation organizations.

According to local economic news released on November 12, the project is expected to be completed by December 2026.

Subak is Bali's traditional agricultural irrigation management system, where farming communities collectively manage water resources. It serves not only an agricultural function but is also deeply intertwined with Bali's religious culture and community life.

The primary goal of this irrigation canal project is to improve water supply for farmland and maintain existing irrigation infrastructure.

For Bali, Subak is more than just a rice field irrigation system—it is a vital cultural heritage. Ongoing maintenance of irrigation facilities helps sustain agricultural production and the continuity of traditional farming communities.

Bali is set to host another major international event at the end of this month.

This regional awards event covers multiple creator categories, including Creator of the Year, Education, Sports, Food, Beauty & Fashion, Entertainment, Live Streaming, and E-commerce.

Beyond honoring Southeast Asian digital creators, the event also reflects the growing influence of community content and the digital industry in the regional economy.
