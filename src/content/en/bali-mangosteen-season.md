**Bottom Line**: The best time to buy mangosteen (Manggis) in Bali is **from January to April each year**, when prices are lowest (around Rp 10,000 per kilogram). Outside the harvest season, prices can spike by 3–4 times. Bali is one of Indonesia’s main mangosteen-producing regions, contributing about 50% of the national yield, and its quality is widely recognized as excellent—so be sure to try some while you're here.

Mangosteen is one of the most widely produced tropical fruits in Indonesia. This sweet-and-tangy fruit typically has a three-month harvest season in each region each year.

However, thanks to Indonesia’s vast geography, mangosteen harvests occur across different regions from November through April. The harvest season usually begins in Sumatra, where farmers start harvesting around November to December, followed by Java and Bali from January to March.

This regional variation in mangosteen harvest seasons across Indonesia means the fruit is available in markets for nearly eight months of the year.

## Mangosteen in Bali

Bali is one of Indonesia’s top mangosteen producers, accounting for 50% of the country’s total output. The mangosteen grown here is of excellent quality and tastes amazing—so when you’re in Bali, don’t miss the chance to enjoy this delicious fruit! In Indonesian, mangosteen is called "manggis."

## Best Season to Buy Mangosteen

Like most fruits, mangosteen is cheaper during the harvest season. At that time, prices are around Rp 10,000 per kilogram. Known as the "Queen of Tropical Fruits," its price can rise significantly—by three to four times—when production drops.

To buy fresh, affordable mangosteen in Indonesia or Bali, the best time is usually between January and April.
