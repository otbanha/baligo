# 🌿 Waking Up Amidst the Rice Fields—An In-Depth Look at Maar Resort & Spa Ubud

If you've spent enough time in Bali, you'll come to realize that the most unforgettable stays aren't the biggest or the most amenity-packed—they're the ones that make you never want to check out.

**Maar Resort & Spa Ubud** is exactly that kind of place.

Officially opening in August 2025, this resort by Ini Vie Hospitality has just ten villas—small and refined. It's not one of those sprawling resorts where you queue at the lobby and share the pool with hundreds of strangers. Instead, it feels like someone took the most beautiful plot of land in Ubud and turned it into a private courtyard for a select few.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1792380840507-158.jpg)

***

### 📍 Location｜Tucked Away on a Rural Lane, and That's a Good Thing

Maar Resort is hidden along a rural lane in the Payangan highlands, north of Ubud, at Jl. Drupadi, surrounded by local homes and rice paddies. This isn't a place you'd stumble upon by chance—you arrive with anticipation, driving in on purpose.

And because of that, the peace here is genuine. No music from neighboring shops, no honking scooters—just the rustle of wind and the chorus of insects.

Nearby attractions are all within a reasonable drive: **Tegallalang Rice Terraces** is just about 10 minutes away, **Ubud City Center** (Monkey Forest, Ubud Palace, traditional art market) is around 15 minutes, and **Neka Art Museum** is similar. The Kepitu Restaurant at The Kayon Jungle Resort, located near the resort, is a jungle-view dining spot well worth a visit—you'll never run out of dining options. The airport is about 40 km away, a 40–50 minute drive by self-drive or private car, and the resort offers a paid airport transfer service.

***

### 🏊 Facilities｜Small, but every detail is on point

The public pool offers a view straight into the jungle and rice fields, as if floating in a sea of green. No parties nearby, no background music—when you jump in in the morning, it feels like it's just you and the entire Ubud jungle.

**Svaha Spa** is the soul of the resort, blending traditional Balinese healing rituals with modern wellness techniques, offering deep treatments like essential oil massages and aromatherapy. In a boutique resort of this scale, the spa is often where you can truly feel the care—because once it becomes a production line, guests immediately notice the difference.

The dining experience is where Maar truly surprises—the resort houses **Cantina Classe Ubud**, an authentic Italian restaurant. In the midst of Ubud's rural jungle, sitting on the terrace with lush greenery all around, enjoying freshly baked handmade pizza—this contrast creates an unforgettable dining memory. Breakfast offers European, Asian, vegetarian, and gluten-free options, plus a kids' menu, making family travel completely worry-free.

There's also a unique cultural experience called **'Journey with Niang Maar'**—the resort invites local elder Niang Maar to become the spiritual symbol of the place. She takes guests on a journey into rural life and the everyday wisdom of Bali. It's not a cultural show for tourists, but a genuine conversation. For families wanting to give their kids a real taste of Bali, or travelers tired of temple sights, this activity is worth a special arrangement.

### 🛏️ Rooms | Few in number, so each one gets the attention it deserves

The room design language is restrained—plenty of natural wood textures, soft indirect lighting, and no over-the-top decor. Staying here doesn't give you that 'this room is just for photos' distance; instead, it feels like someone has thoughtfully set up a home for you.

There are two main room types:

**1 Bedroom Villa with Jacuzzi**: Features a standalone soaking tub and rain-style shower, along with air conditioning, a Smart TV (with Netflix), a minibar, and a coffee machine. The private balcony offers garden or jungle views. Perfect for couples on a honeymoon or anniversary trip.
