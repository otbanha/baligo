## [Room Tour] Hotel Indigo Bali Seminyak Beach｜Bali's Most Magical Beach Resort, with Six Restaurants So You Never Go Hungry!

**Bottom Line**: Hotel Indigo Bali Seminyak Beach is perfect for travelers who want to be **right on the beach, with plenty of dining options and Instagram-worthy spots**—it's the largest Indigo resort in Southeast Asia, featuring six restaurants, three pools, and standard rooms starting at 50㎡, just steps from the beach to watch the sunset; the only downside is that standard rooms don't come with a bathtub.

✨ This time, I'm taking you on a tour of an Indigo hotel I've recently fallen in love with: **Hotel Indigo Bali Seminyak Beach**! Not only is the location perfect and the design magical, but it's also the **largest Indigo resort in Southeast Asia**, with a whopping six restaurants—so you'll never go hungry or run out of choices!

🌴 If you dream of staying by the sea, walking to cafés and boutiques, and sipping a drink at a beach bar in the evening, then **Hotel Indigo Bali Seminyak Beach**, nestled in the heart of Seminyak, will surely win you over. Managed by IHG, this beachfront resort has captivated international travelers since its 2017 opening with its rich local cultural elements, stunning architectural language, and top-notch facilities—an ideal stay that blends art, warmth, and relaxation.

## 🗺️ Location｜Prime Spot, Just Steps from the Beach and Bustling Streets

Hotel Indigo sits right on the first row of Seminyak Beach in southwest Bali—step out the door and you're at the famous Seminyak Beach, with zero distance to the sunset. It's about a 30-minute drive from Ngurah Rai International Airport, and the hotel offers paid airport transfers and free parking, making it perfect for a pre- or post-flight stop or a long, relaxing stay.

![位置 Location｜黃金地段，離沙灘和熱鬧街區只需步行](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_5a54eb8ab9e2ea320a2e58e18da42988.jpg)

Nearby is Seminyak's most vibrant shopping district, all within walking distance:

- Seminyak Square shopping mall
- Famous restaurants and nightclubs like KU DE TA and La Favela
- Numerous independent designer boutiques and art galleries

If you love to wander, snap photos, and eat your way through a place, this is paradise!

The area is a hub for trendy cafes, restaurants, bars, and boutique shops. At night, you can hit the clubs for excitement, while during the day, you can stroll leisurely and grab a coffee. The convenience is unbeatable—making this the prime spot to stay in Seminyak!

### 🍴 Six Restaurants at a Glance (A Hotel That Won't Let You Go Hungry)

**Sugarsand (Dinner & À La Carte Breakfast)**

![六大餐廳總整理（怕你餓到的飯店）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_9da9bf2a98c9fd7b8be0ad61469c2aa2.jpg)

Ocean views + DJ music + stunning landscaping create an incredible atmosphere, serving Japanese fusion cuisine. Dinner presentations are photogenic and taste decent, but the **service is outstanding—they even brought a free birthday cake and sang for a celebration**, which was truly touching. For breakfast, I recommend choosing the à la carte option—it's even better than the buffet! Special highlights include the French brioche, Indonesian fried noodles, and dragon fruit bowl.

Non-resident guests can also enjoy an exquisite three-course lunch or a hearty four-course dinner at SugarSand Restaurant, savoring creative cuisine paired with stunning ocean views.

You can lounge lazily by the pool, sip cocktails, and soak in the tranquility and comfort of a tropical oasis. SugarSand is Seminyak's most vibrant beachfront haven, blending food, drinks, music, and culture—a perfect spot to unwind and soak up the Bali vibe.

## 🛏️ Room Tour｜My Favorite Indigo Room Lately!

This luxurious five-star beachfront resort is one of Bali's best examples of affordable luxury, with off-peak rates starting from around USD$170—great value for money!

Hotel Indigo has a total of 289 rooms and villas, each with a balcony or terrace, and cleverly incorporates Balinese elements like wood carvings, textiles, and local artwork. Rooms are fully equipped with air conditioning, a minibar, coffee and tea, movie channels, a hairdryer, and premium amenities.

- **Standard Rooms (50–69 sqm)**: Available in garden view, ocean view, and pool access versions
- **Suites (up to 107 sqm)**: More spacious, ideal for couples or long-stay travelers
- **Private Pool Villas (300–800 sqm)**: Feature a private living room and pool, perfect for families or groups

### ✅ Pros

- Rooms boast bold design and striking colors—my favorite Indigo so far (surpassing Alishan, Dazhi, etc.)
- Spacious balconies with drying racks for swimsuits
- Impeccable cleanliness, cleaned twice daily; turndown service even refolds clothes and replaces towels
