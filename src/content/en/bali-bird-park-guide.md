## Introduction to Bali Bird Park: A Tropical Paradise of Birds and Blooms

**Bottom line upfront**: Located in Gianyar Regency, Bali Bird Park spans about **2 hectares** and houses over **1,300 birds from 250 species** (including rare varieties from Indonesia, Africa, South America, and Australia). Since opening in the 1990s, it remains a popular nature education spot, perfect for a half-day family eco-interactive outing.

Nestled in Gianyar Regency in central Bali, **Bali Bird Park** has been one of the island's most beloved nature education attractions since its opening in the 1990s. The park covers about **2 hectares**, featuring lush tropical vegetation and pond landscapes, with carefully designed ecological zones that recreate the natural habitats of birds from around the world.

![峇里島鳥園簡介：鳥語花香的熱帶天堂](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_9d386245f4b2e5ff72a2aa378b5a1a8a.jpg)

Currently, the park houses over **1,300 birds spanning more than 250 species**, including rare birds from Indonesia, Africa, South America, and Australia—making it an ideal spot for birdwatching, photography, and educational experiences.

## Eight Themed Ecological Zones

Bali Bird Park is divided into eight main habitat zones, each designed to mimic the climate and vegetation of different regions, recreating the birds' natural environments.

![八大主題生態區](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_aea4c2b914a235be16f57e09e219a799.jpg)

1. **Archipelago Zone**
   Showcases birds from Sumatra, Kalimantan, and Java, including hornbills, golden-necked pigeons, and serpent eagles.
2. **Papua Rainforest Zone**
   One of the most enchanting zones, featuring the magnificent **Bird of Paradise**, cassowaries, and blue-crowned pigeons.
3. **Australian Outback Zone**
   Lively kookaburras and rainbow lorikeets fly freely here—a favorite interactive area for kids.
4. **African Savannah Zone**
   Displays African grey parrots, hornbills, and flamingos, along with African wetland ecosystems.
5. **South America Zone**
   A vibrant collection of macaws, toucans, and Amazon rainforest birds.
6. **Bali Starling Sanctuary**
   A breeding and conservation center for Bali's national bird, the **Bali Mynah**.
7. **Bird Nursery**
   Showcases egg incubation and chick rearing, with a chance to see baby birds hatching.
8. **Komodo Experience**
   One of the few places in Bali where you can observe **Komodo dragons (Varanus komodoensis)** up close.

## Education and Conservation Mission

![教育與保育使命](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_d4c7abe03b460c0714238ead3e94ed93.jpg)

Bali Bird Park carries an important mission of **environmental education and wildlife conservation**. The park houses research and breeding centers and collaborates with multiple international conservation organizations to promote:

- The captive breeding and release program for the Bali myna
- Bird conservation education programs across Indonesia
- Guided school group tours and eco-experience camps

The park also regularly hosts children's nature education activities, teaching kids how to care for animals and respect nature.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**1. Can I buy tickets at the gate?**

Yes, but we recommend [booking online for discounted rates](https://affiliate.klook.com/redirect?aid=116349&aff_adid=1148651&k_site=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.klook.com%2Fzh-TW%2Factivity%2F169-bali-bird-park-bali%2F%3Fspm%3DSearchResult.SearchResult_LIST%26clickId%3D8555ce542e) and fast-track entry.

**2. Can I take photos inside the park?**

Yes, you're free to take photos. Professional photography services within the park are available for an additional fee.

**3. How long does a visit typically take?**

About 3–4 hours. If you want to catch all the shows, it's best to set aside half a day.

**4. Can I bring my own food?**

No. The park has dining facilities with plenty of options.

**5. Can you really see Bali mynas?**

Yes. The park has a dedicated breeding area where visitors can observe this rare bird species up close.
