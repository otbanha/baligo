# Bali GWK Penjor Festival 2026: Free Entry! Enjoy Traditional Bamboo Decor, Cultural Performances & Local Market from November 5 to 8

If you’re in Bali in early November, make sure to visit GWK Cultural Park (Garuda Wisnu Kencana)! The annual Penjor Festival 2026 will be held from November 5 to 8. This year’s highlight: **The Lotus Pond activity area is free to enter—no GWK Cultural Park ticket required—so you can enjoy this festival blending Balinese traditions, music, food, and hands-on experiences.**

Now in its fourth edition, this event is perfect for travelers eager to dive into Balinese culture, experience local festivities, or plan a trip beyond the usual beaches and cafés.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1793864273251-15491.jpg)

## Penjor Festival 2026 Event Details

- **Event Name:** Penjor Festival 2026
- **Dates:** November 5 to November 8, 2026
- **Location:** GWK Cultural Park Lotus Pond
- **Area:** Ungasan, South Bali
- **Event Theme:** Pesta Rasa dalam Balutan Seni dan Budaya (A Festival of Taste Wrapped in Art and Culture)
- **Entry Fee:** Free for the Lotus Pond event area
- **Other Areas:** Festival Park, GWK Statue, and other zones still require regular park admission tickets

Please note that only the Penjor Festival activity area at Lotus Pond is free—this does not mean the entire GWK Cultural Park is free. If you also want to visit the famous GWK giant statue, you'll need to purchase a separate ticket.

## What Is a Penjor? Understanding an Important Balinese Tradition

A Penjor is a highly iconic traditional Balinese decoration.

Typically made from a tall bamboo pole adorned with coconut leaves, rice stalks, fruits, and other ornaments, its curved bamboo form and intricate design are commonly seen at the entrances of Balinese homes, along roadsides, and around temples.

Penjor is closely tied to the important Balinese religious festival of Galungan, symbolizing gratitude and the triumph of good over evil, and carries deep religious and cultural significance.

During the Penjor Festival, GWK Cultural Park's Lotus Pond will showcase a series of Penjors, allowing visitors to admire this traditional craft up close.

For travelers who have only seen Penjors along the streets without understanding their cultural meaning, this is also a great opportunity to learn about Balinese culture.

## Penjor Festival 2026 Event Highlights

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1793864329593-15492.jpg)

### 1. Traditional Penjor Bamboo Decoration Exhibition & Workshop

During the event, Lotus Pond will showcase Penjor decorations featuring Balinese characteristics.

In addition to admiring the traditional bamboo ornaments, a Penjor Workshop will be held on-site, allowing participants to learn about the making process and cultural significance of Penjor.

Furthermore, the event also features a Mini Penjor Competition, encouraging the local community and younger generations to continue Balinese traditions through creativity.

### 2. Traditional Balinese Dance & Music Performances

Penjor Festival is not just about cultural exhibitions; it also includes four consecutive days of music and dance activities.

Local music schools, art groups, and Balinese musicians will perform in rotation, including:

- Tika Pagraky
- Nanoe Biroe
- Ary Kencana and Neli Ambarawati
- Leeyonk Sinatra
- Joyful Music Bali
- Debeat Music Course
- Cressendo
- Komunitas Seni Dana Swara

According to the published schedule, music performances are typically divided into two sessions: early evening sets by music schools and community groups, followed by performances from Balinese musicians later in the evening.

### 3. Bali Local Cuisine and UMKM Market

The event also features a local UMKM (small and medium enterprise) market.

Visitors can browse a variety of Balinese local foods, handicrafts, decorations, and creative products.

Since the Penjor Festival is held close to Bali's Galungan celebration, the market also features items related to festive decorations and ceremonial supplies.

If you enjoy exploring Bali's local culture and unique products, this market is worth setting aside some time to browse at a leisurely pace.

### 4. Balinese Cultural Interactive Experiences

In addition to stage performances and the market, the event offers a variety of hands-on cultural activities:

- Learning Balinese script (Aksara Bali)
- Writing on palm leaves (Lontar)
- Traditional Balinese Penjor-making workshop
- Batik workshop

Among these, writing on Lontar palm leaves is a particularly distinctive cultural experience. For families traveling with children, it also allows kids to engage with and learn about Balinese traditions through hands-on participation.

## Is GWK Cultural Park Worth Visiting?

Located in Ungasan, southern Bali, GWK Cultural Park is a renowned cultural tourist attraction famous for the towering Garuda Wisnu Kencana statue, which stands dozens of meters tall.

Usually, visiting GWK means seeing the giant statue, enjoying cultural performances, and exploring the park’s landscape. But the Penjor Festival offers a different way to experience this cultural park.

In my opinion, if you’ve been to Bali a few times and are looking for something beyond beaches, spas, and beach clubs, this kind of cultural festival is a great choice.

Plus, this year the Lotus Pond event area is free to enter, making it even more appealing for travelers who want to experience Balinese culture without necessarily buying a full GWK park ticket.

## Final Thoughts

The Penjor Festival 2026 will be held from November 5 to 8 at the Lotus Pond in GWK Cultural Park, featuring traditional Balinese bamboo decorations, musical performances, cultural workshops, local food, and an artisan market.

If you happen to be in southern Bali around that time—especially in Jimbaran, Uluwatu, or Nusa Dua—consider adding this event to your itinerary.

**Enjoy Balinese culture for free, join traditional craft workshops, and browse a local market—this is a festival worth discovering for another side of Bali.**
