# Will solo travel to Canggu, Bali be boring?

Having lived in Bali for years, I often get private messages asking: 'I want to go to Canggu alone—will I feel lonely? Is it safe?'

## Why Canggu is great for solo travel

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794493745133-15617.jpg)

Let me cut to the chase: whether you want to meet people, work remotely, or just be alone, Canggu works.

There are plenty of social entry points here. Surf schools, yoga classes, beach clubs, group tours, and coworking spaces—join any of them, and you'll have a chance to chat with someone. If you just want to zone out on your own, no one cares or notices you sitting alone at a café.

Length of stay: If Canggu is just one stop on your Bali trip, I recommend 3 to 5 days. If you're spending the entire trip here, you can stay up to two weeks or more.

## Where to stay depends on how social you want to be

Hostels: Budget-friendly, often host events, easy to make friends, and many also offer private rooms.

Homestays or guesthouses: You get your own room, shared garden or kitchen—not too noisy, not too lonely.

Hotels and villas: Maximum privacy. But a double room costs the same whether one or two people stay, so factor that into your budget.

## When to Go

Dry season (approximately April to October): Sunny, low humidity, best for surfing and beach clubs, but crowded and more expensive.

Rainy season (roughly November to March): It rains often but usually briefly, with fewer crowds and lower prices.

We've just entered the rainy season. Don't stay cooped up in your room when it rains—we've put together a list of [rainy day activities](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/2026-bali-indoor-activities/), including a trampoline park, silver jewelry workshops, and yoga studios in Canggu.

## Solo Travel Safety Tips

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794493677955-15615.jpg)

- Download Grab, Gojek, and offline Google Maps before you go
- If staying more than a few days, buy a local SIM card
- For longer solo day trips, let family or friends know your itinerary
- After dark, stay in well-lit, crowded areas
- Keep your drink close and never accept drinks from strangers
- Store valuables securely and only carry cash you need for the day
- Dress modestly when entering temples
- Be sure to purchase comprehensive travel insurance

## Finally

When traveling solo, most people fear not danger, but having no one to talk to.

Canggu is the opposite. In the morning, someone invites you to surf; after afternoon yoga, someone asks if you want to grab a meal together; in the evening, just find a spot on the beach, and chances are the person next to you is also alone. You don't need to actively make friends—friends will find you.

So stop worrying about whether it's weird to go solo. Just download Gojek/Grab, get your insurance sorted, and on the first day, leave your schedule blank—just walk to Batu Bolong to watch the sunset.
