A quick tip: Should you apply sunscreen or insect repellent first? The correct order is **sunscreen first, then insect repellent**. Here's why: Sunscreen should go on first because it needs to form an even protective layer on your skin to effectively block UV rays. It's best applied 15-30 minutes before heading out to allow absorption. Insect repellent comes second, as its active ingredients (like DEET or other repellents) need to evaporate into the air to work. Applying repellent first can cause sunscreen to dilute or cover it, reducing its effectiveness. **Pro tip:** After applying sunscreen, wait a few minutes for it to absorb, then apply repellent. A 5-10 minute gap is ideal. Some products warn against mixing with sunscreen, so check the label. If you need to reapply sunscreen, remember to reapply repellent too. This ensures both products work their best, keeping you protected from the sun and bugs!

### Frequently Asked Questions

## Should I apply sunscreen or insect repellent first?

Apply sunscreen first, wait 5-10 minutes for it to absorb into your skin, then apply insect repellent. Reversing the order can dilute the sunscreen and cover the repellent, reducing its bug-fighting power.

## Is local insect repellent in Bali any good?

Yes, it's effective and affordable, performing just as well as Taiwanese brands. You can find it at supermarkets and convenience stores, making it easy to stock up locally instead of bringing it from Taiwan.

## Any recommended Indonesian remedies for bug bites?

Try 'Tjing Tjau Balsem' (Green Oil Balm), a common Indonesian ointment for soothing bug bites, headaches, and minor scrapes. It reduces swelling and itching, and is available at supermarkets and pharmacies.

## How to Prepare Medications for Bali Belly?

It's recommended to bring gastrointestinal medications, anti-diarrheal drugs, and cold medicine before your trip. For a detailed self-care checklist and brands available at local pharmacies, see the [Bali Belly Relief Medication Guide](/en/blog/bali-belly-medicine-guide/).
