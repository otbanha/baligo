## 📍 Location & Surroundings

The Ridge Boutique Villas is located in Ubud's Sayan area at Jl. Raya Sayan No. 77, about 34 km (a 1.5-hour drive) from Ngurah Rai International Airport. The hotel boasts breathtaking views of the Ayung River valley and Sayan Ridge, one of Ubud's most iconic landscapes.

Nearby attractions are within walking distance or a short drive: Blanco Museum (about 1.9 km) and Ubud Palace (about 2.2 km) are key cultural landmarks in Ubud. Sayan itself is one of Ubud's most serene and authentic areas, far from the hustle of Ubud Arts Market yet within a 15-minute drive to major sights. The hotel is also surrounded by hidden cafes, yoga studios, and local restaurants, letting you truly experience the Balinese way of life.

***

## 🏨 Facilities Overview

The moment you step into The Ridge, you'll be awestruck by the tranquility and thoughtful design. The hotel features an infinity pool overlooking the majestic Sayan Ridge valley, especially stunning at dusk when sunlight spills across the river valley, taking your breath away.

Highlights of the common areas include: a Spa & Wellness Center (offering traditional Balinese massages and various treatments), sunbathing decks (perfect viewpoints), a restaurant, and a sunset bar. The hotel's dining is exceptional, with breakfast offering a wide selection including Continental, American, and Full English options, along with fresh local specialties and freshly baked bread, even accompanied by champagne. If you prefer to dine in your room, you can enjoy the same quality of meals through room service.

The hotel's standout feature is its 24-hour private butler service, and guests receive a complimentary massage upon check-in—this thoughtful detail ensures a true five-star experience. Additional amenities include yoga classes, fitness sessions, a café, free parking, and airport transfer services. The open-concept bathtub design is unique, turning bathing into a meditative experience.

***

## 🛏️ Room Types

The Ridge Boutique Villas' room designs fully leverage its unique cliffside location. The hotel offers a variety of room types, including the Premiere River View Villa, Premiere Ridge View Villa, and Deluxe Ridge View Villa.

Each villa is independently designed and equipped with air conditioning, a coffee machine, a mini-fridge, a study area, a flat-screen TV, a terrace, and a private bathroom. Rooms are thoughtfully prepared with welcome amenities such as wine or champagne, fruit, and chocolates. The most captivating feature is that each villa boasts a private swimming pool or outdoor bathtub, allowing you to enjoy the magnificent views of the Sayan Ridge from your room—especially at sunrise, when standing on the terrace watching the sunlight gradually illuminate the entire river valley creates a scene absolutely worth capturing with your camera.

The villa's design pays attention to every detail, from furniture arrangement and lighting to the scenic angles, all meticulously planned. For couples or small families seeking complete privacy while still enjoying attentive service, this room type is an ideal choice.

## 💬 Guest Reviews

A British traveler described his five-night stay here as 'the most worthwhile place to stay in Ubud,' highlighting the serene atmosphere, stunning views, warm and attentive service, and the relaxation brought by the complimentary massage. Another German guest praised it as 'a five-star boutique hotel with breathtaking views and meticulous service.'

Common highlights in reviews include: stunning room views (especially at sunrise), attentive and thoughtful staff service, spacious and comfortable rooms, and brand-new, well-functioning facilities. Even when it rains, the hotel provides cover for outdoor areas, allowing guests to still enjoy the scenery in the rain.

The hotel boasts impressive ratings across multiple platforms: a 9.7 out of 10 on Booking.com (based on 114 reviews), with free WiFi scoring a perfect 10, and facilities, cleanliness, and comfort rating 9.6, 9.7, and 9.7 respectively. TripAdvisor gives it a perfect 5.0 rating, ranking it 3rd among 15 hotels in the Sayan area.

## Final Thoughts

The Ridge Boutique Villas is more than just a hotel—it's an immersive Balinese experience. Here, you'll discover that true "five-star" luxury isn't about lavish facilities or price tags, but the subtle details of service: the butler's thoughtful greetings, the morning sun during yoga sessions, and the river valley views enjoyed from your room's bathtub.

Perfect for honeymooners, travelers seeking tranquility and healing, or those wanting to immerse themselves deeply in Ubud and slow down their pace of life. If you only have 3-5 nights in Ubud, consider spending them at The Ridge, letting the sunset over Sayan Ridge and the dawn over the Ayung River valley become the most unforgettable scenery of your Ubud journey.
