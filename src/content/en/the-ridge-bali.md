## 📍 Location & Surroundings

The Ridge Boutique Villas is located in Ubud's Sayan area at Jl. Raya Sayan No. 77, about 34 km (a 1.5-hour drive) from Ngurah Rai International Airport. The hotel boasts stunning views of the Ayung River valley and Sayan Ridge, one of Ubud's most iconic landscapes.

Nearby attractions are within walking distance or a short drive: Blanco Museum (about 1.9 km) and Ubud Palace (about 2.2 km) are key cultural landmarks. Sayan itself is Ubud's most serene and authentic area, far from the bustle of the Ubud Arts Market, yet within a 15-minute drive of major sights. The hotel is also surrounded by hidden cafes, yoga studios, and local restaurants, letting you truly experience the Balinese way of life.

***

## 🏨 Facilities

The moment you step into The Ridge, you'll be awed by the tranquility and thoughtful design. The hotel features an infinity pool overlooking the majestic Sayan Ridge valley, especially breathtaking at sunset when sunlight spills across the river valley.

Public area highlights include a spa & wellness center (offering traditional Balinese massages and various treatments), a sunbathing deck (a perfect viewpoint), a restaurant, and a sunset bar. The dining is exceptional, with breakfast options ranging from Continental, American, to Full English, plus fresh local specialties and baked goods, even accompanied by champagne. Room service offers the same quality for in-room dining.

A standout feature is the 24-hour private butler service, and guests receive a complimentary massage upon check-in—this thoughtful touch delivers a true five-star experience. Additional amenities include yoga classes, fitness sessions, a cafe, free parking, and airport transfers. The open-air bathtub design is unique, turning bathing into a meditative experience.

***

## 🛏️ Room Types

The Ridge Boutique Villas' room designs make the most of their unique cliffside location. The hotel offers several room types, including the Premiere River View Villa, Premiere Ridge View Villa, and Deluxe Ridge View Villa.

Each villa is independently designed and comes equipped with air conditioning, a coffee machine, a mini fridge, a study, a flat-screen TV, a terrace, and a private bathroom. Thoughtful welcome amenities like wine or champagne, fruit, and chocolate are prepared in the room. The biggest draw is that every villa features a private pool or outdoor bathtub, allowing you to soak in the breathtaking views of Sayan Ridge from your room—especially at sunrise, when standing on the terrace and watching the sunlight gradually illuminate the entire river valley is a scene worth capturing with your camera.

The villa's design pays attention to every detail, from the furniture layout and lighting to the carefully planned sightlines. For couples or small families seeking complete privacy while still enjoying attentive service, this room type is an ideal choice.

## 💬 Real Guest Reviews

A British traveler described their five-night stay here as 'the most worthwhile place to stay in Ubud,' highlighting the serene atmosphere, stunning views, warm and attentive service, and the relaxing experience of the complimentary massage. Another German guest praised it as 'a five-star boutique hotel with spectacular views and meticulous service.'

Common highlights in reviews include: breathtaking room views (especially at sunrise), attentive and thoughtful staff, spacious and comfortable rooms, and new, well-functioning facilities. Even when it rains, the hotel provides cover for outdoor areas, allowing guests to still enjoy the scenery.

The hotel boasts impressive ratings across multiple platforms: a 9.7 out of 10 on Booking.com (based on 114 reviews), with individual scores of a perfect 10 for free WiFi, and 9.6, 9.7, and 9.7 for facilities, cleanliness, and comfort, respectively. TripAdvisor gives it a perfect 5 out of 5, ranking it 3rd among 15 hotels in the Sayan area.
