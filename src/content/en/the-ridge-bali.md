## 📍 Location and Surroundings

The Ridge Boutique Villas is located in the Sayan area of Ubud, at Jl. Raya Sayan No. 77, about 34 kilometers from Ngurah Rai International Airport, with a drive time of around 1.5 hours. The hotel boasts stunning views of the Ayung River valley and Sayan Ridge, one of Ubud's most iconic landscapes.

Nearby attractions are within walking distance or a short drive: Blanco Museum (about 1.9 km) and Ubud Palace (about 2.2 km) are key cultural landmarks of Ubud. The Sayan area itself is the most tranquil and authentic part of Ubud, far from the hustle of the Ubud Arts Market, yet within a 15-minute drive to major sights. Around the hotel, you'll find hidden cafes, yoga centers, and local restaurants, letting you truly experience the pace of Balinese life.

***

## 🏨 Facilities Overview

The moment you step into The Ridge, you'll be awed by the surrounding tranquility and thoughtful design. The hotel features an infinity pool that overlooks the majestic valley of Sayan Ridge, especially breathtaking at dusk when sunlight spills over the river valley, leaving you speechless.

Highlights of the common areas include: a Spa & Wellness Center (offering traditional Balinese massages and various treatments), sunbathing decks (perfect viewpoints), a restaurant, and a sunset bar. The hotel's dining is exceptional, with breakfast offering a wide selection including Continental, American, and Full English options, along with fresh local specialties and freshly baked bread, even accompanied by champagne. For those who prefer dining in their room, the same high-quality meals are available through room service.

The hotel's standout feature is its 24-hour private butler service. Upon check-in, guests also receive a complimentary massage—a thoughtful detail that makes you feel truly treated to a five-star experience. Additional amenities include yoga classes, fitness sessions, a café, free parking, and airport transfers. The open-air bathtub design is unique, turning bathing into a meditative experience.

***

## 🛏️ Room Types

The Ridge Boutique Villas' room designs fully leverage its unique cliffside location. The hotel offers several room types, including the Premiere River View Villa, Premiere Ridge View Villa, and Deluxe Ridge View Villa.

Each villa is individually designed and equipped with air conditioning, a coffee machine, a mini-fridge, a study area, a flat-screen TV, a terrace, and a private bathroom. Thoughtful welcome amenities like wine or champagne, fruit, and chocolates await guests. The most captivating feature is that each villa boasts a private pool or outdoor bathtub, allowing you to enjoy the breathtaking views of Sayan Ridge from your room—especially at sunrise, when standing on the terrace watching the sunlight gradually illuminate the entire river valley is a scene absolutely worth capturing with your camera.

The villa's design pays meticulous attention to detail, from furniture arrangement and lighting to the carefully planned views. For couples or small families seeking complete privacy while still enjoying top-notch service, these room types are an ideal choice.

## 💬 Guest Reviews

A British traveler described his five-night stay here as 'the most worthwhile place to stay in Ubud,' highlighting the serene atmosphere, stunning views, warm and attentive service, and the relaxing experience of the complimentary massage. Another guest from Germany praised it as 'a five-star boutique hotel with spectacular views and meticulous service.'

Common highlights in reviews include: stunning room views (especially at sunrise), attentive and thoughtful staff service, spacious and comfortable rooms, and brand-new, well-functioning facilities. Even when it rains, the hotel provides cover for outdoor areas, allowing guests to still enjoy the scenery in the rain.

The hotel boasts impressive ratings across multiple platforms: a 9.7 out of 10 on Booking.com (from 114 reviews), with free WiFi scoring a perfect 10, and facilities, cleanliness, and comfort earning 9.6, 9.7, and 9.7 respectively. On TripAdvisor, it holds a perfect 5.0 rating, ranking 3rd among 15 hotels in the Sayan area.
