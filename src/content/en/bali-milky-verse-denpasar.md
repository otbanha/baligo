# Bali Family Attractions | Milky Verse Space Playground – Let the Kids Burn Energy in an Air-Conditioned Mall in Denpasar

When traveling to Bali with kids, the two biggest worries are scorching sun or sudden downpours. That's where a cool, air-conditioned indoor playground comes in as a perfect backup plan. The newly opened **Milky Verse** in Denpasar is just such a gem.

### What is Milky Verse?

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfvT6XpyZWQ/?utm_source=ig_web_button_share_sheet

Milky Verse is an Indonesian chain of indoor family play centers. The Denpasar location spans about 1,400 square meters and is themed around **space**. It's located at **Ramayana Bali Mall** in Denpasar and officially opened on November 12, 2025.

Inside, there are about **30 play attractions**, and one ticket grants unlimited access. Both kids and adults can join in the fun—it's not just for little ones.

### What to Play?

- **Flying Fox**: This was a highlight at the opening, even tempting adults to give it a try.
- **Ninja Warrior**: An obstacle course facility, perfect for kids who love a challenge.
- **Ball Pit, Slides, Rope Challenge, Rock Climbing**: Travelers have shared that these facilities combined are enough to tire kids out completely.
- **Toddler Zone**: Young children have a separate safe area, so they don't have to crowd with older kids.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794988249589-15700.jpg)

### Why It's Worth Adding to Your Itinerary

**1. Works in rain or scorching sun.** Located inside a mall with full air conditioning, so you don't have to worry about the weather.

**2. Play first, then shop.** After the kids burn off energy, you can grab coffee, eat, or stock up in the same building without needing to move to another spot.

**3. One ticket, unlimited play.** No need to pay per facility, making budgeting easy.

**4. Fun for both adults and kids.** The whole family can join in, making it more memorable than just watching from the sidelines.

### Ticket Price Reference

The opening price was **Rp 90,000 for 2 hours, with free socks included**. At that time, there was also a plush toy promotion: 3 for Rp 100,000. Prices may change at any time, so please check on-site or the official social media announcements before you go.

### Quick Tips Before You Go

- I couldn't find official operating hours for this place, so check their Instagram or mall info before heading out.
- Play areas usually require socks; they provided them at the opening.
- To avoid crowds, go on weekdays.

### Summary

On rainy or scorching days, or when the kids have boundless energy, Milky Verse is a solid choice in Denpasar. Add it to your Bali family itinerary, and both adults and children will have a blast.
