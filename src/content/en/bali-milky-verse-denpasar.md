# Bali Family Attractions | Milky Verse Space Playground – Let the Kids Burn Energy in an Air-Conditioned Mall in Denpasar

When traveling to Bali with kids, there are two things you dread most: scorching sun or sudden downpours. That's when an air-conditioned indoor playground becomes a lifesaver. The newly opened **Milky Verse** in Denpasar is just the spot.

### What is Milky Verse?

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfvT6XpyZWQ/?utm_source=ig_web_button_share_sheet

Milky Verse is an Indonesian chain of indoor family play centers. The Denpasar venue spans about 1,400 square meters and is themed around **space**. It's located at **Ramayana Bali Mall** in Denpasar and officially opened on November 12, 2025.

Inside, there are around **30 play attractions**, and one ticket grants unlimited fun. Both kids and adults can join in—it's not just for little ones.

### What to Play?

- **Flying Fox**: This was a highlight at the opening, even tempting adults to give it a try.
- **Ninja Warrior**: An obstacle course facility, perfect for kids who love challenges.
- **Ball pit, slides, rope challenges, rock climbing**: Travelers have shared that these facilities combined are enough to wear kids out completely.
- **Toddler zone**: Young children have a separate safe area, so they don't have to crowd with older kids.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794988249589-15700.jpg)

### Why is it worth adding to your itinerary?

**1. It works for rainy days or scorching sun.** Located inside a mall with full air conditioning, you don't have to worry about the weather.

**2. Head straight to the mall after playing.** Once the kids are worn out, you can grab coffee, eat, or shop in the same building without needing to move elsewhere.

**3. One ticket, unlimited play.** No extra fees per facility, making budgeting easy.

**4. Fun for both adults and kids.** The whole family can join in, making it more memorable than just watching from the sidelines.

### Ticket Price Reference

The opening price was **Rp 90,000 for 2 hours, with free socks**. At that time, there was also a plush toy promotion: 3 for Rp 100,000. Prices may change at any time, so please check on-site or the official social media announcements before your visit.

### Pre-Trip Tips

- I couldn't find official operating hours for this place, so it's best to check Instagram or mall info before heading out.
- Play areas usually require socks, but this one provided them at opening.
- To avoid crowds, try visiting on weekdays.

### Summary

On rainy days, scorching hot days, or when the kids have boundless energy, Milky Verse is a solid choice in Denpasar. Adding it to your Bali family itinerary ensures fun for both adults and children.
