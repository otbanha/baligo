When people think of Bali, beaches, temples, or spas often come to mind. But this time, my three friends and I decided to try something different. After comparing options, we chose the 'ATV Ride + Ayung River Rafting' combo package—and it turned out to be the most thrilling and unforgettable day of our Bali trip!

![峇里島 ATV 越野車 vs 阿勇河泛舟懶人包：行程排法、必帶物品與新手安全須知](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_41c737cdbc148a72f531b97d2acd93c1.jpg)

## Itinerary Overview: The Perfect Land-and-Water Combo

This day trip is well-designed, blending two distinct adventures: river rafting and off-road riding. The morning features a 2-hour Ayung River rafting session, followed by an Indonesian-style buffet lunch in the jungle, and a 1.5-hour ATV ride in the afternoon. The whole experience runs from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, making for a truly packed day.

## Morning Experience: Exploring the Ayung River

### Safety Briefing and Gear Preparation

At 8:30 AM, the shuttle bus arrived right on time. I have to give props to their transportation—it was an air-conditioned van with comfy seats, and the 30-minute ride felt effortless. After arriving at the rafting starting point, the professional guide gave a thorough safety briefing, covering how to properly wear life jackets, paddling techniques, and emergency procedures.

All safety gear was complete and in good condition, including waterproof helmets, life jackets, and paddles. The guide also double-checked everyone's equipment to ensure it was worn correctly, which was very reassuring.

### Rafting Experience: A Water Adventure Full of Surprises

The Ayung River rafting route is ingeniously designed, lasting about 2 hours with a moderate difficulty level. It features thrilling rapids and calm scenic sections. What surprised us most was discovering several hidden waterfalls along the way! Tucked away in the dense tropical rainforest, these waterfalls are only accessible by bamboo raft—a true hidden gem that most tourists never see.

![漂流過程：驚喜連連的水上冒險](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_78f83f6f2bd34de9e53e3f59a235f6ab.jpg)

One waterfall was particularly spectacular, standing about 15 meters tall. The water cascaded straight down the cliff, and under the sunlight, we could even see a rainbow. Our guide stopped here for about 10 minutes to let us take photos. My friend Sarah was nervous at first, but once she saw the scenery, she got so excited that she kept gasping in amazement throughout the ride.

![漂流過程：驚喜連連的水上冒險](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_12e5e214ed2fc2788efeef3a2bd801a8.jpg)

The most thrilling part of the rafting was navigating through several Class III rapids. Our rubber raft sped through the turbulent water, splashing everywhere. Everyone got soaked but had an absolute blast. Kevin even joked that this was more exciting than the water rides at an amusement park!

## Lunch Time: A Feast for the Senses in the Forest

After the rafting, we arrived at a dining area deep in the forest. The setting was truly stunning—surrounded by lush greenery, fresh air, and the chirping of birds. The open-air design made it feel like we were dining in the embrace of nature.

The buffet offered a rich variety of dishes, including classic Indonesian fried rice (nasi goreng), satay skewers, Indonesian curry chicken, assorted vegetable dishes, and fresh tropical fruits. The quality of the food was surprisingly good, especially the satay—the meat was tender and paired perfectly with the peanut sauce. All four of us were thoroughly satisfied, recharging our energy for the afternoon's ATV adventure.

Before lunch, there were shower facilities available, which was a thoughtful touch. It allowed us to change into clean clothes and enjoy the meal comfortably, ready for the afternoon activities.

## Afternoon Challenge: ATV Off-Roading Through the Countryside

### ATV Safety Briefing and Test Drive

At 1:30 PM, we arrived at the ATV activity base. Just like in the morning, a professional instructor gave a detailed safety briefing and operation tutorial. Operating an ATV isn't difficult—it mainly involves controlling the throttle and brakes—but the instructor still carefully explained turning techniques, uphill and downhill precautions, and more.

Everyone gets a few minutes to test drive at the practice area to ensure they're comfortable before officially setting off. The safety gear, including helmets and boots, is of excellent quality.

### Rural Adventure: Exploring Bali's Most Beautiful Countryside

The ATV route mainly runs through Bongkasa Pertiwi and Taman areas, where Bali's most pristine rural landscapes are preserved. Our path includes rice paddy trails, bamboo tunnels, traditional villages, and hill paths, each offering different scenery and challenges.

![鄉村探險：穿越峇里島最美麗的鄉間](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_cb111d28d880b9d215e5593dce74447a.jpg)

The most unforgettable part was riding through the layered rice terraces, with emerald rice stalks swaying in the breeze and distant mountains silhouetted against the sky, creating a stunning pastoral scene. We also passed several traditional Balinese villages, witnessing locals' daily lives, and children excitedly waving at us—a heartwarming touch.

![鄉村探險：穿越峇里島最美麗的鄉間](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_284e63ef9aa86b0f3931cc37de500341.jpg)

The most thrilling section was the hill path, with some steep slopes and bumpy rocky roads. My friend Amy was a bit nervous at first but quickly adapted, even starting to enjoy the sense of accomplishment from conquering tough terrain. The funniest moment was Kevin, who nearly tipped over on a muddy turn midway—luckily, he reacted fast and steadied the bike. We all burst out laughing at his clumsy move.

### Natural Scenery: Discovering Another Side of Bali

The ATV ride allowed us to delve into Bali's inland areas, revealing a landscape completely different from the coast. Here, there are dense tropical rainforests, clear streams, and small waterfalls hidden in valleys. The ecosystem along the way was rich, with various tropical birds and butterflies, and we even encountered a few wild monkeys.

![自然景觀：發現峇里島的另一面](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_311b1eb06b6d96b8434a358a1fa59c69.jpg)

The most striking moment was stopping by a small stream where the instructor let us rest and take photos. The water was crystal clear, surrounded by pristine tropical vegetation with no trace of human intervention. This untouched natural beauty was truly refreshing and gave us a whole new perspective on Bali.

## Practical Information and Helpful Tips

### Transportation Arrangements

If you choose a package that includes transfers, I highly recommend it. The driver was punctual and professional, so there was no need to worry about transportation at all. From our hotel in Ubud, it took about 30 minutes to get there.

### Essential Packing List

Based on our actual experience, the following items are essential:

- Swimsuit and change of clothes (prepare at least two sets)
- Waterproof bag (to protect your phone and camera)
- Sunscreen (SPF 50 or higher)
- Towel
- Water shoes or sandals (the kind you can wear in the water)
- Personal medication
- Cash (for souvenir photos and tips)

### Important Reminders

- Download WhatsApp: This is crucial! Local operators almost exclusively use WhatsApp for communication, including confirming pickup times and locations.
- Age Limit: ATV activities require participants to be at least 12 years old and accompanied by an adult.
- Weather Factor: During the rainy season, river currents can be stronger, so it's advisable to check weather conditions in advance.
- Insurance: The package includes basic insurance.

### Cost Breakdown

The package we chose covers all essentials: a professional guide, all safety gear, a buffet lunch, shower facilities, bottled water, and basic insurance. Extra costs mainly include souvenir photos and tips for the guide or instructor.
