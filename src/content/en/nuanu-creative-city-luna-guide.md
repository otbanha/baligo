Nuanu Creative City is a massive 44-hectare creative complex located in the Tabanan region of Bali, Indonesia (south of Tanah Lot). Founded in 2020 by Russian tech entrepreneur Sergey Solonin with a total investment of USD$100 million, this ambitious project takes its name '**Nuanu**' from Balinese, meaning '**in progress**'—symbolizing the ever-evolving nature of this creative city. Solonin launched the project after moving to Bali five years ago, aiming to create an ecosystem where art, technology, and nature coexist in harmony.

## Founding Philosophy and Core Principles

The core mission of Nuanu Creative City is rooted in the Balinese philosophy of '**Tri Hita Karana**,' which emphasizes a harmonious balance among three relationships: humans with the divine, humans with each other, and humans with nature. The landmark 'THK Tower' (Tri Hita Karana Tower) within the complex embodies this philosophy. Designed by architect Arthur Mamou Mani using reclaimed wood, this spiraling tower serves not only as a visual centerpiece but also as a symbol of Nuanu's spiritual essence.

Solonin says: 'Our goal is to create a space where art, nature, and technology merge in a balanced and innovative way. This is not just infrastructure—it's an ongoing dialogue with Bali's heritage.' He positions Nuanu as an '**experiential park**' that blends education, wellness, and sustainable living, aiming to become a model for future utopias.

## Sustainability and Environmental Commitment

A standout feature of Nuanu Creative City is its unwavering commitment to environmental sustainability. Of the 44-hectare site, developers pledge to preserve two-thirds (about 70%) as natural landscape, using only 30% for construction. Specific environmental measures include:

- **Tree planting initiative**: 15,000 trees will be planted in the educational zone to boost biodiversity and sequester carbon.
- **Waste Management**: 70% of waste generated within the park is recycled.
- **Ecosystem Protection**: No large trees are cut down; when necessary, trees are relocated to other areas within the park.
- **Mangrove Conservation**: Protect and expand the mangrove ecosystem by replanting mangrove seedlings and native coastal tree species.
- **Zero-Emission Transition**: Gradually adopt zero-emission vehicles to reduce the carbon footprint.

These measures set Nuanu apart from the widespread overdevelopment commonly seen in Bali, making it a model for sustainable development.

## Community Engagement and Global Vision

Nuanu is not just a physical space but a vibrant creative community. Each project partner commits 5% of their monthly revenue to the Nuanu Social Fund, which supports reforestation or social initiatives. The fund has already backed several community projects, such as establishing a computer education center in Beraban village and preserving traditional Balinese music.

Solonin envisions Nuanu's future: "Over the next decade, Nuanu will become a dynamic global hub, attracting the world's most innovative minds to live, collaborate, and visit. It will be a thriving community of visionaries, artists, and changemakers, living and working in thoughtfully designed spaces that inspire creativity."

Nuanu Creative City began operations in July 2024, hosting international events like the Suara Art & Music Festival, CoinFest Web3 Festival, and DEEP Wellness Festival, further cementing its role as a crossroads of innovation and culture.

This creative city represents a new model of urban development, proving that modern construction can address contemporary issues while respecting local traditions, inviting people to join the journey toward a sustainable future. As one observer noted, Nuanu is not just a tourist destination in Bali but "a space to live, work, and create—a city of the future."

## Park Features and Facilities

Nuanu Creative City currently has several independent projects completed, with more underway, aiming to eventually feature 50 diverse spaces. These facilities seamlessly blend Balinese tradition with modern innovation, including:

![The Earth Sentinels（ 地圖位置）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_5dcdfe9ea3388581b2c312023e4c5bc6.jpg)
