**Key Takeaways**: The key to traveling during pregnancy is **thorough preparation and a flexible itinerary**—arrange a Chinese-speaking driver, e-visa, tourist tax, and customs declaration online in advance. On departure day, prepare health certificates and essential medications (stomach medicine, cold medicine). This 5-day, 4-night route was **Sanur → Nusa Lembongan → Ubud → Canggu**, with a chill pace and no rigid schedule, proving that pregnancy travel can still be thoroughly enjoyable.

This Bali trip was an exciting first-time experience for me! I'm very grateful to the friends who shared their tips in the [Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/indonesia.travelguide), which helped me find an amazing Chinese-speaking driver. I'll introduce him in detail at the end of the article.

## Essential Preparations Before Departure:

1. **Excellent Chinese-Speaking Driver**
   Having a Chinese-speaking driver on this trip meant I never had to worry about transportation. His Chinese was very fluent, and he not only took us to all the popular local spots but also flexibly adjusted the itinerary based on time and traffic, making the journey smoother.
2. **Confirm Itinerary with the Driver**
   Before departure, remember to communicate the daily schedule with the driver in advance. The driver will offer professional advice based on our needs, ensuring the best experience at each attraction.
3. **Exchange TWD for USD**
   Before leaving, remember to exchange some New Taiwan Dollars for US Dollars, and then exchange them for Indonesian Rupiah upon arrival in Bali. This is the most cost-effective way to exchange money.
4. **E-Visa**
   Don't forget to apply for the Indonesian e-visa in advance. This will save you time waiting in line at airport immigration, making the start of your trip smoother.
5. **Tourist Tax**
   Bali charges a tourist tax for incoming visitors. It's recommended to pay it online in advance to avoid cumbersome procedures upon arrival.
6. **Customs Declaration Form**
   Remember to fill out the customs declaration form online three days before departure. This will save you a lot of time when entering the country.
7. **Health Certificate and Flight Ticket**
   On the day of departure, remember to get your flight ticket and fill out the health certificate to ensure all documents are complete and correct, avoiding last-minute panic at the airport.
8. **Prepare Essential Medications**
   Although this trip wasn't very long, it's still good to have some basic medications on hand just in case, such as stomach medicine and cold medicine.

These preparations may seem tedious, but they all contribute to a smoother journey, allowing me to fully enjoy every moment in Bali!

## Day 1 Itinerary:

**Arrive in Bali at 15:15**
The plane touched down right on time at Bali's international airport, marking the official start of our long-awaited adventure!

**Present Completed Health Certificate**
After disembarking, the first step was to show our pre-filled health certificate. This process was quick and smooth, showing us that Bali's pandemic protocols are still running efficiently.

**Present Completed Electronic Visa**
Next up was showing our e-visa. Since we had it ready in advance, we skipped the lines and used facial recognition technology to breeze through immigration. It was incredibly convenient and saved us a lot of hassle!

**Present Customs Declaration Form**
The final step was showing our customs declaration, which we also completed online beforehand. The entire entry process went off without a hitch.

**Wait for Luggage**
We cleared immigration so fast that waiting for our luggage ended up taking the longest! 🤣 But once we had our bags in hand, we felt a huge wave of relief.

**Meet Our Driver**
As soon as we exited the airport, our pre-booked driver was already waiting for us. Seeing him made the whole trip feel so much more grounded and real! We were ready to kick off our culinary journey through Bali.

## Day 2 Itinerary (Nusa Lembongan Adventure):

After arriving at the Sanur ferry terminal, we purchased a day trip to Nusa Lembongan on the spot. There were plenty of options available, and we chose a package that included snorkeling and sightseeing—it offered great value for money.

### ❤️ Reflections:

1. **Make sure to prepare your clearance documents in advance!**
   During this trip, I noticed many travelers handling their paperwork only upon arrival, leading to long queues and chaos. So be sure to prepare health certificates, e-visas, customs declaration forms, and other documents in advance for a smoother and faster entry.
2. **Bali has many attractions, but traffic is heavy—don't overpack your itinerary!**
   Bali's attractions are incredibly diverse, but traffic is also intense, especially during peak hours. It's best not to cram your schedule too tightly to avoid wasting time on the road. The best experience here is simply to chill and soak in the local pace of life.
3. **Remember to haggle at market stalls!**
   When shopping at markets, always bargain! For example, I bought a coin purse at Love Anchor, haggling the price from the original 320,000 IDR down to 140,000 IDR, and at another stall, I even got it down to 100,000 IDR. So there's definitely room to negotiate—don't be shy, just go for it! 😅
4. **With limited English and being three months pregnant, I was grateful for a local who spoke Chinese**
   During this trip, though I was three months pregnant and worried about my English skills, I was lucky to meet a local driver named Kang Ge (Wayan Suantara) who speaks Chinese. His thoughtful help made the entire journey so reassuring, especially with route planning and attraction recommendations—he offered plenty of practical advice.
5. **Souvenir recommendation: BALI BANANA banana cake**
   Before heading home, don't forget to grab a box of BALI BANANA banana cake. It's a signature Bali souvenir—soft, sweet, and delicious—and it's available at the airport. You can buy one to try upon arrival, and if you like it, pick up more to take back. They also offer free packing service, making shopping even more convenient.
