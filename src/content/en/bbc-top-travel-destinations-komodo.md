Around this time each year, travel media outlets start rolling out their 'must-visit lists.' This time, BBC's 2026 edition of '[The 20 best places to travel in 2026](https://www.bbc.com/travel/article/20251209-the-20-best-places-to-travel-in-2026)' features a main image of Komodo Island, and it instantly grabs your attention. After all, having spent time in Bali, seeing a nearby spot get international recognition makes you take a closer look.

## Komodo National Park Turns 45

The list notes that Komodo National Park will celebrate its 45th anniversary in 2026, with a new wave of conservation programs set to launch. A more practical change is that getting there has become easier—Labuan Bajo now has direct flights from Singapore and Kuala Lumpur, so you don't have to navigate complicated routes like before.

In recent years, many people have been combining Bali and Komodo into a single trip—one offers island relaxation and cultural experiences, the other wild Komodo dragons and pink sand beaches. The two completely different vibes surprisingly complement each other well. Infrastructure has indeed been catching up, with airports, docks, and accommodations all significantly better than a few years ago.

## Other Spots on the List

Beyond Komodo, this list spans five continents, with each destination chosen for distinct reasons—it's not just a random collection of popular spots.

In Asia and the Middle East, Abu Dhabi is emerging as a cultural hub in the Middle East with several new museums in the Saadiyat Cultural District. Japan's Ishikawa Prefecture hopes visitors will return to the Noto Peninsula, using tourism to aid post-earthquake recovery, while also enjoying Kenroku-en Garden and local sake. As for Phnom Penh, Cambodia—I spent eight years there before moving to Indonesia, so I have a special attachment! Now, with a new airport and a revamped riverside area, it's transformed from a simple transit point into a place worth exploring.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784430595493-pp.jpeg)

▲ Phnom Penh Riverside, Cambodia

In Europe: Scotland's Hebrides offer white sandy beaches and the ancient Callanish Standing Stones, paired with whisky culture—perfect for a leisurely exploration. Montenegro adds hiking routes in the Prokletije Mountains alongside the Bay of Kotor. Portugal's Guimarães celebrates 25 years as a UNESCO World Heritage site while being named European Green Capital. Finland's Oulu, one of the 2026 European Capitals of Culture near the Arctic Circle, focuses on art festivals and saunas.

In the Americas: Chile's Colchagua Valley stretches from the Andes to the Pacific, offering vineyards, stargazing, and colonial towns. The Oregon Coast in the U.S. is ideal for a slow road trip, with cliffs and lighthouses as highlights. Philadelphia will host a series of celebrations and events for the 250th anniversary of American independence. The Colonial Zone of Santo Domingo in the Dominican Republic has just been renovated, coinciding with the 2026 Central American and Caribbean Games. Canada's Slocan Valley will open the 'Japanese Canadian Heritage Trail' to commemorate that chapter of WWII history.

Oceania and Africa: Rarotonga in the Cook Islands boasts blue lagoons and rich Polynesian culture, while Aitutaki is even quieter. Australia's Uluru has a new trail that traverses the desert while introducing the culture of the Anangu people. Kenya's Samburu is recognized for its rare wildlife safaris, elephant rescue center, and stargazing.

Other highlights include Roman ruins in Algeria, the whale and dolphin sanctuary in Loreto, Mexico, Uruguay's beaches and the historic city of Colonia del Sacramento, and eco-tourism on Costa Rica's Osa Peninsula—each holding its own appeal.

### Final Thoughts

Looking at the full list, what struck me most is Komodo making the cut this time. Indonesia's tourism marketing in recent years has become more internationally savvy—not just posting scenic photos, but weaving conservation and sustainability into the narrative. I think that's the right direction. If you're still deciding on your next trip this year, Indonesia deserves a spot on your shortlist—once you've had your fill of Bali, a quick flight to Labuan Bajo to see the Komodo dragons offers a completely different experience.
