### 2024 TripAdvisor Best Hotels Ranking: Indonesian Hotels Shine

TripAdvisor recently released its '2024 Best Hotels' list, where Indonesian hotels secured five positions in the 'Top 25 Hotels in Asia.' Selected through TripAdvisor's 'Travelers' Choice' awards, these rankings are based on guest reviews and ratings. Published on May 2, 2024, the list highlights the outstanding performance of Indonesian hotels across Asia.

![2024年TripAdvisor最佳酒店排行榜：印尼酒店展露頭角](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a2aa5589dd5b39a5450058b7041dcde2.jpg)
