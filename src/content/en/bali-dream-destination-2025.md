**The Most Dreamy Travel Destination of 2025: Bali Tops the List Again**

🌏 **What are the dream travel destinations in the hearts of global travelers?** Which places consistently sit at the top of everyone's bucket list? According to a [Forbes report](https://www.forbes.com/sites/laurabegleybloom/2024/12/28/ranked-the-worlds-top-15-bucket-list-trips-according-to-a-new-report/), a team of analysts from South African travel planning agency Go2Africa delved into global travel trends to answer these questions, compiling a list of the top 15 dream destinations for 2025.

---

### **Bali's Enduring Charm**

### **Bali: Over 2.28 Million Searches**

According to Go2Africa's survey, Bali topped the list as the most popular dream destination with over 2.28 million searches, firmly holding the number one spot.

![峇里島：搜尋熱度超過 228 萬次](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_d4ba57e219f9b6e16abe1a7812239161.jpg)

Analysts point out that Bali offers diverse appeal, catering to honeymooners, adventure seekers, culture enthusiasts, family vacationers, parents with kids, and professional surfers alike. Additionally, Bali is regarded as a spiritual hub, attracting travelers focused on wellness and soul-searching.

Many dream of visiting Bali because of its deep connection to the famous film *Eat Pray Love*. The island is also renowned for world-class surfing, serene beaches, stunning waterfalls, and iconic temples, making it suitable for all types of travelers.

![Julia Roberts主演的Eat, Pray, Love有很多在峇里島拍攝的片段](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_d85492ea48f0a782578fd5e2417169d5.jpg)

### **A Former Champion: Still the All-Time Favorite Destination**

Bali isn't new to the top spot—it was also a travel hotspot in 2019. But in the years that followed, other destinations briefly claimed the crown:

- **2020**: Jordan's ancient city of Petra drew the most traveler attention.
- **2021**: Dubai's Burj Khalifa took the lead.
- In recent years, the Maldives also briefly topped global travel rankings.

### **Regional Travel Preferences: Dream Destinations by Country**

Beyond the global list, Go2Africa also analyzed travel preferences by country, revealing:

- **Switzerland**: Travelers dream of visiting Las Vegas, USA.
- **Vietnam**: The most coveted destination is the Eiffel Tower in Paris.
- **Honduras**: Travelers most want to see the Grand Canyon in the USA.
- **Argentina**: Many dream of experiencing New York City firsthand.
- **Algeria**: The dream destination is the Great Wall of China.

---

### **The Irresistible Allure of African Wildlife Safaris**

While the second-ranked wildlife safari isn't limited to one country, analysts note that Africa is undoubtedly the best continent for experiencing wildlife. "An African safari brings you closer to nature than almost anything else—from majestic lions to elusive aardvarks, every moment is full of wonder."

---

### **Santorini: A Paradise for Photography Lovers**

Ranked third, Greece's Santorini is renowned for its whitewashed buildings and stunning Aegean Sea views. The analyst adds: "Beyond its picturesque architecture, Santorini's volcanic beaches, lunar-like landscapes, and breathtaking scenery make it an exceptional spot for photography."

## **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

### **1. Why is Bali so popular?**

Bali is renowned for its diverse range of activities, stunning natural landscapes, and rich cultural heritage, making it a top choice for all kinds of travelers.

### **2. When is the best time to visit Bali?**

The best time to visit Bali is during the dry season (April to November), when the weather is sunny and perfect for outdoor activities and beach relaxation. While the rainy season brings more showers, the scenery remains enchanting and there are fewer crowds.

### 3. **What local foods are recommended in Bali?**

Don't miss Bali's culinary delights, including Babi Guling (suckling pig), Sate Lilit (minced seafood satay), and Nasi Goreng (fried rice). Fresh seafood and tropical fruits are also worth trying. Check out our guide to the "[Top 10 Foods in Bali](/en/blog/bali-travel-tips/)"

### 4. **Are there many accommodation options in Bali?**

Bali offers tens of thousands of accommodation choices, from luxurious resorts to budget-friendly guesthouses. Whether you're seeking indulgence or traveling on a tight budget, there's something for everyone. Want to avoid a bad pick? Check out our recommendations.

### **5. Do I need a visa for Bali?**

For short-term travel, visitors from some countries enjoy visa-free entry, such as Singapore, Malaysia, and Hong Kong. Travelers from Taiwan or China still need to obtain a visa in advance. See our guide: [Entry Requirements] 4 Essential Steps Before Your 2025 Bali Trip
