Tired of the bustling traffic and crowds in central Ubud? Just turn a corner and step into a hidden paradise! The **Sweet Orange Walk Trail** (connecting the Kajeng and Subak Juwuk Manis rice paddy paths) has become a popular secret walking route in recent years.

The trail is flat and easy to walk with almost no stairs, making it accessible even for those not used to hiking. Surrounded by lush coconut trees, vast rice paddies, and local villages, you'll also find artistic stone carvings and installations along the way.

### 🌟 Trail Highlights & Features

- **Stress-free countryside stroll:** A leisurely walk takes less than an hour, perfect for absorbing phytoncides and clearing your mind.
- **Stunning sunsets and evening glow:** Best visited in the late afternoon, when golden sunlight and orange-red clouds gradually paint the rice paddies, creating an incredibly romantic atmosphere.
- **Local小店 (Sweet Orange Warung):** Midway or at the end, take a break at a charming local-style pavilion shop, sip on a fresh, chilled coconut water, and savor the slow life.

### 📍 Practical Transportation & Route Guide

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1790578864722-rpite.jfif)

- **Entrance Location:** Set your navigation to **The PAON International Restaurant** or near the Ubud Art Museum, then turn into the small alley beside it to find the trailhead.
- **Fee:** Currently free of charge
- **Suggested Itinerary:** After finishing the trail, you can directly connect to the bustling Ubud Market and the palace area, making it perfect for a half-day trip!
