## Why Stay in Kuta

Although Kuta isn't ideal for a long stay (see: [The Most Convenient Place to Stay in Bali, Yet the Least Recommended! Pros and Cons of Kuta](/en/blog/kuta-bali-accommodation-pros-cons/)), here are four reasons we chose to stay in Kuta right after landing:

1. It's the closest area to the airport, so we could relax almost immediately after landing. Traveling with two kids, staying in the nearest spot to the airport minimizes fatigue from the long flight, letting the whole family unwind quickly and start enjoying Bali.
2. It fit our plan to head to Jimbaran and Uluwatu on the second day. Kuta's location is great—not only close to the airport but also convenient for reaching other Bali attractions like Jimbaran and Uluwatu. We planned to visit these two places the next day, so staying in Kuta saved travel time and let us savor more of the journey.
3. It was our first time in Bali, and we wanted to see the liveliest area. Kuta is the most bustling part of Bali, with vibrant streets, diverse dining and shopping options, and stunning beaches. As first-timers, we wanted to soak up Bali's energy and charm right in Kuta, experiencing local culture and atmosphere.
4. We heard Kuta Beach is the best place to learn surfing, and our two boys wanted to give it a try. Kuta Beach is one of Bali's most famous surf spots, with beginner-friendly waves and wide sandy shores, perfect for kids eager to surf. We hoped to witness our boys' courage and effort as they took on the waves, sharing joyful beach moments together.

After weighing these four factors, we decided to spend our first day in Bali in Kuta and found a comfortable hotel near Beachwalk Shopping Center to kick off our Bali adventure.

## Aloft Bali Kuta is a New Marriott Hotel

On the hectic first day of flying, to align with our family's 'leisurely vacation' goal, we didn't want to rush around. The Kuta area, just 15-20 minutes from the airport, was a perfect fit!

Located within the Beachwalk Shopping Center and opened in April 2022, Aloft Kuta is Marriott's younger brand. Its design is simple and comfortable, with relatively affordable rates while maintaining Marriott's quality. The service is friendly, the facilities are new and refreshing—it just feels great (awesome). Although it's next to the mall, entry and exit require passing through X-ray security gates, ensuring safety. With only three floors, Aloft Kuta has many rooms per level. Rooms deeper inside are quieter, away from the hustle of Kuta's beachfront street, offering a good night's sleep in the twin-bed room layout.

![「Let's Play and Eat 胡志明市」現場實拍](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e310dd9b549bdb012b4b52ba5e812f36.jpg)

The room design includes a small sofa area, a balcony, and a separate wet-and-dry bathroom—overall, we were extremely satisfied! The breakfast restaurant is bright and spacious, with floor-to-ceiling windows letting in plenty of sunlight. Though the breakfast selection isn't huge, it offers both Western and Balinese options, refined and delicious. We absolutely loved the overall vibe!

The pool's atmosphere is lush and green, surrounded by trees, offering an endless sense of clarity. We stayed here for two nights, but unfortunately one morning was drizzly, and the other we saved for surfing—since right across from the hotel is the world-famous (beginner-friendly) surf spot, Kuta Beach, how could we not visit?

The best scenery is the people—at Aloft Kuta, it's everywhere you look. The overall service deserves a perfect score; isn't that exactly what a vacation needs?

## Notes & Reflections

✖️ If you don't want to travel all the way to Jimbaran for sunset, just head next door and follow the ‘Beachwalk Shopping’ signs to the ‘Sunset Deck’—you can enjoy a stunning Bali sunset for free! 🥳🥳🥳 It was a delightful surprise during our trip, being able to take in such a beautiful sunset right near the mall, which truly lifted our spirits.

![「Let's Play and Eat 胡志明市」現場實拍](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7442b03d0a7e21248397c17556f37d93.jpg)

‘Let’s Play and Eat Ho Chi Minh City’ live footage

🍽 Restaurants line both sides of the Sunset Deck. I originally planned to grab a drink at ‘Mr. Wayan, by the sea’ on the left to enjoy the sunset 🍸, but all the good seats were reserved, so I just wandered around the deck 👀. Even without finding the perfect spot, strolling under the sunset and soaking in nature’s beauty was still a joy.

🍴 The restaurant on the right, ‘Raa Cha Suki & BBQ Beachwalk,’ seemed to have even better outdoor seating, closer to the sea. If you’re interested, be sure to book in advance! 📲 Next time, I’ll definitely reserve a table to enjoy a perfect combo of seaside dining and sunset.

🌠 In the evening, the Sunset Deck is accompanied by soft music 🎵, creating a wonderful atmosphere of sunset, scenery, and good times 😊. Watching the sun set with music in the background was an incredibly uplifting and refreshing moment.

👀 Bali is vast, but the roads are narrow and single-lane, so traffic jams are common 🚘. If you don’t feel like going out to find food, the Beachwalk Shopping Center is a lifesaver for satisfying hunger 💪. It offers not only a wide range of dining options but also snacks and souvenirs, making it both convenient and affordable.

🔹 1F: A massive candy wonderland ‘Candylicious’ 🍭, bursting with colors and vibrancy—a true paradise for little ones 🫧 (we visited). The kids couldn’t get enough of this candy haven; they had a blast there, and I enjoyed seeing their happiness.

![雜記&心得](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_c6082ea9e6e06a45c3157784ad9da261.jpg)

🔹 3F: ‘MM Juice’ 🍹, recommended by netizens for its tasty juices but reportedly poor food (we didn’t visit). Although I heard the juices here are great, we chose other restaurants instead—maybe I’ll give it a try next time.

🔹3F: "Bebek Tepi Sawah" 🦆, a great choice for those short on time who want to taste authentic Balinese cuisine (did not visit). Although we didn't get to try the food here, we've heard the dishes are very authentic—definitely on the list for next time in Bali.

## Recommended Bali Private Driver

✨ A good driver takes you to heaven ☁️—local eats 🍽, stunning views 📸, and a tour guide too 📚. Highly recommend this Bali driver 👍

📋 Our Bali trip came together last minute, and we were worried about getting between attractions 📸. We planned to just ask our hotel for help, but a week before departure, a Facebook group admin from '[Bali/Indonesia Travel Guide](https://www.facebook.com/groups/indonesia.travelguide)' gave us a hand 💪—and we got a ticket to paradise.

🏡 We stayed with Gede near Kuta. Before returning to work in his hometown, he worked on large cruise ships in the US 🇺🇸, so his English is flawless 👍. He has daughters aged 8 and 10, and a 6-year-old son 👨‍👩‍👧‍👧, so he's incredibly patient with kids 💗—a huge help for family travelers like us 🙇‍♀️.
