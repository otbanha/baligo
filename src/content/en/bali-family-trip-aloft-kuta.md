## Why Stay in Kuta

Although Kuta isn't ideal for a long stay (see: [The Most Convenient Place to Stay in Bali, Yet the Least Recommended! Pros and Cons of Kuta](/en/blog/kuta-bali-accommodation-pros-cons/)), here's why we chose to stay here right after landing—four reasons:

1. It's the closest to the airport, so we could relax almost immediately after landing. Traveling with two kids, staying in the nearest area to the airport minimizes fatigue from the long flight, letting the whole family unwind quickly and start enjoying Bali.
2. It aligned with our plan to head to Jimbaran and Uluwatu on day two. Kuta's location is excellent—not only close to the airport but also convenient for reaching other Bali attractions like Jimbaran and Uluwatu. Staying here saved travel time and let us make the most of our trip.
3. It was our first time in Bali, and we wanted to see the liveliest area. Kuta is the most bustling part of Bali, with vibrant streets, diverse dining and shopping options, and stunning beaches. As first-timers, we wanted to soak up Bali's energy and culture right here.
4. We heard Kuta Beach is the best place for beginners to learn surfing, and our two boys wanted to give it a try. Kuta Beach is one of Bali's most famous surf spots, with gentle waves perfect for beginners and wide sandy shores. We hoped to witness our boys' courage and effort as they took on surfing, sharing joyful beach moments together.

After weighing these four factors, we decided to spend our first day in Bali in Kuta, and found a comfortable hotel near Beachwalk Shopping Center to kick off our Bali adventure.

## Aloft Bali Kuta is a New Marriott Hotel

On the hectic first day of flying, to align with our family's goal of a 'relaxing vacation,' we didn't want to rush between destinations. The Kuta area, just 15–20 minutes from the airport, was a perfect fit!

Located within the Beachwalk Shopping Center and opened in April 2022, Aloft Kuta is Marriott's younger, trendier brand. Its design is simple and comfortable, with relatively affordable rates while maintaining Marriott's quality. The service is friendly, the facilities are brand new, and the overall vibe is fantastic. Although it's next to a shopping mall, entry and exit require passing through an X-ray security gate, ensuring safety. With only three floors, Aloft Kuta has many rooms per floor. Rooms deeper inside are quieter, away from the hustle and bustle of Kuta's beachfront street, ensuring a good night's sleep in the twin-bed room.

![「Let's Play and Eat 胡志明市」現場實拍](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e310dd9b549bdb012b4b52ba5e812f36.jpg)

The room design includes a small sofa area, a balcony, and a separate wet-and-dry bathroom—overall, we were extremely satisfied! The breakfast restaurant is bright and comfortable, with floor-to-ceiling windows letting in plenty of sunlight. While the breakfast selection isn't huge, it offers both Western and Balinese options, all refined and delicious. We absolutely loved the overall atmosphere!

The pool had a lush green vibe, surrounded by trees, offering an endless sense of clarity. We stayed here for two nights, but one morning was drizzly, and the other we saved for surfing—since right across from the hotel is the world-famous (beginner-friendly) surf spot, Kuta Beach. How could we not visit?

The best scenery is the people, and at Aloft Kuta, it's everywhere you look. The overall service deserves a perfect score—isn't that exactly what a vacation needs?

## Notes & Reflections

✖️ If you don’t want to trek all the way to Jimbaran for sunset, just head next door and follow the ‘Beachwalk Shopping’ signs to the ‘Sunset Deck’—you can enjoy a stunning, ‘free’ Bali sunset right there 🥳🥳🥳. It was a delightful surprise on our trip, being able to soak in such a beautiful sunset near the mall, lifting our spirits.

![「Let's Play and Eat 胡志明市」現場實拍](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7442b03d0a7e21248397c17556f37d93.jpg)

‘Let’s Play and Eat Ho Chi Minh City’ live shots

🍽 Restaurants line both sides of the Sunset Deck. I originally planned to grab a drink at ‘Mr. Wayan, by the sea’ on the left and enjoy the sunset 🍸, but all the good spots were reserved, so I just wandered around the deck instead 👀. Even without finding the perfect seat, strolling under the sunset and soaking in nature’s beauty was a treat.

🍴 The restaurant on the right, ‘Raa Cha Suki & BBQ Beachwalk,’ seemed to have even better outdoor seating, closer to the sea. If you’re interested, be sure to book in advance! 📲 Next time, I’ll definitely reserve a spot to enjoy a meal by the sea with that stunning sunset combo.

🌠 In the evening, the Sunset Deck is accompanied by soft music 🎵—sunset, scenery, and good vibes 😊. With the music playing and the sun setting, it was an incredibly joyful moment that left me feeling refreshed.

👀 Bali is vast, but the roads are narrow and single-lane, so traffic jams are common 🚘. If you don’t feel like venturing out for food, the Beachwalk Shopping Center is a lifesaver for hunger pangs 💪. It offers a wide range of dining options, plus snacks and souvenirs—super convenient and affordable.

🔹 1F: A massive candy wonderland ‘Candylicious’ 🍭, bursting with colors—a true paradise for little ones 🫧 (we visited). The kids couldn’t get enough of this candy haven; they had a blast, and I loved seeing their joy.

![雜記&心得](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_c6082ea9e6e06a45c3157784ad9da261.jpg)

🔹 3F: ‘MM Juice’ 🍹—recommended by netizens for tasty juices, though the food is reportedly not great (we didn’t visit). While I heard the juices are good, we opted for other restaurants. Maybe next time we’ll give it a try.

🔹3F: "Bebek Tepi Sawah" 🦆, perfect for those short on time who still want to taste authentic Balinese cuisine (did not visit). Although I didn't get to try the food here, I've heard the dishes are very authentic—definitely on my list for the next Bali trip.

But a week before departure, a Facebook group admin from '[Bali/Indonesia Travel Guide](https://www.facebook.com/groups/indonesia.travelguide)' gave us a hand 💪 — and we got our ticket to paradise.
