Beyond Bali's beaches, villas, and cafés, there's now a brand-new shopping landmark to explore.

Situated in the **Kura Kura Bali Special Economic Zone (SEZ)**, **Sira Village Grand Outlet Bali (GOB)** has become Bali's first large-scale Premium Outlet and the largest outlet shopping village on the island. For travelers who love shopping and hunting for brand discounts, this is a fresh spot worth dedicating half a day or even a full day to on your next Bali trip.

***

## Over 100 International Brands with Year-Round Outlet Discounts

Sira Village brings together **over 100 internationally renowned brands**, offering outlet prices all year round—no need to wait for special sale seasons to snag great deals.

According to the official announcement, prices here are **on average about 30% cheaper than those at regular shopping malls**. Whether you're looking for clothing, footwear, sportswear, or lifestyle goods, you'll find more attractive pricing.

If you're already planning to buy branded items in Bali, it's worth stopping by Sira Village first—you might save a good chunk of your budget.

***

## More Than Just Shopping—A Blend of Food, Art, and Ocean Views

Sira Village isn't your typical enclosed outlet mall; it's designed as an **open-air shopping village**.

The overall architecture is infused with rich Balinese design elements, letting you soak up a vacation vibe even while shopping. Beyond the brand stores, the complex also features:

- Multiple restaurants and specialty cafés
- Local Balinese cuisine
- Art and cultural exhibition spaces
- A coastal promenade
- Viewing areas to catch Bali's stunning sunsets

So even if your travel companions aren't in the mood to shop, they can still relax with afternoon tea or enjoy the ocean views within the complex.

## Real-Time Flight Info at Your Fingertips

One of Sira Village's standout features is its **Live Flight Information** service.

Shoppers can browse stores while checking the status of flights at Ngurah Rai International Airport, without constantly pulling out their phones to look up departure times.

This design is especially suitable for:

- Travelers with late return flights
- Those who have checked out but still have free time
- People who want to use their last few hours to shop for souvenirs

It allows you to plan your final day with greater peace of mind.

***

## Discovering the Kura Kura Bali Special Economic Zone

Sira Village is located within **Kura Kura Bali**, on **Serangan Island (Turtle Island) south of Denpasar**, about a **20-minute drive from downtown Denpasar** and roughly **30 minutes from Ngurah Rai International Airport**, making it very accessible.

Kura Kura Bali is not your typical tourist area—it's a **Special Economic Zone (SEZ)** spanning about **498 hectares**, centered around **ecotourism, wellness, and creative industries**. It blends Bali's traditional culture with sustainable development principles, aiming to create a new-generation resort destination that balances international tourism with local character.

Beyond Sira Village, the area will continue to develop with:

- Green sustainable architecture and low-carbon urban planning
- A marina and seaside leisure facilities
- International conference and creative industry spaces
- Occasional Island Bazaar markets, cultural events, and art exhibitions

Compared to well-established areas like Kuta, Seminyak, and Nusa Dua, Kura Kura Bali feels like an emerging destination still taking shape—one with plenty of promise for the future.

***

## Driving New Tourism Development in Bali

Beyond offering a brand-new shopping experience, the official estimate is that Sira Village will create **over 1,000 job opportunities**, further boosting the development of surrounding tourism, retail, and dining industries, making it a key facility in the Turtle Island Special Economic Zone.

As more international brands and tourism facilities gradually set up, Kura Kura Bali is poised to become another emerging travel hotspot worth watching, alongside Ubud, Seminyak, and Nusa Dua.

***

## Is It Worth Including in Your Bali Itinerary?

If you've traveled to Japan, Korea, or the West, you're likely familiar with Premium Outlets.

In the past, shopping in Bali was mostly concentrated in malls like Beachwalk, Discovery Mall, and Seminyak Village. Now, with the arrival of Sira Village, Bali officially has its own large-scale Premium Outlet.

In my opinion, **if you enjoy shopping for international brands or want to pick up souvenirs before heading home, Sira Village is well worth a visit.** Even if you have no shopping plans, you can explore the rapidly developing Kura Kura Bali Turtle Island Special Economic Zone, savoring delicious food and watching the sunset in a relaxed seaside setting, experiencing a new facet of Bali's future tourism development.
