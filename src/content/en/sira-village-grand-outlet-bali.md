Beyond Bali's beaches, villas, and cafés, there's now a brand-new shopping landmark to explore.

Situated within the **Kura Kura Bali Special Economic Zone (SEZ)** on Turtle Island, **Sira Village Grand Outlet Bali (GOB)** has officially become Bali's first large-scale Premium Outlet and the largest outlet shopping village on the island. For travelers who love shopping and hunting for brand discounts, this is a fresh destination worth setting aside half a day—or even a full day—to experience.

***

## Over 100 International Brands with Year-Round Outlet Discounts

Sira Village brings together **over 100 internationally renowned brands**, offering outlet prices all year long—so you can snag great deals without waiting for a special sale season.

According to the official announcement, prices here are **on average about 30% cheaper** than those at regular shopping malls. Whether it's clothing, footwear, sportswear, or lifestyle goods, you'll find more attractive pricing across the board.

If you're already planning to buy branded items in Bali, it's worth stopping by Sira Village first—you might save a good chunk of your budget.

***

## More Than Just Shopping: A Blend of Food, Art, and Ocean Views

Sira Village isn't your typical enclosed outlet mall—it's built around the concept of an **open-air shopping village**.

The overall architecture is infused with rich Balinese design elements, letting you soak up a vacation vibe even while you shop. Beyond the brand stores, the complex also features:

- Multiple restaurants and specialty cafés
- Local Balinese cuisine
- Art and cultural exhibition spaces
- A coastal promenade
- Viewing spots to catch Bali's stunning sunsets

So even if your travel companions aren't in the mood to shop, they can still relax with afternoon tea or take in the ocean views within the complex.

## Real-Time Flight Info at Your Fingertips

One of Sira Village's standout features is its **Live Flight Information** service.

Shoppers can check the status of flights at Ngurah Rai International Airport while browsing, without having to constantly pull out their phones to track departure times.

This design is especially suitable for:

- Travelers with late return flights
- Those who have checked out but still have free time
- People who want to use the last few hours to shop for souvenirs

It allows you to plan your final day with greater peace of mind.

***

## Getting to Know the Kura Kura Bali Special Economic Zone

Sira Village is located in **Kura Kura Bali**, on **Serangan Island (Turtle Island) south of Denpasar**, about a **20-minute drive** from downtown Denpasar and **30 minutes from Ngurah Rai International Airport**, making it quite convenient to reach.

Kura Kura Bali is not your typical tourist area—it's a **Special Economic Zone (SEZ)** spanning approximately **498 hectares**, with a core focus on **ecotourism, wellness, and creative industries**. It blends Bali's traditional culture with sustainable development principles, aiming to create a new-generation resort destination that balances international tourism with local character.

Beyond Sira Village, the area will continue to develop:

- Green sustainable architecture and low-carbon urban planning
- A marina and seaside leisure facilities
- International convention and creative industry spaces
- Occasional Island Bazaar markets, cultural events, and art exhibitions

Compared to the well-established Kuta, Seminyak, and Nusa Dua, Kura Kura Bali feels more like an emerging destination still taking shape—one with plenty of promise for the future.

***

## Driving New Tourism Development in Bali

Beyond offering a fresh shopping experience, officials estimate that Sira Village will create **over 1,000 job opportunities**, further boosting the surrounding tourism, retail, and dining sectors, and becoming a key facility in the Turtle Island Special Economic Zone.

With more international brands and tourism facilities gradually setting up, Kura Kura Bali is poised to become another emerging travel destination worth watching in Bali, alongside Ubud, Seminyak, and Nusa Dua.

***

## Is It Worth Including in Your Bali Itinerary?

If you've traveled to Japan, Korea, or the West, you're likely familiar with Premium Outlets.

In the past, shopping in Bali was mostly concentrated at malls like Beachwalk, Discovery Mall, and Seminyak Village. Now, with the arrival of Sira Village, Bali officially has its own large-scale Premium Outlet.

I believe **if you enjoy shopping for international brands or want to pick up souvenirs before heading home, Sira Village is well worth a visit.** Even if you have no shopping plans, you can explore the rapidly developing Kura Kura Bali Turtle Island Special Economic Zone, savoring delicious food and watching the sunset in a relaxed seaside setting, experiencing a new facet of Bali's future tourism growth.
