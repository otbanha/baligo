# Bali Latest News | November 24: Heavy Rain Causes 20 Incidents, Coastal High Tide Warning, and New Flight Route Resumes

Bali's rainy season has officially entered a period requiring heightened vigilance. Heavy rainfall from the evening of November 22 to the early hours of November 23 caused 20 disaster incidents across the island, with flooding on some roads in Kuta, Legian, and Denpasar, as well as landslides and a fatality.

Beyond the weather, there's also news on year-end travel. Wings Air will resume direct flights between Jember and Bali on November 27, and Badung will host a major creative economy festival this weekend.

## 🌦️ Bali Weather on November 24: Heavy Rain Possible in Many Areas, Southern Sea Waves Up to 2 Meters

The Indonesian Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) Bali Region III Meteorological Center has released the latest weather forecast for November 24, with light rain expected overall, but moderate to heavy rain and lightning still possible in some areas.

**Bali Weather Overview on November 24:**

| Item | Forecast |
| --- | --- |
| Weather | Light rain, possibly heavier locally |
| Temperature | 20–33°C |
| Relative Humidity | 70–95% |
| Wind Direction | Southeast to southwest winds |
| Wind Speed | 4–31 km/h |
| Northern Sea Wave Height | 0.5–1 meter |
| Southern Sea Wave Height | 0.5–2 meters |

BMKG reminds that most areas of Bali still face the risk of moderate to heavy rain and lightning, especially in mountainous regions, areas near rivers, and low-lying zones prone to waterlogging, so be alert for sudden rainfall.

**Additionally, there's an alert that travelers often overlook: coastal high tide flooding (Rob).**

BMKG Region III has issued a coastal high tide flood warning from November 23 to 25. November 24 coincides with a full moon and lunar perigee, which may raise the highest tide levels in some coastal areas.

Six potentially affected regencies and areas include:

| Area | Potentially Affected Coastal Regions |
| --- | --- |
| Badung | Kuta, South Kuta, North Kuta, Mengwi |
| Gianyar | Blahbatuh, Gianyar, Sukawati |
| Jembrana | Jembrana, Melaya, Mendoyo, Negara, Pekutatan |
| Klungkung | Banjarangkan, Dawan, Klungkung, Nusa Penida |
| Denpasar | South Denpasar, East Denpasar |
| Tabanan | Kediri, Kerambitan, Selemadeg, West Selemadeg, East Selemadeg, Tabanan |

This alert does not mean flooding will definitely occur in these areas, nor does it indicate a full beach closure. It serves as a reminder to monitor tidal changes in low-lying coastal areas, ports, and during beach activities.

**Tips for travelers:**

- If staying in coastal areas like Kuta, Seminyak, Sanur, or Nusa Dua, check for any standing water around your accommodation.
- When heading to the beach for photos or a stroll, pay attention to tide levels and wave conditions.
- For travelers going to Nusa Penida, besides confirming ferry schedules, also keep an eye on dock and coastal conditions.
- Avoid waterfall, mountain, and river valley activities during heavy rain.

## 🚨 Heavy Overnight Rain Causes 20 Disasters in Bali! Flooding on Roads in Kuta, Legian, and Denpasar

From the afternoon of November 22 to the early morning of November 23, heavy rainfall hit many areas of Bali. According to statistics from the Bali Provincial Disaster Management Agency (BPBD) as of 9:00 AM on November 23, there were a total of 20 disaster events across the island.

The rainfall was astonishing:

| --- | --- |
| Tampaksiring | 161 mm |
| Karangasem | 149 mm |
| Selemadeg Barat | 111 mm |

Among these, Tampaksiring reached extreme rainfall levels.

The 20 disaster events are distributed as follows:

- Denpasar: 7 locations
- Badung: 4 locations
- Tabanan: 4 locations
- Jembrana: 2 locations
- Karangasem: 2 locations
- Bangli: 1 location

The main damage includes flooded roads, fallen trees, landslides, and damaged buildings.

**The areas most directly affecting tourists are Kuta and Legian.**

On the morning of November 23, some roads in Kuta and Legian experienced flooding, with water levels reaching up to an adult's calf in certain sections. The Tukad Mati River also saw increased water flow, causing large amounts of plastic waste and wood to pile up under bridges.

Local cleaning crews and relevant authorities have been deployed for cleanup efforts. While some floodwaters have receded, certain areas still require pumping and debris removal.

Additionally, the heavy rain that night and early morning led to two serious incidents:

- In Mengwi, Badung Regency, a young girl fell into a drainage ditch and was swept away by the strong current. She was found on the afternoon of November 23 and tragically did not survive.
- In Sidemen, Karangasem Regency, a 65-year-old woman was buried by a landslide. Rescue teams recovered her body on the morning of November 23.

Both incidents were linked to heavy rainfall, highlighting the dangers near drainage ditches, rivers, and hillsides during the rainy season.

**Reminder for travelers:**

If you're heading from the airport to Kuta, Legian, or Seminyak tomorrow, check road conditions before departing and allow extra travel time.

When driving in Bali during the rainy season, the main thing to avoid isn't the rain itself, but underestimating the risks of standing water, rising rivers, and hillside landslides. If you encounter flooded roads, don't risk driving through them.

## ✈️ Jember-Bali Direct Flights Resume November 27! Wings Air Flies Twice Weekly

Direct flights between Jember in East Java and Bali are set to resume, offering a notable transportation option for travelers planning to combine East Java and Bali trips.

According to an announcement from the Jember Transportation Agency on November 23, Wings Air will restart its Jember-Bali route starting November 27, 2026.

Flight details are as follows:

| Item | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Airline | Wings Air |
| Route | Jember (JBB) – Denpasar (DPS) |
| Start Date | November 27, 2026 |
| Frequency | Every Monday and Friday |
| Aircraft | ATR 72-500 / ATR 72-600 |
| Flight Time | Approximately 1 hour 20 minutes |

Reference schedule:

- Bali to Jember: Departs 08:05 WITA
- Jember to Bali: Departs 09:00 WIB

The currently announced starting fares are:

- Jember → Bali: From IDR 640,000
- Bali → Jember: From IDR 761,000

Actual fares vary depending on departure date and seat availability.

This route was previously launched in December 2025 but suspended in May 2026 due to rising aviation fuel costs. It has now been reinstated for the Christmas and New Year holidays.

For travelers in Bali, this also opens up more possibilities for planning a trip to East Java. Around Jember, you can explore Papuma Beach, Watu Ulo Beach, and other nearby natural attractions.

If you're already traveling in Bali and want to hop over to East Java, this offers a new option without the need for a long car and ferry journey.

## 🏌️ Handara Golf & Resort Wins Asia's Best Eco-Friendly Golf Facility for Second Year! Bedugul Highland Resort Gains International Recognition Again

Handara Golf & Resort Bali, located in the Bedugul highlands of Bali, has once again received the Asia's Best Eco-Friendly Golf Facility award at the World Golf Awards 2026, marking its second consecutive win.

This award recognizes the resort's efforts in environmental protection and sustainable management, coinciding with Handara's 50th anniversary this year.

Handara states that its current eco-friendly initiatives include:

- Conserving and managing water resources
- Reducing waste and single-use plastics
- Promoting Micro Farm and Farm to Table practices
- Using local agricultural products and suppliers
- Strengthening collaboration with the local community

The resort also notes that about 90% of its employees and golf caddies come from surrounding areas, and it partners with 16 local suppliers.

Handara is located on the Bedugul plateau, where the climate is noticeably cooler compared to the coastal resorts in southern Bali.

For travelers who enjoy golf, mountain retreats, and natural surroundings, this news has once again put Handara in the spotlight of the international tourism market.

However, this is an industry award for facilities and sustainable operations, not a ranking of accommodation quality or a price guarantee. Actual bookings should still consider room types, amenities, and recent guest reviews.
