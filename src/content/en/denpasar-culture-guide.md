**Bottom line**: Denpasar was ranked **94th** among the world's top 100 cities in 2025. As Bali's cultural and historical heart, key attractions include the Bajra Sandhi Monument and the Bali Museum. Denpasar, the capital of Bali, has recently been [named one of the world's top 100 cities for 2025](https://www.forbes.com/sites/laurabegleybloom/2025/02/27/the-100-best-cities-in-the-world-to-visit-in-2025-according-to-a-new-report/), ranking 94th. This ancient city is not only Bali's cultural hub but also boasts a rich historical heritage and a vibrant contemporary art scene, making it an excellent alternative for travelers exploring Bali.

## The Unique Charm of Denpasar

Denpasar is more than just Bali's administrative center; it's a vibrant cultural melting pot. With traditional markets, ancient temples, modern art exhibitions, and live music events, it offers visitors an immersive experience of authentic Balinese life. Compared to other popular tourist spots in Bali, Denpasar sees fewer crowds, providing a more localized experience away from the tourist hustle, allowing you to feel the city's unique character.

## Global Top 100 City Ranking

According to [Euromonitor International's ranking](https://www.forbes.com/sites/laurabegleybloom/2025/02/27/the-100-best-cities-in-the-world-to-visit-in-2025-according-to-a-new-report/), Denpasar has entered the global top 100 cities list for the first time, excelling in indicators such as tourism performance, cultural appeal, and sustainability. The 2025 ranking, based on 2024 data, credits Denpasar's success not only to its rich cultural heritage but also to the local government's active promotion of tourism.

## Must-Visit Attractions in Denpasar

### **Badung Market**

![巴東市場（Badung Market）](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-03/1774614720760-badung_market.jpg)

Located in the city center, Badung Market is one of Bali's largest and oldest traditional markets. It's not only a daily shopping spot for locals but also a fantastic place for travelers to experience the local culture of Bali. The Pura Desa temple across the street is one of Bali's most important Hindu temples.

### **Kumbasari Art Market**

![Kumbasari藝術市場](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-03/1774614765807-kumbasari.jpg)

Just a street away from Badung Market, Kumbasari Market is the best place to buy traditional Balinese handicrafts and souvenirs. It's also where locals purchase religious ceremonial items, brimming with a rich cultural atmosphere.

### **Bajra Sandhi Monument**

Located in Puputan Renon Park in Denpasar, this monument commemorates the heroic struggles of the Balinese people during their fight for independence. Visitors can climb to the observation deck for panoramic views of the city.

### **Museum Bali**

The museum houses a vast collection of Balinese historical artifacts, traditional costumes, and ancient manuscripts, making it an excellent place to learn about Balinese culture and history.

![峇里博物館（Museum Bali）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f66647194cd9d3240f658a876a9ce7b7.jpg)

### **Pura Jagatnatha Temple**

This is the most important Hindu temple in Denpasar, dedicated to Sang Hyang Widhi Wasa, the supreme god of Balinese Hinduism. The temple's unique architecture is steeped in a spiritual atmosphere.

![Pura Jagatnatha寺廟](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_36179fe2bb08dd9d6f7150e83ae45bc8.jpg)

### **Taman Tukad Korea Riverside Park**

https://youtu.be/gEcE2e4TFaM

As part of Bali's river revitalization project, Taman Tukad Korea Riverside Park is an emerging attraction in Denpasar. Featuring lush green pathways, rest areas, children's playgrounds, and an open-air performance venue, it's a perfect spot for locals and travelers to unwind.

### **Pasar Kreneng Night Market**

https://youtu.be/FphhiUzB7gM

As Bali's largest night market, Pasar Kreneng is an excellent place to experience local nightlife. It gathers a wide variety of food stalls, offering everything from traditional Balinese dishes to specialties from across Indonesia. The lively atmosphere and diverse culinary choices allow visitors to deeply immerse themselves in Bali's nighttime charm.

### Taman Ayun Temple: A Peaceful Sanctuary in Bali

Taman Ayun Water Temple, about 18 kilometers from Denpasar, was built in 1634 and once served as the royal temple of the Mengwi Kingdom. Renowned for its unique architecture and serene surroundings, it was designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2012.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dksxqobud/image/upload/v1774091103/MixCollage-08-Mar-2025-11-02-AM-4254_wxvvry.jpg)

**Highlights**

1. **Moat and Gardens**
   The temple is surrounded by a wide moat, symbolizing sanctity and separation, paired with meticulously designed gardens that create a serene atmosphere.
2. **Multi-Tiered Shrines**
   The multi-tiered shrines (Meru) within the temple represent the sacred Mount Meru of Hindu mythology, making them a favorite for photography enthusiasts, especially striking against a blue sky.
3. **Traditional Ceremonies**
   Visitors have the chance to witness locals performing traditional ceremonies, offering deep insight into Bali's religious culture. 

Taman Ayun Temple blends natural beauty with cultural depth, making it a must-visit destination for exploring Bali's history and beliefs.

## Denpasar Food

### Kaneshiro Resto: A Japanese All-You-Can-Eat Paradise in Denpasar

Kaneshiro Resto is a renowned Japanese all-you-can-eat hotpot restaurant in Denpasar, beloved by locals and travelers for its high-quality Japanese cuisine and extensive menu. It offers fresh sushi, sashimi, tempura, grilled meats, and other classic Japanese dishes, with all ingredients carefully selected to ensure flavor and quality. The restaurant features a comfortable ambiance and attentive service, perfect for sharing a meal with family and friends. Whether you're a Japanese cuisine enthusiast or looking to try diverse culinary experiences, Kaneshiro Resto will satisfy your taste buds—a must-visit food destination in Denpasar!

### Ameno Coffee and Eatery

Located in Denpasar, Ameno Coffee and Eatery is a place I've visited twice for coffee. It boasts an excellent 4.8-star rating online and is a beloved local café and gathering space. The environment is comfortable and the atmosphere relaxing, highly regarded by coffee lovers and residents alike.

When visiting this shop, you absolutely can't miss their signature coffee. Many reviews highlight its unique blend with rich layers of flavor and intense aroma, perfectly balancing the coffee beans' boldness and smooth texture—it's a stunning sip that leaves a lasting impression, making it a soulful highlight that draws people on a pilgrimage.

### Warung Wardani (Yudistira)

Located in the heart of Denpasar, Warung Wardani - Yudistira is a renowned local spot famous for Indonesian Nasi Campur (mixed rice). The shop is clean and bright, with signature dishes bringing together a variety of classic Indonesian delights, including crispy fried shrimp crackers, flavorful beef chunks, satay, and shredded chicken, all paired with a mildly spicy special sauce—rich and delicious. Thanks to its crowd-pleasing flavors and excellent hygiene, it's not only a hit with tourists but also a popular choice for local businesspeople and family gatherings.

### Ayam Betutu Khas Gilimanuk

This restaurant is a landmark old-timer for tasting Bali's traditional spiced roast chicken (Ayam Betutu). The specialty involves coating a whole chicken with a blend of turmeric, galangal, garlic, lemongrass, and other spices, then slow-cooking it over low heat until the meat falls off the bone, tender and juicy. The rich spice essence fully permeates, and it's served with a special Sambal Matah (raw shallot chili) and stir-fried water spinach, creating a spicy, aromatic, and layered experience—the perfect spot to dive into authentic, bold Indonesian cuisine.

### Warung Babi Guling Pan Ana

Tucked away in a Denpasar alley, Babi Guling Pan Ana is a local hidden gem for crispy roast suckling pig (Babi Guling). The skin is roasted to an incredibly crunchy perfection, while the inner meat stays tender and juicy. A standard set meal includes roast pork, spicy shredded pork, blood sausage, and crispy pork skin, accompanied by unique traditional spices and chili sauce. The overall texture is layered and full of local character—a must-visit for meat lovers and traditional snack enthusiasts.

### Sate Plecing Arjuna

Famous for its unique satay, this spot stands out from the common peanut sauce version by featuring Indonesia's distinctive Plecing sauce—made with plenty of red chili, tomatoes, and shrimp paste, delivering a tangy and spicy kick that whets the appetite. The freshly grilled skewers carry a subtle charcoal aroma, and when dipped in the refreshing yet fiery special chili sauce, they're utterly addictive—a quintessential local street food in the city.

## Denpasar Travel Plan

Denpasar Tourism Board Chair Ni Luh Putu Riyastiti stated that in 2025, Denpasar aims to attract 2.1 million visitors (for reference, Bali receives over 6 million tourists annually). To achieve this goal, the local government will actively promote Denpasar's tourism resources through social media, online campaigns, and partnerships with travel industry players. Additionally, Denpasar plans to enhance the quality of tourist attractions and host diverse cultural events to further boost the city's appeal.

## Bali Arts Festival

![峇里島藝術節](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e2ab81ce50be7b72a8e74be0512b7aef.png)

Held annually from mid-June to mid-July, the Bali Arts Festival is one of Denpasar's most important cultural events. The festival is renowned for its grand opening parade, featuring thousands of dancers and performers showcasing Bali's traditional arts, music, and dance. The main venue, Taman Werdhi Budaya Art Center, is located in downtown Denpasar and offers an unmissable cultural experience for travelers.
