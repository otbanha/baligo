Update: 2026/05/20

**Bottom Line**: Denpasar was ranked **94th** among the world's top 100 cities in 2025, serving as Bali's cultural and historical heart. Key attractions include Bajra Sandhi Monument and Bali Museum, perfect for a half-day to full-day trip to experience the real urban life of Bali beyond the beach resorts.

Bali's capital, Denpasar, was recently [listed among the world's top 100 cities for 2025](https://www.forbes.com/sites/laurabegleybloom/2025/02/27/the-100-best-cities-in-the-world-to-visit-in-2025-according-to-a-new-report/), ranking 94th. This ancient city is not only Bali's cultural center but also boasts a rich historical heritage and a vibrant modern art scene, making it an excellent alternative for travelers exploring Bali.

## The Unique Charm of Denpasar

Denpasar is not just the administrative hub of Bali; it's a vibrant cultural melting pot. With traditional markets, ancient temples, modern art exhibitions, and music events, it offers travelers an immersive experience of authentic Balinese life. Compared to other popular tourist spots in Bali, Denpasar sees fewer visitors, providing a more localized experience away from the tourist crowds, allowing you to feel the city's unique charm.

## Global Top 100 City Selection

According to [Euromonitor International's ranking](https://www.forbes.com/sites/laurabegleybloom/2025/02/27/the-100-best-cities-in-the-world-to-visit-in-2025-according-to-a-new-report/), Denpasar entered the global top 100 cities for the first time, excelling in tourism performance, cultural appeal, and sustainable development. The 2025 ranking, based on 2024 data, attributes Denpasar's success not only to its rich cultural heritage but also to the local government's active promotion of tourism.

## Must-Visit Attractions in Denpasar

### **Badung Market**

![巴東市場（Badung Market）](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-03/1774614720760-badung_market.jpg)

Located in the city center, Badung Market is one of Bali's largest and oldest traditional markets. It's not only a daily shopping spot for locals but also a fantastic place for travelers to experience the vibrant local culture. Across from the market, Pura Desa Temple stands as one of Bali's most important Hindu temples.

### **Kumbasari Art Market**

![Kumbasari藝術市場](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-03/1774614765807-kumbasari.jpg)

Just a street away from Badung Market, Kumbasari Market is the best place to buy traditional Balinese handicrafts and souvenirs. It's also where locals purchase items for religious ceremonies, immersing visitors in a rich cultural atmosphere.

### **Bajra Sandhi Monument**

Located in Puputan Renon Park in Denpasar, this monument commemorates the heroic struggles of the Balinese people during their fight for independence. Visitors can climb to the observation deck for panoramic views of the city.

### **Museum Bali**

The museum houses a vast collection of Balinese historical artifacts, traditional costumes, and ancient manuscripts, making it an excellent place to learn about Bali's culture and history.

![峇里博物館（Museum Bali）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f66647194cd9d3240f658a876a9ce7b7.jpg)

### **Pura Jagatnatha Temple**

This is Denpasar's most important Hindu temple, dedicated to Sang Hyang Widhi Wasa, the supreme god of Balinese Hinduism. The temple's unique architecture is steeped in religious ambiance.

![Pura Jagatnatha寺廟](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_36179fe2bb08dd9d6f7150e83ae45bc8.jpg)

### **Taman Tukad Korea Riverside Park**

https://youtu.be/gEcE2e4TFaM

As part of Bali's river revitalization project, Taman Tukad Korea Riverside Park is an emerging attraction in Denpasar. It features lush walking paths, rest areas, children's playgrounds, and an open-air performance venue, making it a perfect spot for locals and travelers to unwind.

### **Pasar Kreneng Night Market**

https://youtu.be/FphhiUzB7gM

As the largest night market in Bali, Pasar Kreneng is an excellent place to experience local nightlife. It gathers a wide variety of food stalls, offering everything from traditional Balinese dishes to Indonesian specialties from across the archipelago. The lively atmosphere and diverse culinary options allow visitors to deeply immerse themselves in Bali's nighttime charm.

### Taman Ayun Temple: A Peaceful Sanctuary in Bali

Taman Ayun Water Temple, located about 18 kilometers from Denpasar, was built in 1634 and once served as the royal temple of the Mengwi Kingdom. Renowned for its unique architecture and serene surroundings, it was designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2012.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dksxqobud/image/upload/v1774091103/MixCollage-08-Mar-2025-11-02-AM-4254_wxvvry.jpg)

**Key Highlights**

1. **Moat and Gardens**
   The temple is surrounded by a wide moat, symbolizing sanctity and seclusion, complemented by meticulously designed gardens that create a serene atmosphere.
2. **Multi-Tiered Shrines**
   The multi-tiered shrines (Meru) within the temple represent the sacred Mount Meru of Hinduism, making them a favorite for photography enthusiasts, especially against a clear blue sky.
3. **Traditional Ceremonies**
   Visitors have the chance to witness locals performing traditional ceremonies, offering deep insights into Bali's religious culture.

Taman Ayun Temple blends natural beauty with cultural heritage, making it a must-visit destination for exploring Bali's history and beliefs.

![Living World Denpasar](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-03/1774615058614-living_world_denpasar.jpg)

Living World Denpasar is currently the largest and most magnificent shopping mall in Bali, combining fashion, entertainment, and dining to become a popular destination for locals and travelers alike. The mall's design is spacious and bright, incorporating traditional Balinese elements for a comfortable shopping experience. Whether you're shopping, enjoying a meal, or spending leisure time with family and friends, Living World Denpasar is a must-visit modern attraction in Denpasar!

## Denpasar Food

### Kaneshiro Resto: A Japanese All-You-Can-Eat Paradise in Denpasar

Kaneshiro Resto is a renowned Japanese all-you-can-eat hot pot restaurant in Denpasar, beloved by locals and travelers for its high-quality Japanese cuisine and extensive menu. It offers fresh sushi, sashimi, tempura, grilled meats, and other classic Japanese dishes, with all ingredients carefully selected to ensure flavor and quality. The restaurant boasts a comfortable environment and attentive service, perfect for sharing a meal with family and friends. Whether you're a Japanese food enthusiast or looking to try diverse culinary experiences, Kaneshiro Resto will satisfy your taste buds—a must-visit food destination in Denpasar!

### Ameno Coffee and Eatery

Located in Denpasar, Ameno Coffee and Eatery is a place I've visited twice for coffee. With an impressive 4.8-star rating online, it's a beloved local café and gathering space. The environment is comfortable and the atmosphere relaxed, making it a favorite among coffee lovers and locals alike.

When visiting this shop, you absolutely cannot miss their signature coffee. Many reviews highlight its unique blend with rich layers of flavor and aromatic intensity, perfectly balancing the coffee beans' full-bodied and smooth texture. It's a stunning experience from the first sip and a soulful highlight that draws many to make a special pilgrimage here!

### Warung Wardani (Yudistira)

Located in the heart of Denpasar, Warung Wardani - Yudistira is a renowned local spot for traditional Indonesian Nasi Campur. The shop is clean and bright, and its signature dish brings together a variety of classic Indonesian delights, including crispy fried shrimp crackers, flavorful beef chunks, satay, and shredded chicken, all paired with a special mildly spicy sauce that is both rich and delicious. With its universally appealing flavors and excellent hygiene standards, it is not only a favorite among tourists but also a popular choice for local businesspeople and family gatherings.

### Ayam Betutu Khas Gilimanuk

This restaurant is a landmark old establishment for tasting Bali's traditional spiced roast chicken (Ayam Betutu). It specializes in coating a whole chicken with turmeric, galangal, garlic, lemongrass, and various other spices, then slow-cooking it over a long period until the meat is fall-off-the-bone tender and juicy. The rich spice essence fully penetrates the meat, and when paired with the special Sambal Matah (raw shallot chili) and stir-fried water spinach, it creates a spicy and layered flavor profile—the perfect place to experience authentic, bold Indonesian cuisine.

### Warung Babi Guling Pan Ana

Tucked away in the alleys of downtown Denpasar, Babi Guling Pan Ana is a local's secret spot for crispy roast suckling pig (Babi Guling). The skin here is roasted to an incredibly crispy perfection, while the inner meat remains tender and juicy. A standard set meal includes roast pork, spicy shredded pork, blood sausage, and crispy pork skin, all accompanied by uniquely flavored traditional spices and chili sauce. The overall texture is distinct and full of local character, making it a must-visit for meat lovers and traditional snack enthusiasts.

### Sate Plecing Arjuna

Famous for its unique flavored satay. Unlike the common peanut sauce satay, this place specializes in the distinctive Plecing sauce—made with plenty of red chili, tomatoes, and shrimp paste, offering a tangy and spicy appetizing taste. The freshly grilled skewers carry a light charcoal aroma, and when dipped in the refreshing yet palate-tingling special chili sauce, they keep you coming back for more. It's a highly representative local street food gem.

## Denpasar Travel Plan

Denpasar Tourism Director Ni Luh Putu Riyastiti stated that Denpasar aims to attract 2.1 million tourists in 2025 (for reference, Bali sees over 6 million visitors annually). To achieve this goal, the local government will actively promote Denpasar's tourism resources through social media, online campaigns, and partnerships with travel industry players. Additionally, Denpasar plans to enhance the quality of tourist attractions and host diverse cultural events to further boost the city's appeal.

## Bali Arts Festival

![峇里島藝術節](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e2ab81ce50be7b72a8e74be0512b7aef.png)

Held annually from mid-June to mid-July, the Bali Arts Festival is one of Denpasar's most important cultural events. It is renowned for its grand opening parade, featuring thousands of dancers and performers showcasing Bali's traditional arts, music, and dance. The main venue, Taman Werdhi Budaya Art Center, is located in downtown Denpasar and offers an unmissable cultural experience for travelers.
