> The following content is adapted from a travelogue shared by group member [Sannie Lin](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/KZWh2fnM6upSM3uq/):

**Key Takeaways**: For a six-day, five-night trip to Bali with kids, the on-site queue for a visa on arrival took about **20 minutes**—we recommend applying for an e-visa in advance to save time. When exchanging currency, don't just exchange USD$200—while card payments are convenient, **private driver fees often require cash**, so it's best to exchange more from the start (in our case, we needed an additional USD$150 to cover expenses).

Our six-day, five-night Bali trip has come to a perfect end! We're incredibly grateful to the helpful members of our community and the wealth of information they provided, which made this family trip with two kids relaxed, enjoyable, and smooth from start to finish!

Here's a rundown of our preparation and customs process:

✔️ **E-Visa:** Upon arrival, we noticed the queue for visa on arrival took about 20 minutes. Luckily, the new e-visa website shared by the group saved us a lot of time! Using the e-visa was much more convenient, allowing us to start our journey sooner.

Further reading: [Your Guide to Getting Off the Plane, Through Customs, and Out of the Airport](/en/blog/bali-all-indonesia-arrival-guide/) (2025 Latest Edition)

✔️ **USD Exchange:** We initially exchanged USD$200, planning to use cards wherever possible. However, by the end, we ran out of cash to pay our driver for the private car service, so we exchanged another USD$150 at the hotel. This experience taught us that while card payments are convenient, it's important to keep an eye on cash needs, especially when using private driver services, to ensure you don't run short and disrupt your plans!

Here's our detailed itinerary, arranged as follows:

## **Day 1**

After arriving in Bali, we headed straight to our stay at **Bidadari Villa**. Along the way, our driver Agus kindly took us to buy a SIM card and exchange currency. We chose the cheapest 12GB SIM card, which cost about USD$16. The internet speed in Bali was quite stable, and the data was sufficient for our entire trip—whether for navigation or sharing travel moments online, it worked smoothly.

## **Day 2**

![我們在Nukuwera玩River Tubing](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_6568e81ae3a73a6d63bd5d98eebe2e75.jpg)

Today was a full-day private tour in Ubud. Our first stop was **Nukuwera & Pandawa River Tubing**, recommended by [Xiao Jie](https://bit.ly/youtube-jason). Lunch was included, and the whole tubing experience was perfect for families. Even with kids, we had a blast—the little ones absolutely loved their time in the water.

![野生的猴子很容易就暴走，最好保持距離](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_9b47f1081db7082131841f0d5a6803c9.jpg)

Next, we visited **Ubud Palace** to soak in the local cultural vibe. Afterward, we made a special stop at **KUO CUSINE** to pick up some local specialty jams. They had a great variety, and grabbing a few bottles as souvenirs was a no-brainer.

Dinner was pretty spontaneous. Since we had the kids with us, we kept the schedule loose. We found **Warung Pulau Kelapa** on Google at the last minute, and luckily the food was good. The prices were a bit steep, but the atmosphere was lovely and very family-friendly. For us traveling with kids, a comfortable dining setting and convenient location matter more than the cost—it made for a relaxed and enjoyable dinner.

## **Day 3**

Today was our moving day, heading to **Bali Safari Park**. Along the way, we asked our driver Benny to take us to try the burgers at **Ayoo-Ayoo**. This place has a five-star rating on Google, so we went there out of curiosity, and it really didn't disappoint! The burgers tasted fantastic—the meat was tender and juicy, with rich toppings, making it a very satisfying culinary experience for our whole family.

![Ayoo Ayoo](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_1b22614e00a6c81c0cbe97cccd84c7c3.jpg)

Ayoo Ayoo

> Editor's note: I've tried at least 50 different burger joints in Bali, and almost none have been a letdown—every one is delicious!

Next, we stopped by a nearby supermarket to stock up on some essentials before arriving at the park and checking in. That evening, we chose to dine at the **Tsavo Lion Restaurant** within the park. The biggest highlight of this restaurant is that you can enjoy your meal while watching lions through a glass window! The kids were thrilled, and the whole dining experience was both fun and unique.

![Bali Safari Zoo](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_9d3eacc77a4c925c1e1acc7690259fa5.jpg)

After dinner, there was a photo opportunity with the animals outside the restaurant. We opted for this service, and the photographer took many pictures—shots of mom and the older brother, dad and the younger sister, and a family portrait of all four of us. The best part was that the entire set included all digital files, plus one A4-sized print with a beautiful album, all for about USD$23. The photo quality was excellent, and everyone in the family looked great. We felt it was an incredibly worthwhile keepsake of our memories!

Although today's itinerary was mostly about moving from one place to another, it was filled with surprises and heartwarming family moments.

## **Day 4**

Today we moved from **Bali Safari** to **Westin Nusa Dua**. Along the way, we asked our driver Agus to help us find local snacks, including **Siobak (roast pork)** and **Martabak (stuffed pancake)**. Agus not only found suitable small shops but also carefully communicated with the shop owners to prevent us from being overcharged as tourists. This thoughtful service made us feel truly cared for.

> Read more: [Top 10 Foods in Bali](/en/blog/bali-top-10-foods/)

Next, we asked the driver about buying souvenirs and visited **Krisna** and **The Balinese**. When Agus recommended Krisna, he specifically reminded us that if we needed other places, we could let him know anytime, allowing us to decide our own itinerary. This reminded me of a travel experience over a decade ago, when I clearly stated I didn't want to shop but was still forced by a hired driver to visit several shopping factories—I was really furious back then. This time, I felt completely satisfied and relaxed because Agus fully respected our wishes.

For dinner, we enjoyed burgers and Indian cuisine at the resort's **Velada Restaurant**. Overall, today's itinerary made us appreciate the warmth and attentiveness of the locals, making our trip even more enjoyable and memorable.

## **Day 6**

On our final day, we headed to the airport, once again with Agus driving us. We're truly grateful to him and Benny for their patience and understanding with our kids' antics in the car, which made our trip all the smoother.

For our final meal, we stopped at **Made's Warung** at the airport. Of course, we had to order the nostalgic **Nasi Goreng** and **Mie Goreng** one last time. These classic Indonesian fried rice and noodles never get old, and as we savored them, we bid farewell to Bali 👋, marking a perfect end to our journey.

Throughout the trip, we not only enjoyed stunning scenery and delicious food but also created countless beautiful memories with our kids—treasured experiences that will stay with us forever.
