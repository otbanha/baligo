> The following content is adapted from a travelogue shared by group member [Sannie Lin](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/KZWh2fnM6upSM3uq/):

**Bottom Line First**: For a six-day, five-night trip to Bali with kids, the on-site visa-on-arrival queue took about **20 minutes**—we recommend getting an e-visa in advance to save time. As for currency exchange, don't just exchange USD$200—while card payments are convenient, **private driver fees often require cash**, so it's best to exchange more from the start (in this case, an additional USD$150 was needed to cover expenses).

Our six-day, five-night Bali trip has come to a wonderful end! We're incredibly grateful to the helpful members of the online community and the wealth of information they provided, which made this family trip with two kids relaxed, enjoyable, and smooth sailing!

![帶著孩子看世界：六天五夜峇里島親子旅行的旅程安排與小貼士](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_46eaba429c96b06a7d14c92bea5677e0.jpg)

Let's start with our preparation and customs process:

✔️ **E-Visa**: Upon arrival, we noticed the visa-on-arrival queue took about 20 minutes. Luckily, the new e-visa website shared by the community saved us a lot of time! Using the e-visa was much more convenient, allowing us to start our journey sooner.

Further reading: [Everything You Need to Know About Getting Off the Plane, Through Customs, and Out of the Airport](/en/blog/bali-all-indonesia-arrival-guide/) (2025 Latest Version)

✔️ **USD Exchange**: Initially, we exchanged USD$200, planning to use cards wherever possible. But by the end, we found we didn't have enough cash to pay our driver for the private car service, so we exchanged another USD$150 at the hotel. This experience taught us that while card payments are convenient, it's important to keep an eye on cash needs—especially when using private driver services—to ensure you don't run short and disrupt your plans!

> Further reading: [How Much Cash Should You Bring for a Bali Trip?](/en/blog/bali-travel-cash-budget-guide/)

Here is our detailed itinerary, arranged as follows:

## **Day 1**

After arriving in Bali, we headed straight to our stay at **Bidadari Villa**. Along the way, our driver Agus kindly took us to buy a SIM card and exchange currency. We opted for the cheapest 12GB SIM card, which cost around USD$16. The internet speed in Bali was quite stable, and the data was sufficient for our entire trip—whether for navigation or sharing travel moments online, it worked smoothly.

## **Day 2**

![我們在Nukuwera玩River Tubing](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_6568e81ae3a73a6d63bd5d98eebe2e75.jpg)

We went river tubing at Nukuwera

Today was a full-day private tour in Ubud. Our first stop was **Nukuwera & Pandawa River Tubing**, recommended by [Xiao Jie](https://bit.ly/youtube-jason). Lunch was included, and the whole tubing experience was perfect for families. Even with kids, we had a blast—the little ones absolutely loved their time in the water.

After that, we headed to the famous **Sacred Monkey Forest**

![野生的猴子很容易就暴走，最好保持距離](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_9b47f1081db7082131841f0d5a6803c9.jpg)

Next, we visited **Ubud Palace** to soak in the local cultural vibe. Afterward, we made a special stop at **KUO CUSINE** to pick up some local artisan jams. They had a great variety, and grabbing a few jars as souvenirs was a no-brainer.

Dinner was a spontaneous affair. Since we were traveling with kids, we kept the schedule pretty loose. We found **Warung Pulau Kelapa** on Google at the last minute, and luckily the food was good. The prices were a bit steep, but the ambiance was lovely and very family-friendly. For us traveling with children, a comfortable dining setting and convenient location matter more than the cost—it made for a relaxed and enjoyable dinner.

## **Day 3**

Today was our moving day, heading to **Bali Safari Park**. Along the way, we asked our driver Benny to take us to try the burgers at **Ayoo-Ayoo**. This place has a five-star rating on Google, so we went there out of curiosity, and it did not disappoint! The burgers were absolutely delicious—tender, juicy meat with rich toppings. It was a very satisfying food experience for the whole family.

![Ayoo Ayoo](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_1b22614e00a6c81c0cbe97cccd84c7c3.jpg)

Ayoo Ayoo

> Editor's note: The editor has tried at least 50 different burgers in Bali and has never had a bad one—every single one was delicious!

Next, we stopped by a nearby supermarket to stock up on essentials before arriving at the park and checking in. That evening, we had dinner at the park's **Tsavo Lion Restaurant**, which is famous for letting you dine while watching lions through a glass window! The kids were thrilled, and the whole meal was both fun and unique.

After the meal, the restaurant offered a photo session with the animals. We opted for it, and the photographer took many pictures—shots of mom and brother, dad and sister, and the whole family of four. The photos turned out great, and everyone looked wonderful—we felt it was an incredible value for capturing memories!

## **Day 4**

Today, we moved from **Bali Safari** to **Westin Nusa Dua**. Along the way, we asked our driver Agus to help find local snacks, including **Siobak roasted pork** and **Martabak egg pancake**. Agus not only found suitable small shops but also carefully communicated with the owners to ensure we weren't overcharged as tourists. This thoughtful service made us feel truly cared for.

> Further reading: [Top 10 Foods in Bali](/en/blog/bali-top-10-foods/)

Next, we asked the driver about buying souvenirs, and we went to **Krisna** and **The Balinese** to shop. When Agus suggested Krisna, he specifically reminded us that if we needed other places, we could let him know anytime, allowing us to decide our own itinerary. This reminded me of a travel experience over a decade ago, when I clearly stated I didn't want to shop but was still forced by the driver to visit several shopping factories—I was really furious back then. This time, I felt completely satisfied and relaxed because Agus fully respected our wishes.

For dinner, we dined at **Velada Restaurant** within the resort, enjoying burgers and Indian cuisine. The restaurant had a very cozy atmosphere, and the food was delicious—the burgers were flavorful, and the Indian dishes were a delightful surprise, with spices perfectly balanced, rounding off our fourth day of the trip beautifully.

Overall, today's itinerary let us experience the warmth and attentiveness of the locals, making our trip even more enjoyable and memorable.

## **Day 6**

On our final day, we headed to the airport, once again relying on Agus to drive us. We’re deeply grateful for his and Benny’s patience and tolerance with our kids’ noise in the car—it made our trip so much smoother.

For lunch, we stopped at **Made's Warung** in the airport. For our farewell meal, we had to order the nostalgic **Nasi Goreng** and **Mie Goreng**. These classic Indonesian fried rice and noodles never get old, and as we savored them, we waved goodbye to Bali 👋, bringing our journey to a perfect close.

Throughout the trip, we not only enjoyed stunning scenery and delicious food but also created countless wonderful memories with our kids—treasured experiences that will stay with us forever.
