## **Is Bali a Good Place for a Honeymoon?**

Yes! Bali is one of the best honeymoon destinations, offering the following advantages:

1. **Romantic Atmosphere**: Bali is considered a romantic getaway, boasting stunning beaches, sunrise and sunset views, as well as luxurious resorts and private villas, allowing couples to create unforgettable memories in a romantic setting.
2. **Diverse Activities**: Bali offers a wide range of activities, including water sports like snorkeling and diving, cultural experiences such as visiting temples and watching Balinese dance performances, and indulging in delicious cuisine and spa treatments.
3. **Beautiful Natural Environment**: Bali features breathtaking natural landscapes, including lush green rice terraces, majestic volcanoes and waterfalls, and unique flora and fauna.
4. **Luxurious Accommodation Options**: Bali has a variety of luxury accommodations, from private villas to resorts and boutique hotels, allowing couples to find the perfect stay based on their preferences and budget.
5. **Warm Climate**: Bali enjoys a warm tropical climate, suitable for vacations year-round, enabling couples to visit anytime to enjoy the sun and beaches.

## How to Get to Bali?

You can choose to fly directly to Bali. Bali's capital is Denpasar (airport code: DPS). Currently, Taiwan's China Airlines and EVA Air offer direct flights from Taiwan to Bali. Additionally, other Asian cities such as Singapore, Hong Kong, Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, and Ho Chi Minh City also have direct flights available. Be sure to check flight schedules and prices for each option and choose the one that best fits your itinerary.

## How Long Should You Stay in Bali?

For a Bali honeymoon, it's recommended to stay at least 10 nights to explore different areas, surrounding islands, and have time for both adventure and relaxation.

**What You Can Do in 10 Nights:**

- Explore hidden beaches in southern Bali
- Head to Ubud for art and nature experiences
- Hike Mount Batur to catch the sunrise
- Visit Nusa Penida or other nearby islands
- Enjoy spa treatments and other relaxing activities

You can adjust your itinerary based on your interests and budget. Here are some suggestions:

- If you love beaches, consider relaxing on some of the hidden beaches in the south.
- If you're into culture, spend more time exploring Bali's temples and villages.
- If you enjoy natural beauty, we recommend spending extra time in Ubud.
- If you're seeking adventure, try outdoor activities like hiking, snorkeling, or surfing.

## Should I Bring Cash or Tip?

In Bali, credit and debit cards are widely accepted, but it's wise to carry some cash for small vendors, island trips, or tipping. Here are some tips:

- The only official currency in Bali is the Indonesian Rupiah. It's recommended to bring some US dollars to exchange locally.
- Tipping isn't mandatory, but for good service, a tip of 20,000 to 50,000 IDR is appreciated.
- At restaurants, the bill includes an 11% tax and a 5% to 10% service charge.
