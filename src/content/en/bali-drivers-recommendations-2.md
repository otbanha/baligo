Update: August 10, 2026

**What This Page Is**: The **second page** of recommended Bali private car drivers, focusing on those who can communicate in Chinese. It features **19 drivers and 258 ride reviews posted by group members in a Facebook group**. The numbers under each driver are direct links to the original posts, which you can click to verify.

Typical rates are around **Rp 600,000–800,000 / 10 hours / 5-seater car** (per vehicle, not per person, for areas south of Ubud). Starting July 1, 2026, there is an additional fuel surcharge (Rp 50,000 for half-day, Rp 100,000 for full-day). Details on cross-zone surcharges, overtime fees, and booking procedures are all organized on [Page 1](/en/blog/bali-private-car-drivers-guide/).

> When contacting a driver, be clear about: **dates, number of days, number of people and luggage, pickup and drop-off locations, and places you want to visit**. Also, ask for the total amount including any cross-zone surcharges. ⚠️ On Line, you cannot add friends across regions using an ID; you need to **scan the QR Code**.
