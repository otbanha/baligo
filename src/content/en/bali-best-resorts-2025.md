Bali has once again been crowned the best travel destination in Asia! According to the annual travel awards from *[DestinAsian](https://destinasian.com/update/2025-destinasian-readers-choice-awards-full-rankings)* magazine, Bali was voted by readers as the **Best Island in Asia** for 2025, beating out renowned destinations like the Maldives, Thailand's Phuket, Vietnam's Phu Quoc, and Malaysia's Langkawi, showcasing its enduring and unmatched allure.

## **Bali Retains Title of Best Island in Asia for 18 Consecutive Years**

*DestinAsian* magazine, known for its extensive English-speaking readership, selects the year's best travel destinations through votes from seasoned travelers. The magazine notes: "Our readers have once again chosen Bali as the most beloved island destination in the Asia-Pacific region, marking the **18th consecutive year** Bali has earned this honor, solidifying its position as Indonesia's hottest travel hotspot."

![峇里島蟬聯18年亞洲最佳島嶼](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2e5f947f6aa60b97f7aa679736ea5334.png)

This paradise, often called the "Island of the Gods," is renowned for its pristine beaches, lush green rice terraces, famous surf spots, and deep cultural heritage. The magazine adds: "Bali's unique culture and artistic spirit inspire travelers from around the globe, and with its picturesque coastline and numerous luxury resorts set amid idyllic landscapes, the island remains irresistibly appealing to modern visitors."

## **Bali's Airport and Top Resorts Also Earn Accolades**

Beyond the island itself, Bali's infrastructure and luxury accommodations also shone in this year's *DestinAsian* awards.

Bali's **I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport** ranked fourth in the 2025 "Best Airport" category, trailing only Singapore Changi Airport, Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport, and Hong Kong International Airport.

## **Bali Continues to Be the World's Most Popular Travel Destination**

Bali is not only highly acclaimed across Asia but also shines brightly on the global tourism stage. According to **TripAdvisor's 2025 Traveler's Choice Awards**, Bali was named **the world's second most popular travel destination**, trailing only London and surpassing Dubai in third place. Other top ten destinations include internationally renowned cities like Paris, Rome, Bangkok, and Marrakech.

### **FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions**

## **1. Why has Bali been voted Asia's Best Island for 18 consecutive years?**

Bali captivates travelers with its enchanting natural landscapes, profound cultural heritage, world-class resorts, and a wealth of activities, making it an ideal destination.

## **2. What emerging attractions in Bali are worth exploring in 2025?**

Amed in East Bali, Lovina and Pemuteran in North Bali, along with Medewi and West Bali National Park in West Bali, are set to become popular new hotspots next year.

## **3. What are some of Bali's globally renowned luxury resorts?**

These include world-class resorts like The Ritz-Carlton Bali, Sofitel Nusa Dua, St. Regis Bali, Apurva Kempinski, and Mandapa.

## **4. How does Bali's international airport rank among global airports?**

I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport in Bali was named the fourth best airport in the world for 2025.

## **5. What is the best season to visit Bali?**

Bali is a year-round destination, but the dry season (April to October) offers sunny weather, making it the ideal time for outdoor activities and beach vacations.
