## Why You Must Visit Finns Beach Club

**1. A 170-Meter Beachfront**

Finns Beach Club offers a 170-meter stretch of oceanfront, giving you unobstructed views of the Indian Ocean. It's one of the best places in Bali to watch the famous sunset.
  **2. Breathtaking Sunset Views**

As you relax by the sea, you'll witness stunning sunsets that often go viral on social media. These views are perfect for photos and make for an ideal romantic dinner setting.
  **3. Diverse Pools**

Finns Beach Club boasts four stunning pools, the most popular being a 30-meter infinity pool. This pool blends seamlessly with the ocean, offering breathtaking panoramic views that are perfect for Instagram-worthy photos. Beyond the infinity pool, Finns also features other pools ideal for families and group gatherings, providing diverse options to suit different guests' needs.
  **4. Nine Bars and Five Restaurants**

At Finns Beach Club, the dining and drinking options are abundant. You can sample a wide variety of cocktails across nine bars or savor delicious dishes at five restaurants, including a sushi bar and a Mediterranean restaurant. Whether you love fresh sushi or traditional wood-fired pizza, there's something for everyone. Don't miss their signature cocktails, which add an extra layer of fun to your vacation experience.
  **5. Indulging in Luxury Day Beds**

At Finns Beach Club, booking a day bed is the ultimate way to experience the venue. From single beds to party beds, a range of luxurious options lets you choose the perfect spot for your needs. Each day bed comes with dedicated service, allowing you to enjoy dining and other conveniences without leaving your seat.

## FINNS Beach Club FAQ

### Q: What are the opening hours of FINNS Beach Club?

FINNS Beach Club and FINNS VIP Beach Club open daily at 10 AM, year-round except for Nyepi Day (the Hindu Day of Silence). On Nyepi, nearly all commercial activity on the island comes to a halt, including FINNS. (Read more: [Understanding Nyepi Day](/en/blog/2025-bali-nyepi-day-guide/))

### Q: What time does FINNS Beach Club close?

FINNS Beach Club and FINNS VIP Beach Club close at 12 AM (midnight) every night.

### Q: Is there an entry fee for FINNS Beach Club? Is it free to enter?

There is no entry fee for FINNS Beach Club. Entering the beach club and using most facilities is free; guests only pay for drinks, food, smoking products, and any services they use.

FINNS VIP Beach Club also has no entry fee.

Special events (such as New Year's Eve parties or anniversary celebrations) require tickets, and ticket costs cannot be used to offset guests' food and beverage purchases.

### Q: Does FINNS Beach Club have a minimum spend?

Unlike most beach clubs in Bali, FINNS Beach Club has no minimum spend. Guests only pay for the services they use and the food and drinks they consume.

However, if guests wish to use a Day Bed, there is a minimum spend requirement. This minimum spend can be used on food and beverages at any FINNS bar, restaurant, and kitchen.

### Q: What payment methods does FINNS accept?

FINNS accepts almost all credit cards (except WISE cards) and cash payments. There are also ATMs on-site for guests to withdraw cash.

### Q: What is the entrance fee for two people?

FINNS Beach Club offers free entry. If guests want to book a bed, a minimum spend is required. For two people, it's recommended to book 2 single beds, which can be in the Beach Club or VIP area.

### Q: Do I need to book a bed to enter FINNS Beach Club?

No. Guests can enter FINNS anytime, as long as there is space available. Guests can use the seating in the restaurant and bar areas for free.

However, if guests want the best beach club experience, it's highly recommended to book a Day Bed online in advance (with a minimum spend requirement). This ensures guests can sit in their preferred spot with a guaranteed seat.

### Q: Will the Day Bed booking fee be fully deducted from my food and beverage spend?

Yes! Once guests pay the booking fee, the entire amount will be credited toward their spending at FINNS Beach Club or FINNS VIP Beach Club.

However, these credits must be used on the day of the guest's booking—they cannot be carried over to future visits and must be used all at once, with no refunds for any unused portion of the booking fee.

It's worth noting that there is an exception to this rule: if a guest purchases a ticket for a special event, such as a New Year's Eve party, the entry fee paid will not be deducted from the minimum spend.

### Q: What's the difference between a VIP bed and a regular bed?

VIP Beach Club offers exclusive perks and services, including: a special menu selection, complimentary drinking water, free daily snacks at 3 PM, free lockers, cold towels, sunscreen and face mist, sunglasses cleaning service, power bank rental, premium high-speed Wi-Fi, surf services, and valet parking. All bed bookings come with complimentary towels.

### Q: Can seven of us book a luxury bed together? Is there an extra charge?

A luxury bed can accommodate up to 6 people.

For groups of 7–10, we recommend booking a super luxury bed or two luxury beds side by side.

Advance booking is strongly recommended to ensure you can sit together.

### Q: What does 'arrive by 1 PM' mean? Does it mean guests can arrive anytime but must leave by 1 PM, or that they need to arrive before 1 PM and can stay as long as they like?

If a guest books an 'arrive by 1 PM' slot, they must arrive and be seated at their reserved bed before 1 PM. If they don't, FINNS reserves the right to release the booking and transfer it to other guests.

If this happens, guests will be placed on a waitlist upon arrival until a bed becomes available. During this time, guests can open a tab and start using the minimum spend.

If you're unsure whether you can arrive before 1 PM, it's recommended to book the 'Arrive Anytime' option for full flexibility. This means your bed will be held until you arrive. Either way, you're welcome to stay as long as you like.

### Q: We're running late and can't make it before 1 PM. Can you hold a bed for us?

As mentioned, FINNS releases beds at 1 PM. When guests arrive, they'll be placed on a waitlist and can use their minimum spend at the on-site bar or restaurant until a new bed is assigned.

### Q: What's the difference between 'Basic' and 'Walk In'?

Basic refers to an online booking, while Walk In means guests show up without a reservation, and bed availability depends on open spots at the time of arrival.

Since FINNS can get busy, if there are no open spots when guests arrive, they'll be placed on a waitlist and can wait in the bar or restaurant area.

It's highly recommended that guests make a reservation before coming to FINNS to guarantee a bed upon arrival and enjoy booking perks like a discounted minimum spend.

### Q: Are there special arrangements for honeymoons or birthday celebrations?

FINNS offers a special cake service that must be pre-ordered at least 4 days before your visit.
