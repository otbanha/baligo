The one-way walk takes 20-30 minutes (allow 2-2.5 hours including breaks), and it's free with no admission fee. The best time to visit is **around sunrise at 6-7 AM** to avoid the heat and unshaded exposure.

Whenever friends ask me to recommend a place near central Ubud where they can soak in the natural beauty, should I suggest the Tegalalang Rice Terraces? No—that spot has become way too commercialized in recent years! Instead, let's recommend the Campuhan Ridge Walk, a place easily reached on foot and known as the 'Ubud Skywalk'.

First off, the name 'Campuhan' has many incorrect transliterations online. When pronounced in Indonesian, it's closer to 'Cham-poo-han'. A 'Ridge Walk' is a specific type of hike or walk, usually along a ridge or its crest, allowing participants to enjoy sweeping views on both sides.

Today, we're introducing the Campuhan Ridge Walk, a hidden gem near central Ubud that offers the tranquility and beauty of nature. This trail runs along a ridge, providing stunning 360-degree panoramic views from above, far from the city's hustle—unlike the Tegalalang Rice Terraces, which have lost their natural charm to commercialization.

## How to Get to the Ubud Skywalk?

A place this secluded should, by all logic, be far from any bustling commercial area—but surprisingly, the Campuhan Ridge Walk is only 640 meters (about a 10-minute walk) from the [Ubud Water Palace Starbucks](https://youtu.be/hYkf6sjCtqg). Along the way to the trail entrance, you'll pass museums, a shopping area with many small shops, and even a magical stretch that feels like stepping through time:

### How long does the Ubud Skywalk take?

The Campuhan Ridge Walk, from the entrance shown on the map above to the end of the ridge, takes about 20 to 30 minutes one way if you walk without stopping. You can continue for another 5 to 10 minutes to reach Karsa Kafe and other small eateries in the area for coffee or a bite.

If you stop to enjoy the scenery or take photos along the way, it may take longer. Keep in mind this is one-way time, and you'll need to retrace your steps back. If you plan to include this in your itinerary, you should set aside at least 2 to 2.5 hours.

### Best time to visit the Ubud Skywalk

It's best to start the Campuhan Ridge Walk as early as possible around sunrise. Since the trail sits on a ridge with little shade, it quickly becomes quite hot. I recommend setting out between 6:00 AM and 7:00 AM, or even starting your morning walk before the sun rises!

You can also choose to do this walk before sunset. While it starts off quite hot, the temperature is very pleasant by the end—it's a great option too.

We don't recommend hiking the Campuhan Ridge Walk in the afternoon or evening. During the day, it can get quite hot and unpleasant due to limited shade from the sun. At night, there's no lighting, so you'll miss all the scenery and the walk will become much more difficult.

### Do you need to pay an entrance fee for the Ubud Sky Walk?

The Campuhan Ridge Walk is a free trail with no ticket required. Visitors can freely access and enjoy this beautiful nature trail, exploring the scenery and ridge landscapes of the Ubud area without any additional cost. Remember to respect the local environment, keep the trail clean, and follow local regulations.
