**Alunê** is the brand-new beach club at W Bali – Seminyak, debuting in September 2026 as the successor to the iconic WOOBAR. Its name draws from the rhythm of waves and nature, blending coastal fine dining, signature cocktails, and a music-driven community experience.

The design is inspired by the ocean and sunset light, featuring a glowing bar that bathes in golden-orange hues at dusk, front-row daybeds with ocean views, and exclusive beachfront cabanas with private Jacuzzis. ## Pet Heaven: Therapeutic Cuddles with Furry Friends ([Map Guide](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1il-sYTIMNMSVrVWqSnUPgdlsbCfMPP0&usp=sharing)🗺️)

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1788959029113-pet_nirvana.jpg)

If you're an animal lover or just looking to unwind, Pet Nirvana in Bali is a must-visit! As Bali's first luxury pet interaction park, it's located in the heart of Seminyak and combines a zoo, café, and amusement park all in one.

Home to over 15 dogs, 4 cats, 2 raccoons, and a variety of other adorable animals, it's the perfect place for close encounters. You can feed them, play with them, swim alongside them, or simply relax with a drink at the café. With a stellar 4.8/5 rating, visitors rave about it—calling it "the highlight of my trip" and saying "I come here every time I'm in Bali."

**The best part** is that it's not just about animal interactions — there's also open spaces, a spacious café, dining packages, and even a swimming experience. Whether you want to stop by for 2 hours or spend half a day, there's plenty of fun to be had. Packages start from just Rp. 312,500, offering great value.

## Explore Art Galleries ([Map Guide](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1il-sYTIMNMSVrVWqSnUPgdlsbCfMPP0&usp=sharing)🗺️)

![逛藝廊（地圖指南）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f1f5d88037295eb5c94f58e320a777fc.jpg)

Seminyak is one of Bali's most artistic areas, offering not only beautiful beaches and upscale restaurants but also many art galleries worth exploring. Travelers who enjoy contemporary art or seek exquisite artworks should not miss these galleries in Seminyak. Here are a few recommended galleries:

### 1. **Purpa Fine Art Gallery**

Purpa Fine Art Gallery showcases contemporary artworks, focusing on unique creations from local and international artists. The works here span diverse styles, from abstract to realism, ensuring every visitor finds something they love. The gallery's elegant atmosphere is perfect for leisurely appreciating each painting.

### 2. **Nyaman Gallery**

Nyaman Gallery is a fusion of contemporary and traditional art, dedicated to promoting emerging artists. The artworks here span painting, sculpture, photography, and more, allowing visitors to appreciate the beauty of different materials and expressions. Highly regarded both locally and internationally, Nyaman is one of Seminyak's artistic highlights.

### 3. **Reservoart**

Reservoart primarily showcases avant-garde works by contemporary artists, emphasizing the collision and fusion of color and form. This gallery is forward-thinking and a must-visit for art collectors or travelers interested in contemporary art.

### 4. **Buddha Gallery**

Buddha Gallery specializes in various Buddhist art pieces, from carvings and paintings to Buddhist ornaments. Travelers who appreciate Eastern religious art or are curious about Buddhist culture can find unique Buddha statues or handicrafts here as souvenirs or collectibles.

### 5. **The Gallery**

The Gallery focuses on displaying contemporary artworks, featuring carefully selected paintings and sculptures. It regularly hosts exhibitions that attract art enthusiasts and collectors, making it a great spot to soak up the artistic atmosphere in Seminyak.
