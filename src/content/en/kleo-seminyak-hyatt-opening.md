**Bottom line**: Kleo Seminyak is Hyatt's first **JdV by Hyatt** hotel in Southeast Asia, **opening in July 2025** in Seminyak. It offers a relaxed, locally inspired boutique lifestyle stay, perfect for travelers seeking personalized design and a break from cookie-cutter luxury hotels.

Kleo Seminyak marks the proud debut of the JdV by Hyatt brand in Southeast Asia, launching in July 2025 in the energetic Seminyak district, making it one of the most buzzworthy accommodations in the area.

![【凱悅JdV插旗峇里島】2025/07新飯店 Kleo Seminyak](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_bd8bb7aac4749701f20259e706c43985.jpg)

**JdV by Hyatt** is Hyatt Hotels Corporation's boutique lifestyle brand, with its full name derived from the French **"Joie de Vivre,"** meaning "joy of living."

This brand champions **relaxed, locally connected, and socially vibrant** stays, steering away from the formality of traditional luxury hotels and instead emphasizing personalized design and community ties.

As a result, every JdV hotel has its own unique theme and style—never cookie-cutter—often weaving in local art, music, dining, and lifestyle.

## 📍 Location

Southeast Asia's first JdV by Hyatt—Kleo Seminyak—is set in Bali's liveliest area, **Seminyak**, with an unbeatable location: **a three-minute walk to Petitenget Beach**, where you can soak up sea breezes and sunset views anytime. It's also close to **Seminyak Beach** and **Eat Street**, easily reachable on foot or by a short ride. This spot lets guests enjoy both beachside relaxation and urban energy—morning strolls on the sand and evenings browsing design boutiques or savoring local and international cuisine. The hotel is well-connected, with a 30–50 minute taxi or Grab ride to the airport.

## 🏖 Amenities

Kleo Seminyak is a blend of 'boutique hotel + social space,' offering:

- **Rooftop Pool & Rooftop Social Bar**: Swim while overlooking sunset sea views, and enjoy evening drinks and gatherings.
- **Zia Tina Italian Restaurant**: Helmed by a chef from Puglia, Italy, serving handmade pasta, thin-crust pizza, and creative dishes using local ingredients.

## 🛏 Rooms

With around 60 rooms, the hotel features bold mid-century modern design, blending exotic colors and art installations like sculpted walls and chandeliers, while bathrooms embrace Zen aesthetics. The rooms combine modern simplicity with Balinese elements, featuring soft tones, great natural light, and attention to detail and comfort. Each room includes:

- Air conditioning, flat-screen TV, safe, coffee/tea making facilities
- Spacious bathroom with premium amenities
- Some rooms have a balcony overlooking the pool or street
