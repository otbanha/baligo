**Bottom Line**: Kleo Seminyak is the first hotel under Hyatt's **JdV by Hyatt** brand in Southeast Asia, **opening in July 2025** in Seminyak. It offers a relaxed, locally-inspired boutique lifestyle stay, perfect for travelers seeking personalized design and a break from conventional high-end hotel vibes.

Kleo Seminyak is the proud debut of the JdV by Hyatt brand in Southeast Asia, starting operations in July 2025 in the energetic Seminyak area, making it one of the most buzzworthy stays locally.

![【凱悅JdV插旗峇里島】2025/07新飯店 Kleo Seminyak](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_bd8bb7aac4749701f20259e706c43985.jpg)

**JdV by Hyatt** is a boutique lifestyle hotel brand under Hyatt Hotels Corporation, with its full name derived from the French **“Joie de Vivre,”** meaning "joy of living."

This brand emphasizes **relaxed, locally immersive, and socially vibrant** stays, steering away from the formality of traditional luxury hotels and instead highlighting personalized design and connections with the local community.

As a result, each JdV hotel has its own unique theme and style, never cookie-cutter, often weaving in local art, music, dining, and lifestyle.

## 📍 Location

Southeast Asia's first JdV by Hyatt—Kleo Seminyak—is located in Bali's liveliest area, **Seminyak**, with an excellent position—**_about a three-minute walk to Petitenget Beach_**, perfect for soaking up sea breezes and sunset views anytime. It's also close to **Seminyak Beach** and **Eat Street**, easily reachable on foot or by a short drive. This spot lets guests enjoy both beachside relaxation and urban energy—morning walks on the sand and evenings exploring design boutiques or savoring local and international cuisine. The hotel is well-connected, with a 30–50 minute taxi or Grab ride to the airport.

## 🏖 Facilities

Kleo Seminyak is all about blending 'boutique stays with social spaces,' offering:

- **Rooftop Pool & Rooftop Social Bar**: Swim while taking in sunset ocean views, and enjoy evening drinks and gatherings.
- **Zia Tina Italian Restaurant**: Helmed by a chef from Puglia, Italy, serving handmade pasta, thin-crust pizza, and creative dishes that fuse local ingredients.

## 🛏 Guest Rooms

With around 60 rooms, the hotel features bold mid-century modern design, blending exotic colors and art installations like sculpted walls and chandeliers, while the bathrooms embrace Zen aesthetics. The room design combines modern minimalism with Balinese elements, featuring soft tones, great natural light, and attention to detail and comfort. Each room includes:

- Air conditioning, flat-screen TV, safe, and coffee/tea making facilities
- Spacious bathrooms with high-quality amenities
- Select room types feature a balcony overlooking the pool or street view
