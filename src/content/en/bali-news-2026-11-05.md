## 🎭 East Bali's Seraya Culture Fest Starts Tomorrow! Three Days of Traditional Gebug Ende Performances

The Seraya Culture Festival in Karangasem, East Bali, will take place from November 5 to 7, marking its fifth edition.

With the theme 'Harmony of Art, Tradition, Nature & Culture,' the event is centered at Ki Kopang Square and Bias Lantang Beach, blending traditional culture, art performances, nature experiences, and community activities.

The highlight this year remains the traditional Gebug Ende performance, a competitive ritual using rattan shields and sticks, rooted in local culture and historically tied to rain-prayer ceremonies.

During the festival, Gebug Ende is scheduled to take place daily from 4 PM to 6 PM.

Beyond the traditional performances, the event also includes:

- Traditional Balinese dance competitions
- Yoga sessions
- Seaside fishing fun
- Local music performances
- Community blood donation drive
- Local SME product exhibition

Performers at the event will include Lolot Band, Ray Peni, and DJ Mahesa.

### Is It Worth a Special Trip?

If you're exploring eastern Bali or have a passion for traditional culture and local festivals, Seraya Culture Fest is worth considering.

Seraya is located in Karangasem, quite far from the popular accommodation areas in southern Bali. It's best planned as part of an eastern Bali exploration itinerary rather than a round trip from Seminyak or Canggu.

The event runs from November 5 to 7, and travelers can arrange their visit based on local transportation and accommodation.

## 🏡 19 New Private Pool Villas in Canggu! The Arc House Canggu Officially Launches

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1793799842590-15488.jpg)

Bali’s Canggu adds another boutique accommodation brand.

Hong Kong-based hotel group Portier Hotels officially launched The Arc House boutique hotel series on November 4, with its first property, The Arc House Canggu, now operating in Bali.

Located near Batu Bolong Beach in Canggu, this property focuses on intimate, design-driven, and personalized service, featuring 19 suite villas, each with a private pool.

Unlike large resorts, The Arc House Canggu aims to offer a more private stay experience while giving guests easy access to Canggu’s restaurants, cafés, boutiques, and beaches.

Portier Hotels also plans to expand the brand to Hong Kong and Melbourne, with the Hong Kong location expected to open in 2026 and Melbourne in early 2027.

### A New Option in Canggu’s Accommodation Market

Canggu has long been a popular area in Bali, especially among travelers who love cafés, design-forward stays, beaches, and nightlife.

The Arc House Canggu takes a small boutique approach with 19 private pool villas, positioning itself differently from large hotels or typical villa rentals.

If you appreciate design and private pool accommodations, it’s worth adding to your Canggu stay list.

Since the brand has just officially launched, actual room rates, guest reviews, and long-term performance will take time to accumulate.

## 🚌 Important Notice for Trans Metro Dewata Riders: Temporary Change in Pick-up/Drop-off Location at Tabanan Kediri Terminal

Bali's public bus service, Trans Metro Dewata (TMD), has temporarily adjusted its pick-up and drop-off point at the Kediri Terminal in Tabanan due to ongoing station renovation work.

Starting November 4, TMD buses will no longer enter the Kediri Terminal. Instead, passengers will be picked up and dropped off on the road in front of the terminal.

This adjustment is made to accommodate the renovation of Kediri Terminal, ensuring passenger safety by avoiding the construction zone while keeping public transport running smoothly.

The renovation of Kediri Terminal has a budget of approximately 1.94 billion Indonesian Rupiah and includes the following work:

- Resurfacing and improvement of the terminal floor
- Renovation of the main entrance gate
- Construction of an entrance featuring traditional Balinese architectural elements
- Addition of traditional sculptures and decorations
- Overall improvement of public spaces

Construction is expected to be completed by the end of December 2026.

### How does this affect travelers?

If you plan to take the Trans Metro Dewata to Tabanan, especially when passing through Kediri Terminal, please note that the boarding and alighting points have been temporarily moved to the front of the station.

Currently, only the passenger boarding and alighting locations have been adjusted—the bus route itself is not suspended.

Since construction is still ongoing, travelers are advised to check the latest stop information before arriving and follow on-site signs near the station.

## ⛈️ Landslide near Kehen Temple in Bangli Damages 4 Shops! Ongoing Rainy Season Hazard Risks

Bangli Regency in Bali was recently hit by heavy rainfall and strong winds. Local disaster management authorities have reported multiple incidents that occurred on the evening of November 1, including a landslide near Kehen Temple.

The landslide took place in the Cempaga area, damaging four shops beneath the parking area of Kehen Temple.

In addition, areas such as Bangli, Susut, and Tembuku also experienced falling rocks, fallen trees, building damage, and road damage.

Local disaster management authorities reported that the incident has not caused any fatalities so far, and heavy machinery and personnel have been deployed for cleanup operations.

Meteorological authorities have warned that Bali is gradually entering the rainy season, with the peak of the 2026–2027 rainy season expected in February 2027.

### What should you pay attention to when visiting Bangli attractions recently?

Kehen Temple is one of the key cultural attractions in Bangli, and many travelers also plan to visit nearby areas.

During the rainy season, roads in mountainous areas and around temples may experience falling rocks, water accumulation, or localized landslides due to heavy rainfall.

If you plan to visit Bangli or other mountainous attractions, it is advisable to check local road conditions and avoid staying on slopes, riverbanks, or areas prone to rockfalls during heavy rain.

This incident mainly occurred on the evening of November 1 and does not mean Kehen Temple is fully closed. Travelers should still refer to the latest announcements from the attraction and local government.
