# Bali Ubud Solo Travel Recommendation! YouTuber Casper Stays at a Five-Star Resort, Explores a Secret Rice Field Trail and Tirta Empul

On his second trip to Bali, beyond the beach resort vibes of Seminyak and Kuta, Casper discovers a completely different way to travel: slowing down in Ubud and savoring a few leisurely days.

This time, YouTuber Casper returns to Bali and chooses to focus his trip on the central town of Ubud for a three-day, two-night solo adventure. He skips the rush of sightseeing and avoids cramming his itinerary, instead checking into a five-star resort in the heart of town, strolling through Ubud's streets, and visiting nearby cultural attractions.

From the Sacred Monkey Forest and Ubud Palace to an unexpected rice field trail and the culturally rich purification ritual at Tirta Empul, this journey showcases Ubud's unique charm, so different from Bali's southern beaches.

## Alaya Resort Ubud: Stay in the City Center with Rice Field Views

For this trip, Casper chose to stay at **Alaya Resort Ubud** for three consecutive nights.

While he usually prefers budget-friendly accommodations when traveling solo, this time he decided to splurge a little and pick a five-star resort where he could truly relax and enjoy himself.

The hotel's biggest advantage is its location—right in the center of Ubud, within walking distance to the Sacred Monkey Forest and Ubud Palace, with plenty of restaurants and shops nearby.

What makes it even more special is that the hotel rooms offer views of the rice paddies, allowing guests to soak in Ubud's signature laid-back atmosphere even while staying in the bustling town center.

Casper also shared the hotel's breakfast and complimentary activity experiences, including a hands-on workshop where guests make their own herbal tea and body scrub. For travelers looking to pack more activities into their stay, this is a noteworthy feature.

## Strolling Through Central Ubud: Sacred Monkey Forest, Ubud Palace, and Coconut Ice Cream

After checking into the hotel, Casper began exploring central Ubud.

His first stop was the famous **Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary**. This isn't just a place to see monkeys—it's a nature reserve that blends tropical forest, ancient temples, and Balinese culture.

Compared to the monkeys at Uluwatu Temple, Casper found the ones here relatively more docile. Still, he reminds everyone to keep an eye on their belongings and avoid feeding or provoking the monkeys.

Next, he headed to Ubud Palace, making a point to stop along the way for a taste of the local favorite: Tukies Coconut Shop's coconut ice cream.

Made with fresh coconut meat, coconut chips, and palm sugar syrup, this ice cream is a perfect way to cool down while exploring Ubud's streets in the heat.

## A Hidden Gem Discovered by Chance: Sweet Orange Walk Trail Rice Paddy Path

One highlight of this Ubud trip that I find particularly noteworthy is the **Sweet Orange Walk Trail**, which Casper stumbled upon by accident.

The entrance to this path is tucked away in a small alley near Ubud Palace, not particularly conspicuous, and easy to miss if you're not paying attention.

At the start, the path is quite narrow, even making Casper wonder if he had taken a wrong turn. But as he continued, the view suddenly opened up to reveal a vast expanse of lush green rice paddies.

Along the way, besides the rural scenery, you can also see stones engraved with messages left by travelers from around the world.

There's no fixed destination here—it's perfect for strolling at your own pace and experiencing a different side of Ubud beyond the city center.

If you've already explored Ubud Palace and the traditional market and are looking for a quieter place to wander, this path is worth considering.

## Tirta Empul: Experience a Traditional Balinese Purification Ritual

On the second day, Casper headed to the famous **Tirta Empul** temple near Ubud to personally experience a traditional Balinese purification ritual.

Tirta Empul is renowned for its holy spring water and is one of Bali's most important Hindu temples. Many local devotees come here to pray and perform purification ceremonies, and it also attracts foreign travelers eager to learn about the local culture.

This time, Casper didn't just tour the temple—he actually got into the water to take part in the ritual, with a local guide explaining the process.

From preparing the traditional sarong and learning the rules of worship to experiencing the different water spouts in sequence, the entire experience left a deep impression on him.

He also mentioned that the spring water was colder than expected, making him a bit nervous at first, but after completing the entire ritual, he felt a wonderful sense of physical and mental relaxation.

If you're planning to visit Tirta Empul Temple, it's advisable to learn about the dress code and ceremonial etiquette in advance, and to respect the local religious culture rather than treating it merely as a photo spot.

## Bali Supermarket Souvenir Must-Haves: Casper's Three Favorite Snacks

At the end of the trip, Casper also made a special trip to the local supermarket to pick up souvenirs.

This time, he shared three snacks he personally loves:

- **Merubail Roasted Coconut Chips:** A local Balinese brand with thin, crispy texture and a strong coconut aroma—a snack he always buys on every visit.
- **YAVA Spicy Nuts:** A savory-sweet-spicy flavor that's quite addictive, perfect for those who enjoy bold-tasting snacks.
- **SilverQueen Chocolate:** A well-known Indonesian chocolate brand with plenty of nuts, reasonably priced, and great for gifting to friends or colleagues.

If you're tired of buying only coffee, soap, or traditional pastries every time you visit Bali, these supermarket snacks are worth adding to your souvenir list.
