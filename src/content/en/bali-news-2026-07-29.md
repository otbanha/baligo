# Hot Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers (2026/7/29)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth paying attention to today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Reminder: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali in the Coming Days

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves to parts of Bali in the coming days, especially along the coast and for marine activities, where caution is advised.

**Traveler Tips:** If you're planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to double-check ferry schedules and weather conditions before heading out. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique, Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently experienced an alleged theft incident involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This case serves as a reminder to international visitors that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, must undergo legal proceedings accordingly.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, please adhere to local laws and store regulations. If a consumer dispute arises, first communicate with the shop or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill volume through modern waste treatment technology while addressing the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key measure in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, expected to improve the overall quality of the tourism environment in the future.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, consider reducing the use of single-use plastic items and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to help protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded again that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits and cannot engage in activities inconsistent with their tourist visa purposes. This reminder comes after several recent incidents where foreigners sparked controversy by organizing events.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, marathons, or commercial activities, it is advisable to confirm compliance with Indonesian visa regulations and relevant laws in advance.
