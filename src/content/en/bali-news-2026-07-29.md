# Hot Topics for Bali Travelers + News Roundup (2026/7/29)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth paying attention to today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves to parts of Bali in the coming days. Caution is especially advised for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join snorkeling, diving, or dolphin-watching tours, please double-check ferry schedules and weather updates before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique Prompts Police to Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A yoga apparel boutique in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, leading to a police investigation. The incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against crimes like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Tips:** When shopping in Bali, always adhere to local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to resolve it with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could impact your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially advanced its Waste-to-Energy (WtE) initiative, aiming to reduce landfill volume through modern waste processing technology while addressing long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to enhance overall travel quality in the future.

**Traveler Tips:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at accommodations and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permits

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits and cannot use tourist visas for activities outside their purpose. This reminder follows recent controversies involving unauthorized events by foreigners.

**Traveler Tips:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial activities, check that they comply with Indonesian visa and regulations in advance.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Online Controversies Haven't Affected Overall Travel Safety

Regarding recent high-profile social incidents, the Bali provincial government states that overall public safety and tourism order remain stable. Authorities have instructed police and relevant units to step up patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to worry excessively over isolated cases.

**Traveler Tips:** Maintain general safety awareness during your trip—secure your belongings, follow traffic rules, and stay updated on official announcements. Most visitors can still enjoy a worry-free vacation.
