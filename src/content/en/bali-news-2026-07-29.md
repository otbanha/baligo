# Key Topics and News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the five most noteworthy travel news stories today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Parts of Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has reported that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves to some areas of Bali in the coming days. Extra caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea excursions such as snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before heading out. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight rain jacket.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, must comply with legal procedures.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, adhere to local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, communicate with the shop or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill waste through modern processing technology while addressing long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, expected to improve overall travel quality.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, minimize the use of single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at accommodations and attractions to help preserve Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain proper permits and cannot use tourist visas for activities inconsistent with their visa purpose. This reminder follows recent controversies over unauthorized events by foreigners.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, runs, or commercial activities, verify compliance with Indonesian visa and regulations in advance.
