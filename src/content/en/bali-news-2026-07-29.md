# Hot Topics for Bali Tourists + News Roundup (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth watching today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Bali Still Expects Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali in the coming days. Caution is especially advised for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea excursions like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, please reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique Prompts Police to Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A yoga apparel boutique in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, and the individual is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against crimes such as theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Tips:** When shopping in Bali, please adhere to local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to resolve it with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could impact your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially advanced its Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill volume through modern waste treatment technology while addressing the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key measure in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to enhance the overall quality of the travel environment in the future.

**Traveler Tips:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at accommodations and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permits

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded again that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must apply for the necessary permits and cannot use tourist visas for activities that violate visa purposes. This reminder comes after several recent controversies involving foreigners organizing events.

**Traveler Tips:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, marathons, or commercial activities, check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa and regulations.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Viral Incidents Do Not Affect Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent high-profile social incidents, the Bali provincial government stated that overall public safety and tourism order remain stable. Authorities have instructed police and relevant units to step up patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to over-worry about isolated events.

**Traveler Tips:** Maintain general safety awareness during your trip, such as safeguarding valuables, following traffic rules, and staying updated on official announcements. Most travelers can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
