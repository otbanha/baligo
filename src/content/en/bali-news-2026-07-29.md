# Bali Travel Hot Topics & News Roundup (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the 5 most noteworthy travel news stories today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali, especially along the coast and for maritime activities. Caution is advised.

**Traveler Tips:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or engage in snorkeling, diving, or dolphin-watching trips, be sure to reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique, Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A recent incident at a yoga apparel boutique in Ubud involving an alleged theft by a foreign tourist has led to a police investigation. This serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws on criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal procedures.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, always follow local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, communicate with the shop or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill waste through modern processing technology while addressing long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, expected to enhance the overall quality of the travel environment.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at accommodations and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain proper permits and cannot use tourist visas for activities inconsistent with their visa purpose. This reminder follows recent controversies over unauthorized events by foreigners.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, runs, or commercial activities, ensure compliance with Indonesian visa and related regulations in advance.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Online Buzz Incidents Do Not Affect Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent widely discussed social incidents, the Bali Provincial Government has stated that overall public security and tourism order remain stable. It has instructed the police and relevant authorities to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to be overly concerned about isolated events.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, simply maintain general safety awareness—such as keeping valuables secure, following traffic rules, and staying updated on official announcements. Most visitors can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
