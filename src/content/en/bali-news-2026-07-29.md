# Hot Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new developments in weather, safety management, and sustainability that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth watching today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) has reported that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali. Caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you're planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to double-check ferry schedules and weather updates before heading out. For mountain attractions, it's a good idea to bring a lightweight rain jacket.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, adhere to local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try communicating with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill waste through modern waste treatment technology while addressing the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, expected to improve the overall quality of the travel environment in the future.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, help minimize single-use plastic consumption and follow waste sorting guidelines at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment together.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that anyone planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that fall outside their intended purpose. This reminder comes after several recent incidents involving foreigners hosting events that sparked controversy.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to organize public classes, workshops, running events, or commercial activities, it's advisable to check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa regulations and relevant laws.
