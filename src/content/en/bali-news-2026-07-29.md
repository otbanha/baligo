# Hot Topics and News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth paying attention to today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Reminder: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali in the Coming Days

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves to parts of Bali in the coming days. Coastal areas and marine activities require extra caution.

**Traveler Advice:** If you plan to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join snorkeling, diving, or dolphin-watching tours, please reconfirm ferry schedules and weather information before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, must comply with legal procedures.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, follow local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try communicating with the shop or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy project, aiming to reduce landfill volume through modern waste treatment technology while addressing long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, expected to enhance the overall quality of the travel environment.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, help by reducing single-use plastic consumption and sorting waste according to accommodation and attraction guidelines to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities remind foreign nationals that planning commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia requires proper permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities inconsistent with their purpose. This reminder follows recent controversies involving foreigners hosting events.

**Traveler Advice:** General travel, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, runs, or commercial events, verify compliance with Indonesian visa and regulations in advance.
