# Top Topics and News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Below are the five most noteworthy travel news items today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali, particularly along the coast and for marine activities, where caution is advised.

**Traveler Tips:** If you plan to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, please reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a light raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique, Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Tips:** When shopping in Bali, always abide by local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to resolve it with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy project aimed at reducing landfill waste through modern processing technology, while also tackling the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, promising to improve the overall quality of the travel environment in the future.

**Traveler Tips:** During your visit, consider reducing your use of single-use plastics and follow waste sorting guidelines at your accommodation and attractions to help preserve Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that anyone planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that fall outside their intended purpose. This reminder comes after several recent incidents where foreigners sparked controversy by organizing events without authorization.

**Traveler Tips:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial activities, it's advisable to check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa regulations and relevant laws.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Online Controversies Have Not Affected Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent social incidents that have drawn public attention, the Bali Provincial Government has stated that overall public security and tourism order remain stable. Authorities have instructed the police and relevant agencies to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to be overly concerned about isolated incidents.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, simply maintain general safety awareness—such as safeguarding your belongings, following traffic rules, and keeping an eye on official announcements. The vast majority of visitors can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
