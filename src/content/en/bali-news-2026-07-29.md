# Hot Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new developments in weather, safety management, and sustainability that travelers should note. Here are the five most noteworthy travel news items today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali, particularly along the coast and for marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you're planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to double-check ferry schedules and weather conditions before heading out. For mountain attractions, it's advisable to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique, Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler's Tip:** When shopping in Bali, always follow local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to resolve it with the shop or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project aimed at reducing landfill waste through modern processing technology while tackling the long-standing garbage issue in tourist areas. This initiative is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, promising to improve the overall quality of the travel environment.

**Traveler's Tip:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that anyone planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that fall outside their intended purpose. This reminder comes after several recent incidents involving foreigners hosting events that sparked controversy.

**Traveler's Tip:** General travel, attending legal events, or private gatherings are not affected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, runs, or commercial events, be sure to check whether they comply with Indonesian visa and other regulations in advance.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Online Controversies Have Not Affected Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent social incidents that have drawn public attention, the Bali Provincial Government has stated that overall public security and tourism order remain stable. Authorities have instructed the police and relevant agencies to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to overreact to isolated incidents.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, simply maintain general safety awareness—such as safeguarding your belongings, following traffic rules, and keeping an eye on official announcements. Most visitors can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
