# Bali Tourist Hot Topics + News Roundup (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the 5 most noteworthy travel news stories today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Reminder: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali in Coming Days

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves to parts of Bali in the coming days, particularly along the coast and for maritime activities, where caution is advised.

**Traveler Advice:** If you plan to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or participate in marine activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, please reconfirm boat schedules and weather information before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A recent incident at a yoga apparel boutique in Ubud involving an alleged theft by a foreign tourist has led to a police investigation. This case serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal procedures.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, always comply with local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try communicating with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project aimed at reducing landfill waste through modern processing technology while addressing long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, expected to enhance the overall quality of the travel environment.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that any commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia require proper permits. Tourist visas do not permit activities beyond their intended purpose. This reminder follows recent controversies involving unauthorized events organized by foreigners.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, runs, or commercial events, ensure compliance with Indonesian visa and regulatory requirements in advance.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Online Hot Topics Do Not Affect Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent social incidents that have drawn attention online, the Bali Provincial Government has stated that overall public security and tourism order remain stable. It has instructed the police and relevant authorities to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to over-worry about isolated incidents.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, simply maintain general safety awareness—such as keeping valuables secure, following traffic rules, and staying updated on official announcements. Most travelers can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
