# Hot Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth watching today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Reminder: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali in the Coming Days

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali in the coming days. Extra caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you're planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to double-check ferry schedules and weather forecasts before heading out. For mountain area visits, it's recommended to bring a lightweight rain jacket.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique, Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft by a foreign tourist, and the individual involved is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, must comply with legal procedures.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, always follow local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to resolve it with the store or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy project aimed at reducing landfill waste through modern processing technology, while also addressing long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to enhance the overall quality of the travel environment.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, consider reducing the use of single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to help protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that anyone planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that fall outside their intended purpose. This reminder comes after several recent incidents where foreigners sparked controversy by hosting events without authorization.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are not affected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial events, it's advisable to check whether they comply with Indonesian visa and other regulations in advance.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Viral Incidents Do Not Affect Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent high-profile social incidents, the Bali Provincial Government has stated that overall public safety and tourism order remain stable. Authorities have instructed police and relevant agencies to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to overreact to isolated events.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, simply maintain general safety awareness—such as keeping valuables secure, following traffic rules, and staying updated on official announcements. Most visitors can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
