# Hot Topics for Bali Travelers + News Roundup (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new developments in weather, safety management, and sustainability that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth watching today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali in the Coming Days

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali, especially along the coast and for marine activities, where caution is advised.

**Traveler Tips:** If you're planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before heading out. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, must comply with legal procedures.

**Traveler Tips:** When shopping in Bali, always follow local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try discussing it with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid letting a minor oversight disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy project aimed at reducing landfill waste through modern processing technology, while also addressing long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This initiative is a key step in promoting sustainable tourism on the island, with the potential to significantly improve the overall quality of the travel environment.

**Traveler Tips:** During your visit, help reduce single-use plastic consumption and sort your waste according to accommodation and attraction guidelines to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that anyone planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that fall outside their intended purpose. This reminder follows several recent incidents where foreigners sparked controversy by organizing events without authorization.

**Traveler Tips:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are not affected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, fun runs, or commercial activities, it's advisable to check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa regulations and other relevant laws.
