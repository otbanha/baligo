# Bali Travel Hot Topics & News Roundup (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new developments in weather, safety management, and sustainability that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth watching today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Reminder: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) reports that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali. Caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Advice:** If you plan to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join snorkeling, diving, or dolphin-watching tours, please reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a light raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal procedures.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, comply with local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, first communicate with the shop or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy project aimed at reducing landfill volume through modern waste treatment technology while addressing long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, expected to enhance the overall quality of the tourism environment.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastic consumption and follow waste sorting rules at accommodations and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that hosting commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia requires proper permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities inconsistent with their purpose. This reminder follows recent controversies involving foreigners organizing events.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial events, verify compliance with Indonesian visa and regulations in advance.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Online Controversies Do Not Affect Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent social incidents that have drawn attention, the Bali Provincial Government has stated that overall public safety and tourism order remain stable. Authorities have instructed police and relevant agencies to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to overreact to isolated incidents.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, simply maintain general safety awareness—such as safeguarding your belongings, following traffic rules, and keeping an eye on official announcements. Most visitors can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
