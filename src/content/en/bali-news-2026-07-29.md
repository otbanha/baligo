# Key Topics and News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, several updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development are worth noting for visitors. Here are the top 5 travel news stories to watch today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has warned that despite the onset of the dry season, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and elevated waves in parts of Bali in the coming days. Extra caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea excursions like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, please reconfirm ferry schedules and weather updates before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to carry a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique Prompts Police Reminder on Local Laws

A yoga apparel boutique in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign traveler, who is now under police investigation. The incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against crimes such as theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Tips:** When shopping in Bali, always comply with local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to resolve it with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill volume through modern waste treatment technology while addressing the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is also a key measure in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to enhance the overall quality of the tourism environment in the future.

**Traveler Tip:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at accommodations and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have once again reminded that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits and cannot use tourist visas for activities that violate visa purposes. This reminder comes after several recent controversies involving foreigners hosting events.

**Traveler Tip:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, marathons, or commercial activities, check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa and regulations.
