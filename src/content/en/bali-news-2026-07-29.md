# Bali Travel Hot Topics & News Roundup (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the 5 most noteworthy travel news stories today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and High Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has warned that despite the onset of the dry season, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali, especially along the coast and for maritime activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you plan to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea excursions like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, please reconfirm boat schedules and weather conditions before departure. For mountain attractions, it's advisable to bring a light raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique, Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A recent incident at a yoga apparel boutique in Ubud involving an alleged theft by a foreign tourist has led to a police investigation. This case serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, always comply with local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try communicating with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project aimed at reducing landfill waste through modern processing technology while addressing long-standing waste issues in tourist areas. This initiative is a key step toward sustainable tourism, promising to improve the overall quality of the travel environment.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastics and follow waste sorting guidelines at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that organizing commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia requires proper permits. Tourist visas do not authorize activities beyond tourism. This reminder follows recent controversies involving unauthorized events by foreigners.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, runs, or commercial events, ensure compliance with Indonesian visa and regulatory requirements beforehand.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Online Controversies Have Not Affected Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent social incidents that have drawn attention, the Bali Provincial Government has stated that overall public order and tourism order remain stable. It has instructed the police and relevant authorities to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to worry excessively due to isolated incidents.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, maintain general safety awareness, such as keeping valuables secure, following traffic rules, and paying attention to official announcements. Most travelers can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
