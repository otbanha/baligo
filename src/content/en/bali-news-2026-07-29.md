# Top Topics and News Roundup for Bali Travelers (2026/7/29)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the five most noteworthy travel news stories today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali in Coming Days

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves to parts of Bali in the coming days. Extra caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea excursions like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before heading out. For mountain area attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, adhere to local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, communicate with the shop or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill waste through modern processing technology while addressing long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, expected to improve the overall quality of the travel environment.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, help by reducing single-use plastic consumption and sorting waste according to accommodation and attraction guidelines, contributing to the preservation of Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that anyone planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that violate visa purposes. This reminder follows recent controversies involving unauthorized events hosted by foreigners.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial activities, it's advisable to confirm compliance with Indonesian visa and related regulations in advance.
