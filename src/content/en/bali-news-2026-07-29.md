# Key Topics and News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the five most noteworthy travel news items today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has warned that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali in the coming days. Caution is especially advised for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Advice:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea excursions like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, please reconfirm ferry schedules and weather updates before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique Prompts Police Reminder on Local Laws

A yoga apparel boutique in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, and the individual is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against crimes such as theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, please adhere to local laws and store policies. If any disputes arise, try to communicate with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid disrupting your trip due to a momentary oversight.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill volume through modern waste treatment technology while addressing the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is also a key measure in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to enhance the overall quality of the tourism environment in the future.

**Traveler Tips:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastic items and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have once again reminded that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must apply for the necessary permits and cannot use tourist visas for activities that violate visa purposes. This reminder comes after several recent controversies involving foreigners hosting events.

**Traveler Tips:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial events, be sure to check whether they comply with Indonesian visa and related regulations.
