# Key Topics and News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth following today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has warned that despite the onset of the dry season, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali in the coming days. Extra caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea excursions like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, please double-check ferry schedules and weather updates before heading out. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique Prompts Police to Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A yoga apparel boutique in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, and the individual is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against crimes such as theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal procedures.

**Traveler Tips:** When shopping in Bali, always abide by local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to resolve it with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill volume through modern waste treatment technology while addressing the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is also a key measure in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to enhance the overall quality of the tourism environment in the future.

**Traveler Tips:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded again that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits in accordance with regulations. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that violate visa purposes. This reminder comes after several recent incidents involving foreigners hosting events that sparked controversy.

**Traveler Tips:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, fun runs, or commercial events, it's advisable to check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa and related regulations.
