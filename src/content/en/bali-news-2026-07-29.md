# Top Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the five most noteworthy travel news stories today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) reports that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali. Extra caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you're planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before heading out. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently experienced an alleged theft incident involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This case serves as a reminder to international visitors that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Tips:** When shopping in Bali, always adhere to local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try communicating with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill waste through modern waste treatment technology while addressing long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to improve the overall quality of the travel environment.

**Traveler Tips:** During your visit, consider reducing the use of single-use plastics and follow waste sorting guidelines at your accommodation and attractions to help preserve Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that anyone planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that fall outside their intended purpose. This reminder comes after several recent incidents where foreigners sparked controversy by organizing events without authorization.

**Traveler Tips:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, runs, or commercial activities, it's advisable to check in advance whether they comply with Indonesia's visa regulations and related laws.
