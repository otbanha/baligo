# Hot Topics for Bali Travelers + News Roundup (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Below are the five most noteworthy travel news items today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali in the Coming Days

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali in the coming days. Extra caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you plan to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea excursions like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, please reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a light raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal procedures.

**Traveler Tips:** When shopping in Bali, adhere to local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try communicating with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill waste through modern processing technology while addressing long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, expected to improve the overall quality of the travel environment.

**Traveler Tips:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastic consumption and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits and cannot use tourist visas for activities inconsistent with their visa purpose. This reminder follows recent controversies involving foreigners hosting events.

**Traveler Tips:** General travel, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, running events, or commercial activities, check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa and related regulations.
