# Hot Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development are worth noting for visitors. Here are the top 5 travel news stories to watch today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has reported that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali. Coastal and marine activities require extra caution.

**Traveler Tips:** If you're planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to check ferry schedules and weather updates before heading out. For mountain attractions, it's advisable to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal procedures.

**Traveler's Tip:** When shopping in Bali, always follow local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try communicating with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any minor oversight affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill waste through modern processing technology while tackling long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, promising to improve the overall quality of the travel environment.

**Traveler's Tip:** During your stay, help protect Bali's environment by reducing single-use plastics and sorting your waste according to accommodation and attraction guidelines.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that hosting commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia requires proper permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that fall outside their intended purpose. This reminder comes after several recent incidents involving foreigners organizing events that sparked controversy.

**Traveler's Tip:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial activities, it's advisable to check whether they comply with Indonesian visa and other regulations in advance.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Viral Incidents Have Not Affected Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent high-profile social incidents, the Bali Provincial Government has stated that overall public security and tourism order remain stable. Authorities have instructed the police and relevant agencies to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to be overly concerned about isolated events.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, simply maintain general safety awareness—such as keeping valuables secure, following traffic rules, and staying updated on official announcements. The vast majority of visitors can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
