# Bali Travel Hot Topics & News Roundup (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth following today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali, especially along the coast and for marine activities. Caution is advised.

**Traveler Tips:** If you plan to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join snorkeling, diving, or dolphin-watching tours, reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a light raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique, Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently experienced an alleged theft incident involving foreign tourists, with the suspects now under police investigation. This case serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws on criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal procedures.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, always comply with local laws and store regulations. If a dispute arises, try communicating with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid minor oversights affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill waste through modern processing technology while tackling long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, expected to enhance the overall travel environment.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permits

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits and cannot use tourist visas for activities outside their purpose. This reminder follows recent controversies involving unauthorized events by foreigners.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, runs, or commercial activities, check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa and other regulations.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Viral Incidents Do Not Affect Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent social incidents that have drawn public attention, the Bali Provincial Government stated that overall public security and tourism order remain stable. It has instructed the police and relevant authorities to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to overreact to isolated events.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, simply maintain general safety awareness—such as keeping valuables secure, following traffic rules, and staying updated on official announcements. Most visitors can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
