# Hot Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the five most noteworthy travel news items today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has reported that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali in the coming days. Caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, please reconfirm boat schedules and weather updates before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a light raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique Prompts Police to Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A yoga apparel boutique in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, and the individual is under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against crimes like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Tips:** When shopping in Bali, always adhere to local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to resolve it with the shop or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could affect your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Program to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) program, aiming to reduce landfill volume through modern waste treatment technology while addressing the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to enhance the overall quality of the travel environment in the future.

**Traveler Tip:** During your trip, help protect Bali's environment by reducing the use of single-use plastics and following waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without a Permit

Bali's immigration authorities have once again reminded that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits and must not use tourist visas for activities that violate visa purposes. This reminder comes after several recent controversies involving foreigners hosting events.

**Traveler Tip:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are not affected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, fun runs, or commercial activities, it's advisable to check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa and related regulations.
