# Bali Travel Hot Topics & News Roundup (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth watching today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) reports that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali, especially along coastal areas and for marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you're planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to check ferry schedules and weather updates before heading out. For mountain attractions, it's advisable to bring a lightweight rain jacket.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique, Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently became the scene of an alleged theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler's Tip:** When shopping in Bali, be sure to follow local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try communicating with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any minor oversight affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Boost Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) initiative aimed at reducing landfill use through modern waste processing technology, while also tackling the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to enhance the overall quality of the travel environment in the future.

**Traveler's Tip:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastic consumption and follow waste sorting guidelines at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment together.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have once again reminded that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits and cannot use tourist visas for activities that violate visa purposes. This reminder comes after several recent incidents where foreigners sparked controversy by organizing events.

**Traveler's Tip:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are not affected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial activities, it's advisable to check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa regulations and related laws.
