# Hot Topics for Bali Travelers + News Roundup (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Below are the five most noteworthy travel news stories today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali in the Coming Days

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves to parts of Bali in the coming days. Caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, please reconfirm ferry schedules and weather information before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler's Tip:** When shopping in Bali, always abide by local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try communicating with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid letting a minor oversight disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill volume through modern waste treatment technology while addressing the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, expected to improve the overall quality of the travel environment in the future.

**Traveler's Tip:** During your trip, help minimize the use of single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that anyone planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that fall outside their intended purpose. This reminder comes after several recent incidents where foreigners sparked controversy by organizing events without authorization.

**Traveler's Tip:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are not affected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial activities, it's advisable to verify compliance with Indonesian visa regulations and relevant laws in advance.
