# Hot Topics for Bali Travelers + News Roundup (2026/7/29)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Below are the 5 most noteworthy travel news items today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali, especially along the coast and for sea activities, where caution is advised.

**Traveler Tips:** If you plan to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join snorkeling, diving, or dolphin-watching tours, please reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a light raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique, Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, adhere to local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, first communicate with the shop or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill volume through modern waste treatment technology while addressing long-standing waste issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, expected to improve the overall quality of the tourism environment.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, help by reducing the use of single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits and cannot use tourist visas for activities inconsistent with their visa purpose. This reminder follows recent controversies involving foreigners hosting events.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, runs, or commercial events, it's advisable to confirm compliance with Indonesian visa and related regulations in advance.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Online Controversies Have Not Affected Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent social incidents that have drawn public attention, the Bali Provincial Government has stated that overall public security and tourism order remain stable. Authorities have instructed the police and relevant agencies to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to be overly concerned about isolated incidents.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, simply maintain general safety awareness—such as keeping valuables secure, following traffic rules, and staying updated on official announcements. Most visitors can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
