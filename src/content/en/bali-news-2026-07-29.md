# Bali Travel Hot Topics & News Roundup (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, several new developments in weather, safety management, and sustainability are worth noting for travelers. Here are the top 5 travel news stories to watch today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has reported that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali. Caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you're planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before heading out. For mountain destinations, it's a good idea to bring a lightweight rain jacket.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, always follow local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try communicating with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any minor oversight affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project aimed at reducing landfill waste through modern processing technology, while also tackling the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to enhance the overall quality of the travel environment in the future.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastic consumption and follow waste sorting guidelines at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that anyone planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that fall outside their intended purpose. This reminder comes after several recent incidents where foreigners sparked controversy by organizing events without authorization.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are not affected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, fun runs, or commercial activities, it's advisable to check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa regulations and relevant laws.
