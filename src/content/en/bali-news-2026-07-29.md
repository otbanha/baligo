# Top Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, several updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development are worth noting for visitors. Here are the five most important travel news stories to watch today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) has reported that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali. Caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you're planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to double-check ferry schedules and weather forecasts before heading out. For mountain destinations, it's a good idea to pack a lightweight rain jacket.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Tip:** When shopping in Bali, always follow local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to resolve it with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) initiative aimed at reducing landfill waste through modern processing technology while tackling the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, promising to enhance the overall quality of the travel environment in the future.

**Traveler Tip:** During your trip, help protect Bali's environment by reducing the use of single-use plastics and sorting your waste according to the rules at your accommodation and attractions.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that anyone planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that fall outside their intended purpose. This reminder comes after several recent incidents involving foreigners hosting events that sparked controversy.

**Traveler Tip:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are not affected. However, if you plan to organize public classes, workshops, races, or commercial activities, it's advisable to check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa regulations and relevant laws.
