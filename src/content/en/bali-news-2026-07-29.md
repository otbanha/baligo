# Hot Topics for Bali Travelers + News Roundup (2026/7/29)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth paying attention to today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves to parts of Bali in the coming days. Extra caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, please double-check ferry schedules and weather updates before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique Prompts Police Reminder for Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A yoga apparel boutique in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, and the individual is under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against crimes such as theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, must comply with legal procedures.

**Traveler Tips:** When shopping in Bali, always adhere to local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to communicate with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill volume through modern waste processing technology while addressing the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is also a key measure in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to enhance the overall quality of the tourism environment in the future.

**Traveler Tip:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Hold Events Without Permits

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded again that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits and cannot use tourist visas for activities that violate visa purposes. This reminder comes after several recent controversies involving foreigners organizing events.

**Traveler Tip:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial activities, it's advisable to check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa and relevant regulations.
