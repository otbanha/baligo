# Hot Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new developments in weather, safety management, and sustainability that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth paying attention to today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali in Coming Days

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves to parts of Bali in the coming days. Extra caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you're planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before heading out. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight rain jacket.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently experienced a suspected theft incident involving a foreign tourist, and the individual involved is under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder to foreign visitors that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, must undergo legal proceedings accordingly.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, please adhere to local laws and store regulations. If a consumer dispute arises, first communicate with the store or seek police assistance to avoid letting a momentary oversight affect your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Continuously Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy project, aiming to reduce landfill volume through modern waste treatment technology while addressing the long-standing waste issues plaguing tourist areas. This is also a key measure in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to enhance the overall quality of the tourism environment in the future.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, consider reducing the use of single-use plastic items and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to help protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have once again reminded that foreign nationals planning to hold commercial events, public events, or community activities in Indonesia must apply for the necessary permits and cannot use a tourist visa for activities inconsistent with its purpose. This reminder comes after several recent incidents where foreigners sparked controversy by hosting events.

**Traveler Advice:** General travel, attending legal events, or private gatherings are not affected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, running events, or commercial activities, it is advisable to confirm compliance with Indonesian visa and relevant regulations in advance.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Online Controversies Have Not Affected Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent social incidents that have drawn public attention, the Bali Provincial Government has stated that overall public security and tourism order remain stable. Authorities have instructed the police and relevant agencies to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to be overly concerned about isolated incidents.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, simply maintain general safety awareness—such as keeping your belongings secure, following traffic rules, and staying updated on official announcements. Most visitors can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
