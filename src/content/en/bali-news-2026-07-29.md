# Top Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new developments in weather, safety management, and sustainability that travelers should note. Here are the five most noteworthy travel news stories today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Parts of Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves to some areas of Bali in the coming days. Extra caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea excursions like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to reconfirm ferry schedules and weather conditions before heading out. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight rain jacket.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder to international visitors that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler's Tip:** When shopping in Bali, always follow local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to resolve it with the shop or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project aimed at reducing landfill waste through modern processing technology, while also tackling the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, promising to improve the overall quality of the travel environment in the future.

**Traveler's Tip:** During your trip, help by reducing the use of single-use plastics and sorting your trash according to accommodation and attraction guidelines, contributing to the preservation of Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have once again reminded that foreign nationals planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits and cannot use tourist visas for activities that violate visa purposes. This reminder comes after several recent incidents where foreigners sparked controversy by organizing events.

**Traveler's Tip:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial activities, it's advisable to check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa and relevant regulations.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Online Hot Topics Have Not Affected Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent social incidents that have drawn public attention, the Bali Provincial Government stated that overall public security and tourism order remain stable. It has instructed the police and relevant authorities to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to be overly concerned about isolated incidents.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, simply maintain general safety awareness—such as keeping valuables secure, following traffic rules, and paying attention to official announcements. Most visitors can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
