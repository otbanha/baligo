# Bali Travel Hot Topics & News Roundup (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth paying attention to today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Reminder: Bali Still Expects Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves in the Coming Days

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali, especially along the coast and for marine activities, so caution is advised.

**Traveler Advice:** If you plan to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, please reconfirm ferry schedules and weather information before departure. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique, Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, always comply with local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to resolve it with the shop or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Improve Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy project aimed at reducing landfill waste through modern processing technology, while also addressing long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to enhance the overall quality of the travel environment.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, help reduce single-use plastic consumption and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to protect Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that anyone planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that fall outside their intended purpose. This reminder comes after several recent incidents where foreigners sparked controversy by organizing events without authorization.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are not affected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial events, it's advisable to check whether they comply with Indonesian visa and regulatory requirements in advance.
