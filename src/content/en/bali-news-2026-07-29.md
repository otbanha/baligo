# Key Topics and News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, several new developments in weather, safety management, and sustainability are worth noting for visitors. Here are the top 5 travel news stories to watch today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) reports that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali. Coastal and marine activities require extra caution.

**Traveler Tips:** If you're planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, double-check ferry schedules and weather updates before heading out. For mountain areas, it's advisable to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique, Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft case involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal procedures.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, please adhere to local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to communicate with the shop owner or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight that could disrupt your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill waste through modern waste treatment technology while addressing the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, with the potential to improve the overall quality of the tourism environment in the future.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, consider reducing the use of single-use plastics and follow waste sorting guidelines at your accommodation and attractions to help preserve Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded again that foreigners planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits and cannot engage in activities inconsistent with their tourist visa purposes. This reminder comes after several recent incidents where foreigners hosted events that sparked controversy.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are not affected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial events, it is advisable to check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa and related regulations.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Viral Incidents Have Not Affected Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent high-profile social incidents, the Bali Provincial Government has stated that overall public safety and tourism order remain stable. Authorities have instructed police and relevant agencies to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to be overly concerned about isolated events.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, simply maintain general safety awareness—such as safeguarding your belongings, following traffic rules, and keeping an eye on official announcements. Most visitors can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
