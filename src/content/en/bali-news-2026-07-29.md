# Hot Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 29, 2026)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new updates on weather, safety management, and sustainable development that travelers should note. Here are the 5 most noteworthy travel news stories today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Reminder: Bali Still Expects Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves in the Coming Days

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) has stated that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves to parts of Bali in the coming days. Extra caution is advised, especially for coastal and marine activities.

**Traveler Advice:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or join sea excursions like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, please reconfirm ferry schedules and weather information before heading out. For mountain attractions, it's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident Reported at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, must comply with legal procedures.

**Traveler Advice:** When shopping in Bali, adhere to local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, first communicate with the shop or seek police assistance to avoid any oversight affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially initiated a Waste-to-Energy (WtE) project, aiming to reduce landfill waste through modern processing technology while addressing long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, expected to improve overall environmental quality for visitors.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, help by reducing single-use plastic consumption and sorting waste according to accommodation and attraction guidelines to preserve Bali's environment.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded that foreign nationals planning to hold commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain proper permits and cannot use tourist visas for activities inconsistent with their visa purpose. This reminder follows recent controversies involving unauthorized events by foreigners.

**Traveler Advice:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are unaffected. If you plan to host public classes, workshops, runs, or commercial activities, verify compliance with Indonesian visa and regulatory requirements in advance.

***

## 🏖️ Bali Provincial Government: Recent Online Controversies Have Not Affected Overall Travel Safety

In response to several recent social incidents that have drawn public attention, the Bali Provincial Government has stated that overall public security and tourism order remain stable. Authorities have instructed the police and relevant agencies to continue strengthening patrols and management in popular tourist areas, urging travelers not to be overly concerned about isolated incidents.

**Traveler Advice:** During your trip, simply maintain general safety awareness—such as safeguarding your belongings, following traffic rules, and keeping an eye on official announcements. Most visitors can still enjoy their vacation with peace of mind.
