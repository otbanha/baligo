# Hot Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers (2026/7/29)

As Bali continues to welcome the peak summer travel season, there are several new developments in weather, safety management, and sustainability that travelers should note. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth following today.

***

## 🌧️ BMKG Advisory: Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Higher Waves Expected in Bali

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) has warned that although the dry season has begun, atmospheric conditions may still bring brief heavy rain, strong gusts, and higher waves in parts of Bali, particularly along the coast and for marine activities.

**Traveler Tips:** If you're planning trips to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or activities like snorkeling, diving, or dolphin watching, be sure to check ferry schedules and weather updates before heading out. For mountain attractions, it's advisable to bring a lightweight raincoat.

***

## 🚨 Theft Incident at Ubud Boutique; Police Remind Travelers to Follow Local Laws

A boutique yoga apparel store in Ubud recently reported a suspected theft involving a foreign tourist, who is now under police investigation. This incident serves as a reminder that Indonesia enforces strict laws against criminal offenses like theft, and all individuals, regardless of nationality, are subject to legal proceedings.

**Traveler's Tip:** When shopping in Bali, always follow local laws and store policies. If a dispute arises, try to resolve it with the shop or seek police assistance to avoid any mishaps affecting your trip.

***

## ♻️ Bali Launches Waste-to-Energy Project to Enhance Tourism Environment

Bali has officially launched a Waste-to-Energy initiative aimed at reducing landfill use through modern waste processing technology, while also tackling the long-standing garbage issues in tourist areas. This is a key step in Bali's push for sustainable tourism, promising to improve the overall quality of the travel environment in the future.

**Traveler's Tip:** During your trip, consider reducing your use of single-use plastics and follow waste sorting rules at your accommodation and attractions to help keep Bali's environment pristine.

***

## 📋 Immigration Reiterates: Foreigners Must Not Host Events Without Permission

Bali's immigration authorities have reminded foreign nationals that anyone planning to host commercial, public, or community events in Indonesia must obtain the necessary permits. Tourist visas cannot be used for activities that fall outside their intended purpose. This reminder comes after several recent incidents where foreigners sparked controversy by organizing events without authorization.

**Traveler's Tip:** General tourism, attending legal events, or private gatherings are not affected. However, if you plan to host public classes, workshops, races, or commercial activities, it's advisable to check in advance whether they comply with Indonesian visa regulations and relevant laws.
