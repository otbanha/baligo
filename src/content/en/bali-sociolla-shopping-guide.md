Living in Indonesia, friends often ask me: 'Where should I buy skincare?' My answer is always Sociolla. It's Indonesia's most iconic beauty retail chain, offering everything from affordable local brands to popular Korean labels. Before heading home, I always recommend stopping here for a restock.

## Why I Love Shopping at Sociolla

Products are officially labeled as authentic and certified by Indonesia's BPOM.

Click & Collect service is available.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794051086295-15527.jpg)

The store uses electronic price tags, so you can check prices, promotions, and reviews on the spot without having to look up your phone.

## 🇰🇷 Popular Korean Picks

### 💗 Laneige Lip Sleeping Mask (Berry)

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794045158147-15524.webp)

I use this as an essential nighttime treatment after sun exposure. Apply a thick layer before bed, and your lips will feel soft and hydrated the next morning.

### 🤍 Beauty of Joseon Dynasty Cream

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794050746322-15525.webp)

The texture is quite thick and a bit sticky, but it absorbs quickly after spreading, leaving a natural dewy finish with a subtle floral and herbal scent. I use it as the last step in my skincare routine, gently patting it onto my face. For those with oily skin, a small amount is recommended.

## 🇮🇩 Indonesian Local Brand

### 🍯 Glad2Glow Propolis Hyaluronic Acid Barrier Essence Toner

#### Benefits (Propolis Hyaluronic Acid Barrier Essence Toner)

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794051567330-15528.jpg)

- Propolis helps soothe redness and care for acne-prone skin.
- Contains 4 types of hyaluronic acid, which the brand says quickly hydrates and improves dryness and tightness.
- Paired with ceramides and centella asiatica to help strengthen the skin barrier.

#### Simple Usage

1. After cleansing, pour an appropriate amount into your palm.
2. Gently pat all over your face until absorbed.
3. For extra hydration, you can also soak a cotton pad and use it as a mask.

### ☀️ Azarine Hydrasoothe Sunscreen Gel SPF45 PA++++

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794051900359-15529.jpg)

#### Benefits

- SPF45 protects against UVB, PA++++ protects against UVA.
- Watery gel texture, refreshing with a cooling sensation, leaves no white residue.
- Contains aloe vera, green tea, and bee propolis for a hydrating feel.

#### Simple Usage

1. Apply as the last step of your skincare routine.
2. Take two finger-length amounts and spread evenly over your face.
3. Use before going out, reapply every 3 to 4 hours.

**Tip**: Some users report pilling under thick foundation, so it's best used alone after skincare or test with a small amount first.

### 🌿 Somethinc 5% Niacinamide + Moisture Sabi Beet Serum

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794051994979-15530.webp)

#### Benefits

- Contains 5% niacinamide and 72.5% centella asiatica water to help brighten dull skin.
- Improves rough, uneven skin texture.
- Contains beet and vitamin E for a hydrating, smooth feel.
- Fragrance-free.

#### Simple Usage

1. Use after cleansing and toner, morning and evening.
2. Apply an appropriate amount and gently pat over the entire face; wait for absorption before applying lotion or cream.
3. Avoid layering with AHA, BHA, or PHA exfoliating products on the same day; use them on alternate days.

### 🌙 Avoskin Miraculous Retinol Toner

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794052079070-15531.jpg)

#### Benefits

- Contains 1% Actosome Retinol to help smooth and firm the skin.
- Combined with niacinamide, peptides, and vitamin E for both brightening and skincare.
- Gentle texture, user reviews say it's suitable for those new to retinol.

#### Simple Usage

1. Recommended for nighttime use; after washing your face, apply an appropriate amount to your entire face.
2. For first-time use, test on a small area first, starting with once a week, then gradually increase frequency.
3. Be sure to apply sunscreen the next morning.

Seminyak: Seminyak Village

Denpasar: Living World Mall, Level 21 Mall, Plaza Renon

Next time you're in Bali, consider adding Sociolla to your itinerary—stock up on all your skincare essentials before heading home.
