## Bali November 16 Weather Outlook: Rainfall Predicted in Many Areas, Watch for Showers in Tabanan and Bangli

The latest forecast from Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) indicates that several regencies in Bali may experience rainfall on November 16, 2026, with the region still in a rainy season pattern.

According to BMKG's current regency forecasts:

| Area | November 16 Forecast |
| --- | --- |
| Denpasar | Cloudy |
| Badung | Light rain |
| Gianyar | Light rain |
| Klungkung | Cloudy |
| Tabanan | Light to moderate rain |
| Bangli | Light rain |
| Karangasem | Light rain |
| Buleleng | Light rain |
| Jembrana | Light rain |

Temperatures across the island are forecast to range from 19–32°C, with relative humidity potentially reaching up to 99%.

In terms of sea conditions, BMKG predicts wave heights of approximately 0.5–1.25 meters in northern Bali waters and 1–2 meters in southern waters. Sea conditions should still be confirmed with port and ferry company announcements on the day of departure.

For travelers, if you have plans for Ubud, Bedugul, Jatiluwih Rice Terraces, or waterfall spots tomorrow, it's advisable to allow for weather flexibility. The rainy season doesn't mean all-day rain, but afternoon outdoor activities may be affected by localized showers.

If you're heading to offshore islands like Nusa Penida or Nusa Lembongan, it's also recommended to check sea conditions and ferry schedules before departing.

Source: BMKG Bali Regency Forecast and BBMKG Region III.

## **Sanur Electric Shuttle Bus Introduces QRIS Tap! Promotional Fare of 68 IDR from November 15 to 30**

Bali's digital payment applications are now extending to local tourism transportation.

On November 14, during the Baligivation 2026 event in Sanur, the Bank Indonesia Bali branch officially introduced QRIS and QRIS Tap payment functionality to the electric shuttle buses operated by Desa Adat Intaran.

This service primarily operates within Sanur's local tourist areas, allowing residents and visitors to pay for transportation digitally.

Current shuttle bus details are as follows:

- Operator: Desa Adat Intaran
- Number of vehicles: 12
- Capacity per vehicle: Up to 10 passengers
- Regular fare: 5,000 IDR per person
- Payment methods: QRIS and QRIS Tap

To promote digital transactions, Indonesian banks and the Bali government have launched a limited-time promotion:

Promotion period: November 15–30

Special price per person per trip: Rp68

This promotional price symbolizes the 68th anniversary of the Bali provincial government and is subject to daily quotas.

For travelers, this initiative marks a new step in digitizing local shuttle transportation in Sanur. However, actual routes, daily quotas, and payment support details should be confirmed on-site.

## Bali and Japan Deepen Cooperation! Focus on Tourism, Investment, and Youth Employment in Japan

Bali Governor Wayan Koster aims to strengthen collaboration between Bali and Japan, advancing exchanges from diplomatic and cultural levels to more concrete economic and social partnerships.

Koster's proposed areas of cooperation include:

- Tourism industry cooperation
- Japanese corporate investment in Bali
- Balinese youth working in Japan
- Technical and vocational skills training
- Strengthening exchanges between local governments and Japanese companies

The governor specifically noted that graduates from Bali's vocational high schools (SMK) who possess skills aligned with Japanese market demands could pursue more overseas job opportunities in the future.

Koster also mentioned that some Balinese youth currently working in Japan have given positive feedback on their work environment and income.

However, this discussion primarily outlined directions for cooperation, without announcing any new formal employment plans, recruitment quotas, or specific investment agreements.

For Bali, Japan is not only a key international tourism market but also a potential important partner for future talent development and industry collaboration.

## **Bangli Holds I Gusti Ngurah Rai Hero Memorial Flag Procession! To Be Handed Over to Gianyar Tomorrow**

Bangli Regency in Bali held a procession on November 15 to commemorate Indonesian national hero I Gusti Ngurah Rai, featuring flags and historical documents, as part of the Hari Pahlawan (Heroes' Day) observances on November 10.

Organized by the Bangli Regency government, the procession began with the handover of the Pataka flag and Ngurah Rai historical documents from Klungkung Regency, followed by a ceremony at Alun-Alun Bangli in downtown Bangli.

The procession then passed through several areas in Bangli, including:

- Peninjoan
- Tembuku
- Suter
- Manikliyu
- Landih
- Pengotan
- Kintamani
- Susut
- Penglipuran Hero Cemetery (TMP Penglipuran)

Bangli Regent Sang Nyoman Sedana Arta expressed hope that this event will help the younger generation learn about the history of I Gusti Ngurah Rai and Bali's role in Indonesia's independence movement.

The procession will continue on November 16 and be handed over to the Gianyar Regency government.

This event is a local historical commemoration ceremony, not a tourist-oriented performance. If travelers encounter the procession on certain roads in Bangli or Gianyar tomorrow, they may need to anticipate brief traffic disruptions.
