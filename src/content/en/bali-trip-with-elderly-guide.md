**Key Takeaways**: Practical tips for traveling to Bali with seniors—① Complete the health QR code, e-visa, and customs declaration online before arrival (now integrated into the All Indonesia entry card for one-stop processing); ② **Hire a private driver for the entire trip**, communicate the itinerary with the driver in English via LINE, and arrange a Chinese-speaking guide before departure; ③ Focus the itinerary on **Ubud's mountain and cultural experiences**, keep a slow pace, schedule ample rest, and only move to a southern beach hotel at the end for relaxation before heading to the airport.

> The following content is adapted from a travelogue shared by group member [Claire Chen](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/17yjQ17JHi/):

On this Bali trip with my parents, the main focus was on mountain scenery and local culture, so we spent most of our time in the Ubud area. Only toward the end of the trip did we gradually move toward the airport, choosing to relax at a hotel near the southern beaches. The itinerary covered several must-visit attractions, and I deliberately planned relaxing activities and ample rest time for my parents.

## 【Preparing for Bali Entry】

The entry process for Bali can actually be prepared before departure. There are many online guides for the application process. I've organized the steps after clearing customs for your reference:

**First Step: Health QR Code Scan**

- Register your health QR code in advance here: [Click to Register](https://sshp.kemkes.go.id/register?travel_mode=Air)

**Step 2: Apply for a Visa in Advance to Skip On-Site Queues**

- Applying for a tourist visa early lets you bypass the on-site queuing process: [Visa Application](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/)
- You can also submit the traveler responsibility declaration at the same time: [Love Bali](https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id/)

**Step 3: Customs Declaration with Departure QR Code**

- This step can be completed before departure: [Customs Declaration Portal](https://ecd.beacukai.go.id/)

With these preparations in place, your arrival process in Bali will be much smoother, saving you the time of filling out forms and waiting in line. If you're still unclear about the process, check out the detailed guide here. ## 【Tips for Communicating About Private Car Hire and Itinerary Planning】

For this private car hire arrangement, initial communication with the driver was done in English via LINE. As the departure date approached, we also invited a Chinese-speaking guide named Ani to assist with our itinerary. When discussing with your driver, it's helpful to provide the following information in advance so they can better assist with planning:

![（Claire Chen提供）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_696ef9532aca81d419e61d7f96d61b1b.jpg)

(Provided by Claire Chen)

1. **Basic Information**
2. - **Number of travel days**, **hotel locations**, **flight details**, and **group size**, so the driver can help plan the route and arrange pickups.
3. **Itinerary Discussion Mode**
4. - **Preliminary Planning**: This is the approach I used. First, I browsed popular Bali itineraries in the community, sketched out a rough travel framework, and booked hotels separately. Then, I shared the accommodation info and initial itinerary with the driver for confirmation and a quote.

- **Full Delegation to the Guide**: Ideal for those without a specific plan. Just provide **travel days**, **preferences**, and **group characteristics**, such as the age range of participants, and the guide can tailor the arrangements accordingly.

5. **Itinerary Preferences**
6. - **Travel Companions' Age Group**: Different combinations—such as families with kids, seniors, or friends—can affect itinerary preferences, so it's helpful to inform your driver and guide in advance.

- **Special Itinerary Requests**: For example, mountain activities (like relaxing, hiking, or a sunrise volcano tour) or water activities (such as snorkeling, diving, or river tubing). This information helps the guide tailor suitable experiences along the way.
- **Dining Needs**: If you have specific food preferences (e.g., craving local cuisine or unique restaurants), let your guide know ahead of time so they can recommend convenient spots along the route.

7. **Language Options**
   Our private drivers primarily offer English service. If you're comfortable communicating in English, there's no need to hire a separate Chinese-speaking guide. On my trip, the first and last days were relaxed, so I just went with the driver. For the middle days with longer distances, I hired a Chinese-speaking guide named Ani to accompany me throughout. During pre-trip communication, you can also flexibly use ChatGPT or Google Translate to ensure both sides clearly understand each other's needs.

With this level of preparation and communication, your private car tour will run more smoothly, better align with your travel needs, and deliver a fulfilling Bali experience.

## 【Recommended Private Car and Guide Services for First-Time Bali Travelers】

This is my first time writing a recommendation, especially for those visiting a Southeast Asian island for the first time, particularly travelers who want to skip the hassle of detailed itinerary planning. The local drivers and guides in Bali were incredibly helpful, making the entire trip easy and enjoyable. Here are some of my tips and insights:

1. **SIM Card and Currency Exchange Needs**
   If you need a local SIM card or currency exchange, you can always let your driver or guide know. I had already taken care of these details in Taiwan, so I could start the trip right upon arrival.
2. **Itinerary Planning Tips**
   Some locations in Bali are quite far apart by car, so it's best to prioritize accommodation when planning your itinerary to avoid unnecessary back-and-forth. Arranging your schedule around your stay makes the trip more relaxed and pleasant.
3. **Activity Needs**
   If you have specific day trips or other activities in mind, you can discuss them with your driver or guide. They will help arrange everything smoothly, so you just need to follow along without worrying about the details.
4. **Transparent Pricing**
   The private car fees for this trip were transparent, adjusted based on the daily route, and settled each day. Entrance fees and additional day trip costs were calculated separately and could be discussed and confirmed before departure.
5. **Thoughtful Company of Chinese-Speaking Guide Ani**
   On the itineraries where Ani accompanied us, she stayed with us throughout and introduced Bali's culture, history, and customs along the way. This companionship was especially valuable for me and my elderly family members! Additionally, if we wanted to buy souvenirs, Ani would take us directly to purchase them or buy them on our behalf based on photos we provided. It's best to let her know in advance so she can arrange it properly.
6. **Helpful Communication and Photography**
   Ani and the driver not only helped take photos throughout the trip but also assisted with communicating accommodation details at the hotel front desk, so we didn't have to worry about language barriers.
7. **Local Traffic and Driver Skills**
   Traffic in Bali can be congested at times, especially in the city, but the driver's skills were very steady, making us feel safe even on mountain roads. We were also lucky to encounter almost no traffic jams during this trip.
8. **Companionship Like Family**
   Throughout the journey, the care from the driver and guide felt like being with family or friends. It not only made the trip smoother but also brought immense ease. I'm grateful for their company and care during this journey.
