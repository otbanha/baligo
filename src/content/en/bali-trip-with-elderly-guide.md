**Key Takeaways**: Proven tips for traveling to Bali with seniors—① Complete the health QR code, e-visa, and customs declaration online before arrival (now integrated into the All Indonesia entry card for one-stop processing); ② **Hire a private car for the entire trip**, communicate the itinerary with the driver in English via LINE, and arrange a Chinese-speaking guide before departure; ③ Focus the itinerary on **Ubud's mountain culture**, slow down the pace, schedule ample rest, and only move to a southern beach hotel at the end for relaxation before heading to the airport.

> The following content is adapted from a travelogue shared by group member [Claire Chen](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/17yjQ17JHi/):

On this Bali trip with my elderly parents, the main focus was on mountain scenery and local culture, so we spent most of our time in the Ubud area. Only toward the end of the trip did we gradually move toward the airport, choosing to relax for a while at a hotel near the southern beach. This itinerary covered several must-visit attractions, and I deliberately planned relaxing activities and ample rest time for the seniors.

## 【Bali Entry Preparation】

The entry process for Bali can actually be prepared before departure. There are many online guides for the application process. I've organized the steps after clearing customs as follows for your reference:

**First Step: Health QR Code Scan**

- Register your health QR code in advance here: [Click to Register](https://sshp.kemkes.go.id/register?travel_mode=Air)

**Step 2: Apply for a Visa in Advance to Skip On-Site Queues**

- Applying for a tourist visa early lets you bypass the on-site queuing process: [Visa Application](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/)
- You can also submit a traveler responsibility declaration at the same time: [Love Bali](https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id/)

**Step 3: Outbound QR Code Customs Declaration**

- This step can be completed before departure: [Customs Declaration Portal](https://ecd.beacukai.go.id/)

With these preparations in place, your arrival process in Bali will be much smoother, saving you the hassle of filling out forms and waiting in line on the spot. If you're still unclear about the process, check out the detailed guide here. ## 【Tips for Communicating About Private Driver and Itinerary Planning】

For this trip, the private driver arrangement was initially discussed in English via LINE. Closer to departure, we also invited a Chinese-speaking guide named Ani to assist with our itinerary. When discussing with your private driver, it's recommended to provide the following information in advance to help them better coordinate:

![（Claire Chen提供）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_696ef9532aca81d419e61d7f96d61b1b.jpg)

（Provided by Claire Chen）

1. **Basic Information**
2. - **Number of travel days**, **hotel locations**, **flight details**, and **group size**, so the driver can help plan routes and arrange pickups.
3. **Itinerary Discussion Approach**
4. - **Preliminary Planning**: This is the method I used. First, I browsed popular Bali itinerary ideas within the community, sketched out a rough travel framework, and booked hotels separately. Then, I shared the accommodation details and a preliminary itinerary with the driver for confirmation and a quote.

- **Leave it entirely to the guide**: Ideal for those without a specific plan. Just provide your **trip length**, **preferences**, and **group details**—such as the age range of participants—and the guide will tailor the arrangements accordingly.

5. **Itinerary Preferences**
6. - **Travel Companions' Age Group**: Different combinations like families with kids, seniors, or friends can influence itinerary preferences. Let the driver and guide know in advance.

- **Special Activity Requests**: For example, mountain excursions (like relaxing, hiking, or sunrise volcano tours) or water activities (such as snorkeling, diving, or river rafting). This information helps the guide incorporate suitable experiences into the itinerary.
- **Dining Needs**: If you have specific food preferences (e.g., wanting to try local cuisine or unique restaurants), inform the guide ahead of time so they can recommend restaurants along the route.

7. **Language Choice**
   Our private car drivers primarily offer English service. If you're confident in English communication, there's no need to hire a Chinese-speaking guide. On my trip, the first and last days were relaxed, so the driver sufficed; for the middle days with longer distances, I hired a Chinese-speaking guide named Ani for full-day support. During pre-trip communication, you can flexibly use ChatGPT or Google Translate to ensure both sides understand each other's needs.

With this level of preparation and communication, a private car trip will go more smoothly, better aligning with your travel needs and delivering a fulfilling Bali experience.

## 【Recommended Private Driver and Guide Services for First-Time Bali Travelers】

This is my first time writing a recommendation, especially for those visiting a Southeast Asian island destination for the first time, particularly travelers who want to skip the hassle of detailed itinerary planning. The local drivers and guides in Bali were incredibly helpful, making the entire trip relaxed and enjoyable. Here are some tips and insights:

1. **SIM Card and Currency Exchange Needs**
   If you need a local SIM card or currency exchange, you can let your driver or guide know anytime. I had already taken care of these details back in Taiwan, so I could start the trip right upon arrival.
2. **Itinerary Planning Tips**
   Travel times between some locations in Bali can be long, so it's best to prioritize accommodation locations when planning your itinerary to avoid unnecessary back-and-forth. Arranging your itinerary around your stay can make the trip much more relaxing.
3. **Activity Needs**
   If you have specific day trips or other activities in mind, you can discuss them with your driver or guide. They'll help arrange everything smoothly, so you can just follow along without worrying about the details.
4. **Transparent Pricing**
   The private driver fees for this trip were transparent, adjusted based on the daily route, and settled each day. Entrance fees and additional day trip costs were calculated separately and could be discussed and confirmed before the trip.
5. **Thoughtful Company of Chinese-Speaking Guide Ani**
   On the itineraries Ani accompanied, she stayed with us throughout and introduced Bali's culture, history, and customs along the way. This companionship was invaluable for me and my elderly family members! Also, if we wanted to buy souvenirs, Ani would take us directly to purchase them or buy them on our behalf based on photos we provided. It's best to let her know in advance so she can arrange it properly.
6. **Helpful Communication and Photography**
   Ani and the driver not only helped take photos throughout the trip but also assisted with communicating accommodation details with hotel front desks, so we didn't have to worry about language barriers.
7. **Local Traffic and Driver Skills**
   Traffic in Bali can be congested at times, especially in the city, but the driver's skills were very steady, making us feel safe even on mountain roads. We were also lucky to encounter almost no traffic jams during this trip.
8. **Companionship Like Family**
   Throughout the journey, the driver and guide's care felt like that of family or friends, not only making the trip smoother but also bringing immense ease. Grateful for their company and support on this trip.
