## 🏖️ Diamond Beach Suddenly Fully Closed! Popular Nusa Penida Attraction Suspends Operations

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_ca4724a8eeb3ab1c2e10fa29582c9546.jpg)

If you have plans for Nusa Penida soon, take note of this.

The Klungkung Regency government announced today that **Diamond Beach is temporarily fully closed** until local tourism operators complete the necessary permit procedures.

Authorities stated that some of the attraction's tourism facilities and operating permits are still incomplete, so all tourist activities have been suspended.

Located on the southeastern side of Nusa Penida, Diamond Beach is one of the island's most famous spots, known for its white sand, massive rock formations, and stunning coastal views.

This is not a full closure of Nusa Penida Island, but rather **Diamond Beach itself suspending tourist activities**.

So if you've recently joined a Nusa Penida day trip and the itinerary originally listed Diamond Beach, it's best to confirm with your travel agency or driver whether the spot has already been changed.

**Atuh Beach is still open**, but the situation may change depending on official updates.

## 🌊 Body of Polish Tourist Swept Away by Waves Found! Jembrana, Bali, Again Highlights Beach Safety

A 44-year-old Polish tourist was recently swept away by waves at **Tembles Beach, Jembrana** in western Bali, with search efforts continuing until today.

Bali Basarnas confirmed the body was found today, discovered at nearby **Air Kuning Beach**.

This incident serves as another reminder that calm-looking waters on Bali's beaches don't necessarily mean safety.

Especially along parts of the west and south coasts, strong currents, wave conditions, and tides can create dangerous rip currents at certain spots.

If you see:

**Red flags, warning signs, or local lifeguards telling you not to enter the water, never ignore them just for a photo or a swim.**

These incidents are usually not because tourists 'can't swim,' but because they underestimate the power of ocean currents.

## 🇺🇸 Bali Police Strengthen Cooperation with U.S. Consular Security Officials! Focus on Tourist Area Safety

Bali Police today met with the **Regional Security Officer (RSO) of the U.S. Consulate General in Surabaya** to discuss tourism safety and security conditions in Bali.

Polda Bali stated that since Bali is an international tourist destination, including a large number of American visitors, both sides will continue to enhance security information exchange and coordination.

Bali Police are also deploying personnel through **Ditpamobvit** at certain tourist attractions and key facilities, setting up police posts to prevent crime and boost tourists' sense of safety.

This news does not mean that Bali is suddenly facing a major security threat.

A more accurate interpretation is:

**Bali is strengthening its security coordination mechanism with foreign embassies and consulates.**

For the average traveler, it's business as usual—no need to change your itinerary.

## ✈️ AirAsia Partners with Pegasus! More Options from Bali to Europe

AirAsia has announced its first codeshare partnership with Turkish low-cost carrier **Pegasus Airlines**, further expanding air connectivity between Asia and Europe.

The partnership was announced on September 2, with Bali highlighted as a key tourism market in this international aviation network expansion.

For travelers heading from Taiwan to Bali, this doesn't necessarily mean direct flights from Bali to Europe will start tomorrow.

What's truly noteworthy is:

**In the future, AirAsia's network combined with the codeshare will offer more transit options from Southeast Asia to Europe.**

As Bali remains a key tourism market for AirAsia, the ongoing expansion of its international route network also helps sustain the flow of international visitors to Bali.

## 🏃 Bali United's Home Opener Tomorrow! New Season Kicks Off Against PSS Sleman

If you happen to be in Bali tomorrow evening and love football, this match is worth noting.

**Bali United** will face **PSS Sleman** at **Kapten I Wayan Dipta Stadium** in Gianyar on **September 4 at 20:00 WITA**, marking the opening match of the 2026/27 Super League season.

This isn't your typical tourist activity, but for travelers looking to experience local life in Bali, watching an Indonesian professional football match offers a rare and unique travel experience.

If you're staying around Ubud or Gianyar, consider adding this match to your itinerary for tomorrow evening.

However, expect increased traffic and crowds around the stadium on match day—**allow extra time if you're driving or taking a private car to the venue.**
