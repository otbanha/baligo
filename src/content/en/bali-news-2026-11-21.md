## 🌦️ BMKG Forecasts Cloudy Skies on November 21! Central-Southern and Eastern Areas Watch for Thunderstorms, Southern Sea Waves May Reach 2 Meters

The Indonesian Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) Bali Region III Meteorological Center has released the latest forecast for November 21, indicating mostly cloudy conditions across Bali, but central, southern, and eastern areas should still be alert for moderate to heavy rain and lightning.

**Overall Weather Overview:**

| Item | November 21 Forecast |
| --- | --- |
| Weather | Cloudy, localized rain |
| Temperature | 20–34°C |
| Relative Humidity | 60–95% |
| Wind Direction | East to south winds |

BMKG specifically warns that central, southern, and eastern areas may experience moderate to heavy rain accompanied by lightning. Southern seas also have the potential for waves reaching 2 meters or higher.

For regional forecasts, Gianyar may see moderate rainfall, while Badung, Tabanan, Denpasar, and other areas should still watch for localized rain.

**Impact on Travelers:**

- In areas like Ubud, Kintamani, and Bedugul, it's advisable to bring rain gear.
- For waterfalls, rice terraces, and mountain attractions, check the weather and on-site conditions before heading out.
- Travelers heading to Nusa Penida or Nusa Lembongan should confirm the latest sea conditions and ferry schedules with the boat company.
- For beach activities and water sports, always follow on-site safety instructions—don't assume the shore looks calm and ignore the sea conditions.

There is currently no island-wide closure of attractions, so travelers don't need to cancel all outdoor activities just because the rainy season has arrived. However, it's wise to keep your itinerary flexible.

## 🏖️ Large Amounts of Marine Debris Wash Ashore at Kuta Beach! Badung Has Cleared 200 Tons Since November 5

The southwest coast of Bali is now being affected by the west monsoon season. Recently, large amounts of trash carried by ocean currents have washed ashore at Kuta and other beaches in Badung, prompting local cleaning crews to step up their efforts.

According to a report by detikBali on November 20, the Badung Regency Environment and Sanitation Agency (DLHK) has removed approximately 200 tons of marine debris from various coastal areas since November 5, 2026.

Authorities noted that the increase in debris is linked to the West Wind Season (Musim Angin Barat) and changes in ocean currents.

The waste currently being cleaned up mainly includes:

- Large logs and branches
- Bamboo and leaves
- Organic matter drifting in from the sea
- Plastic bottles and single-use packaging

To speed up cleanup, Badung Regency has divided the coastline into five cleaning zones, deploying 20 garbage trucks and 2 large loaders to transport the marine debris away.

Wood waste is sent to the Mengwitani waste processing facility for shredding, while other residual waste is taken to the Suwung regional landfill.

**Impact on Travelers:**

This is a common seasonal phenomenon around the west coast of Bali before and after the rainy season, particularly affecting Kuta, Legian, and nearby beaches due to drifting marine debris.

If you're planning to stroll along Kuta Beach, take sunset photos, or enjoy seaside activities in the coming days, don't assume all parts of the beach will be clean.

This doesn't mean Kuta Beach is fully closed, but some sections may have accumulated trash and cleaning vehicles in operation. Actual conditions vary with ocean currents and cleanup progress.

## ✈️ Qatar's Ambassador Proposes Adding 2 to 3 Daily Direct Flights to Bali! Qatar Airways May Expand Capacity

Air connectivity between Bali and the Middle East could be further strengthened.

Qatar's Ambassador to Indonesia, H.E. Dr. Sultan bin Mubarak Saad Al-Dosari, met with Bali Governor Wayan Koster on November 19 to propose increasing the frequency of Qatar Airways' direct flights to Bali, aiming to attract more travelers from Qatar and the Middle East.

The ambassador noted that Bali holds strong appeal in the Qatari and Middle Eastern tourism markets, but as traveler demand grows, air capacity needs to be enhanced.

His proposed idea:

**Add 2 to 3 direct flights per day.**

The ambassador also stated that coordination with the Indonesian Ministry of Transportation has been made on related matters, and expressed hope to continue communication with the Bali government.

Bali Governor Wayan Koster expressed support for the proposal, noting that increasing international flights would help boost tourism connectivity and economic cooperation.

This news is also noteworthy for travelers from Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Singapore. Middle Eastern routes not only serve local passengers but also serve as important transit hubs for some European travelers heading to Bali.

However, this remains a proposal to increase flights, not a confirmed expansion plan. No official schedule, ticket prices, or actual operation dates for the new routes have been announced yet.

The event is co-organized by the Ubud Hotel Association and PHRI Gianyar, with the main venue located at:

Central Parking, Monkey Forest Ubud

**Two-Day Event Schedule:**

| Date | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| November 21 (Saturday) | 21K Cross Country, Squad Race, Kids Dash |
| November 22 (Sunday) | 5K, 10K |

The 21K trail run weaves through Ubud's surrounding rice fields and natural landscapes, offering a distinct experience from typical city road races.

The event is more than just a competition—it aims to promote healthy living, local culture, and community engagement, allowing participants to discover Ubud from a different perspective.

**For travelers heading to Ubud this weekend:**
