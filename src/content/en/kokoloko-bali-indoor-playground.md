# A Cool Place to Let Kids Burn Off Energy in Kuta

An indoor children's adventure playground called KOKOLOKO has opened at Mal Bali Galeria in Kuta, Kuta, created by the team behind the major family entertainment brand Timezone. The playground soft-launched in June 2026 and officially opened in August, featuring **over 25 rides and attractions**, ideal for kids aged 2 to 12.

The story follows three friends: Koko the kangaroo, Pip the possum, and Ozzy the koala. They set off from Perth in a camper van to travel the world, and kids can follow them from one station to the next within the playground.

## What to Do in the 6 City-Themed Zones

The playground is divided into 6 city-themed zones, with activities summarized as follows:

| Themed Zone | Activities |
| --- | --- |
| Tokyo | Drive kids' go-karts in a neon city setting |
| London | Ride a mini train through a themed town, with role-play at a fire station, clinic, farm, and supermarket |
| Bali | Kuta Krafts workshop for hands-on crafts, sand play, scooter riding, and beach-themed games |
| Perth | Giant treehouse, climbing, slides, swaying rope bridges, and ninja obstacle challenges |
| Seoul | K-Pop stage performances, arcade games, racing simulators, large slides, and a dress-up area |
| Dubai | Trampolines and large obstacle courses |

Active kids can head straight to the Perth and Dubai zones. Those who enjoy pretend play or crafts will prefer the London and Bali zones. The Kuta Krafts workshop lets kids create and color their own souvenirs to take home.

## Location and Opening Hours

- Location: 2nd Floor, Mal Bali Galeria, next to Timezone (MBG Extension of the mall)
- Address: Jl. Bypass Ngurah Rai, Kuta, Badung, Bali 80361
- Opening Hours: Daily 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM

## Rules to Know Before Entering

The following is compiled from the official FAQ and entry terms.

- **Socks**: Socks must be worn at all times. Children need non-slip socks, and adults cannot go barefoot. You can bring your own or purchase them on-site.
- **Adult Supervision**: Children under 6 must be accompanied by an adult at all times. The entry terms allow children aged 6 and above to play on their own after parents check in, but the FAQ states that adult supervision is required inside the venue. It's best to confirm on-site.
- **Consent Form**: Must be signed by a parent or guardian before entry.
- **Food & Drinks**: Outside food is not allowed. Baby food is permitted, and the entry terms also list baby milk and bottled water.
- **Entry & Exit**: It is recommended to arrive 10 minutes before your scheduled session. Tickets are for single entry only; you can purchase additional time on-site if you want to stay longer.
- **Rescheduling**: Tickets are non-refundable and non-transferable. To reschedule, you must contact the venue at least 24 hours in advance.
- **Health**: Do not enter if you have a cold, fever, or any infectious disease.

Some facilities have age or height restrictions; please refer to the on-site signage for details.
