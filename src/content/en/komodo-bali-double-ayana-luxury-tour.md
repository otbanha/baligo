## Komodo National Park: A Must-Visit at Least Once in a Lifetime

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784818112469-d2.jpg)

When it comes to Komodo, the most anticipated experience is undoubtedly seeing the world's only wild Komodo dragons.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784852852632-komodo-yang-ukurannya.jpg)

Witnessing these ancient giants, which have survived for millions of years, roaming freely in the national park is a thrill that no zoo can match. Exploring with a professional ranger ensures a safe and insightful understanding of their ecology and habits.

## Check In at the Dreamy Pink Beach

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784852530083-cha_sohyeon_470268978_2599201363597197_3141589152870947112_n.jpg)

One of the world's truly naturally formed pink sand beaches, Komodo's Pink Beach is a rare gem.

The fine pink sand paired with crystal-clear blue water creates a dreamy gradient under the sunlight—whether for photos or a leisurely stroll, it feels like stepping into a postcard, making it a must-visit beach on many travelers' bucket lists.

## Swim with Giant Manta Rays

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784852787912-263db4fdfa7702e1cb8cab55fd91e902.jpg)

The waters around Komodo offer world-class snorkeling conditions.

With glass-clear seas and vibrant coral reefs, the highlight is meeting graceful giant manta rays at Manta Point. Watching them glide effortlessly is one of the most unforgettable travel memories for many.

## Padar Island's Three-Colored Bays: Breathtaking Beauty

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784852741889-573872739_1243124247857585_1146304472999484845_n.jpg)

Many first discover Komodo through the iconic landscape of Padar Island.

From the viewpoint, you can take in three bays of different colors at once, set against a unique volcanic terrain—a world-class spectacle and Komodo's most photogenic landmark.
