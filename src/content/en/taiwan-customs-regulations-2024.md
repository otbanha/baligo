According to the latest regulations from the Ministry of Finance's Customs Administration, effective June 26, 2024, the duty-free allowance for luggage items carried by arriving passengers has been raised from NT$20,000 to NT$35,000 (approximately USD$645 to USD$1,129), allowing travelers to shop freely! But do you know which items are prohibited from being brought into Taiwan? Many people may not be aware, so to avoid having your items confiscated by customs, be sure to understand Taiwan's entry regulations—don't waste your money!

## Duty-Free Allowance Raised to NT$35,000 (Approx. USD$1,129)

The duty-free allowance for luggage items carried by arriving passengers has been raised from NT$20,000 to NT$35,000 (about USD$645 to USD$1,129) starting June 26, benefiting all nationals traveling abroad for summer shopping. Additionally, under the current 'Regulations for the Inspection and Taxation of Luggage Items Carried by Arriving Passengers,' each passenger is allowed a duty-free allowance of NT$20,000 (approx. USD$645) for personal or household luggage items, excluding controlled goods and tobacco/alcohol. Amounts exceeding this must be declared. However, this does not apply to those clearly engaging in commercial activities or frequent travelers with violation records.

## Taiwan Entry Regulations and Restrictions

![台灣入境規定限制](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_c1d6ac4830b9bdc7166a59c589adf9fb.png)

### Food Items

- Raw milk, fresh milk: Not allowed, including fresh milk roll cakes
- Fresh, chilled, frozen, or cooked vacuum-packed meats: Not allowed
- Fresh truffles: Not allowed
- Dried meat or meat floss: Prohibited regardless of packaging, including vacuum-sealed
- Foie gras: Not allowed in glass jars, but allowed in metal packaging
- Canned goods and instant noodles: Allowed
- Mentaiko (marinated cod roe) and salmon roe: Allowed
- Fish floss: Allowed
- Long-life milk, milk powder, cheese, and yogurt: Allowed
- Canned food (soft cans): Allowed, must indicate high-temperature sterilization
- Rice: Allowed, up to 1 kg
- Tea leaves: Allowed, up to 1 kg
- Dried shiitake mushrooms: Allowed, up to 1 kg
- Dried chili peppers: Allowed, up to 6 kg
- Cooked peanuts and garlic: Allowed, up to 1 kg
- Bird's nest: Allowed, market value must not exceed NT$20,000 (approx. USD$645); must declare if exceeding

### Cosmetics and Skincare

Non-medicated cosmetics (including hair dye, perms, sunscreen, and whitening products) such as face masks, eye creams, lip masks, toners, and serums: up to 12 bottles per type, with a total of no more than 36 bottles. If the total value of a single item exceeds NT$20,000 (approx. USD$645), it must be declared for tax.

### Contact Lenses

If you carry more than the following duty-free allowances for personal use, you must apply to the Ministry of Health and Welfare's Food and Drug Administration for a "Medical Device for Personal Use" permit and declare it to customs.

1. Daily disposable contact lenses: Up to 60 lenses per prescription strength, limited to 1 brand and 2 prescription strengths.
2. Corrective lenses: No more than 1 pair.

### Tobacco and Alcohol

Adult travelers aged 20 and above (starting January 1, 2023, the age limit for carrying alcohol upon entry is adjusted to 18, but the age for carrying tobacco remains 20) may bring tobacco and alcohol products within the following quantities. These limits include all tobacco and alcohol products in both carry-on and checked luggage (including items purchased at duty-free shops, on board, abroad, or brought out and back into the country). For more details, please refer to the Taipei Customs Office of the Ministry of Finance's "Tobacco and Alcohol" page.

＊Tobacco:

- Duty-free allowance (no declaration required): 200 cigarettes, or 25 cigars, or 1 pound of tobacco.
- Limited allowance (with declaration): 5 cartons of cigarettes (1,000 cigarettes), or 125 cigars, or 5 pounds of tobacco.

(★Tip: According to the Tobacco Hazards Prevention Act, all e-cigarettes and heated tobacco products that have not passed health risk assessment are completely banned from manufacturing, importing, selling, supplying, displaying, advertising, and using. When shopping abroad, remember that no matter the quantity, you cannot import or bring them into the country. Whether as gifts or for personal use, you risk penalties, and they cannot be used as a means of profit. If caught, according to Article 26 of the Tobacco Hazards Prevention Act, a maximum fine of NT$5 million (approximately USD$161,290) may be imposed, and the goods will not be returned and will be destroyed.)

►For related information, please visit the Health Promotion Administration's e-cigarette prevention section.

＊Alcohol:

- Duty-free allowance (no declaration required): up to 1 liter total
- Limited allowance (with declaration): up to 5 liters total (but alcohol from mainland China, which is not open for import, is limited to 1 liter)

►Duty-free allowance explanation:

1. Within duty-free limit: No customs declaration needed.
2. Exceeding duty-free limit: Must declare at the red channel; tobacco and alcohol products within the limited allowance can be released after inspection and payment of duties.

►Limited allowance explanation:

1. Within limited allowance: After deducting the duty-free portion, the remainder is released upon payment of duties.
2. Exceeding limited allowance: Must provide an import license for tobacco and alcohol, file an import declaration in accordance with Article 17 of the Customs Act under the importer's name, or arrange for the excess to be returned.

### Medications

For regulations on carrying personal medications upon entry, please refer to the following:

＊錠狀、膠囊狀食品：每種最多12瓶（盒、罐、包、袋），合計不超過36瓶（盒、罐、包、袋）。 ＊非處方西藥：每種最多12瓶（盒、罐、條、支），合計不超過36瓶（盒、罐、條、支）。 ＊一般處方西藥：依據是否攜帶醫師處方箋或證明文件限量（針劑產品須攜有處方箋或證明文件）：

1. Without documents: Limited to a 2-month supply.
2. With documents: Not exceeding the reasonable amount prescribed, with a maximum of a 6-month supply.

(★ For all three categories above, you must apply for a "[Personal Use Drug Import Permit](https://oap.fda.gov.tw/B101?type=2)" from the Taiwan Food and Drug Administration, and declare it to customs upon entry to bring it in.)

＊管制處方西藥：須攜帶醫師處方箋或證明文件，並以治療本人疾病者為限，不得超過所開立之用量，且以6個月用量為限。

(★ You must apply for a "[Declaration Form for Patients Carrying Controlled Drugs into and out of the Republic of China](https://www.fda.gov.tw/TC/siteContent.aspx?sid=196)" from the Taiwan Food and Drug Administration, and declare it to customs upon entry and exit.)

＊中藥：

1. Chinese medicinal herbs: Maximum 1 kg per type, with a total weight not exceeding 12 kg.
2. Oral Chinese medicine preparations: Maximum 12 bottles (boxes, cans, strips, tubes) per dosage form, with a total not exceeding 36 bottles (boxes, cans, strips, tubes), and a total weight not exceeding 1.2 kg.

(★ You must apply for a "[Personal Use Import Permit for Chinese Medicinal Herbs and Preparations](https://oap.fda.gov.tw/B101?type=2)" from the Taiwan Food and Drug Administration, and declare it to customs upon entry.)

### Cash and Jewelry

If carrying amounts exceeding the limits, please fill out the "[Republic of China Customs Declaration Form](https://taipei.customs.gov.tw/singlehtml/2851?cntId=cus2_179094_2851)" and the "[Entry/Exit Declaration Form for Carrying Cash, Negotiable Instruments, Gold, and Articles by Passengers or Crew](https://taipei.customs.gov.tw/singlehtml/2851?cntId=cus2_179096_2851)" or go to the Single Window for Port and Trade to conduct an "[Online Pre-Declaration Registration](https://portal.sw.nat.gov.tw/APGW/MI50!goToMI50INDEX?request_locale=zh_TW)" → Proceed through the Red Channel to declare to customs for clearance.

＊新臺幣：限額10萬元。超額部分須退運(由旅客自行封存於海關，出境時再攜出)。

＊人民幣：限額2萬元。超額部分須退運(由旅客自行封存於海關，出境時再攜出)。

＊外幣：等值美金1萬元。可全數攜入。

＊無記名有價證券：等值美金1萬元。可全數攜入。

＊黃金：等值美金2萬元。總價值超過等值美金2萬元者，於辦理報關手續前，須另向經濟部國際貿易局(電話：02-23510271)申請「[輸入許可證](https://www.trade.gov.tw/ProcessList/ProcessList.aspx?nodeID=4366&pid=3)」

*Diamonds, gemstones, or platinum exceeding personal use: valued at USD$500,000 or more. If the total value exceeds USD$20,000, you must additionally apply for an "[Import Permit](https://www.trade.gov.tw/ProcessList/ProcessList.aspx?nodeID=4366&pid=3)" from the Bureau of Foreign Trade, Ministry of Economic Affairs (Tel: 02-23510271) before completing customs clearance procedures.

## Prohibited Items

- Stun guns, flashlight-style stun guns
- Tear gas spray, pepper spray, anti-assault spray
- Small knives, folding knives, throwing darts, stun guns
- All types of drugs, possession of pharmaceutical raw materials, and substances prohibited or used for producing or converting into drugs
- Marijuana, THC, or products containing cannabis components (CBD requires an import permit)
- Counterfeit goods
- All meat products and meat floss (including ham, jerky, sausages, etc.), as well as beef, lamb, pork, duck, and goose are strictly prohibited
