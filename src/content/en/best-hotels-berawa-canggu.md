# Canggu Berawa Accommodation Recommendations: 10 Options by Need, Choose the Right Area for a Stress-Free Stay

If you ask me which area in Canggu is best to stay, I usually recommend Berawa. Located on the southwest coast of Canggu, it offers surf spots, cafes, yoga studios, and boutique shops, with stunning sunsets over the Indian Ocean in the evening. While it used to be quieter, it now attracts couples, families, solo travelers, and digital nomads, yet the overall vibe remains relaxed.

I've personally stayed at Citadines Berawa Beach Bali Resort and LV8, and what impressed me most were the room types ideal for longer stays: equipped with a kitchenette, balcony, and desk, with good breakfast quality and leisure facilities by the pool that make you want to stay in. Below are 10 options, categorized by need.

## A Few Tips When Choosing

Book early during peak seasons: July–August and the Christmas–New Year period are peak seasons, so it's best to reserve well in advance.

Look out for packages: Some accommodations bundle breakfast or airport transfers, which can be more cost-effective.

Getting around: Berawa is one of the easier areas to navigate in Bali. You can ride a scooter, take Grab or Gojek, or use shuttle services. Walking is also possible, but given the daytime heat, it's better to call a ride for longer distances.

## Berawa or Batu Bolong?

If you prefer fewer people and less traffic, Berawa is more comfortable than Batu Bolong. The two are just a short Grab ride apart.

Airport distance: It takes about 45 to 60 minutes from the airport, depending on traffic.

Accommodation prices generally range from mid-range to high-end. Before booking, check each property's page for current rates and deals.
