## 🌧️ Heavy Rain Warning Issued for Bali! Showers Persist in Some Areas Until October 31

Bali's meteorological agency has issued the latest weather warning, forecasting moderate to heavy rainfall in parts of the island from October 28 to 31. The rain will be unevenly distributed, so travelers should stay alert to local weather changes when visiting different areas.

From October 28 to 29, the warning covers Badung, Bangli, Gianyar, Karangasem, and Tabanan; from October 30 to 31, it includes Badung, Buleleng, Jembrana, Karangasem, and Tabanan.

Additionally, waves in some southern Bali waters may reach up to 3 meters, and seas in the Badung Strait and Lombok Strait also require caution.

### How Does This Affect Travelers?

If you're planning to visit Ubud, Kintamani, or eastern Bali attractions soon, allow extra travel time and bring rain gear. Mountain roads may be slippery during rain, and outdoor attractions could adjust their opening hours due to weather conditions.

If you plan to visit Nusa Penida or Lembongan Island, reconfirm ferry schedules before departure. Sea conditions may differ from land weather—even if it's not raining at your departure point, maritime traffic could still be affected.

This is a regional weather warning, not an indication that it will rain continuously across all of Bali. Travelers should adjust their itineraries based on the actual weather at their destination and the latest official announcements.

## 💱 Bali's Central Bank Reminds Currency Exchange Providers to Maintain Tourist Trust! Confirm Exchange Rates Before Exchanging Money

The Indonesian Bank's Bali branch recently reminded non-bank foreign currency exchange providers to maintain transparent and reliable services to safeguard tourist trust.

Currency exchange is a common need for international travelers upon arriving in Bali. However, exchange rates, service methods, and transaction processes can vary between providers, so travelers should carefully confirm details before exchanging money.

### Key Points to Remember When Exchanging Money in Bali

- **Confirm the exchange rate first:** Don't just look at the big signs outside the store; verify the actual transaction rate.
- **Count your cash on the spot:** After the transaction, check the amount in front of the counter.
- **Watch the calculation method:** Calculate the expected amount of Indonesian Rupiah you'll receive in advance to avoid misunderstandings over rates or fees.
- **Choose licensed providers:** Prioritize currency exchange shops with official business licenses and clear signage.

If you're used to paying with credit cards or digital payments, it's still wise to exchange a small amount of cash for minor purchases, parking, or shops that only accept cash.

The key takeaway here isn't that all money changers in Bali are problematic, but that travelers should develop the habit of confirming exchange rates and counting cash on the spot to avoid unnecessary disputes.

## ✈️ Indonesia's Domestic Flight Discount Ends January 10, 2027! Tourism Operators Hope for Extension

The Indonesian Tourism Operators Association Bali Chapter (ASITA Bali) recently stated that the government's tax incentives for domestic flight tickets are expected to end on **January 10, 2027**. Operators worry that after the incentives end, domestic travelers' willingness to visit Bali may be affected.

Domestic travelers are a crucial part of Bali's tourism market, especially during holidays and off-peak seasons. Domestic airfare impacts the cost of family trips, group tours, and short getaways.

ASITA Bali hopes the government will reassess the timeline of the incentive policy, considering an early start or extending the measures into the off-peak season to mitigate the impact of airfare on tourism demand.

### How Does This Affect Travelers Planning a Trip to Bali?

Currently, this news mainly reflects tourism operators' concerns about market changes after the policy ends. It does not mean airfares have already increased, nor does it indicate the government has announced an extension of the incentives.

If you plan to travel to Bali from other Indonesian cities in late 2026 or early 2027, compare fares and baggage policies across different airlines, and keep an eye on subsequent announcements from airlines and the government.

International travelers departing from Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Singapore should not directly view this domestic flight discount as a discount on their international flights.

## 🏡 Green Zone Illegal Accommodations in Bali Under Scrutiny Again! Land Use Management Becomes a Tourism Development Focus

Bali's regional council has recently turned its attention once again to the issue of tourist accommodations appearing in green zone areas, noting that there is still room for improvement in land use management and the enforcement of relevant regulations.

As the development of villas, guesthouses, and other short-term accommodations in Bali continues to grow, whether the construction and land use of some operators comply with regulations has become a topic of discussion for local government and the council.

Green and agricultural lands not only serve productive functions but are also closely tied to Bali's natural landscapes, traditional culture, and environmental carrying capacity. Inadequate land use management could, over the long term, impact the rice terrace scenery, local livelihoods, and the sustainable development of tourist areas.

### What should travelers pay attention to when booking a villa?

Currently, this news reflects the local council's focus on land management and enforcement, not that all villas in Bali are in violation, nor does it announce that travelers cannot stay in accommodations near green zones.

When booking accommodations, it is advisable to confirm the operator's identity, actual address, booking terms, and cancellation policy. If booking through private social media accounts, ensure that the payment recipient and accommodation details are consistent.

For travelers, this issue is worth keeping an eye on, but there is no need to assume that already-booked accommodations are problematic based on these discussions. If the government later announces specific rectification measures, then assess whether they affect individual stays based on official regulations.
