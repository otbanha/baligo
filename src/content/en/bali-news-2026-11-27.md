# **Bali Latest News | November 27: Extreme Weather Alert, New Ride-Hailing Regulations, and North Bali Eco-Cultural Festival**

Bali is bracing for a more pronounced rainy season shift at the end of November. BMKG has issued a fresh warning that from November 27 to 30, the island may experience moderate to extremely heavy rain, with wave heights in some seas reaching up to 2.5 meters.

On the transportation front, Bali has officially implemented new regulations for ride-hailing car rentals, covering vehicle age, driver qualifications, and platform operations. Additionally, North Bali is hosting an eco-cultural festival this weekend, while Kuta will feature an international tattoo art exhibition.

## **🌧️ BMKG Warns of Extreme Weather from November 27 to 30! Watch for Heavy Rain, Strong Winds, and Landslides Island-Wide**

The Indonesian Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) Regional III Meteorological Center issued a fresh warning on November 26, alerting Bali residents and travelers to potentially heavy rainfall and strong winds from November 27 to 30.

BMKG noted recent changes in atmospheric circulation, including airflow convergence, the Madden-Julian Oscillation (MJO), and Equatorial Rossby Waves, which could intensify rainfall across Bali.

**Island-Wide Weather Overview for November 27:**

| Item | Forecast |
| --- | --- |
| Overall Weather | Moderate Rain |
| Temperature | 22–33°C |
| Relative Humidity | 65–98% |
| Wind Direction | East to Southwest Winds |
| Wind Speed | 4–30 km/h |
| Northern Sea Wave Height | 0.25–1 meter |
| Southern Sea Wave Height | 0.25–2 meters |

**Special Alert: November 27–30**

BMKG warns that moderate to extreme rainfall and strong winds may hit all nine regencies and cities in Bali, including:

- Badung
- Denpasar
- Gianyar
- Tabanan
- Bangli
- Buleleng
- Jembrana
- Klungkung
- Karangasem

Potential hazards include:

- Flooding on roads and in low-lying areas
- Flash floods
- Landslides in mountainous areas
- Fallen trees and damage to infrastructure from strong winds

**Extra caution is also needed for marine activities.**

BMKG warns that from November 27 to 30, wave heights in the following waters may reach 1.25–2.5 meters:

- Waters south of Bali
- Badung Strait (Selat Badung)
- Southern Bali Strait
- Southern Lombok Strait

This is especially important for travelers heading to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, and other offshore islands.

**Reminder for Travelers:**

If you have outdoor plans these days, pay extra attention to Kintamani, Bedugul, waterfalls, and mountain roads.

If you're taking a boat or joining snorkeling or diving activities, confirm sea conditions and operational status with the boat company before departure.

There is no island-wide closure of attractions at this time, but during heavy rainfall, it's not advisable to risk driving through flooded roads or heading out to sea in poor conditions just to stick to your itinerary.

## **🚗 Bali's New Ride-Hailing Regulations Take Effect! Vehicle Age Limit of 10 Years, Minimum Engine Displacement of 1,000cc**

If you frequently use GrabCar or Gojek car services in Bali, this new regulation is worth noting.

According to a report by Bali Post on November 25, the Bali Provincial Government has officially implemented Provincial Regulation No. 8 of 2026 (Perda Provinsi Bali No. 8 Tahun 2026), which regulates special rental transportation services (Angkutan Sewa Khusus, ASK) booked via mobile apps.

This regulation was actually announced and took effect on October 27, 2026, and recent media reports have further clarified the relevant rules.

**Vehicle Specifications:**

| Item | New Regulation |
| --- | --- |
| Gasoline/fuel vehicle age | Maximum 10 years |
| Minimum engine displacement | 1,000cc |
| Minimum power for electric vehicles | 70kW |
| Vehicle registration | Must be registered in Bali |

**New Requirements for Drivers:**

- Must meet relevant driving qualifications and regulations.
- Must have at least 6 months of driving experience.
- Must be able to communicate in Indonesian and English.
- Proficiency in Balinese is a priority.
- Must maintain a good service attitude and dress neatly.

**Ride-hailing platforms are also subject to regulations:**

- Must possess a valid Indonesian legal entity status.
- Must establish a branch office in Bali.
- Must partner with ASK operators that hold valid operating permits and quotas.
- Must provide operational data and a digital management platform for provincial government oversight.

Additionally, the regulations stipulate that the government will set vehicle operating quotas, as well as upper and lower fare limits, and conduct periodic reviews.

**What impact will this have on travelers?**

The focus of this regulation is to institutionalize ride-hailing services in Bali, covering vehicle safety, driver qualifications, and passenger rights.

However, it cannot be directly inferred that all ride-hailing platforms will immediately adjust their services, nor can this ASK car rental regulation be equated with rules for motorcycle ride-hailing services like GoRide.

For travelers, the most notable aspect is that vehicle specifications, legal operating qualifications, and fare management for ride-hailing services may gradually change in the future.

Currently, the government has not announced the actual transition timeline for all platforms, nor is there evidence that travelers need to complete any additional procedures at this stage.

## **🪸 North Bali's Pemuteran Bay Festival Returns November 27–29! Free Entry, Blending Marine Conservation and Culture**

If you happen to be in northern Bali this weekend, the Pemuteran Bay Festival 2026 is worth checking out.

This annual event focuses on ecotourism, local culture, and marine conservation, held in the village of Pemuteran, Buleleng Regency.

According to the official Indonesian tourism portal, the festival runs from November 27 to 29, with free admission.

**Event Details:**

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event Name | Pemuteran Bay Festival 2026 |
| Date | November 27–29 |
| Location | Pemuteran, Buleleng |
| Theme | Foresting the Ocean |
| Entry Fee | Free |

Activities include:

- Traditional Balinese music and cultural performances
- Local food market
- Yoga and outdoor activities
- Marine conservation lecture
- Coral reef restoration workshop
- Biorock coral restoration activities
- Coastal and environmental conservation activities

One of Pemuteran's most notable features is its long-running Biorock coral restoration program.

This technology uses underwater metal structures and low-voltage electrical currents to promote mineral deposition and aid coral reef restoration. As a result, Pemuteran has become a representative community-based marine conservation tourism destination in Indonesia.

For travelers who enjoy snorkeling, diving, and natural ecology, this event is more than just a typical music or cultural festival—it's also a chance to see how locals combine tourism with marine conservation.

**One important note: different versions of the event dates have appeared.**

A DetikBali report on October 26 cited the Bali Provincial Tourism Office's calendar at the time, listing November 26 to 28; the current official Indonesian Ministry of Tourism event page lists November 27 to 29.

Therefore, if you're driving all the way from southern Bali, it's best to confirm the latest schedule with the organizers rather than relying solely on earlier event calendars.

Additionally, while entry to the event is free, activities such as diving, snorkeling, boat rides, and certain specific experiences may require an additional fee.

## **🎨 Kuta International Tattoo Art Exhibition Takes Place November 27–29! International Tattoo Artists Gather, Top Prize IDR 50,000,000**

This weekend in Kuta, there's another special international event: the Island of Gods International Tattoo Convention 2026.

This is Bali's first-ever international tattoo convention, inviting tattoo artists and studios from Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Vietnam, the Philippines, Australia, Greece, the United States, and South Korea to participate.

**Event Details:**

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Event Name | Island of Gods International Tattoo Convention |
| Date | November 27–29 |
| Time | 10:00–22:00 |
| Location | Discovery Kartika Plaza Hotel |
| Address | Jl. Kartika Plaza, Kuta |
| Top Prize | IDR 50,000,000 |

Each of the three days features different live tattoo competitions:

- **November 27:** Fine Line, Black & Grey, Anime, Small Tattoo, etc.
- **November 28:** Japanese/Asian, Neo Traditional, Large Color and Black & Grey Tattoos, etc.
- **November 29:** Realism, Ornamental, Blackwork, Collaboration, and the final Best of Show.

The Best of Show winner will receive a prize of 50 million Indonesian Rupiah, along with a specially crafted art trophy.

In addition to live tattooing and competitions, the event also features art exhibitions, talks, traditional Balinese cultural performances, and live music.

Travelers interested in tattoo culture, body art, and the international creative industry can consider adding this event to their weekend itinerary.

Entry is via e-tickets; after purchase, attendees must exchange them for wristbands on-site as proof of admission. Actual ticket prices and purchase conditions are subject to the official ticketing page.
