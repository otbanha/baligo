## 🗞️ Today's Highlights: Tabanan's Nuanu Creative City Opens Up Strategically, Welcoming a New Public Park Landmark This Summer

- **CEO Confirms 'Nuanu Park' Will Open Within Weeks, Creating a More Accessible, No-Barrier Public Space**:
Located along the coast north of Canggu, the sprawling 44-hectare green tech and art hub—**Nuanu Creative City—has announced the official opening of a new public park, 'Nuanu Park,' this summer, serving as the first stop for visitors**. In an interview, Nuanu CEO Lev Kroll stated that with the recent completion of Luna Beach Club, Aurora Media Park, and the Southern Hemisphere's first wolf sanctuary lodge, the new park aims to make the creative city 'more open, everyday-friendly, and accessible to the public,' shedding its past image of being overly serious, closed-off, or highly commercialized.
- **The 2026 Bali International Photography Festival (FOTO Bali Festival) Is Now Underway at Nuanu**:
As a strategic prelude to this summer's grand opening, **Nuanu Creative City is currently hosting the island's only major international photography event—the 2026 FOTO Bali Festival (running from June 3 to 7)**. This year, the event has garnered significant international buzz, receiving nearly 700 submissions from photographers across 80 countries. A renowned curatorial team has selected 36 top artists from 24 nations to showcase their work throughout the park under the core theme 'Afterimage.' Daily entry tickets start at an affordable IDR 50,000, which already includes access to the Nuanu grounds, making it a top cultural choice for travelers exploring Tabanan in early June.

## 📄 What Travelers Are Asking: September's World Pickleball Championships (WPC) Player Prep Updates

- **Leading the 'Racket Sports Tourism' Trend! The 5th WPC Pickleball World Championships Lands in Sanur**:
The world's fastest-growing new sport, blending tennis and badminton—**the World Pickleball Championship (WPC) will be held from September 20 to 26 this year at the Liga Tennis Sports Club in Sanur**. Organizers reveal that this edition will be the largest in the event's history, expected to draw a wave of international professional players and hundreds of social fitness-loving independent travelers to Sanur.
- **Sanur's major infrastructure upgrades are positioning it as a premier hub for high-end sports and wellness tourism on the island**:
Sports analysts point out that the WPC Pickleball Championship's precise focus on Sanur is largely due to the area's recent significant infrastructure upgrades, including a new shuttle bus system, revamped coastal promenades, and a mature international medical and wellness zone. The arrival of this high-profile event not only brings a fresh wave of quality long-stay visitors during Sanur's traditional September low season but also steadily transforms Bali's southeastern coast into a globally competitive benchmark destination for 'sports and wellness tourism (Wellcation).'
