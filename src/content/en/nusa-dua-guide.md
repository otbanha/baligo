## Nusa Dua/Tanjung Benoa Water & Land Activities

![努沙杜瓦/南灣水上活動是全峇里島最精彩的！](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_6187bcb04aa5ffae5567a3796249bd83.png)

Water activities in Nusa Dua and Tanjung Benoa are the most exciting in all of Bali!

While the beachfront resorts in Nusa Dua and Tanjung Benoa are perfect for travelers who prefer not to leave their hotels, this area still offers a wealth of exciting activities, especially water sports.

Water activities in Nusa Dua are relatively limited compared to Tanjung Benoa, as most five-star resorts have private beaches, turning the coastline into an exclusive vacation haven.
