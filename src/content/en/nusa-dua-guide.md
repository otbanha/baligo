The beachfront resorts in Nusa Dua / Tanjung Benoa are perfect for travelers who prefer not to leave their hotel, but there are still plenty of exciting activities to enjoy here, especially water sports.

Compared to Tanjung Benoa, water activities in Nusa Dua are more limited. This is because most five-star resorts have private beaches, turning the coastline into an exclusive vacation haven. This unique layout means it's not easy to find traditional water sports operators on most beaches. Fortunately, in the Nusa Dua Beach area, you can still find some operators offering thrilling water activities. These water sports providers are concentrated on this small stretch of beach, offering a one-stop service for visitors seeking aquatic adventures.

### Nusa Dua Beach (📌 [Location Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1J7gQWKO_Xc6R04wSQZjxBDapEoTrSa0&usp=sharing))

![努沙杜瓦海灘（Nusa Dua Beach）（ 位置地圖）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_acb04fe54ab144df2f9cfbfb9b7b0522.png)

At Nusa Dua Beach, you can dive into a thrilling array of water sports. Start with a high-speed banana boat ride, zipping across the crystal-clear sea on a bouncy inflatable, feeling the wind in your face. If you crave soaring through the sky, parasailing is a must-try adventure—guided by a professional instructor, you'll glide above the azure horizon, taking in breathtaking coastal views. Seeking speed and control? Jet skis churn through the waves, offering an exhilarating blend of velocity and excitement.
