## Nusa Dua/Tanjung Benoa Water & Land Activities

![努沙杜瓦/南灣水上活動是全峇里島最精彩的！](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_6187bcb04aa5ffae5567a3796249bd83.png)

Water activities in Nusa Dua/Tanjung Benoa are the most exciting in all of Bali!

The beachfront resorts in Nusa Dua/Tanjung Benoa are perfect for travelers who prefer not to leave their hotels, but the area still offers plenty of thrilling activities, especially water sports.

Water activities in Nusa Dua are relatively limited compared to Tanjung Benoa, as most five-star resorts have private beaches, turning the coastline into an exclusive vacation haven. This unique layout means traditional water sports operators are not easily found on most beaches. Fortunately, on Nusa Dua Beach, you can still find operators offering exciting water activities. These water sports providers are concentrated on this small stretch of beach, offering one-stop services to meet visitors' aquatic adventure needs.
