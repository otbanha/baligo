## ATV FAQ

### **Q. Can children ride along?**

**A.** Yes, most ATV operators in Bali allow children to ride as **passengers with an adult**. However, specific age limits vary by operator. Generally, young children can ride as passengers with a parent or instructor, but it's best to confirm the operator's policy before booking.

### **Q. What is the recommended age to ride alone?**

**A.** Most ATV operators in Bali require riders to be **12 years or older** to drive independently, but the following factors should also be considered:

✅ **Height** – If the child is too short, they may not be able to control the ATV steadily.

✅ **Arm strength and control** – ATVs require sufficient arm strength to steer and manage the throttle; children with weaker physical strength are not recommended to drive alone.

✅ **Trail difficulty** – For **simple mud trails**, children aged 12 and up are usually fine. However, for more challenging routes like **off-road, hills, or river crossings**, it's recommended that riders be **16 or older** to drive solo.

📌 **Quick Tip**

🔹 If children are too young or too short but still want to try ATV, they can opt for a **Tandem ATV**, riding together with a parent or instructor.

### **Q. Where is it safe to keep your passport and money while riding an ATV?**

**A.** When riding an ATV in Bali, the routes often go through muddy, river, or rugged terrain, so it's wise to keep your passport and cash secure to avoid loss or water damage. Here are a few safe storage options:

✅ **Use lockers provided by the operator** 🔐

Most ATV operators offer lockers where you can lock up your valuables for safety. However, it's best to check in advance if they're free or if you need to bring your own small padlock.

✅ **Leave them with your private driver** 🚗

If you have a private driver you trust, you can ask them to hold your valuables temporarily—a popular choice among travelers. Still, it's a good idea to carry a small amount of cash on you for emergencies.

✅ **Carry a waterproof bag** 💦

If you prefer to keep your passport, cash, or phone with you, use a high-quality waterproof bag, such as a **waterproof waist pouch, phone case, or dry bag**. You can hang it around your neck, wear it crossbody, or strap it to your waist to prevent loss or water damage from bumps and splashes.

### Q. What's the best time to join the ATV tour? Will it be too hot starting at 11 AM?

The road to the ATV site can be hot, but the ride itself won't be! Most ATV tracks are located in **forests, valleys, or near rivers**, with plenty of **shade from trees** to block direct sunlight.

Most ATV routes **cross streams or muddy areas**, so you'll likely get wet and splattered with mud—which actually keeps you cool rather than feeling stuffy.

### **Q. Will I get completely covered in mud?**

It depends, but **you'll likely get some mud on you**. However, operators provide **shower facilities**, so you can rinse off and change into clean clothes afterward.

### **Q. What should I bring for the ATV ride?**

When riding an ATV in Bali, you'll need to prepare a few items to make the experience more comfortable, safe, and to minimize post-ride inconvenience. Here's a recommended packing list:

✅ **Change of clothes** 👕👖 – ATV rides involve splashing through water and mud, so your clothes will almost certainly get soaked or dirty. It's best to bring a **full set of spare clothes**.

✅ **Shoes** 👟🩴 – Most operators provide rain boots directly.

✅ **Waterproof bag** 💦 – Protect your phone, passport, cash, and other valuables from water damage or loss.

✅ **Insect repellent** 🦟 – ATV routes pass through forests or rice paddies where mosquitoes and bugs may be present, so it's recommended to bring insect repellent.

✅ **Towel** 🏖️ – Useful for wiping sweat, drying off, or a quick clean-up.

✅ **Plastic bag / wet-dry bag** 🛍️ – Use it to store wet clothes after the ATV ride, keeping other items clean.
