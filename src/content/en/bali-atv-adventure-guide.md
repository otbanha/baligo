## Important Reminders

On a Bali adventure trip, experiencing the thrill of an ATV quad bike ride is truly unforgettable. However, before joining this activity, we must emphasize the importance of finding a reputable operator. These operators should provide basic medical coverage to ensure your safety during the activity.

One of our group members got injured during an ATV ride, but luckily the ATV operator was very responsible and covered all medical expenses. This incident reminded us how crucial it is to choose a reliable operator and to be extra cautious during the activity.

**When you join an ATV adventure, keep these points in mind:**

1. During the initial test ride, if the vehicle feels off or hard to control, don't hesitate—**ask for a replacement immediately**.
2. Only **children aged 12 and above** can ride on their own, but still consider factors like height and arm strength.
3. ATVs have powerful horsepower and torque—watch out for sudden acceleration, control your speed, and avoid dangerous situations.
4. Make sure the operator provides proper safety gear, professional guides, and basic medical coverage.

This way, you can enjoy your ATV quad bike adventure while ensuring your safety and well-being.

## ATV FAQ

### **Q. Can children ride along?**

**A.** Yes, most ATV operators in Bali allow children to **ride with an adult**, but specific age limits vary by operator. Generally, young children can ride as passengers with a parent or instructor, but it's best to check the operator's policy before booking.

### **Q. At what age can children ride on their own?**

**A.** Most ATV operators in Bali require riders to be **12 years or older** to drive independently, but you should also consider the following:

✅ **Height** – If the child is too short, they may not be able to control the ATV steadily.

✅ **Arm strength and control** – ATVs require a certain amount of arm strength to steer and handle the throttle; children with weaker physical strength are not recommended to drive alone.

✅ **Trail difficulty** – For **simple mud trails**, children aged 12 and up are usually fine. However, for more challenging routes like **off-road, hills, or river crossings**, it's recommended that riders be **16 or older** to drive solo.

📌 **Quick Tip**

🔹 If children are too young or too short but still want to try ATV, they can opt for a **Tandem ATV**, riding together with a parent or instructor.

### **Q. Where is it safe to keep your passport and money while riding ATV?**

**A.** When riding ATV in Bali, the routes often go through muddy, river, or rugged terrain, so it's wise to store your passport and cash securely to avoid loss or water damage. Here are a few safe storage options:

✅ **Use lockers provided by the operator** 🔐

Most ATV operators offer lockers where you can lock up valuables for safety. However, it's best to check in advance if they're free or if you need to bring your own small padlock.

✅ **Leave them with your private driver** 🚗

If you have a private driver you trust, you can ask them to hold your valuables temporarily—this is a common choice among travelers. Still, it's recommended to carry a small amount of cash on you just in case.

✅ **Carry a waterproof bag** 💦

If you prefer to keep your passport, cash, or phone with you, use a high-quality waterproof bag, such as a **waterproof waist pouch, phone pouch, or dry bag**. You can hang it around your neck, wear it crossbody, or strap it to your waist to keep items safe from bumps or water.

### Q. What's the best time to join an ATV session? Will it be too hot starting at 11 AM?

The road to the ATV site can indeed be hot, but the ATV experience itself won't be too hot! Most ATV riding areas are located in **forests, valleys, or near rivers**, with plenty of **shade from trees** along the way, so there's no direct strong sunlight.

Most ATV routes **cross streams or muddy areas**, so you'll inevitably get wet, even splashed with mud, which actually keeps you cool rather than feeling stuffy.

### **Q. Will I get completely covered in mud?**

It depends, but **you'll likely get some mud on you**. However, the operators provide **shower facilities**, so you can rinse off and change into clean clothes afterward.

### **Q. What should I bring for ATV riding?**

There are a few things you'll want to prepare for an ATV ride in Bali to make the experience more comfortable, safe, and less messy afterward. Here's a suggested packing list:

✅ **Change of clothes** 👕👖 – ATV rides involve splashing through water and mud, so your clothes will almost certainly get wet or dirty. It's best to bring a **full set of spare clothes**.

✅ **Shoes** 👟🩴 – Most operators provide rain boots directly.

✅ **Waterproof bag** 💦 – To protect your phone, passport, cash, and other valuables from water damage or loss.

✅ **Insect repellent** 🦟 – ATV routes often pass through forests or rice paddies where mosquitoes may be present, so it's a good idea to bring repellent.

✅ **Towel** 🏖️ – Handy for wiping off sweat, drying off, or a quick clean-up.

✅ **Plastic bag / wet-dry bag** 🛍️ – To store wet clothes after the ATV ride and keep your other belongings dry.
