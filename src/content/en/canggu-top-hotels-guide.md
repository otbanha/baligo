## 13. Oshom Bali

![13.Oshom Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f7670907705e51bad1bd83b39e8f8eb9.jpg)

Opening in November 2024, Oshom Bali is a boutique hotel with just 11 rooms, nestled within the Nuanu creative city in Tabanan, Bali. Set against a cliff and overlooking a black sand beach, the hotel also features seven mangrove treehouses, a saltwater pool, a refined restaurant, and a soon-to-open peachy-orange rooftop bar. The rooms boast minimalist luxury with floor-to-ceiling windows, smart amenities, and traditional Indonesian handicrafts, along with nightly bedtime stories and a meditation space for ultimate relaxation. Founder Daisy Angus curates creative activities like painting workshops and 'bedside talks' to inspire creativity.

Oshom is surrounded by rich facilities, just a short drive from Lumeira, the world's largest wood-fired steam healing dome, offering Finnish sauna and other wellness experiences. The hotel restaurant serves both Balinese and Western cuisine, and it's a one-minute walk to [Luna Beach Club](/en/blog/canggu-guide/), where you can enjoy the pool, bar, and beach parties. Oshom blends art, nature, and inspiration, making it an ideal choice for a unique getaway.
