**Bottom Line**: Located within the Greenkubu Restaurant and Swing forest in Ubud, Hikaria is an immersive nighttime light trail spanning about **1 kilometer**. Themed around Balinese mythology (Sanjara, the god of light, and Wisaka, the god of shadow), it opens after sunset and offers a unique evening-to-night experience suitable for all ages.

Stepping into Hikaria in Ubud, Bali feels like crossing the boundary between reality and fantasy—a symphony of light and shadow unfolding under the night sky. This revolutionary light art brand, created by **Timedoor Indonesia** and **Run-Hun Sha**, is set in the forest at Greenkubu Restaurant and Swing. After sunset, it transforms into a magical wonderland of light, drawing travelers seeking a unique nighttime experience.

## A Perfect Fusion of Technology and Tradition

Hikaria is more than just a light show; it's a seamless blend of traditional storytelling and cutting-edge technology. Leveraging **Timedoor Indonesia**'s expertise in digital innovation and **Run-Hun Sha**'s deep experience in immersive entertainment, Hikaria uses advanced projection mapping and interactive visuals to create a truly magical journey for visitors of all ages.

## Step Into the Light Narrative of Balinese Mythology

Inspired by traditional Balinese mythology, Hikaria tells the legend of Sanjara, the god of light, and Wisaka, the god of shadow. As you stroll along the approximately 1-kilometer trail, you'll experience an **immersive nighttime light art walk**. Advanced projection mapping technology blends perfectly with the natural surroundings, creating an atmosphere that is both serene and dreamlike, as if stepping into a fairy tale.

One of Hikaria's most celebrated features is that every corner offers a perfect photo opportunity. From striking light installations to the iconic giant moon, each turn captures the magical moments of the night. Notably, the venue offers **professional complimentary photography services**, with photographers stationed at the best spots to capture lasting memories for visitors.

## Tips and Precautions

Based on the experiences of frequent visitors, here are some tips for visiting Hikaria:

- **Attire and Gear**: The venue features natural terrain with dark and uneven areas, so be sure to wear **closed-toe comfortable shoes**. Since it's an outdoor environment, it's advisable to bring some **insect repellent** to ensure comfort during your exploration.
- **Weather Considerations**:
  Activities will **proceed as usual in light rain**, with umbrellas provided on-site.
  If **heavy rain or extreme weather** occurs, you can choose:
  - - **Full refund**, or **free rescheduling** to another date
- **Time Planning**: Weekends and peak hours are very crowded, and you may need to wait **20-30 minutes** to enter, so please allow plenty of time in your itinerary.
- **Suitable For**: This place is perfect for a romantic date or a family outing, as visitors of all ages can find something to enjoy.
