## What is e-CD?

**Bottom line**: The Indonesia Electronic Customs Declaration (e-CD) is now integrated into the **All Indonesia** one-stop online application system. After completing it, you'll receive a confirmation email with a QR Code. **You must show the QR Code for scanning at the customs checkpoint before exiting the airport**; your passport must be valid for **at least 6 months** from the date of entry.

The Indonesia Electronic Customs Declaration (e-CD) is an online customs form that all travelers to Indonesia must complete. You can access the electronic form online and enter the required information. Once you've completed the e-CD, you'll receive an email with the completed form and a QR Code. You must proactively show the QR Code on your phone or a printed copy for scanning at the final 'Customs Declaration' checkpoint before leaving the airport.

Details of the e-CD form: You must provide up-to-date information about your identity, travel plans, and passport data. Your passport must be valid for **at least 6 months** from the date of entry into Indonesia. You also need to declare the items you're bringing into Indonesia, and note that some items are prohibited under any circumstances.

## Here are some tips for filling out the e-CD form:

1. Go to <https://allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id/>
2. You can fill out the e-CD form in advance, or complete it online upon arrival at the Indonesia/Bali airport
3. Fill out the e-CD form within a maximum of **3 days** before entry
4. You'll receive a QR Code; save it on your phone (preferably a screenshot) or print it out
5. If you're traveling as a family, you can fill out one form for the whole family
6. Filling out the e-CD form is mandatory for both Indonesian citizens and foreigners
7. Can't find 'Taiwan' in the country list? Select 'Others' and enter 'Taiwan'
