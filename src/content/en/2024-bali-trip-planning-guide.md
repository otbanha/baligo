Are you already planning an overseas trip for 2024?

If you want to skip the research phase, start with the [Bali Trip Planner](/en/trip-planner/) to generate an itinerary based on your travel style, then come back to this guide for the details.

This guide introduces Taiwan's public holidays for 2024 and offers several leave-hacking strategies, so you can enjoy the longest vacations with the least annual leave. Taiwan has 116 public holidays in 2024, including four long weekends of three days or more: New Year's Day, Lunar New Year, Qingming Festival, and Dragon Boat Festival. The Mid-Autumn Festival and Double Tenth Day fall on Thursdays, so there are no consecutive holidays for those.

Here are some leave-hacking tips to help you enjoy the longest breaks with minimal annual leave:

**New Year's Long Weekend**: Originally 3 days off, take additional leave from Jan 2 to Jan 5 for a 9-day break.

**Lunar New Year Long Weekend**: Originally 7 days off, take additional leave from Feb 5 to Feb 7 for a 12-day break. Or take leave on Feb 16 and 17 for an 11-day break.

**228 Peace Memorial Day**: Originally 1 day off, take additional leave on Feb 26, 27, 29, and Mar 1 for a 9-day break.

**Qingming Festival Long Weekend**: Originally 4 days off, take additional leave from Apr 1 to Apr 3 for a 9-day break.

**Labor Day** Originally a 1-day holiday, by taking April 29, 30, May 2, and 3 off, you can enjoy 9 days off.

**Dragon Boat Festival** Originally a 3-day holiday, by taking June 11–14 off, you can enjoy 9 days off.

**Mid-Autumn Festival** Originally a 1-day holiday, by taking September 16, 18–20 off, you can enjoy 9 days off.

**Double Tenth Day** Originally a 1-day holiday, by taking October 7–9 and 11 off, you can enjoy 9 days off.

**Best to avoid:** Lunar New Year is a peak travel season in Bali, drawing tourists from around the world, especially from East Asian countries where it's the only long holiday of the year. February 10, the first day of the Lunar New Year, is a national holiday in Indonesia, and February 8 coincides with Isra' Mi'raj (a national holiday in Indonesia). Combined with the weekend, Indonesia has a flexible four-day break from February 8 to 11. Plus, with the Indonesian presidential election on February 14, many locals are likely to take February 12 and 13 off for a seven-day holiday, which is expected to cause major traffic jams in Bali. **Can join:** February 28 to March 9, Galungan & Kuningan: This is one of Bali's most important festivals, lasting about 10 days, celebrating the creation of the universe and the triumph of good over evil. During this time, families make offerings to their ancestors, seeking blessings, and hold ceremonies at temples to honor the gods. Schools in Bali close for two weeks, highlighting the festival's significance!

**Avoid:** Indonesia's Eid al-Fitr holiday in 2024 starts on April 8 (Friday) and lasts until April 15. Eid is Indonesia's most important holiday, celebrating the end of the Muslim fasting month (April 10), but it's also a time for locals to spend with family and friends, much like the Chinese Lunar New Year. Typically, around April 5, Bali begins to see an influx of Indonesian tourists, with the island packed with locals—making it the busiest of Bali's five peak seasons. This is a holiday you should avoid visiting Bali!

**Can attend:** October 3–12: Navaratri is a Hindu festival celebrating the victory of the goddess Durga. It typically occurs in September or October each year, lasting nine days, during which people engage in fasting, prayers, flower offerings, and dancing. The final day of Navaratri is Dussehra, commemorating Prince Rama's defeat of the demon king Ravana. If you plan to visit Bali around Taiwan's National Day (Double Tenth Day), you can enjoy a nine-day holiday while also experiencing the sacred atmosphere of Navaratri, adding depth to your trip.
