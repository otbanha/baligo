**Bottom Line**: Located in the Bedugul highlands of central-north Bali, Bali Farm House is a family-friendly farm styled like a European estate. You can feed alpacas and miniature horses up close. Since the mountain climate is cool, **bring warm clothes**. Tickets usually include dining credit, making it a perfect stop for a cool family outing paired with a day-trip charter.

When you think of Bali, sun, sand, and surf usually come to mind. But did you know? In the northern highlands of Bali, there's an adorable farm that feels like a Tuscan European estate—Bali Farm House! Not only is the weather cool and refreshing, but it's also home to a cast of star animals from around the world waiting to interact with you. For families with kids, this spot will quickly become the highlight of your trip!

## Highlights at a Glance!

**Step Inside and Feel Like You're in a European Village**

With its clean grounds, thoughtfully designed buildings, and exotic charm, Bali Farm House completely shatters the typical farm image. Forget muddy paths and messy fences—here, you'll find stunning scenery that makes you want to snap photos nonstop!

![亮點搶先看！](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-06/1781074971810-mixcollage-10-jun-2026-01-55-pm-9955.jpg)

**Get Up Close with Animal Stars**

Who are the main stars of the farm? The **alpacas** from South America! Their innocent, adorable expressions and fluffy coats will melt the hearts of both kids and adults. Beyond alpacas, you'll also see Australian donkeys, Dutch ponies, Czech goats, and even African ostriches! The best part? You can buy food to feed them yourself. Watching them approach gently is an experience that's priceless in its soothing effect.

## Family Fun! A Kid's Paradise

Parents bringing kids to Bali, don't miss this spot! Bali Farm House is a dream playground tailor-made for families:

**Massive Outdoor Playground**: At the heart of the farm is a large kids' play area with swings, slides, and climbing frames galore. Children can run wild and burn off energy while parents relax in the nearby seating area, watching the little ones play and enjoying the cool mountain breeze.

![親子同樂！這裡是孩子的天堂](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-06/1781075236478-mixcollage-10-jun-2026-02-07-pm-4042.jpg)

**Safe and Clean Environment**: The entire park is kept spotlessly clean with well-laid ground surfaces, so no worries about kids getting muddy. The farm also pays special attention to hygiene in the animal zones, giving parents peace of mind when letting children interact with the animals.

**Activities for All Ages**: Whether you have a toddler or a school-aged child, there's something for everyone. Little ones can happily watch animals and feed rabbits, while older kids can take on the challenge of pony rides or interact with larger animals.

![親子同樂！這裡是孩子的天堂](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-06/1781075356728-caption_8_.jpg)

**Family Photo Hotspot**: The farm is filled with thoughtfully designed backdrops, from European-style buildings to cute animal statues—every shot is a family photo album moment! Don't forget to snap plenty of pictures with the alpacas; they're sure to be the highlight of your social media feed.

![親子同樂！這裡是孩子的天堂](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-06/1781075294249-caption_9_.jpg)

## Must-Try Experiences

**Feeding Alpacas**: This is the number one experience you absolutely can't miss! Hold out some feed as the alpacas approach with their big, innocent eyes, gently nibbling the food from your hand. The thrill of such close contact is incredibly soothing, even for adults! Kids will be squealing with excitement. Make sure your camera is ready to capture those adorable moments of interaction.

![必玩體驗推薦](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-06/1781075609363-14-15-1_14_11zon_43_11zon.webp)

**Feeding Rabbits**: A heartwarming activity designed especially for little ones! The farm has a charming rabbit area home to fluffy bunnies of all kinds. Children can gently hold the rabbits or feed them carrots and veggies. Rabbits are slow and gentle, making them perfect for younger kids who might be a bit nervous around larger animals. Watching your child carefully feed a rabbit is a tender, unforgettable sight.

![必玩體驗推薦](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-06/1781075806094-photo20frame.webp)

**Riding the Mini Train**: This is a wildly popular attraction that has kids screaming with joy! The farm features a cute sightseeing train that slowly chugs through the park. Along the way, you can enjoy the cool mountain breeze and take in the European-style scenery, passing by various animal zones for a different perspective on alpacas, donkeys, and ponies. The train moves at a steady, gentle pace, perfect for little ones, and gives parents a chance to rest while soaking up quality family time. Kids always beg to 'ride again'—its charm is irresistible!

![必玩體驗推薦](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-06/1781075646939-unnamed.webp)

**Picnic with Donkeys**: This is the farm's signature activity! Enjoy a meal while friendly donkeys wander around you—an experience you won't find anywhere else. The donkeys have gentle temperaments, often delighting and surprising children, while parents can snap plenty of fun photos.

![必玩體驗推薦](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-06/1781075666762-picnic1.webp)

**Pony Riding**: A perfect activity for little ones! The farm features Shetland ponies from the Netherlands, small and adorable with steady personalities. Staff provide careful guidance, allowing children to safely enjoy the riding experience and create unforgettable memories.

![必玩體驗推薦](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-06/1781075738355-image00001_1_.jpg)

**Explore the Diverse Animal Zone**: Beyond the star animals, don't miss Australian donkeys, Czech goats, African ostriches, and other creatures from around the world. Walk around and explain each animal's unique traits to your kids, blending fun with learning!

![必玩體驗推薦](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-06/1781075890343-tec_balifarmhouse_2.jpg)

**Opening Hours**: Daily 09:00 - 17:00 (last entry at 16:00)

## Pre-Trip Tips

- Outside food and drinks are not allowed in the park
- Wear comfortable clothes and sturdy shoes, and don't forget sunscreen
- To save money, buy tickets on travel platforms in advance—they often have discounts.
- Plan at least half a day, as kids usually won't want to leave!
- Remember to bring a camera or fully charged phone—you'll be snapping photos nonstop.
- The mountain weather can change quickly, so bring a light jacket.
