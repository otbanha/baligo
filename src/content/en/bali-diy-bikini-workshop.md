# # Don't Just Buy a Bikini in Bali! I Want to Make One Myself—Three DIY Bikini Workshops to Recommend 👙

Ladies, after living in Bali for so many years, whenever friends come to visit, the most common question I get is: 'Is there any unique experience to try?'

I used to recommend spas or afternoon tea. But in the past two years, the one thing I most want to take my girlfriends to is this: making your own bikini.

Not picking one from a store—choosing the colors, matching the beads, and finally wearing it—all decided by you. The joy of wearing a swimsuit you made yourself to the beach for photos is truly something special. Here, I've rounded up the three workshops currently available in Bali, from Canggu to Uluwatu.

## 💛 Mer.Made Bikini Atelier (Pererenan)

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794556840057-15631.jpg)

Mer.Made is Bali's first bikini workshop. The name combines the French word for 'sea' with 'creation.' The founder says they want everyone here to feel beautiful and take that moment home. I really love this philosophy.

Each session is capped at six people, all seated around a small table. The vibe is like crafting with your girlfriends—no pressure at all.

**How it works:** First, decide how you want to wear it, then choose your base color, and finally add beads, shells, and various accessories. The bases are all locally made in Bali.

**Practical Info:**

- Cost: Official website Essential package IDR 1,650,000 per person (Signature package also available, offering more unique accessories)
- Address: Jl. Pantai Pererenan No.74, Canggu, Kec. Mengwi, Kabupaten Badung, Bali 80351
- Hours: Daily 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- Tip: Choosing materials and matching takes time; it's best to book online in advance and allow plenty of time to browse.

## 🤍 Salome Bikini Studio (Canggu)

Located in the heart of Canggu, this studio offers a more refined experience. The two-hour workshop lets you design and create your own bikini from start to finish, using sustainable fabrics like recycled nylon.

What I love most is that it thoughtfully allows you to **choose the cup shape and bottom coverage based on your comfort**. Everyone's body is different, so picking what feels and looks best on you is key.

Additionally, Salome also hosts workshops at private villas, perfect for birthday parties or girls' trips, and offers a simple DIY version to try at home.

**Practical Info:**

- Duration: Approximately two hours
- Website: salomestudios.co
- Sizes suggested for adult women XS to L

## 🌊 Bonjour Bali Atelier (Uluwatu)

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794556868513-15632.jpg)

Located inside Ulu Cliffhouse in the south, this studio sits right on the cliff edge, letting you craft your own bikini while gazing at the Indian Ocean.

What makes it truly special is the **interchangeable jewelry straps**. You can string together your own unique set of straps using pearls, natural stones, shells, and beads. When you want a new look, simply swap them out—one swimsuit can be styled in countless ways. Each session includes a top, bottom, and one set of jewelry straps.

**Practical Info:**

- Duration: Approximately 60 to 90 minutes
- Price: The Atelier experience is listed on the official website at IDR 2,500,000; a single set of straps (two pieces) is IDR 799,000
- Open daily; advance booking recommended

## 🌺 So Which One Should You Choose?

- **Staying around Canggu or Pererenan:** Mer.Made or Salome — you're right there
- **Want to enjoy the sea view while crafting:** Bonjour — pair it with southern attractions on the same day
- **Love beads and accessories:** All three work, but Bonjour's beaded shoulder straps are the most unique
- **Coming with your bestie or mom:** Salome can be set up at a villa — perfect for a party
