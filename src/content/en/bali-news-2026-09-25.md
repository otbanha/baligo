# Topics of Concern for Bali Tourists + News Roundup | September 25, 2026

## 🚨 11-Year-Old Boy Swept Away by Current While Swimming in Buleleng, Search Resumes This Morning

This is a recent travel safety incident that occurred yesterday and is still being addressed today.

Around 4 PM on September 24, **an 11-year-old boy was swept out to sea by currents while swimming at Gerokgak Beach in Buleleng**. He had entered the water with two other children, and about half an hour later, all three were carried offshore. Two were rescued by locals, but the 11-year-old remains missing.

Upon receiving the report, the Buleleng Search and Rescue Center dispatched teams with rubber boats to search both at sea and along the coast. The first day's search continued until around 9:30 PM without finding the boy, and **rescue operations resumed on the morning of September 25**.

This incident serves as a stark reminder for families visiting Bali: **just because you see others swimming at a beach doesn't mean the current conditions are safe for entering the water.**

Especially recently, the waters off southern Bali have seen higher waves, and currents remain a risk that tourists often overlook. When bringing children to the beach, it's best to choose spots with lifeguards and designated safe zones, and always heed on-site warnings.

## 🛡️ Penglipuran Village Entry Fee Now Includes Travel Accident Insurance

If you're planning a visit to Bali's popular cultural attraction **Penglipuran Village (Penglipuran Tourism Village)** soon, here's a service worth knowing about.

Penglipuran has now incorporated **travel safety insurance** into its entry fee, a measure that has been in place for about four months.

The site management explained that with an average of **2,000 visitors per day** and some roads within the village having significant elevation changes, the insurance aims to enhance visitor protection during their trip.

Management stated that the travel insurance is primarily part of the site's service package, designed to boost visitors' sense of security and overall travel quality.

This approach reflects how some of Bali's popular attractions are gradually integrating **safety assurance into the tourism product itself**, rather than just charging an entry fee.

## 🚢 New Celukan Bawang Port Accelerates Preparations, May Eventually Divert Java-Bali Traffic

There's new development in northern Bali's transportation scene.

Pelindo is accelerating preparations for **Celukan Bawang Port's Pier 3 (Dermaga 3)**, primarily to handle the potential increase in vehicle traffic during the **Christmas and New Year (Nataru)** period at the end of this year.

Currently, the pier is undergoing lighting improvements, the installation of steel plates for the Roro vessel ramp, and the widening and reinforcement of the access road to the pier.

The planned route is **Jangkar Port in East Java – Buleleng's Celukan Bawang Port**, and Pier 3 will prioritize serving private vehicles.

The main goal of this measure is to help relieve pressure on the traditional main inter-island route, **Ketapang–Gilimanuk**, during major holidays like Nataru (Christmas to New Year).

Whether this diversion route will be officially adopted, along with the specific operational details, is still being decided by the government and relevant authorities.

For regular travelers, this is a transportation development worth following long-term; if it becomes operational, **driving from Java to Bali could offer more options.**

## 👗 Nusa Dua International Events Continue: Bali International Fashion Week Attracts 15 Countries

If you're in Nusa Dua these days, you might notice a particularly high number of international events happening.

**Bali International Fashion Week (BIFW) 2026** is being held at The Nusa Dua, running from **September 24 to 26**.

This year, participants included designers, models, academics, and creative industry professionals from **15 countries**. In addition to fashion shows, the event also featured networking and industry activities.

The event was also positioned as part of the promotion of Bali's tourism and creative industries.

Notably, another major event, **BMW Motorrad Days Bali 2026**, will be held at The Nusa Dua from September 24 to 26, expected to attract over 1,500 local riders and visitors, along with approximately 500 international participants.

So if you're staying in Nusa Dua during this period, expect the surrounding activities and crowds to be livelier than usual.

##  🌊 Bali Sea Conditions on September 25 Still Require Caution, Southern Waters Could See Waves Up to 2.5 Meters

Today's BMKG forecast indicates that **September 25 will bring mostly sunny to partly cloudy weather across Bali**, with no light to extreme rainfall warnings issued for any regency.

However, sea conditions still need attention.

Wave heights in southern Bali waters could reach **2.5 meters** today, while northern waters range from 0.5 to 1.5 meters. Winds are mainly from the east to southeast, with speeds of 4 to 30 km/h.

Today's forecast: Badung around 22–31°C, Gianyar around 21–30°C, Klungkung around 23–30°C, and Denpasar around 23–30°C.

For general city sightseeing, the impact is minimal. However, if you have **snorkeling, diving, speedboat rides, surfing, or other water activities** planned today, it's best to directly check the sailing status with the boat company and activity operators.

Especially when heading to outlying islands, don't just rely on 'whether Bali is sunny today.'
