9 people, 9 large suitcases, and a full 9 days—that was the reality we faced at the start of this family trip to Bali. I even tacked on 5 solo days at the end. Looking back, I have only one thought: it was absolutely perfect. This post covers the real experiences with private drivers, villas, and Seminyak hotels, along with related guides from this site to help you plan your own itinerary.

## How We Structured the Trip: Family Villas First, Solo Time Later

The trip was split into two parts. The first half was a family trip with 9 people, complete with a private driver and two villas: 2 nights in Ubud and 2 nights in Uluwatu. The second half was my 5-day solo getaway, all spent in Seminyak, where I switched between two similarly priced hotels for comparison.

> This arrangement of 'villas first, hotels later' aligns with the accommodation strategy outlined in this site's [Bali Private Villa Guide](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-private-villa-recommendations/), which suggests starting with a villa for private space and flexible itineraries, then switching to a hotel for convenient amenities and professional service—striking a balance. With a large group, the advantages of a villa are even more pronounced. For tips on choosing three-, four-, or five-bedroom family villas, check out [Group Travel Villa Recommendations](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-group-villa-stay/).

> If you're still deciding how to divide your trip into zones, this site's [Accommodation Area Comparison](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-accommodation-area-guide/) breaks down transportation, shopping, nightlife, and natural scenery in Kuta, Legian, Seminyak, Canggu, and Ubud. For first-timers, the [Ultimate Beginner's Guide](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-ultimate-guide-2026/) recommends a combination of the south (Seminyak or Kuta) and Ubud, so you can enjoy both beaches and culture. This trip built on that foundation by adding a stop in Uluwatu.

## Choosing a Private Driver: Compared 3 or 4 Options, Finally Went with Ah Zhong

Before departure, we carefully compared 3 or 4 private driver services, with the only non-negotiable requirement being a Chinese-speaking driver. In the end, we chose Ah Zhong, and looking back, it was the best decision of the entire trip.

The car was incredibly comfortable, the air conditioning was super cold, his driving was very steady, and his Chinese was fluent. The price was also very reasonable.

Why is hiring a private car so important for family groups? [Gobaligo's guide to private car hire](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-private-car-hire-guide/) lays out the benefits clearly: someone to guide you through currency exchange and buying a SIM card, so you don't have to figure it out on your own; a driver who knows the roads, making the trip safer and smoother; help keeping an eye on luggage; extra support when traveling with kids; and in case of unexpected situations, the driver is the first person who can help. We experienced almost all of these firsthand with A-Zhong, and I'll share the details in the next section.

> If you're still looking for a driver, check out the [Bali Top Private Car Driver List](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-private-car-drivers-guide/) compiled from the community on this site. If you have concerns about language communication, you can also take a look at the [Chinese-Speaking Drivers, Guides, and Private Car Tour Guide](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-chinese-tour-guide/). As for pricing, it's best to ask the driver directly for a quote.

## A-Zhong's Thoughtfulness: From a Bottle of Water to Currency Exchange, Supermarkets, and Family Photos

What touched us most was A-Zhong's thoughtfulness.

**Lending us money to buy water.** At first, he knew we hadn't exchanged money yet, and it was hot—everyone might be thirsty—so he proactively lent us money to buy water, and we paid him back after we exchanged money.

**Taking us to a safe currency exchange.** Later, he took us to a super safe exchange with great rates. [Gobaligo's currency exchange guide](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-currency-exchange-guide/) warns against roadside exchange booths and also lists [common exchange scams](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-money-exchange-scams-2/). Having a driver take you directly to a reliable place really saves a lot of hassle.

**Recommending better local supermarkets.** The supermarket he recommended was even better than the ones we found through our own research, and much more fun to browse. If you want to check out some options first, flip through [Gobaligo's Bali Supermarket Map Guide](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-supermarket-guide-map/). Stocking up the fridge after checking into a villa makes things a lot more convenient.

**Always ready for last-minute needs.** A-Zhong was always responsive to our last-minute requests. I'll save the story about moving luggage for the accommodation section.

**He's amazing at taking photos.** His camera skills are practically professional. I've come to realize that Balinese people are truly masters of cinematography. He captured so many beautiful family photos for us on this trip.

## 5 Days of Freedom in Seminyak: A Side-by-Side Test of Two Similarly Priced Hotels

For the second half of my trip, I had 5 days of personal freedom and chose to stay in convenient Seminyak. I tested two hotels at similar price points, and the contrast was striking.

| Accommodation | Price for 2 Nights (with Breakfast) | Room | Breakfast | Other Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BOUTIQUE SEMINYAK FORMERL (My Recommendation) | Around NT$2,500 (USD$81) | Slight musty smell upon entering, but you get used to it | Very generous and delicious | Free golf cart shuttle to the beach or town; live music at the ground-floor restaurant on Wednesdays and Fridays, dinner around NT$300 (USD$10) |
| D'primahotel Petitenget Seminyak 2 (Disappointing) | Around NT$2,000 (USD$65) | Decent, no musty smell | Didn't suit my taste at all | Nearby construction, a bit noisy; no conditioner provided in the bathroom |

### Recommended: BOUTIQUE SEMINYAK FORMERL

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794201641405-15563.jpg)

Breakfast is a major highlight here—very generous and delicious. I even tried snake fruit for the first time. The staff were incredibly sweet and friendly; when they saw I couldn't open it, they kindly peeled it for me.

The best part is the free golf cart shuttle, which takes you directly to the beach or town—super convenient. The ground-floor restaurant also has live music on Wednesdays and Fridays, creating a fantastic atmosphere. Dinner prices are very reasonable, around NT$300 (USD$10) per meal. I highly recommend spending one evening here for dinner and music.

### Disappointment: D'primahotel Petitenget Seminyak 2

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1794201773702-15564.jpg)

I moved here because reviews said the breakfast was good, but it turned out to be a huge letdown—the breakfast just didn't suit my taste. The room was average, no musty smell, but there was construction nearby, so it was a bit noisy.

The worst part? The bathroom didn't provide conditioner. Bali's water is quite hard, and without conditioner, my hair felt unbearably dry and rough. This was the only accommodation I've stayed at that didn't offer conditioner, so a heads-up: bring your own.

## South Seminyak or North Seminyak? I Choose the South, Closer to the Sea

After staying at both, I personally prefer South Seminyak, where BOUTIQUE is located. This area is close to the beach, making it a leisurely stroll to Seminyak Square and the sand.

North Seminyak (around D'primahotel) seems convenient for transport, but the traffic is just too heavy, lacking that relaxed vacation vibe. The difference in atmosphere between the two areas is like comparing 'Taipei Main Station' to 'Taipei 101 Xinyi District.'

## Lessons from This Trip for Your Next One

- **Compare driver prices; a Chinese-speaking driver is essential.** We compared 3 or 4 options and finally chose A-Zhong—the best decision of the entire trip.
- **Don't hesitate to ask for things at the villa.** A casual question about the mango tree led the butler to pick a whole bag and even cut them for us.
- **Don't panic if the villa is down a narrow alley.** When the big car couldn't fit, A-Zhong dispatched a scooter team to carry all 9 large suitcases in.
- **I prefer South Seminyak.** It's close to the beach, and you can easily walk to Seminyak Square and the sand.
- **Bring your own conditioner.** The water in Bali is quite hard, and the only hotel that didn't provide conditioner during this trip was one of the places I stayed.

This 9-day Bali trip was absolutely perfect. If you're also planning to visit Bali, I highly recommend hiring a driver named A-Zhong.
