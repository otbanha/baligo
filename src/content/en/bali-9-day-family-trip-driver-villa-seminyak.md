9 people, 9 large suitcases, and a full 9 days—that was the challenge we faced at the start of this Bali family trip. I even tacked on 5 extra days of solo time afterward. Looking back, I can only say one thing: it was absolutely perfect. This article covers the real experiences with private drivers, villas, and Seminyak hotels, along with related guides from this site to help you plan your own itinerary.

## How We Planned It: Family Villas First, Solo Time Later

The trip was split into two parts. The first half was a 9-person family trip with a private driver and two villas: 2 nights in Ubud and 2 nights in Uluwatu. The second half was my 5-day solo getaway, all spent in Seminyak, where I even switched between two similarly priced hotels for comparison.

> This 'villas first, hotels later' arrangement aligns with the accommodation strategy outlined in [Bali Private Villa Guide](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-private-villa-recommendations/), which suggests staying in villas for the first half to enjoy private space and flexible itineraries, then switching to hotels for convenient amenities and professional service—striking a balance. For large groups, villas really shine; if you're wondering how to choose three-, four-, or five-bedroom family villas, check out [Group Travel Villa Recommendations](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-group-villa-stay/).

> If you're still deciding how to split your trip across areas, [this site's accommodation area comparison](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-accommodation-area-guide/) breaks down transportation, shopping, nightlife, and natural scenery in Kuta, Legian, Seminyak, Canggu, and Ubud. For first-timers, the [Ultimate Beginner's Guide](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-ultimate-guide-2026/) recommends a combo of the south (Seminyak or Kuta) and Ubud to enjoy both beaches and culture. On this trip, we added Uluwatu as an extra stop on that foundation.

## Choosing a Private Driver: Compared 3 or 4 Options, Finally Went with A-Zhong

Before departure, we seriously compared 3 or 4 private driver services, with the only non-negotiable requirement being a Chinese-speaking driver. In the end, we chose A-Zhong, and looking back, it was absolutely the best decision of the entire trip.

The car was incredibly comfortable, the AC was ice-cold, his driving was super steady, and his Chinese was fluent. The price was also very reasonable.

Why is a private driver so important for a family group? We experienced almost all of these with Ah Zhong, and I'll share the details in the next section.

## Uluwatu Villa Puri Balangan (2-night stay): So Big the Whole Family Gasped

This place is ridiculously huge! The moment we walked in, it was like a scene from a classic Chinese novel—everyone was 'wow, wow, wow' in awe, excitedly exploring the rooms. The butler came out with welcome drinks, but couldn't gather us all together—it was hilarious.

The pool water was perfectly warm; we were still soaking in it at 10 or 11 PM, enjoying the stunning night view.

The biggest surprise was a mango tree in the yard. We casually asked about it, and without a word, the butler picked a whole bagful for us and even cut them up. They turned out to be black-fragrant mangoes—incredibly aromatic and sweet. Pure bliss.

This one also costs around USD$550 for two nights, again excluding breakfast.

Uluwatu sits at the southern tip of Bali, famous for its cliffside ocean views. ## 5 Days of R&R in Seminyak: A Side-by-Side Test of Two Hotels at Similar Prices

The second half of my trip—5 days of personal downtime—I chose to stay in convenient Seminyak and tested two hotels at similar price points. The verdict is strong.

| Accommodation | Two-Night Price (with breakfast) | Room | Breakfast | Other Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BOUTIQUE SEMINYAK FORMERL (My Pick) | About USD$81 | Slight musty smell on arrival, but you get used to it | Very generous and delicious | Free golf cart shuttle to the beach or town; live music at the ground-floor restaurant on Wednesdays and Fridays, dinner around USD$10 |
| D'primahotel Petitenget Seminyak 2 (Disappointing) | About USD$65 | Decent, no musty smell | Didn't suit my taste at all | Nearby construction, a bit noisy; no conditioner in the bathroom |

### Recommended: BOUTIQUE SEMINYAK FORMERL

Breakfast is a major highlight here—generous and delicious. I even tried snake fruit for the first time. The staff are incredibly sweet and friendly; when they saw I couldn't open it, they kindly peeled it for me.

The best part is the free golf cart shuttle that takes you straight to the beach or town—super convenient. The ground-floor restaurant also has live music on Wednesdays and Fridays, creating a fantastic atmosphere, and dinner prices are very reasonable, around USD$10 per meal. I highly recommend spending one evening here for dinner and music.

### Disappointing: D'primahotel Petitenget Seminyak 2

I moved here because of good reviews about the breakfast, but it was a letdown—the breakfast really didn't suit my taste. The room was decent, no musty smell, but there was nearby construction that made it a bit noisy.

The most frustrating part? No conditioner in the bathroom. Bali's water is quite hard, and without conditioner, your hair gets incredibly dry and tangled. This was the only place I stayed that didn't provide conditioner—just a heads-up to bring your own.

## South Seminyak or North Seminyak? I Choose the South, Closer to the Sea

After staying in both areas, I personally prefer South Seminyak, where BOUTIQUE is located. This area is very close to the beach, making it a leisurely stroll to Seminyak Square and the sand.

North Seminyak (around D'primahotel) seems convenient for transport, but the traffic is just too heavy, lacking that relaxed vacation vibe. The difference in atmosphere between the two areas is like comparing 'Taipei Main Station' to 'Taipei 101 Xinyi District'.

## Lessons from This Trip for Your Next One

- **Compare driver prices first; a Chinese-speaking driver is essential.** We compared 3 or 4 options and finally chose A-Zhong—the best decision of the entire trip.
- **Don't hesitate to ask for what you need at the villa.** A casual question about the mango tree led the butler to pick a whole bag of mangoes and even cut them for us.
- **Don't panic if the villa is down a narrow alley.** When the big car couldn't fit, A-Zhong sent a scooter team to haul all 9 large suitcases inside.
- **I prefer the southern part of Seminyak.** It's close to the beach, and you can easily walk to Seminyak Square and the sand.
- **Bring your own leave-in conditioner.** The water in Bali is quite hard; the only hotel on this trip that didn't provide conditioner was here.

This 9-day Bali trip was absolutely perfect. If you're planning a trip to Bali, I highly recommend hiring driver A-Zhong.
