In the tropical paradise of Bali, choosing the right mode of transport is a key concern for many travelers. For those looking to relax and wander around, figuring out the best way to get around becomes an important question.

A fellow traveler named Pingping, planning a trip to Bali, asked about transportation: if the two of them plan to explore near their hotel, with markets, beaches, restaurants, and spas all within a 15-minute walk, should they rent a scooter or use a ride-hailing app? Since everyone says Bali is prone to traffic jams, she doesn't want to waste time stuck in traffic for short distances.

In response, another traveler named James shared his personal experience. He said that before going to Bali, he had considered renting a scooter, but after actually trying Gojek (a scooter taxi service), he found it incredibly convenient! The scooter he had rented earlier just sat unused, and even on the way back to the airport, he didn't want to ride it again.

The advantages of using a ride-hailing app to book a scooter for short trips include on-demand service, very affordable prices, no worries about safety or police checks, and no need to find parking or pay parking fees at your destination. This convenience allows travelers to plan their itineraries more easily and enjoy the scenery without the hassle of transportation. Especially for those looking to move around short distances and relax on vacation, this no-driving option adds to the joy of the trip.

This way, she can easily enjoy the short-distance sights and activities in the city without wasting time in traffic.

### Frequently Asked Questions

## Is it safe to use a ride-hailing app for short trips on a motorbike?

Safe and convenient—no need to worry about traffic accidents or police checks when driving a rented motorbike, and you won't have to find parking or pay parking fees. Ideal for solo short-distance trips in the city.

## Should I use ride-hailing or hire a private driver for visiting multiple attractions in one day?

I recommend hiring a private driver. For visiting two or more attractions in a day, traveling across different areas, or moving between accommodations or to/from the airport with luggage, a private driver offers more flexibility and peace of mind than repeatedly booking rides. It's also affordable when split among a group.

## For a solo female traveler, is ride-hailing or a private driver better?

For safety reasons, I recommend a private driver, especially at night or when heading to non-downtown areas. Having a trusted, consistent driver is more reassuring than hailing different strangers each time.
