# 🆕 Brand Launch November 2026｜Arch-Designed Private Pool Villas in Canggu - The Arc House Canggu

Hong Kong hotel group Portier Hotels unveiled its new boutique lifestyle collection, The Arc House, on November 4, 2026, with the first property opening in Canggu, Bali. Locations in Hong Kong and Melbourne are set to follow. Minimalist interiors, archways as architectural signatures, and local artworks give each hotel its own character. This Canggu outpost focuses on private pool villas, making it a must-bookmark for anyone seeking a design-forward new stay this year.

📍 Location｜The hotel is near Batu Bolong Beach, surrounded by modern dining, independent concept stores, wellness spaces, and nightlife—right in the heart of Canggu's liveliest area. According to the hotel, it's about a 10-minute drive to Batu Bolong Beach; booking platforms also list walkable restaurants like Nostimo Greek Grill.

🏨 Facilities｜Rooftop Pool: An infinity pool on the rooftop offers sweeping views, especially stunning at sunset. Each villa has its own private pool, while the rooftop pool is a shared space—perfect for a change of scenery just one floor up.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1793800346194-15477.jpg)

MYKAZA: An Indonesian-Japanese restaurant blending Japanese culinary techniques with Balinese flavors, open to both guests and the local community.

Mello Spa: Located on the ground floor of the main building; treatments and schedules can be arranged with the hotel team.

Parking: Booking platforms indicate free parking and valet service are available.

🛏️ Accommodations｜The hotel features 19 suite villas with private pools, all duplex-style according to the official website. Photos show vaulted ceilings paired with patterned artworks and striped cushions, tea sets, and bathrooms with long countertops and deep soaking tubs—an overall minimalist yet refined aesthetic. Check-in starts at 3:00 PM, check-out at 12:00 PM; some booking platforms note breakfast is included, served from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM, but always confirm on the booking page.
