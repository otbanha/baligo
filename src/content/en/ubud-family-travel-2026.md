## Family Travel Tips

### 1. **Climate and What to Wear**

Ubud is located inland on Bali, with a cooler climate than the coast. However, during summer break (June to August), it's still the dry season with plenty of daytime sun. During winter break, Bali is in the rainy season, so you're likely to encounter brief showers when out and about. Here's what to pack for the kids:

- Lightweight, breathable clothing
- Sun hat and sunglasses
- High-SPF sunscreen
- Comfortable walking shoes (great for rice field treks or exploring attractions)
- Rain gear/raincoat (handy for light showers)
- Insect repellent (more bugs during the rainy season)

If you plan to visit the surrounding highlands (like Mount Batur or Kintamani), temperatures can vary significantly between morning and evening, so bring a light jacket or warm clothing. For those interested, check out the [Mount Batur Sunrise Hiking Guide](/en/blog/batur-sunrise-hiking-guide/)

### 2. **Health and Safety**

- **Mosquito protection**: Ubud's natural environment has plenty of insects, so it's advisable to use locally purchased insect repellent and choose accommodations with mosquito nets.
- **Drinking Water**: Avoid drinking tap water directly; it's recommended to buy bottled water or use boiled water.
- **Medical Preparation**: Pack common medications such as fever reducers, anti-diarrhea medicine, motion sickness pills, and band-aids. For reference: [What essential medicines and supplies should you bring for a trip to Bali, Indonesia? How to buy mosquito repellent and sunscreen?](/en/blog/bali-travel-medicine-checklist/) There are clinics in Ubud town, but for serious cases, it's best to head to an international hospital in southern Bali.

***

### 3. **Getting Around**

Transportation in Ubud is mainly by private car hire. It's advisable to book a reputable service and bring a car seat for younger children. Roads in Ubud town are narrow, and some attractions (like rice terraces or temples) require walking, so opt for a lightweight baby carrier or stroller. If your child is very young, avoid long drives and choose attractions close to your accommodation to minimize back-and-forth travel.

### 4. **Dining Options**

Ubud offers a diverse range of restaurants, from local cuisine to international dishes. Recommendations:

- Choose restaurants with good hygiene standards.
- Avoid overly spicy or heavy foods (such as babi guling, or roast pork).
- Many restaurants offer kids' menus, and you can also choose international chain restaurants to ensure the flavors suit children.

### 5. **Cultural Etiquette**

Ubud is the cultural heart of Bali, and locals place great importance on traditional customs. Teach children to respect local practices, such as:

- Wear a sarong and remain quiet when entering temples.
- Do not touch religious items or statues casually.
- Ask for permission before taking photos of locals.

### 11. Bali Exotic Marine Park — A Must-Visit Ocean Park for Families

![11. 峇里島海豚樂園 Bali Exotic Marine Park — 親子必訪海洋樂園](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-05/1779866556839-_2026-05-27_14.22.19.png)

Letting kids interact closely with dolphins is the biggest highlight of the Bali Exotic Marine Park. Located near Ubud, this family-oriented marine park combines rich marine life exhibits, educational programs, and interactive experiences, making it a perfect choice for a family trip.

Swimming with Dolphins: The park's most popular activity is dolphin encounters. Under the guidance of professional trainers, children can safely enter the water to play with these intelligent dolphins. It's not just fun—it's a precious lesson in marine ecology, teaching kids about dolphin behavior and conservation.

Rich Marine Exhibits: The park also features displays of sharks, sea turtles, tropical fish, and other marine creatures, each with detailed explanations to spark children's curiosity about the ocean. Regularly scheduled marine knowledge lectures introduce various marine life in an easy-to-understand way.

Family-Friendly Facilities: The park offers ample shaded areas, children's restrooms, and dining zones, ensuring comfort throughout your visit. With convenient parking and easy access, it's an excellent choice for a half-day trip.

Warm Tips: Bring towels and a change of clothes, and don't forget to apply sunscreen. The park is very popular, so try to book in advance to avoid waiting. Suitable for children aged 3 and up.

## **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

**Is Ubud suitable for traveling with kids?**

Ubud is a great destination for family travel, offering rich cultural attractions, natural scenery, and kid-friendly facilities such as animal parks, rice terrace views, and family-friendly restaurants.

**What other kid-friendly activities are there in Ubud?**

We've compiled 54 kid-friendly activities in Ubud—click [here](/en/blog/ubud-with-kids-guide/) to check them out.

**Are there any accommodations in Ubud with water slides?**

None that we know of. If you're specifically looking for family-friendly resorts with water slides, check out our curated list of 15 such resorts. Click [here](/en/blog/bali-family-resorts-water-slides/)

**What's the weather like in Ubud? What should I pack?**

Ubud has a warm and humid climate. It's best to pack lightweight, breathable clothing, sunscreen, and rain gear to handle sudden showers.

**Is the food in Ubud safe for kids?**

Ubud has many restaurants offering both international and local cuisine. Choose places with good hygiene standards and be mindful of drinking water safety.

**Is transportation in Ubud convenient? Is it suitable for traveling with kids?**

For families, transportation in Ubud mainly relies on taxis/ride-hailing services and private car hires. Opting for a private driver is recommended for convenience and safety when traveling with kids.

**Are there medical facilities in Ubud? What if my child gets sick?**

Ubud has clinics and pharmacies, but for serious cases, it's best to head to an international hospital in southern Bali. Travel insurance is highly recommended.

**Do attractions in Ubud require tickets? Are there discounts for kids?**

Most attractions require admission tickets, with children under 6 usually entering for free. Some places offer discounted tickets for children aged 6–12.

**Is Ubud stroller-friendly?**

Most areas in Ubud have uneven surfaces, so it's recommended to use a baby carrier or a lightweight stroller, and avoid using them in crowded or rough spots.
