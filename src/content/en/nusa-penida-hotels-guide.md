## Nusa Penida Accommodation Guide – Frequently Asked Questions

Before diving into where to stay on Nusa Penida, let's answer some common questions from first-time visitors:

### Where is the best area to stay on Nusa Penida?

The best area to stay on Nusa Penida is the west coast, home to Crystal Bay, Kelingking Beach, and Angel's Billabong.

Beyond the stunning beaches and breathtaking resorts, you'll find plenty of cafes and restaurants to easily while away the hours. Plus, the island's most beautiful sunset spots are located in this area.

### Is Nusa Penida Suitable for Families?

Overall, Nusa Penida is a great place for families, and you can find many family-friendly resorts. The island boasts beautiful beaches, crystal-clear waters, and friendly locals.

Plus, you can easily get around your neighborhood by scooter or on foot.

### Is It Worth Staying on Nusa Penida?

A trip to Nusa Penida is absolutely worth it, and I highly recommend staying for one or two nights instead of just taking a day trip. This allows you more time to deeply experience this charming island—not just skimming the surface, but truly feeling its raw and majestic beauty.

While a day trip is convenient for quickly visiting the most popular spots, staying overnight gives you greater freedom to explore secluded gems at a leisurely pace, and even catch the most ethereal views at sunrise or sunset. Another advantage of an overnight stay is that you can completely avoid the overwhelming crowds of day-trippers; many famous attractions are nearly empty in the early morning or late evening, offering a completely different atmosphere.

So, if your schedule allows, give yourself a little more time to fully experience the unique charm of Nusa Penida.

### Where Is the Nightlife on Nusa Penida?

The best nightlife on Nusa Penida is in the north, where you'll find beautiful cocktail bars offering happy hour specials.

The nights on Nusa Penida aren't as lively as in Bali, but you can still find some bars to enjoy cocktails and soak in the evening.

For example, I recommend Penida Colada Beach Bar, where the friendly staff and delicious food keep it open until 11 PM.

### Is accommodation expensive on Nusa Penida?

Nusa Penida is slightly pricier than Bali, but you can still find reasonably priced accommodations and eat cheaply at local restaurants. You can find hotels for around $50 per night and dine at local eateries for no more than $5 to $10 per person.

### Which is better: East Nusa Penida or West Nusa Penida?

Overall, the most popular spots are concentrated on the west coast, as it boasts the island's main tourist attractions.

However, the east side is also beautiful and less crowded, making it a great choice too.

### Best places to stay on Nusa Penida

There's no single best area to stay on Nusa Penida; you should base your choice on the attractions you want to visit and set up your base there.

That said, Nusa Penida is a relatively small island—you can drive from one end to the other in just an hour—so no matter where you stay, you'll have the chance to explore all the sights.
