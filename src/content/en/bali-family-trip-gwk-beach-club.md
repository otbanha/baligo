## GWK Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park

**Experience history and culture at Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park**

![「Let's Play and Eat 胡志明市」現場實拍](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_482457fa6320b8833b4987bb3367de92.jpg)

"Let's Play and Eat Ho Chi Minh City" live shot

In Bali, the Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park in Jimbaran, south of the airport, is a fantastic spot blending history and culture. Let's explore this vibrant and enchanting place together.

Video intro: Get to know Jimbaran in 3 minutes

**Understanding the Legend of Bali: The Story of Garuda**

Before stepping into Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park, let's take a moment to learn the story behind it. In Balinese legend, the filial Garuda is a divine eagle who, to save his imprisoned mother, sought help from Vishnu, one of the three supreme gods of Hinduism. After defeating evil forces, he willingly became the mount of Vishnu, the most revered deity in Hinduism, showcasing his loyalty and courage.

![「Let's Play and Eat 胡志明市」現場實拍](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_dbf3ddf735e4b600ced86bc0fffeb1a0.jpg)

"Let's Play and Eat Ho Chi Minh City" live shot

The tallest Garuda statue in the park stands 21 stories high, ranking as the fourth tallest statue in the world and the tallest structure in Bali, making it the most iconic landmark.

**Exploring Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park: A Journey Full of Surprises**

![「Let's Play and Eat 胡志明市」現場實拍](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_58de5112e860fd8065c1f38427286259.jpg)

"Let's Play and Eat Ho Chi Minh City" live shot

Although Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park is still under expansion, it has already become a must-visit destination for travelers. Whether you're seeking historical culture or admiring magnificent sculptures, this place has it all. The entrance fee is quite affordable, including a drink and an hourly spectacular show. Additionally, free shuttle service from the parking lot to the ticket counter is provided, making your journey even more convenient.

**How to Explore: A Relaxing Tour of Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park**

![「Let's Play and Eat 胡志明市」現場實拍](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_cbd1422938230952219c169102ac1d64.jpg)

"Let's Play and Eat Ho Chi Minh City" live shot

Since the Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park is quite large, it's recommended to use the shuttle service to get around under the Bali sun—it only costs 30,000 Indonesian Rupiah. This not only saves energy but also lets you better appreciate the park's scenery. Of course, if you prefer walking, feel free to explore at your own pace.

**Don't Miss the Spectacular Performances**

Beyond admiring the giant statues, GWK regularly hosts free performances like the Kecak fire dance. These captivating shows transport you into the mythical world of Bali. Be sure to check out other free performances in the park to enrich your journey.

![GWK的免費表演時刻表（僅供參考，以現場為準）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_8b068fe5175e2b091689326dbc8a1b43.png)

GWK Free Performance Schedule (for reference only, subject to on-site changes)

GWK offers timed free performances on the hour, with walk-in entry. If you don't want to spend extra to see the Kecak fire dance at Uluwatu, don't miss the free fire dance show here at 18:00!

We caught the 14:00 performance 'The Enchanting Beauty,' which lasted about 20 minutes—an interactive musical show that engaged the audience.

## **Kelly's Warung Beach Club: Enjoy Unbeatable Ocean Views**

Our second stop on the trip was a hidden gem: 'Kelly's Warung.'

![「Let's Play and Eat 胡志明市」現場實拍](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_1593d1e7d34e8cb4ed77625ef989f9f9.jpg)

Live footage from 'Let's Play and Eat Ho Chi Minh City'

The Uluwatu area is famous for its stunning coastline, and Kelly's Warung Beach Club is one of the spots where visitors can fully soak in the breathtaking ocean views.

**Explore the Unbeatable Ocean Views of Uluwatu**

At Kelly's Warung Beach Club, you can dip your feet in the cool pool water while gazing at the endless blue coastline stretching before you. Although many beach clubs come with a hefty price tag, Kelly's Warung offers great value for money—no entrance fee, so you can simply kick back and enjoy the seaside fun.

**Discover a Hidden Beach Club in Uluwatu**

Uluwatu is home to many well-known beach clubs, but Kelly's Warung is a hidden gem. It may be a bit tricky to find, but once you arrive, you'll realize it's a fantastic spot to unwind.

![「Let's Play and Eat 胡志明市」現場實拍](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_15a12a69ea37b9c5189e1056a084ad12.jpg)

"Let's Play and Eat Ho Chi Minh City" Live On-Site

Kelly's Warung looks easy to reach on Google Maps, but in reality, we kept wandering around nearby, struggling to find the entrance. Driver Gede had to ask around repeatedly—what a hassle!

After parking, you need to follow a small path downhill, navigating several staircases and slopes. Just when your legs are about to give out, Kelly's Warung appears right by the beach.

**What Makes Kelly's Warung Special**

![「Let's Play and Eat 胡志明市」現場實拍](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e44fadb70fda303ed523a6e8114d7b28.jpg)

"Let's Play and Eat Ho Chi Minh City" Live On-Site

Perched right on the beach, Kelly's Warung offers unbeatable value with no entrance fee and stunning ocean views that make you feel like you're on a European coastline. Dining prices are moderate, and they provide shower facilities for guests—so you can savor your meal while enjoying the cool sea breeze.

## Uluwatu Temple: Chasing a Romantic Sunset Where Sea and Sky Meet

![「Let's Play and Eat 胡志明市」現場實拍。神的住所「Meru」烏魯瓦圖寺廟不能進入，👣但沿著寺廟左右兩旁的小路前進，每一幕都是美輪美奐的無價美景💯](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_699d6b5bfb6c9c768a3ad042408e70ef.jpg)

"Let's Play and Eat Ho Chi Minh City" live shot. The sacred "Meru" at Uluwatu Temple is off-limits, 👣 but follow the paths on either side of the temple, and every scene is a priceless masterpiece 💯

Uluwatu Temple is a landmark at the southernmost tip of Bali, drawing countless visitors with its stunning coastline and dramatic cliffs at sunset. However, beyond the natural beauty, there are a few things to keep in mind.

**Beware of Monkeys Snatching Items**

The monkeys at Uluwatu Temple are highly aggressive and often snatch items from visitors' hands, so it's wise to stay alert and avoid unnecessary trouble.

**Explore the Scenery Around the Temple**

While you can't enter the temple itself, walking along the paths on either side reveals breathtaking natural landscapes. Every spot offers priceless views worth savoring.

**Witness the Traditional Kecak Fire Dance**

Two traditional Kecak fire dance performances are held daily, and visitors simply cannot miss them. This show blends natural beauty with cultural performance, creating a dreamlike experience.

**Notes and Tips**

![🗒每天兩場的傳統《Kacak火舞》，不容錯過，👉火舞售票處在園區裡面👈，進入園區後再另外購票](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_4139d1a27b7804fcabab19dca0472c64.jpg)

🗒 Two traditional Kecak fire dance shows daily—unmissable. 👉The fire dance ticket booth is inside the park👈, so purchase tickets after entering.

Entrance ticket prices vary depending on visitor status, so buy accordingly. Arrive early to secure ideal seats, and stay alert during the performance to avoid any issues.

**About the Fire Dance**

The Kecak fire dance primarily reenacts a segment of the Hindu epic Ramayana, featuring scenes like the monkey king's battle with demons. With dazzling flames and vibrant costumes, it's mesmerizing! For those wanting to learn more about this famous Hindu performance, [click here](/en/blog/kecak-fire-dance-bali/) for a detailed introduction I've written. It's a spectacular show that almost everyone finds well worth the price!

**Practical Tips**

- Seating is first-come, first-served; arrive early to snag a prime spot facing the ocean, with the temple on the right and the sunset on the left.
- The show lasts about 45 minutes, followed by a 10-minute photo session with the performers—make the most of it!
- Uluwatu's dramatic cliffs, sunset, and fire dance are truly a sight to behold.
- Both men and women must wear a sarong if their lower body attire is above the knee; if it's knee-length or longer, just tie a sash—provided free on-site.
- To interact with performers (like the Monkey King) during the fire dance, choose seats near the railings on either side.
- It's recommended to pre-purchase fire dance tickets via Klook to skip the on-site queue!
- Park entrance tickets must be bought on-site, but avoid buying fire dance tickets only upon arrival—snap photos with the cliffs first, as it gets extremely crowded!
