## 🌊 Waves Up to 4 Meters Off South Bali! Extra Caution Needed for Sea Trips Tomorrow

The latest marine forecast from BMKG indicates **waves up to 4 meters possible in parts of South Bali waters**, prompting local authorities and relevant agencies to urge fishermen, shipping operators, and marine tourism activities to stay vigilant. NusaBali also highlighted this sea condition as a top news story today.

For travelers, this news matters more than just a simple 'will it be sunny today.'

## 🚨 British Man Overstays by 305 Days! Caught by Immigration While Riding a Motorcycle and Deported

Bali immigration enforcement has a new case on its hands.

A **47-year-old British man** was deported by Bali immigration authorities for overstaying his visa and has been placed on the blacklist.

Even more shocking, he was found to have **overstayed by 305 days**.

Immigration officers noticed the foreigner's traffic behavior during a routine patrol, and upon checking his identity, discovered his stay permit had long expired. The man was eventually deported on September 11.

This incident has little impact on short-term tourists, but it's a stark reminder for long-term expats in Bali:

**Overstaying isn't as simple as 'just pay a fine and stay a few extra days.'**

If the overstay period is extremely long, in addition to fines (Rp. 1,000,000 per day, totaling around USD$18,000 for 305 days), you may also face **deportation and a re-entry ban**.

## 🚧 Munggu Irrigation Canal Encroached! Badung Orders Demolition of Illegal Structures

The Badung government announced today a piece of news related to land and agricultural management in the Munggu area.

After completing a technical assessment, the Badung Public Works and Spatial Planning Agency (PUPR) determined that **some irrigation channels in Subak Munggu have been blocked, and unauthorized buildings have appeared in the affected area**.

The government has ordered the removal of these illegal structures to restore irrigation functions.

On the surface, this has no direct impact on tourists, but it touches on a very sensitive issue that has emerged in Bali in recent years:

**Farmland turned into villas.**

In recent years, large amounts of farmland in Bali have been developed into villas, guesthouses, and commercial facilities. Local authorities are now paying closer attention to protected agricultural land (LSD) and the Subak irrigation system.

For travelers, there is no short-term impact; but from the perspective of Bali's tourism development, this is a policy direction worth monitoring over the long term.

## 🐋 8-Meter Whale Shark Carcass Found on Jembrana Beach! Experts Conduct Autopsy Before Burial

A massive whale shark was found dead and stranded two days ago on Pengeragoan Beach in Jembrana.

This male whale shark is approximately **8 meters** long, about 3.5 meters wide, with a mouth spanning 2.15 meters.

Relevant agencies, including the Marine Resources Management Unit, Jaringan Satwa Indonesia, the Indonesian military (TNI), and the police, arrived at the scene to handle the situation. A veterinarian performed a **necropsy (animal autopsy)** to understand the shark's condition before death and possible causes.

After the examination, the carcass was buried using an excavator around 5:30 PM.

The police also issued a special reminder to onlookers:

**Do not climb onto the whale shark carcass, and do not cut or remove any parts on your own.**

Whale sharks are marine species under conservation concern, so on-site handling must be carried out by relevant authorities.
