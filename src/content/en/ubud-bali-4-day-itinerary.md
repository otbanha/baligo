**Bottom line**: Recommended 4-day Ubud itinerary—vegan restaurant Wild Fire (signature Gado-Gado salad), Sacred Monkey Forest (**watch your belongings; monkeys will steal items from your backpack**, buy tickets in advance to skip the queue), and a leisurely stroll through the countryside around Campuhan Ridge Walk. The pace is relaxed, and you can enjoy a full 4 days without changing hotels.

Ubud is a must-visit for every traveler to Bali. This YouTube influencer [[Jits into the Sunset](https://www.youtube.com/@JitsIntoTheSunset)] shares videos online that introduce this cultural hub.

## Arriving in Ubud

Upon arriving in Ubud, they first dined at Wild Fire, a highly-rated vegan restaurant. The setting was serene, the food vibrantly colorful, especially the signature Gado-Gado salad—fresh vegetables and beans drizzled with peanut sauce, refreshing and appetizing, making them instant regulars.

## Sacred Monkey Forest

The next morning, they headed to Ubud's most famous attraction—the Sacred Monkey Forest. Home to over 700 wild monkeys, they had a rare chance to observe these agile, adorable creatures up close, watching them chase, play, and interact affectionately, vividly displaying instincts not far from our human ancestors. But then came an amusing twist: while they were focused on the monkeys, a cheeky little monkey opened their backpack and stole a wallet containing cash and bank cards! Luckily, a staff member quickly arrived, lured the monkey away with food, and safely retrieved the wallet. This gave them a surprising and unforgettable travel experience.

## Countryside Stroll

Leaving the Monkey Forest, they followed a path into the countryside around Ubud. The scenery was delightful, with lush green rice paddies stretching out in every direction, exuding a peaceful and leisurely vibe. They eventually reached a serene rice field that felt like a hidden paradise, reminding them of their earlier trip to Costa Rica.

This path, known as the Campuhan Ridge Walk, is covered in a dedicated article: [Just a Short Walk from Ubud Center Lies This Paradise? A Guide to the Campuhan Ridge Walk](/en/blog/ubud-campuhan-ridge-walk/)

## Art Market

Wandering through Ubud offers not only natural beauty but also a hub for Bali's art and culture. They explored the bustling art market, admiring wood carvings, silk, ceramics, and other handicrafts. The wife even experienced the most relaxing massage of her life at a temporary massage booth, completely unwinding both body and mind.

## Purification at Tirta Empul

For their final stop, they visited Bali's holiest temple, Tirta Empul. It was a full moon night, and the temple was packed with local devotees coming to bathe and purify themselves, showcasing the unique Hindu faith. Amidst this thousand-year-old heritage site, they were deeply moved by the devout atmosphere and touched by the temple's hospitality in offering free candies and fruits.
