**Bottom line**: Recommended 4-day Ubud itinerary—dine at the vegetarian restaurant Wild Fire (signature Gado-Gado salad), visit the Sacred Monkey Forest (**watch your belongings; monkeys may steal items from your backpack**; buy tickets in advance to avoid queues), and take a leisurely stroll through the countryside around the Campuhan Ridge Walk. The pace is relaxed, and you can enjoy four full days without switching hotels.

Ubud is a must-see for all Bali travelers. This YouTube influencer [[Jits into the Sunset](https://www.youtube.com/@JitsIntoTheSunset)] posted videos online that introduce this cultural hub.

## Arriving in Ubud

Upon arriving in Ubud, they first dined at Wild Fire, a highly-rated vegetarian restaurant. The ambiance was serene, and the food was vibrant in color—especially the signature Gado-Gado salad, drizzled with peanut sauce over fresh vegetables and legumes, offering a refreshing and appetizing taste that instantly won them over.

## Sacred Monkey Forest

The next morning, they headed to Ubud's most famous attraction—the Sacred Monkey Forest. Home to over 700 wild monkeys, they had a rare chance to observe these agile and adorable creatures up close, watching them chase, play, and interact affectionately, vividly displaying instincts not far removed from human ancestors. However, an amusing incident occurred: while they were focused on the monkeys, a cheeky little monkey opened their backpack and stole a wallet containing cash and bank cards! Fortunately, staff arrived in time, lured the monkey away with food, and safely retrieved the wallet. This gave them a surprising and unforgettable travel experience.

## Countryside Stroll

Leaving the monkey forest, they followed a path into the countryside around Ubud. The scenery along the way was delightful, with lush green rice fields everywhere, exuding a peaceful and leisurely atmosphere. Eventually, they reached a tranquil rice paddy that felt like a hidden paradise, reminding them of their previous trip to Costa Rica.

We have a dedicated article about this path, known as the Campuhan Ridge Walk: [A Paradise Just a Short Walk from Ubud Center? Introducing the Campuhan Ridge Walk](/en/blog/ubud-campuhan-ridge-walk/)

## Art Market

Strolling through Ubud offers not only stunning natural scenery but also a deep dive into Bali's artistic and cultural heart. They explored the bustling art market, admiring various handicrafts like wood carvings, silk, and ceramics. The wife even experienced the most relaxing massage of her life at a temporary massage pavilion, completely unwinding both body and mind.

## Purification at Tirta Empul

For their final stop, they visited Bali's most sacred temple, Tirta Empul. It was a full moon night, and the temple was crowded with local devotees coming to bathe and purify themselves, showcasing the unique Hindu faith. Amidst this thousand-year-old heritage site, they were deeply moved by the devout atmosphere and touched by the temple staff's warm hospitality, who offered free candies and fruits.
