**Bottom line first**: Recommended 4-day Ubud itinerary—vegetarian restaurant Wild Fire (signature Gado-Gado salad), Sacred Monkey Forest (**watch your belongings; monkeys will steal items from your backpack**, buy tickets in advance to avoid queues), and a leisurely countryside stroll around Campuhan Ridge Walk. The pace is relaxed, and you can enjoy four full days without changing hotels.

Ubud is a must-visit for every traveler to Bali. This YouTube influencer [[Jits into the Sunset](https://www.youtube.com/@JitsIntoTheSunset)] posted videos online, taking viewers through this cultural hub.

## Arriving in Ubud

Upon arriving in Ubud, they first dined at Wild Fire, a highly-rated vegetarian restaurant. The setting was serene, the food vibrantly colorful, especially the signature Gado-Gado salad—fresh vegetables and beans drizzled with peanut sauce, refreshing and appetizing, making them instant regulars.

## Sacred Monkey Forest

The next morning, they headed to Ubud's most famous attraction—the Sacred Monkey Forest. Home to over 700 wild monkeys, they had a rare chance to observe these agile, adorable creatures up close, watching them chase, play, and interact affectionately, vividly showcasing instincts not far from human ancestors. But then came an amusing twist: as they focused on the monkeys, a cheeky little monkey opened their backpack and stole a wallet containing cash and bank cards! Luckily, staff arrived quickly, lured the monkey away with food, and safely retrieved the wallet. This gave them a surprising and unforgettable travel experience.

## Countryside Stroll

After leaving the Monkey Forest, they followed a small path into the countryside around Ubud. The scenery along the way was delightful, with lush green rice fields everywhere, exuding a peaceful and leisurely atmosphere. They eventually arrived at a serene rice paddy, feeling as if they had stepped into a hidden paradise, which reminded them of their earlier trip to Costa Rica.

This path, known as the Sky Walk, is covered in a dedicated article: [A Paradise Just a Walk from Ubud Center? Introducing the 'Ubud Sky Walk' Campuhan Ridge Walk](/en/blog/ubud-campuhan-ridge-walk/)

## Art Market

Strolling through Ubud offers not only natural landscapes but also a hub for Bali's art and culture. They explored the bustling art market, admiring various handicrafts like wood carvings, silk, and ceramics. The wife even experienced the most relaxing massage of her life at a temporary massage pavilion, completely unwinding her body and mind.

## Purification at Tirta Empul

For their final stop, they visited Bali's most sacred temple, Tirta Empul. It was a full moon night, and the temple was packed with local devotees coming to bathe and purify themselves, showcasing the unique Hindu faith. Amidst this thousand-year-old heritage site, they were deeply moved by the devout atmosphere and touched by the temple's warm hospitality, offering free candies and fruits.
