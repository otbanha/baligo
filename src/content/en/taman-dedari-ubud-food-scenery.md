### Exploring Taman Dedari (Fairy Garden) in Ubud, Bali: History and Natural Beauty

![探索峇里島烏布的Taman Dedari（仙女園）：歷史與自然之美](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_921321dbf778f7e34410e97542da931c.jpg)

**Bottom Line First**: Taman Dedari is a fairy-themed sculpture park along the Ayung River in Ubud, spanning 1.5 hectares with around 50 giant Dedari statues up to 10 meters tall. It also houses The Royal Pita Maha restaurant, where you can dine with a view (English service, reasonable prices). Perfect for travelers who want to snap photos and enjoy a scenic lunch or afternoon tea—food and scenery in one go.

Located in Ubud, Bali, Taman Dedari (Fairy Garden) is a tourist attraction that opened in early 2021, situated in Kedewatan Village, Ubud District, Gianyar Regency, along Jalan Raya Kedewatan. The name comes from 'dedari,' meaning 'fairy,' inspired by the legend of Rsi Markandeya. It is said that while meditating near the Ayung River, this sage witnessed fairies descending from the sky, a magical sight that gave the river its name, symbolizing beauty and sanctity.

### History and Cultural Background of Taman Dedari

Taman Dedari is not just a beautiful garden but a place rich in history and cultural significance. The park's centerpiece—the Dedari fairy statues—are designed based on the legend of Rsi Markandeya. According to the tale, Rsi Markandeya saw angels descending to the Ayung River during his meditation, which inspired the park's name. Taman Dedari was established not only for commercial purposes but also to preserve and promote Balinese culture and traditions.

![Taman Dedari的歷史與文化背景](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_507d4792f3af26b330c57419a7fe12e2.jpg)

### Highlights of the Park

Taman Dedari is located within The Royal Pita Maha restaurant and hotel complex, covering 1.5 hectares along the Ayung River, offering stunning natural scenery. The most striking feature is the giant Dedari statues, standing up to 10 meters tall—these are not just artistic masterpieces but also symbols of Balinese culture. In addition, the park features around 50 statues of various sizes, including four about 10 meters high, four about 5-6 meters high, and smaller ones, all arranged along the Ayung River, showcasing exquisite carving skills.

### A Perfect Blend of Nature and Art

Taman Dedari is not just a place to appreciate art, but also a wonderful spot to enjoy natural beauty. The park is shaded by lush trees and features babbling streams, showcasing Ubud's unique natural scenery. Whether you're taking photos or simply soaking in the views, it offers a sense of tranquility and beauty. Additionally, there are multiple photo spots throughout the park where visitors can capture stunning moments—whether against the backdrop of towering green hills or focusing on magnificent statues, you're sure to take breathtaking pictures.

![自然與藝術的完美結合](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_1862dd5bf57adc27c8b2cc0bc568e380.jpg)

### Food and Leisure Experience

Beyond nature and art, Taman Dedari also offers a rich culinary experience. The on-site restaurant, part of The Royal Pita Maha, serves a variety of unique Balinese dishes and drinks. Here, visitors can enjoy delicious meals while taking in the views of the Ayung River and the grandeur of the Dedari statue. The restaurant offers reasonable prices, friendly staff who speak fluent English, ensuring a thoughtful dining experience.

There are plenty of great spots for photos and check-ins throughout the park, so feel free to snap away!
