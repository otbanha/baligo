One of the easiest things to overlook before traveling abroad is packing your luggage. Every year, many travelers have items confiscated at the airport, face boarding delays, or even get fined for bringing the wrong things.

According to international aviation safety standards and the latest requirements from various airlines, there are a few items in 2026 that are particularly easy to trip over.

## 🔋 Power Banks and Bluetooth Earbuds Must Be in Carry-On

Electronic products containing lithium batteries, such as:

- Power banks
- Bluetooth earbuds
- Camera batteries
- Phones, tablets
Handheld fans with built-in lithium batteries should always be carried in your hand luggage, never in checked baggage. The main reason is that if a lithium battery overheats or catches fire, it's easier to handle promptly in the cabin.

Handheld fans are a common pitfall item in recent years—if they have a built-in lithium battery, they must be carried on board.

## 💧 Liquids over 100ml should be checked

- Cosmetics and lotions under 100ml: can be carried on board.

- Shampoo, sunscreen, and skincare products over 100ml: should be placed in checked luggage.
Liquids carried on board usually need to be placed in a clear zip-lock bag for easy security screening.

## ☂️ Selfie sticks and tripods: watch the length

Folding umbrellas can generally be carried on board.

Selfie sticks and tripods longer than 60cm may need to be checked by some airlines, so it's best to confirm the airline's rules before departure.

If a selfie stick or tripod exceeds 60 cm, some airlines may require it to be checked in, so it's best to confirm the airline's regulations before departure.

## 🔥 Only One Lighter Allowed

Generally, you can only bring one lighter, which must be carried on your person or in your carry-on luggage; it is not allowed in checked baggage.

## 🍖 Don't Bring Meat Floss, Sausages, or Egg Yolk Pastries Back to Taiwan

Food products containing meat, such as:

- Meat floss pastries
- Sausages
- Cured meat
- Egg yolk pastries with meat

Bringing these back to Taiwan is high-risk and can result in heavy fines if discovered, so it's best not to bring any at all.

## ✅ Quick Summary

The three most common mistakes:

1. Putting power banks in checked luggage.
2. Carrying liquids over 100ml in carry-on bags.
3. Bringing meat products back to Taiwan.

Additionally, airline regulations may vary slightly, so it's best to check the official airline website before departure. Checking your luggage in advance not only helps you avoid security issues but also makes your journey smoother from the moment you check in. ✈️😊
