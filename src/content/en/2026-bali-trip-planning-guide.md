## **1.** Define Your Travel Style and Needs

Considering your travel style is a crucial part of planning your Bali trip.

Understanding your travel style is the foundation of planning a Bali itinerary.

- **Travel Companions**: Are you traveling with family, friends, or solo? This will influence your arrangements. For family trips, choose kid-friendly activities; for trips with friends, focus more on adventure and unique experiences.
- **Travel Purpose**: Are you looking to relax or seek adventure? This will determine the pace of your itinerary—whether it's a leisurely stroll or a challenging exploration.
- **Budget**: Your budget influences choices in accommodation, dining, and activities. For budget travel, consider more affordable lodging and free attractions.
- **Preferred Attractions**: Bali offers a diverse range of attractions. It's recommended to filter based on your interests to create a personalized itinerary.
