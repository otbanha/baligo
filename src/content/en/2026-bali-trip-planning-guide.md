## **1.** Define Your Travel Style and Needs

Considering your travel style is a key part of planning your Bali trip.

Understanding your travel style is the foundation for planning your Bali itinerary.

- **Travel Companions**: Are you traveling with family, friends, or solo? This will influence your arrangements. Family trips with kids call for family-friendly activities, while trips with friends can focus more on adventure and unique experiences.
- **Travel Purpose**: Is it for relaxation or adventure? This determines the pace of your itinerary—whether it's a leisurely stroll or a challenging exploration.
- **Budget**: Budget affects choices in accommodation, dining, and activities. Budget travelers can opt for more affordable stays and free attractions.
- **Preferred Attractions**: Bali offers a diverse range of attractions. Select based on your interests to create a personalized itinerary.
