# Bali Jet Car Experience｜Steer Your Own Water Supercar at Tanjung Benoa, Plus Jet Ski & Banana Boat Options

Water activities are among the most thrilling things to do in Bali. If you've already tried banana boats and jet skis and want something different, the **Jet Car** is worth adding to your itinerary.

This isn't about being towed along. You take the wheel yourself, cruising along the coastline in a 'supercar' on the water—turn whenever you like, even at high speeds.

## What Is the Jet Car Water Supercar?

The Jet Car is a water activity experience on Klook, set in the waters of Tanjung Benoa in southern Bali—often called 'South Bay.' This area is Bali's hub for water sports, with parasailing,海底漫步, banana boats, jet skis, and snorkeling all concentrated here.

Before the activity starts, a certified instructor gives a **safety briefing** and introduces you to the vehicle. Once you're wearing a life jacket, you can take the wheel and head out on your own.

## Three Packages to Choose From

This activity page offers three options, each a **15-minute** experience:

- **Jet Car Experience**: Drive a Jet Car yourself — the highlight of the event
- **Jet Ski**: Classic high-speed fun on the water
- **Banana Boat**: A group activity perfect for screaming with friends

All three meet at the same location. If you want to fully enjoy the water, you can focus on one option or mix and match based on your itinerary.

## What’s Included?

The page lists the following inclusions:

- Safety gear
- Lockers
- Shower facilities
- Basic insurance

After playing, you can take a quick shower and store your belongings—a thoughtful touch for travelers like us.

## Who is it for?

- **Thrill-seekers**: Those who want to steer their own course and set their own pace
- **Couples**: Share the thrill of speed on the water together
- **Friends traveling together**: The banana boat option is especially fun and lively
- **Solo travelers**: Instructors are on-site to guide you, so no need to worry about being alone
