# Bali Canggu Travel Guide

![峇里島Canggu長谷/倉古旅遊攻略](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f62143bd1a7a4f1f22631c0e674aa569.jpg)

Bali's Canggu is the hottest area in Bali right now, no contest! This enchanting spot draws countless visitors, whether you want to soak up stunning beach vibes or hunt down trendy cafés and restaurants—Canggu has something for every type of traveler.

Below, we introduce 100 Canggu attractions that are absolute must-visits on your trip, giving you a peek into this trendy yet traditional gem. We've prepared a guide map and detailed descriptions, including over 30 food recommendations, 3 newly opened beach clubs, 3 family-friendly parks, and tons of must-see spots... A total of 100 attractions collected—your one-stop guide to becoming a Bali travel expert!

## Batu Bolong Beach Surfing (👉🏼 Map: [Canggu Must-Visit Spots](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing))

Batu Bolong Beach is the most popular beach in the Canggu area. The waves here are a bit bigger, making it better suited for those with some surfing experience, but there are plenty of surf schools on the sand to choose from. It's not just a great place to unwind—it's also an ideal spot to learn how to surf. As you step onto the soft sand and listen to the crashing waves, you'll experience an unbeatable beach vibe.

## **Shopping in Canggu (👉🏼 Map: [Canggu Must-Visit Spots](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing)**)**

Canggu is one of Bali's top shopping destinations, especially at [Love Anchor](https://www.loveanchorcanggu.com/), [Canggu Center](https://www.canggucenter.com/), and [Shocked Dog Market Canggu](https://shockeddogcanggu.com/). ### **La Brisa's Sunday Market (👉🏼 Map: [Canggu Must-Visit Spots](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing)**)**

https://youtube.com/shorts/f0N6FE2dxys

La Brisa is a renowned beach club in the Canggu area, and their Sunday Market is an exciting weekly event. At this market, you'll have the chance to discover handmade crafts by local artists, unique artisanal goods, and fresh local produce. It's not only a great place to savor local flavors but also an opportunity to interact with locals and expats, experiencing their warm hospitality.

La Brisa's Sunday Market offers a unique shopping experience, allowing you to bring back some unique and beautiful memories from your Bali trip.

### Samadi Sunday Market (📌[Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing))

![薩瑪迪假日市集 Samadi Sunday Market (地圖)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_89b4424ac71b1812936b12f6f58b9ec5.jpg)

[Samadi Sunday Market](http://www.samadisupermarket.com/) is a popular Sunday market in the Canggu area. It is located near Echo Beach. The market is open every Sunday from 8 AM to 4 PM.

Samadi Sunday Market is known for its organic produce and handmade crafts. The market features a variety of stalls selling fresh fruits, vegetables, meats, fish, and grains. Additionally, there are many stalls selling handmade clothing, jewelry, home goods, and souvenirs.

Samadi Sunday Market is a great place to buy local products and support local businesses. It's also a wonderful spot to relax and enjoy food and music.

### **Tropicalife Canggu (👉🏼Map: **[Must-Visit Spots in Canggu](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing)**)**

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1790409098689-tropicallife.jpeg)

Tropicalife Canggu (also known as Tropicalife Bazaar) is located in the lively neighborhood of Canggu. It's an open-air creative market and cultural space with a tropical island vibe. It brings together local independent brands, exquisite handmade crafts, island-style clothing, and a variety of delicious snacks. It perfectly showcases Bali's thriving local craftsmanship and community energy, making it an ideal place for travelers to find unique souvenirs and soak in a relaxed vacation atmosphere.

### Lestari Thrift Shop Canggu (👉🏼Map: [Must-Visit Spots in Canggu](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing))

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1790409538207-lestari_thrift_shop_canggu.jpg)

Lestari Thrift Shop is a popular hybrid space that combines thrift shopping for secondhand clothing with a relaxing café.

This two-story standalone building features a bright, spacious interior with a hipster vibe. The first floor houses a cozy coffee area where guests can enjoy coffee and light bites, while the second floor is a clothing section neatly displaying a wide array of second-hand men's and women's apparel, unique bags, and accessories. Unlike traditional cramped thrift stores, it offers a comfortable shopping experience, making it a perfect spot in Bali for sustainable fashion, vintage finds, and photo-worthy moments.

## Visit Tanah Lot Temple (👉🏼 Map: [Must-Visit Spots in Canggu](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing))

Just north of Canggu, you absolutely can't miss the ancient and sacred site of Tanah Lot Temple (Pura Tanah Lot). Built on a massive rock formation jutting out into the sea, it's one of Bali's most iconic seaside temples. At sunset, the view is especially breathtaking, with golden sunlight illuminating the temple and creating an unforgettable scene. Beyond the stunning scenery, you'll also feel the deep spiritual and cultural significance of the place—a truly soulful and historically rich visit. For a detailed guide and visiting etiquette, [check here](/en/blog/bali-tanah-lot-guide/).

## Beach ATV

![沙灘ATV](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_27db942cd662ee9234e50f4f6aa3e50e.png)

Most ATV adventures in Bali take place in the jungle, weaving through dense tropical vegetation and rugged terrain for a thrilling blend of excitement and nature. However, Bali's beach ATV adventures offer a completely different experience, letting you race along the sand while taking in stunning coastal views. The sound of crashing waves, the gentle breeze on your face, and the magnificent coastline add a unique charm to this adventure. This distinctive combination of ocean, sand, and ATV riding creates an unforgettable visual and sensory feast.

Riding an ATV on the beach isn't just an exhilarating activity—it's also a great group adventure. You can ride with friends or family through traditional villages, lush green rice paddies, and finally onto the vast beach. Experienced and professional guides will lead you along carefully designed tracks, providing all necessary safety gear and equipment to ensure your adventure is both safe and fun. Don't forget to capture action shots of you or your team riding along the coast with the blue ocean as a stunning backdrop, creating precious memories.

Explore Bali from a new height at the highest seaside viewpoint in Indonesia—the iconic Ferris wheel in Canggu! At 42.5 meters above ground, take in the stunning vistas of Bali's tallest structure: lush landscapes and an idyllic coastline where sandy beaches and the sea harmoniously coexist.

Whether it's the golden sunset painting the entire coastline in warm orange-red hues, or the serene early morning when the first sunlight kisses the azure sea, this is a paradise for photography enthusiasts.

We recommend visiting around dusk, so you can enjoy the daytime blue ocean views and then wait for the dazzling night lights to come on, making this Ferris wheel ride the most unforgettable memory of your Bali trip!

### 3. Magic Garden 🌸

![3.Magic Garden](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_c8bbf58bba752051e9dd287f40fdb9e5.jpg)

Located near Tanah Lot Temple, **Magic Garden** is a nature park that opened in November 2024, perfect for families and kids. Spanning 3,600 square meters, this attraction boasts rich plant life and diverse interactive experiences that are both educational and fun.

Highlights include the **Orchid Garden** and **Butterfly Garden**, where children can observe butterflies up close and learn about their role in maintaining natural balance. There's also a **Plant Museum** showcasing over 400 rare plant species and 500 types of orchids, allowing kids to explore nature and conservation while having fun.

Magic Garden not only captivates with its beautiful landscapes but also promotes sustainability, preserving 70% green space and planting over 15,000 trees. Tickets cost just IDR 150,000 (about USD$5), making it an affordable and ideal choice for family outings.

### 4. Aurora Media Park

A new attraction in Canggu, Bali—**Aurora Media Park Bali**—is a landmark blending nature with multimedia interactive technology, located in the Nuanu Creative Area of Tabanan Regency. This open-air forest experience, about 1 kilometer long, uses **AI technology** to create an immersive environment of light and sound interaction. Spanning roughly 5,000 square meters of natural forest, it delivers a multi-sensory impact on sight, hearing, and spirit, as if stepping into another dimension. Through AI, visitors can experience magical light interactions, such as responsive beams, dynamic shadows, and interactive mirrors, crafting a unique journey of discovery.

Aurora Park is not just a nighttime attraction but an immersive space combining art, nature, and technology, perfect for travelers who love exploration and adventure. From glowing bamboo lights and spiral sundials to the magical interactions of the Crystal Oasis, it's an experience that leaves you spellbound.
