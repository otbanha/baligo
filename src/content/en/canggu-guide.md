## Batu Bolong Beach Surfing (👉🏼 Map: [Canggu Must-Visit Spots](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing))

Batu Bolong Beach is the most popular beach in Canggu. The waves here are a bit bigger, making it better suited for experienced surfers, but there are still plenty of surf schools on the sand to choose from. It's not just a great place to unwind—it's also an ideal spot to learn surfing. As you step onto the soft sand and listen to the waves crash, you'll feel an unmatched beach experience.

## **Shopping in Canggu (👉🏼 Map: **[Must-Visit Spots in Canggu](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing)**)**

Canggu is one of Bali's top shopping destinations, especially at [Love Anchor](https://www.loveanchorcanggu.com/), [Canggu Center](https://www.canggucenter.com/), and [Shocked Dog Market Canggu](https://shockeddogcanggu.com/). These three spots offer a diverse range of goods, from local handicrafts to trendy fashion, satisfying all your shopping cravings. Wander through these unique stores and you might find some one-of-a-kind Balinese souvenirs.

### **La Brisa's Sunday Market (👉🏼 Map: **[Must-Visit Spots in Canggu](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing)**)**

https://youtube.com/shorts/f0N6FE2dxys

La Brisa is a renowned beach club in the Canggu area, and their Sunday Market is an exciting weekly event. At this market, you'll discover handcrafted items from local artists, unique handmade goods, and fresh local produce. It's not only a great spot to experience local flavors but also a place to interact with locals and expats, soaking up their warm hospitality.

La Brisa's Sunday Market offers a unique shopping experience, allowing you to bring back some distinctive and beautiful memories from your Bali trip.

### Samadi Sunday Market (📌[Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing))

![薩瑪迪假日市集 Samadi Sunday Market (地圖)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_89b4424ac71b1812936b12f6f58b9ec5.jpg)

[Samadi Sunday Market](http://www.samadisupermarket.com/) is a popular Sunday market in the Canggu area. It's located near Echo Beach. The market is open every Sunday from 8 AM to 4 PM.

Samadi Sunday Market is known for its organic produce and handmade crafts. The market features a variety of stalls selling fresh fruits, vegetables, meats, fish, and grains. Additionally, there are many stalls offering handmade clothing, jewelry, home goods, and souvenirs.

Samadi Sunday Market is a great place to buy local products and support local businesses. It's also a wonderful spot to relax and enjoy good food and music.

### **Tropicalife Canggu (👉🏼Map: **[Canggu Must-Visit Spots](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing)**)**

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1790409098689-tropicallife.jpeg)

Tropicalife Canggu (also known as Tropicalife Bazaar) is located in the lively neighborhood of Canggu. It's an open-air creative market and cultural space brimming with island vibes. Here, you'll find local independent brands, exquisite handmade crafts, island-style clothing, and a variety of delicious snacks. It perfectly showcases Bali's thriving local craftsmanship and community energy, making it an ideal spot for travelers seeking unique souvenirs and a laid-back vacation atmosphere.

### Lestari Thrift Shop Canggu (👉🏼Map: [Canggu Must-Visit Spots](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing))

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1790409538207-lestari_thrift_shop_canggu.jpg)

Lestari Thrift Shop is a popular hybrid space that combines thrift store treasure hunting with a casual café.

This two-story standalone building features a bright, spacious interior with a trendy, artsy vibe. The first floor houses a cozy coffee area where guests can enjoy coffee and light bites, while the second floor is a clothing section neatly displaying a wide array of secondhand men's and women's apparel, unique bags, and accessories. Unlike traditional cramped thrift stores, it's a comfortable place to browse, making it a perfect spot in Bali for sustainable fashion, vintage finds, and photo-worthy moments.

## Visit Tanah Lot Temple (👉🏼 Map: [Must-See Spots in Canggu](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1BZB_5sqY7fAPbfZJkSKFuZI7ar1UTF8U&usp=sharing))

Just north of Canggu, an ancient and sacred site you absolutely can't miss is Tanah Lot Temple (Pura Tanah Lot). Built atop a massive coastal rock, this is one of Bali's most famous sea temples. Beyond the scenery, you'll also feel the deep spiritual and historical significance of local religious culture—a truly enriching visit. ## Beach ATV

![沙灘ATV](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_27db942cd662ee9234e50f4f6aa3e50e.png)

Most ATV adventures in Bali take place in the jungle, weaving through dense tropical vegetation and rugged terrain for an adrenaline-fueled nature experience. However, Bali's beach ATV adventures offer a completely different thrill—racing across the sand while taking in breathtaking coastal views. The sound of crashing waves, the gentle sea breeze on your face, and the stunning shoreline scenery add a unique charm to this adventure. This blend of ocean, sand, and ATV excitement creates an unforgettable visual and sensory feast.

Riding an ATV on the beach is not only an exhilarating activity but also a perfect group adventure. You can ride with friends or family through traditional villages, lush green rice paddies, and finally reach the vast shoreline. Experienced and professional guides will lead you along carefully designed tracks, providing all necessary safety gear and equipment to ensure your adventure is both safe and fun. Don't forget to capture action shots of you or your team riding along the coast, with the stunning blue ocean as your backdrop, creating unforgettable memories.

### 3. Magic Garden🌸

![3.Magic Garden](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_c8bbf58bba752051e9dd287f40fdb9e5.jpg)

Located near Tanah Lot Temple, **Magic Garden** is a nature park that opened in November 2024, perfect for families and kids. Spanning 3,600 square meters, this attraction boasts lush plants and diverse interactive experiences that are both educational and fun.

Highlights of the park include the **Orchid Garden** and **Butterfly Garden**, where children can observe butterflies up close and learn about their role in maintaining natural balance. The park also features a **Plant Museum** showcasing over 400 rare plant species and 500 types of orchids, allowing kids to gain knowledge about nature and conservation while having fun.

Magic Garden not only captivates with its beautiful landscapes but also promotes sustainability, preserving 70% of its green space and planting over 15,000 trees. Admission is just IDR 150,000 (approximately USD$5), making it an affordable and ideal choice for family outings.

A new attraction in Canggu, Bali—**Aurora Media Park Bali**—is a landmark blending nature with interactive multimedia technology, located in the Nuanu creative area of Tabanan Regency. This open-air forest experience, stretching about 1 kilometer, uses **AI technology** to create an immersive environment of light and sound interaction. Spanning approximately 5,000 square meters of natural forest, it delivers a multi-sensory impact on sight, hearing, and spirit, making visitors feel as if they've entered another dimension. Aurora Park is not just a nighttime attraction but an immersive space combining art, nature, and technology, perfect for travelers who love exploration and adventure.
