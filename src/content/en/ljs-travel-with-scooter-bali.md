> The following content is adapted from a travel diary shared by group member [Lin Jinsheng](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/16zBggKw15/):

I want to share our **8-day, 7-night scooter 🛵 self-guided tour of Bali** as a couple. Exploring every corner of the island on two wheels was an exhilarating and liberating experience for us. The biggest tip for riding a scooter in Bali is: **"Yield! Yield! Yield!"** Whether at a narrow T-junction or a chaotic intersection, knowing when to give way is crucial. Ride with "boldness and caution," follow the rhythm of the traffic ahead, speed up when needed, slow down when necessary, and don't hesitate. This way, your entire journey will be both safe and enjoyable. Once you get the hang of it, riding in Bali can actually be a lot of fun!

## Accommodation in Kuta

**Day 1**: After arriving at the hotel in the afternoon, we didn't waste any time and immediately rented a scooter to kick off our self-guided adventure. We took a casual spin around Kuta and Seminyak to get familiar with the roads and traffic vibe. For dinner, we chose the locally famous **Wahaha Restaurant**, where their grilled pork ribs are incredibly flavorful—tender, juicy, and perfect for hearty eating. That night, we stayed at **The Vira Bali Boutique Hotel**, located right in Kuta and very close to the airport, making it convenient for first-day arrivals. The hotel also offers a complimentary shuttle service and free coffee and snacks from 6 PM to 10 PM, perfect for relaxing after a day of travel.

**Day 2**: In the morning, we planned a leisurely **half-day beach trip to Nusa Dua**. The sand here is clean, the water crystal clear, and the atmosphere is much quieter than Kuta or Canggu, making it perfect for a stroll and some relaxation. In the afternoon, we headed to the newest large shopping mall, **ICON Bali**, which combines shopping, dining, and local attractions. The air-conditioned environment was also a great escape from the midday heat.

https://youtu.be/hdIKOX5wghU

By evening, we made a special trip to Denpasar's famous **Asoka Market Night Market**, the largest and liveliest night market in Bali. The stalls were packed with options, and the local snacks were both affordable and delicious. We ordered babi guling (suckling pig rice), bakso (meatball soup), and a serving of nasi goreng (Indonesian fried rice), each costing just 25K IDR (less than USD$1), leaving us full and satisfied. We returned to Kuta for the night, recharging for the next day's adventure.

> Editor's Note: **Asoka Market** is often grouped together with the neighboring **Kereneng Night Market** as one massive, bustling night market area with thousands of stalls. From authentic Babi Guling (roast pork) to colorful batik fabrics, handmade tableware, snacks, and all kinds of desserts, it has everything. Visitors start pouring in from 4 PM, stepping into a vibrant universe of food and culture.

## Ubud Accommodation

**Day 3**: This day marked the highlight of our trip. We left our large luggage at the hotel in Kuta, packed two 7 kg carry-ons secured with cargo straps, and slung on small backpacks for a light journey. We rode a scooter straight to our accommodation in Ubud, dropped off our bags, and officially began our adventure. Our first stop was **Pura Ulun Danu Bratan**, perched at an altitude of 1,200 meters. This lakeside water temple seemed to float on the lake, surrounded by mountains and mist, creating a dreamlike scene straight out of a postcard.

Back in Ubud, we had dinner at **Pison Ubud**. Their signature 'Ubud Dirty Duck Rice' lived up to its reputation—crispy fried duck paired with fragrant spiced rice, rich and satisfying.

**Day 4**: The highlight of this day was the **Mount Batur sunrise**. We woke up at 3 AM, quickly got ready, and set off on our scooter. The ride from Ubud to the volcano was about 45 kilometers, taking roughly 70 minutes. Instead of a strenuous hike, we headed straight to the **Sukawana Sunrise Spot** viewpoint. Here, we could easily watch the sunrise over the volcano without the exhaustion of climbing, enjoying a sense of freedom. As the sun rose from behind the clouds, painting the volcano and lake in golden light, every bit of the early wake-up felt worth it.

The layered green terraces, swaying coconut trees, and stunning views made every shot postcard-worthy.

The sunset at Uluwatu Temple is one of the most unforgettable sights in Bali for many travelers. The temple is built into the cliffside, which drops straight into the Indian Ocean. As the sun slowly sinks into the sea, the entire sky turns golden and orange-red, reflecting off the dramatic coastline—an atmosphere both romantic and sacred. Pairing this with the famous Kecak dance performance makes for a truly powerful experience of Bali's unique blend of culture and nature.

**Day Six**: Today's itinerary was more relaxed, and we visited the **Pandawa Beach area**. Originally a hidden secret beach, it's now developed into a full tourist zone. Once inside, there's a special experience not to be missed: you must buy a ticket for a shuttle cart (30K IDR per person) to reach **Tanah Barak**, known as the 'Blue Road.' The main attraction here is the towering limestone cliffs on both sides, which some describe as 'Bali's version of Japan's Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route snow walls,' only made of limestone. This scene is very popular among young people, offering dramatic photo opportunities.

![這處壯觀的岩壁近年成為許多網紅口耳相傳的打卡景點，因為角度與光影的變化，無論怎麼拍都顯得特別好看。要前往這裡，必須先從 Pandawa Beach 入口進入，再搭乘園區內的接駁車才能抵達。沿途景色也相當迷人，抵達後更能感受到岩壁與藍天大海交織出的震撼畫面，是拍照愛好者不能錯過的美拍地點。](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_4f8fe37b3a9f45664aad73384dc6a559.jpg)

This impressive cliff area has become a popular check-in spot among influencers in recent years, thanks to the interplay of angles and light that makes every shot look particularly good. To get here, you must first enter through the Pandawa Beach entrance and then take the park's shuttle. The scenery along the way is also captivating, and upon arrival, you'll be struck by the dramatic contrast of the cliffs against the blue sky and sea—a must-visit photo spot for photography enthusiasts.
