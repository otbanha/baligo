**Bottom line**: Spanning 90 hectares, the Ayana Bali Resort features **4 distinct hotel zones** (AYANA/RIMBA, etc.), **14 pools** (including the Instagram-famous H2O Pool), the renowned sunset bar **Rock Bar** (reservations recommended), a private cliffside beach, and an 18-hole golf course—perfect for travelers wanting to spend multiple days exploring the same complex.

Ayana Resort in Bali is an extremely popular accommodation spot on the island. A video released by YouTuber "Designer Play" takes viewers through his wonderful experience there.

Right from the start, he's captivated by the stunning views inside and outside his room. Floor-to-ceiling windows open onto lush green lawns and a distant coastline, bathed in sunlight and accompanied by birdsong and the hum of insects—creating a truly laid-back vacation vibe.

!["設計師不累designer play" 視頻截圖](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_6256d9da21f172190b812ba94e8f1cfd.jpg)

Screenshot from "Designer Play" video

On the first morning after checking in, he starts his day with a swim in the villa's infinity pool, soaking in the breathtaking, otherworldly scenery.

!["設計師不累designer play" 視頻截圖](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0786af7ca3ef2945dc26299c1d33e173.jpg)

Screenshot from "Designer Play" video

As evening falls, he changes into casual attire and takes a resort golf cart to the world-famous Rock Bar. Upon arrival, a host leads him to the best open-air seat with a panoramic view of the Indian Ocean. Watching the sun slowly set, he enjoys a live romantic music performance while sampling a flavored hookah, adjusting its different tastes to his liking. After dinner, he reluctantly leaves one of the world's top ten most beautiful bars.

!["設計師不累designer play" 視頻截圖](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a3dedf9508b6892ebd211a0be43b660d.jpg)

Screenshot from "Designer Play" video

On the second day, after switching to a different villa room type, he continued exploring the many pool areas of Ayana. He started with a swim in the Rimba pool, taking in the tropical floral scenery by the poolside. Then he took a shuttle to Ayana Resort, where he swam to his heart's content in the large pool, occasionally sliding down the water slides a few times. Finally, he arrived at the H2O pool, the most popular Instagram-worthy photo spot, where he also snapped some travel souvenir photos.

!["設計師不累designer play" 視頻截圖](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7c29bcc10ea65ed9bddc844e6ba079c1.jpg)

"Designer Play" video screenshot

Beyond the pools, he also made a special trip down the cliff to Ayana's private beach to play in the water and sand. The fine white sand stretched out, half soft white sand and the other half covered in smooth pebbles, which felt especially comfortable underfoot. He rarely relaxed so much, playing freely on the beach, building sandcastles with beach tools, and clicking away to capture the beauty of the scenery.

During this time, he didn't miss the chance to taste local flavors, ordering dishes full of Indonesian flair like roast duck, grilled fish, and grilled chicken, or ordering rice and fried chicken snacks in the villa, greedily savoring the unique local tastes.

At the end of the trip, Designer Play shared his observations on Balinese culture. Due to the devout Hindu faith, there are restrictions on modernization, such as buildings not exceeding the height of coconut trees. But it is precisely this adherence to traditional culture that preserves the unique local customs and flavors. In this vacation paradise, he immersed himself in a lazy and leisurely atmosphere, fully enjoying this unforgettable journey.

## About Ayana Resort

Bali's Ayana Resort is a massive resort complex beloved by travelers, spanning 90 hectares. The entire complex features four distinct hotel zones, each representing a different level of luxury, 19 restaurants and bars, 14 different pools, an 18-hole golf course, a kids' club, sunset yoga, the famous Rock Bar, a private beach, and more—not to mention unparalleled ocean views!
