**Location**｜The hotel is in Seminyak's Double Six neighborhood (Jl. Arjuna), about a 4-minute walk to Double Six Beach and Seminyak Beach. Nearby spots like Santorini, Blue 9 Beach Bar, Cocoon Beach Club, and Bintang Supermarket are all within a few minutes' walk. For shopping and dining, Eat Street and Seminyak Square are about a 20-minute walk away, and the airport is roughly a 30-minute drive.

**Facilities**｜The hotel features an outdoor pool, garden, restaurant, and bar, with a 24-hour front desk, room service, and free WiFi. Airport transfers are also available. The SPA offers treatments like full-body massage, back massage, and foot baths. Pool hours are from 9 AM to 6 PM. Breakfast options include Continental, American, and Asian. Parking is limited.

**Rooms**｜The hotel offers a diverse range of room types:

- **The Standard Room**: 23 sqm, the entry-level room, priced at USD$30 per night in low season.
- **The Triple Room**: Ideal for three guests sharing.
- **The Big Sky Room with Swing**: Features a swing design.
- **The Suite with Private Pool**: 67 sqm, comes with a private pool, the highest-tier room in the property.

The rooms are equipped with a safe, desk, iron, and free WiFi.

**Review**｜Rated 8.6 out of 10 on Agoda, labeled as 'Excellent,' with guests often praising the friendly staff. Couples rate the location 8.7. Some guests mention the front desk team provides great service, and the rooms are clean and comfortable.
