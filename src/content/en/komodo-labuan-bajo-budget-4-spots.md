## Budget Travel in Komodo / Labuan Bajo: 4 Hidden Gems Under Rp 1,000,000

When you hear Komodo Island / Labuan Bajo, the first thing that comes to mind is often 'expensive.' You need to charter a boat, book accommodation, and plan an itinerary.

But I think this destination offers a lot of flexibility. A local Indonesian media outlet compiled a budget-friendly guide, keeping costs between Rp 500,000 and Rp 1,000,000, highlighting four worthwhile spots. I’ve reorganized it for Taiwanese travelers. For a complete guide on seeing Komodo dragons, liveaboard experiences, and transportation, pair this with the article [Labuan Bajo Travel: A Complete Guide to Komodo Dragons, Pink Beach, and Liveaboard Experiences](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/komodo-island-labuan-bajo-travel/).

## 1. Padar Island: Hike to the Summit for a View of Three-Colored Beaches

https://www.instagram.com/p/DSMvXNbkYDh/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&xtok=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==

Padar Island is part of Komodo National Park, administratively located in West Manggarai, East Nusa Tenggara (NTT). It’s famous for having three beach colors—white, red, and black—all on one island.

Once you hike to the summit, the panoramic view opens up, which is why Padar has become the iconic image of Labuan Bajo. Besides hiking and photography, you can also swim, snorkel, and sunbathe on the island.

Entrance fees for international visitors vary by activity type:

- Trekking: Rp 400,000
- Photography: Rp 550,000
- Birdwatching: Rp 750,000
- Fishing: Rp 800,000
- Videography: Rp 750,000

Most travelers choose trekking—Rp 400,000 gets you up the mountain for the views.

## 2. Kanawa Island: Go Here If You Just Want to Unwind

https://www.instagram.com/reel/CyA9-A4Bra5/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&rpxt=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==

Kanawa Island, also in West Manggarai, boasts crystal-clear waters, coral reefs, and white sandy beaches, making it great for diving too. If you want to set aside a day to fully relax, this island is a perfect choice.

Here's a breakdown of costs:

- Entrance fee: Rp 50,000
- Boat rental: Rp 400,000 to Rp 600,000, approximately 1.5 hours one way
- Accommodation (per night): Tent Rp 170,000 to Rp 200,000, Bale (gazebo-style hut) Rp 275,000, Bungalow (2 people) Rp 460,000

For an overnight stay on the island, a tent is the easiest option.

## 3. Pink Beach (Pantai Pink): The sand really is pink

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfPbNSpO0w1/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&srtk=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==

Pink Beach is located in West Flores. The pink color comes from Foraminifera, microorganisms that give coral a reddish pigment. When crushed coral mixes with white sand, it creates a soft pink hue.

There’s plenty to do in this area:

- Snorkeling
- Spotting Komodo dragons
- Climb the nearby Bukit Sabana hill for a panoramic view

Costs:

- Entrance fee: Rp 50,000 for foreign visitors
- Boat: Rp 30,000 to Rp 40,000 for a public boat, Rp 800,000 to Rp 900,000 for a private charter
- Accommodation: Rp 346,000 to Rp 1,000,000

On a budget? Take the public boat. Want flexibility? Charter one—both options are available.

## 4. Wae Rebo: A Traditional Village in the Misty Mountains

https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dc-QuHZx1x6/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&xtok=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==

Located in the Satar Mese district of Manggarai, Wae Rebo is a highland traditional village. Cone-shaped thatched huts are surrounded by green hills, making it a perfect spot for slow travelers and photography enthusiasts.

Getting there involves about a 4-hour motorbike ride followed by a hiking section. Costs are as follows:

- Scooter rental: Rp 75,000 per day
- Car rental: Rp 800,000 to Rp 1,000,000 per day
- Guide: Rp 250,000 per person
- Village entry fee: Rp 325,000 per person for overnight stays, Rp 225,000 per person for day trips

## Tips Before You Go

- Get in shape before heading to Wae Rebo—you'll be hiking mountain trails.
- Prices for boats and accommodations vary widely across attractions, so set your budget first before planning your itinerary.
- Keep every spot clean to preserve their beauty for years to come.
- Prices above are from an August 2026 report; be sure to check with boat operators, accommodations, or official channels for the latest rates before you go.
- If departing from Bali, domestic flights to Labuan Bajo take about 1 hour and 15 minutes. For transportation and park entry rules, check out [this Komodo guide](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/komodo-island-labuan-bajo-travel/).

## Final Thoughts

I believe you don’t necessarily need to explore Labuan Bajo with a 'luxury charter boat' approach. Public boats to Pink Beach cost just Rp 30,000 to Rp 40,000, and a tent on Kanawa Island starts from Rp 170,000 per night. With a budget-friendly style, you can still take in the islands, mountain views, and traditional villages.

But honestly, the hardest part of a Komodo trip has never been 'where to go'—it’s how to piece together scattered spots, flights, boats, and accommodations into a smooth, hassle-free journey.
