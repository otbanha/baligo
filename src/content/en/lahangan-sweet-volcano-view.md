**Bottom Line Up Front**: Located in eastern Bali, Lahangan Sweet offers a **360-degree unobstructed view** with an up-close, eye-level perspective of Mount Agung. **Sunrise or sunset** are the prime shooting times, and the signature shot is the ‘treehouse platform.’ While it’s gone viral on social media, the spot still retains a low-key, tranquil vibe—perfect for travelers who love photography and want a half-day side trip.

**If you’re a fan of stunning natural scenery or hunting for that perfect Instagram shot, Lahangan Sweet is a must-add to your itinerary! It offers a 360-degree panorama of Bali’s most iconic Mount Agung, especially breathtaking at sunrise or sunset. 🌅**

## **About Lahangan Sweet**

Lahangan Sweet has quickly gained fame thanks to captivating photos on social media, yet it still maintains a low-key and serene atmosphere. It’s an excellent spot to admire the grandeur of Mount Agung, ideal for photography lovers or anyone wanting to soak in Bali’s natural beauty.

- **Entrance Fee**: 20,000 IDR per person
- **Getting There**: Drive to the nearby parking area, then walk or take an off-road vehicle to the viewpoint
- **Best Time to Visit**: Early morning or late afternoon during the dry season (avoid the fog and rain of the wet season)

***

## **How to Get There? 🚶‍♀️🚗**

1. **Arriving at the Parking Area**
   When you spot a wooden sign reading **Lahangan Sweet**, you're almost there! Park your vehicle by the roadside, then either walk or opt for an off-road vehicle shuttle.
2. **Walk or Off-Road Vehicle**
3. It's about a 10-minute walk from the parking area to the ticket booth, followed by another 5-minute walk to reach the viewpoint.

- If you'd rather avoid the uphill climb, you can take an off-road vehicle driven by a local driver for just around 50,000 Indonesian Rupiah (about USD$2), saving both time and effort!

4. **Reaching the Viewpoint**
   Once you reach the top, you'll find several viewing platforms scattered along the way, offering different angles of Mount Agung and the surrounding fields.

![有很多平台、不同的造景提供拍照](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_bf45a9e0eb142d561e514108cdda3688.jpg)

There are many platforms with various setups for photo opportunities.

## **Photo Tips 📸**

Lahangan Sweet not only boasts stunning natural scenery but also features viewing platforms specifically designed for photography. Some platforms have mirrors, and local residents skillfully use reflection techniques to help you capture dreamy photos that look like a 'Mirror of the Sky.'

- **Service Tips**: After taking photos, feel free to give a small tip to the locals (around 50,000 Indonesian Rupiah). Their photography skills will definitely not disappoint!
- **Instagram Advice**: Wear bright-colored clothing to stand out against the green fields and blue sky for the best effect.

***

## **Best Time to Visit ⏰**

- **Dry Season (May to October)**: Clear views make it the best time for photography and sightseeing.
- **Rainy Season (November to April)**: While the valley is lush green, you'll often encounter thick fog or rain, and Mount Agung may be completely obscured.

**We recommend visiting at sunrise or sunset—the interplay of light and shadow creates a more enchanting scene, and you'll avoid the midday heat!**

## **Tips and Friendly Advice**

1. **Extra Costs**: If you choose the off-road vehicle transfer, you'll need to pay an additional fee, but it saves you energy.
2. **Physical Demands**: If you're in good shape, the walk isn't difficult—it only takes about 15 minutes to reach the spot.
3. **Avoid Crowds**: The midday period (11 AM to 2 PM) sees fewer visitors, perfect for those who prefer peace and quiet.
4. **Bring Cash**: Local entrance fees and tips are mostly paid in cash, so it's advisable to carry small amounts of Indonesian Rupiah.

As for what "Lahangan Sweet" means?

- **Lahangan**: Indonesian for "open space" or "open area," referring to a vast viewpoint with expansive scenery.
- **Sweet**: English for "sweet, pleasant," perhaps describing the picturesque landscape that brings a sweet and joyful feeling.

Thus, **Lahangan Sweet** can be interpreted as "a scenic open viewpoint with sweet beauty." The name highlights the natural beauty and comfortable atmosphere of the spot, which is why it's attracting more and more travelers to explore!
