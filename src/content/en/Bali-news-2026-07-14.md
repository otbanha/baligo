# Hot Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers (July 14, 2026)

### ✈️ Bali Airport Launches Gebogan Floral Parade for Summer

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1783943951958-baliportalnews_-semarakkan-libur-sekolah-bandara-i-gusti-ngurah-rai-hadirkan-parade-gebogan.jpeg)

Bali's international airport has introduced a traditional Gebogan floral parade during the summer break, allowing visitors to soak in the island's culture upon arrival and creating a new photo-worthy highlight.

### 🌾 Ulun Danu Beratan Temple Adjusts Ticket Prices

Starting July, Ulun Danu Beratan Temple has adjusted its admission fees, with adult tickets rising from IDR 75,000 to IDR 100,000.

> Ticket prices for these attractions fluctuate with the peak season, so check the latest reference prices on the **[Bali Ticket Price List](/en/tickets/)** before you go.

### 🛣️ Uluwatu's New Traffic System Now Permanent

The Badung Regency government has announced that the one-way traffic and traffic diversion measures in Uluwatu will be implemented permanently, aiming to ease congestion during peak season. Travelers heading to Uluwatu Temple or Melasti Beach are advised to leave early.

### 🎏 July Marks Bali's Kite Season

Every July is the peak season for the Bali Kite Festival, with large-scale kite events held at places like Padanggalak Beach in Sanur, offering a chance to see traditional giant kites stretching dozens of meters long.

### ☀️ July Marks the Start of the Best Travel Season

July is Bali's dry season, with stable weather and little rainfall, making it ideal for snorkeling, diving, and island-hopping trips. However, popular attractions and restaurants also see a noticeable increase in crowds.
