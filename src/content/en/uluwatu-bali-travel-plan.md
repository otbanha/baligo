**Bottom line**: The ultimate one-day Uluwatu itinerary is — **Padang Padang Beach** (white sand surfing spot) → **Karang Boma Cliff** or **Nyang Nyang Beach** (hidden gem beach) → **Balangan Viewpoint** (best sunset spot) → **Uluwatu Temple** to catch the Kecak fire dance finale. This is compiled from the on-site exploration video by YouTuber 'Gypsy in Sneakers', covering attractions and trendy cafes in the four districts of Pecatu, Ungasan, Uluwatu, and Pandawa.

The YouTube influencer 'Gypsy in Sneakers' thoroughly showcases and introduces the tourist attractions in the Uluwatu area of Bali through her video.

She first explains how Uluwatu, once a laid-back surfing town, is gradually becoming Bali's hottest emerging tourist destination. Then she introduces the main districts of the area, including Pecatu, Ungasan, Uluwatu, and Pandawa, giving viewers a clearer picture of Bali's southern peninsula.

She first takes viewers to the famous Padang Padang Beach, which not only boasts white sand but is also a popular surfing spot, even featured in the movie 'Eat, Pray, Love'.

Introduction to Padang Padang Beach

Next up is Karang Boma Cliff, a steep and towering spot that's challenging but offers stunning views. She then showcases the breathtaking seascape of Dreamland Beach and the remote, hidden gem Nyang Nyang Beach, often called the 'last secret paradise'.

At Balangan Viewpoint, she enjoyed what's widely considered the best sunset view in the area. Beyond the beach, she also visited several popular local Instagram-famous restaurants, such as Drifter Cafe and Nourish Cafe, to sample local cuisine.

At Uluwatu Temple, she not only admired the stunning views of this cliffside temple but also witnessed the iconic traditional Kecak fire dance performance.
