## La Cabane's Location and Atmosphere

La Cabane Bali sits quietly near Balangan Beach in Jimbaran/Uluwatu, just a few minutes' walk from the sand. The owner, Céline, is a French woman who started with just two bamboo huts; now it has grown into five eco-friendly cottages and a three-bedroom villa with a private pool. Unlike a bustling resort, this is a healing corner where you can truly hear the sea breeze and smell the flowers and plants.

Although La Cabane has only a few rooms, each one is thoughtfully designed to offer a completely different experience.

* **Bungalow Mao (with Private Splash Pool)**
Perfect for small families or groups of friends, two King Size beds provide spacious comfort, complemented by a private plunge pool for maximum privacy.
* **Bungalow Paz**
Designed with just one king bed, it's ideal for couples seeking a warm and romantic atmosphere.
* **Deluxe Bungalow Lua (32㎡)**
Choose between a King Bed or Sofa Bed for ample space, especially suited for travelers who want to spend more time relaxing in the room.
* **Deluxe Bungalow Mar (42㎡)**
Larger in size, the sofa bed option adds flexibility, making it a popular choice for long-stay travelers.
* **Three-Bedroom Villa (with Private Pool)**
La Cabane's showstopper, accommodating families or small groups with a private pool and open-plan living space that feels like an exclusive vacation villa.

Each cottage is crafted from bamboo, wood, and natural materials, seamlessly blending Balinese local elements with European refined aesthetics. When the lights come on at night, the ambiance feels like stepping into a romantic tale set in a tropical jungle.

## Too Popular, Hard to Book

It’s worth noting that **La Cabane, due to its small size and immense popularity, is extremely difficult to book**. Rooms often fill up months in advance during peak season, and even in the off-season, early planning is usually required. Many travelers adjust their itineraries just to spend an extra night here. If you’re tempted, it’s best to book as soon as your travel dates are set—otherwise, you might miss out.

## Exploring and Enjoying Nearby

Staying at La Cabane, you won’t have to worry about boredom:

* **Balangan Beach**: A serene white sand beach just a short walk away.
* **Uluwatu Temple**: A prime spot for watching the Kecak fire dance and catching the sunset.
* **Padang Padang and Pandawa Beach**: World-class surfing spots, also perfect for pure relaxation.
* **Dining Options**: From Gooseberry and Single Fin to Ulu Garden, romantic dinners and charming bistros are all within reach.

## Quick Tips & Info

* **Check-in**: After 2 PM; Check-out: 12 PM noon.
* **Child Policy**: Children are allowed but at an extra charge; the atmosphere leans more toward adults or couples.
* **Price**: Average around USD$241 per night—slightly higher, but well worth it for the privacy and experience.
* **Transport**: About 30 minutes from the airport; it's best to arrange a transfer in advance.
