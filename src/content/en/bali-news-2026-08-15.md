# Hot Topics & News Roundup for Bali Travelers

## 💰 Indonesia's VoA May Rise to 1 Million IDR! Still Under Discussion

Indonesia's Directorate General of Immigration is evaluating adjustments to the **Visa on Arrival (VoA)** fee. Currently, the cost for travelers to obtain a VoA upon arrival in Indonesia is **500,000 IDR**, but a new proposal could raise it to **1 million IDR**.

It's important to note that this is **not yet a confirmed price hike**. The proposals under discussion include: if travelers apply online in advance for an **e-VoA**, the fee could be **750,000 IDR**; while those who apply for a VoA upon arrival in Indonesia might pay **1 million IDR**.

So for now, travelers heading to Bali **still pay the current fee of 500,000 IDR** — don't see '1 million IDR' and assume it's already in effect.

This news is worth paying attention to because, once implemented, visa costs will significantly increase for family trips, group travel, and frequent Bali visitors.

## 🛂 Bali Immigration Continues to Tighten Management of Foreigners — Don't Cross the 'Visa Purpose' Red Line

Bali has recently been stepping up immigration management for foreigners, not only checking residency documents but also monitoring whether foreigners' actual activities on the island comply with their visa regulations.

In recent months, there have been multiple cases in Bali where foreigners were penalized for **violating their stay purposes, working illegally, or engaging in unauthorized activities**.

For regular tourists, there's no need to worry too much; the most important thing is to ensure your passport, VoA/e-VoA, and stay period are correct, and not to use a tourist visa for work or business activities that require other permits.

## 🚗 Traffic Adjustments at Sayan–Sindu Intersection Starting Tomorrow, Some Routes to Ubud May Be Affected

The **Simpang Tiga Sayan–Sindu** intersection in Gianyar will undergo traffic engineering adjustments starting August 14, with some roads leading to the intersection closed or rerouted.

For travelers heading to Ubud or the Sayan area—especially those using chartered cars, self-driving, or arranging private drivers—these road adjustments could directly impact travel time.

If you have plans for attractions on the western side of Ubud tomorrow, it's best not to schedule transfers between spots too tightly, and let your driver adjust the route based on Google Maps and real-time traffic conditions.

## 🏨 Bali Accommodation Demand Continues to Rise, Summer Vacation Boosts Tourism and Business Activity

According to the latest data from Bank Indonesia's Bali branch, major business activities on the island have recently been driven by school holidays and tourism demand, with **tourism and construction sectors showing continued growth**.

This aligns with Bali's current peak tourist season. For visitors, popular areas may still see high demand for accommodations, chartered cars, and popular restaurants on weekends.

If you're making last-minute plans to visit Bali in mid-August—especially to **Seminyak, Canggu, Ubud, Nusa Dua, and Nusa Penida**—it's best not to leave accommodation and transportation until the very last moment.

## 🏄 Medewi Surf Competition Returns, Putting West Bali Back on Surf Travelers' Radar

After a four-year hiatus, the **Medewi Board Riders Challenge** in Jembrana, West Bali, has returned to Medewi Beach. The event drew surfers from near and far, reigniting interest in Medewi as a surf travel destination.

Medewi is known for its long waves and laid-back vibe, with far lower tourist density compared to popular surf spots like Kuta or Canggu.

If you've already explored South Bali's famous beaches, Medewi is well worth adding to your next Bali road trip itinerary.
