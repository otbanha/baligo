If you think the only worthwhile buys in Bali are rattan bags, sarongs, and handmade souvenirs, then the brand STELAR is worth getting to know.

STELAR is a design brand rooted in Bali, blending Indonesian traditional weaving techniques into modern handbag designs to create bags that balance resort style with everyday practicality. The brand has made its way into international retail outlets like Selfridges in the UK and Lane Crawford in Hong Kong, showcasing the design potential of Indonesian craftsmanship.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1792228784456-152.png)

## Brand Story | From Balinese Artisans to the Global Market

STELAR was founded by Lorna Watson. With extensive experience in the luxury goods and jewelry industry, she connected with a family of weavers in eastern Bali, where generations of traditional craftsmanship inspired her designs.

Her goal was not simply to repackage traditional weaves, but to use modern design to integrate these time-honored techniques into contemporary life, appealing to a broader range of consumers.

From its initial designs to entering international luxury retail channels, STELAR has gradually built its brand identity, transforming Bali's handwoven crafts from mere travel souvenirs into design pieces that can hold their own in the global fashion market.

## Design Features | Natural Materials and Hand Weaving

STELAR’s products are defined by handwoven craftsmanship, using natural plant fibers, bamboo, and leather to blend traditional techniques with modern silhouettes.

The brand’s designs avoid excessive ornamentation, instead highlighting texture, weaving details, and structural forms. From everyday totes and crossbody bags to vacation-ready clutches, wallets, hats, and other accessories, there’s a range of styles to choose from.

If you appreciate natural materials but want to steer clear of mass-produced bags, STELAR’s designs are worth a look. They preserve the organic feel of handwoven work while offering a more contemporary, stylish edge than typical woven souvenirs—pairing beautifully with dresses, linen outfits, or simple casual wear.

## More Than Just Bags: Honoring the Artisans Behind Them

STELAR’s brand philosophy goes beyond product design, emphasizing the preservation of traditional craftsmanship and the growth of artisan communities.

The brand states that its products are made by artisan communities in different regions, rather than being concentrated in large factories. Some items also come with unique digital identification, allowing customers to learn more about the craft and the makers behind each piece.

For handwoven goods, each item may vary slightly due to materials and the making process—a key difference from mass-produced products.

## Is STELAR Considered an International Luxury Brand?

STELAR is not a major international luxury group like Chanel or Loewe, but rather a brand defined by craftsmanship, design, and collaboration with artisans.

However, it has entered international retail channels such as Selfridges and Lane Crawford, indicating that its designs have gained attention in the global market. The brand also emphasizes traditional craftsmanship, community collaboration, and sustainable development, and is set to achieve B Corporation certification in 2026.

Therefore, rather than positioning STELAR as a luxury brand in the traditional sense, it's better seen as a distinctive label that brings Balinese and Indonesian handicrafts into the international design market.
