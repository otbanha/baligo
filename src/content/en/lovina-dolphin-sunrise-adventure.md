The following content is adapted from a travelogue shared by group member [Feilin Huang](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/15fgq7Bt9T/):

**Bottom Line**: For a six-day, five-night trip, we recommend this route: **Seminyak (beach/La Brisa dinner) → Nusa Penida → Tampaksiring → Lovina dolphin-watching (north)**. Pre-arranging your e-visa and tourist tax speeds up immigration, but baggage claim can still take about an hour. We suggest hiring a local driver to help with transfers between regions.

This time, we chose Bali as our travel destination for a six-day, five-night adventure. As a couple, we wanted to experience the island's culture, natural scenery, and cuisine. Our itinerary included visits to Seminyak's beaches, the spectacular Nusa Penida, and traditional Tampaksiring, with the help of a local driver to make our trip smoother. Let's dive into the highlights of our journey!

We arrived at the airport around 2:40 PM. Thanks to our pre-arranged e-visas, immigration was a breeze, and the tourist tax was handled easily. Unfortunately, waiting for our luggage took an hour, which made us a bit anxious.

Since Agus had a last-minute commitment, his nephew **Oddy** picked us up instead. He first took us to exchange money, then quickly drove us to **La Brisa** so we could enjoy dinner before sunset.

### Day 3

📍**Penglipuran Village**, ranked as one of the top three cleanest villages in the world. With fewer tourists in the morning, we could quietly soak in the local beauty.

![Day 3](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_139f7058772c6fa1200a75cee97ac9af.jpg)

Here, we also tried a local specialty—**snake fruit**—and **Loloh Cemcem** (similar to herbal tea), which took some getting used to. According to Agus, it's made from coconut water and ground plant leaves, with a sweet-and-sour taste. While not bad, I really couldn't get used to it. Give it a try if you get the chance 😆.

![Loloh Cemcem](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_191755c6fb20e4272bb5923d0a7dfab8.jpg)

Loloh Cemcem

[Editor's note: Loloh Cemcem is a traditional drink from Penglipuran Village. Cemcem leaves are one of the village's main specialties. Locals turn these leaves into a fresh, health-boosting beverage.]

[This green health drink is often thought to be bitter. Many believe cemcem leaves naturally taste bitter when processed into food. In fact, that's a misconception. You won't taste bitterness when drinking Loloh Cemcem because palm sugar syrup balances it out.]

[During preparation, cemcem leaves are mixed with other plants like jatropha leaves, betel nut leaves, and coral tree leaves. These four leaves are pounded, then steeped in warm water. Afterward, cinnamon and palm sugar syrup are added to neutralize any bitterness. To enhance the flavor, some sellers add coconut meat. Adding ice makes it even more refreshing! It's the perfect thirst-quencher in Bali's heat—your thirst will vanish instantly.]

[Loloh Cemcem isn't just a cooling drink; it also offers health benefits, like lowering high blood pressure and improving digestion. Because of these perks, visitors to Penglipuran Village often try it. It's also easy to find—almost every local sells it, usually right in front of their homes. A bottle of Loloh Cemcem is very affordable, so you can try it without worrying about the price.]

Before the trip, we contacted several drivers, but Agus was the fastest to respond, with a proactive attitude and very reasonable prices, making him feel incredibly reliable and trustworthy.

### Why Recommend Agus Jon Devn?

🫶🏻 **Customized Itinerary Planning**

At first, we only vaguely told Agus which attractions we wanted to visit, and he immediately tailored the most efficient route for us based on our needs. Not only that, but if anything changed on the fly, he could adjust at any time—super flexible, so we never had to worry about our plans falling apart. Agus wasn’t just a driver; he also helped us book tours and massages! The prices were even cheaper than what we found online or on local menus. It felt like having a local insider’s privilege, saving us a good chunk of money without even trying.

🫶🏻 **Excellent Time Management**

Every time we set a meeting time, Agus was not only punctual but often early, which left us both surprised and a little embarrassed—he was always ready before us, waiting by the car with a smile when we got in 🤣.

🫶🏻 **Impromptu Guide & Cultural Interpreter**

Once we arrived at the attractions, Agus didn’t just drop us off; he came along with us, acting as an impromptu guide, enthusiastically sharing the local culture and history. Even though our English wasn’t great and we struggled to follow at times, his passion still made us feel his deep connection to the land. When shopping at the market, Agus even helped us pick out fruit and haggle with the local vendors. Often, the prices they initially quoted us dropped significantly once he stepped in, making us feel like we had a shopping ally! 😆

🫶🏻 **Unbeatable Driving Skills**

His driving really put me at ease. I usually get carsick easily, but every time I was in Agus's car, I slept soundly without any discomfort. On two days we stayed in a treehouse villa, the roads were incredibly narrow and winding—a challenge for most drivers, but no problem at all for Agus. Truly impressive skills! 👍👍

One day we arranged a day trip to a nearby island, and Agus kindly let us leave our luggage in his car, so we could travel light without hauling heavy bags around. This thoughtful service really made a difference!
