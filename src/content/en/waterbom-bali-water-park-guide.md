## **Take Your Bali Trip to the Next Level!**

![讓你的峇里島之旅HIGH到最高點！](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_3367ef3d85a6593784914b7333f8c98f.jpg)

**Bottom line**: Waterbom Bali is **Asia's #1 water park** (winning the Best Water Park in Southeast Asia award in 2024). Located at **Jl. Kartika Plaza in Kuta** (across from Discovery Mall), it's open daily **9:00 AM–6:00 PM** and spans 5 hectares. Must-try rides include the near-vertical slides and the lazy river—perfect for all ages. Booking tickets online in advance is cheaper than buying at the gate.

Imagine this: screaming down a near-vertical slide in a tropical paradise, then floating lazily along a river with a coconut cocktail in hand, surrounded by lush greenery... That's the magic of Waterbom Bali!

![讓你的峇里島之旅HIGH到最高點！](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_5c80a6141d256ec92d7c933153320560.jpg)

This is the holy grail of water parks—**ranked #1 in all of Asia**! Since opening in 1993, Waterbom has been a sparkling gem in the heart of Kuta. Fresh off winning the 'Best Water Park in Southeast Asia' award in 2024, it's now aiming for the world champion title!

### 📍 Park Location

- **Address**: Jl. Kartika Plaza, Tuban, Kuta (right across from Discovery Mall—super easy to find!)
- **Hours**: Daily 9:00 AM–6:00 PM
- **Area**: 5 hectares of tropical water paradise

## 🎢 Ride Exposé: A Complete Experience from Heaven to Hell

Pictures can't capture how fun each ride is, so we've included videos for you to experience firsthand!

![遊樂設施大揭秘：從天堂到地獄的完整體驗](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_45b0eb26bf156e5b260472badcb9c28b.jpg)

### 💀 Daredevil Zone - For the Brave Only

Ready to scream until you lose your voice?

**🎯 Climax - The Trap Door Drop** The floor beneath you suddenly opens, and you plummet straight down... no joke, this will make you question your life!

**🎯 Boomerang - Human Slingshot** Want to feel what it's like to be toyed with by nature? This U-shaped slide will bounce you back and forth like a marble, and we guarantee your legs will be jelly when you get off!

**🎯 Smash Down 2.0 - Free Fall Extreme** An almost vertical slide that feels like jumping off a skyscraper. We suggest not eating too much before this one... you know why 🤢

**🎯 Twin Racers - Friendship Destroyer** Racing side by side with a friend? No, this is a head-to-head speed slide where you battle it out to the finish!

**⚡ Fast n Fierce - Twin Speed Demons** Two side-by-side open slides, designed for thrill-seekers chasing the ultimate rush! There's only one rule here: the faster, the better! Once you slide down, you'll be itching to go again—that adrenaline surge is downright addictive!

**🌆 Pipeline - Sky Viewing Deck** Want the best panoramic view of the entire park and the Kuta skyline? Pipeline sits right above the infamous Climax! The transparent tube lets you soak in the scenery while getting tossed around in sharp turns... though at this speed, the view might just blur into a streak!

**🎢 The Drop - Double Death Ride** This is the ultimate test for brave couples or best friends! Dropping from 17.5 meters high, you first twist through the AquaLucent colorful light tunnel, where dazzling hues and lights make you momentarily forget the terror ahead... then come three high-G impact points, ending with a wild 9-meter plunge! Two people clinging to a floatie, screaming together—just imagining it makes your legs weak!

**🐍 Green Vipers - Twin Serpents** A 20-meter-high choice between two snakes! Open or enclosed? The two routes wind through the trees at different paths and speeds, like two giant green serpents chasing each other through the tropical jungle, finally meeting in the same pool. The open slide lets you feel the wind, while the enclosed one offers a mysterious adventure in the dark! Which one will you pick? Or will you conquer both?

### 🌊 Medium Thrill Zone - Just the Right Adrenaline

Don't want to scare yourself silly, but still crave some excitement?

**Constrictor** - A 250-meter serpentine tube, spiraling down in the dark with a buddy

**Python** - Scream with friends on a big floatie—perfect for filming and posting online!

**Tailspin** - A 140-meter spiral slide that spins you dizzy, leaving you lost in every direction

### 🏝️ Chill Relax Zone - Vacation Mode On

**Lazy River** 🐌 This is the park's ultimate chill zone! A 500-meter circular river winding through tropical gardens, where you can float lazily on a tube, sip a cocktail, and gaze at the blue sky... Just be careful—you might drift away for an entire day!

**Aquaplay - Kid's Paradise** A water play castle designed especially for little ones, while parents can keep an eye on them from the poolside bar with a drink in hand.

**🏔️ Zuluu Hill - A Playground for Little Adventurers**

⭐NEW! This is the park's newest kids' hill play area, featuring a two-story mini peak with six slides waiting for brave little warriors to conquer!

All slides end in safe, shallow pools, so parents can relax in the shade of big trees or jump in and play with the kids! It's truly a paradise for family fun~

**FlowRider Surf Machine** 🏄‍♂️ An extra charge of IDR 150,000, but absolutely worth it! Learn to surf on artificial waves without worrying about getting cut by coral when you wipe out.

## 🍽️ Food Map: Way Beyond Typical Theme Park Eats!

### Main Battlefield Restaurants

**Wantilan Food Court** - A United Nations of Food Indonesian, Western, vegetarian, gluten-free... they've got it all! And the best part is, it's **actually delicious**, not the usual 'edible enough' theme park fare.

**Savannah Café** - Instagram-worthy photo spot with stunning bamboo decor. Their salad bowls, smoothies, and coffee are all highly Instagrammable—a favorite among health-conscious travelers.

**Sliders Bar & Grill** - A carnivore's paradise. Craving big bites of meat with ice-cold beer? Their burgers and BBQ will make you forget you're on a diet.

## 🏖️ Bungalow Rentals - VIP Paradise

**Ultimate Comfort & Privacy** Want that true vacation vibe? Rent a bungalow! The park offers **51 bungalows** in various locations, from secluded private corners to spots near the lively activity center—there's one that perfectly fits your needs!

![涼亭租借 - VIP專屬天堂](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0d006239532e8d89eaf2a8a0f983f0b5.png)

Each bungalow comes with unique amenities and inclusions: 🛏️ Comfortable loungers and sunshade 🧊 Dedicated ice bucket service 🍽️ In-bungalow dining delivery 🏊‍♀️ Prime viewing spots 💆‍♀️ Some bungalows even offer spa services

**Why rent a bungalow?**

- It gives you a 'home base' feeling, with a place to keep your belongings
- A place to lie down when you're tired
- A private space to hang out and chat with friends
- Escape the crowds and truly relax

Remember: cabanas are in high demand—**book ahead**! Especially during peak season and weekends; if you're late, you'll just have to watch others lounging in theirs...

### **🎯 Cabana Rental Guide**

**Two ways to book:**

**1. Online Booking (Recommended) 🌐**

- Reserve through the [Waterbom official website](https://www.waterbom-bali.com/), but you'll need to purchase tickets at the same time
- Only 50% of cabanas are available for online booking; the rest must be booked on-site

**2. On-Site Purchase 🏪**

- Purchase at the park's locker counter
- On-site purchase depends on daily availability
- Risk: may sell out during peak hours

3. **Gazebo Capacity/Price**

Gazebos come in sizes for 6, 8, 12, and 16 people, with prices ranging from IDR 550,000 (about USD$18) to IDR 3,000,000 (about USD$97)

### **Is there seating if you don't rent a gazebo?**

![如果不租用涼亭有地方坐嗎？](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e7ff9441c193635e2e6702586150eb77.png)

Absolutely! The park thoughtfully provides several free seating options:

**Free Seating Areas:**
🌳 **Natural shade under big trees** - Plenty of large trees throughout the park, offering cool shade
🏖️ **Sun loungers** - Limited availability, **first come, first served**! Usually all taken by afternoon
🪑 **Poolside seating** - Chairs available around all pools
🍽️ **Restaurant dining area** - Grab a seat with an order, perfect for escaping the sun while eating

**Pro tip for grabbing a spot:**

- Rush in right when it opens at 9 AM to claim your spot
- Choose an area close to the attractions you want to play most
- Bring a towel to 'claim your spot' (but don't overdo it!)
- If you can't snag one, grab a drink at a restaurant and sit back to relax

## 🚗 Getting There: How to Reach This Water Paradise

### Prime Location

Right in the heart of Kuta, across from Discovery Mall, and just a short walk to the beach!

### Multiple Transport Options

**🏍️ Rent a Scooter** - For the brave, but Kuta traffic is wild, so be cautious
**🚗 Hire a Driver** - Most comfortable, especially for long trips from Ubud
**📱 Grab/Gojek** - Most affordable, but expect traffic jams during peak hours
**🚕 Taxi** - Always negotiate the fare, or you'll get overcharged

## ⚡ Ultimate Play Guide: How to Explore Like a Local

![終極遊玩攻略：像內行人一樣玩](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_6175291d21efae227993402759a9e029.jpg)

### 🕘 Time Management Tips

**Early Bird Gets the Worm** – Rush in right when it opens at 9 AM!

- Fewer crowds, no lines, cooler temperatures
- Snag the best sun loungers
- Take photos without strangers photobombing

**Best Season** – Dry season from April to October, but March and November have the fewest crowds

![時間管理術](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_57ccf26df215e54ea7cd2588e66e6a71.jpg)

### 🎒 Packing Checklist

**Must-Have Gear:**

✅ Swimsuit (make sure it fits snugly—slides will try to strip you)
✅ Quick-dry towel (or rent one for a fee)
✅ Non-slip sandals (the ground gets hot enough to fry an egg)
✅ Waterproof sunscreen (Bali sun is no joke)
✅ Waterproof phone pouch (essential for selfies; also sold at the park for IDR 300k)

**Absolutely Do Not Bring:**

❌ Jewelry (it will disappear on the slides)
❌ Outside food (not allowed, and the park food is actually good)
❌ Glass containers (safety reasons)

### 🧠 Insider Tips from Seasoned Travelers

- **Lockers are a lifesaver** – Secure your belongings so you can play freely
- **Pre-load your wristband** – Avoid constantly running to reload
- **Stay hydrated** – Playing in the sun can easily lead to dehydration
- **Don't eat too much before riding Climax** – Trust me, you'll regret it 🤮

## ⚠️ Safety First: Don't Let Accidents Ruin Your Memories

![安全第一：不要讓意外毀了美好回憶](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_dee9cbe4d91d15d3cf88d2e34b562c7f.jpg)

### Basic Safety Tips

- Listen to the lifeguards (they want to avoid trouble more than you do)
- Don't force it if you're not tall enough (safety matters more than pride)
- Avoid thrill rides if you're too full or too hungry
- The floor is slippery, watch your step.

### Park Amenities

- Free children's life jackets available for borrowing
- Lifeguards stationed throughout the park
- Medical station on standby
- Regular safety inspections on facilities—safer than you think

## ❓ FAQ for First-Timers

**Q: Is one day enough?** A: In theory, 4-6 hours is enough, but you won't want to leave! We recommend setting aside a full day to take it slow.

**Q: Is it too thrilling for kids?** A: There's a dedicated children's area, and the staff are great with little ones.

**Q: Is renting a cabana worth it?** A: For large groups or if you want a VIP experience, absolutely! Having a home base makes a huge difference.

**Q: Will it be crowded?** A: Go early and you'll be fine. Plus, compared to other theme parks, the wait times here are pretty short.

**Q: Is the food really good?** A: Honestly, it's way better than the food at most water parks! And the prices are reasonable too.
