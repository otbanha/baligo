## 2026 Global Top 50 Culinary Destinations Revealed! Indonesia Takes 7th Place Worldwide, Surpassing Singapore and Malaysia, with Taiwan Also Making the List

When planning a trip, do you first look for attractions or food?

In recent years, 'culinary tourism' has become one of the fastest-growing trends in the global travel market. Many people are willing to fly to another country just for a bowl of noodles, a plate of grilled meat, or even a traditional dish only found there.

Travel And Tour World recently released the [**2026 Global Top 50 Culinary Destinations**](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/ay8cbrn0dk9j/), based primarily on ratings from the renowned global food platform TasteAtlas. The results show that Asia remains a core hub of the global food landscape, with **Indonesia ranking 7th worldwide**, not only surpassing Singapore and Malaysia but also becoming one of the standout countries in Southeast Asia.

For travelers who love exploring local culture, this list also offers a 'global food travel checklist' worth saving.

***

## Indonesia Takes 7th Place Worldwide, Becoming One of the Most Notable Food Countries in Southeast Asia

https://youtube.com/shorts/tviqX2pab_I

Indonesia ranks 7th globally this time, making it the highest-ranked Southeast Asian country on the list.

The greatest charm of Indonesian cuisine lies in its more than 17,000 islands, over 300 ethnic groups, and hundreds of local food cultures—each region boasts distinct cooking methods and signature dishes.

From the spice-rich **Rendang**, the national favorite **Nasi Goreng**, and **Sate**, to coconut milk curries, an array of seafood, and street snacks, Indonesia has become a food paradise that wins over travelers on their very first visit.

Focusing on the most popular tourist destination, Bali, you can experience traditional Balinese cuisine, Indonesian national dishes, and international fare all in one place—making it the perfect first stop for many travelers to fall in love with Indonesian food.

***

## Asia Still Dominates the Global Food Scene

A closer look at the rankings reveals that Asia remains the most iconic food region in the world.

Besides Indonesia, countries like Japan, China, India, Vietnam, Thailand, and South Korea also rank high, showing that Asian food culture has become a major draw for international travelers.

Many of these dishes have already taken the world by storm, such as:

- Japanese sushi and ramen
- Chinese hotpot, xiaolongbao, and Peking duck
- Korean BBQ and fried chicken
- Vietnamese pho
- Thai tom yum kung
- Indonesian rendang and nasi goreng

***

## China Ranks 5th Globally, with an Impressive Variety of Cuisines

China secured the **5th** spot in the global rankings this time.

Due to its vast territory, Chinese cuisine is one of the world's richest food cultures.

From Sichuan's fiery mala, Cantonese dim sum, Jiangzhe cuisine, and Peking duck to Xinjiang lamb skewers, each region boasts completely distinct culinary characteristics.

In recent years, food tourism in China has continued to grow, with Chengdu, Chongqing, Guangzhou, Xi'an, Beijing, and Shanghai emerging as top foodie cities.

***

## Malaysia Ranks 9th Globally, a Melting Pot of Cultures Yields Exquisite Cuisine

Malaysia ranks **9th** in the world.

Blending Malay, Chinese, and Indian cultures, Malaysia offers an incredibly diverse culinary landscape.

Including:

- Nasi Lemak
- Bak Kut Teh
- Hainanese Chicken Rice
- Penang Laksa
- Char Kway Teow
- Satay

These are all popular dishes that many travelers make a special trip to savor.

***

## Singapore Ranks 12th Globally, a Small Nation Yet an Asian Food Capital

Singapore ranks **12th** globally.

Despite its small size, it brings together Chinese, Malay, Indian, and Peranakan cuisines.

Many travelers even consider Hawker Centres to be Singapore's biggest attraction.

For example:

- Hainanese Chicken Rice
- Chili Crab
- Bak Kut Teh
- Hokkien Prawn Mee
- Laksa

These are world-renowned dishes.

***

## Taiwan ranks 24th globally, yet its cuisine remains beloved by international travelers

Taiwan ranks **24th** globally this time.

Night market culture remains its biggest highlight.

Including:

- Beef noodle soup
- Soup dumplings
- Braised pork rice
- Bubble milk tea
- Taiwanese popcorn chicken

For years, these have been the must-try iconic dishes for foreign visitors to Taiwan.

Although it didn't crack the top 10, Taiwan remains one of Asia's most iconic food destinations.

# Indonesia's Top 10 Food Travel Destinations at a Glance! From Aceh to Papua, every stop has must-try classic dishes

Beyond being named the 7th best food travel destination globally, Travel And Tour World has also curated a list of **Indonesia's top 10 food cities/regions worth visiting**. These places offer more than just food—they provide a deep dive into local history, ethnicity, and culture through cuisine.

***

## 1. West Sumatra

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784948982406-_2026-07-25_10.09.11.png)

West Sumatra is hailed as Indonesia's most iconic food hub and the birthplace of **Padang cuisine (Masakan Padang)**.

Local dishes are renowned for their generous use of spices, coconut milk, chili, and slow-cooking techniques, resulting in rich, complex flavors.

### Must-Try Dishes

- **Rendang**: Repeatedly voted one of the world's best dishes.
- **Nasi Padang**: White rice served with a choice of dozens of side dishes.
- **Sate Padang**: Skewers with a rich, spiced sauce, distinctly different from Javanese satay.
- **Dendeng Balado**: Spicy beef jerky.

## 2. Yogyakarta

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784949089128-_2026-07-25_10.11.10.png)

Yogyakarta is not only Indonesia's cultural capital but also a key representative of Javanese cuisine.

Compared to other regions, Yogyakarta's dishes tend to be sweeter, offering a distinct local character.

### Must-Try Foods

- Gudeg (Jackfruit Stewed with Chicken)
- Bakpia Pathok (Mung Bean Pastry)
- Sate Klathak (Iron Skewer Lamb Satay)
- Oseng Mercon (Super Spicy Beef)

## 3. Bali

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784949179713-_2026-07-25_10.12.40.png)

For international travelers, Bali is undoubtedly the easiest place to experience Indonesian cuisine.

Beyond upscale restaurants, roadside Warungs serve plenty of authentic Balinese dishes.

### Must-Try Foods

- **Babi Guling (Roast Suckling Pig)**
- **Ayam Betutu (Spiced Braised Chicken)**
- **Sate Lilit (Balinese Fish Satay)**
- **Lawar (Spiced Coconut and Vegetable Mix)**

## 4. Jakarta

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784949254556-_2026-07-25_10.14.03.png)

As the capital, Jakarta brings together dishes from all over Indonesia.

If you're short on time, Jakarta lets you taste all of Indonesia in one go.

### Must-Try Foods

- Soto Betawi
- Kerak Telor
- Nasi Uduk
- Gado-Gado

## 5. Bandung

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784949360121-_2026-07-25_10.15.48.png)

Bandung is famous for its creative street food and is a favorite food city among young people.

### Must-Try Foods

- Batagor
- Siomay
- Seblak
- Mie Kocok

## 6. Surabaya

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784949449271-_2026-07-25_10.17.16.png)

East Javanese cuisine is bold, hearty, and full of flavor.

### Must-Try Dishes

- Rawon (Black Beef Soup)
- Rujak Cingur
- Lontong Balap
- Sate Klopo

## 7. Makassar

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784949543909-_2026-07-25_10.18.17.png)

South Sulawesi has a rich beef-centric culinary culture.

### Must-Try Dishes

- Coto Makassar
- Konro (Beef Rib Soup)
- Pallubasa
- Pisang Epe (Grilled Banana)

## 8. Medan

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784949632335-_2026-07-25_10.20.17.png)

Medan blends Chinese, Malay, and Batak cultural influences.

### Must-Try Dishes

- Soto Medan
- Bika Ambon
- Durian
- Mie Aceh

## 9. Aceh

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784949729079-_2026-07-25_10.21.42.png)

Acehnese cuisine is heavily influenced by Arab and Indian flavors.

### Must-Try Foods

- Mie Aceh
- Gayo Coffee
- Ayam Tangkap
- Kuah Beulangong

## 10. Papua

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784949844039-_2026-07-25_10.23.50.png)

Papua's food culture is completely different from the rest of Indonesia.

The staple food is not rice, but sago.

### Must-Try Foods

- Papeda (sago porridge)
- Yellow Fish Soup (Ikan Kuah Kuning)
- Grilled Fish
- Various sago dishes

# Why Indonesia Ranks Among the World's Top 10?

Indonesia's ranking at 7th globally isn't just because a few famous dishes are popular—it's the country's entire culinary culture that showcases remarkable diversity.

Key reasons include:

- **Over 17,000 islands**, each nurturing unique local cuisines.
- **More than 300 ethnic groups**, creating a rich and varied food culture.
- A long history of spice use, blending influences from Indian, Middle Eastern, Chinese, and European cuisines.
- High-quality local food can be enjoyed everywhere, from street stalls and traditional markets to upscale restaurants.
- Popular travel destinations like Bali, Jakarta, and Yogyakarta make it easy for visitors to sample regional specialties during their trips.

Whether it's your first visit to Indonesia or you've been to Bali many times, you're sure to discover a more diverse and captivating side of the country through its food!
