**Bottom Line**: Bali's sea turtle nesting season runs from **April to October** each year. **BSTS (Bali Sea Turtle Society)** in Kuta is **open daily from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM**, making it the easiest conservation spot to visit. In Sanur, you can also choose Sea Turtle Village or Sindu Dwarawati for a family-friendly nature education outing. Actual release events depend on the hatching schedule.

If you think Bali is only about romantic beaches, sunsets, and resorts, you're missing the island's most touching side—**the story of sea turtle conservation**. From April to October each year, mother turtles return to Bali's shores to lay eggs, while local communities, volunteers, and foundations work on rehabilitation, giving travelers a chance to witness the moment tiny hatchlings race to the sea.

This article compiles the **latest spots for viewing and participating in sea turtle conservation in 2025**, covering everything from Kuta to Sanur!

## 🐢 1️⃣ BSTS — Bali Sea Turtle Society (Kuta)

![1⃣ BSTS — Bali Sea Turtle Society（庫塔）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_10d8cb87fd3795c13748ec3da6d89767.jpg)

📍 **Location**: Kuta Beach (next to Kuta Beach; see the [Kuta Guide Map](/en/blog/kuta-guide/) for directions)

🌐 **Official Website**: <https://baliseaturtle.org/>

📅 **Opening Hours**: Daily 9:00 AM–5:00 PM (activities depend on the hatching schedule)

BSTS is one of the most trusted sea turtle conservation organizations in Bali, primarily patrolling and incubating eggs on Kuta Beach. Whenever baby turtles hatch, they host a free **Baby Turtle Release** event, allowing visitors to help the hatchlings return to the ocean at sunset.

🚗 **Transportation Tips**:

- About a 15-minute drive from the airport
- Take Gojek/Grab or a Blue Bird taxi directly

💡 **Helpful Advice**:

Event times are not fixed; **follow BSTS's official IG or Facebook for announced schedules** to avoid missing out.

## 🐢 2️⃣ Sea Turtle Village (Sanur)

![2⃣ Sea Turtle Village（沙努爾）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_653a7621066e1f56aaf7e49601283f11.jpg)

📍 **Location**: Sindhu Beach, Sanur. See the [Sanur Guide Map](/en/blog/sanur-guide/) for details.

🌐 **Official Info**: [Sea Turtle Village Sanur](https://www.hyatt.com/content/dam/hotel/propertysites/assets/regency/dpsbl/documents/en_us/home/Sea-Turtle-Village-Activities.pdf)

📅 **Opening Hours**: Approximately 8:00 AM–5:00 PM

This is a small turtle conservation site voluntarily established by two local five-star hotels, Hyatt and Andaz. Though modest in scale, it’s perfect for a family outing. You can see small hatchery pools and display walls, learning about the journey of sea turtles from egg to hatchling.

🚗 **Transport Tips**:

- About a 35-minute drive from the airport
- Taxi or private car recommended

💡 **Best Time to Visit**:

Early morning or late afternoon is most pleasant, with soft sunlight ideal for photos 📸

![4⃣ Serangan Island Turtle Conservation & Education Center（海龜](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_210d4b94f50f241c3bd9234150ff0593.jpg)

### Other Experiences

Some beachfront five-star hotels organize baby sea turtle release events. For instance, **The Oberoi Bali** on Seminyak Beach and **Westing Resort** in Nusa Dua have their own turtle conservation programs. After a brief period of care, visitors can participate in releasing the hatchlings into the ocean.


#### 🌊 Sea Turtle Season & Etiquette

- 🐣 **Best Season**: Bali's sea turtle nesting season runs mainly from March to September, with peak activity from May to August
- ☀️ **Best Time**: Early morning or late afternoon (more activities, softer light)
- 🚫 **Important Notes**:
- - Do not touch or pick up the turtles
    - Turn off flash when taking photos
    - Wear light-colored clothing and keep your voice down
    - Consider supporting conservation centers with a donation of any amount
    - If you encounter a turtle laying eggs on the beach, keep a safe distance, avoid loud noises, and refrain from using flash photography to avoid startling them.

## 💬 In Summary: An Unforgettable Life Lesson

Watching tiny sea turtles, just a few centimeters long, crawl step by step toward the deep blue Indian Ocean on a Bali beach—that moment of awe is beyond words.

This is more than just an experience during a journey—it's a connection with nature and a lesson in respecting life.

However, in recent years, controversies surrounding 'sea turtle conservation centers' have surfaced—some questioning whether they've become overly commercialized or truly adhere to conservation principles. From my personal visits, **most sea turtle conservation centers in Bali do not charge an entrance fee**, relying instead on donations from visitors and modest income from small activities. These centers are often run by local communities or volunteers, with their core mission rooted in conservation and education.

Don't forget, the process of sea turtles laying eggs on the beach is incredibly fragile—**a single party or an accidental step can destroy an entire nest of eggs**. That's precisely why these conservation centers remain essential for protecting sea turtles and their habitats.

It may not be perfect, but if we visit with respect and support—**offering less criticism and more understanding and donations**—we can help sustain these efforts to protect these tiny lives.

🌺 Whether you're in Kuta, Sanur, or Turtle Island, don't miss out on this gentle hope that belongs to Bali.
