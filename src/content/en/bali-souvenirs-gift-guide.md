## Balinese Cultural Handicrafts

Bali's handicrafts are world-renowned for their exquisite craftsmanship and unique designs. These include:

- **Wood Carvings**: Wood carving is one of Bali's signature crafts. From deity statues and animals to everyday items, these pieces are full of detail and vitality, making them excellent home decor choices.
- **Stone Carvings**: Similar to wood carvings, Bali's stone carvings showcase the artisans' exceptional skills. You can find these exquisite pieces in temples, gardens, or as indoor decorations.

![砂岩雕刻的迷你石雕](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_edcff6e24f592177be3b581f0d4042eb.png)

Miniature sandstone carvings

- **Textiles**: Balinese textiles are known for their vibrant colors and traditional patterns. Whether it's sarongs, scarves, or tablecloths, they let you feel the local charm.
- **Handicrafts**: Small handwoven items like bracelets and necklaces.
- **Incense**: Bring the scent of Bali home. For a detailed guide, check [here](/en/blog/bali-art-souvenirs-guide/).
- 👜 **Balinese Woven Bags**

![峇里島文化手工藝品](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0da8524d97ec3c0a0ab9714a64bb7364.png)

These handmade woven bags are known for their minimalist, natural, and stylish designs. Whether a small clutch or a large tote, they are highly practical. Prices start from **Rp 50,000, with basic styles around Rp 150,000, and larger or more intricate designs reaching up to Rp 500,000**—a popular choice for gifts or personal use.

## Local Food Specialties

![美食特產](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_08d17f1959db86dd24612c06e5c7ebca.png)

If you're a food lover, Bali's local specialties are not to be missed. Bringing some home not only extends the flavors of your journey but also lets you share the joy with friends and family.

- **Coffee**: Balinese coffee is renowned for its unique flavor and high quality. Coffee from Kintamani, in particular, has a subtle fruity aroma that travelers love. Civet coffee (Kopi Luwak) is also a famous Balinese product, widely available in supermarkets with many different brands. When buying, be cautious—given the scale of production and rarity, there's a high chance of encountering fakes.
- **Spices**: Bali offers a wide variety of spices, from chili, white pepper, bay leaves, turmeric, to cloves. These spices not only enhance cooking but also make practical souvenirs.

## Perfume / Essential Oils / Skincare

### Secret Garden Perfume

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1788835682425-images_5_.jfif)

If you're looking for a souvenir that's both classy and captures the essence of Bali, **Secret Garden perfume** is well worth adding to your shopping list. Secret Garden is built on the philosophy "Inspired by Earth, Made for You," and at Secret Garden Village in Bali, it combines beauty, fragrance, and cultural experiences. The complex even features a dedicated Fragrance Bar where you can sample a wide range of scents.

What's interesting about Secret Garden perfume is that **many people only start regretting 'why did I only buy one bottle?' after returning home** 😂 At the time in Bali, you might think, 'Let's just try one bottle,' but once you're back, you find yourself loving the scent more and more, realizing it's perfect for personal use and as a gift. Secret Garden offers not just perfumes but also diffusers, bath products, and more—plenty of variety. **So if you find a scent you truly love, I'd recommend buying several bottles; otherwise, you'll wish you'd bought more once you're home.**

If you want to purchase these diffusers or related fragrance products, besides visiting the Secret Garden Village headquarters in Tabanan to tour the lab, you can easily find dedicated counters at Beachwalk Shopping Center, Discovery Mall, and some Watsons stores in the city. For travelers on a tight schedule, **the duty-free shops at Ngurah Rai International Airport are an excellent spot to stock up, often featuring 'buy two get one free' deals or beautifully packaged diffuser gift sets. As of 2026, a local green shopping initiative is underway, and bringing your own shopping bag may earn you extra sample gifts. I recommend leaving a bit of time before your flight to grab this signature Balinese scent at the airport counter, letting the joy of your vacation linger back home.**

If you're buying for several friends and family, I highly recommend these small packages—they're inexpensive and easy to share.

ELLIPS hair oils and masks are available at most supermarkets.

### UTAMA SPICE Incense Sticks, Lip Balm, and Facial Oil

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1788832599418-utama-spice-products-.jpeg)

**UTAMA SPICE** is a natural skincare brand from Ubud, Bali, founded in 1989. Its philosophy is rooted in Bali's traditional herbal remedies and the use of plants. The brand offers a wide range of products, from incense sticks, essential oils, and lip balms to facial care and body oils. On the official website, you can find lip balms in flavors like Cocoa, Peppermint, and Tangerine, as well as products such as Argan Oil and Oily Face Serum. If you're looking for souvenirs with a "Bali vibe," I'd recommend the incense sticks or lip balms—they're small and easy to carry. For someone who loves natural skincare, facial oils are also a great option. However, UTAMA SPICE has a huge variety of items, so be sure to check which one you're buying, as the uses and ingredients differ across products.
