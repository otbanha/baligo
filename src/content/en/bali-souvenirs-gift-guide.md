# Balinese Cultural Handicrafts

Balinese handicrafts are world-renowned for their exquisite craftsmanship and unique designs. These include:

- **Wood Carvings**: Wood carving is one of Bali's signature crafts. From deities and animals to everyday items, these carvings are full of detail and vitality, making them excellent choices for home decor.
- **Stone Carvings**: Similar to wood carvings, Balinese stone carvings showcase the artisans' exceptional skills. You can find these exquisite pieces in temples, gardens, or as indoor decorations.

![砂岩雕刻的迷你石雕](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_edcff6e24f592177be3b581f0d4042eb.png)

Miniature sandstone carvings

- **Textiles**: Balinese textiles are known for their vibrant colors and traditional patterns. Whether it's a sarong, scarf, or tablecloth, they all let you feel the local charm.
- **Handicrafts**: Handwoven items like bracelets and necklaces.
- **Incense**: Bring the scent of Bali home. For a detailed guide, check [here](/en/blog/bali-art-souvenirs-guide/).
- 👜 **Balinese Woven Bags**

![峇里島文化手工藝品](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0da8524d97ec3c0a0ab9714a64bb7364.png)

These handmade woven bags are known for their minimalist, natural, and stylish designs. Whether it's a small clutch or a large tote, they are highly practical. Prices start from **Rp 50,000, with basic styles around Rp 150,000, and larger or more intricate designs reaching up to Rp 500,000**—a popular choice for gifts or personal use.

# Local Food Specialties

![美食特產](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_08d17f1959db86dd24612c06e5c7ebca.png)

If you're a food lover, Bali's specialty foods are a must-try. Bringing some local delicacies home not only extends the joy of your trip but also lets you share this delight with friends and family.

- **Coffee**: Balinese coffee is renowned for its unique flavor and high quality. Coffee from Kintamani, in particular, has a subtle fruity aroma that visitors love. Civet coffee (Kopi Luwak) is also a famous Balinese specialty, available in many supermarkets under various brands. However, be cautious when purchasing—due to production scale and rarity, there's a high chance of buying counterfeit products.
- **Spices**: Bali offers a wide variety of spices, including chili, white pepper, bay leaves, turmeric, and cloves. These spices not only enhance cooking flavors but also make practical souvenirs.

## Secret Garden Perfume

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1788835682425-images_5_.jfif)

If you're looking for a more refined souvenir that captures the essence of Bali, **Secret Garden Perfume** is well worth adding to your shopping list. Secret Garden operates under the philosophy "Inspired by Earth, Made for You," and at Secret Garden Village in Bali, it combines beauty, fragrance, and cultural experiences. The complex even features a dedicated Fragrance Bar where you can sample a wide range of scents.

One interesting thing about Secret Garden Perfume: **many people only start regretting "why did I only buy one bottle?" after returning home.** 😂 While in Bali, you might think you'll just try one bottle, but once you're back and fall more in love with the scent, you realize it's perfect for personal use and as a gift. Secret Garden offers not just perfumes but also diffusers, bath products, and more—plenty of choices. **So if you find a scent you truly love, I'd recommend buying several bottles. Otherwise, you'll wish you'd bought more once you're home.**

If you want to purchase these diffusers or related fragrance products, you can visit the Secret Garden Village headquarters in Tabanan to tour the lab, or easily find counters at Beachwalk Shopping Center, Discovery Mall, and some Watsons stores in the city. For travelers on a tight schedule, **the duty-free shops at Ngurah Rai International Airport are an excellent spot to stock up, often offering "buy two get one free" deals or beautifully packaged diffuser gift sets. As of 2026, a local green shopping initiative is underway, and bringing your own shopping bag may earn you extra sample gifts. I recommend leaving some time before your flight to grab this signature Bali scent at the airport counter, extending the joy of your vacation back home.**

## Frangipani Soap

- Bean-to-bar premium chocolate

## TICKET TO THE MOON Backpack

## Silver Queen Chocolate

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1795409443290-15834.jpg)

Despite its imported-sounding name, Silver Queen is actually Indonesia's own national chocolate, exported to Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, and the Philippines. Indonesia is a top cocoa-producing country, and Silver Queen rivals imported brands in taste. Their signature cashew variant lists cashews before cocoa mass on the ingredient label, offering crunchy nuts paired with smooth, rich milk chocolate. For a change, try the almond version for an even crispier texture. The sleek silver packaging looks premium without breaking the bank, and individually wrapped bars make for easy sharing—available at Bali supermarkets. Just check for nut allergies before gifting.
