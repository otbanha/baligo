# Balinese Cultural Handicrafts

Balinese handicrafts are world-renowned for their exquisite craftsmanship and unique designs. These include:

- **Wood Carvings**: Wood carving is one of Bali's signature crafts. From deities and animals to everyday items, these carvings are full of detail and life, making them excellent home decor choices.
- **Stone Carvings**: Similar to wood carvings, Balinese stone carvings showcase the artisans' exceptional skills. You can find these exquisite pieces in temples, gardens, or as indoor decorations.

![砂岩雕刻的迷你石雕](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_edcff6e24f592177be3b581f0d4042eb.png)

Miniature sandstone carvings

- **Textiles**: Balinese textiles are known for their vibrant colors and traditional patterns. Whether it's a sarong, scarf, or tablecloth, they all let you feel the local charm.
- **Handicrafts**: Small handwoven items like bracelets and necklaces.
- **Incense**: Bring the scent of Bali home. For a detailed guide, check [here](/en/blog/bali-art-souvenirs-guide/).
- 👜 **Balinese Woven Bags**

![峇里島文化手工藝品](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0da8524d97ec3c0a0ab9714a64bb7364.png)

These handmade woven bags are known for their minimalist, natural, and stylish designs. Whether you prefer a small clutch or a spacious tote, they are highly practical. Prices start from **Rp 50,000, with basic styles around Rp 150,000, and larger or more intricate designs reaching up to Rp 500,000**—making them a popular choice for gifts or personal use.

# Local Food Specialties

![美食特產](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_08d17f1959db86dd24612c06e5c7ebca.png)

If you're a food lover, Bali's local specialties are a must-try. Bringing home some of these treats not only extends the joy of your trip but also lets you share the experience with friends and family.

- **Coffee**: Balinese coffee is renowned for its unique flavor and high quality. Coffee from Kintamani, in particular, has a subtle fruity aroma that visitors love. Luwak coffee is another famous product, widely available in supermarkets under various brands. However, be cautious—given the scale of production and rarity, there's a high chance of buying counterfeit products.
- **Spices**: Bali offers a wide variety of spices, including chili, white pepper, bay leaves, turmeric, and cloves. These spices not only enhance your cooking but also make practical souvenirs.

## Secret Garden Perfume

The complex even features a dedicated Fragrance Bar where you can sample a variety of scents.

Otherwise, you'll wish you'd bought more once you're home.**

If you want to purchase this diffuser or related fragrance products, you can visit the Secret Garden Village headquarters in Tabanan to tour the lab, or easily find counters at Beachwalk Shopping Center, Discovery Mall, and select Watsons stores in the city. For travelers on a tight schedule, **the duty-free shops at Ngurah Rai International Airport are an excellent spot to stock up, often offering deals like "buy two get one free" or beautifully packaged diffuser gift sets. As of 2026, a local green shopping initiative is underway, and bringing your own shopping bag may earn you extra sample gifts. I recommend leaving a bit of time before boarding to grab this signature Bali scent at the airport counter, extending the joy of your vacation back home.**

## Frangipani Soap

## TICKET TO THE MOON Backpack

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1788834353680-o1cn01c29jxw1xyru46euzr_2215653026455.webp)

**Ticket to the Moon (TTTM)** is a souvenir brand with strong "Bali roots." Founded in Bali in 1996, the brand first gained fame for its hammocks, later expanding into backpacks, sling bags, travel accessories, and upcycled products made from leftover materials.
