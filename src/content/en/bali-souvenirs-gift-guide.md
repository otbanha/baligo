## Balinese Cultural Handicrafts

Bali's handicrafts are world-renowned for their exquisite craftsmanship and unique designs. These include:

- **Wood Carvings**: Wood carving is one of Bali's signature crafts. From deities and animals to everyday items, these carvings are full of detail and vitality, making them excellent home decor choices.
- **Stone Carvings**: Similar to wood carvings, Bali's stone carvings showcase the artisans' exceptional skills. You can find these exquisite pieces in temples, gardens, or as indoor decorations.

![砂岩雕刻的迷你石雕](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_edcff6e24f592177be3b581f0d4042eb.png)

Miniature sandstone carvings

- **Textiles**: Balinese textiles are known for their vibrant colors and traditional patterns. Whether it's sarongs, scarves, or tablecloths, they all let you feel the local charm.
- **Handicrafts**: Small handwoven items like bracelets and necklaces.
- **Incense**: Bring the scent of Bali home. For a detailed guide, check [here](/en/blog/bali-art-souvenirs-guide/).
- 👜 **Bali Woven Bags**

![峇里島文化手工藝品](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0da8524d97ec3c0a0ab9714a64bb7364.png)

These handmade woven bags are known for their minimalist, natural, and stylish designs, whether small clutch bags or large totes—they're all practical. Prices start from **Rp 50,000, with basic styles around Rp 150,000, and larger or more intricate designs can go up to Rp 500,000**, making them a popular choice for gifts or personal use.

## Local Food Specialties

![美食特產](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_08d17f1959db86dd24612c06e5c7ebca.png)

If you're a food lover, Bali's local specialties are a must-try. Bringing some home not only extends the joy of your trip but also lets you share the experience with friends and family.

- **Coffee**: Balinese coffee is renowned for its unique flavor and high quality. Coffee from Kintamani, in particular, has a subtle fruity aroma that visitors love. Luwak coffee is also a famous Balinese product, available in many brands at any supermarket. When buying, be cautious—given the scale of production and rarity, there's a high chance of getting a fake.
- **Spices**: Bali offers a wide variety of spices, from chili, white pepper, bay leaves, turmeric, to cloves. These spices not only enhance your cooking but also make practical souvenirs.

## Perfume / Essential Oils / Skincare

### Secret Garden Perfume

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1788835682425-images_5_.jfif)

If you're looking for a more refined souvenir that captures the essence of Bali, **Secret Garden Perfume** is well worth adding to your shopping list. Secret Garden is built on the philosophy "Inspired by Earth, Made for You," and at Secret Garden Village in Bali, it combines beauty, fragrance, and cultural experiences. The complex even has a dedicated Fragrance Bar where you can sample multiple scents.

What's interesting about Secret Garden perfume is that **many people only regret not buying more after they return home.** 😂 At the time in Bali, you might think, "Let me just try one bottle," but once you're back, you'll find yourself loving the scent more and more, realizing it's perfect for personal use and as a gift. Secret Garden offers not just perfume but also diffusers, bath products, and more—plenty of options. **So if you find a scent you truly love, I'd recommend buying several bottles; otherwise, you'll wish you'd bought more once you're home.**

If you want to purchase these diffusers or related fragrance products, you can visit the Secret Garden Village headquarters in Tabanan to tour the lab, or easily find counters at Beachwalk Shopping Center, Discovery Mall, and some Watsons stores in the city. For travelers on a tight schedule, **the duty-free shops at Ngurah Rai International Airport are an excellent spot to shop, often offering deals like 'buy two get one free' or beautifully packaged diffuser gift sets. As of 2026, a local green shopping initiative is underway, and bringing your own shopping bag may earn you extra sample gifts. I recommend leaving some time before your flight to stock up at the airport counter on this signature Bali scent, extending the joy of your vacation back home.**

### Frangipani Soap

**Frangipani (Plumeria) Soap** is a wonderfully Balinese souvenir that instantly evokes a vacation vibe.
