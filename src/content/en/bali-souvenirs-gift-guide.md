# Balinese Cultural Handicrafts

Balinese handicrafts are world-renowned for their exquisite craftsmanship and unique designs. These handicrafts include:

- **Wood Carvings**: Wood carving is one of Bali's signature crafts. These carvings, ranging from deities and animals to everyday items, are full of detail and vitality, making them excellent choices for home decor.
- **Stone Carvings**: Similar to wood carvings, Balinese stone carvings showcase the artisans' exceptional skills. You can find these exquisite pieces in temples, gardens, or as indoor decorations.

![砂岩雕刻的迷你石雕](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_edcff6e24f592177be3b581f0d4042eb.png)

Miniature sandstone carvings

- **Textiles**: Balinese textiles are known for their vibrant colors and traditional patterns. Whether it's a sarong, scarf, or tablecloth, they all let you feel the local charm.
- **Handicrafts**: Small handwoven items like bracelets and necklaces.
- **Incense**: Bring the scent of Bali home. For a detailed guide, check [here](/en/blog/bali-art-souvenirs-guide/).
- 👜 **Balinese Woven Bags**

![峇里島文化手工藝品](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0da8524d97ec3c0a0ab9714a64bb7364.png)

These handmade woven bags are known for their simple, natural, and stylish designs. Whether it's a small clutch or a large tote, they're incredibly practical. Prices start from **Rp 50,000, with basic styles around Rp 150,000, and larger or more intricate designs can go up to Rp 500,000**, making them a popular choice for gifts or personal use.

# Local Food Specialties

![美食特產](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_08d17f1959db86dd24612c06e5c7ebca.png)

If you're a food lover, Bali's local specialties are not to be missed. Bringing some home not only extends the joy of your trip but also lets you share these delightful flavors with friends and family.

- **Coffee**: Balinese coffee is renowned for its unique flavor and high quality. Coffee from Kintamani, in particular, has a subtle fruity aroma that's beloved by visitors. Civet coffee (Kopi Luwak) is also a famous Balinese product, widely available in supermarkets with many different brands. However, be cautious when buying—given the scale of production and rarity, there's a high chance of encountering counterfeit products.
- **Spices**: Bali offers a wide variety of spices, from chili peppers, white pepper, bay leaves, turmeric, to cloves. These spices not only enhance your cooking but also make practical souvenirs.

## Secret Garden Perfume

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1788835682425-images_5_.jfif)

If you're looking for a more refined souvenir that captures the essence of Bali, **Secret Garden perfume** is well worth adding to your shopping list. Secret Garden operates under the philosophy 'Inspired by Earth, Made for You,' and at Secret Garden Village in Bali, it combines beauty, fragrance, and cultural experiences. The complex even features a dedicated Fragrance Bar where you can sample a variety of scents.

What's interesting about Secret Garden perfume is that **many people only regret not buying more once they're back home** 😂 At the time in Bali, you might think to just try one bottle, but after returning, you find yourself loving the scent more and more, realizing it's perfect for personal use and as a gift. Secret Garden offers not just perfumes but also diffusers, bath products, and more—plenty of options. **So if you find a scent you truly love, I'd recommend buying several bottles; otherwise, you'll wish you had bought more once you're home.**

If you want to purchase these diffusers or related fragrance products, besides visiting the Secret Garden Village headquarters in Tabanan to tour the lab, you can easily find counters at Beachwalk Shopping Center, Discovery Mall, and some Watsons stores in the city. For travelers on a tight schedule, **the duty-free shops at Ngurah Rai International Airport are an excellent spot to shop, often offering deals like 'buy two get one free' or beautifully packaged diffuser gift sets. As of 2026, a local green shopping initiative is underway, and bringing your own shopping bag may earn you extra sample gifts. I recommend setting aside some time before boarding to stock up on this signature Balinese scent at the airport counter, extending the joy of your vacation back home.**

## Frangipani Soap

**Frangipani (Plumeria) soap** is a classic Bali-inspired souvenir. ## **Chocolate Monggo**

**Chocolate Monggo** is a **Belgian Artisan Chocolatier** based in Bali. Founded by a Belgian engineer who moved to Bali, the brand uses local spices and organic cacao to create island-inspired chocolates with a distinct sense of place.

**Highlights:**

- Uses high-quality Indonesian cacao beans
- Bean-to-bar premium chocolate
- Variety of flavors: pure cacao, mango dark chocolate, and more
- Beautifully packaged, perfect for gifting

**Where to buy:** The flagship store in Ubud has the most extensive selection, with a glass workshop where you can watch the production process; there are also branches in Seminyak and Kuta.

## TICKET TO THE MOON Backpack
