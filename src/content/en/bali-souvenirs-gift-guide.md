# Balinese Cultural Handicrafts

Bali's handicrafts are world-renowned for their exquisite craftsmanship and unique designs. These include:

- **Wood Carvings**: Wood carving is one of Bali's signature crafts. From deities and animals to everyday items, these carvings are full of detail and vitality, making them excellent home decor choices.
- **Stone Carvings**: Similar to wood carvings, Bali's stone carvings showcase the artisans' exceptional skills. You can find these exquisite pieces in temples, gardens, or as indoor decorations.

![砂岩雕刻的迷你石雕](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_edcff6e24f592177be3b581f0d4042eb.png)

Miniature sandstone carvings

- **Textiles**: Balinese textiles are famous for their vibrant colors and traditional patterns. Whether it's sarongs, scarves, or tablecloths, they all let you feel the local charm.
- **Handicrafts**: Small handwoven items like bracelets and necklaces.
- **Incense**: Bring the scent of Bali home. For a detailed guide, check [here](/en/blog/bali-art-souvenirs-guide/).
- 👜 **Bali Woven Bags**

![峇里島文化手工藝品](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0da8524d97ec3c0a0ab9714a64bb7364.png)

These handmade woven bags are known for their simple, natural, and stylish designs. Whether it's a small clutch or a large tote, they're incredibly practical. Prices start at **Rp 50,000, with basic styles around Rp 150,000, and larger or more intricate designs can go up to Rp 500,000**—a popular choice for gifts or personal use.

# Local Food Specialties

![美食特產](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_08d17f1959db86dd24612c06e5c7ebca.png)

If you're a food lover, Bali's local specialties are a must. Bringing home some of these treats not only extends the joy of your trip but also lets you share the experience with friends and family.

- **Coffee**: Balinese coffee is renowned for its unique flavor and high quality. Coffee from Kintamani, in particular, has a subtle fruity aroma that visitors love. Civet coffee (kopi luwak) is also a famous Balinese product, available in many brands at almost any supermarket. When buying, be cautious—given the production scale and rarity, there's a high chance of encountering fakes.
- **Spices**: Bali offers a wide variety of spices, from chili peppers, white pepper, bay leaves, and turmeric to cloves. These not only enhance your cooking but also make practical souvenirs.

## Secret Garden Perfume

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1788835682425-images_5_.jfif)

If you're looking for a more refined souvenir that captures the essence of Bali, **Secret Garden perfume** is well worth adding to your shopping list. Secret Garden operates under the philosophy 'Inspired by Earth, Made for You,' and at Secret Garden Village in Bali, it combines beauty, fragrance, and cultural experiences. The complex even features a dedicated Fragrance Bar where you can sample a variety of scents.

What's interesting about Secret Garden perfume is that **many people only regret not buying more once they're back home** 😂. At the time in Bali, you might think to grab just one bottle to try, but after returning, you'll find yourself loving the scent more and more, realizing it's perfect for personal use and as a gift. Secret Garden offers not only perfumes but also diffusers, bath products, and more—plenty of options. **So if you find a scent you truly love, I'd recommend buying several bottles; otherwise, you'll wish you'd bought more once you're home.**

If you want to purchase these diffusers or related fragrance products, aside from visiting the Secret Garden Village headquarters in Tabanan to tour the lab, you can easily find dedicated counters at Beachwalk Shopping Center, Discovery Mall, and some Watsons stores in the city. For travelers on a tight schedule, **the duty-free shops at Ngurah Rai International Airport are an excellent spot to stock up, often featuring 'buy two get one free' deals or beautifully packaged diffuser gift sets. As of 2026, a local green shopping initiative is underway, and bringing your own shopping bag may earn you extra sample gifts. I suggest leaving some time before boarding to grab this signature Balinese scent at the airport counter, extending the joy of your vacation back home.**

Whether at the boutique near Ubud Market or at Bali's duty-free shops, this brand is easy to find! 💖 These fragrant, locally-inspired products are both affordable and practical, making them perfect gifts! 🎁

## **Chocolate Monggo**

![Chocolate Monggo](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a91fa60eb71f580eac358c12a830f694.jpg)

**Chocolate Monggo** is a **Belgian Artisan Chocolatier** based in Bali. Founded by a Belgian engineer who moved to Bali, it uses local spices and organic cacao to create island-inspired origin chocolates.

**Highlights:**

- Uses high-quality Indonesian cacao beans
- Bean-to-bar premium chocolate
- Multiple flavors: pure cacao, mango dark chocolate, etc.
- Beautifully packaged, perfect for gifting

**Quality:** Clearly labeled cacao percentages, a mid-to-high-end chocolate brand, and one of Bali's most popular chocolate souvenirs.

**Where to buy:** The flagship store in Ubud has the fullest selection, with a glass workshop to watch the production process; there are also branches in Seminyak and Kuta.

**Personal Recommendation:** If you prefer something not too sweet, go for the original cocoa flavor—you won't be disappointed. I regretted not buying more after returning to Taiwan! (Recommended by [All Is Self · Soul Awakening](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/17AMwFcBD5/))

## TICKET TO THE MOON Backpack

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1788834353680-o1cn01c29jxw1xyru46euzr_2215653026455.webp)

**Ticket to the Moon (TTTM)** is a souvenir brand with strong "Bali roots." Founded in 1996 in Bali, the brand first gained fame for its hammocks, later expanding into backpacks, sling bags, travel accessories, and upcycled products made from leftover materials.

Their backpacks are designed to be lightweight, durable, and travel-friendly, available in sizes like 15L, 25L, and the 6L Mini Backpack. I especially recommend these as gifts for those who dislike items that "disappear after eating"—they're not just souvenirs but practical items you can use on your next trip. The brand also emphasizes upcycling and sustainability, with some products crafted from leftover fabric from hammock production. If you're looking for a quality, practical item that represents Bali, TTTM is at the top of my list.

## Gery Chocolate Cookies

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1790484209838-gery_snack.jpeg)

**Gery Snack Bantal Cokelat** is a chocolate-flavored snack from the Gery brand under Indonesia's Garudafood. Shaped like little pillows, it's called **Bantal** (meaning "pillow" in Indonesian). Its biggest appeal isn't "premium" but rather **affordable, lightweight, and full of that everyday Indonesian snack vibe**.

Garudafood's Gery also offers other chocolate-flavored snacks worth checking out at Indomaret, Alfamart, or larger supermarkets.
