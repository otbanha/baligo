If you and your partner are dreaming of an exotic, magical honeymoon or a place to rekindle your romance, choosing Bali will not disappoint!

**Bottom line**: The top three romantic activities for a Bali honeymoon—**Ubud Swing** (choose from Aloha Swing, Bali Swing, or Desa Swing for ethereal couple photos), **visit a coffee plantation** (Satria Luwak Coffee for a civet coffee-making workshop), and enjoy private time at a secluded villa. Below is the full list of ten romantic activities.

Among Indonesia's 18,000 islands, Bali paints a romantic picture with its tropical rainbow-hued coastlines, towering volcanoes, lush rice terraces, and dense jungles.
