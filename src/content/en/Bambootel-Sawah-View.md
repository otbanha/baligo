### An Adults-Only Bamboo Luxury Retreat Amid Rice Fields - Bambootel Sawah View

Bali is full of stunning accommodations, but the truly remarkable ones make you never want to leave. **Bambootel Sawah View** is exactly that caliber. With all-bamboo architecture, rice field vistas, private pools, and top-tier ratings of 4.9 on Google, 4.9 on Tripadvisor, and 9.7 on Booking.com, this adults-only resort in the Tampaksiring highlands has become one of Bali's iconic stays that's as beautiful in photos as it is to experience.

***

## 📍 Location & Surroundings

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-08/1786028968964-l0222212000q7gl8rrfc56_w_1280_853_r5.webp)

Though often listed under 'Ubud hotels' on booking platforms, Bambootel Sawah View is actually located in **Tampaksiring**, about 11 kilometers (a 30-40 minute drive) from central Ubud. The Tirta Empul Holy Water Temple and Gunung Kawi rock-cut shrines are both within a 10-minute drive, making it an ideal base for exploring the spiritual sites north of Ubud.

To be clear: this isn't for travelers who need to commute daily to Ubud's market or downtown restaurants. It's better suited for a 'check in and unwind for two or three days' rhythm, treating the resort itself as the destination rather than just a place to sleep.

The entire resort is centered around bamboo architecture, complemented by rice paddies, valleys, and jungle landscapes, creating an atmosphere that feels warmer in person than in photos. The property features multiple pools, with some private pool villas offering direct front-row views of the rice fields.

For dining, both Nini Restaurant and Maniq Restaurant receive high praise from guests. Breakfast is not a standard buffet but a refined meal with presentation and choices. Cocktails also frequently appear in positive reviews, with many travelers initially planning to dine out but ending up staying at the hotel for several consecutive evenings. Note that dining prices are on the higher side, and dinner options are relatively limited, so you'll need to budget a bit more.

The resort is **Adults Only**, offering a quiet and private environment with no Kids Club or family-friendly facilities.

Room options include villas with private heated pools, suites with Jacuzzis, one/two/three-bedroom villas, and suites with direct access to shared pools. Cleanliness and comfort receive top ratings across all platforms.

A few things to note: Bamboo houses and some villas feature semi-open designs, with rice paddies and jungle right next to them—cricket sounds, geckos, and insects are part of the natural environment. If you're sensitive to bugs, it's recommended to choose more enclosed options like the Suite with Pool Access or Deluxe Suite with Jacuzzi. Additionally, views can vary significantly within the same room type depending on location, floor, and orientation, so it's wise to request confirmation of front-row rice field views when booking.

## 👥 Who It's For

An ideal choice for honeymoons, couples, and anniversary trips, as well as travelers looking to completely unwind in Bali and capture unique accommodation content. Not suitable for families with children (adults only) or those who prefer to use their stay as a base for daily outings.
