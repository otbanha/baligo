After arriving in Indonesia or Bali, follow these steps in order to quickly start enjoying your vacation:

1. **Deplane**  
   When you arrive at Bali Airport, follow the signs and get off the plane.
2. **Apply for/Pay Visa on Arrival**  
   If you haven't applied for an e-Visa in advance, your first stop is the Visa on Arrival (VoA) counter at the airport to queue for a visa on arrival.  
   ▶️ VoA or e-Visa? Check out the [Complete Guide to Indonesia/Bali eVisa (eVoA)](/en/blog/bali-evisa-application-guide/)
3. **Fill Out the Online Health Declaration**  
   To prevent the spread of infectious diseases such as monkeypox and human metapneumovirus, travelers must complete the "Health Declaration (SSHP)."  
   ▶️ How to fill it out? See the SSHP guide
4. **Queue at Immigration - Passport/Visa Check and Stamp**  
   At the immigration counter, wait in line for your passport and visa documents to be inspected. Ensure your passport and visa documents are complete.
5. **Automated Clearance Service**  
   If you hold an e-Visa, skip step 4 and proceed directly to the "Automated Gate" at Jakarta International Airport and Bali International Airport.  
   ▶️ How to use automated clearance? See the [Bali Automated Clearance Guide](/en/blog/2025-bali-automatic-customs/)
6. **Collect Luggage**  
   After completing immigration inspection/automated clearance, proceed to the baggage claim area to collect your luggage.
7. **Customs Baggage Check**  
   The final step is passing through customs. Please fill out the e-Customs Declaration (e-CD) in advance and present it to customs officers. Be sure to follow regulations and do not bring prohibited items such as fruits, plants, or excess foreign currency.  
   ▶️ Detailed customs declaration rules and guide: [e-Customs Declaration Guide](/en/blog/bali-e-customs-declaration/)
8. **Tourist Levy**  
   From February 14, 2024, international tourists entering Bali must pay this in advance.  
   ▶️ How to pay? See the [Tourist Tax Guide](/en/blog/2026-bali-tourism-tax-guide/)

> Further Reading: [What documents do you need for Bali entry?](/en/blog/indonesia-bali-visa-evoa-faq/)

**Time to start your vacation!**  
After clearing customs, congratulations on completing the entry process! Now you can head to the arrival hall to meet your pre-booked driver, family, or friends, and begin your wonderful journey!
