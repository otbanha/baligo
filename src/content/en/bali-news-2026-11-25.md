# **Bali Latest News | November 25: Heavy Rain Warning, Offshore Speedboat Checks, and Foreigner Deportations**

Bali's rainy season continues to bring weather and transportation challenges. BMKG's latest forecast indicates that on November 25, some areas may still experience moderate to heavy rain and thunderstorms, with spots like Bedugul, Kintamani, and Besakih needing to watch for changing weather.

Additionally, as the Christmas and New Year travel peak approaches, Bali Police have begun stepping up maritime traffic safety inspections, especially for speedboats heading to and from Nusa Penida. Immigration has also announced the latest foreigner deportation cases, while an Indian cultural festival is underway in Sanur.

## **🌧️ BMKG Warns of Heavy Rain in Some Areas on November 25! Kintamani, Bedugul, and Besakih on Alert for Thunderstorms**

The Indonesian Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) Bali Region III Meteorological Center issued a latest warning on November 24, predicting moderate to heavy rain with possible thunderstorms in parts of Bali from November 24 to 27.

**November 25 Island-Wide Weather Overview:**

| Item | Forecast |
| --- | --- |
| Overall Weather | Light rain, locally stronger possible |
| Temperature | 20–33°C |
| Relative Humidity | 70–98% |
| Wind Direction | Southeast to Southwest |
| Wind Speed | 4–30 km/h |
| Northern Sea Wave Height | 0.25–1 meter |
| Southern Sea Wave Height | 0.5–2.5 meters |

BMKG has issued rainfall warnings for different time periods:

- **November 25 to 8 AM on November 26:** Moderate rainfall possible in Badung, Bangli, Buleleng, Karangasem, and Tabanan.
- **November 26 to 8 AM on November 27:** Heavy rain alert for Badung, Buleleng, and Tabanan; moderate rainfall expected in Bangli, Denpasar, Karangasem, Gianyar, and Klungkung.

Additionally, BMKG specifically noted that the following tourist areas may experience rain and thunderstorms on November 25:

- Bedugul
- Kintamani
- Besakih (Mother Temple)

**Impact on Travelers:**

If you have plans for Kintamani's volcano views, Bedugul's lake area, or Besakih Mother Temple tomorrow, keep your itinerary flexible and avoid outdoor mountain activities during thunderstorms.

Wave heights in southern waters could reach 2.5 meters. Travelers heading to Nusa Penida or Nusa Lembongan should check with speedboat companies to confirm normal operations before departure.

The rainy season doesn't mean it will rain all day, but it's best not to pack waterfall, mountain, and sea activities too tightly these days.

## **🚤 Bali Police Deploy 4 Patrol Boats! Fast Boat Safety Checks Intensified for Nusa Penida**

As the Christmas and 2027 New Year travel season approaches, the Bali Police Maritime Unit (Ditpolairud Polda Bali) has launched integrated port security patrols, deploying 4 patrol boats to strengthen management at all maritime entry points in Bali.

According to a November 24 report by Bali Post, police are particularly focused on the Badung Strait (Selat Badung), including fast boat routes from Sanur and Kusamba to Nusa Penida.

This safety management effort is divided into two aspects:

**Port Inspections**

Police, together with port authorities, conduct pre-departure checks at each port, mainly verifying:

- Whether the number of passengers on board matches the passenger manifest.
- Whether there is any overloading.
- Whether the number of life jackets matches the passenger count.
- Whether fire extinguishers and life rafts are in working order.

If a vessel's equipment does not meet safety requirements, it will not be permitted to depart.

**Maritime Patrols**

Four patrol boats will strengthen monitoring of accident-prone routes, especially speedboats traveling to and from Nusa Penida, to prevent speeding, dangerous overtaking, or cutting into the paths of large vessels.

Police will also collaborate with BMKG, Basarnas (the Indonesian search and rescue agency), and the Navy to adjust patrols and safety management based on weather and sea conditions.

**Impact on Travelers:**

This measure is particularly important for passengers taking speedboats from Sanur to Nusa Penida and Kusamba to Nusa Penida.

Recommendations:

- Choose speedboat operators that are officially licensed and equipped with safety gear.
- Do not board overloaded vessels just to save time.
- Check that life jackets and safety equipment are available before departure.
- During the rainy season, don't just rely on the ferry company's original schedule—confirm whether departures are running normally that day.

This enhanced inspection does not mean a complete suspension of inter-island routes; rather, it aims to reduce the risk of maritime accidents before the peak season at year-end.

## **🛂 Bali Immigration Deports 5 More Foreigners! Including Overstays and a Tattoo Shop Run on a Visit Visa**

Bali's Denpasar Immigration Detention Center (Rudenim Denpasar) announced on November 24 that it recently deported five foreigners who violated Indonesian immigration regulations, hailing from Russia, Yemen, India, and Australia.

Among these cases, the disclosed violations include:

| Nationality | Violation |
| --- | --- |
| Russian | Overstayed by 45 days |
| Indian | Overstayed by 187 days |
| Yemeni | Overstayed by 64 days |
| Australian | Ran a tattoo business in Kuta on a visit visa |
| Australian | Overstayed by 787 days, more than two years |

The case of the Australian running a tattoo business in Kuta on a visit visa is particularly noteworthy.

Immigration authorities stated that holding a visit visa does not permit engaging in unauthorized commercial activities in Bali, leading to deportation under the law.

Another Australian overstayed by 787 days, exceeding two years.

Teguh Mentalyadi, head of the detention center, emphasized that foreigners in Indonesia must comply with the same immigration rules, and deportation procedures are carried out after completing administrative formalities and preparing travel documents.

**Reminders for travelers and long-term residents:**

- VOA and eVOA must be used within the allowed stay period or extended legally.
- A visitor visa is not a work permit.
- Even if you're running a small business in Bali, make sure you have the right visa and work permit.
- If you plan to stay long-term, don't wait until your visa is about to expire to handle the extension.

General tourists don't need to worry too much about such cases, but visa validity and whether your activities match the permit scope are indeed things you can't overlook.

## **🎭 Odisha Parab Indian Culture Festival Comes to Sanur! Free Entry, Last Day November 25**

If you happen to be near Sanur tomorrow, keep an eye out for a special international cultural event.

The Odisha State Tourism Department, in collaboration with the Indian Consulate General in Bali, is hosting the Odisha Parab 2026 cultural showcase at The Meru Sanur from November 24 to 25.

This event highlights the historic maritime trade and cultural exchange between India's Odisha and Bali, inspired by Odisha's famous Bali Yatra maritime tradition.

| Item | Details |

The event features:

For travelers staying in Sanur or vacationing nearby, this is a chance to experience the culture, traditional attire, dance, and cuisine of eastern India—without paying an extra admission fee.
