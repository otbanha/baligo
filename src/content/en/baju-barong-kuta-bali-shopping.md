When vacationing in Bali, everyone wants to snag the most iconic national attire—the Barong shirt (Baju Barong)—along with various tropical souvenirs. But did you know? While the well-known large souvenir centers frequented by tourists are convenient, they often come with a hefty 'tourist markup.'

Today, we're unlocking a shopping secret that **only locals know, with genuinely great prices and unbeatable value**: Baju Barong Oleh Oleh Khas Bali in Kuta.

## 1. Why Is This a Price-Friendly Secret Known Only to Locals?

https://www.instagram.com/reel/C8OhZxGyTLe/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&vrfl=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==&srtk=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

Based on authentic local reviews and insights from Indonesian visitors, this shop is highly recommended by insiders for several key reasons:

1. **Wholesale-Level Sweet Prices, the More You Buy the Better the Deal** The biggest draw here is the price tags displayed in the store (like multi-item discount deals). Compared to souvenir shops in tourist areas that often inflate prices, this place offers very fair pricing, even attracting locals or guides to bring friends for bulk shopping. Whether you buy 3, 4, or more items, the prices are highly competitive—a top choice for budget-conscious travelers.
2. **A Full Range of Classic Barong Shirts and Resort Wear** The store specializes in comfortable, breathable rayon Barong shirts perfect for tropical climates, casual tees, and various vacation dresses. With a wide variety of styles and colors, it's ideal for family matching outfits, couple sets, or for personal use and gifts.
3. **No Haggling Stress, Shop with Ease** Forget bargaining under the hot sun at traditional markets. Here, prices are clearly marked, and the selection is abundant. Besides clothing, you can also pick up sun hats, bags, and local snacks—a hidden gem for efficiently checking off your shopping list.
