## The Charm of Ubud

### Natural Beauty

![自然美景](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_196e1575dee8964008cb839fae2973d5.png)

Ubud is renowned for its natural beauty, nestled among rice fields and forested areas, surrounded by river valleys. In Ubud, you can enjoy lush green hillsides, terraced rice paddies, waterfalls, and pristine river scenery.

### Art and Culture

![藝術與文化](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_1c5aa41f7f1a617f73a2295d168fb493.jpg)

Beyond natural beauty, Ubud is also famous for its Balinese art and culture. Most local residents in Ubud are artists, including painters, craftsmen, and dance artists. Along Ubud's streets, you can easily find galleries, wood carving, and stone carving shops. Additionally, Ubud boasts many museums, art galleries, and souvenir shops selling local handicrafts.

Every day, Balinese dance performances take place in various areas of Ubud. Moreover, Ubud is home to numerous Balinese Hindu temples and a beautiful palace.

## Distance and Travel Time

### Distance from Bali Airport to Ubud

Bali has only one international airport, Ngurah Rai International Airport. If you depart from Ngurah Rai Airport to central Ubud, the distance is about 40 kilometers, and the drive takes approximately one and a half to two and a half hours.

If you're staying in Seminyak, the distance to central Ubud is about 32 kilometers, with an average travel time of 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours.

However, given the worsening traffic congestion, it's wise to add extra buffer time to your estimated travel time!

## Best Transportation in Ubud

Knowing the best way to get around a tourist area is key to a great vacation—so what's the best transportation in Ubud?

Transportation in Ubud is similar to other tourist areas in Bali: there's no proper public transit, and the only option is a private vehicle. Therefore, the best ways to get around Ubud are by hiring a private driver or renting a scooter.

### Riding a Scooter – Ubud Transportation Guide

Scooters are the most widely used mode of transport in Bali, including the Ubud area. Most Balinese people ride scooters to get around. If you're interested in riding a scooter during your Ubud vacation, you can easily find scooter rental services.

Just search Google for "Bali scooter rental," and you'll find rental services near your accommodation. You can also ask the rental service to deliver the scooter to your hotel. Rental prices depend on the scooter model and rental duration—the longer you rent, the cheaper the rate. Fuel is not included! So remember to fill up the tank before a long trip.

Additionally, riding a motorcycle requires an International Driving Permit. You can obtain one in your home country or in Bali. Please note that when riding a motorcycle in Bali, you must wear a helmet at all times and get used to driving on the right side of the road, which is opposite to Taiwan. If you feel uncertain about the traffic conditions, you should choose other means of transportation.
