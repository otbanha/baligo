# Villa FEH | A Designer Villa in Bali with a 25-Meter Private Pool

If you're vacationing in Bali and prefer not to spend every day exploring attractions, but instead want a place where you can simply enjoy staying in, Villa FEH is worth considering. The highlight of this three-bedroom villa is its 25-meter private pool, complemented by tropical-inspired design and lush greenery. Just swimming, taking photos, and chatting with family or friends within the villa can give you a truly relaxing holiday vibe.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1791993061118-130.jpg)

## Location | Umalas Area, Convenient for Exploring Canggu and Seminyak

Villa FEH is situated in the Umalas area (part of Canggu), with easy access to popular spots like Canggu and Seminyak. Whether you're looking for cafes, restaurants, or shopping, the location offers decent convenience.

However, traffic in Bali can be unpredictable—places that look close on the map might take longer to reach by car. If your trip focuses on Canggu and Seminyak, this location is worth considering; but if you plan to head to Ubud or Uluwatu daily, factor in the travel time.

## Facilities | 25-Meter Private Pool, Never Bored Staying In

While most villa pools are mainly for splashing around, Villa FEH's 25-meter private pool is the standout feature. Not only can you swim, but the surrounding tropical plants and architectural design also enhance the vacation vibe.

In my opinion, the best way to enjoy a villa like this is not to pack your schedule too full. Swim in the morning, lounge by the pool in the afternoon, or snap some photos when the light is perfect—these moments feel far more like a vacation than rushing through multiple attractions.

Guest reviews also mention butler service, room cleaning, pool maintenance, and private chef services. However, it's still recommended to confirm before booking which services are included in the room rate and which require an additional fee.

## Rooms | Three Bedrooms, Ideal for Families or Friends Booking the Whole Villa

Villa FEH features three bedrooms, perfect for family trips or a getaway with a few close friends to Bali. Compared to booking separate hotel rooms, the biggest advantage of renting a whole villa is that everyone can share the pool and common spaces, making it more comfortable to eat, chat, or play in the water together.

The villa's design blends modern architecture with tropical elements, creating a natural connection between indoor and outdoor spaces. If you enjoy unique accommodations or want a spot that's great for photos during your trip, this design is a definite plus.

For actual occupancy, bed types, and bathroom configurations, it's best to check the latest information on the booking page, especially when traveling in a group—these details often matter more than how nice the photos look.

## Reviews | Pool and Design Praised, but Noise Issues to Note

Based on current booking platform data, Villa FEH has guest reviews on Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda, and Trip.com. Ratings displayed on each platform include Airbnb 4.91/5, Booking.com 9.1/10, Agoda 8.7/10, and Trip.com 9.8/10. Ratings and review counts may change over time, so it's best to check the latest data before booking.

Guests generally love the pool, architectural design, and overall space, with some reviews also praising the staff's service. However, high scores don't mean there are no drawbacks.

Some guests have reported being affected by music from parties at nearby villas on weekends. Additionally, past reviews have mentioned issues like bathtub water filling, cookware, and a few ants. These are individual guest experiences and don't necessarily reflect current conditions, but if you value sleep quality or equipment completeness, it's wise to confirm with the host before booking.

Also, if you're expecting a pool you can jump into anytime, remember to ask if it has a heating system. Past guests have noted that the water can be quite cool when there's no direct sunlight.

## Ending｜Perfect for Those Who Want to Fully Enjoy Villa Life

What makes Villa FEH truly special isn't just the three bedrooms—it's the 25-meter private pool and the overall design that invites you to slow down.

If you're planning a trip to Bali with family or friends, craving plenty of private space, and love swimming and taking photos, this villa deserves a spot on your accommodation list. However, it's wise to check the latest reviews, weekend noise levels, and whether services come with extra fees before booking.
