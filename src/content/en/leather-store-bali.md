When traveling to Bali, beyond the sun, sand, and resorts, hunting for high-quality local handmade leather goods is a shopping highlight for many travelers. Whether you're looking for ready-made accessories or want to design and customize one-of-a-kind leather pieces, Bali has plenty of highly recommended spots.

You might think custom bags are pricey! But in Bali, you'll find heavenly prices that surprise you—not only are the designs unique, but you can also craft them exactly to your taste.

This guide takes you through Bali's popular custom leather experiences and local favorite shops in Seminyak, Canggu, and Ubud!

## Highlights of Bali's Custom Leather Experience

The biggest joy of ordering leather goods in Bali lies in the high degree of freedom and incredible value for money:

- **Heavenly Prices & High Freedom**: Think custom bags are expensive? Here, heavenly prices let you easily own a personalized leather piece. You can choose from a wide range of unique designs or fully customize your own bag, selecting the leather, color, and style you want.
- **Wide Variety**: Many quality shops offer not only various bags but also sandals, boots, wallets, and more.

## Popular Leather Shops Mentioned in Reviews & Their Locations

Below is a curated list of highly recommended shops in popular areas like Seminyak, Canggu, and Ubud, offering exquisite leather goods and customization services, along with price references:

1. **Saura Leather**

    - **Location**: Near Double Six Lane in Seminyak
    - **Highlights**: As shared in online reviews, this shop boasts a wide array of beautiful options, from sandals, boots, and wallets to various bags. You can freely choose your preferred leather, color, and style, and even customize a bag that's uniquely yours—a truly immersive experience.

2. **Seminyak Leather Bali**

    - **Location**: Seminyak area (Jl. Raya Basangkasa No.15)
    - **Highlights**: Highly rated online, this store offers high-quality handmade leather goods, including boots, sandals, bags, and jackets. It's known for its friendly service and professional custom orders.

3. **Nam Leather**

    - **Location**: Multiple branches in Canggu and Ubud
- **Highlights**: In the artsy, bohemian enclave of Canggu and the creatively charged streets of Ubud, you'll find plenty of design-forward leather workshops and independent ateliers. These spots also offer leather selection and customization services, letting you wander through rice paddies and artistic villages while easily ordering a one-of-a-kind wallet, bag, or pair of sandals. Nam Leather, beloved for its premium genuine leather and meticulous craftsmanship, is a fantastic place to pick up quality leather goods, with friendly staff and a wide range of styles.

## Price Guide

Custom genuine leather sandals start at around **$45 USD**, while small to medium custom leather bags generally range from **$50 to $70 USD**. Larger or more complex designs cost about **$75 to $100 USD**. For bespoke leatherwork, this offers incredible value.

If you're dreaming of owning a personalized leather piece that reflects your style while in Bali, set aside some time to explore Seminyak, Canggu, or Ubud. Pick your favorite leather and design, enjoy unexpectedly high-quality craftsmanship at amazing prices, and take home a truly unique souvenir from your Bali adventure!
