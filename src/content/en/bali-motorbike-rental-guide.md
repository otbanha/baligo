**Bottom Line First**: Most Bali travelers are advised to **hire a private driver** rather than rent a scooter. Renting a scooter costs around Rp 50,000–80,000 per day—the cheapest and most flexible option—but requires an International Driving Permit, carries a high risk of accidents (with serious liability for foreigners), and often involves police stops. Hiring a private driver costs about Rp 600,000–800,000 per day (including driver, fuel, and parking), offering safety and comfort without any documents needed. Only travelers with scooter riding experience and itineraries focused on a single area should consider renting. Below is a full comparison across 12 points.

When exploring this beautiful island, **choosing your travel style** becomes a key consideration for visitors. Should you opt for the freedom of renting a scooter or the comfort of a private driver? This 'Bali Scooter Rental Guide' will take you deep into the pros and cons of both travel methods, helping you make an informed decision for your Bali adventure. Whether you seek thrill and spontaneity or prioritize comfort and safety, this guide offers practical advice for an unforgettable Bali trip.

## 12 Comparison Points: Private Driver vs. Renting a Scooter

![12項比較項目：包車 VS 租摩托車](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_d4d13794f3492c137a0f6b2dd1600eb6.png)

Here’s a breakdown of each comparison point:

### **Flexibility:**

- **Renting a Scooter:** Offers the highest flexibility, allowing you to go wherever you want and stop as long as you like. For adventurous, exploratory travelers, it provides maximum freedom.
- **Private Driver:** Itinerary and destinations must be planned in advance, but you can still set your own pace.

### Price:

- **Renting a Scooter:** The cheapest option, costing around 50,000 to 80,000 IDR per day. It's a budget-friendly choice for travelers on a tight budget.
- **Private Car Hire:** More expensive, averaging 600,000 to 800,000 IDR per day. While pricier, it offers a more comfortable and safer mode of transport. The price includes a driver, a five-seater car, fuel, the driver's meals, and parking fees. Tolls and entrance fees are not included. (See [Bali Private Car Hire Guide](/en/blog/bali-private-car-hire-guide/))

### Required Documents:

1. **Renting a Scooter:** You must first apply for an International Driving Permit. Tourists are required to hold an IDP and ensure the vehicle has a valid STNK (vehicle registration certificate) to comply with local regulations.
2. **Private Car Hire:** No documents are required. This eliminates the hassle of a driver's license, making it convenient for travelers without a motorcycle license or IDP.

### Safety:

- **Renting a Scooter:** Safety is very low because Bali's traffic is congested, and many foreigners who have never ridden a scooter in their lives drive recklessly on the roads. Moreover, if an accident occurs, remember the reality that "foreigners are always at fault." Regardless of who is to blame, stay humble and proactively offer to pay for damages. If the accident causes significant damage to someone else's property or results in injury, serious cases may involve criminal law. Trust me, this will be a very bad situation for you. Besides staying alert and following traffic rules, you might want to pray for safety beforehand. There have also been cases of Taiwanese tourists getting a flat tire on a scooter in remote areas—unexpected incidents are hard to prevent.

A recent news story that has sparked heated discussion on overseas Bali travel forums involves a Polish couple who had an accident while riding a scooter on the nearby island of Nusa Penida during their vacation. One of them died on the spot, and the other was seriously injured. Tragedies involving tourists driving themselves in Bali are all too common. Travelers should reconsider driving their own vehicles in Bali to avoid such tragedies—it's just not worth it! Police also urge rental companies to ensure renters have the ability to drive before handing over the keys and advise tourists to check the details of their travel insurance before riding. Many tourists do not have travel insurance or are unaware of the scope of coverage before renting, leaving them unprotected in the event of an accident.
- **Private Car Hire:** The safest option, as a professional driver handles the driving, reducing all risks associated with traffic.

### Police Checks:

- **Renting a Scooter:** Foreign tourists riding scooters often encounter police stopping them for not wearing helmets or violating traffic rules, resulting in fines. Many tourists also face random checkpoints where police demand money without reason. Be mindful of traffic regulations and police checkpoints.
- **Private car hire:** Not subject to this restriction. Eliminates the risk of police checks due to traffic violations or random stops.

### Extreme Sun or Rain:

- **Renting a scooter:** Prone to sunburn or getting soaked, especially when stuck in traffic jams.
- **Private car hire:** No need to endure the scorching sun or rain, offering a more comfortable travel environment.

### Guided Tour Service:

- **Renting a scooter:** No guided tour service; you need to research attractions on your own. Suitable for travelers who enjoy independent adventures.
- **Private car hire:** The driver can provide guided commentary, helping you understand Bali's culture and history. Ideal for travelers who want a deeper local experience.

### Introducing Hidden Local Food Gems:

- **Renting a scooter:** Hard to discover hidden local food gems; relies on your own exploration.
- **Private car hire:** Drivers are usually familiar with local dining options and can recommend specialty dishes, offering a richer culinary experience.

### Parking:

- **Renting a scooter:** Parking is limited, especially in busy areas, requiring extra effort to find a suitable spot.
- **Hiring a private driver:** No parking worries at all—just hop out to explore, and the driver handles everything.

### Getting lost:

- **Renting a scooter:** Bali's roads are winding and unfamiliar to newcomers, making it easy to get lost. GPS often leads to impassable routes.
- **Hiring a private driver:** Your driver acts as your personal navigator, eliminating the risk of getting lost for a more stress-free travel experience.

### Luggage care:

- **Renting a scooter:** You'll need to keep an eye on your luggage and figure out how to secure it safely.
- **Hiring a private driver:** The driver can watch over your luggage, giving you peace of mind to enjoy your adventures.

### For Longer Distances

- **Renting a Scooter:** Although Bali isn't huge, many attractions are over an hour apart, making scooters less ideal for hopping between sights.
- **Private Driver:** Using a private driver for sightseeing might not be as nimble as a scooter in traffic, but you get air conditioning, scenic drives, chats, and even a nap—perfect for longer journeys.

### For a Dedicated Photographer

- **Renting a Scooter:** Traveling on your own scooter with a partner makes it tough to capture those stunning Bali landscapes without a tripod.
- **Private Driver:** Most Bali private drivers have solid photography skills and know the best spots for epic travel shots. Just ask, and they'll snap plenty of photos you'll love!

With VoiceTra, you can smoothly get through your entire trip. If your English isn't great, we recommend downloading it—there are plenty of tutorials online.

## Conclusion

Renting a scooter in Bali might seem cheaper at first glance, but it comes with many more details to manage. If an accident occurs, you risk canceling your plans or even having to extend your stay—easily losing more than you save. On the other hand, hiring a private car or using Gojek offers a more convenient and worry-free travel experience.

By now, you should know how to make a smart choice between renting a scooter and hiring a driver based on your preferences and needs, ensuring your adventure in Bali is smoother and more enjoyable.
