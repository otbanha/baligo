**Bottom Line First**: Spanning 90 hectares, Ayana Bali is divided into **4 distinct luxury hotel zones** (Ayana Resort, RIMBA, Villas, etc.), featuring **19 restaurants and bars, 14 pools, and an 18-hole golf course**. Its iconic **Rock Bar requires advance reservations** to secure the best sunset seats, making it ideal to spend 2-3 days exploring the diverse amenities within the same complex.

Bali's Ayana Bali is a sprawling resort complex beloved by travelers, covering 90 hectares. Across this vast expanse, Ayana Bali offers a wealth of facilities and activities for an unforgettable getaway. The entire property boasts 19 restaurants and bars, 14 different pools (check out all pool images on the [official website](https://www.ayana.com/bali/facilities/)), an 18-hole golf course, a kids' club, sunset yoga, the famous Rock Bar, a private beach, and, of course, unparalleled ocean views!

https://youtube.com/shorts/twUxgOqdq3E

Within the Ayana Bali complex, there are four distinct hotel zones, each representing a different level of luxury. Think of them as four five-star resorts with varying prices and styles, spread across different parts of the Ayana Bali grounds. They are:

- **Ayana Resort**: The heart of Ayana, offering a range of comfortable rooms and suites, along with numerous facilities and activities.
- **Ayana Villas**: For those seeking more privacy and luxury, Ayana Villas is an excellent choice. It features private villas where you can indulge in tranquility and exclusivity.
- **Ayana Segara**: The newest addition to Ayana, this zone offers a resort experience closer to the sea.
- **Rimba by Ayana**: Another standout option under the Ayana umbrella, Rimba provides diverse accommodations and amenities. Though not beachfront, it still delivers a luxurious resort experience.

No matter which hotel zone you choose, you'll have access to all the facilities and activities within the Ayana Bali resort, ensuring unparalleled vacation enjoyment. Ayana Bali is renowned not only for its luxurious accommodations and refined amenities but also for its stunning coastline and exceptional service.

Let's start with an introduction to Ayana Resort.

## Ayana Resort

![Ayana Resort Ocean View Suite](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_254ad6da007ff427eb99377385b6f4c9.jpg)

Ayana Resort Ocean View Suite

Ayana Resort is the largest resort within Ayana Bali. It houses over 500 rooms and suites, situated in the prime area of the complex with the most convenient access. Room and suite sizes range from 50 to 200 square meters, accommodating 2 to 6 guests.

The guest rooms and suites at Ayana Resort are decorated in traditional Balinese style, spacious and comfortable. Each room is equipped with modern amenities, including an LCD TV, complimentary Wi-Fi, and a minibar. Some suites also feature a private pool, balcony, or terrace.

Ayana Resort offers a wide range of public facilities, including 9 restaurants, 4 swimming pools, a spa, a fitness center, a yoga pavilion, and a kids' club. It is a mid-range option among the four accommodation choices.

RIMBA by Ayana

The word 'Rimba' means 'forest' in Indonesian. The RIMBA hotel area is not by the sea but is nestled within a lush tropical rainforest environment.

It's especially ideal for families with children, and with access to all of Ayana's shared facilities, it becomes a highly attractive option. Whether you're visiting Bali for a honeymoon, a romantic getaway, or simply seeking a great-value vacation, RIMBA by AYANA—with the lowest room rates—is worth considering.

### Bonus Highlight 5: SAKA Museum

Beyond its breathtaking natural beauty and top-tier service, the Ayana resort complex now boasts a cultural treasure! The SAKA Museum, newly opened in 2024, is a museum dedicated to Bali's rich culture. Through a variety of exhibits and interactive installations, visitors can delve into the history, traditions, and art of Bali.

![加分亮點5: SAKA Museum](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_3d01909c96a70fef9e0966181009f5d4.jpg)

**Museum Highlights**

- **Saka Calendar:** The SAKA Museum takes its name from Bali's ancient Saka calendar, which is closely tied to many of the island's festivals and ceremonies.
- **Cultural Exhibits:** The museum houses a rich collection of Balinese artifacts, including sculptures, paintings, and textiles, offering a glimpse into the intricacy and uniqueness of Balinese traditional crafts.
- **Interactive Experiences:** Beyond static displays, the museum features numerous interactive installations that allow visitors to personally experience Balinese traditional culture, such as making traditional instruments or learning Balinese dance.
- **Architectural Design:** The museum's architecture blends modern and traditional elements, creating a comfortable and artistic environment for exploration.

**Why Visit the SAKA Museum?**

- **Dive into Balinese Culture:** If you're curious about Balinese culture, the SAKA Museum is an absolute must-visit.
- **Enrich Your Travel Experience:** Beyond soaking up the sun and hitting the beach, a visit to the museum offers a deeper understanding of this beautiful island.

The SAKA Museum is not only a new highlight of the AYANA resort complex but also a true gem of Balinese culture. If you're staying at Ayana, be sure to set aside some time to explore the SAKA Museum—you'll leave with lasting memories.

### Bonus Highlight 14: Thalassotherapy Seawater Spa Pool

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1785485172375-thalassotherapy_2-1_zw3i9w_2016x0.webp)

When visiting the Ayana Resort, you absolutely cannot miss the world-renowned **Thalassotherapy Seawater Spa Pool**. This warm seawater therapy pool, which harnesses natural seawater from the Indian Ocean, utilizes the concept of thalassotherapy. Through mineral-rich seawater, varying water temperatures, and powerful hydrotherapy jets, it helps relax muscles, improve blood circulation, and boost metabolism, allowing your body and mind to fully unwind.

The entire spa pool features **12 hydrotherapy massage stations**, including a counter-current walkway, jet massage, bubble bath, hydrotherapy bed, and shoulder and neck massage. You can follow the guided circuit to experience full-body relaxation from your feet to your shoulders. Even better, the pool faces the magnificent Indian Ocean, so you can soak in the warm seawater while enjoying breathtaking ocean views, and in the evening, watch the stunning Balinese sunset—making it one of Ayana's most iconic healing experiences.

If you're staying at Ayana, it's recommended to set aside about an hour to fully enjoy this facility, then pair it with a sauna or steam room for a more complete therapeutic effect. For those wanting to experience Bali's top-tier resort atmosphere, the Thalassotherapy Seawater Spa Pool is definitely worth adding to your must-visit list.

### Bonus Highlight 15: Cashless Payment System

The entire Bali AYANA resort has now fully transitioned to a cashless payment system, offering travelers the primary benefits of **security** and **convenience**. Guests no longer need to carry large amounts of Indonesian rupiah cash while swimming or at the beach, completely eliminating worries about losing wallets or getting banknotes wet, while also avoiding potential scams at off-site currency exchange shops. Whether using credit cards, mobile payments, or charging expenses to your room number (Room Charge), this seamless payment process allows guests to immerse themselves more fully in the resort atmosphere, free from the interruptions of tedious cash counting.

Additionally, the full adoption of digital payments has significantly improved the resort's operational efficiency and financial transparency. With contactless card payments or QR code scans, checkout at restaurants and shops has become much faster, and guests can clearly track every expense through digital billing upon checkout. Amid growing health awareness, reducing physical currency contact also adds a layer of health protection to the journey. This model not only aligns with modern digital travel trends but also makes the service at this top-tier resort feel more streamlined and contemporary.

### Bonus Highlight 16: Free **AYANA Rewards** Membership

**This is my top-recommended money-saving tip!**

Whenever I stay at AYANA, I always [sign up for the free **AYANA Rewards** membership](https://rewards.ayana.com/?lang=en) first, because most purchases within the resort come with a **10% discount**.

Why is it a must? Because prices at AYANA's restaurants and bars are subject to an additional **10% service charge + 11% government tax**, meaning the final bill is **21%** higher than the menu price. First-time visitors are often shocked when they see the bill.

While the membership discount can't fully offset these extra charges, it at least helps you save a portion of the cost. If you plan to spend your days within the resort—having lunch or dinner beyond breakfast, or visiting Rock Bar for a few drinks—the savings really add up.

**I recommend completing your membership registration before check-in. It's free and risk-free, making it one of those must-do little tasks when visiting AYANA.**
