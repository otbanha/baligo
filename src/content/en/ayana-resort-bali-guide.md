**Bottom Line First**: Ayana Bali spans 90 hectares and is divided into **4 hotel zones with different luxury levels** (Ayana Resort, RIMBA, Villas, etc.), featuring **19 restaurants and bars, 14 pools, an 18-hole golf course**, and the iconic **Rock Bar, which requires advance booking** to secure the best sunset seats. Plan 2-3 days to fully enjoy the diverse facilities within the same complex.

Ayana Bali in Bali is a massive resort complex beloved by travelers, covering 90 hectares. Across this vast expanse, Ayana Bali offers a rich array of facilities and activities for an unforgettable vacation. The entire complex includes 19 restaurants and bars, 14 different pools (check out all pool images on the [official website](https://www.ayana.com/bali/facilities/)), an 18-hole golf course, a kids' club, sunset yoga, the famous Rock Bar, a private beach, and more—plus unparalleled ocean views!

https://youtube.com/shorts/twUxgOqdq3E

Within the Ayana Bali complex, there are four distinct hotel zones, each representing a different level of luxury. Think of them as four five-star resorts with varying prices and styles, spread across different parts of the Ayana Bali grounds. They are:

- **Ayana Resort**: The core of Ayana, offering a range of comfortable rooms and suites, along with numerous facilities and activities.
- **Ayana Villas**: If you seek more privacy and luxury, Ayana Villas is an excellent choice. It features private villas where you can indulge in tranquility and exclusivity.
- **Ayana Segara**: The newest addition to Ayana Resort, offering a vacation experience closer to the sea.
- **Rimba by Ayana**: Another outstanding option under the Ayana umbrella, Rimba provides diverse accommodations and amenities. Though not beachfront, it still delivers a luxurious resort experience.

No matter which hotel zone you choose, you can enjoy all the facilities and activities within the Ayana Bali resort, experiencing unparalleled vacation fun. Ayana Bali is renowned not only for its luxurious accommodations and refined amenities but also for its stunning coastline and exceptional service.

Let's start with an introduction to Ayana Resort.

## Ayana Resort

![Ayana Resort Ocean View Suite](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_254ad6da007ff427eb99377385b6f4c9.jpg)

Ayana Resort Ocean View Suite

Ayana Resort is the largest resort within Ayana Bali, boasting over 500 rooms and suites in the prime area of the property with the most convenient access. Room and suite sizes range from 50 to 200 square meters, accommodating 2 to 12 guests.

The rooms and suites at Ayana Resort are decorated in traditional Balinese style, offering spacious comfort. Each room is equipped with modern amenities, including LCD TVs, complimentary Wi-Fi, and minibars. Some suites also feature private pools, balconies, or terraces.

Ayana Resort offers a wide range of public facilities, including 9 restaurants, 4 swimming pools, a spa, fitness center, yoga pavilion, and kids' club. It is a mid-range option among the four accommodation choices.

### Bonus Highlight 1: Rock Bar

The Rock Bar mentioned above is a world-renowned sunset bar offering food, cocktails, and entertainment. Perched on the Jimbaran coastline, it's part of the Ayana Resort. Its name comes from being suspended on a low rock cliff. Rock Bar provides unparalleled views,

### Bonus Highlight 5: SAKA Museum

Beyond its stunning natural scenery and top-tier service, the AYANA Resort complex now boasts a cultural gem! The newly opened SAKA Museum in 2024 is a museum dedicated to Bali's rich culture, offering visitors an in-depth look into the island's history, traditions, and art through various exhibits and interactive installations.

![加分亮點5: SAKA Museum](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_3d01909c96a70fef9e0966181009f5d4.jpg)

**Museum Highlights**

- **The Saka Calendar:** The museum takes its name from Bali's ancient Saka calendar, which is closely tied to many of the island's festivals and ceremonies.
- **Cultural Exhibits:** The museum houses a rich collection of Balinese artifacts, including sculptures, paintings, and traditional attire, giving visitors a glimpse into the exquisite craftsmanship and uniqueness of Balinese traditions.
- **Interactive Experiences:** Beyond static displays, the museum features numerous interactive installations where visitors can personally experience Balinese culture, such as making traditional instruments or learning Balinese dance.
- **Architectural Design:** The museum's architecture blends modern and traditional elements, creating a comfortable and artistic environment for exploration.

**Why Visit SAKA Museum?**

- **Deepen Your Understanding of Balinese Culture:** If you're curious about Balinese culture, the SAKA Museum is an unmissable attraction.
- **Enrich Your Travel Experience:** Beyond enjoying the sun and beaches, a visit to the museum allows you to gain a deeper appreciation of this beautiful island.

The Saka Museum is not only a new highlight of the Ayana Resort complex, but also a shining gem of Balinese culture. If you're staying at Ayana, be sure to take some time to visit the Saka Museum—you'll surely leave with a wealth of inspiration.

### Bonus Highlight 14: Seawater Thalassotherapy Pool

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1785485172375-thalassotherapy_2-1_zw3i9w_2016x0.webp)

During your stay at AYANA Resort, don't miss the world-renowned **Thalassotherapy Seawater Pool**. This warm-water therapy pool, fed by natural seawater from the Indian Ocean, leverages the concept of thalassotherapy — using mineral-rich seawater, varying water temperatures, and powerful hydrotherapy jets to relax muscles, boost circulation, enhance metabolism, and deeply soothe both body and mind.

The pool features **12 hydrotherapy stations**, including a counter-current walkway, jet massage, bubble bath, hydrotherapy bed, and shoulder and neck massage. You can follow the guided circuit to experience full-body relaxation from your feet to your shoulders. Even better, the pool overlooks the vast Indian Ocean — soak in the warm seawater while taking in stunning ocean views, and catch the enchanting Bali sunset in the evening. It's one of AYANA's most iconic healing experiences.

If you're staying at AYANA, set aside about an hour to fully enjoy this facility, then pair it with a sauna or steam room for an even more complete wellness experience. For those seeking the ultimate Bali resort vibe, the Thalassotherapy Seawater Pool is a must on your itinerary.

### Bonus Highlight 15: Cashless Payment System

The AYANA Bali resort has fully transitioned to a cashless payment system, offering travelers two key benefits: **security** and **convenience**. Guests no longer need to carry large amounts of Indonesian rupiah while at the pool or beach, eliminating worries about lost wallets or wet bills, and avoiding potential scams at off-site currency exchange shops. Whether using credit cards, mobile payments, or charging expenses to your room (Room Charge), this seamless payment process lets guests immerse themselves in the vacation vibe without interruptions from counting cash.

Moreover, the fully digital payment system significantly boosts the resort's operational efficiency and billing transparency. With contactless card swipes or QR code scans, checkout at restaurants and shops becomes much faster, and guests can easily track every expense through an electronic bill at checkout. In an era of heightened hygiene awareness, reducing physical currency contact adds a layer of health protection to your trip. This model not only aligns with modern digital travel trends but also makes the service at this top-tier resort feel more streamlined and contemporary.

### Bonus Highlight 16: Free **AYANA Rewards** Membership

**This is my top money-saving tip!**

Whenever I stay at AYANA, I always [register for the free **AYANA Rewards** membership](https://rewards.ayana.com/?lang=en) first, because most purchases within the resort get a **10% discount**.

Why is it a must? Because prices at AYANA’s restaurants and bars already come with an additional **10% service charge + 11% government tax**, meaning your final bill will be **21%** higher than the menu price. First-timers often get a shock when they see the check.

While the membership discount can’t fully offset these extra fees, it does help cut down some of the cost. If you plan to stay within the resort for a few days—having lunch or dinner beyond breakfast, or hitting up Rock Bar for a few drinks—the savings really add up.

**I recommend completing your membership registration before check-in. It’s free and there’s nothing to lose—it’s one of those little must-dos when visiting AYANA.**
