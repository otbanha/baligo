**Bottom Line First**: Ayana Bali spans 90 hectares and is divided into **4 hotel zones with different luxury levels** (Ayana Resort, RIMBA, Villas, etc.), featuring **19 restaurants and bars, 14 pools, and an 18-hole golf course**. The iconic **Rock Bar requires advance reservations** to secure the best sunset seats, making it ideal to spend 2-3 days exploring the diverse facilities within the same complex.

Ayana Bali in Bali is a sprawling resort complex beloved by travelers, covering 90 hectares. Across this vast expanse, Ayana Bali offers a rich array of facilities and activities for an unforgettable getaway. The entire complex boasts 19 restaurants and bars, 14 different pools (check out all pool images on the [official website](https://www.ayana.com/bali/facilities/)), an 18-hole golf course, a kids' club, sunset yoga, the famous Rock Bar, a private beach, and more—plus unparalleled ocean views!

https://youtube.com/shorts/twUxgOqdq3E

Within the Ayana Bali complex, there are four distinct hotel zones, each representing a different level of luxury. Think of them as four five-star resorts with varying prices and styles, spread across different parts of the Ayana Bali grounds. They are:

- **Ayana Resort**: The core of Ayana, offering a variety of comfortable rooms and suites, along with numerous facilities and activities.
- **Ayana Villas**: If you seek more privacy and luxury, Ayana Villas is an excellent choice. It features private villas where you can fully indulge in tranquility and exclusivity.
- **Ayana Segara**: The newest addition to the Ayana resort, offering a closer-to-the-sea vacation experience.
- **Rimba by Ayana**: Another outstanding option under the Ayana umbrella, Rimba provides diverse accommodations and amenities. Though not beachfront, it still delivers a luxurious resort experience.

No matter which hotel zone you choose, you can enjoy all the facilities and activities within the Ayana Bali resort, experiencing unparalleled vacation fun. Ayana Bali is renowned not only for its luxurious accommodations and refined amenities but also for its stunning coastline and exceptional service.

Let's start with an introduction to Ayana Resort.

## Ayana Resort

![Ayana Resort Ocean View Suite](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_254ad6da007ff427eb99377385b6f4c9.jpg)

Ayana Resort Ocean View Suite

Ayana Resort is the largest resort within Ayana Bali. It features over 500 rooms and suites, located in the prime area of the complex with the most convenient access. Room and suite sizes range from 50 to 200 square meters, accommodating 2 to 6 guests.

The guest rooms and suites at Ayana Resort are decorated in traditional Balinese style, spacious and comfortable. Each room is equipped with modern amenities, including an LCD TV, complimentary Wi-Fi, and a minibar. Some suites also feature a private pool, balcony, or terrace.

Ayana Resort offers a wide range of public facilities, including 9 restaurants, 4 swimming pools, a spa, a fitness center, a yoga pavilion, and a kids' club. It is a mid-range option among the four accommodation choices.

## RIMBA by Ayana

RIMBA by Ayana

The word 'Rimba' means 'forest' in Indonesian. The RIMBA hotel area is not by the sea but is nestled within a lush tropical rainforest environment.

RIMBA by AYANA offers the best value for money among all accommodations at Ayana Bali, as guests can share all of Ayana Bali's facilities. This includes 19 restaurants and bars, 14 swimming pools, a private beach, Rock Bar, a kids' club/playground, and more across the Ayana resort. This means RIMBA guests can easily explore everything the Ayana resort has to offer while enjoying a more affordable stay.

Although the RIMBA hotel area is somewhat secluded, its luxurious accommodations are no less impressive. It's especially ideal for families with children, and with access to all of Ayana's shared facilities, it becomes an incredibly attractive option. Whether you're visiting Bali for a honeymoon, a romantic getaway, or simply seeking a great-value vacation stay, RIMBA by AYANA—with the lowest room rates—is worth considering.

### Bonus Highlight 5: SAKA Museum

Beyond its breathtaking natural scenery and top-tier service, the Ayana Resort now boasts a cultural treasure! The SAKA Museum, newly opened in 2024, is a museum dedicated to Bali's rich culture. Through a variety of exhibits and interactive installations, it offers visitors an in-depth look into Bali's history, traditions, and art.

![加分亮點5: SAKA Museum](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_3d01909c96a70fef9e0966181009f5d4.jpg)

**Museum Highlights**

- **Saka Calendar:** The SAKA Museum takes its name from Bali's ancient Saka calendar, which is deeply tied to many of the island's festivals and ceremonies.
- **Cultural Artifacts:** The museum houses a rich collection of Balinese artifacts, including sculptures, paintings, and textiles, offering a glimpse into the intricacy and uniqueness of Balinese traditional crafts.
- **Interactive Experiences:** Beyond static displays, the museum features numerous interactive installations, allowing visitors to personally experience Balinese traditions, such as making traditional instruments or learning Balinese dance.
- **Architectural Design:** The museum's architecture blends modern and traditional elements, creating a comfortable and artistically inspiring environment for exploration.

**Why Visit the SAKA Museum?**

- **Deep Dive into Balinese Culture:** If you're curious about Balinese culture, the SAKA Museum is an absolute must-visit.
- **Enrich Your Travel Experience:** Beyond enjoying the sun and beaches, visiting the museum offers a deeper understanding of this beautiful island.

The SAKA Museum is not only a new highlight of the Ayana resort complex but also a shining gem of Balinese culture. If you're staying at Ayana, be sure to take some time to visit the SAKA Museum—you'll surely leave with a wealth of new insights.

### Bonus Highlight 14: Thalassotherapy Seawater Spa Pool

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1785485172375-thalassotherapy_2-1_zw3i9w_2016x0.webp)

When visiting the Ayana Resort, you absolutely cannot miss the world-renowned **Thalassotherapy Seawater Spa Pool**. This warm seawater therapy pool, which harnesses natural seawater from the Indian Ocean, utilizes the concept of thalassotherapy. Through mineral-rich seawater, varying water temperatures, and powerful hydrotherapy jets, it helps relax muscles, improve blood circulation, and boost metabolism, providing complete physical and mental rejuvenation.

The entire spa pool features **12 hydrotherapy massage stations**, including a counter-current walkway, jet massage, bubble bath, hydrotherapy bed, and shoulder and neck massage. Guests can follow the guided circuit to experience full-body relaxation from head to toe. Even better, the pool faces the magnificent Indian Ocean, allowing you to soak in warm seawater while enjoying breathtaking ocean views. In the evening, you can also witness Bali's stunning sunsets, making it one of Ayana's most iconic healing experiences.

If you're staying at Ayana, it's recommended to set aside about an hour to fully enjoy this facility, then pair it with a visit to the sauna or steam room for a more complete therapeutic effect. For those wanting to experience Bali's top-tier resort atmosphere, the Thalassotherapy Seawater Spa Pool is definitely worth adding to your must-visit list.

### Bonus Highlight 15: Cashless Payment System

The current Bali AYANA resort has fully transitioned to a cashless payment system, offering travelers the primary advantages of 'security' and 'convenience.' Guests no longer need to carry large amounts of Indonesian rupiah cash while at the pool or beach, completely eliminating worries about losing wallets or paper bills getting wet, while also avoiding potential fraud risks at off-site currency exchange shops. Whether using credit cards, mobile payments, or charging expenses to their room number (Room Charge), this seamless payment process allows guests to immerse themselves more fully in the resort atmosphere, without being interrupted by tedious cash counting.

Additionally, the full adoption of digital payments has significantly boosted the resort's operational efficiency and financial transparency. With contactless card swiping or QR code scanning, checkout at restaurants and shops has become much faster, and guests can clearly track every expense through electronic bills when checking out. Amid heightened health awareness, reducing physical currency contact also adds a layer of health protection to the journey. This model not only aligns with modern digital travel trends but also makes the services at this top-tier resort feel more streamlined and contemporary.

### Bonus Highlight 16: Free **AYANA Rewards** Membership

**This is my top-recommended money-saving tip!**

Whenever I stay at AYANA, I always [sign up for the free **AYANA Rewards** membership](https://rewards.ayana.com/?lang=en) first, because most purchases within the resort come with a **10% discount**.

Why is it a must? Because prices at restaurants and bars within the AYANA resort already include an additional **10% service charge + 11% government tax**, meaning the final bill will be **21%** higher than the menu prices. First-timers often get a shock when they see the bill.

While the membership discount can't fully offset these extra charges, it can at least help you save a portion of the cost. If you plan to spend a few days within the resort—having lunches and dinners beyond breakfast, or visiting Rock Bar for a few drinks—the savings can really add up.

**I recommend completing your membership registration before check-in. It's free and comes with no downsides, making it a must-do little task when visiting AYANA.**
