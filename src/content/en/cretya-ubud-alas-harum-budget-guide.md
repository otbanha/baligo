## Learn How to Avoid Crowds and Save Money

Alas Harum and Cretya Ubud are places where you can spend an entire day in Bali! Imagine escaping the hustle and bustle of daily life and immersing yourself in the captivating world of Cretya and Alas Harum. This destination is perfect for both adventure seekers and those looking for a peaceful retreat.

Cretya Ubud, part of Alas Harum, is an absolutely secluded, luxurious, and fun three-tiered pool club that lets you escape the noise, enjoy tranquility, and blend into the beauty of nature.

This kind of upscale pool club usually doesn't come cheap! Let's talk about how to enjoy the luxury of Cretya Ubud's pool club without spending a fortune.

The entrance fee for Alas Harum is around $3.50 USD, granting you access to all its facilities. Inside, Cretya Ubud Pool Club offers a variety of options, including cabanas, day beds, hammocks, and VIP lounges, where you can sip your favorite cocktails while taking in the surrounding natural beauty. However, all these options come with a minimum spend, ranging from $130 to $260 USD.

And yet, our entire day's cost was just $20 (no minimum spend) for a poolside spot, including food and drinks. How did we pull that off?

First, make sure you arrive early so you can grab the best seats! The prime spot is on the ground floor, right by the pool opposite the shelves and changing rooms. This is the ultimate gem—shaded and with direct pool access, no need to spend over $200 on a daybed under the scorching sun. These seats have no minimum spend, meaning you don't have to pay extra!

## Reminders

Before you come, there are a few things you should know. Alas Harum is family-friendly, but Cretya is mainly for adults. The three-tiered pool area, including the bar and restaurant, is restricted to guests aged 18 and above.

In the pool area, visitors no longer need to carry their passports. However, identity checks may still occur. So, bring some form of ID, especially if you think you look too young. If you plan to book a floating breakfast at 7 AM, be prepared for the pool water to be very cold! Even under the midday sun, the water remains chilly. Also, there are no lockable lockers, only limited open shelves. So, it's best not to bring any valuables. And remember, the shelves can get crowded during peak hours.

## Getting Around

Reaching Alas Harum and Cretya Ubud is easy—just a 15-minute drive from Ubud’s center. You can use Grab or Gojek to hail a car or scooter for a convenient trip. We recommend not hiring a full-day driver to explore Alas Harum and Cretya. With so many activities and inviting pools, you might get immersed and waste precious time on a hired car. To save both time and money, skip having a driver wait outside, allowing you to fully enjoy the complete experience here.
