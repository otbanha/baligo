# Top Topics & News for Bali Travelers

## 🚋 Is Bali Really Getting a Tram? Kuta–Canggu Light Rail Plan Targets 2027 Construction

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-08/1786628608328-tram.png)

▲AI-generated image

The Badung Regency government is advancing a new public transit plan for the Kuta–Canggu corridor, proposing an **Autonomous Rail Transit (ART)** system—a tram-like, self-driving rail network.

Bali Governor Wayan Koster stated the goal is to **begin construction in 2027**, pending the completion of relevant regulations. The Badung Regency government is also preparing road and land consolidation to ease worsening traffic congestion in Kuta, Seminyak, and Canggu.

For travelers, this isn't a new mode of transport you can hop on tomorrow, but it marks a significant shift in Bali's future transit landscape. If realized, it could greatly impact travel between the airport, Kuta, and Canggu.

## 🛂 Bali Immigration Checks 66 Foreigners from 27 Countries in One Week

Bali's immigration office recently conducted the Dharma Dewata operation, checking **66 foreigners from 27 countries** in a single week, primarily to verify residence permits, activities, and compliance with Indonesian immigration regulations.

This also continues Bali's recent tightening of regulations on foreign nationals' activities and residency.

For travelers, the most important thing is still to ensure that your visa, length of stay, and activities in Indonesia comply with the rules. In particular, working, running a business, or engaging in other activities beyond the scope of a tourist visa can lead to immigration issues.

## 🦠 Bali Reports 45,365 Animal Bite Cases and 7 Deaths from Rabies This Year

This is a travel safety news story I believe is well worth including in tomorrow morning's briefing.

According to the latest data from the Bali Health Office, as of August 2026, the island has recorded **45,365 cases of bites from rabies-carrying animals (HPR)** , with **7 deaths**. On August 13, authorities also conducted a rabies vaccination campaign in Denpasar.

For travelers, this isn't meant to make you fear every dog you see, but rather to serve as a reminder: **don't pet unfamiliar dogs casually, and don't ignore a wound just because it looks small.**

If you are bitten or scratched by an animal like a dog or monkey, wash the wound immediately and seek medical help as soon as possible.

## 🎨 Jia CURATED 2026 Opens Today, Turning Sanur Beach into a Design and Art Exhibition

If you're in Bali tomorrow, you still have time to catch this event.

Jia CURATED 2026 runs from **August 13–17** at Pengembak Beach in Sanur, with the theme 'Nature Weave,' blending design, architecture, craftsmanship, art, and culture. The event features exhibitions, installations, talks, and workshops, all set right by the beach. According to official information, the event is open daily from 12:00 PM to 10:00 PM.

The "Architecture in Scale" exhibition brings together 25 architectural models from various Asian countries, including renowned teams like Kengo Kuma and IBUKU Studio.

If you happen to be staying in Sanur, Denpasar, or Nusa Dua, this makes for a better afternoon cultural outing than simply browsing a shopping mall.

## 🎉 Countdown to Buleleng Festival 2026: Major events kick off in Singaraja from August 18

A notable upcoming event in North Bali is the **Buleleng Festival (Bulfest) 2026**, taking place from **August 18 to 23** in Singaraja.

This year's theme highlights "Buleleng Tempo Dulu," bringing old Bali's street scenes, traditional cuisine, music, and culture back to the city center; the event also features themed zones blending tradition, modern creativity, and sustainability.

Notably, local authorities have already prepared **8 parking zones** to manage the crowds, and some roads in central Singaraja may see traffic adjustments during the event.

If you have a North Bali trip planned soon, this is an event to keep an eye on in the coming days; if you'd rather avoid crowds and traffic disruptions, you can also steer clear of the event's core area.
