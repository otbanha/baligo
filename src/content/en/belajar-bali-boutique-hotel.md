Belajar Bali Boutique Hotel & Retreat is a highly acclaimed boutique stay in the Canggu area. If you're planning a trip to Bali and looking for a small hotel with strong design aesthetics, excellent service, and a unique style, this might be one to add to your list. Below, we'll discuss its highlights, potential areas for improvement, and our recommendations.

![長谷波西米亞精品住宿推薦 Belajar Bali Boutique Hotel and Retreat](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7aa22519bdbfa6d3826a5a36aa6f9e3f.jpg)

***

## Quick Overview

Belajar Bali Boutique Hotel and Retreat is just 2.8 km from Batu Bolong Beach. It's a 5-star boutique resort hotel with only eight rooms, blending tranquility and luxury.

![快速一覽](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2e1137f6dfc10b014965bb6cbc578bab.jpg)

Outdoor Pool: Guest rooms feature private terraces with pool views, creating a relaxing vacation atmosphere.

Thoughtful Service: A 24-hour front desk, luggage storage, and a shared kitchen are available for guests' convenience.

Design Style: Each room is equipped with air conditioning, a desk, a safe, and a private bathroom, showcasing a simple yet Balinese-inspired aesthetic.

Convenient Location: It's also 2.8 km from Canggu Beach, about 8.4 km from Petitenget Temple, and just 17 km from Ngurah Rai International Airport.

![快速一覽](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b7da17399ac0cb739f0ada56fbb8724c.jpg)

***

## Highlight 1: Design & Atmosphere

Based on guest reviews, this hotel scores exceptionally high on design and atmosphere. One guest simply said: "The aesthetics of the property were beautiful. Comfortable stay…" Another review noted: "Stunning Instagram-worthy setting. Beautiful big and comfortable beds, lots of light and mirrors. Lovely pool area…"

Overall, it strikes a great balance between "vacation vibes" and "design flair": the interiors, common spaces, and pool area all exude a relaxing yet stylish atmosphere. If you're into photography or appreciate accommodations with a touch of aesthetic appeal, this place is sure to impress.

***

## Highlight 2: Service & Cleanliness

Many guests praised the attentive and thoughtful service. One wrote: "The staff were absolutely phenomenal, super helpful and friendly… They made us coffee in the mornings and evenings as we sat by the pool." In terms of cleanliness, it earned a stellar score of 9.6/10.

This is crucial for a stress-free getaway—especially if you want to unwind without worrying about the little things. Great service plus a clean environment can sometimes matter more than even the best location.

***

## Highlight 3: Location & Atmosphere Balance

The hotel is located near Batu Bolong in the Canggu area, offering a convenient location with good access to transport and daily essentials. According to one review: "I love the location, next to the shops – walking distance to everything… Loved the quietness of the villa at night so silence and peaceful."

Others have noted that despite being right off the main road, it remains quiet at night. This is a big plus for many travelers: easy access during the day without sacrificing peace at night.

***

### Points to Improve / Notes

* Room size: Some guests have pointed out that "the rooms are really small." If you're used to spacious accommodations or traveling with children, it's best to check whether the room type suits your family's needs.
* Slightly tricky access: One review mentioned, "The way to the property is a little difficult from the main road you enter a small lane and mostly cabs refuse to go inside." If you're traveling with young kids or heavy luggage, it's advisable to ask the hotel in advance about shuttle services or arrival instructions.
* Best suited for: Most guests feel the hotel is ideal for couples or pairs. One wrote, "Feels like you'd want to come with a partner or get a group of friends to get the entire complex to make the most of the experience." If you're traveling as a family, consider whether the space and activities are suitable for children.

***

### Booking Tips

1. **Photo spots**: With its strong design aesthetic and beautiful common areas (pool area, Boho-style interiors), it's very photogenic. Plan your shoots for early morning or dusk when the light is soft and crowds are thin.
2. **Family travel considerations**: While the space may not be as expansive as a large resort, if your children are young, ask about twin rooms or the possibility of adding an extra bed. You could also plan some playtime for the kids (e.g., exploring the area around the hotel) to make up for the limited room space.

***

In summary, Belajar Bali Boutique Hotel & Retreat is a small boutique hotel with elegant design, excellent service, and a convenient location. It's perfect for travelers seeking quality accommodation, or for couples and small groups. If you're planning a stay in Canggu and prefer a quiet yet refined option, definitely add this to your list!
