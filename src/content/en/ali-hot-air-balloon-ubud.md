# Can You Really Ride a Hot Air Balloon in Bali? Soar 50 Meters Above Ubud’s Rice Fields—Is This Dreamy Experience Worth It?

If you think you have to fly all the way to Cappadocia in Turkey for a hot air balloon ride, you might not know—**Bali’s Ubud actually offers a real hot air balloon experience!**

And it’s located at the renowned Tanah Gajah, a Resort by Hadiprana.

Recently updated by Bali.live in August 2026, this experience lifts the balloon to about **50 meters high**, offering a bird’s-eye view of Ubud’s surrounding rice fields, tropical greenery, and villages—making it a rare aerial sightseeing experience in Bali.

## 🎈 Where Is the Bali Hot Air Balloon?

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_6ed4e23babe897f0039aae5be80a2e83.jpg)

This experience takes place at Tanah Gajah in Ubud, a resort known for its rice fields, lush greenery, and serene atmosphere.

Unlike the typical hot air balloon adventures you might imagine, this one **isn’t a free flight**.

The balloon is tethered to the ground with ropes, rising and descending vertically to a maximum height of about 50 meters, so there’s no need to worry about it drifting off to other areas.

From above, you can see vast green rice paddies, tropical forests, and rural scenery around Ubud; if the weather is clear enough and visibility is good, you might also catch distant mountain views in the background.

In other words, it's more like:

**"Sending you up to a viewing platform 50 meters high"**

rather than flying across all of Bali in a hot air balloon.

## 🌅 Available at Both Sunrise and Sunset

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-08/1787803340858-mixcollage-27-aug-2026-12-00-pm-6546.jpg)

The official schedule currently offers two main time slots:

**Sunrise session: around 6:30 AM**

You ascend just as dawn breaks, watching Ubud slowly wake up from above. If early morning mist lingers over the rice paddies and valleys, the atmosphere is stunningly beautiful.

**Sunset session: around 5:30 PM**

If you prefer sunset and a romantic vibe, the sunset session is a better fit.

Especially for couples traveling, proposing, or celebrating anniversaries, the evening launch with the sunset feels much more like a 'date' than the morning one. The operator also notes that actual flight times may be adjusted due to wind and weather conditions.

## ☁️ Only 7–10 minutes—isn't that too short?

This is the first question many people ask when they hear about the hot air balloon experience.

The answer is: **It is indeed short.**

According to official information, the actual flight time is about **7–10 minutes**, reaching a maximum height of around 50 meters, and it doesn't fly across the landscape.

So if you're expecting a Cappadocia-style hot air balloon ride soaring over vast terrain, this might leave you thinking, 'That ended way too fast.'

But if you treat it as a **unique Bali experience plus an aerial photo session**, the perspective shifts entirely.

Especially since Ubud is famous for its rice paddies, forests, and valley views—looking down from 50 meters offers a completely different angle than any ground-level viewpoint.

## 💰 How much does it cost?

The official price from Tanah Gajah is currently:

IDR 2,662,000++ / per session

Maximum of **2 adults** per session, with a total weight limit of 150 kg. Passenger age is restricted to **3–70 years old**.

Additionally, the official package currently includes one free digital photo.

If you bring your own photographer to shoot on-site, an additional venue fee will be charged; drones are prohibited within the resort area.

However, actual pricing, packages, and booking terms may change, so it's recommended to check the latest official information before departure.

## ❤️ Who is this suitable for?

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-08/1787803399053-unnamed.webp)

I think this experience is best suited for the following types of travelers:

**Couples**

The sunset session plus hot air balloon ride already has a very romantic vibe, and pairing it with dinner or other resort experiences makes it even more complete.

**Honeymooners**

If you've already planned a few days in Ubud and want to add a more unique experience, a hot air balloon ride is certainly more memorable than the usual cafés or spas.

**Family Travel**

Officially, children aged 3 and above can join, and up to two adults can accompany them, so it's worth considering even if you're bringing kids.

**For Those Wanting Unique Photos**

This might actually be its biggest draw.

After all, 'floating in a hot air balloon over Bali's rice terraces' is a subject that easily makes your photos stand out from typical travel snapshots.

## ⚠️ Check the Weather Before Booking

The biggest variable for a hot air balloon ride is **weather and wind conditions**.

Even if you've already booked, the activity may be canceled at the last minute if the wind or weather isn't suitable for flying. This isn't just a scheduling issue—it's a safety concern.

So if you're specifically planning a Bali trip around the hot air balloon, I wouldn't recommend scheduling it on the last day before your flight back home.

It's best to leave some flexibility in your schedule.

Additionally, the official requirement is to book in advance, and each time slot needs at least two reservations to operate, so don't just show up and ask on the spot.

## 📍 My Take: Worth Experiencing, But Don't Expect a 'Real Flight'

If you ask me whether it's worth it, I'd say:

**It's worth it, but only if you know exactly what you're paying for.**

This isn't a long hot air balloon ride like in Cappadocia; it's a tethered ascent experience fixed to the ground.

The actual time in the air is only about 7 to 10 minutes, and the price isn't exactly cheap.

On the other hand, there are very few experiences in Bali right now that let you enjoy the Ubud rice terraces from the air like this.

If you've already visited the Ubud Palace, Tegallalang Rice Terrace, and the Campuhan Ridge Walk, and are looking for something a bit more unique, then this hot air balloon ride is actually quite interesting.

**Especially during sunrise or sunset, paired with Ubud's rice fields and valley views, it truly makes for a unique Bali memory.**
