This package not only includes guided visits to scenic spots but, more importantly, features a professional photographer who follows us throughout the day, using both a camera and a drone to capture every precious moment. For travelers like us who want stunning photos without missing out on the experience itself, this arrangement was absolutely perfect!

Right next door, we found the fantastic Beachwalk Shopping Center, packed with brand-name stores and restaurants that kept us happily exploring. Even better, crossing the street from the hotel brought us straight to Kuta Beach, where we learned to surf every day, feeling the thrill of the Indian Ocean waves. In the late afternoon, we enjoyed breathtaking sunsets from the beachfront pool. After dark, we strolled through the lively streets, sampling a wide variety of local and international cuisine. Kuta's nightlife gave us a taste of Bali's youthful, vibrant side.

## Itinerary Start – Departing Kuta at 8:00 AM

At eight in the morning, our photographer and guide Udin arrived promptly at our hotel in Kuta to pick us up. This professional photographer not only took photos for us but also served as our guide and driver, making the entire trip much more convenient and personalized.

As the car left Kuta, we began our journey to Ubud. The scenery gradually shifted from a coastal city to lush rural landscapes, heightening our excitement for the trip ahead.

## First Stop: Ubud Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary

Upon arriving at the Ubud Monkey Forest, our photographer purchased our entrance tickets, which were included in the package service. Stepping into this sacred forest, we were immediately awestruck by the dense jungle and ancient stone carvings.

Here, the mischievous monkeys became the perfect supporting characters in our photos. The photographer skillfully used professional cameras to capture precious moments of us interacting with them. The ancient temple structures, surrounded by lush greenery, felt especially mystical, with every angle offering a perfect backdrop. We spent nearly an hour here.

## Second Stop: Tegallalang Rice Terraces

Leaving the Monkey Forest, we headed to the famous Tegallalang Rice Terraces. The photographer again handled the entrance tickets, allowing us to enjoy this green wonder without any worries.

![第二站：德哥拉朗梯田](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_c9715ea0e3f5452bc5324c6403d129ca.jpg)

![第二站：德哥拉朗梯田](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0a56ec0011c1598693f0ffae9cb5a748.jpg)

Standing on the viewing platform, the layered rice paddies stretched to the horizon like green staircases, leaving us in awe. The photographer used a drone to capture the terraces' magnificent panorama from various heights and angles. We also strolled through the terraces, letting the photographer capture our natural expressions as we immersed ourselves in this beautiful landscape.

## Lunch Break

Near the Tegallalang Rice Terraces, we chose a local restaurant for lunch. Although the meal was at our own expense, being able to savor our chosen Balinese dishes while overlooking the terraced fields made it especially meaningful. The photographer also took this chance to show us a preview of the shots taken earlier.

## Third Stop: Tibumana Waterfall

After lunch, we headed to the final stop of the day—Tibumana Waterfall.

It's a bit of a distance from Ubud, but that's precisely why you can enjoy a serene, uncrowded experience. After a quick nap in the car, we arrived at the parking lot. Since our tour included all entrance fees, we just followed Udin. Walking along the path through the lush tropical rainforest, the sound of the waterfall grew clearer. Tibumana Waterfall cascaded down like silver threads, splashing into the emerald pool.

At three in the afternoon, we began our journey back to Kuta. In the car, the photographer shared amusing anecdotes from his shoots: 'Last week, a couple was proposing at the waterfall, and a monkey almost snatched the ring...'

Throughout the trip, the photographer not only professionally captured every precious moment but also introduced us to the history and cultural background of each site in fluent English, turning the journey into not just sightseeing but a deep cultural experience.
