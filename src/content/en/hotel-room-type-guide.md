**Bottom line upfront**: A common booking mistake—**"Double Bed Room" means a room with one double bed, not two separate beds**; if you want two separate beds, choose **"Twin Bed Room"**. Always check the actual definition of the English room type name before booking to avoid arriving and finding the bed setup doesn't match your expectations.

When choosing accommodations, the names of different room types can often be confusing, especially if we rely solely on literal translations, which can lead to misunderstandings. To ensure you pick the perfect room every time you book, let's carefully explore the differences among several common room types.

## 1. Double Bed Room ≠ Twin Bed Room

![2024剛開幕 Holiday Inn Resort Bali Canggu 的 double room, 收錄於「Canggu Top 11 你不能錯過的住宿推薦」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_c202fe1efa750793438b4dc51b3ef8cd.jpg)

[The double room at the newly opened Holiday Inn Resort Bali Canggu in 2024, featured in "Top 11 Canggu Accommodations You Can't Miss"](/en/blog/canggu-top-hotels-guide/)

First off, "Double Bed Room" does not mean a room with two beds. This room type typically features one double bed, ideal for two people sharing a single bed. If you prefer two separate beds, you should opt for a "Twin Bed Room."

## 2. Twin Bed Room

![Mövenpick Resort & Spa Jimbaran 的 Twin Bed Room 收錄於「推薦峇里島13家庭親子度假村」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_309bc96bfe45ee523bbce147e1724e8b.jpg)

[The Twin Bed Room at Mövenpick Resort & Spa Jimbaran, featured in "Top 13 Family-Friendly Resorts in Bali"](/en/blog/bali-family-resorts-kids-club/)

"Twin Bed Room" refers to a room with two single beds, perfect for friends, colleagues, or family members who each want their own sleeping space. This differs from a double bed, as each bed can be used independently, avoiding the issue of cramped sleeping quarters.

## 3. Queen Room vs. King Room

![Ayana Villas 裡的 king size 床，收錄於「金巴蘭住宿推薦」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b05e8ba8add16887fd7b682631c5ca21.jpg)

[The king-size bed at Ayana Villas, featured in 'Jimbaran Accommodation Guide'](/en/blog/jimbaran-beachfront-hotels-guide/)

"Queen Room" and "King Room" are room types named after bed sizes. A Queen Room features a queen-size bed, slightly larger than a standard double, comfortably accommodating two guests. A King Room, on the other hand, boasts a spacious king-size bed, making it an ideal choice for couples. These room types are a definite highlight for travelers seeking high comfort.

It's worth noting that international bed size classifications differ significantly from those in Taiwan. **Queen Size Bed Size Differences**

- International booking sites' Queen Bed: 152 cm wide × 203 cm long. Taiwan's standard 5-foot bed: 152 cm wide × 188 cm long. The width is the same.
- Taiwan's queen size (6-foot bed): 182 cm wide × 188 cm long.

**King Size Bed Size Differences**

- International standard King Bed: 76 × 80 inches (approx. 193 cm × 203 cm)
- Taiwan's king size 7-foot bed: 182 cm wide × 212 cm long.

These differences mean that when browsing international booking sites or purchasing international brand mattresses, it's essential to understand regional variations.

## 4. Suite

![FuramaXclusive Ocean Beach Seminyak 的雙人suite。收錄於「水明漾的濱海精緻主題住宿指南」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_461c02f27551ed0d744434a10c1b9b21.jpg)

[The double suite at FuramaXclusive Ocean Beach Seminyak, featured in 'Seminyak's Coastal Boutique Accommodation Guide'](/en/blog/seminyak-beach-resorts-guide/)

A "Suite" refers to a room that includes a separate bedroom and living area, offering more spacious accommodations. Suites are commonly found in upscale hotels and are ideal for long-stay travelers or families. Beyond living space, they may also feature a small kitchen, a sitting area, or even a balcony with views, enriching the overall stay experience.

## 5. Studio

![The River Studio ，收錄於「Canggu 16間私人泳池別墅推薦」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a71d09b55969fb57a332768ae1fbce57.jpg)

[The River Studio, featured in "16 Private Pool Villa Recommendations in Canggu"](/en/blog/canggu-villas-guide/)

A "Studio" is similar to an open-plan apartment, typically combining the bedroom, living room, and kitchen into one cohesive space. This room type is suitable for short or long stays by solo travelers or couples, offering a private yet compact layout.

## 6. Family Room

![Hard Rock Bali 家庭房，收錄在「峇里島親子樂園：十五家擁有滑水道溜滑梯的親子渡假村」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_59bf19edf13be74385d34d2e11c0abd4.png)

[Hard Rock Bali Family Room, featured in "Bali Family Paradise: 15 Resorts with Water Slides for Kids"](/en/blog/bali-family-resorts-water-slides/)

A "Family Room" is designed for families, often featuring multiple beds or sofa beds that can accommodate three to four guests. This room type is perfect for travelers with children, providing ample space and amenities to make family trips more relaxed and comfortable.

## 7. Connecting Room/Adjoining Rooms

![Maison Aurelia 連通房。 收錄在「Sanur沙努爾21家住宿推薦」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_de51fe89c909a2df0afc19bb8dfcf376.jpg)

[Maison Aurelia Connecting Room, featured in "21 Accommodation Recommendations in Sanur"](/en/blog/sanur-luxury-budget-resorts/)

"Connecting Rooms" or "Adjoining Rooms" refer to two adjacent rooms with an internal connecting door, ideal for travelers who want to stay close while maintaining privacy. This room type is best suited for families or group trips, especially when traveling with teenagers or elderly family members, offering a convenient and secure lodging experience.

## 8. Deluxe Room vs. Superior Room

![The Udaya Resort and Spa 豪華房，收錄在「烏布26家特色住宿」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_69d11bbbfbcce39e4b628e19da372766.jpg)

[The Udaya Resort and Spa Deluxe Room, featured in "26 Unique Accommodations in Ubud"](/en/blog/ubud-resorts-guide/)

"Deluxe Room" and "Superior Room" typically offer more upscale decor and views. Deluxe rooms are usually larger and come with added amenities like better bedding, scenic windows, or a balcony. Superior rooms, on the other hand, are often located on higher floors for better views or feature distinctive designs, catering to guests seeking a more refined experience.

## 9. Ocean View Room / Garden View Room

![2023開幕Anantara Uluwatu Bali Resort海景套房，收錄在「烏魯瓦圖 Uluwatu 12間無敵海景的住宿推薦」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_d419164ddf0dff8c8df0280c8be0eda2.jpg)

[2023-opened Anantara Uluwatu Bali Resort Ocean View Suite, featured in "12 Accommodations with Breathtaking Sea Views in Uluwatu"](/en/blog/uluwatu-bali-villas-resorts-guide/)

These room types are named based on the view from the window. An "Ocean View Room" offers a window or balcony directly facing the sea, perfect for a tropical island getaway, while a "Garden View Room" provides lush garden scenery, creating a relaxing atmosphere.

## 10. Villa

![烏布 The Purist Villas & Spa，收錄在「峇里島推薦villa大全」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_c101363a13237407d6e2bda528bfc4b8.jpg)

[Ubud's The Purist Villas & Spa, featured in "The Ultimate Guide to Bali's Recommended Villas"](/en/blog/bali-private-villa-recommendations/)

A "Villa" is typically a standalone building with a private entrance and personal space, equipped with amenities like a private pool and courtyard. This room type is often found in resorts and is the top choice for those seeking independence and privacy, ideal for honeymoons or family gatherings, offering a luxurious home-like experience.

In Bali, there are two other resort-friendly room types—"Bamboo Villa" and "Family Villa"—which are also perfect for travelers looking to enjoy a unique vacation experience on island destinations like Bali.

## 11. Bamboo Villa

![峇里島很多有特色的bamboo villas，請看「9家令人嘆為觀止竹屋別墅」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_9e29ac8b042c3e76181cc5143fbc4bcc.jpg)

[Discover Bali's unique bamboo villas in "9 Breathtaking Bamboo Villa Stays"](/en/blog/ubud-treehouse-bamboo-villa-stays/)

"Bamboo Villa" refers to a villa built primarily from bamboo, embodying eco-friendliness and harmony with nature. These bamboo villas in places like Bali are known for their simple, natural design, offering a peaceful escape from the hustle and bustle, where guests can enjoy fresh air and natural beauty. With open layouts that ensure excellent ventilation and cool breezes, they are perfect for travelers who appreciate unique design and sustainable living, adding a raw, memorable touch to the entire trip.

## 12. Family Villa

![Amarin Seminyak 最多有四間房，收錄在「三房/四房以上的家庭別墅住宿推薦」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_11c2a1c4adfc9e2a7779e39b3930a500.jpg)

[Amarin Seminyak offers up to four bedrooms, featured in "Recommended Family Villas with 3+ Bedrooms"](/en/blog/bali-group-villa-stay/)

"Family Villa" is designed for families, typically featuring multiple bedrooms, a spacious living room, and an outdoor lounge area that can accommodate the whole family. These villas often come with a private pool, garden, and kitchen facilities, making them convenient for families with children or elderly members. Especially popular in resort destinations like Bali, family villas allow travelers to enjoy the fun of a group trip while having enough private space, ideal for longer stays or a more relaxed family vacation.

## Summary

These 12 room types each have their own unique features, catering to different travelers' needs—from solo business trips to family holidays. Every room type offers a distinct accommodation experience. Whether it's a cozy double bed, a **Twin Bed Room** perfect for friends sharing, or the distinctive **Bamboo Villa** and **Family Villa**, choosing the right room type can make your trip even better.

When booking, consider your own needs and preferences, weigh the pros and cons of these room types, and find the ideal accommodation for you. I hope this guide helps you choose the perfect stay for your journey, making your Bali trip an unforgettable memory!
