# Bali Tourist Hot Topics + News Roundup (August 2, 2026)

## ✈️ North Bali International Airport Project Gains Central Government Support Again, Listed as National Development Priority

The Indonesian government has once again voiced support for accelerating the construction of the North Bali International Airport. After a recent briefing from the development team, President Prabowo reaffirmed his commitment to pushing forward this major project. The plan has been officially included in the 2025–2029 National Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMN). The proposed second international airport will be capable of handling large aircraft like the Boeing 777 and Airbus A380, and will be built under a private investment model, aiming to boost air capacity while driving tourism and economic growth in Buleleng Regency and the northern, western, and eastern regions.

**Traveler Advice:** This project is still in the planning and promotion phase. For now, all international flights will continue to use Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) as the main gateway, so there's no need to adjust existing itineraries, but it's worth keeping an eye on future construction progress.

***

## 🌊 Southern Waters Wave Heights Up to 3.5 Meters, Caution Advised for Water Activities

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) forecasts that wave heights in Bali's southern waters could reach up to **3.5 meters** on August 2. Strong winds and large waves are expected in southern and offshore areas, urging those engaging in water activities to stay vigilant.

**Traveler Advice:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, go surfing, diving, or take a speedboat, be sure to reconfirm ferry schedules and sea conditions with the boat company before departure, and follow the instructions of crew members and lifeguards.

***

## 🇮🇩 GWK to Host Indonesian Independence Carnival with Cultural Events in August

To celebrate the 81st anniversary of Indonesian independence, **GWK Cultural Park (Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park)** will host the "Pesta Rakyat" series of events in August, featuring traditional performances, live music, food, markets, and family activities, allowing visitors to immerse themselves in the festive atmosphere of Indonesia's National Day. Official pre-sale tickets for the event are now available.

**Traveler Tip:** If you're visiting Bali in mid-August, consider heading to GWK to not only admire the iconic Garuda Wisnu statue but also join the annual National Day celebrations.

***

## ⚓ Benoa Harbor Enhances Water Sports Safety Management to Boost Tourism Quality

The management of Benoa Harbor has announced ongoing efforts to strengthen water sports safety at Tanjung Benoa, including improving dock order, tightening operator safety regulations, and enhancing visitor convenience, aiming to reduce accident risks and provide a safer marine tourism environment.

**Traveler Tip:** When participating in activities like banana boats, parasailing, jet skis, or boat trips, choose licensed operators and wear a life jacket at all times. Do not remove safety gear for photos.

***

## 🛍️ Bali's First Premium Outlet Officially Opens, Creating a New Shopping Landmark

Located in the **Kura Kura Bali Special Economic Zone**, **Sira Village Grand Outlet Bali** has officially opened, becoming Bali's first Premium Outlet. The complex brings together over 100 brands, with official reports indicating prices are on average about 30% lower than typical shopping malls. It also combines dining, art spaces, ocean views, and real-time flight information services, making it a promising new shopping and leisure destination.

**Traveler's Tip:** If you're staying in the Sanur or Denpasar area, consider setting aside half a day or a full day to visit Sira Village. You can enjoy outlet shopping, dining, and sunset by the sea—all in one trip, satisfying both shopping and relaxation needs.
