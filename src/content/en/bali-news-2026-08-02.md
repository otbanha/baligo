# Bali Travel Hot Topics & News Roundup (August 2, 2026)

## ✈️ North Bali International Airport Project Gains Central Government Support, Listed as National Development Priority

The Indonesian government has once again expressed support for accelerating the construction of the North Bali International Airport. After hearing the latest briefing from the development team, President Prabowo reaffirmed the commitment to push forward with this major project. The plan has been officially included in the 2025–2029 National Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMN). This proposed second international airport will be capable of handling large aircraft like the Boeing 777 and Airbus A380, and will be built under a private investment model. The goal is to boost air traffic capacity while driving tourism and economic growth in Buleleng Regency and the northern, western, and eastern regions.

**Traveler Advice:** The project is still in the planning and promotion phase. For now, all international flights continue to use Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) as the main gateway, so there's no need to adjust existing itineraries. However, it's worth keeping an eye on future construction progress.

***

## 🌊 Southern Waters Wave Heights Up to 3.5 Meters, Caution Advised for Water Activities

Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysical Agency (BMKG) forecasts wave heights of up to **3.5 meters** in Bali's southern waters on August 2. Strong winds and high waves are expected in the southern region and offshore areas, urging those engaging in water activities to stay vigilant.

**Traveler Advice:** If you have plans to visit Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, go surfing, diving, or take a speedboat, be sure to reconfirm with the boat company about schedules and sea conditions before departure, and follow the instructions of the crew and lifeguards.

***

## 🇮🇩 GWK to Host Indonesian Independence Day Carnival, Multiple Cultural Events in August

To celebrate the 81st anniversary of Indonesia's independence, **GWK Cultural Park (Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park)** will host the "Pesta Rakyat" series of events in August, featuring traditional performances, music shows, food, markets, and family activities, allowing visitors to experience the festive atmosphere of Indonesia's National Day. Official pre-sale tickets for the event are now available.

> Ticket prices for these attractions fluctuate with the peak season, so check the latest reference prices on the **[Bali Ticket Price List](/en/tickets/)** before you go.

**Traveler Tip:** If you're visiting Bali in mid-August, consider a trip to GWK to not only admire the iconic Garuda Wisnu Kencana statue but also join the annual National Day celebrations.

***

## ⚓ Enhanced Water Sports Safety Management at Benoa Harbor to Boost Tourism Quality

The management of Benoa Harbor has announced ongoing efforts to strengthen water sports safety management in Tanjung Benoa, including improving dock order, tightening operator safety regulations, and enhancing visitor convenience, aiming to reduce accident risks and provide a safer marine tourism environment.

**Traveler Tip:** When participating in activities like banana boat rides, parasailing, jet skiing, or boat trips, choose licensed operators and wear a life jacket at all times. Do not remove safety gear for photos.

***

## 🛍️ Bali's First Premium Outlet Officially Opens, Creating a New Shopping Landmark

Located in the **Kura Kura Bali Special Economic Zone**, **Sira Village Grand Outlet Bali** has officially opened as Bali's first premium outlet. The complex features over 100 brands, with official claims that prices are on average about 30% cheaper than regular shopping malls. It also combines dining, art spaces, ocean views, and real-time flight information services, poised to become a new shopping and leisure destination.

**Traveler Tip:** If you're staying in the Sanur or Denpasar area, set aside half a day or a full day to visit Sira Village for outlet shopping, food, and sunset views by the sea—all in one trip to satisfy both shopping and relaxation needs.
