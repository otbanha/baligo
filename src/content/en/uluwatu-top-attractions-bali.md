**Bottom Line Up Front**: The number one must-do in Uluwatu is **Uluwatu Temple** (perched on a 17-meter cliff over the Indian Ocean, one of Bali's three most famous temples), offering stunning sunset views. But beware—the local monkeys are notoriously aggressive and will snatch your belongings, so be sure to **remove your glasses and jewelry** before entering. Another highlight is the evening fire dance performance.

Every moment spent in Uluwatu feels like a slice of heaven—and we're not exaggerating! This part of Bali is seriously underrated, and today we're taking you through the top 10 attractions and must-do activities in Uluwatu.

## #10: Visit Uluwatu Temple

![第十名：參觀Uluwatu Temple](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b953b86f0cdb3e5f162eec96ac861d2d.jpg)

Uluwatu Temple is considered one of Bali's most iconic temples, standing alongside Tanah Lot and Besakih. Perched on a 17-meter cliff above the Indian Ocean, it's one of Bali's three most famous temples and embodies the island's spiritual foundation. Beyond its religious significance, you'll be drawn by the breathtaking ocean views. It's also a prime spot to catch a spectacular sunset. Just watch out for the mischievous monkeys—they're among the wildest in Southeast Asia, so be sure to remove your glasses and jewelry.

## #9: Watch the Fire Dance Performance

![第九名：觀賞火舞表演](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_8e1e2cb735b2c791a156b870f1022afc.jpg)

Visitors to Uluwatu Temple can also enjoy the famous Kecak fire dance. This unique Balinese dance requires no musical instruments—instead, a large group of men chant in unison as accompaniment. Like all Balinese dances, it tells a story through movement. The best place to watch the fire dance in Bali is the stage at Uluwatu Temple, where you can enjoy the stunning sunset backdrop behind the performers.

## 8th Place: Enjoying Sunset Views

![Single Fin日落美景](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7913efd720450c575668b4f446c9e488.jpg)

Single Fin Sunset Views

Perched on the southwestern coastline, Uluwatu's cliffs offer breathtaking views of the Indian Ocean, making it one of Bali's top spots to watch the sunset. Witnessing the sunset in front of Uluwatu Temple is a truly unique experience. If you've already seen that, head to Single Fin bar to sip a cocktail while watching surfers ride the waves in the golden twilight, or visit Rock Bar for another stunning sunset spot.

## 6th: Explore the Hidden Gem of Suluban Beach

While Suluban Beach (also known as Blue Point Beach) isn't ideal for sunbathing, it's absolutely worth a visit to see its mysterious caves!

https://youtu.be/hQbBTktqEqY

It's a hotspot for surfers, who use these caves to access the sea and ride the wild waves. You'll need to descend a long staircase and pass through natural limestone caves to reach the shore. This one-of-a-kind beach is perfect for photo ops, and you can also watch surfers heading out into the ocean—a truly captivating sight.

## 5th Place: Visit GWK Cultural Park

![第五名：遊覽GWK文化園區](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_6c3c61665afdc5719285358d3e0c4bd9.jpg)

The Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park is the largest cultural park in Indonesia. It is dedicated to the Hindu god Wisnu and his mythical bird mount, Garuda. The park features Indonesia's tallest bronze statue of Wisnu riding Garuda, a major highlight. At this cultural landmark, you can also shop and enjoy traditional Balinese dance performances.

## 4th Place: Balangan Viewpoint

![第四名：Balangan Viewpoint](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_bc2912da85b54c0124095c6a7ac446b1.jpg)

Balangan cliff sunset point is a charming spot renowned for its stunning coastal views. It's one of the best places in Bali to watch a spectacular sunset, where you can witness the sun slowly sink below the horizon from the cliff's edge, leaving an unforgettable scene. You can also enjoy hiking over the rocks to soak in the natural beauty, or simply relax on the beach.

It's also a fantastic location for wedding photos, with many couples choosing Balangan cliff sunset point to capture precious memories. In this romantic setting, you can seize beautiful moments with your loved one and create an eternal imprint of your love.

## 3rd Place: Visit Green Bowl Beach

Green Bowl Beach is one of the most secluded beaches in Uluwatu, yet it's a paradise for travelers seeking peace and solitude. Its fine white sand, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and tranquil, cool atmosphere make it hard to leave. The beach stretches about 30 meters long, hidden beneath a 75-meter-high cliff. At the base of the cliff walls, two large limestone caves offer shelter. However, getting there requires descending over 300 steep steps.

https://youtu.be/8mzJm_UrjSg

The best time to visit is during low tide, which is also ideal for snorkeling. There are no drinks or meals sold on the beach, so don't forget to bring plenty of drinking water.

## Second Place: Tanah Barak Cliff / The Blue Highway

![第二名：Tanah Barak Cliff 斷崖公路/藍色公路](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7ae1b58d8b51712366caf4610c73688a.jpg)

Bali's Tanah Barak Cliff (also known as Pantai Batu Barak) is a hidden gem that has recently become a popular spot. This beach is a newly opened location, distinguished by its stunning blue waters and colorful rocks. Besides the magnificent sea views, the road here is carved through massive limestone cliffs, with the distant azure ocean and towering rock walls on both sides creating a powerful visual impact. You can also climb the central rock for better photo angles. Tanah Barak is about a 30-minute drive from the airport, and entry requires a ticket (currently priced at IDR 8,000 for adults and IDR 3,000 for children). It's open daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

## Number 1: Surfing and Diving

![第一名：衝浪與潛水](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f237c3322eff064a02804f4e6cffcc22.jpg)

Thanks to the excellent surf breaks along the Uluwatu coastline, this area is a surfer's paradise and a major draw for visitors to Uluwatu. There are numerous surf camps and lesson spots nearby, such as Uluwatu Beach, Padang Padang Beach, Bingin Beach, Green Bowl Beach, and Suluban Beach, among many others. Take Uluwatu Beach, for example—it's hailed as Uluwatu's biggest attraction. Besides its famous cliffside temple, it's also a popular spot for surfing and various water activities. You can comfortably watch the sunset from the sand or enjoy delicious food at nearby restaurants, and you'll surely be captivated by this beach's charm. Whether you're seeking the thrill of surfing or just want to relax and take in the view, this is an unmissable destination.
