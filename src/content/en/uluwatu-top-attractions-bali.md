**Bottom Line Up Front**: The number one must-do in Uluwatu is **Uluwatu Temple** (perched on a 17-meter cliff over the Indian Ocean, one of Bali's three most famous temples), offering stunning sunset views. But watch out—the local monkeys are incredibly aggressive and will snatch your belongings. Be sure to **remove your glasses and jewelry** before entering. Another highlight is the evening fire dance performance.

Every moment spent in Uluwatu feels like a heavenly experience—and we're not exaggerating! Bali's Uluwatu is highly underrated, and today, we'll take you through the top 10 attractions and must-do activities in Uluwatu.

## 10th: Visit Uluwatu Temple

![第十名：參觀Uluwatu Temple](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b953b86f0cdb3e5f162eec96ac861d2d.jpg)

Uluwatu Temple is considered one of Bali's most iconic temples, standing alongside Tanah Lot and Besakih Temple. Perched on a 17-meter cliff over the Indian Ocean, it is one of Bali's three most famous temples, embodying the essence of Balinese spirituality. Beyond its religious significance, you'll be drawn by the breathtaking ocean views. You can also enjoy spectacular sunsets here. But beware of the mischievous monkeys—they're among the wildest in Southeast Asia, so be sure to remove your glasses and jewelry.

## 9th: Watch the Fire Dance Performance

![第九名：觀賞火舞表演](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_8e1e2cb735b2c791a156b870f1022afc.jpg)

Visitors to Uluwatu Temple can also enjoy the famous Kecak fire dance. This unique Balinese dance requires no musical instruments—instead, a large group of men chant in unison as accompaniment. Like all Balinese dances, it tells a story through movement. The best place to watch the fire dance in Bali is the stage at Uluwatu Temple, where you can admire the stunning sunset backdrop behind the dancers.

## 8th: Enjoy the Sunset Views

![Single Fin日落美景](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7913efd720450c575668b4f446c9e488.jpg)

Single Fin Sunset Views

Perched on the southwest coast, the cliffs of Uluwatu offer sweeping views of the Indian Ocean at sunset, making it one of Bali's best spots to watch the sun go down. Witnessing the sunset in front of Uluwatu Temple is a truly unique experience. If you've already seen that, head to Single Fin bar to sip a cocktail while watching surfers ride the waves in the golden twilight—Rock Bar is another excellent sunset spot.

## 6th Place: Exploring the Hidden Gem of Suluban Beach

While Suluban Beach (also known as Blue Point Beach) isn't ideal for sunbathing, it's absolutely worth a visit to catch a glimpse of its mysterious caves!

{{video1}}

This is a hotspot for surfers, who use the caves to access the sea and ride the wild waves. You'll need to descend a long staircase and pass through natural limestone caves to reach the shore. This unique beach is perfect for photo ops, and you can also watch the thrilling sight of surfers heading out to sea.

## 5th Place: Visiting the GWK Cultural Park

![第五名：遊覽GWK文化園區](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_6c3c61665afdc5719285358d3e0c4bd9.jpg)

The Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park is the largest cultural park in Indonesia. It is dedicated to the Hindu god Wisnu and his mythical bird mount, "Garuda." The park features Indonesia's tallest bronze statue of Wisnu riding Garuda, a major highlight. At this cultural destination, you can also shop and enjoy traditional Balinese dance performances.

## 4th Place: Balangan Viewpoint

![第四名：Balangan Viewpoint](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_bc2912da85b54c0124095c6a7ac446b1.jpg)

Balangan cliff sunset point is a charming spot renowned for its stunning coastal views. It's one of the best places in Bali to watch a spectacular sunset, where you can stand on the cliff's edge and see the sun slowly dip below the horizon, leaving an unforgettable beautiful scene. Additionally, you can enjoy hiking over the rocks to soak in the natural beauty, or simply relax on the beach.

This is also a fantastic spot for wedding photos, with many couples choosing to capture precious memories at the Balangan Cliff Sunset Viewpoint. In this romantic setting, you can share beautiful moments with your loved one and leave an eternal imprint of your love.

## Third Place: Visit Green Bowl Beach

Green Bowl Beach is one of the most secluded beaches in Uluwatu, yet it serves as a peaceful haven for travelers seeking quiet solitude. Its fine white sand, crystal-clear blue waters, and serene, cool atmosphere make it hard to leave. The beach stretches about 30 meters long, hidden beneath a 75-meter-high cliff. At the base of the cliff walls, two large limestone caves offer shelter. However, reaching it requires descending over 300 steep steps.

{{video2}}

The best time to visit is during low tide, which is also ideal for snorkeling. There are no drinks or food sold on the beach, so be sure to bring plenty of drinking water.

## Second Place: Tanah Barak Cliff Road / Blue Road

![第二名：Tanah Barak Cliff 斷崖公路/藍色公路](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7ae1b58d8b51712366caf4610c73688a.jpg)

Bali's Tanah Barak Cliff (also known as Pantai Batu Barak) is a hidden gem that has recently become a popular attraction. This beach is a newly opened spot, distinguished by its stunning blue waters and colorful rock formations. Beyond the magnificent sea views, the road here is carved through massive limestone cliffs. The distant azure ocean and towering rock walls on both sides create a striking visual impact, and you can climb the central rock for better photo angles. Tanah Barak is about a 30-minute drive from the airport, and entry requires a ticket (current prices are IDR 8,000 for adults and IDR 3,000 for children). It is open daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

## Number 1: Surfing and Diving

![第一名：衝浪與潛水](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f237c3322eff064a02804f4e6cffcc22.jpg)

Thanks to the world-class surf breaks along Uluwatu's coastline, this area is a surfer's paradise and a major draw for visitors. There are plenty of surf camps and lessons nearby, with spots like Uluwatu Beach, Padang Padang Beach, Bingin Beach, Green Bowl Beach, and Suluban Beach—the list goes on. Take Uluwatu Beach, for example: it's often hailed as Uluwatu's biggest attraction. Beyond its famous cliffside temple, it's a hotspot for surfing and other water activities. You can comfortably watch the sunset from the sand or enjoy a meal at a nearby restaurant—this beach's charm is undeniable. Whether you're chasing the thrill of surfing or just want to soak it all in, this is a must-visit destination.
