**Bottom line**: Wanna Jungle Pool & Bar is a three-tiered infinity pool club under Kayon Jungle Resort in Ubud. **Non-guests are welcome**—just grab a day pass on Klook, which includes a food credit redeemable for poolside meals. To snap photos of the pool without the crowds, **arrive in the morning**. For lunch, go for the signature Balinese crispy duck, Bebek Kayon (perfect for sharing between two).

When visiting Ubud, Bali’s cultural heart, don’t miss this Instagram-famous day pool club—Wanna Jungle Pool & Bar! With its stunning three-tier infinity pool, it’s become a must-visit spot for influencers.

## **Rice Terrace-Inspired Pool Blends Perfectly with Nature**

![梯田概念泳池 與自然完美融合](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f6fc53ee9b26048fd4f3d333e111e58f.jpg)

Stepping into Wanna Jungle, you’ll be instantly amazed by the unique pool design! The architects have cleverly incorporated Bali’s iconic rice terraces into the pool layout, with cascading levels that complement the surrounding tropical jungle. Every angle offers a magazine-worthy shot. Best of all, even if you’re not a resort guest, you can buy a day pass to enjoy this paradise.

## **Entry Ticket Includes Food Credit for Five-Star Dining**

The entry ticket thoughtfully includes a food credit, which can be used directly for meals and drinks. We chose to have lunch here, and highly recommend their signature dish, 'Bebek Kayon'—Balinese crispy duck! The generous portion features crispy skin, served with three distinctive dipping sauces: a red chili onion sauce, Indonesian fried chili (Cabe Goreng), and a green sauce made from local chilies (not Mexican guacamole!). The mildly spicy, appetizing flavors keep you coming back for more.

## **Travel Tips**

- Visit in the morning when it's less crowded for cleaner pool shots
- The crispy duck is generous—enough for two to share
- Bring a waterproof phone pouch so you can snap poolside photos without worry
- In the evening, the pool lights up, creating a whole new romantic atmosphere

Next time you're in Ubud, don't forget to add this hidden jungle terrace pool to your itinerary—it'll make your Bali trip even more memorable!
