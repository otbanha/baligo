For crowd-free pool photos, **arrive in the morning**. For lunch, the signature Bali crispy duck, Bebek Kayon, is a must (perfect for sharing between two).

In Ubud, the cultural heart of Bali, you absolutely can't miss the latest Instagram-famous day pool club—Wanna Jungle Pool & Bar! This stunning three-tiered infinity pool has become a must-visit spot for influencers.

## **A Terrace-Inspired Pool Blending Perfectly with Nature**

![梯田概念泳池 與自然完美融合](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f6fc53ee9b26048fd4f3d333e111e58f.jpg)

Step into Wanna Jungle and you'll be instantly amazed by its unique pool design! The designers have cleverly incorporated Bali's iconic rice terrace landscape into the pool's shape, with cascading levels that complement the surrounding tropical jungle. Every angle offers a magazine-cover-worthy shot. Best of all, even if you're not a resort guest, you can buy a day pass to enjoy this paradise.

## **Entry Includes Food Credit for Five-Star Dining**

The entry ticket thoughtfully includes food credit that can be used directly for dining. We chose to have lunch here, and highly recommend their signature dish, 'Bebek Kayon'—Bali's crispy fried duck! The generous portion of duck has a perfectly crispy skin, served with three distinctive dipping sauces: a red chili and onion sauce, Indonesian fried chili (Cabe Goreng), and a green sauce made from local chilies (not Mexican guacamole!). The mildly spicy, appetizing flavors keep you reaching for more.

Whether you're looking to snap stunning photos, savor delicious food, or simply soak up the vacation vibe, Wanna Jungle Pool & Bar can fulfill all your dreams of a Bali getaway!

## **Travel Tips**

- Visit in the morning when there are fewer people for cleaner pool shots
- The crispy duck is quite generous—even two people can share it easily
- Bring a waterproof phone case so you can freely capture poolside photos
- In the evening, the pool lights up, creating a whole new romantic atmosphere

Next time you're in Ubud, don't forget to add this hidden jungle terrace pool to your itinerary for a more memorable Bali trip!

## More to Explore

### Wanna Gangga Pool & Bar (📍Map: [Immerse in Scenic Beauty](/map/ubud/))

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeLlvuToBPO/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&cplk=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==

Nestled in Ababi, Karangasem, in eastern Bali, **Wanna Gangga Pool & Bar** is a stunning new landmark from the renowned luxury resort group The Kayon, located right next to Tirta Gangga (the Holy Water Temple Garden).

It perfectly continues the group's signature design aesthetic, boasting majestic mountain and lush green views that are seamlessly integrated into open, airy leisure spaces. Whether you're soaking up the sun by the pool or savoring fine drinks and international cuisine, you'll be enveloped in a tranquil, far-from-the-crowd atmosphere—making it an ideal spot to unwind while exploring eastern Bali.

### Wanna Valley Pool & Bar (📍Map: [Immerse in Scenic Beauty](/map/ubud/))

https://www.instagram.com/p/Dd0pIetsN2S/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link

Also under the renowned The Kayon group, this brand-new venue opened in 2026. Compared to the group's other more remote locations, it's **much closer to central Ubud**, making it easier for travelers to enjoy a secluded jungle retreat while still having convenient access to explore the town and get around.

Surrounded by Ubud's iconic lush river valleys and tropical rainforests, this place perfectly continues the group's high-quality design. In addition to offering exquisite poolside relaxation spaces and premium dining and wine service, it allows guests to easily immerse themselves in the healing natural beauty—making it an excellent destination that combines resort privacy with convenience.
