**Key Highlights**: The six-day route is **Ubud (first two days) → Nusa Penida island-hopping (days three to five) → Jimbaran (day six) to bid farewell to Bali**. Real-life tip: The ferry departing from Sanur port **once delayed by up to 2 hours**, so allow buffer time for island-hopping; on the east coast of Nusa Penida, don't miss Diamond Beach and Atuh Beach. Detailed daily itinerary and timing are below.

This is a fantastic six-day independent itinerary shared by our community member "[Sophie](https://www.facebook.com/soficia.chen)" during her trip to Bali! It combines references from "[Xiao Jie Indonesia](https://www.youtube.com/@j_indonesia)" videos and our "Bali Travel Guide" tips. Let's take a look at this adventure-filled journey she planned herself!
