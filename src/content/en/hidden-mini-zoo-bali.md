If you're planning a family trip to Bali or simply love animals, Hidden Mini Zoo Bali has likely already caught your eye. Located in Sukawati, this mini zoo has racked up nearly 4,000 reviews on Google with a stellar 4.8-star rating—what makes visitors so smitten?

This article covers ticket prices, opening hours, real reviews, and a comparison with Bali Safari & Marine Park, so you'll have all the info before you set off.

## Basic Information

**Official Name**: Hidden Mini Zoo Bali
**Also Known As**: Hidden Canyon Mini Zoo (same place, just different naming conventions)
**Address**: Banjar Sakih, Jl. Pandu, Guwang, Sukawati, Gianyar, Bali
**Opening Hours**: Daily 09:00–17:00 (last entry at 16:30)
**Google Rating**: 4.8 / 5 (approx. 3,959 reviews)
**Recommended Visit Duration**: 1–2 hours

## Ticket Prices (IDR)

According to the latest info, Hidden Mini Zoo Bali tickets are IDR 250,000 for adults and IDR 200,000 for children.

**Important Notes**:

- Last ticket redemption for general admission is at 16:00, and for the full package at 15:30
- Each adult ticket can be redeemed for one free drink at the restaurant near the exit.
- Infants under 2 years old are usually admitted for free.

## What Do Google Reviews Say?

### Positive Feedback: Interactive Experiences Steal the Show

Nearly all the praise centers on the chance to touch, feed, and take photos. Visitors mention getting up close with pythons, spiders, Komodo dragons, bats, owls, and hedgehogs, with staff on hand to ensure safe interactions.

One review reads: 'The interactive experience was fantastic—you can touch and feed the animals, and the kids loved it. The staff were also very welcoming; it's worth a return visit.'

Another visitor specifically commended the animal welfare: 'You really get to see so many animals here... Overall, the animals looked healthy, with clean water. For a zoo, the welfare standards here are pretty good.'

Another guest noted: 'The staff are super friendly and speak Chinese. The animals are exceptionally well cared for—plump with shiny coats. Definitely worth a visit, Bagus!'

### Things to Keep in Mind

Not all reviews are five-star. Some visitors feel the ticket price is a bit high for the size of the park, and the tour can be completed in about **1 hour**. Others mention that if you don't add the "special interactive experience" when purchasing tickets in advance, the price to add it on-site will be higher.

**Which animals can you touch?**
According to reviews and official information, animals you can interact with in the contact area include snakes, lizards, owls, hedgehogs, bats, skunks, spiders, and even monkeys. Staff arrange interactions based on the animals' condition, and there are signs at the handwashing stations saying "Don't be rough with the animals." If any inappropriate behavior occurs, the interaction is stopped immediately.

**Free drinks at the exit**
With an adult ticket, you can redeem one free drink at the restaurant near the exit; meals are extra. Some visitors have ordered pancakes and gave positive reviews.

**Is it suitable for kids?**
Absolutely. Many reviewers mention this is a "family-friendly" attraction where children can observe and even touch animals up close, and the staff provide simple explanations.

## Hidden Mini Zoo vs Bali Safari & Marine Park

Many people hesitate: should they go to Hidden Mini Zoo or Bali Safari? The two are very different in nature. Here's a quick comparison table:

| Item | Hidden Mini Zoo | Bali Safari & Marine Park |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Size** | Small, can be explored in 1–2 hours | Large, 40 hectares, recommended 4–6 hours |
| **Core Experience** | Holding animals up close, taking photos | Safari rides to see predators, animal shows, elephant performances |
| **Ticket Price (Adult)** | IDR 250,000 | From IDR 750,000 (Jungle Hopper package) |
| **Best For** | Young adults seeking high interaction, families with toddlers | Families willing to spend a full day, better for school-age children |
| **Animal Welfare Reviews** | Generally positive, animals appear well-cared for | Mixed reviews, some visitors feel the enclosures are too small |

**In short**: Hidden Mini Zoo sells **intimacy**, while Bali Safari sells **scale and shows**.

## Practical Tips

1. **It's recommended to buy tickets online in advance** — prices are usually better than at the gate, and you can choose whether to add the full experience package.
2. **Set aside 2 hours to half a day** for interacting with the animals.
3. **Bring insect repellent** — since the park is next to rice paddies, there may be more mosquitoes.
4. **Pack wet wipes or hand sanitizer** — staff will guide you to wash your hands before and after interactions, but it's more convenient to have your own.

## Conclusion

Hidden Mini Zoo Bali isn't a large zoo, but its strength lies in **close-up interactions** and the **staff's enthusiasm**. Its 4.8-star rating mainly comes from visitors' satisfaction with being able to touch the animals and their appreciation for how well the animals are cared for. If you love animals, are traveling with kids, or want a unique experience in Bali, it's worth fitting into a half-day itinerary.

Remember to buy tickets online in advance, go early to avoid the crowds, and enjoy the surreal feeling of having a giant python coil around your body.
