**Key Highlights**: This seven-day, six-night travelogue connects three distinct style areas of Bali—**Seminyak** (staying at Indigo Seminyak, walking to Double Six Beach for sunset, boutique shopping), **Ubud** (cultural experiences), and **Canggu** (trendy, modern vibe). Each area has a clear personality, perfect for travelers who want to experience Bali's diverse facets without sticking to just one spot.

Since the pandemic, I've traveled to quite a few countries, but this seven-day, six-night trip to Bali has become the one I most want to revisit. Whether in Seminyak, Ubud, or Canggu, each area has its own unique charm, offering travelers different experiences and memories.

## The Enchanting Vibe of Seminyak

First, I arrived in lively Seminyak, where the bustling, modern energy is undeniably one of Bali's hallmarks. I chose to stay at the **Indigo Seminyak** hotel, which boasts an excellent location—within walking distance of the famous Double Six Beach. Every evening at dusk, I'd order a drink, face the ocean, and wait for the sunset. In that moment, a deeply relaxing calm washed over me. This kind of laid-back living makes you completely forget the daily grind—it's like a slice of paradise.

## Cultural Experiences in Ubud

Next, I headed to Ubud, the cultural heart of Bali. This area is steeped in artistic vibes and pastoral landscapes, offering a stark contrast to the bustling energy of Seminyak. I first visited the traditional market near the Ubud Palace, where stalls overflow with everything from handmade crafts to local specialties, each corner brimming with the thrill of discovery. I recommend setting aside plenty of time to 'treasure hunt,' as every stall might hold the unique souvenir you've been dreaming of.

https://youtu.be/s16gdTOjonU

Wandering through Ubud's exotic alleyways, I stumbled upon two unforgettable restaurants—**Kebun Bistro** and **Kusi**. Both offer a distinct taste of Ubud through their dishes and ambiance. ## Trendy and Stylish Canggu

The final stop of our trip was Canggu, the trendiest new area in Bali. The streets are lined with a variety of concept stores and cafes, each with its own unique design style that makes you want to explore every one. Whether you're shopping or indulging in food, Canggu's diversity ensures every traveler finds something they love.

The **Komu Hotel** we stayed at had slightly thin walls, letting us "enjoy" the nightclub noise from our room, but the hotel's modern design was fresh and the overall experience was quite good—especially for travelers who love contemporary style, offering great value for money.
