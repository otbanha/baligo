## North Kuta Villa Safe Theft Under Police Investigation 🔐

A 32-year-old Russian tourist reported that he placed USD$1,500 in the villa safe on November 6. When checking out on November 9, he found only USD$700 missing, a loss of USD$800. The safe showed no signs of forced entry, and there were no indications of a break-in through doors or windows. Passports and watches inside the safe were untouched. After notifying the front desk, villa staff reviewed surveillance footage and questioned housekeeping, but could not determine the cause. The case was reported via 110. As of November 10, police had not announced any arrests or confirmed whether the money was stolen or disappeared for other reasons. During your stay, keep valuables in your carry-on bag or hotel safe, and check amounts regularly.

## Pelaga Glamping Retreat Landslide Search Concluded ⛰️

On November 1, a landslide occurred at Eco Park Pelaga Glamping Retreat, leaving two guests missing. On November 6, the Badung Regency government announced it was reviewing the resort's permits. On November 10, the search operation ended with the two still unfound. When choosing camping or tent accommodations in mountainous or forested areas, check the site's drainage and safety conditions first, and stay alert to terrain changes during rainy weather.

## Five Foreign Nationals Deported, Including Over Seven Years of Overstay 🛂

Immigration authorities reported that five foreign nationals (from Nigeria, China, and Iran) were deported between November 2 and 4 for reasons including overstaying, suspected theft, public disturbance, and misuse of investor residence permits. One 37-year-old Nigerian man had overstayed by 2,885 days, nearly eight years. A Chinese man was deported on November 4 after his investor residence permit was revoked. Tourists in Bali must leave by their visa expiration date and comply with local regulations.

## Two Chinese Men Arrested for In-Flight Theft ✈️

According to reports, two Chinese men allegedly stole USD$1,500 in cash from an Australian passenger's backpack while on Batik Air flight 6508 from Jakarta to Bali on November 1. The Australian passenger filed a report on November 2, and airport police, working with aviation security, arrested the two. Police noted that there have been three or more similar thefts in aircraft cabins from October to early November. When flying, keep your carry-on bag within sight at all times, store valuables on your person, and avoid placing them in overhead bins—use bins within your line of sight instead.
