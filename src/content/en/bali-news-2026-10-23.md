## ⛽ Bali Fuel Supply Update: Odd-Even Plate Policy Expires Today—Cancellation Still Unconfirmed

Bali's recent fuel distribution issues are gradually improving. The government has added seven fuel tankers and increased reserve targets from roughly 11–12 days to 15 days, aiming to boost gas station restocking efficiency and prevent widespread queues.

The provincial government previously extended the odd-even plate policy for subsidized Pertalite fuel until **Friday, October 23**. However, as of October 22, no sufficiently reliable official announcement has been found to confirm whether this measure has been formally lifted or extended again.

Bali's tourism authorities previously stated that daily foreign visitor arrivals in mid-October remained around 22,000 to 24,000, with no significant decline. However, tourism operators have reported that some drivers have been delayed for pickups due to queuing for fuel, and some bookings have had to be adjusted or canceled.

### Impact on Travelers

Travelers renting motorbikes, self-driving, or arranging private drivers in Bali should still confirm vehicle fuel levels and the day's refueling rules in advance. If planning trips to Ubud, Kintamani, North Bali, or East Bali, allow extra travel time.

**Please note: October 23 is the previously announced expiration date, but this does not automatically mean all refueling restrictions will be lifted that day.** Check with your rental provider or driver for the latest updates before heading out.

## 🌊 East Bali's Diving Town Hosts Ocean Culture Festival—Tulamben Events Run Through October 24

Located in northeastern Bali, Tulamben will host the **Tulamben Ocean Harmony Festival 2026** from October 22 to 24, blending the local marine environment, diving culture, and community activities.

Tulamben is renowned for its shore diving, with rich marine ecosystems in nearby waters, making it a popular spot for diving enthusiasts to explore Bali's underwater world.

The event lineup includes coastal cultural ceremonies, art performances, an underwater photography competition, beach and coral reef clean-up activities, as well as local food and handicraft stalls, aiming to raise awareness about marine conservation and local culture.

### Is it worth adding to your itinerary?

If you're already planning to dive, snorkel, or explore the coastal areas of East Bali, you can consider incorporating the event into your trip. However, Tulamben is a drive away from Bali's main southern tourist areas, so it's not recommended to go just for a short event without factoring in travel time.

If you go in the water during the event, be sure to follow the safety regulations of diving operators; when participating in beach activities, please also adhere to the on-site marine conservation arrangements.

## ✈️ New Convenience Store Opens in Bali Airport's Domestic Arrivals Area, Offering Another Option for Transit and Pick-Up Passengers

Bali's Ngurah Rai International Airport announced on October 21 that M Mart convenience store has opened in the **domestic arrivals area**.

For travelers arriving in Bali on domestic Indonesian flights, this provides another option to buy drinks, snacks, and travel essentials, especially convenient for those just off the plane and heading to their hotel or waiting for a ride.

It's important to note that the new store is located in the domestic arrivals area, which doesn't necessarily mean international passengers can easily access it after clearing immigration. If you're arriving on an international flight from Taiwan, Hong Kong, or Singapore, you should check which stores are accessible based on your terminal and route.

This facility upgrade will not change the airport's entry or transit regulations, but it is practical news for travelers who need a quick restock at the airport.

## 📚 Ubud Writers & Readers Festival Runs Until October 25, with Literary Events Across Multiple Venues in Ubud

The 2026 Ubud Writers & Readers Festival kicked off on October 21 and will continue until **Sunday, October 25**. The event brings together Indonesian and international writers, journalists, poets, and thinkers, offering talks, workshops, book launches, readings, and cultural performances that allow travelers to explore stories from Indonesia and around the world from diverse perspectives.

This year's theme is "Samarasā: Awareness, Empathy, Action," with main events and related programs spread across multiple venues in Ubud.

If you're staying in Ubud during these days, keep an eye out for talks, author meet-ups, or evening events that catch your interest. Some sessions require tickets or advance registration, so not all events are open for walk-ins.

For travelers unfamiliar with literary events, this can also be a unique way to experience Ubud's cultural life beyond the usual café, rice terrace, or temple tours.

## 🌕 Full Moon Events on October 23: Different Experiences at Uluwatu and Ubud

October 23 falls during the full moon period, with various wellness, cultural, and leisure activities arranged across multiple areas in Bali.

On that day, Ubud offers yoga, workshops, and other mind-body wellness experiences, while southern Uluwatu features full moon-themed activities. Content, costs, and entry methods vary by venue, so travelers should check the latest information from event organizers.

It's worth noting that Balinese Hindu religious ceremonies follow their own calendar and local traditions, so not all full moon events should be viewed as the same type of religious celebration. General commercial activities are also distinct from formal temple rituals.

If you're looking to arrange an evening in Bali that's different from the beach or nightclub scene, you can choose activities based on your accommodation area. When attending temples or traditional ceremonies, please respect local dress codes, photography rules, and participation guidelines.
