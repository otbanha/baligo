## The History of Sanur

Sanur is a historic coastal town on Bali, with roots stretching back centuries. In Bali's history, Sanur was once an important port and fishing village, attracting ships from across the Indonesian archipelago and beyond for trade. Over time, it became a hub for commerce and cultural exchange on the island.

After Indonesia gained independence in the mid-20th century and began nationwide development, Bali's tourism industry took off under President Sukarno—and Sanur was where it all started! The island's very first five-star hotel was built right here in Sanur.

Today, thanks to its natural reef protection, Sanur boasts calm, gently sloping golden beaches, making it a perfect family-friendly vacation spot. It's also a popular retirement destination for expats from around the world who prefer a quiet, laid-back town over the nightlife and crowds.

### Water Activities

In Sanur, the top activity is water sports! Canoeing and stand-up paddleboarding (SUP) are two of the most popular water activities here. You can glide across the calm sea, soaking in the serene ocean atmosphere, or challenge your balance and strength. This beautiful stretch of water offers both fun and a challenge, ensuring a delightful time.

Video of Xiao Jie Canoeing

https://youtu.be/Xp5gyMslrVE?si=mPledHphy4xTtRUO

You can rent kayaks and stand-up paddleboards from vendors on the beach, mostly concentrated around Pantai Karang. At this spot marked on the map, there are also many local vendors selling swimsuits, floaties, water guns, water toys, and sand-digging tools (📌[Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=12W9y9WtufmH2a430BAds6QtTa5TyHoE&usp=sharing)). In 2023, both kayaks and stand-up paddleboards cost 25K IDR per hour, which is about USD$1.

Bopel Beach is another family-friendly area in Sanur (📌[Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=12W9y9WtufmH2a430BAds6QtTa5TyHoE&usp=sharing)), featuring a playground on the sand and rentals for umbrellas and lounge chairs. Public restrooms and changing rooms are also available.

### Beachside Promenade

![海灘步道](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_d124800f990b3fd545451b4c77015a4f.jpg)

You can't talk about Sanur without mentioning its four-kilometer-plus coastal promenade! This long walkway is one of Sanur's most distinctive features. ### Museum Le Mayeur (📌[Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=12W9y9WtufmH2a430BAds6QtTa5TyHoE&usp=sharing))

![Museum Le Mayeur （地圖）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a7eadec18bd85e377d455169550dbd25.jpg)

Museum Le Mayeur is housed in the former home of Belgian painter Adrien-Jean Le Mayeur and his Balinese wife, Ni Pollok, whose story fills the place with romance and history. ## Sanur Shuttle Bus

![Sanur 的接駁專車](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f9725710c3d0c3d75eeb1eacb77c99a6.jpg)

Starting August 2025, Sanur will offer a shuttle bus that operates exclusively within the Sanur area, providing visitors with a "hop-on, hop-off" mode of free movement.

This shuttle bus features an open-air design, allowing passengers to enjoy natural ventilation while taking in the scenery along the route. Payment is made via electronic payment.

Current bus routes include:

- Mertasari Beach
- Pangembak Street
- Mertasari Street
- Danau Poso Road
- Danau Tamblingan Road
- Danau Toba Road
- The final stop is the SMP Wisata area near the Sanur Special Economic Zone

For detailed stops and explanations, please see [here](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/sanur-tourist-shuttle-launch/)
