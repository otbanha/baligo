## The History of Sanur

Sanur is a historic seaside town on Bali, with a history dating back centuries. In Bali's past, Sanur was an important port and fishing village, attracting ships from across the Indonesian archipelago and beyond for trade. Over time, it became a hub for commerce and cultural exchange on the island.

After Indonesia gained independence in the mid-20th century and began national development, Bali started to grow under President Sukarno, and Sanur was the birthplace of Bali's tourism industry! The island's first five-star hotel was built right here in Sanur.

Today, thanks to its protected reef, calm waters, and gently sloping golden beaches, Sanur has evolved into a family-friendly vacation haven. It's also a popular retirement spot for expats from around the world seeking a quiet, laid-back town without the nightlife and crowds.

### Water Activities

In Sanur, the top activity is water sports! Canoeing and stand-up paddleboarding (SUP) are two of the most popular water activities here. You can glide across the calm sea, soaking in the serene ocean atmosphere, or challenge your balance and strength. This beautiful stretch of sea offers both fun and challenge, ensuring a delightful time.

You can rent kayaks and stand-up paddleboards from vendors on the beach, mostly concentrated around Pantai Karang. At this spot marked on the map, there are also many local vendors selling swimsuits, floaties, water guns, water toys, and sand-digging tools (📌[Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=12W9y9WtufmH2a430BAds6QtTa5TyHoE&usp=sharing)). In 2023, both kayaks and stand-up paddleboards cost 25K IDR per hour, which is about USD$1.

### Beachside Promenade

![海灘步道](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_d124800f990b3fd545451b4c77015a4f.jpg)

You can't talk about Sanur without mentioning its over four-kilometer-long beachfront promenade! This long coastal walkway is one of Sanur's most distinctive features. Stroll along the path lined with shops, go for a jog, or rent a bike and ride leisurely with your kids on the flat trail. The beach on one side of the promenade, with its length and variety, guarantees you'll find a spot all to yourselves—with big trees, lovely shade, and no smokers nearby! Just lay down a mat and relax in your own private slice of paradise.

### Museum Le Mayeur (📌[Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=12W9y9WtufmH2a430BAds6QtTa5TyHoE&usp=sharing))

![Museum Le Mayeur （地圖）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a7eadec18bd85e377d455169550dbd25.jpg)

Museum Le Mayeur is housed in the former home of Belgian painter Adrien-Jean Le Mayeur and his Balinese wife, Ni Pollok, a setting steeped in romance and history.
