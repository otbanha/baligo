# Bali's New Gem: The Huntley Canggu — Classic California Charm by the Tropical Coast

If you're tired of Bali's ubiquitous 'bohemian style' and 'raw concrete industrial look,' The Huntley Canggu offers a refreshing surprise. This newly opened luxury villa resort, which debuted last year, transplants the elegant aesthetics of 1950s California resort towns right into Canggu's tropical coastline, creating a 'Palm Springs under the palm trees.' With 13 individually designed villas, private pools, mid-century marble decor, and a prime location near two major beach clubs, it's worth considering if you want to stay in Bali with style without sacrificing a vacation vibe.

## 📍 Location & Surroundings

The Huntley Canggu sits in the Berawa neighborhood of Canggu, near the main Jalan Pantai Berawa road, tucked away on a quiet residential street. This location is arguably 'Canggu's sweet spot': offering a sense of seclusion without being too far from key attractions.

From the villa, Finns Beach Club is just a 10-minute walk away; Atlas Beach Club is equally close. If you want to experience Canggu's beach club culture (sipping a cocktail while watching the sunset), you won't have to worry about Gojek traffic jams from here. Berawa Beach and Canggu Beach are within walking distance, a must for surf enthusiasts.

Dining options nearby are plentiful. A few streets away, you'll find El Mono (serving Mexican specialties) and various cafes and restaurants around Canggu Square. If you prefer to stay in, Parque Café is on-site, offering all-day dining from breakfast to dinner.

One thing to note: As a rapidly developing tourist area, traffic in Canggu can be a bottleneck, especially during peak holiday hours. The drive from the airport to the resort takes about 50 minutes, but during rush hours or holidays, this time can double. However, once you arrive at The Huntley, the quiet residential street instantly shields you from the main road's hustle and bustle.

## 🏨 Facilities Overview

As soon as you step into the resort, you'll immediately feel a distinct classic Californian style. The design team set aside the common 'Balinese tradition' and instead took a mid-century modern approach—warm beige tones, clean lines, marble and terrace finishes, paired with bougainvillea vines scattered across the property. The result is a visual experience that looks like something from Instagram but feels even richer in person.

**Key public facilities** include:

- **24-hour front desk and concierge service**: Thoughtful check-in and check-out experience
- **Parque Café restaurant and bar**: Dining options from breakfast to dinner, offering coffee, light bites, and full meals
- **Fitness center**: Equipped with modern exercise machines
- **Outdoor pool**: A shared pool area, perfect for socializing and relaxation
- **Sauna and ice bath facilities**: Traditional hot-cold therapy wellness practices, a standout feature of the resort
- **Bicycle rental**: A great tool for exploring Canggu on two wheels
- **Laundry service and luggage storage**: Convenient daily amenities

The overall atmosphere of the resort is 'refined but not overdone.' The lobby isn't lavish or flashy—instead, it's clean and impactful. During the day, sunlight filters through the arched architectural lines, casting a play of light and shadow; at night, soft lighting illuminates the garden pathways, making an evening stroll a true pleasure.

## 🛏️ Room Overview

The resort features 13 standalone villas, each designed with a mid-century aesthetic, offering options from 1 to 4 bedrooms.

**Standard Amenities** (all room types):

- Private swimming pool (the resort's signature highlight)
- Tempur-Pedic premium mattress with top-tier bedding
- Fully equipped kitchen or kitchenette, ideal for self-catering
- Free WiFi, Bluetooth speaker, and 32-inch smart TV
- Air conditioning, minibar, and in-room safe
- Luxurious bathroom: standalone bathtub, rain shower, bathrobes, and more
- Terrace with garden furniture, perfect for enjoying morning coffee

**Room Selection**

- **1-Bedroom Villa**: Ideal for couples or solo travelers. Features a king-size master bedroom and a private pool with two loungers.
- **2-Bedroom Villa**: Available in Standard and Deluxe versions. The Standard offers a king bed plus a daybed lounge area; the Deluxe features a two-story design with more spacious layouts, crafted for vacation living. Perfect for friends traveling together or small families.
- **3-Bedroom Villa**: Accommodates up to 6 guests with three king beds (convertible to twin configurations), a private pool, and an entertainment area. Suitable for larger families or groups of friends.
- **4-Bedroom Villa (Premium)**: The resort's flagship room type, designed for large gatherings or luxury getaways. It comes with a special **rooftop gym**, private sauna, ice bath, jacuzzi, and a massive 5-meter-wide daybed. With two separate living areas, even big families can enjoy privacy.

All room types come with a private pool. You won't have to share the pool area with strangers, allowing you to enjoy your water time entirely at your own pace. The spaces are designed with privacy and views in mind—each villa has its own entrance and garden, set apart from others, so there's no sense of crowding.
