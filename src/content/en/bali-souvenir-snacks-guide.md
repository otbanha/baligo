**Bottom Line**: Your must-buy Bali snack souvenir list—**Balinese peanuts, Balinese coffee, Indonesian shrimp crackers, Indonesian fried rice seasoning**, plus the hidden gem recommended by locals, **Pie Susu** (cheese milk tart)—all unique treats hard to find in Taiwan.

Bali is more than just a captivating travel destination; it's a paradise for food enthusiasts. As you set foot on this tropical haven, beyond admiring stunning beach scenery, joining local cultural activities, exploring ancient temples, and enjoying romantic sunsets, there's one essential thing you shouldn't miss: savoring Bali's delicious cuisine. And when you're ready to leave this beautiful land, don't forget to bring home some food souvenirs to let your taste buds continue experiencing Bali's magic!

In the video below, we introduce some distinctive Balinese souvenirs: Balinese peanuts, Balinese coffee, Indonesian shrimp crackers, and Indonesian fried rice seasoning. Finally, we have a secret weapon to share with you: one of Bali's must-buy souvenirs—Pie Susu.

Whether you're a food adventurer or want to share Bali's flavors with family, these culinary treasures are worth savoring one by one. During your Bali trip, be sure to set aside time to experience the local food culture and make your journey even more vibrant! For more insights into these treats, check out our video and let your taste buds feel Bali's unique charm.
