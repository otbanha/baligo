# Beji Guwang Hidden Canyon Trekking Review: An Adrenaline-Pumping Hidden Canyon Adventure in Bali

If you've already explored Bali's beaches, waterfalls, and spas and are craving a truly active experience, consider **Beji Guwang Hidden Canyon**.

What sets this place apart is the massive rock walls and narrow canyon carved by years of river flow. The real adventure isn't just snapping photos from the outside—it's **scrambling over rocks, wading through water, climbing, and even swimming through the canyon**.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1790504777980-68e02426ce5f4092e5c2dd7dc9c99e4c.jpeg)

## Not a Walk, But a Play

The Beji Guwang Canyon Trekking on Klook typically lasts about **4 hours** as a private tour, including hotel pickup and a local guide. The activity takes you into the river, with some sections requiring swimming—water levels can even reach your neck in deeper spots.

So don't be fooled by the stunning photos; this isn't a leisurely hike.

**This is a canyon adventure that will leave you completely soaked.**

Recent Tripadvisor travelers have noted that you'll need to swim against the current, climb over rocks, and avoid submerged rocks; another visitor mentioned encountering stronger currents at the end of the rainy season, so a decent level of fitness is recommended. ## Does Rain Affect the Trip?

Yes, it does.

Since the activity takes place in the riverbed, heavy rain the night before can change the water level and flow.

So when planning a trip to Beji Guwang Hidden Canyon, check not only the weather on the day but also the rainfall from the previous few days.

**After heavy rain, when the water volume is higher, the difficulty can increase significantly.**

## What to Wear?

Simple:

**Quick-dry clothing + water shoes.**

Don't wear slippers, and don't bring anything that can't get wet.

Valuables like phones and cameras should be waterproofed—on-site options also provide waterproof bags.
