## 1. November 8–11: High Tide Alert for Southern Coast, Elevated Water Levels Around New Moon 🌊

The Bali Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) Denpasar office warns that from November 8 to 11, some coastal areas of Bali may experience high tides and potential seawater intrusion. The six affected areas include southern Jembrana, southern Tabanan, Badung coast, Denpasar coast, Gianyar coast, and southern Klungkung. The new moon on November 9 may cause even higher tides. Officials have not closed any beaches or banned swimming. Beachgoers are advised to stay alert, as low-lying areas may temporarily flood.

## 2. Snatch Thefts in Kuta and Sanur: Police Warn Tourists Not to Wear Flashy Jewelry 🚨

Police in Kuta arrested two teenagers, aged 15 and 17, suspected of repeatedly snatching necklaces from foreign tourists; they have confessed to the crimes. In another incident, a Polish tourist had a gold necklace worth 80 million Indonesian rupiah (Rp80 million) stolen. On November 7, an Italian woman was injured while cycling in Sanur when a motorcyclist snatched her bag—the incident was caught on CCTV. Police urge tourists to avoid wearing flashy jewelry. When out, carry your bag on the side away from the road, and leave valuables in your hotel.

## 3. Good News for Accommodation: Villa Occupancy Up in October, Prices Softening 🏡

According to the latest data from real estate platform Brixfox, Bali's vacation villa occupancy rose in October, while average nightly rates dropped, and some owners have also lowered their selling prices. For travelers planning to book, this is a good time to compare prices—shop around before committing.

## 4. Seraya Cultural Festival Opens in East Bali: Rain-Prayer Ritual Gebug Ende Draws Crowds 🎭

The fifth Seraya Cultural Festival kicked off on Thursday, November 5, in eastern Bali, drawing hundreds of locals and foreign tourists. The opening ceremony featured the traditional Gebug Ende ritual, a rattan stick fight originally performed to pray for rain. For those seeking Balinese culture beyond the south, the east is a great choice—check the schedule for upcoming events before heading out.

## 5. Rainy Season Forecast: Arriving Later Than Usual, Starting Late November to December 🌦️

BMKG predicts this year's rainy season will begin in late November or December, with the peak expected in February 2027. Among the island's 20 climate zones, only 3 will see rainfall by late November, while the remaining 17 won't experience consistent rain until December. About 70% of the climate zones are at least 30 days later than usual, with some areas delayed by 60 to 70 days. Meteorologists warn that the delayed rainy season could lead to more concentrated and intense downpours.
