# Bali Souvenir Recommendation: Silver Queen Cashew & Almond Chocolate – Affordable Elegance

Deciding what souvenirs to bring back to Taiwan is always a head-scratcher on every trip abroad. Spend too much and it hurts; go too cheap and it feels insincere.

If you're heading to Bali, the answer is right on the supermarket shelves: **Silver Queen Chocolate**. The star is the **cashew** variety, with a strong recommendation for the **almond** version too. Grab both for gifting options, and they're just as satisfying for personal snacking.

## Looks Like an Import, But It's Indonesia's Own Chocolate

The name Silver Queen sounds very Western, leading many to mistake it for a foreign brand. But it's a homegrown Indonesian label from Garut, West Java, and has been a beloved national snack since the 1950s.

Its backstory is also intriguing. The chocolate factory was originally started by the Dutch, later changed hands, and eventually was taken over by a young Chinese entrepreneur from Myanmar. According to reports, in 1955 it landed a major order from the presidential palace, becoming a treat for state guests.

Its expansion beyond Indonesia hasn't slowed. Silver Queen is now exported to Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, and the Philippines. Domestically, a 2023 survey ranked it as the most recognized chocolate brand in Indonesia.

## Tastes as Good as Imported Brands, Because the Cocoa Is Grown Locally

Indonesia is one of the world's top cocoa-producing countries, ranking sixth in cocoa bean production and third in processing volume, trailing only the Netherlands and Ivory Coast. This means that many famous chocolates around the world may have their raw materials and even production right here in Indonesia! Silver Queen is a chocolate brand that grew up on this land, with ingredients sourced close to the source, giving it a naturally rich and robust cocoa aroma.

This quality has also earned recognition. In Indonesia's 2021 youth-voted chocolate brand rankings, Silver Queen stood alongside imported names like Cadbury and Toblerone, even ranking ahead of them.

## Top Pick: Cashew Variety, a Transparent Ingredient List

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1795409257633-15833.jpg)

**Milk chocolate with cashews** is Silver Queen's most classic and beloved signature flavor.

There's a little story behind the cashews. In tropical heat, chocolate tends to melt and lose its shape. Legend has it that the founder, aiming to make the chocolate more durable, came up with the idea of adding cashews. This unexpected twist later became the brand's hallmark.

What's even more reassuring is the ingredient list. **Cashews rank third, ahead of cocoa mass and cocoa butter**, meaning the proportion of cashews exceeds that of cocoa mass. Plus, it contains both cocoa mass and cocoa butter, not just relying on artificial flavorings.

Here's how it tastes:

- The milk chocolate is smooth and sweet, melting instantly on the tongue
- The cashew pieces are crunchy, with a distinct nutty aroma
- The sweetness is just right, not cloying, so you can easily enjoy several pieces at once

## Bonus: Almond Version – A Top Pick for Crunchy Texture Lovers

If the gift recipient prefers crunchy, firm nuts, the **almond version** is an excellent second choice.

Unlike the round, milky richness of cashews, almonds offer a drier, crispier texture that pairs beautifully with smooth milk chocolate, creating layers of flavor in every bite. Buying both versions lets the recipient choose their favorite, and it sparks conversation when shared at gatherings.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-11/1795409443290-15834.jpg)

## Why It Makes a Great Souvenir

**1. Affordable Elegance**
The silver-toned packaging is simple and classy, making it a stylish gift. The price is budget-friendly, so buying multiple packs to share won't break the bank.

**2. Easy to Share**
Individual chocolate bars are perfect for colleagues, friends, neighbors, or elders. Hand one out to each person in the office, and everyone will be delighted.

**3. Easy to Find and Carry**
Readily available at supermarkets and convenience stores across Bali, no need to travel far. The chocolate bars are lightweight and compact, fitting perfectly into suitcase gaps.

**4. A Story to Tell**
When gifting, you can say: 'It looks like an import, but it's actually Indonesian chocolate, grown right in the cocoa-producing region. It's just as delicious as big-name brands.' The recipient will likely ask, 'Really?' while taking a bite.

## Shopping Tips

- Both the cashew and almond versions contain nuts. Before gifting, make sure the recipient doesn't have a nut allergy.
- In hot weather, avoid placing them in direct sunlight or stuffy areas. For the trip back, pack them in the middle of your luggage with clothes for extra peace of mind.
- To make the gift more special, mix and match a few bars of cashew and almond varieties—the recipient will be pleasantly surprised.

## Conclusion

Bali offers plenty of souvenir options, but if you want something that's **delicious, beautifully packaged, easy to gift, and budget-friendly**, Silver Queen is a smart choice. Go for the cashew variant first, and add the almond one if you want an extra crunchy option. Next time you're at a Bali supermarket, make sure to stop by the chocolate aisle and grab a few extra bars to take home!
