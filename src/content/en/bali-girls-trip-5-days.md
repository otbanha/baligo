**Key Highlights**: This girls' five-day, four-night itinerary features **Mount Batur sunrise trekking → Ubud Art Market → Snorkeling at Nusa Penida → Jungle yoga**. For Mount Batur, we recommend booking a jeep safari or sunrise hiking tour. On the way back, you can take a Gojek motorcycle to connect with Ubud's food scene and handicraft shopping—a perfect blend of adventure and relaxation for a girls' trip.

During this five-day, four-night Bali journey, Clairejenjen and her friends explored the island's unique and diverse charms. Their itinerary was packed with colorful activities and unforgettable experiences, from watching the sunrise to snorkeling and jungle yoga—every moment brimming with surprise and delight.

Screenshot from Clairejenjen's video

Early on the second day, they climbed Mount Batur to witness Bali's unique sunrise. The trek wasn't easy, but when they reached the rest stop halfway up, the stunning views and fresh air filled them with excitement. Winding along the mountain path, they finally made it to the summit, watching the sun slowly rise in the morning glow—a moment that brightened their spirits like the first rays of light.

Afterwards, they took a Gojek motorcycle back to their vacation villa, enjoyed a hearty breakfast, and then headed to Ubud's art market to soak in the unique charm of the local culture. At the market, they sampled various Balinese specialties, admired local handicrafts and artworks, feeling as if they were in a vibrant, exotic small town.

Next, they took a boat to Nusa Penida for some snorkeling fun. Donning diving gear, they explored the mysterious world beneath the sea. A mix of nervousness and excitement from their first try—when they submerged and saw colorful corals and vibrant schools of fish, the joy was beyond words. Despite some fear, they bravely overcame it and reveled in the endless fun and wonder the ocean offered.
