If your impression of Bali is stuck on the hustle of Kuta or the nightlife of Seminyak, The Meru Sanur might just redefine what a 'Bali vacation' means for you.

![The Meru Sanur｜沙努爾五星療癒度假村 全套房海景＆頂級 SPA 2026 - Go Bali Go](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-04/1776438711480-549267171.jpg)

This five-star all-suite resort opened in late 2024 on Sanur's quiet coast, boasting an overall Agoda rating of 9.1 and winning the 2025 Traveler's Choice Award. Perched on the most serene stretch of Bali's eastern shoreline, it's all about high-end healing—from its architecture and dining to its spa, every detail feels deliberately unhurried. That's a rare find in Bali.

***

## 📍 Location & Surroundings

![位置與周邊環境](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-04/1776438777658-a654724c299c2c110131e3bcb1612476.webp)

The Meru Sanur sits at the northern end of Sanur, right by the white sand beach, just a few minutes' walk from the famous Sanur Promenade. This 8-kilometer coastal path is a daily haunt for locals jogging, cycling, and strolling—walk it at sunrise, and you'll feel like it's just you, the sea breeze, and the light.

This area isn't as noisy as Kuta or as secluded as Ubud—it's that serene vibe where you'll hate to leave after a few days. Nearby spots to explore include:

Also worth noting: The Meru's entire site is part of the Sanur Special Economic Zone for Health (Sanur SEZ), which also houses the Bali International Hospital and Bali's first ethnobotanical herb garden. For travelers with medical or wellness needs, this location is a strategic choice.

## 🏨 Facilities Overview

![設施介紹](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-04/1776438822906-151d9dfd097595970022cdd4bf5833d3.webp)

The first thing that hits you at The Meru is the 'tunnel corridor'—a walkway from the driveway to the lobby lined with coarse marble and mirrored ceilings, flanked by stone statues playing traditional Balinese instruments. Stepping through that corridor into the soaring lobby? Honestly, it's a bit breathtaking.

The lobby is spacious and airy, with dark marble floors paired with woven rattan walls, and the sound of flowing water fills the entire space, creating an upscale yet welcoming atmosphere.

### Key Facilities at a Glance:

**Two Swimming Pools** — One is an Olympic-sized pool, while the other is a quiet-area exclusive pool, ideal for guests who want to avoid the noise of children.

![主要設施一覽：](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-04/1776438855819-0267ab8bb759f1442ea29ba0efff2808.webp)

**Svasana Spa** — The resort's signature treatment is the "Meru Signature Massage," a deep relaxation massage, along with Sound Healing Therapy using singing bowls, which blends traditional Balinese rituals for an authentic healing experience. The spa facilities are currently being expanded to offer more space in the future.

[https://www.instagram.com/reel/C8MarZDPr-k/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C8MarZDPr-k/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==)

**Arunika Restaurant** — Featuring an open terrace design overlooking the pool, many guests highlight sitting here with coffee and watching the sunrise as a memorable moment. The menu takes a modern approach to local ingredients, with Cassava Ice Cream being a popular dessert and the Coral Trout Pepes (steamed coral trout in banana leaf) also worth trying. A complimentary breakfast buffet is included, offering a wide variety of choices.

![主要設施一覽：](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-04/1776438923066-15a21f702dde56fb3a63b2543690ebbf.webp)

**Other Facilities** — Complimentary bicycle rentals, beach yoga classes, boxing lessons, a Kids Club, a fitness center, 24-hour butler service, and a business center. The resort comprises four buildings connected by sky bridges, all designed for natural ventilation, so moving around never feels stuffy.

Main room types:

- **Tropical Suite (83m²)** — The entry-level option, still spacious, with a private balcony or terrace. The bathroom features a rain shower and soaking tub, and some rooms offer garden views.
- **Premier Tropical Suite (125m²)** — Includes a separate bedroom and living area; some ground-floor units come with a private garden, ideal for travelers wanting more outdoor space.
- **Meru Suite Ocean View (160m²)** — Offers more complete ocean views, with the bathtub directly facing the sunrise direction—so soaking in the morning while watching the sunrise is truly achievable.
- **Presidential Suite (209m²)** — Need we say more? It's the largest and finest room available.

Each suite is equipped with a walk-in closet, rain shower, separate wet and dry bathroom areas, private balcony or terrace, smart TV (with streaming capabilities), minibar, and 24-hour room service.

For travelers wanting sea views and to enjoy Sanur's sunrise, the Ocean View room type is highly recommended—worth the upgrade.

## 👥 Who It's For

**Honeymooners and couples** are the best fit—Sanur is peaceful, the sunrises are stunning, spa resources are abundant, and the spacious suites ensure you won't feel cramped. **Families with children** are also well-suited, thanks to a well-equipped Kids Club and child-friendly pool areas. Additionally, **long-stay wellness travelers** or those with medical or recuperation needs will find The Meru's location within the Sanur SEZ special economic zone very convenient.
