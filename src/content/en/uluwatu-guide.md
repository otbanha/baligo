**Bottom line**: Three must-dos in Uluwatu—① **Secret beach exploration** (six cliffside hidden gems like Suluban Beach, Padang Padang Beach, and Green Bowl Beach) ② **Uluwatu Temple for cliff views and Kecak fire dance** ③ World-class surfing spots. The name 'Ulu' means 'end of the land,' and 'Watu' means 'rock,' making the southern tip's sea-cliff terrain the island's most dramatic cliff area. Here's the full list of 50 recommendations.

Uluwatu sits at the southern tip of Bali, covering a vast area—essentially the entire southern region. The name is fascinating: 'Ulu' means 'end of the land,' while 'Watu' means 'rock.' The terrain is mostly rocky, creating a rugged, varied landscape of rolling hills and small peaks shaped by massive sea cliffs.

Uluwatu is famous for its stunning cliff views, hidden beaches tucked beneath cliffs, cliffside Balinese temples, and world-class surf spots. Though the beaches here are small, they're the most beautiful on Bali's mainland!

### Uluwatu Temple (📌[Location Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1caV88xn9M89ulNo9cVhNF6dyakGSo0g&usp=sharing))

![烏魯瓦圖神廟 Uluwatu Temple (位置地圖)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_73f1698ba36e1b9c8e46a30af016165b.jpg)

Uluwatu Temple is one of Bali's iconic sea temples, perched on towering cliffs in the Uluwatu area with sweeping views of the Indian Ocean. Built in the 11th century, it's among the oldest temples on the island. Beyond its religious significance, Uluwatu Temple is famous for its breathtaking sunset views over the cliffs, drawing crowds every evening.

A word of caution: the monkeys here are quite mischievous. Keep a close eye on anything in your hands, your backpack, and especially your glasses or sunglasses — they might snatch them!

![Balangan Viewpoint (位置地圖)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a8d6822261073280c90836feccfdebd6.jpg)

Balangan Cliff Sunset Point is a captivating spot renowned for its stunning coastal views. It's one of the best places in Bali to witness a spectacular sunset, where you can watch the sun slowly sink into the horizon from the cliff's edge, leaving behind an unforgettable scene. You can also enjoy hiking through the rocks to soak in nature's beauty or simply relax on the beach.

It's also an excellent location for wedding photos, with many couples choosing Balangan Cliff Sunset Point to capture precious memories. In this romantic setting, you can seize beautiful moments with your loved one, creating timeless tokens of love.

### Pandawa Beach/Tanah Barak Cliff Road/Blue Road (📌[Location Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1caV88xn9M89ulNo9cVhNF6dyakGSo0g&usp=sharing))

Bali's Pandawa Beach (also known as Tanah Barak Cliff) is a hidden gem that has recently become a popular attraction. This newly opened beach is unique for its stunning blue waters and colorful rocks. Beyond the magnificent sea views, the road here is carved through massive limestone cliffs, with the distant azure ocean and towering rock walls on either side creating a powerful visual impact. Pandawa Beach is about a 30-minute drive from the airport and requires an entrance fee. It's open daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
