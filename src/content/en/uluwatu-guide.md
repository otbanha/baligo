**Bottom line**: Three must-dos in Uluwatu—① **Hidden beach adventures** (Suluban Beach, Padang Padang Beach, Green Bowl Beach, and five other cliffside secret beaches) ② **Uluwatu Temple for cliff views and Kecak fire dance** ③ **World-class surf breaks**. The name 'Ulu' means 'land's end' and 'Watu' means 'rock,' making this southern tip the most dramatic cliff landscape on the island. Here are all 50 recommendations.

Uluwatu sits at the southern tip of Bali, covering a vast area—essentially the entire southern end can be called Uluwatu. The name is fascinating: 'Ulu' means 'end of the land,' while 'Watu' means 'rock.' Thanks to its rocky terrain, Uluwatu's landscape is incredibly varied and rugged, with massive sea cliffs, rolling hills, and small knolls.

Uluwatu is famous for its stunning cliffs, hidden beaches tucked beneath them, cliffside Balinese temples, and world-class surf spots. While the beaches here aren't large, they're the most beautiful on mainland Bali!

### Uluwatu Temple (📌[Location Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1caV88xn9M89ulNo9cVhNF6dyakGSo0g&usp=sharing))

![烏魯瓦圖神廟 Uluwatu Temple (位置地圖)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_73f1698ba36e1b9c8e46a30af016165b.jpg)

Uluwatu Temple is one of Bali's iconic sea temples, perched on towering cliffs in the Uluwatu area overlooking the magnificent Indian Ocean. Built in the 11th century, it's among the oldest temples on the island. Beyond its religious significance, Uluwatu Temple is famous for its breathtaking sunset views over the cliffs, drawing crowds every evening.

A word of caution: the monkeys here are mischievous—watch out for anything in your hands, your backpack, and especially your glasses or sunglasses, as they might snatch them!

### Pandawa Beach/Tanah Barak Cliff (📌[Location Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1caV88xn9M89ulNo9cVhNF6dyakGSo0g&usp=sharing))

Bali's Pandawa Beach (also known as Tanah Barak Cliff) is a hidden gem that has recently become a hot spot. This newly opened beach boasts stunning blue waters and colorful rocks that make it unique. Beyond the breathtaking sea views, the road here is carved through massive limestone cliffs, with the azure ocean in the distance and towering rock walls on both sides creating a dramatic visual impact. Pandawa Beach is about a 30-minute drive from the airport, and an entrance fee is required. It's open daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
