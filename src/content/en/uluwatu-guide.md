**Bottom Line**: Three must-dos in Uluwatu: ① **Secret beach exploration** (Suluban Beach, Padang Padang Beach, Green Bowl Beach, and four other cliffside hidden beaches) ② **Uluwatu Temple for cliff views and Kecak fire dance** ③ World-class surfing spots. The name 'Ulu' means 'land's end' and 'Watu' means 'rock,' making this southern tip's sea-cliff terrain the most dramatic on the island. Here are all 50 recommendations.

Uluwatu sits at the southern tip of Bali, covering a vast area—essentially the entire southern region can be called Uluwatu. The name is fascinating: 'Ulu' means 'end of the land,' while 'Watu' means 'rock.' Because the terrain is mostly rocky, Uluwatu's landscape is incredibly varied and rugged, formed by massive sea cliffs with rolling hills and small peaks.

Uluwatu is famous for its stunning cliffs, multiple hidden beaches nestled between rock faces, cliffside Balinese temples, and world-class surf spots. While the beaches here aren't large, they're among the most beautiful on Bali's mainland!

### Uluwatu Temple (📌[Location Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1caV88xn9M89ulNo9cVhNF6dyakGSo0g&usp=sharing))

![烏魯瓦圖神廟 Uluwatu Temple (位置地圖)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_73f1698ba36e1b9c8e46a30af016165b.jpg)

Uluwatu Temple is one of Bali's iconic sea temples, perched on a towering cliff in the Uluwatu area overlooking the magnificent Indian Ocean. Built in the 11th century, it's among the oldest temples on the island. Beyond its religious significance, Uluwatu Temple is famous for its breathtaking sunset views over the dramatic cliffs, drawing crowds every evening.

A word of caution: the monkeys here are quite mischievous—watch out for anything in your hands, your backpack, and especially your glasses or sunglasses, as they might snatch them!

### Pandawa Beach/Tanah Barak Cliff (📌[Location Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1caV88xn9M89ulNo9cVhNF6dyakGSo0g&usp=sharing))

![Pandawa Beach/Tanah Barak Cliff 斷崖公路/藍色公路 (位置地圖)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e249047f24fcaf820748a7f940fc9d01.jpg)

Bali's Pandawa Beach (also known as Tanah Barak Cliff) is a hidden gem that has recently become a popular attraction. This newly opened beach features stunning blue waters and colorful rocks that make it unique. Beyond the breathtaking sea views, the road here is carved through massive limestone cliffs, with the distant azure ocean and towering rock walls on both sides creating a dramatic visual impact. Pandawa Beach is about a 30-minute drive from the airport, and an entrance fee is required. It's open daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
