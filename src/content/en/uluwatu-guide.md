**Bottom line**: Three must-dos in Uluwatu — ① **Secret beach exploration** (six cliffside hidden beaches like Suluban Beach, Padang Padang Beach, Green Bowl Beach) ② **Uluwatu Temple for cliff views and Kecak fire dance** ③ World-class surfing spots. The name 'Ulu' means 'end of the land' and 'Watu' means 'rock' — the entire southern tip is Bali's most dramatic cliff landscape. Here's the full list of 50 recommendations.

Uluwatu sits at the southern tip of Bali, covering a vast area — essentially the entire southern region can be called Uluwatu. The name is fascinating: 'Ulu' means 'end of the land,' while 'Watu' means 'rock.' Thanks to its rocky terrain, Uluwatu's landscape is incredibly varied and rugged, shaped by massive sea cliffs, rolling hills, and small peaks.

Uluwatu is famous for its stunning cliff views, hidden beaches tucked beneath cliffs, cliffside Balinese temples, and world-class surf spots. While the beaches here aren't large, they're the most beautiful on Bali's mainland!

### Uluwatu Temple (📌[Location Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1caV88xn9M89ulNo9cVhNF6dyakGSo0g&usp=sharing))

![烏魯瓦圖神廟 Uluwatu Temple (位置地圖)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_73f1698ba36e1b9c8e46a30af016165b.jpg)

Perched on towering cliffs in the Uluwatu area, Uluwatu Temple is one of Bali's iconic seaside temples, offering breathtaking views of the Indian Ocean. Built in the 11th century, it's among the oldest temples on the island. Beyond its religious significance, the temple is famous for its stunning sunset vistas over the dramatic cliffs, drawing crowds every evening.

A quick heads-up: the monkeys here are quite mischievous—watch out for anything in your hands, your backpack, and especially your glasses or sunglasses, as they might snatch them!

### Pandawa Beach/Tanah Barak Cliff (📌[Location Map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1caV88xn9M89ulNo9cVhNF6dyakGSo0g&usp=sharing))

Bali's Pandawa Beach (also known as Tanah Barak Cliff) is a hidden gem that has recently become a hot spot. This newly opened beach boasts stunning turquoise waters and colorful rocks that make it unique.
