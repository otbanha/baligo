# Must-Do Adventures in Ubud: UTV Off-Roading + Rafting/Swing Complete Experience

_Race through rice terraces, conquer raging rivers, and soar through the skies in a single day. How to make the most of your Ubud UTV experience?_

## Why Choose UTV Over ATV?

In Ubud's off-roading scene, you'll find two options: **UTV/buggy** (side-by-side or front-back seating with steering wheel control) and **ATV** (front-back seating, similar to a motorcycle).

**Simply put: UTV/buggy wins on stability, comfort, and beginner-friendliness.** If it's your first time, you want to share the adventure with friends, or you're on a family trip, UTV/buggy is clearly the better choice. ATV is better suited for experienced riders seeking a thrilling, hands-on ride.

## Must-Know Before You Go

### 📋 **What to Wear & Bring?**

**Clothing:**

- Lightweight, quick-dry clothes (you'll get wet rafting)
- Waterproof sandals or hiking shoes (no flip-flops)
- Hat, sunglasses, sunscreen (SPF 50+)

**Items:**

- 🔐 Waterproof bag/phone pouch (essential!)
- 🧴 Towel and change of clothes
- 💵 Cash for tips (Rp 50,000-100,000)
- 📷 Camera or GoPro (to capture the moments)

### 🌤️ **Best Season**

- ✅ **Dry Season (April-October)** — Good road conditions, clear views
- 🌧️ **Rainy Season (November-March)** — Lush green rice terraces, fewer crowds, but slippery roads
