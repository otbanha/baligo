# Ultimate Ubud Adventure: UTV Off-Road + Rafting/Swing Combo

_Race through rice terraces, conquer rapids, and soar through the sky in one day. How to make the most of your Ubud UTV experience?_

## Why Choose UTV Over ATV?

In Ubud's off-road scene, you'll find two options: **UTV** (side-by-side seating with a steering wheel) and **ATV** (tandem seating, similar to a motorcycle).

**Simply put: UTV wins for stability, comfort, and beginner-friendliness.** If it's your first time, you want to share the adventure with a friend, or you're on a family trip, UTV is the clear choice. ATV is better for experienced riders craving a more thrilling, hands-on ride.

### ⏰ **9:00 - 12:00 - UTV Off-Road Adventure**

**You'll drive the UTV through:**

## Must-Know Before You Go

### 📋 **What to Wear & Bring?**

**Clothing:**

- Lightweight, quick-dry clothes (rafting will get you wet)
- Waterproof sandals or hiking shoes (no flip-flops)
- Hat, sunglasses, sunscreen (SPF 50+)

**Items:**

- 🔐 Waterproof bag/phone pouch (essential!)
- 🧴 Towel and change of clothes
- 💵 Cash for tips (Rp 50,000–100,000)
- 📷 Camera or GoPro (to capture the moments)

### 🌤️ **Best Season**

- ✅ **Dry Season (April–October)** — Good road conditions, clear views
- 🌧️ **Rainy Season (November–March)** — Lush green rice terraces, fewer crowds, but slippery roads
