# Ubud Superhero Zipline + Rice Terrace Cultural Tour: Fly Over Bali's Rice Fields Like a Superhero

Most people come to Ubud to see rice terraces, swing on swings, and browse markets. This experience is a bit different: you lie face-down, arms spread, and soar above the rice paddies.

It's the 'Ubud: Bali's Longest Superhero Zipline + Rice Terrace Cultural Tour' on Klook, run by local operator U Fun Field. It combines a high-altitude zipline, photo ops, and a rice field cultural tour into one itinerary—balancing thrills and culture.

## Why It's Worth Adding to Your Itinerary

### 1. Bali's Longest Superhero Zipline

The superhero zipline is a prone-style ride—you fly horizontally over Ubud's private rice fields. Beneath you, a sea of green terraces unfolds, giving you that true 'superhero soaring through the sky' feeling.

### 2. 6 Free Photo Spots, Including Bali's Wings

No need to rush off after your ride. The park has 6 free photo spots, including the popular 'Wings of Bali.' Other setups like the dreamcatcher swing, flower swing, dream chaser swing, crescent moon swing, bird's nest, beehive, and Instagram frames all make for great photos.

### 3. Every Package Includes a Private Rice Terrace Cultural Tour

This is the biggest difference between this tour and a typical zipline experience. A local guide will take you into private rice terraces, visit a farmer's hut, learn about traditional rice cultivation, and even try carrying a rice basket yourself, with opportunities to interact with local farmers. After the thrill, you slow down to explore Bali's rice terrace culture—a wonderfully paced experience.

## 4. Insurance Included, No Queuing

The activity is clearly marked as safe and legal with insurance coverage, and there's no waiting in line, so you can spend your time playing and taking photos.

## How to Choose from Four Packages

Each package offers different inclusions, so be sure to read the details before booking.

Superman Zipline: Bali's Longest Rice Terrace Zipline – Focuses on the Superman zipline experience, best for first-timers.

Private Rice Terrace Tandem Zipline Combo – Perfect for couples or pairs, gliding together over the rice fields.

Ultimate 4-in-1 Games + Rice Terrace Superman Zipline – Includes 4 giant swings, aerial bike, sky cycle, dual zipline, plus the Superman zipline—choose this if you want to do it all.

Scenic zipline experience over rice fields: a standard zipline with 2 tracks gliding along private rice paddies at a relaxed pace.

## Tips & Suggestions

- Start early: The morning light in Ubud's rice terraces is soft and perfect for photos.
- Wear comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes: You'll be moving around on zip lines and swings, so comfort is key.
- Bring the whole family: Kids aged 5 and up can join in, so parents and children can fly together.
- Plan it as a half-day Ubud trip: Zip line and photos in the morning, lunch in Ubud at noon, then explore the market or a café in the afternoon—a perfect pace.
