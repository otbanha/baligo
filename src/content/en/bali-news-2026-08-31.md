# Bali Tourist Hot Topics & News Roundup | August 31, 2026

## 🔥 Forest Fire on Mount Batur! About 2 Hectares Burned

Bali has reported another dry-season fire today.

A forest fire broke out in the Kintamani area of Mount Batur (Gunung Batur), burning approximately **2 hectares of land**.

The fire started around noon on August 30 in the Seked area of Batur Selatan Village, Bangli. Authorities remain on-site to prevent new flare-ups.

Police noted that the recent hot weather, combined with strong winds, means even small sparks from burning trash or discarded cigarette butts can quickly ignite a blaze.

Notably, **the Seked area also experienced a fire on August 11**.

For travelers planning to visit Kintamani or Mount Batur for sunrise or trekking, the entire mountain is not currently closed. However, if your itinerary involves forests, mountainous areas, or remote roads, it's best to check the latest conditions before heading out.

And this once again shows: **Although Bali is nearing the end of the dry season, the risk of fire still exists.**

## 🤿 Tulamben Enters Diving Season! Around 200 Tourists Dive Daily

If you love diving, this news is actually one of the more exciting updates today.

Eastern Bali's **Tulamben** has recently entered diving season, with a large number of foreign tourists coming for scuba diving and snorkeling, with the most popular attraction still being the famous **USAT Liberty wreck**.

Local operators say that on average, about **200 or more tourists** come to dive each day. Since July, the number of visitors has noticeably increased.

Tulamben's highlight is the Liberty wreck, which is very close to the shore, making it highly appealing to both beginner and advanced divers.

However, local operators also remind that **the number of tourists is not fixed every day**. About two weeks ago, due to murky waters and larger waves, the number of visitors dropped significantly.

So if you're planning a Tulamben dive trip soon, besides considering the season, you should also keep an eye on **the day's sea conditions**.

## 🏛️ New City Landmark in Singaraja! Praja Mandala Becomes a Popular Public Space

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-08/1788103531054-praja-mandala-1788100809474_169.jpeg)

A new public space worth noting has recently emerged in **Singaraja**, northern Bali.

Located in the city center, **Praja Mandala Singa Ambara Raja** has been revitalized to blend historic architecture, streetscapes, music, and public events, gradually becoming a fresh leisure spot for locals and visitors alike.

This area was originally known as **Singaraja's 'Titik Nol'** (Point Zero). The Buleleng government later redeveloped it with a heritage district concept, officially opening it at the end of July this year.

Now, from afternoon to evening, you can see young people taking photos, cyclists resting, and music enthusiasts performing here.

For most travelers, the main draw isn't large-scale attractions but the **more everyday urban vibe of northern Bali**.

If you've already visited Lovina and want to explore more of northern Bali, consider spending extra time in Singaraja.

## 🚧 Badung Cracks Down on Illegal Structures! 3 Unauthorized Buildings Demolished

The Badung Regency government launched an operation against illegal buildings today, with the Satpol PP (local civil service police) and environmental agencies jointly handling **3 unauthorized structures**.

Officials stated that these buildings were constructed without complying with relevant land and building regulations, hence the crackdown.

This is actually consistent with Bali's recent policy direction of strictly regulating villas, accommodations, and land use.

For travelers, this doesn't mean Bali has suddenly 'banned building villas,' but rather that authorities are conducting stricter checks on:

**Whether the land is legal, whether the building is legal, and whether the usage complies with regulations.**

So if you're planning to buy property, long-term rent a villa, or even invest in accommodation projects in Bali, this kind of news is worth paying close attention to.

## 🌬️ Strong Winds in Buleleng Topple Multiple Trees — Road Advisory for North Bali Travelers

Today, strong winds hit Buleleng, causing **trees to fall** in several locations. One tree even collapsed onto a shophouse in Sukasada, resulting in approximately **IDR 75 million** in damages.

Local media reports that at least **6 trees fell** today, temporarily blocking some road sections.

This is linked to the current weather conditions bringing strong winds to parts of Bali.

For travelers, if you're heading toward **Singaraja, Lovina, Munduk, or the mountainous areas of North Bali** tomorrow, it's advisable to pay close attention to road conditions.

Especially for those riding scooters, when strong winds hit, don't just focus on the rain—**fallen branches, toppled trees, and road obstacles also require caution.**
