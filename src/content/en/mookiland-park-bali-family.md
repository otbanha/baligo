**Bottom Line**: Located in Kerobokan near Seminyak, Mookiland Park spans 5,000 square meters, featuring a dedicated zone for toddlers aged 2-4 and active areas like rock climbing and obstacle courses, plus a 500-square-meter semi-covered section (perfect for rainy or hot days). The inflatable equipment meets European EN14960 safety standards, and birthday party venue rentals are available—ideal for a full day of family relaxation with kids.

In the midst of busy work lives, parents often struggle to meet their children's entertainment needs. Whether visiting major attractions, playgrounds, or hiring babysitters, these options rarely satisfy kids' desire to explore and play. Mookiland Park in Kerobokan, Bali, provides a safe, comfortable, and fun environment for children, allowing parents to relax in the park or at nearby restaurants. This family-friendly park is thoughtfully designed with a wealth of activities and facilities, ensuring kids enjoy a full day of fun.

## **What is Mookiland Park?**

Mookiland Park is a family-oriented playground covering 5,000 square meters. Before opening in Bali in 2023, it was an indoor playground brand operating in Jakarta since 2016. They previously partnered with some of Jakarta's largest shopping malls and event venues to set up temporary children's play areas, such as Lippo Mall Kemang, Kota Kasablanka, and Taman Anggrek.

## **Main Attractions**

Mookiland Park offers a variety of play equipment, including rock climbing walls, obstacle courses, and inflatable castles, ensuring there's something for every child. These facilities not only help kids burn off energy but also let them enjoy endless fun.

## **Zones for Different Age Groups**

Mookiland has specially designated zones for different age groups to ensure every child can participate in age-appropriate activities. For example, toddlers aged 2-4 have their own play area, while more active kids can explore climbing and adventure zones to their heart's content.

## **High-Quality Equipment and Safety**

Mookiland's facilities come from world-class playground suppliers, with inflatable trampolines manufactured to European safety standard EN14960. They strictly adhere to European safety regulations, ensuring maximum capacity limits, daily safety inspections, and proper safety distances around each activity.

## **Bali's Largest Playground**

Mookiland Family Park is one of the largest children's playgrounds in Bali, offering a wide range of facilities for endless fun. Whether it's a climbing wall, obstacle course, or bounce castle, every child can find their own happy place here. Additionally, the park features a 500-square-meter semi-covered area, perfect for playing on hot or rainy days, ensuring non-stop enjoyment.

## **Restaurant with Scenic Views**

Mookiland's restaurant is a standout highlight, offering a variety of food options that let parents and kids savor delicious meals while taking in beautiful views. Overlooking serene rice paddies, the lush greenery contrasts with the clear blue sky, creating a relaxing atmosphere far from the hustle and bustle of southern Bali. Guests can dine on the semi-open terrace or unwind in a more casual garden area with bean bags.

## **Perfect Birthday Parties**

Mookiland Park is an ideal venue for birthday parties. With its colorful decor and lively ambiance, it provides a magical party space for kids. From interactive play zones and inflatable castles to a Ninja Warrior playground, children can enjoy a wide range of activities. Mookiland's professional team will plan and execute every detail, ensuring the birthday party becomes an unforgettable memory.

## **Quality Time for Parents and Kids**

At Mookiland Park, children can engage in friendly competitions and create wonderful memories with other kids. The vibrant design and playful environment encourage imagination and pure joy. Parents can also relax and attend to their own matters, knowing their children are playing in a safe setting.

Mookiland Park is the perfect family playground, offering a wide variety of activities and facilities that let kids play to their heart's content while parents enjoy some well-deserved relaxation. Whether you're looking for an ideal play space or planning an unforgettable birthday party, Mookiland Park is your top choice.
