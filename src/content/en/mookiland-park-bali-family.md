In the midst of busy work and life, parents often struggle to keep up with their children's entertainment needs. Whether visiting major attractions, playgrounds, or hiring babysitters, these options often fall short of satisfying kids' desire to explore and have fun.

Mookiland Park, situated in Kerobokan, Bali, offers children a safe, comfortable, and fun-filled environment, allowing parents to relax in the park or at its café. This family-friendly park is thoughtfully designed with a wealth of activities and facilities, keeping kids entertained for an entire day. In 2026, a new water play area was added, making the experience even more complete.

## What is Mookiland Park?

Mookiland Park is a family-oriented playground spanning 5,000 square meters. It opened in Bali in 2023, with its brand originating from an indoor playground that operated in Jakarta since 2016. The team previously set up temporary children's play areas in some of Jakarta's largest shopping malls and event venues, such as Lippo Mall Kemang, Kota Kasablanka, and Taman Anggrek.

## Main Attractions

Mookiland Park features a variety of facilities, including a climbing wall, obstacle course, and inflatable castle, ensuring there's something for every child. Kids can burn off endless energy while parents enjoy peace of mind.

The official event page also highlights several standout features:

- **Lost Castle (New in 2026):** A multi-functional inflatable castle measuring 17 meters long and 6 meters wide.
- **Crazy Net:** An indoor suspended play net featuring a Tarzan rope, boxing ball, and large exercise ball.
- **Fun Zip:** An 8-meter dual zipline surrounded by safety nets.
- **Verticale Limite:** An inflatable climbing wall where you climb up and slide down the other side.
- **Jungle:** A low inflatable play area designed specifically for children under 6.
- **Giant Games:** Oversized building blocks, Connect Four, Jenga, beanbag toss, and a foosball table near the restaurant.

## New in 2026: Water Zone – Kids' Aquatic Play Area

https://www.instagram.com/reel/Df_jJlsStlQ/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&lqvp=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==

The Water Zone, added in 2026, offers three age-based play options—from toddlers to thrill-seeking older kids—all included in the admission ticket at no extra cost.

#### Oasis | Ages 0–6

This is an approximately 10 x 11 meter splash pad with gentle water jets, a mini slide, and small obstacles. Little ones can chase the water streams while parents stay close enough to watch and reach them.

#### Water Splash｜Ages 4 to 10

This is a large inflatable water play area with bigger slides and water cannons. After the toddler zone, kids usually crave more excitement, and this fits the bill perfectly.

#### Maxi Slide｜Ages 5 and up, adults can join too

This is a dual water slide, 6 meters high and 25 meters long. Two kids can race side by side, and parents can slide down together as well.

## Tips Before You Splash

- Remember to bring swimsuits and a change of clothes.
- The water attractions only have age requirements; the official guidelines do not list height restrictions.

## Zones Tailored for Different Ages

Mookiland has specially designed zones for different age groups, ensuring every child can enjoy activities suited to their age. Toddlers aged 2 to 4 have their own dedicated play area, while more active kids can let loose in the climbing and exploration zones. The entire park is suitable for children aged 2 to 12.

## High-Quality Equipment and Safety

Mookiland’s facilities come from world-class playground suppliers, with inflatable trampolines manufactured to European safety standard EN14960. The park strictly follows European safety regulations, including maximum capacity per play area, daily safety inspections, and safe distances around activities.

## Bali’s Largest Playground

Mookiland Family Park is one of the largest children’s playgrounds in Bali. Whether it’s the climbing wall, obstacle course, bounce castle, or the newly opened water play area, every child can find their happy place here. The park features a 500-square-meter semi-covered area along with semi-indoor spaces, so kids can play even in hot weather or rain.

## Operating Information

- Open daily from 9:00 AM to 9:30 PM.
- One ticket covers all water and inflatable attractions.
- Suitable for children aged 2 to 12.

## **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)**

**What age group is Mookiland Park suitable for?**
The park is primarily designed for children aged 2 to 12, with attractions for both toddlers and older kids. The official FAQ answers “Is it suitable for toddlers and older children?” with “Absolutely!”

**What ages are the three Water Zone attractions suitable for?**
Oasis is for ages 6 and under, Water Splash is for ages 4 to 10, and Maxi Slide starts at age 5.

**Do water facilities cost extra?**
No. The entrance fee already includes the water play area, so one ticket gives you access to both the water and inflatable attractions.

**Can parents join the water slides?**
Yes. The Maxi Slide is a double water slide standing 6 meters tall, suitable for both kids and parents. Moms and dads can race their children side by side.

**What should I bring?**
According to the official Instagram, bring a swimsuit for the water slides and a change of clothes so you can slip into something dry afterward.

**What are the opening hours?**
Open daily from 9:00 AM to 9:30 PM. You can visit during the day or early evening to avoid the midday sun.

**Can I go when it's hot or rainy?**
Yes. The official FAQ says 'Certainly!' The large semi-indoor play area is a safe choice during hot weather or light rain, with shade, umbrellas, fans, and partially covered zones throughout the park.

**Is there food available inside?**
Yes. The Snack restaurant offers views of the rice paddies, so parents can sit down with a drink and enjoy the scenery while the kids play.

**Can I make a reservation?**
Yes. The website has a 'Book now' link that directs you to the GuestApps booking page, or you can get in touch via the Contact us page.

**Can I host a birthday party?**
Yes. The website has a dedicated birthday party page—just head there for details if you're planning a celebration.
