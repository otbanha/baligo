**Bottom Line First**: The Varuna Show, an underwater theater performance that sparked buzz upon its 2025 debut, is located at Marine Safari Bali in Gianyar. Together with the adjacent Bali Safari Park, they form 'Bali Safari and Marine Park' (one destination, two worlds), perfect for a day trip combining a land zoo and an underwater theater experience.

On this Bali trip, nothing left a deeper impression than experiencing the Varuna Show at Marine Safari Bali! This underwater theater, blending magic, lights, and acrobatics, completely redefined my expectations of marine park performances.

![2025年開幕即轟動，印尼的時代雜誌也介紹過](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b7affae461c64b52b610dcb3c036bdb5.jpg)

It made waves right after its 2025 opening, even featured by Indonesia's Time magazine.

## 🚗 Transport & Entry

Located in Gianyar, Marine Safari Bali sits right next to the famous Bali Safari Park. The two parks are now collectively called '[Bali Safari and Marine Park](https://www.balisafarimarinepark.com/)', as the official tagline goes: 'One Destination, Two Worlds'.

![交通與入園](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a852c7ca33d5bd372e588be693151a71.jpg)

Since the location isn't in a busy area, I recommend taking a private car to get there. The park is bigger than I imagined, and as soon as you walk in, you're swept up by the tropical landscaping and cheerful atmosphere. We bought the [Day Varuna Premium package](https://affiliate.klook.com/redirect?aid=116349&aff_adid=1063961&k_site=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.klook.com%2Factivity%2F142815-marine-safari-bali%2F%3Fspm%3DActivity.TopNavigation.SelectLanguage%26clickId%3D5cf91e78e5%26utm_medium%3Daffiliate-alwayson%26utm_source%3Dnon-network%26utm_campaign%3D40584%26utm_term%3D%26utm_content%3D) in advance on Klook. Although it wasn't cheap, it was absolutely worth every penny!

## 🍽 Surprises at the Underwater-Themed Restaurant

The package includes lunch at a meticulously designed 'Underwater World' themed restaurant. The entire space is bathed in blue tones, with walls adorned with coral, seaweed, and other ocean elements, paired with special lighting effects that make you feel like you're dining in an underwater palace. The meal is a refined Western-style set menu, and we especially recommend their Balinese specialty drinks. The kids couldn't get enough of the adorable desserts either.

![海底主題餐廳的驚喜](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_dde920a2ff3ff6a19c243c058206babf.jpg)

## 🎭 Varuna Show: A Spectacular Debut

Our Premium seats were in the central area, offering an excellent view. As the lights dimmed, the entire theater instantly transformed into a vast underwater world.

The performance centered on the adventurous tale of Varuna, the guardian of the deep sea, with the most breathtaking moments coming from the meticulously designed stage effects:

- Water curtain projections created illusions of the deep ocean
- Acrobats gracefully 'danced underwater' (achieved through special costumes and lighting effects)
- Stage mechanisms seamlessly shifted scenes, from coral reefs to deep-sea canyons in one fluid motion
- Surround sound made you feel completely immersed

## 📸 Photo Ops with the Performers

After the show, the staff guided us to a photo area. The kids were thrilled to pose with performers in their dazzling costumes—these photos became some of our most treasured souvenirs from the trip.

## 🐬 Other Exciting Activities

After watching the Varuna Show, we also experienced what's included in the Premium package:

- Jungle Odyssey: Get up close with wildlife (takes about an hour; we recommend entering right when it opens to avoid crowds and get great photos)
- Fun Zone: A variety of rides for both adults and kids to enjoy together
- Sea Lion & Dolphin Show: Highly interactive (3:00 PM)
- Pachamaya Fire Show: A primal, powerful performance (12:00 PM & 3:30 PM)
