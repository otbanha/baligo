Many travelers may not have heard of Bali’s Bukit Peninsula, but the simplest way to define it is everything south of Ngurah Rai International Airport.

Once overlooked, the Bukit Peninsula has rapidly emerged as a booming travel hotspot and the fastest-growing resort area on the island. It is mainly divided into three zones: Jimbaran, Uluwatu, and Nusa Dua on the east coast. With a rich variety of attractions and stunning resorts, the peninsula caters to all kinds of travelers—whether you’re after Jimbaran’s sunset beaches, Nusa Dua’s five-star hotels, or Uluwatu’s world-class surf breaks.

![機場以南的這個區域就是武吉半島](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_523299ccfa5907d72644967293d8df35.png)

The area south of the airport is the Bukit Peninsula.

The Bukit Peninsula is full of hidden corners waiting to be explored, and visitors could easily spend their entire vacation here. If you’re curious about this little gem at the bottom of Bali’s map, now is the perfect time to discover all the top attractions in the area. Whether you’re planning a value-packed getaway or a budget-friendly short trip, the Bukit Peninsula is one of the most popular destinations right now, ready for you to uncover.

For the Balinese people, the Bukit Peninsula has long held deep spiritual and cultural significance. It is home to the famous Uluwatu Temple (Pura Luhur Uluwatu), perched on a cliff overlooking the Indian Ocean with breathtaking views. Said to have been built in the 11th century, it is one of Bali’s six directional temples, playing a profound role in the island’s spiritual life.

Though historically remote and undeveloped, the Bukit Peninsula has transformed into a world-renowned surf and resort paradise. After Bali’s tourism boom in the 1970s and 1980s, the area began to develop rapidly. Its beautiful beaches, luxury resorts, and secluded surf spots have made it a top destination for international travelers.

If you’re planning a trip to the Bukit Peninsula, here are some recommended itineraries:

1. **Relax in Jimbaran**: Upon arrival, check into a beachfront resort and spend the day soaking up the sun and waves at Jimbaran Beach. In the evening, enjoy a candlelit dinner on the sand with fresh local seafood as the sun sets.
2. **Explore Local Culture**: Visit the Jimbaran Fish Market to experience the life of local fishermen, then head to the Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park (GWK) to admire the towering eagle statue and explore the cultural exhibits.
3. **Soak in Uluwatu’s Views and Surf Vibe**: Take a morning surf lesson at Uluwatu Beach, then visit Uluwatu Temple and find a cliffside restaurant for sunset dinner.
4. **Discover Hidden Beaches**: Uluwatu’s secret beaches are perfect for an afternoon of relaxation—see details in [Bali’s Six Hidden Beaches Guide](/en/blog/bali-hidden-beaches-guide/).
5. **Outdoor Activities and Paragliding**: Pandawa Beach is a hidden gem, and you can also try paragliding for a bird’s-eye view of the entire Bukit Peninsula.

Whether you're an adrenaline-seeking surfer or a traveler in search of serene relaxation, the Bukit Peninsula offers an unforgettable experience for every visitor. Here, stunning landscapes blend seamlessly with culture, from luxurious resorts to local temples that leave you longing to stay.

## Uluwatu: Surf and Culture Drive a Tourism Boom

Bali's Uluwatu is one of the island's most popular areas, and recent visitor numbers show this clifftop paradise is more in demand than ever. It draws international surfers and culture enthusiasts alike, fulfilling every expectation of a Bali getaway.

https://youtu.be/JX6B-63zQn0

Among its highlights, Padang Padang Beach is one of Uluwatu's most famous spots, welcoming a record number of visitors this peak season. Known for its dramatic cliff scenery and waves that attract surfers, it's a must-visit. Beyond Padang Padang, Thomas Beach, Uluwatu Beach, Bingin Beach, and Suluban Beach, as well as Pandawa Beach and Green Bowl Beach in the south, are all essential stops for anyone exploring Uluwatu.

Padang Padang Beach's managing manager, I Wayan Wijana, stated that since the start of 2024, the beach has averaged around 1,000 to 1,200 visitors daily, with numbers reaching 1,500 to 2,000 during peak season. Most tourists come from Australia and various parts of Asia, while domestic Indonesian visitors account for about 30% of the total.

Most of the beaches here are not large, but their unique high waves attract professional surfers from around the world. Additionally, the white sandy shores add to their unparalleled charm.

Beyond surfing, Uluwatu also integrates cultural activities, regularly hosting Kecak fire dance performances that allow visitors to enjoy this traditional Balinese dance against the sunset. Uluwatu Temple is the heart of cultural exploration, where you can not only watch the Kecak performance at sunset but also experience the locals' centuries-old reverence for nature firsthand.

Furthermore, to protect the cliffs of Uluwatu Temple, authorities have invested 82 trillion Indonesian rupiah in reinforcement projects, expected to be completed by 2025, ensuring this sacred site continues to serve as Bali's spiritual beacon.

Uluwatu combines stunning natural scenery with rich cultural activities, attracting travelers from around the world, making it an irresistible resort destination in Bali.

## Nusa Dua: A Luxurious Resort Experience

https://youtu.be/zxDZJ93EqA4

Nusa Dua is renowned for its five-star beachfront accommodations and beautiful white sand beaches. With a quiet and safe atmosphere, it attracts family travelers and honeymooners, making it Bali's upscale resort destination. Here are the must-see activities and attractions in Nusa Dua:

### **Five-Star Beachfront Accommodations**

Nusa Dua is home to numerous five-star resorts, such as the St. Regis, The Mulia, and Hilton, offering infinity pools, private beaches, and top-notch spa services—making it the ultimate choice for a luxurious getaway.

### **Bali Collection Shopping Mall**

https://youtu.be/3AqPucmnSLE

Bali Collection is a boutique shopping center in Nusa Dua, featuring a mix of local and international brand stores, along with handicraft shops and local restaurants, allowing travelers to effortlessly enjoy shopping and dining. The pleasant atmosphere makes it a great spot for strolling, shopping, and relaxing.

### **Water Sports at Tanjung Benoa Beach**

Located just north of Nusa Dua, Tanjung Benoa Beach is a hotspot for water activities. From snorkeling and banana boat rides to parasailing, there's something for every thrill-seeker looking to enjoy a day of aquatic fun.

### **Fine Dining Experiences**

Nusa Dua's upscale restaurants offer a diverse range of culinary options, from local dishes to international cuisine. Whether you're enjoying a romantic candlelit seafood dinner on the beach or dining at a French or Japanese restaurant, the food scene here is filled with romance and elegance.

# A One-Day Itinerary for Uluwatu and Jimbaran

## Option 1: The Uluwatu Adventure

![第一種玩法：烏魯瓦圖探險之旅](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_134f994f3e774c47d5e56694a2b1052e.png)

### **Morning: Explore the Majestic Uluwatu Temple and Cliffside Views**

Start your day with an adventure by heading to the iconic **Uluwatu Temple (Pura Luhur Uluwatu)**. Perched on a 70-meter cliff overlooking the vast Indian Ocean, the morning light casts a serene and sacred glow over the temple — a perfect spot for quiet meditation and soaking in breathtaking natural beauty.

As you explore the temple, take a stroll along the surrounding paths to discover hidden corners, and keep an eye out for the lively monkeys — the true 'locals' here. Just be sure to secure your belongings, as these cheeky creatures are known for their playful antics.

### **Midday: Surfing and Lunch at Dreamy Beaches**

Uluwatu is famous for its world-class surf breaks. If you're into surfing, head to **Padang Padang Beach** or **Suluban Beach**, where you'll find waves suitable for all skill levels. Even if you're not a surfer, you can still soak up the sun and enjoy the refreshing sea breeze on these stunning shores.

For lunch, stop by a beachfront café or a restaurant with ocean views to savor fresh local seafood. Take your time to relax and recharge for the rest of the day's adventures.

## Second Way to Play: Jimbaran Seafood and Cultural Experience

![第二種玩法：金巴蘭海鮮與文化體驗](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_6e4d3f6a3f09b6ef0d2c063e9bc05d69.jpg)

### **Morning: Visit the Fishing Village and Jimbaran Market**

Jimbaran is famous for its traditional fishing village, so we recommend visiting the **Jimbaran Fish Market** early in the morning to experience the daily life of local fishermen. The market is brimming with fresh seafood, and you can even buy some local catch to have a seafood restaurant cook it for you on the spot. This is a great opportunity to deeply experience Bali's local culture.

### **Midday: Cultural Experience and Relaxation**

In the latter part of the morning, we suggest visiting the **Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park (GWK Park)** and having lunch there. This park is home to one of the world's tallest statues—the **Garuda Wisnu Kencana statue**. The cultural park showcases Bali's rich heritage and is a great place to learn about Hindu mythology and Balinese culture. You can also watch traditional dance and music performances here, soaking in the area's vibrant cultural atmosphere.
