## Family Travel Tips

### 1. Climate & Clothing

Bali has a tropical climate. Summer (June–August) is the dry season, hot with strong sun. Dress kids in light, breathable clothes, sun hats, sunglasses, and high-SPF sunscreen. Winter is the rainy season, so expect brief showers.

![1. 氣候與穿著](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7c08d86c6df883cdd0f82bbf4a41a31c.png)

If you're planning a volcano sunrise trip—whether hiking or by jeep—bring warm clothes. Check out the [Mount Batur Sunrise Hiking Guide](/en/blog/batur-sunrise-hiking-guide/) for more.

### 2. Health & Safety

- **Mosquito protection**: Mosquitoes are common in Bali. Local repellents work best, and choose accommodations with mosquito nets.
- **Drinking Water**: Avoid drinking tap water directly. It's recommended to buy bottled water or use boiled water.
- **Medical Preparation**: Pack common medications such as fever reducers, anti-diarrhea medicine, motion sickness pills, and band-aids. For reference: [What essential medicines and supplies should you bring for a Bali trip? How to buy mosquito repellent and sunscreen?](/en/blog/bali-travel-medicine-checklist/)

![2. 健康與安全](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_1fe90182feb7b0f11bdd7ee503a63a11.png)

### 3. **Transportation & Getting Around**

Traffic in Bali can be chaotic, so it's best to book a reputable private car service and arrange a child safety seat. If your child is young, avoid long drives and choose attractions close to your accommodation to minimize back-and-forth travel.

![3. 交通與移動](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_5d7d530bfe7d4a2f4a1ffbbb9f5e99b7.jpg)

### 4. **Dining Choices**

Bali offers a wide variety of restaurants, but children's stomachs can be sensitive. Choose eateries with good hygiene standards and avoid overly spicy or strongly flavored dishes. For example, Bali's famous babi guling (suckling pig) is often very spicy and may not be suitable for kids!

Additionally, many restaurants offer children's menus, and you can also opt for international chain restaurants to ensure the food suits your child's palate.

**Quick Reference:**

### 5. **Cultural Etiquette**

The people of Bali are friendly and warm, but local culture places great emphasis on manners. Teach children to respect local customs, such as staying quiet when entering temples and not touching religious objects casually.

## **8 Family-Friendly Activities in Seminyak:**

Seminyak is one of Bali's most stylish areas, perfect for families who enjoy modern amenities and beach activities.

### **1. Surfing Adventure**

![1.冲浪冒險之旅](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_acd3def2c723eb7ba857e888a6b2173f.jpg)

Bali is one of the world's best surfing destinations for the whole family! In particular, **Seminyak Beach** and **Double Six Beach** in Seminyak are not only a paradise for surf enthusiasts but also excellent spots for family fun. With gentle slopes and mild waves, these beaches are ideal for kids and parents to experience the thrill of surfing together!

Here, you'll find many professional **family surf schools** with instructors who are not only experienced but also skilled at helping children enjoy the excitement of surfing safely. These schools offer surf gear for the whole family, from small boards for kids to professional boards for adults—everything you need!

**Family Surfing Tips:**

1. **Beginner Lessons**: If the whole family is trying surfing for the first time, sign up for beginner lessons. Instructors will start with basic moves, making it easy for everyone to get started.
2. **Family Challenge**: Parents and kids can compete to see who can stand the longest or ride the farthest, adding interactive fun!
3. **Beach Games**: If you're tired from surfing, build sandcastles or play frisbee on the beach while soaking up the sun and sea breeze.

Whether you're a child or an adult, you'll find your own surfing rhythm here and create unforgettable family memories!

### 2. Bali's Water Wonderland — Waterbom Bali

![2.峇里島的歡樂水世界——Waterbom Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_4a40d018acfd204d09d0db5b3cc0c0dc.jpg)

**Waterbom Bali** is about a 40-minute drive from Seminyak. This massive water park is one of the most popular in Asia, offering a perfect blend of relaxation for adults and pure magic for kids. Spread across **3.8 hectares**, the park features **26** meticulously designed water slides and a variety of fun aquatic attractions, guaranteeing a full day of non-stop fun for the whole family!

**Family-Friendly Highlights:**

1. **Thrilling Water Slides**: For the adventurous family members, try the high-speed slides like "Climax" or "Smash Down 2.0" for an adrenaline-pumping rush from the top!
2. **Relaxing Lazy River**: If you need to unwind, the whole family can float along the **Lazy River** on inner tubes, soaking up the leisurely vibes.
3. **Kids' Zone**: The park also has a dedicated shallow area and smaller slides designed for little ones, ensuring they can play safely and joyfully!

## **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

**1. Is Bali suitable for traveling with babies or toddlers?**

Yes! Bali is very suitable for traveling with babies or toddlers, and you can even [hire a babysitter](/en/blog/bali-family-travel-guide-2/).

**2. Do restaurants in Bali offer kids' meals?**

Many restaurants offer kids' meals and family-friendly seating.

**3. Do I need to bring a stroller to Bali?**

If your child is small, it's recommended to bring a lightweight stroller; however, some areas have uneven roads, so a baby carrier may be needed.

**4. What are the medical facilities like in Bali?**

Major cities like Kuta, Seminyak, and Sanur have international hospitals that can handle travel emergencies.
