# Bali Travel Hot Topics + News Roundup (July 26, 2026)

This week's updates cover international routes, tourist attractions, traveler rights, and shopping and leisure. Here are three stories most relevant to visitors.

***

## ✈️ China Eastern Airlines Launches Direct Beijing-Bali Flights, Making Travel More Convenient for Chinese Visitors

China Eastern Airlines has officially launched a direct route from Beijing Daxing International Airport to Bali's Ngurah Rai International Airport, operating three flights per week. This is the first direct flight connection between Beijing and Bali. The new route enhances convenience for Chinese travelers heading to Bali and is expected to boost international visitor numbers.

**Traveler Tip:** If you're traveling from China to Bali, keep an eye on direct flights from Beijing to save on transit time. Travelers from other regions can also expect more flight options during peak seasons.

***

## ⚠️ Canggu Expat Running Club Accused of Restricting Indonesian Participation, Immigration Launches Investigation

A Canggu-based running club, primarily made up of expats, has come under fire for allegedly restricting Indonesian locals from joining its events, sparking accusations of discrimination. After the controversy went viral on social media, Bali's immigration office launched an investigation to determine whether the activities are legal and whether they violate any regulations.

**Traveler Tip:** When joining running clubs, yoga sessions, fitness groups, or other community activities in Bali, it's best to choose events that are open, legal, and inclusive to all visitors. This helps avoid any unpleasant surprises that could affect your travel experience.

***

## 🏆 ICON Bali Wins World Real Estate Silver Award

Located in Sanur, the **ICON Bali** shopping center has won the **FIABCI World Prix d'Excellence Awards 2026** World Silver Award, making it one of Indonesia's most notable commercial developments this year. Combining shopping, dining, ocean views, and cultural events, the mall has become a new landmark in Sanur in recent years.
