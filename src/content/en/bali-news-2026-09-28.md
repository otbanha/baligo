# Key Topics and News for Bali Travelers | September 28, 2026

## 

## 🌊 Nusa Dua Adds Coastal Protection Structures, Bukit Peninsula Shoreline Safeguarded

Nusa Dua today announced a longer-term but noteworthy engineering project.

ITDC, which manages The Nusa Dua tourist area, is currently **building two groins (jetties)** aimed at reducing coastal erosion and protecting the shoreline.

In addition to the coastal protection project, Nusa Dua is also simultaneously working on:

- Road improvements around the Bukit Peninsula
- Waterblow entrance renovations
- Road upgrades on Nusa Dharma Island
- Sewage and rainwater infiltration system upgrades
- Overall environmental improvement of the tourist area

As of the first week of September, overall infrastructure construction progress has reached **58.53%**.

This project won't significantly impact short-term travel itineraries, but if you're staying in Nusa Dua soon, be aware that some areas may have ongoing construction.

In the long run, the focus of such projects isn't just about 'beautifying the scenic area,' but also includes **protecting the coastline, Waterblow, and key tourist facilities like the Bukit Peninsula**.

## 🛣️ Batur–Kintamani Road Has Potholes and Cracks; Repairs Expected to Start by October at the Earliest

If you have a day trip planned to **Kintamani, Mount Batur, or Lake Batur (Kintamani Caldera Lake)**, this news is worth noting.

Currently, sections of the road from **Jalan Batur all the way to Jalan Raya Kintamani** have developed potholes and asphalt cracks, especially near **Pura Ulun Danu Batur**, where visible road damage can be seen.

The good news is that the repair contract for this road has already been tendered and signed, with work expected to begin as early as:

**October to November 2026**

Construction has begun.

Currently, road conditions in the main tourist area of Kintamani are relatively good, with issues concentrated on certain sections leading into the Batur area. So if you're heading to Batur by private car soon, you usually don't need to cancel your trip, but **once construction starts, there may be local traffic controls or increased travel time**, so it's worth checking again then.

## ☀️ Dry weather continues until the end of September, with rising temperatures in parts of Bali

***

Bali is still experiencing a relatively dry weather pattern.

BMKG indicates that **the dry atmospheric conditions are expected to persist until the end of September**, with maximum temperatures also rising in parts of Bali and Nusa Tenggara.

Although we're nearing October, **don't assume that just because it's late September, Bali will definitely start raining**.

Daytime temperatures can still be quite hot, and the dry weather also raises the risk of forest and land fires. Bali's BPBD has recorded multiple forest and land fires in the past week.

For travelers, **sun protection and staying hydrated remain important** when visiting outdoor attractions during the day.

## 🏝️ Tanah Lot prepares early for the year-end peak season, improving services and tourist facilities

***

## 🏝️ Tanah Lot Temple Prepares for Year-End Peak Season, Enhancing Services and Tourist Facilities

With about three months to go until the year-end travel peak season, **Tanah Lot** is already gearing up.

The management unit of the Tanah Lot tourist area is currently tidying up services and facilities, preparing in advance for the long holidays and travel peak at the end of 2026.

Current preparations include improvements to the tourist area's environment, visitor flow, and related service facilities, aiming to complete them before the influx of travelers at year-end.

This is also a noteworthy signal:

**Bali's year-end peak season has actually already entered its preparation phase.**

If you're planning a trip to Bali for Christmas, New Year's, or the New Year holiday, you can start paying attention over the next few weeks to:

**hotel prices, crowd levels at popular attractions, and bookings for private drivers and restaurants.**

Especially in hotspots like Tanah Lot, Ubud, Canggu, Seminyak, and the southern beaches, year-end travel times simply can't be estimated based on regular days.
