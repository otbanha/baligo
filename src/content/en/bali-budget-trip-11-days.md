At 6 PM, we checked into our hotel in Ubud, officially starting this journey. Bali's night brims with tropical vibes, the air carrying a faint floral scent that instantly brings relaxation and joy. ## **Day 2 (Thursday, January 2) — Jungle Exploration and White-Water Rafting Adventure**

### **ATV Off-Roading and Rafting Experience**

- **Meeting Time**: 9:15 AM
- **Why We Recommend It**: Perfect for adventure-loving travelers!

In the morning, we drove ATVs through jungles, rice paddies, and streams. The scenery along the way was stunning, and the instructors patiently guided us on safety, making it easy even for beginners to get the hang of it. Next, we headed to the river for about 2 hours of rafting. Wear light clothing or just a swimsuit—you'll definitely get wet! During the rafting, the lush tropical rainforest on both sides of the crystal-clear river was a feast for the eyes, with occasional monkeys leaping through the trees, a delightful surprise.

## **Day 5 (January 5, Sunday) — Bali Safari & Marine Park**

### **Bali Safari Zoo**

  **Ticket Cost**: USD$98 per person (paid by card)
  **Itinerary**: Departed at 9:00 AM, returned at 4:00 PM

![野生動物園](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_67233c741fc10e5b047c021c1c9298ee.jpg)

Safari Park

Bali Safari Park is a fantastic destination for families and animal lovers, and we spent the whole day exploring the grounds. Besides seeing large animals like elephants and lions, there are plenty of interactive activities, such as feeding giraffes and taking photos with tiger cubs. The park's safari tram ride was especially thrilling, allowing us to observe various animals up close.

If you're traveling with kids, I highly recommend this itinerary—they'll have a blast. The dining options at the park are also decent; we had lunch there before continuing our adventure, only reluctantly heading back to the hotel at 4:00 PM.

### **Ulun Danu Beratan Temple**

  **Entrance Fee**: 150,000 IDR
  **Drive Time**: About 1 hour 30 minutes from Ubud hotel

Ulun Danu Beratan Temple / Super soft and adorable civet cats

Ulun Danu Beratan Temple is a must-visit attraction. Nestled by the lake, the temple reflects off the crystal-clear waters, creating a picturesque, almost fairy-tale-like scene. Here, we also got to hold and feed adorable civet cats—perfect for photos with family or friends. You can also sample civet coffee on-site, priced at around 71,500 IDR per cup, with a rich, distinctive aroma that’s an unmissable experience!

## **Day 8 (January 8, Wednesday) — Water Sports Adventure at Tanjung Benoa**

### **Tanjung Benoa Water Activities**

  **Cost**: 2,700,000 Indonesian Rupiah

On this day, we headed to Tanjung Benoa for water activities, including snorkeling, canoeing, and banana boat rides. A key tip: we didn't book tickets in advance on Klook, which led to much higher prices on-site—a small regret of the trip. We chose **Flying Fish** and **Parasailing**, costing us a total of 2,700,000 IDR (about USD$174 for two)...

If you plan to join water activities at Tanjung Benoa, don't make the same mistake I did by not researching beforehand—I ended up paying several times more! **Be sure to book online in advance** to save a significant amount of money and avoid being overcharged on-site.
