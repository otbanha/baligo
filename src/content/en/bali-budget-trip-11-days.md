## Detailed Itinerary

## **Day 1 (Jan 1, Wed) — Arrival in Bali, Vacation Mode On**

At 6 PM, we checked into our Ubud hotel, officially kicking off the trip. Bali's night brims with tropical vibes, the air carrying a faint floral scent that instantly relaxes and delights. After dropping our bags, we headed to the lively streets for dinner, with plenty of restaurant options. We turned in early to rest up for the next day's adventures.

## **Day 2 (Jan 2, Thu) — Jungle Exploration and Whitewater Rafting**

### **ATV Ride and Rafting Adventure**

- **Meeting Time**: 9:15 AM
- **Why We Recommend It**: Perfect for adventure lovers!

In the morning, we drove ATVs through jungles, rice paddies, and streams. The scenery was stunning, and the guides patiently gave safety instructions—even beginners got the hang of it quickly. Next, we headed to the river for about 2 hours of rafting. Wear light clothes or a swimsuit, as you'll definitely get wet! Along the way, the tropical rainforest on both sides of the blue river was mesmerizing, and we even spotted monkeys leaping in the trees—a delightful surprise.

## **Day 5 (Sunday, January 5) — Bali Safari & Marine Park**

### **Bali Safari Zoo**

**Admission Fee**: USD$98 per person (paid by card)
**Schedule**: Departed at 9 AM, returned at 4 PM

![野生動物園](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_67233c741fc10e5b047c021c1c9298ee.jpg)

Safari Park

Bali Safari & Marine Park is a fantastic attraction for families and animal lovers—we spent the whole day exploring the park. Besides seeing large animals like elephants and lions, there are plenty of interactive activities, such as feeding giraffes and taking photos with tiger cubs. The park's safari tram ride, in particular, offers an up-close view of various animals, making for an incredibly thrilling experience.

If you're traveling with kids, I highly recommend this itinerary—they'll have an absolute blast. The zoo also has decent dining options; we had lunch there and continued exploring until 4 PM before reluctantly heading back to the hotel to rest.

## **Day 6 (Monday, January 6) — Nature & Culture Tour: Ulun Danu Beratan Temple, Tirta Empul Temple & Tegallalang Rice Terraces**

### **Ulun Danu Beratan Temple**

  **Entrance Fee**: 150,000 IDR
  **Drive Time**: About 1 hour 30 minutes from Ubud hotel

![水神廟 / 超軟超可愛的麝香貓](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_677e8fafd966063cdb53b173ed3256d0.jpg)

Ulun Danu Beratan Temple / Super soft and adorable civet cats

Ulun Danu Beratan Temple is a must-visit attraction. Nestled by the lake, the temple reflects off the crystal-clear waters, creating a picturesque scene that feels like stepping into a fairy tale. Here, we also got to hold and feed civet cats for photos—these little creatures are incredibly cute and perfect for memorable shots with family or friends. You can also sample civet coffee on-site, priced at around 71,500 IDR per cup, with a rich aroma that's truly unique—an experience not to be missed!

### **Tirta Empul Temple**

  **Entrance Fee**: 100,000 IDR

Tirta Empul is a renowned religious site in Bali, where many locals come for purification rituals. Although we didn't participate in the holy water bathing, just watching the ceremonies gave us a profound sense of spirituality. If you're particularly interested in religious culture, it's worth a visit; if time is limited, you can decide based on your schedule.

## **Day 8 (Wednesday, January 8) — South Bay Water Sports Adventure**

### **South Bay Water Sports**

**Cost**: 2,700,000 Indonesian Rupiah

On this day, we headed to South Bay for water sports, including snorkeling, canoeing, and banana boat rides. A key note: we didn't book tickets in advance on Klook, which resulted in much higher on-site prices—a small regret of the trip. We chose **Flying Fish** and **Parasailing**, and the total for the two of us came to 2,700,000 IDR (approximately USD$87 for two)...

If you're planning to join water activities in Tanjung Benoa, don't make the same mistake I did by not researching in advance—I ended up paying several times more! **Be sure to book tickets online beforehand**. Not only will you save a lot of money, but you'll also avoid being treated like a pushover on the spot.
