## Avoid Peak Travel Season

First, let's start with choosing your travel dates! Try to avoid visiting Bali during peak season, when flights, accommodation, and activity prices typically skyrocket. Opting for the off-season or less busy periods not only saves you money on flights and lodging but also lets you enjoy a more peaceful travel experience.

So when is Bali's peak travel season? Generally, the dry season with lower rainfall—roughly April to September—is the best time to visit Bali, making it the peak season. Wondering about the downsides of avoiding peak season? [Check here](/en/blog/bali-best-time-to-visit/) to learn about Bali's rainy season.

Beyond seasonal distinctions, these five periods also see crowds flooding into Bali, ranked by the volume of people and traffic:

1. Indonesia's Eid al-Fitr holiday is a significant occasion. During this roughly ten-day break, a large number of domestic Indonesian tourists flock to this paradise island for a vacation. It's also the peak season with the highest crowds in Bali!

2. The Christmas-New Year holiday period typically attracts travelers from around the world, as it's a long break for most Western countries, and Indonesians also try to take time off. Therefore, the Christmas-New Year holiday ranks as the second busiest period for crowds and traffic. Planning and booking your accommodation early can secure better rates. Many hotels and websites offer early booking discounts, sometimes saving you up to 30%.

## **Subscribe to Promotional Emails**

Subscribe to email notifications from hotels and booking websites to stay updated on the latest promotions and special deals. They sometimes offer limited-time discounts, allowing you to book rooms at more favorable rates.

## **Pay in Local Currency**

When paying for your hotel room with a credit card, always choose to pay in the local currency. This helps you avoid unnecessary currency conversion fees, allowing you to save as much money as possible.
