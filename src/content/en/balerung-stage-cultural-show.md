If you want to deeply experience Bali's traditional culture, the performance at Balerung Stage is an absolute must-see! This traditional stage in Ubud features classic Balinese dance and music weekly, allowing visitors to connect with the island's profound cultural legacy. Unlike commercialized tourist shows, Balerung Stage is renowned for its authenticity and pure artistic expression, making you feel as if you've stepped back into Bali's past, captivated by the charm of dance and music.

### Jason's Deep Cultural Experience

[Jason Indonesia](https://bit.ly/youtube-jason)'s school flew from Jakarta to Bali on September 17 for a four-day, three-night cultural field trip. The itinerary included an in-depth cultural immersion, allowing students to explore Bali's art and traditions. Early on September 18, they arrived at Balerung Stage in Ubud for a workshop on Gamelan music and Legong dance. This rare opportunity offered a precious cultural experience that typical tourists rarely get to enjoy! These music and dance forms are steeped in history and cultural depth, providing students with sensory and spiritual inspiration beyond the classroom. Watch their workshop below to learn more about this invaluable journey.

### A Feast for the Senses

Sitting in the audience at Balerung Stage, you immediately feel a strong cultural atmosphere. One viewer shared: 'It was a feast for the eyes and ears—the dance was breathtakingly beautiful, and the Gamelan gong orchestra's music was irresistible, making me feel like I had traveled back in time to Bali's past.' She mentioned booking via WhatsApp, and the theater replied within five minutes, arranging front-row center seats so she could enjoy the spectacular performance up close.

Whether it's the enchanting Legong dance or the Barong dance symbolizing the struggle between good and evil, the dancers' intricate gestures and graceful movements keep your eyes glued to the stage. These performances not only showcase dance artistry but also offer a spiritual journey.

### An Unforgettable Cultural Experience

Another audience member noted that they attended the 'Authentic Peliatan Legong & Barong' performance and praised the dancers' elegant movements and the deep ambiance of the Gamelan music. This show not only introduced them to traditional Balinese culture but also made the entire evening truly special.

### An Indoor Stage Perfect for Rainy Days

Balerung Stage's indoor design makes it especially suitable for watching performances during the rainy season. One audience member noted, 'When it was pouring outside, we enjoyed a fantastic show in the comfort of the indoors.' This is definitely a plus for anyone planning to visit Bali during the rainy season.

### Exceptional Value for the Price

You might be surprised that such a high-quality performance costs only USD$5. Whether you're a culture enthusiast or just looking for a special evening activity, the show at Balerung Stage is absolutely worth seeing.

Address: [Kompleks Puri Guruh Sindang Paridesa Br Teruna Peliatan, Ubud 90571](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1jrOaKVOZ4jpcB_kprr67w3ltg3WPbf4&usp=sharing)

Performed by renowned Peliatan dancers and musicians every Friday at 7:30 PM

If you're planning a trip to Bali, be sure to experience Balerung Stage for yourself. Every performance here is steeped in rich history and tradition, allowing you to truly feel the essence of Balinese culture!
