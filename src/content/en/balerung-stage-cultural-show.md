If you want to deeply experience Bali's traditional culture, the performance at Balerung Stage is an absolute must-see! Located in Ubud, this traditional stage hosts classic Balinese dance and music every week, allowing audiences to connect with the island's rich cultural heritage. Unlike commercialized tourist shows, Balerung Stage is renowned for its authenticity and pure artistic expression, making you feel as if you've stepped back into Bali's past, captivated by the charm of dance and music.

### Jason's Deep Cultural Experience

[Jason Indonesia](https://bit.ly/youtube-jason)'s school flew from Jakarta to Bali on September 17 for a four-day, three-night cultural field trip. This activity was specially arranged for an in-depth cultural immersion, allowing students to explore Bali's art and traditions. Early on September 18, they arrived at Balerung Stage in Ubud for a workshop on Gamelan music and Legong dance. It was a rare opportunity—a precious cultural experience that typical tourists can hardly access! These music and dance forms are steeped in rich history and cultural depth, offering students sensory and spiritual inspiration beyond classroom learning. You can watch their workshop process below to learn more about this invaluable experience.

### A Feast for the Senses

Sitting in the audience at Balerung Stage, you immediately feel a strong cultural atmosphere. One audience member shared: 'It was a feast for the eyes and ears—the dance was breathtakingly beautiful, and the Gamelan gong orchestra's music was irresistible. I felt like I had traveled back in time to Bali's past.' She mentioned that she booked via WhatsApp, and the theater replied within five minutes, arranging front-row center seats so she could enjoy the stunning performance up close.

Whether it's the enchanting Legong dance or the Barong dance symbolizing the struggle between good and evil, the dancers' intricate gestures and graceful movements make it impossible to look away. These performances not only showcase dance artistry but also offer a spiritual journey.

### An Unforgettable Cultural Experience

Another audience member also mentioned attending the 'Authentic Peliatan Legong & Barong' performance, praising the dancers' elegant movements and the deep ambiance of the Gamelan music. The show not only introduced them to traditional Balinese culture but also made the entire evening truly special.

### An Indoor Stage Perfect for Rainy Days

Balerung Stage's indoor design makes it especially suitable for watching performances during the rainy season. One audience member noted, 'When it was pouring outside, we enjoyed a fantastic show in the comfort of the indoors.' This is definitely a plus for those planning to visit Bali during the rainy season.
