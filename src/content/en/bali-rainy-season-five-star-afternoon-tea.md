# Don't Let Bali's Rainy Season Ruin Your Trip! 5-Star Hotel Afternoon Tea Recommendations for Elegant Indoor Moments During Afternoon Showers

We covered this in another article: [Bali's Rainy Season Delayed! Southern Region Starts in Mid-December, Don't Mistake Early November Downpours!](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-rainy-season-2026-delayed/) The southern area (Denpasar, Kuta, Canggu, Uluwatu) is expected to officially enter the rainy season between December 11 and 20. If you encounter a few sudden, heavy downpours in Bali right now, they're more likely localized convection during the seasonal transition, not the start of the rainy season.

The main reason for the delay is a strong El Niño phenomenon, pushing Indonesia's rainy season about 20 to 30 days later than usual across the country.

## Once the Rainy Season Arrives, Rain Will Be More Concentrated

Meteorological agencies predict that 18 out of 20 regions across the island will have a shorter rainy season this year, with rainfall potentially more concentrated and individual storms possibly stronger. February 2027 is expected to be the wettest month island-wide.

In other words, the weather around December may be more stable than usual, but once the rainy season kicks in, it could bring consecutive heavy rains. Keeping your itinerary flexible will make things much easier.

## Afternoon Showers Are Perfect for Afternoon Tea

During the rainy season, showers often hit in the afternoon or evening. So the trick is simple: **pack your mornings with outdoor activities, and when the rain comes in the afternoon, switch to an indoor culinary treat.**

And Bali has no shortage of places that turn an afternoon downpour into pure pleasure.

## Nusa Dua｜Selasar Deli at The Apurva Kempinski Bali: An Afternoon in a Soaring Wooden Lobby

The Apurva Kempinski Bali in Nusa Dua is a resort built into the hillside, where afternoon tea is served at Selasar Deli. The lobby and dining area feature a soaring wooden structure adorned with Balinese carvings, with a central pond stocked with koi fish—just walking in sets the mood. Weekend afternoon tea offers unlimited sweet and savory bites over three hours, including coffee, tea, and juices. Our author recommends the truffle cheese sandwich, Indonesian satay skewers, coconut mousse, and mango passion fruit tart. If you move to the terrace seating, you can also enjoy layered pools, tropical gardens, and views of the Indian Ocean.

## How to Arrange a More Relaxed Rainy-Season Afternoon Tea

- Schedule outdoor activities in the morning and afternoon tea in the afternoon, so you're prepared for any weather.
- Popular hotel afternoon teas and Sunday brunches should be booked in advance.
- Check the weather forecast before heading out during the rainy season, and allow extra travel time.
- Pair afternoon tea and attractions in the same area to avoid traveling far in the rain.
