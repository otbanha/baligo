# Bali Travel Hot Topics & News Roundup (August 1, 2026)

As the summer travel peak continues, travelers should keep an eye on new attractions and tourism developments, as well as Indonesian policy changes and news about Bali's new airport.

## 🛍️ Bali's First Premium Outlet Opens, Adding a New Landmark to the Kura Kura Bali Special Economic Zone

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbVpAngh5Vm/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

Located in the **Kura Kura Bali Special Economic Zone (KEK Kura Kura Bali)** , **Sira Village Grand Outlet Bali** officially opened on **July 31**, becoming Bali's first large-scale premium outlet. The mall brings together over **100 international and well-known brands**, offering year-round outlet discounts. Officials state that prices are on average about **30% cheaper** than regular shopping malls.

Beyond shopping, the complex integrates Balinese architecture, dining options, art and cultural spaces, and seaside views. Travelers can also check flight status via the mall's **Live Flight Information** display, allowing them to shop, dine, and enjoy sunset views all in one place. Officials estimate the outlet will create over **1,000 jobs**, further boosting tourism in the Kura Kura Bali SEZ and surrounding areas.

**Traveler tip:** Consider setting aside half a day to visit Sira Village for shopping. Besides year-round discounted goods, you can also enjoy the sea views and sunset, making it a new shopping and leisure destination.

## ✈️ North Bali International Airport Plan Accelerated, Now Part of National Development Blueprint

The Indonesian government has once again voiced support for accelerating the construction of North Bali International Airport. After hearing the latest progress report from developer PT Pembangunan Bali Mandiri (Pembari), President Prabowo Subianto reaffirmed his commitment to pushing forward this major project. The plan has been officially included in the 2025–2029 National Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMN), and will become Bali's second international airport.

According to the plan, the new airport will be capable of handling large wide-body aircraft such as the Boeing 777 and Airbus A380. It will be funded entirely by private investment, without using central or local government budgets. Officials hope this will boost Bali's aviation capacity and drive tourism and economic growth in Buleleng Regency, as well as the northern, western, and eastern regions.

**Traveler Tip:** The North Bali International Airport is still in the planning and promotion phase. For now, all international flights to Bali continue to use Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) as the main gateway, so there's no need to adjust existing itineraries—but it's worth keeping an eye on future construction timelines.

## 🛂 Indonesia Considers Limited Dual Citizenship for Overseas Talent and Professionals

The Indonesian government is considering amending the nationality law to allow limited dual citizenship for certain overseas Indonesian talents, professionals, and eligible individuals. For now, this is still in the legislative discussion phase and does not represent a full opening to all applicants. It has no impact on entry requirements for general foreign tourists.

**Traveler Tip:** Regular tourists can continue to enter under the current visa system without needing to adjust travel plans due to this policy discussion.

## ✈️ Bali Summer Flights Maintain High Load Factors—Check In Early Recommended

As summer winds down, Ngurah Rai International Airport continues to welcome a high volume of international travelers, with many routes maintaining strong load factors. Airlines remind passengers that check-in and security wait times may be longer than usual during peak season.

**Traveler Advice:** For international flights, it is recommended to arrive at the airport at least 3 hours early and complete online check-in in advance to save waiting time.

***

## 🌊 Water activities still require attention to weather changes; check ferry schedules before departing for island trips

Some areas may still experience brief showers and changing sea conditions recently. While most flights and speedboat operations remain unaffected, authorities remind the public to stay updated on the latest marine forecasts.

**Traveler Advice:** If you have plans to visit islands like Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or the Gili Islands, it's recommended to reconfirm ferry schedules with the company on the day of departure.

***

## ♻️ Bali continues to promote high-quality tourism, with public facility improvements gradually completed

In recent months, various projects including beach renovations, traffic improvements, and public facility upgrades have been steadily advancing, showing that Bali is gradually moving toward high-quality, sustainable tourism, aiming to provide travelers with a more comfortable and safer experience.

**Traveler Advice:** If you encounter local construction or traffic adjustments during your trip, please follow on-site instructions. Once completed, these improvements will help enhance the overall travel quality.
