# Bali Travel Hot Topics + News Roundup (August 1, 2026)

The summer travel peak season continues. Beyond new attractions and tourism infrastructure, recent Indonesian policy changes and news about Bali's new airport are also worth noting for travelers.

## 🛍️ Bali's First Premium Outlet Opens, Adding a New Landmark to the Turtle Island Special Economic Zone

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbVpAngh5Vm/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

Located in the **Kura Kura Bali Special Economic Zone (KEK Kura Kura Bali)**, **Sira Village Grand Outlet Bali** officially opened on **July 31**, becoming Bali's first large-scale premium outlet. The mall brings together over **100 international and well-known brands**, offering year-round outlet discounts. Officials say prices are on average about **30% cheaper** than regular shopping malls.

Beyond shopping, the complex integrates Balinese architecture, gourmet restaurants, arts and cultural spaces, and seaside views. Travelers can also check flight status via the mall's **Live Flight Information** system, allowing them to shop, dine, and enjoy the sunset all in one place. Officials estimate the outlet will create over **1,000 jobs** and further boost tourism development in the Turtle Island Special Economic Zone and surrounding areas.

**Traveler Tip:** Consider setting aside half a day to visit Sira Village for shopping. Besides year-round discounted goods, you can also take in the sea views and sunset, making it a new shopping and leisure destination.

## ✈️ North Bali International Airport Plan Accelerates, Now Included in National Development Plan

The Indonesian government has once again expressed support for accelerating the construction of the North Bali International Airport. After hearing the latest progress report from developer PT Pembangunan Bali Mandiri (Pembari), President Prabowo Subianto reaffirmed his commitment to pushing forward with this major project. The plan has been officially included in the 2025–2029 National Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMN) and will become Bali's second international airport.

According to the plan, the new airport will be capable of handling large wide-body aircraft such as the Boeing 777 and Airbus A380. It will be funded by private investment, without using central or regional government budgets. Officials hope this will boost Bali's aviation capacity and drive tourism and economic growth in Buleleng Regency, as well as the northern, western, and eastern regions of the island.

**Traveler Advice:** The North Bali International Airport is still in the planning and promotion phase. For now, all international flights to Bali continue to use Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) as the main gateway, so there is no need to adjust existing itineraries. However, it's worth keeping an eye on future construction timelines.

## 🛂 Indonesia Considers Limited Dual Citizenship for Overseas Talent and Professionals

The Indonesian government is considering amending its nationality law to potentially allow limited dual citizenship for certain overseas Indonesian talents, professionals, and eligible individuals. This is still in the discussion phase and is not a full opening for all applicants. It has no impact on entry requirements for general foreign tourists.

**Traveler Advice:** Regular tourists can continue to enter under the current visa system without needing to adjust travel plans due to this policy discussion.

## ✈️ Bali Summer Flights Maintain High Load Factors, Early Check-In Recommended

As summer draws to a close, Ngurah Rai International Airport continues to see a high volume of international travelers, with many routes maintaining strong load factors. Airlines remind passengers that check-in and security wait times may be longer than usual during peak season.

**Traveler Advice:** For international flights, it's recommended to arrive at the airport at least 3 hours early and complete online check-in in advance to save time at the gate.

***

## 🌊 Sea activities still require attention to weather changes; for island trips, it's recommended to confirm ferry schedules before departure

Recent localized areas may still experience brief showers and changing sea conditions. While most flights and speedboat operations remain unaffected, officials remind travelers to stay updated on the latest marine weather information.

**Traveler Advice:** If you're planning trips to outlying islands like Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, or the Gili Islands, it's recommended to reconfirm ferry schedules with the boat company on the day of departure.

***

## ♻️ Bali continues to promote high-quality tourism, with public facility improvements gradually completed

In recent months, various projects including beach renovations, traffic improvements, and public facility upgrades have been steadily advancing, signaling Bali's gradual shift toward high-quality, sustainable tourism, aiming to offer travelers a more comfortable and safer experience.

**Traveler Advice:** If you encounter localized construction or traffic adjustments during your trip, please follow on-site instructions. Once completed, these improvements will help enhance the overall travel quality.
