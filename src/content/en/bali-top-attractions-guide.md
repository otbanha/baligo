To make your Bali trip flow seamlessly, we've grouped 20 must-visit spots into five regions: Central Ubud, Southern Coast, Eastern Culture, Northern Mountains, and Offshore Islands. ## 1. The Heart of Culture & Spirituality: Central Ubud & Surroundings (Ubud & Central)

This region is Bali's cultural birthplace, filled with tropical rainforests, emerald rice terraces, waterfalls, and ancient temples—perfect for travelers seeking soul cleansing and artistic vibes.

### 1. Tegallalang Rice Terraces

https://youtu.be/op78Dk8CjL4

Layered green lines recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage site, stunning like a painting under sunlight. Stroll along the paddies and don't miss the iconic 'sky swing'—feel your dress flutter in the tropical breeze for ultimate romance.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ 'The scenery is beautiful, with impressive layered rice terraces. There are many sky swings to try, great for photos. But the steps are steep—walking down and back up requires some stamina.'

[> 8 Best Jungle Swing Recommendations: Dress Rental Tips, Photo Angles, and Crowd Avoidance Guide](/en/blog/ubud-jungle-swings-photo-guide/)

### 2. Tirta Empul Temple (Pura Tirta Empul)

https://youtube.com/shorts/fiYs5GKdINU

The underground springs here have been flowing for centuries. Locals and travelers from around the world queue to step into the pools, placing their heads under the water cascading from carved stone spouts to wash away impurities—a deeply ritualistic and spiritual cleansing experience.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ "A very sacred and energetic place! Waiting in line for the purification ritual takes about an hour. The spring water is incredibly cold and clear, and after washing, my restless mind truly calmed down. Remember to follow the rules and wear a sarong when entering."

> [Complete Guide to Tirta Empul Temple in Bali: The Thousand-Year Purification Ritual, Taboos, and Must-See Highlights](/en/blog/tirta-empul-temple-guide/)

### 3. Ubud Palace & Market

https://youtube.com/shorts/s16gdTOjonU

Located in the cultural heart of Bali, Ubud Palace showcases exquisite stone carvings, while just across the street lies the bustling Ubud Market. Filled with island-style woven bags, handmade silver jewelry, and wood carvings, it's a paradise for artsy souls and fashion lovers to hunt for treasures.

https://youtube.com/shorts/Oh-RNfjhQhM

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ "The palace is exquisite and compact, with free entry. The market across the street is fantastic to explore—rattan bags, wood carvings, and accessories galore. But you must haggle starting from 30% of the asking price; the bargaining itself is part of the fun!"

> [Ubud Palace: A Gem of Balinese Culture and History | Opening Hours, Tickets, Performance Times, Nearby Attractions, and Day Trip Recommendations](/en/blog/ubud-royal-palace-tour-guide/)

### 4. Penglipuran Traditional Village

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_1c121f8a31678b02905c23f897ac7235.jpg)

This ancient settlement has perfectly preserved its traditional architectural layout and way of life. With neatly arranged bamboo houses and exquisite garden landscapes, walking through the car-free streets feels like time has stood still.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ "The entire village is very clean and tidy, with unique traditional bamboo houses. The villagers are friendly and even invite you into their courtyards for a visit. Highly recommended for experiencing authentic Balinese traditional culture."

> [【Bali Cultural Attractions】Penglipuran Village Guide: Step into the Cleanest and Most Ancient Village on the Island and Its Bamboo Forest Secret Spot](/en/blog/penglipuran-village-bali-experience/)

### 5. Ayung River

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e76f3c320362392046cda14d6de1b379.jpg)

Head to Ubud's Ayung River for an exhilarating white-water rafting adventure! The river stretches over ten kilometers, flanked by pristine tropical rainforests, mysterious stone carvings, and dramatic canyon waterfalls—a perfect way to get up close with nature.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ "White-water rafting was super fun! The tropical rainforest scenery along the way was as beautiful as Jurassic Park, and we even saw stone carvings on the cliffs. The guide was humorous and entertaining. My arms were a bit sore after paddling, but it was totally worth it!"

> [【Bali Rafting FAQ】Can you go if you can't swim? Age limits, safety tips, and a full comparison of Ayung River vs. Telaga Waja River](/en/blog/bali-rafting-ayung-telaga-waja/)

### 6. Tegenungan Waterfall

https://youtube.com/shorts/BBIXmPYUPZo

Hidden amidst lush greenery, this is the closest and most accessible massive waterfall to Ubud city center. A torrent of water cascades down from a great height, sending up a misty spray. The area features several stylish viewing platforms and Instagram-worthy photo spots.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ "Not too far from Ubud city center. The waterfall has an incredibly powerful flow—very impressive! The path is well-maintained, with lots of art installations perfect for Instagram photos. It only takes about 10 minutes to walk down, making it ideal for those who don't want a long hike."

> [Waterfall Bar in Ubud, Bali: OMMA Dayclub – Embrace the Chill Vibes and Leisurely Moments Under the Sun](/en/blog/ubud-omma-dayclub-tegenungan-waterfall/)

## 2. Chic Beaches & Sunset Luxury: South Bali

This area is home to the island's most exclusive beach clubs, trendy boutiques, and breathtaking clifftop sunsets—the ultimate hub for experiencing luxurious island nightlife.

### 7. Tanah Lot Temple

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_36a21198d37ccb139f68e96a327f9b33.jpg)

Perched on a massive coastal rock, this temple becomes isolated amid surging waves during high tide. As the sun sets, golden light spills across the sea, outlining the temple's mystical silhouette.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ “I suggest arriving around 5 PM to catch both the pre-sunset and post-sunset magic hours. At low tide, you can walk down to the temple’s base to touch the holy water. The sunset here is an absolute must-see once in a lifetime!”

> [Detailed guide to Tanah Lot, including tides and nearby attractions](/en/blog/bali-tanah-lot-guide/)

### 8. Uluwatu Temple

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_cbc08e1711f806a3dc723f3ad3f4e928.jpg)

Perched on a steep cliff 70 meters high, this temple offers sweeping views of the Indian Ocean. In the late afternoon, you can watch the Kecak Dance performed by dozens of dancers using only vocal harmonies, set against a sunset backdrop in an open-air amphitheater.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ “The temple built on the cliff is incredibly majestic, with unbeatable sunset ocean views. The evening Kecak dance is breathtaking, performed entirely with vocal harmonies. Watch out for the aggressive monkeys here—they’ll snatch glasses and hats, so keep your belongings secure.”

> [Bali Uluwatu Temple Travel Guide: Must-See Highlights and Practical Tips](/en/blog/uluwatu-temple-travel-guide/)

### 9. Seminyak

This area is home to the island’s most stylish concept stores, high-end spas, and several famous beach clubs. In the evening, lounge on bean bags on the sand, sip a cocktail, and listen to DJ sets, enjoying the ultimate island luxury.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ “A super trendy area! Plenty of chic curated shops and top-notch Instagram-worthy cafes. At night, head to a beach club to listen to DJs and watch the sunset—it’s pure vacation vibes.”

{{block:smk}}

### 10. Canggu

Canggu, which has skyrocketed in popularity in recent years, is a hub for digital nomads and Western surfers. Its lifestyle is lazily charming, filled with Instagram-worthy organic, vegan brunch spots and free-spirited souls.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ "A paradise for surfers and digital nomads, with a very laid-back and free atmosphere. It's full of healthy, organic brunch spots. Renting a scooter to zip through the alleys makes you feel as relaxed as a local."

{{block:canggu}}

### 11. Jimbaran Beach

Renowned for having one of the 'world's most beautiful sunsets.' As night falls, the entire beach is set with tables, chairs, and twinkling candlelight. You can walk barefoot on the sand, enjoying freshly grilled seafood BBQ while watching the magical purple and red hues paint the sky.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ "Enjoying the sea breeze and charcoal-grilled seafood BBQ with candlelight on the table while watching the sunset—the atmosphere is incredibly romantic. Although the seafood prices are a bit high, it's totally worth it for the unbeatable sunset views and ambiance!"

{{block:jim}}

### 12. Kuta Beach

As the earliest developed and most famous beach, Kuta features gentle, consistent waves, making it a globally recognized paradise for beginner surfers. The beach is lined with large shopping malls and countless food stalls, creating a vibrant and bustling atmosphere.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ "The beach is long with soft sand, and the waves are perfect for surfing beginners. There are many instructors along the roadside offering one-on-one lessons. The surrounding area is very lively with great amenities, making shopping and eating very convenient."

{{block:kuta}}

## 3. Sacred Ruins & Royal Aesthetics: The Eastern Karangasem Region (East Bali)

Far from the crowds, the eastern part of the island boasts the most sacred volcanic-backdrop architecture and exquisite royal water palaces, making it a paradise for photography enthusiasts.

### 13. Pura Lempuyang (Gates of Heaven)

https://youtu.be/H-IocbLNpE4

Located at Pura Lempuyang, the Gates of Heaven perfectly frame the distant Mount Agung between two massive split gates. Through the photographer's masterful use of reflective mirrors, it feels as if you've stepped into a magical realm where the gods reside.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ “The scenery is absolutely stunning! But be prepared—to get that classic reflection shot, we waited in line for a number and stood for nearly 3 hours. Luckily, the view of Mount Agung nearby was spectacular, making the wait worthwhile.”

### 14. Tirta Gangga Water Palace

https://youtu.be/ysUVgnICbg0

This water palace, originally built by a king, blends local and Chinese architectural aesthetics. A stone-carved walkway stretches across the pond, and as you stroll along it, schools of large, vibrantly colored koi fish swim around you, creating an ethereal atmosphere.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ “The water palace is incredibly beautiful, full of royal charm. The most fun part is buying a pack of fish food and feeding the koi while walking on the stone-carved path in the water. Huge, plump fish gather all around, making for stunning photos!”

> [【Bali East Tour Guide】Gates of Heaven, Tirta Gangga, Ujung Water Palace: A Complete Guide to Scenic Spots, Photos, and Transportation](/en/blog/bali-east-tour-guide/)

## 四、 Misty Lakes and Volcanic Views: Northern Mountain Secrets (North Bali & Kintamani)

Located in the high-altitude north, the climate is cool and pleasant, offering dramatic black volcanic lava flows, serene lakes, and picturesque misty temples.

### 15. Pura Ulun Danu Bratan (Ulun Danu Beratan Temple)

![15. 烏倫達努水神廟 Pura Ulun Danu Bratan](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-06/1781497269627-ulun-danu-bratan.webp)

Surrounded by ethereal mist and the serene Lake Bratan, when the water level rises, the temple appears to float gracefully on the surface. The cool climate and picturesque scenery create an otherworldly sense of tranquility.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ "Located in the mountains, the weather is cool and even a bit chilly. The temple on the lake appears and disappears in the mist, truly like a paradise on earth. It's completely different from the lively southern beaches—a place that brings you peace."

> [Bali Kintamani Mount Batur Lake Guide: Sunrise Hiking × Royal Boat × Lakeview Cafés × Mountain Playground × Hot Springs](/en/blog/batur-lake-volcano-guide/)

### 16. Kintamani / Mount Batur

https://youtu.be/mx2zwlUovew

The best vantage point to take in Mount Batur and Lake Batur. In recent years, many stylish viewpoint cafés have opened here, with large glass windows framing the dramatic black volcanic lava formations and the shimmering lake into a living painting.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ "The climate here is so cool, completely different from the heat in the south! Sitting in a viewpoint café, looking out at the black volcanic lava formations and the lake, the view is incredibly expansive. Highly recommend a visit."

> [Top 13 Volcano View Cafés & Restaurants in Kintamani, Bali 🌄☕](/en/blog/kintamani-volcano-cafes-restaurants/)

## 5. God's Lost Pearl: Nusa Islands & Surrounding Islands

Just a short speedboat ride from the south, these islands boast the most pristine, breathtaking cliffside ocean views and Tiffany-blue jelly-like seas on the entire island.

### 17. Kelingking Beach (Nusa Penida)

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_efc65c3d29b2aad1d70da8ac6ec61dd5.jpg)

Looking down from the cliff, the massive reef stretching into the sea resembles an adorable T-Rex. The Tiffany-blue waters blend with the pure white sand, creating a breathtakingly beautiful scene.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ "The view at T-Rex Bay is absolutely stunning! But if you want to go down the steep cliff to the beach, the path is very rugged and difficult—you must wear sneakers and stay hydrated. Just watching from the viewpoint is already worth it."

### 18. Diamond Beach (Nusa Penida)

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_ca4724a8eeb3ab1c2e10fa29582c9546.jpg)

Named after the diamond-shaped rock formation standing in the sea. The most stunning feature is the hand-carved white limestone staircase along the cliff, set against an endless deep blue gradient ocean view—every snapshot looks like a fashion shoot.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ "The white limestone staircase paired with the Tiffany-blue gradient sea view is breathtakingly beautiful! The stairs are a bit steep and the wind is strong, so be careful when taking photos. The massive diamond-shaped reef on the beach looks incredibly majestic."

### 19. Broken Beach & Angel's Billabong (Nusa Penida)

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_1531b3a5efc742043f3f9dad6e1bf6db.jpg)

Broken Beach is a massive circular natural sea arch. Just a few minutes' walk away, Angel's Billabong is a turquoise rock pool that resembles a natural infinity pool at low tide, showcasing the wonders of nature.

- _💬 Real Google Review:_ "Nature's masterpiece! The natural sea arch is incredibly spectacular. Next door, Angel's Billabong at low tide is as calm as a transparent infinity pool, revealing the rock textures underneath—absolutely gorgeous."

> [【Bali - Complete Nusa Penida Guide】Maps, Ferries, Accommodation, Transportation, Private Drivers, Activities, Photography, Attractions](/en/blog/nusa-penida-guide/)

### 20. Devil's Tears (Nusa Lembongan)

https://youtube.com/shorts/exdiGPLkVjw

Located on the offshore island of Nusa Lembongan, this is a curved cliff formed by long-term wave erosion. When the massive Indian Ocean waves crash against the rocks, they shoot up several meters of mist, often revealing a beautiful rainbow under the sunlight.

- _💬 Google Review:_ "When the big waves crash against the rock crevices, they kick up massive sprays and mist—the sound is absolutely thrilling on-site! If you're lucky, you might even spot a rainbow in the spray. But don't stand too close to the edge; safety first."

> [Nusa Lembongan & Ceningan Travel Guide: Explore Attractions, Food, and Transport on Bali's Nearby Paradise](/en/blog/nusa-lembongan-ceningan-guide/)
