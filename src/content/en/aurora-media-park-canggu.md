Canggu's newest attraction—**Aurora Media Park Bali**—is a landmark that blends nature with multimedia interactive technology. Located in the Nuanu Creative District of Tabanan Regency, this site offers an open-air forest experience stretching about 1 kilometer, filled with interactive light and sound games. It uses artificial intelligence (AI) to create an immersive multimedia environment. Visitors can explore roughly 5,000 square meters of natural forest, experiencing a stunning fusion of sight and sound while also finding spiritual inspiration—as if stepping into an entirely new dimension.

Aurora Park officially opened to the public in August 2024. According to Nuanu founder Sergey Solonin, every visitor to Aurora Park can deeply feel the connection between art, nature, and technology. He says, 'The immersive experience here sparks inspiration and wonder, and even after leaving, that feeling lingers in your heart.'

## Highlights:

**Gate (Entrance)**

As soon as visitors step through the entrance of Aurora Park, they feel the magic of digital interaction. Sensor technology automatically shoots white beams into the night sky, as if announcing their arrival to the universe.

**Penjor (Bamboo Lanterns)**  
Made of bamboo, these installations are inscribed with Indonesian phrases symbolizing light. The words and lights constantly shift colors, creating a dreamy atmosphere.

**Sundial**  
A sundial installation that 'weaves' light and shadow in a spiral pattern, artistically showcasing the passage of time. Laser and LED lights combine with contemplative music, allowing visitors to feel time flowing by.

**Bahasa Pillar (Language Pillar)**  
The pillar is inscribed with Indonesian poetry, with Sanskrit letters projected onto it, depicting a journey of light.

**Cone (Conical Installation)**  
An oval mirrored installation made of stainless steel, located in the middle of the river. Interactive light projections create dynamic ripples on the water's surface; when visitors walk across the bridge in front, sensor technology activates the installation.

**Wishes Circle**  
Six interactive mirrors surround flickering lights, allowing visitors to see their reflections accompanied by contemplative music.

**Helix Root**  
A rattan-woven installation combined with 3D spiral lighting, connecting the bridge to a striking sculpture in the forest.

**Crystal Oasis**  
This installation showcases glowing stalactites and crystals, shaped like the palm of Mother Earth. When visitors stand in front, the crystals and surrounding area begin to shimmer with multicolored lights, creating a magical interactive experience.

Aurora Park Bali is not just a nighttime attraction but an immersive space blending art, nature, and technology—perfect for travelers who love exploration and adventure.

Aurora Media Park tickets cost Rp. 165,000. The ticket includes:

- Entry to Nuanu Creative City and a guided tour of the park's interactive installations
- Experience of installations combining art, nature, and technology
- A 40-minute jungle trail exploration

Aurora Media Park is open daily from 7:00 PM, inviting visitors to explore its illuminated pathways and interactive art installations, which come alive after dark. The park also hosts various events and festivals, adding vibrancy to Nuanu's cultural scene. It's not only an ideal spot for nighttime exploration but also an immersive space perfect for adventurous travelers seeking a unique experience.
