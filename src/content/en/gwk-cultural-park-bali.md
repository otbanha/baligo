## Complete Guide to Bali's Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park

**Bottom Line Up Front**: GWK Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park is open daily from **09:00-21:00** (last entry at 20:00). The must-see is the **Kecak fire dance at Lotus Pond at 18:00 daily**, plus 15 traditional performances rotating across three venues from 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Located on the Bukit Ungasan in southern Bali, the park's limestone cliffs are popular photo spots.

Garuda Wisnu Kencana (GWK) Cultural Park sits on the Bukit Ungasan in southern Bali, a magnificent cultural landmark blending art, mythology, and natural scenery. It houses one of the world's tallest statues and is one of the best places to explore Balinese traditional culture and mythological stories.

## **The Mythological Story of Garuda Wisnu**

In Hindu mythology, Garuda is a divine bird symbolizing strength and loyalty, while Wisnu (Vishnu) is the preserver god among the Hindu trinity. Legend has it that Garuda, to rescue his mother enslaved by demon gods, fought against gods and demons, ultimately obtaining the secret of the 'water of life' (Amrita) and offering it to Wisnu. Deeply moved, Wisnu allowed Garuda to become his mount, and together they have since guarded the world, symbolizing justice and peace.

![Garuda Wisnu的神話故事簡介](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_8d4e8695ec97e45be528a94384b90527.jpg)

The statue at GWK Cultural Park captures the heroic pose of Garuda carrying Wisnu, embodying the Balinese people's reverence and perseverance for their faith and culture.

## **The Sculptor's Monumental Achievement**

Standing 122 meters tall, this statue was created by renowned Indonesian artist Nyoman Nuarta. He began designing it in 1990, and despite economic crises and countless obstacles, it was finally completed in 2018. The statue is assembled from over 1,500 components, weighing more than 4,000 tons in total. The giant Garuda Wisnu statue is made of copper, with its outer shell using over 25,000 square meters of copper, which was then shaped and welded with stainless steel. The copper statue also serves as a lightning rod.

The entire project took nearly 30 years and cost a total of USD$100 million. It is not only the tallest statue in Indonesia and the fourth tallest in the world, but also the tallest Hindu deity statue globally, embodying the artist's boundless passion and perseverance for cultural heritage.

## **GWK Cultural Park Tour Route**

Want to visit GWK Cultural Park but not sure about the tour route? Don't worry—here's a complete guide to exploring GWK Cultural Park.

**Getting to the Park** After arriving at the parking lot, visitors can take a free shuttle bus to two stops: the ticket office at Plaza Bhagawan or Jendela Bali Restaurant.

### **Tour Route**

![園區地圖](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_31624c4a0f0c3b2778d3c5262ecb6b5f.jpg)

Park Map

**1. Tirta Amerta (Sacred Spring Pool)** Once you've purchased your ticket, your tour begins. The first thing you'll see is the Sacred Spring Pool, a welcoming feature at the park's entrance. According to GWK Cultural Park's Operations Director Stephanus, this design echoes the story of Garuda and Lord Vishnu.

**2. Plaza Kura-Kura (Turtle Square)** The first stop is Turtle Square, a lush little garden with a pond, trees, and turtle statues. At the center of the pond stands a statue of Lakshmi, the goddess of wealth and abundance, where visitors can toss coins and make a wish.

**3. Plaza Wisnu (Vishnu Square)** Follow the steps up to Vishnu Square, where you'll find a statue of Lord Vishnu, the protector of the universe—a perfect spot for photos.

Inside the square, there is an eternal spring from Parahyangan Somaka Giri. Remarkably, despite being located in a limestone highland area, this spring has never dried up and is said to have existed long before the GWK Cultural Park was established.

**4. Garuda Plaza** After admiring the eternal spring, head down the stairs to reach Garuda Plaza. Here, you'll find the Garuda head statue—the mount of Lord Vishnu—which is one of the most popular photo spots for visitors.

**5. Garuda Sineloka Mini Cinema** Near Garuda Plaza, there is a mini cinema that screens an animated story about a young Garuda striving to obtain holy water. The film plays every hour from 10:30 AM to 7:30 PM.

**6. GWK Statue** To reach the GWK Statue, you can either walk or rent an electric cart. Along the way, you'll pass Festival Park, Lotus Pond, and Titi Banda Bridge.

Upon arriving at the GWK Statue, visitors with premium tickets can enter the statue and climb to the 9th and 23rd floors to enjoy panoramic views of Bali from above.

All visitors can take photos and videos in front of this 122-meter-tall statue, with two main angles to choose from: the front and the left side.

**7. Art Performances** To conclude your visit, enjoy various art performances at the Amphitheater, Lotus Pond, and Vishnu Plaza.

**8. Shopping & Dining** Near the exit, you'll find the Kencana Souvenir Shop and ASANA Art Gallery. Visitors can also dine at the Bali Window Restaurant while enjoying the sunset.

**Visit Duration** On average, visitors need 3 to 4 hours to fully explore all areas of the cultural park. It is recommended to allow ample time to deeply experience every corner of GWK Cultural Park!
