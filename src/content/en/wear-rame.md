# Bali's Local Brand Wear Rame Guide｜Sculpting Activewear, Yoga Wear & Backless Bodysuits—One Visit and You Might Lose Control!

If you're traveling to Bali, beyond buying coffee, fragrances, and souvenirs, add **Wear Rame** to your shopping list! This is a women's athleisure brand rooted in Bali, focusing on Runway-inspired Athleisure—blending the comfort of stretch activewear with stylish, sculpting, and subtly sexy designs.

If you love yoga wear, Pilates outfits, or clothes that enhance your silhouette and photograph beautifully, this store is worth a visit. Especially for those who adore backless cuts, cross-back designs, and cropped styles—be prepared to curb your shopping urges!

## What is Wear Rame?

Wear Rame was founded by Brenda Angelia. The brand's story began in 2018 when she was still **studying at a university in Taipei**, starting the business from her dorm room, which later evolved into a fashion athleisure brand closely tied to Bali. The name 'Rame' comes from Indonesian, meaning lively, joyful, and energetic; the brand's connection to Bali stems from the founder's love for the island.

The brand's philosophy goes beyond making clothes look good—it aims to help women express their style and confidence through what they wear. According to the official description, Wear Rame has its own production team in Bali, emphasizing ethical manufacturing, local craftsmanship, and more sustainable design practices.

It's not your typical yoga wear brand; instead, it blends sportswear, everyday outfits, and vacation fashion. Some styles are versatile enough to pair with a jacket, sneakers, or sandals for a casual day out, even without hitting the gym.

## What's Worth Buying at Wear Rame?

### 1. Tailored Silhouettes That Flatter the Female Form

What makes Wear Rame so appealing is that their clothing prioritizes both athletic function and visual lines. From fitted bodysuits and sports bras to high-waisted leggings, you'll find designs that hug the body.

If you usually prefer sleek, figure-flattering outfits, these pieces are a great place to start. However, the actual effect of tailored styles can vary depending on your body shape, size, and fabric choice. It's best to try them on in-store rather than relying solely on online photos to find the perfect fit.

### 2. Backless, Cross-Back, and Cropped Designs

If you find regular activewear too plain, Wear Rame offers plenty of more stylish options, such as backless bodysuits, unique strap cuts, cropped sports tops, and sets that pair well with high-waisted pants.

Among them, the **Kamari Backless Onesie** is one of the brand's signature backless bodysuit styles; the Basic Nyx collection includes sports bras and high-waisted pants, perfect for mixing and matching based on your personal style.

These pieces aren't just for yoga or Pilates—they also work as part of a vacation wardrobe. When traveling to Bali, if you love taking photos and want to look stylish without spending too much time coordinating outfits, these sets or one-piece designs are super convenient.

### 3. Not Just Activewear, but Everyday Fashion Pieces Too

Wear Rame's product range also includes draped trousers, cover-ups, and other athleisure items. For example, the Celeste Draped Flare Pants offer a different vibe from typical tight yoga leggings with their flowing drape and flared silhouette.

This is what I find noteworthy about this type of brand: the same piece of clothing doesn't have to be worn only for exercise. With the right style and pairing, it can easily transition to a café, shopping trip, or everyday travel.

## What Are the Reviews for Wear Rame?

Based on publicly available customer feedback, Wear Rame has received overwhelmingly positive reviews.

The brand's official website shows a customer rating of approximately **4.86 out of 5 stars, with 487 reviews** (based on the data displayed on the site at the time). The site also features reviews for different products, indicating that the number of reviews varies by item.

However, high ratings don't necessarily mean every item is right for everyone. Before purchasing, it's still a good idea to check the specific style you're interested in, paying close attention to details like size, cut, fabric, and intended use.

Additionally, don't judge activewear solely by its looks when it comes to heavy sweating or high-intensity workouts. If your main activities are hot yoga, running, or high-intensity cardio, be sure to check the material and functional descriptions of individual items before buying—don't just order because the design looks good.
