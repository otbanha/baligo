This travelogue was contributed by group member [Serena](https://www.facebook.com/100004740466944/), documenting a family trip to Bali with five adults and two young children. During this trip, they spent seven wonderful days with their guide and driver, Xiao Zhong from Pacung Bali. Through a detailed itinerary and spot recommendations, they explored every corner of Bali, savored local cuisine, enjoyed a variety of water activities, and created countless precious memories and beautiful photos.

**Bottom Line**: For a 7-day, 6-night trip with 5 adults and 2 kids, we recommend staying at **Beachwalk Residence in Kuta** (a three-bedroom apartment with Beachwalk Shopping Center right downstairs—ideal for large families). The itinerary covers Jimbaran Beach dining, South Bali/Uluwatu attractions, and is perfect for families with elderly members and children who need more living space and convenient amenities.

## Afterword

Here are some top Bali must-try, must-visit, and avoid-the-pitfall recommendations:

1. **Natys Restaurant**: Don't miss their seafood platter and beef bourguignon! They've recently opened branches in many locations, ensuring consistent quality.
2. **Tanjung Benoa Water Sports**: Especially recommend the undersea walking activity for diving beginners. Though it's a man-made attraction, it's a unique experience. Avoid Turtle Island—it's quite boring and lacks distinctive animals. Also, be sure to haggle; you can get 20-40% off.

   [Reserve Tanjung Benoa Banana Boat Combo](https://affiliate.klook.com/redirect?aid=116349&aff_adid=804752&k_site=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.klook.com%2Fzh-TW%2Factivity%2F9106-banana-boat-bali%2F%3Fspm%3DSearchResult.SearchResult_LIST%26clickId%3D461f80f005)
3. **Uluwatu Cliff Road**: The scenery here is stunning, perfect for photos. Visit in the morning for fewer crowds.
4. **Uluwatu Temple, Tanah Lot Temple, Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park**: All worth a visit. Uluwatu Temple and Tanah Lot Temple offer beautiful views and great photo ops. At Garuda Wisnu Kencana, if your budget allows, take the shuttle to the statue area to avoid heatstroke.
5. **Ubud Swing**: Features various activities like zip-lining and sky cycling. Nearby, there's a fantastic pork rib fried noodle restaurant, but it's a bit hidden—ask my driver for directions.
6. **La Brisa**: If you plan to go to the innermost bar, don't bring water out of the car; the restaurant will ask you to pour it all out.
7. **Beachwalk Residence**: For family trips, consider staying at [Beachwalk Residence](https://www.agoda.com/partners/partnersearch.aspx?pcs=1&cid=1961347&hid=25918345) [Trip](https://hk.trip.com/hotels/bali-hotel-detail-78158227/hai-tan-man-bu-gong-yu/?Allianceid=6817581&SID=311634184&trip_sub1=&trip_sub3=D18793707) [Booking](https://booking.tpm.li/Smp4FEqy). It offers family-style whole-floor apartments, walking distance to shopping malls and close to the beach—great value overall.
8. **Traffic is quite heavy during Chinese New Year**, with travel times taking 30 minutes to 1.5 hours longer than Google Maps estimates. Especially for longer distances to Ubud, arrival times can be way off. Roads near the palace are narrow, causing severe congestion.
9. **Rainy season contingency**: Since the Lunar New Year period is still peak rainy season in Bali, save this guide if you're worried about rain: [Bali Rainy Season: What to Do When It Rains in Bali? 60 Indoor Activity Alternatives](/en/blog/2026-bali-indoor-activities/)
