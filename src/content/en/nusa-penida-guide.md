## Introduction to Nusa Penida

Nusa Penida is a small offshore island of Bali, reachable by a roughly 45-minute ferry ride from the main island. It’s famous for its untouched, raw natural beauty, featuring dramatic cliffs, sea caves, beaches, and other fascinating landscapes that blend with the azure waters to create a paradise-like scene! The word 'Nusa' means 'island' in Indonesian, so Nusa Penida is also translated as 'Penida Island' or 'Pulau Penida,' and sometimes simply transliterated as 'Nusa Penida.'

## How to Get to Nusa Penida

There are two ways to reach Nusa Penida from Bali. Let's start with the independent route. Taking a ferry from Sanur Harbor on Bali's east coast is the most common way to get to Nusa Penida. Sanur Harbor is about 30 minutes from Kuta and an hour from Canggu. You can book your ferry ticket in advance and head to the harbor. From there, you'll catch a ferry to Nusa Penida. While it's a budget-friendly option, it does take time and effort to plan.

### **How to Take a Ferry from Bali to Nusa Penida (Canggu, Kuta, Seminyak)?**

For those looking to travel from Bali to Nusa Penida, you'll need to know that speedboats to the island depart from one location: Sanur—on the southeast coast of Bali, about 40 minutes from Kuta and 80 minutes from Canggu. The new port is called Pemelisan Harbor.

If the weather is too rough or the waves are too high, boats will not run. This can be a bit unpredictable.

### **How to Get from Ubud to Nusa Penida?**

Getting from Ubud to Nusa Penida is more complicated due to the greater distance. Currently, there are no speedboat tickets available for booking.

However, you can arrange transportation from Ubud to Sanur port. The drive/ride takes about an hour, but frequent traffic jams may make it longer.

### **What is it like taking a boat from Sanur to Nusa Penida?**

When taking a speedboat from Sanur to Nusa Penida, you must book your ticket in advance. If you haven't pre-booked, you'll be swarmed by 'enthusiastic' touts at the port offering some absurd prices.

Once you board and set off, the trip from Sanur port to Nusa Penida takes about 45 minutes. The old 'slow boats' are no longer used for tourist travel as they were somewhat dangerous and even capsized in the past. Depending on the weather and sea conditions, it could be a calm, short boat ride or a wild ocean rollercoaster ride!

### **Luggage Restrictions**

Speedboats from Bali to Nusa Penida usually have luggage restrictions, allowing a maximum of two suitcases with a total weight of 30 kilograms. However, they don't actually weigh your luggage. They'll place it on the deck and cover it with a large tarp to keep it from getting splashed.
