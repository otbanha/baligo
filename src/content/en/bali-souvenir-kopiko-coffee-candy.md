# Bali Souvenir Recommendation: Kopiko Coffee Candy – Indonesia's National Coffee Flavor Sold in Over 80 Countries

Your suitcase is nearly bursting, and you still haven't found souvenirs for that row of colleagues at the office?

That's when the pack of Kopiko coffee candy on the supermarket shelf becomes the perfect solution. Light, small, and affordable enough to hand out generously, everyone will instantly recognize, 'Ah, this is coffee.'

## What is Kopiko? Indonesia's Beloved Coffee Candy Since Childhood

Kopiko is a brand under Indonesia's Mayora Indah food company. Launched in 1982, it has become the country's most iconic coffee candy. Its tagline, 'Gantinya Ngopi,' means 'a substitute for drinking coffee'—one candy delivers the aroma of a whole cup.

## From Indonesia to the World: A Globally Renowned Coffee Candy

Kopiko isn't just a snack for Indonesians. This coffee candy is now sold in over 80 countries, from Asia to supermarket shelves in Europe and the Americas. Manufacturer Mayora Indah is considered the world's largest coffee candy producer, thanks to the Kopiko brand.

It's especially popular in South Korea, appearing in K-dramas like 'Vincenzo' and 'Hometown Cha-Cha-Cha.' Bring some back as a gift, and your friends might exclaim, 'Hey, isn't this from that show?'

A coffee candy that started in Indonesia and traveled the world—buying it in its homeland makes it extra special.

## How to Choose Your Flavor?

The common candy flavors are Original Coffee and Cappuccino:

- **Original Coffee**: Rich coffee aroma with a hint of bitterness balanced by sweetness—the classic choice, perfect for first-time buyers.
- **Cappuccino**: More milky, with a smoother and softer texture—perfect for those who prefer lattes or milky coffee.

To satisfy different tastes, grab a few packs of each flavor so everyone can pick their favorite when sharing.

## How to Eat Kopiko Coffee Candy? How Many a Day Is Just Right?

**1–2 pieces at a time, slowly suck on them**
On overseas packaging, a serving is labeled as 2 candies, so 1–2 pieces at a time is ideal. As hard candies, they're perfect for slow melting, keeping you company all day with a longer-lasting coffee aroma.

**Keep the daily amount within the range of 'one cup of coffee'**
The brand has previously stated that 4–5 candies contain about as much caffeine as one cup of coffee. Think of it this way: if you're using Kopiko to replace a cup of coffee today, spread that amount out over several sessions. If you've already had coffee, tea, or an energy drink that day, have fewer candies.

**Different flavors have different caffeine levels**
Original coffee and cappuccino have different caffeine contents, with cappuccino usually being milder. Variations may also exist between versions, so always check the label on your pack.

**Don't forget the sugar**
A single candy may seem low in calories, but eating many at once can quickly add up in calories and sugar—keep track of how many you've had in a day.

**When is the best time to eat them?**

- Have one during your morning commute or when you need an afternoon pick-me-up
- Keep them as an on-the-go snack while driving, rushing through your itinerary, or on long trips
- If you want to sleep well at night, avoid eating too much before bed.

## Gift-Giving Tips

Kopiko contains milk, soy, and sulfites, so check the ingredient label before giving it to friends with allergies. For those sensitive to caffeine, pregnant women, children, or people with specific health conditions, it's best to review the packaging and consult a doctor if needed. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to keep it fresh during your trip home.

Add a little note saying 'Just 1–2 pieces a day is perfect,' and they'll appreciate the thought.
