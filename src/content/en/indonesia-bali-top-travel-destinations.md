## 15 Must-Visit Countries in a Lifetime: Why Indonesia's Bali Ranks Third Globally?

**Bottom Line First**: In the CEOWORLD 2024 "Countries to Visit in Your Lifetime" ranking, Indonesia scored **65.15 points, ranking third globally** (first: Thailand with 72.15 points, second: Greece with 67.22 points). The evaluation criteria cover cultural appeal, natural landscapes, and overall travel experience. Bali's rice terraces, temples, and Komodo dragons are key highlights that boosted Indonesia's ranking.

According to the latest "[Countries to Visit in Your Lifetime 2024](https://ceoworld.biz/2024/04/15/worlds-best-countries-to-visit-in-your-lifetime-2024/)" ranking by CEOWORLD magazine, Thailand tops the list with 72.15 points, followed closely by Greece, while our beloved **Indonesia** takes an impressive **third place globally** with 65.15 points! This ranking evaluates factors like cultural appeal, natural landscapes, and overall travel experience. Today, let's dive deep into these dream destinations, with a special focus on Indonesia—especially the unique charm of Bali!

## 🌏 Ranking Highlights at a Glance

1. **Thailand (72.15 points)**: From Bangkok's neon-lit night markets to Chiang Mai's jungle adventures, paired with Phuket's turquoise waters and white sands, Thailand is a true "all-around travel king."
2. **Greece (67.22 points)**: Santorini's blue-domed churches and Athens' Parthenon perfectly blend romance and history.
3. **Indonesia (65.15 points)**: **Bali's** rice terraces and temples, Komodo's dragons, Java's Borobudur... diverse experiences make it stand out!
4. **Portugal (64.32 points)**: Porto's wine aromas and Lisbon's trams, Europe's best value pick.
5. **United Kingdom (60.53 points)**: London's classic charm and Scotland's highland scenery, a paradise for culture lovers.

![排行榜亮點速覽](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2495e9c4fc826f35ce1505a4070ec856.png)

Other contenders include South Africa (safari!), Peru (Machu Picchu!), and Italy (food and art!), while classic destinations like India, France, and Spain also made the list.

**Notably, Taiwan ranked 37th on this list**—though it didn't break into the top 15, the island's cuisine, hospitality, and natural landscapes remain beloved by travelers!

## 🌴 Why Indonesia Ranks Third Globally? 4 Must-Visit Reasons for Bali

As travel bloggers deeply rooted in Bali tourism, let's count down Bali's unbeatable advantages:

![為什麼印尼能成為全球第三？峇里島的4大必去理由](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2e9d69d057faf60bdcc212332604619b.jpg)

### 1. A Double Feast of Nature and Culture

- **Bali**: The morning mist over Ubud's rice terraces and the sunset at Tanah Lot—every shot is a postcard!
- **Volcano Adventures**: The lunar landscape of Mount Bromo and the blue flames of Mount Ijen offer awe-inspiring experiences beyond imagination.
- **World Heritage Sites**: Java's **Borobudur** and **Prambanan** temple complexes bear witness to millennia of Buddhist and Hindu civilizations.

### 2. High-Value Luxury Experiences

![2. CP值超高的奢華體驗](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_56ab0ceda63196ea577a807d5be9054a.jpg)

Compared to the high costs in Europe, America, and Taiwan, Bali lets you enjoy **five-star villas, cliffside spas, and private beach dinners** for half the budget. Check out [private pool villas in Seminyak](/en/blog/seminyak-private-villas-guide/), [the unbeatable sunset at Ayana's Rock Bar in Jimbaran](/en/blog/ayana-resort-bali-guide/), [jungle-view hotels in Ubud](/en/blog/ubud-resorts-guide/), and [five-star resorts in Nusa Dua](/en/blog/nusa-dua-resorts-guide/).

### 3. Unique Local Experiences

- **Traditional Dance**: Watch the Legong dance and the Kecak fire dance, where myth and art intertwine.
- **Hands-On Workshops**: Take classes in pottery, silver jewelry, Batik, or cooking skills, and bring home exclusive souvenirs.
