## 15 Must-Visit Countries in a Lifetime: Why Indonesia's Bali Ranks Third Globally?

**Bottom Line First**: In the 2024 "Countries to Visit in Your Lifetime" ranking by CEOWORLD, Indonesia placed **third globally with 65.15 points** (first: Thailand at 72.15, second: Greece at 67.22). The evaluation criteria include cultural appeal, natural landscapes, and overall travel experience. Bali's terraced rice fields, temples, and Komodo dragons are key highlights that boosted Indonesia's ranking.

According to the latest "[Countries to Visit in Your Lifetime 2024](https://ceoworld.biz/2024/04/15/worlds-best-countries-to-visit-in-your-lifetime-2024/)" ranking by CEOWORLD magazine, Thailand tops the list with 72.15 points, followed closely by Greece, while our beloved **Indonesia** secured an impressive **third place globally** with 65.15 points. Today, let's explore these dream destinations, with a special focus on Indonesia—especially the unique charm of Bali!

## 🌏 Ranking Highlights at a Glance

1. **Thailand (72.15 points)**: From Bangkok's neon-lit night markets to Chiang Mai's jungle adventures, paired with Phuket's turquoise waters and white sands, Thailand is a true "all-round travel king."
2. **Greece (67.22 points)**: Santorini's blue-domed churches and Athens' Parthenon perfectly blend romance and history.
3. **Indonesia (65.15 points)**: **Bali's** terraced rice fields and temples, Komodo's dragons, Java's Borobudur... diverse experiences make it stand out!
4. **Portugal (64.32 points)**: Port wine aromas in Porto and Lisbon's trams, Europe's best value pick.
5. **United Kingdom (60.53 points)**: London's classic charm and Scotland's highland scenery, a paradise for culture lovers.

![排行榜亮點速覽](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2495e9c4fc826f35ce1505a4070ec856.png)

Other notable entries include South Africa (safari!), Peru (Machu Picchu!), Italy (food and art!), while classic destinations like India, France, and Spain also made the list.

**It's worth noting that Taiwan ranked 37th on this list**—though not in the top 15, the island's cuisine, hospitality, and natural landscapes remain beloved by travelers!

## 🌴 Why Did Indonesia Rank Third Globally? 4 Must-Visit Reasons for Bali

As travel bloggers deeply rooted in Bali, let's dive into the island's unbeatable advantages:

![為什麼印尼能成為全球第三？峇里島的4大必去理由](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2e9d69d057faf60bdcc212332604619b.jpg)

### 1. A Dual Feast of Nature and Culture

- **Bali**: The morning mist over Ubud's rice terraces and the sunset at Tanah Lot Temple—every shot is a postcard!
- **Volcanic Adventures**: The lunar landscape of Mount Bromo and the blue flames of Mount Ijen offer awe-inspiring thrills beyond imagination.
- **World Heritage Sites**: Java's **Borobudur** and **Prambanan** temple complexes bear witness to millennia of Buddhist and Hindu civilizations.

### 2. High-Value Luxury Experiences

![2. CP值超高的奢華體驗](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_56ab0ceda63196ea577a807d5be9054a.jpg)

Compared to the high costs in Europe, the US, and Taiwan, Bali lets you enjoy **five-star villas, cliffside spas, and private beach dinners** on half the budget. Check out [Seminyak's private pool villas](/en/blog/seminyak-private-villas-guide/), [Jimbaran's Ayana Rock Bar for unbeatable sunsets](/en/blog/ayana-resort-bali-guide/), [Ubud's jungle-view hotels](/en/blog/ubud-resorts-guide/), and [Nusa Dua's five-star resorts](/en/blog/nusa-dua-resorts-guide/).

### 3. Unique Local Living

- **Traditional Dance**: Watch the Legong dance and Kecak Fire Dance, where mythology and art intertwine.
- **Hands-On Experiences**: Take classes in pottery, silver jewelry, Batik, or cooking to bring home exclusive souvenirs.
