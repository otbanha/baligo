When staying at AYANA Bali, beyond enjoying the pools, ocean views, and resort facilities, planning your dining is a key part of the experience.

The AYANA estate includes AYANA Resort, RIMBA, and AYANA Segara, offering a wide range of restaurants—from international buffet breakfasts and Italian cuisine to Japanese teppanyaki, beachfront seafood dinners, and Balinese cultural feasts with traditional dance.

But not every restaurant is worth your time. If you're staying for two or three nights, how do you choose without blowing your budget? Which spots are for a proper meal, and which are more about the views and atmosphere?

This guide highlights the restaurants worth knowing within the AYANA estate, combining public guest reviews, official restaurant info, and dining scenarios to help you find the best fit for your needs.

## AYANA Restaurants at a Glance

| Restaurant | Main Feature | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Padi | International breakfast, Asian dishes, lotus pond views | Those who enjoy a leisurely breakfast |
| To’Ge | International cuisine, all-day dining, poolside setting | Those who value convenience and a diverse menu |
| Karang | Modern style, pool views, international cuisine | AYANA Segara guests |
| HONZEN Japanese Restaurant | Japanese teppanyaki, sushi, Omakase | Japanese food lovers who value the dining experience |
| Damar Terrace | Asian and Indonesian cuisine, lotus pond setting | Family travelers, those wanting a formal dinner |
| Sami Sami | Wood-fired pizza, pasta, sea views | Italian cuisine enthusiasts |
| Rock Bar | Cliff views, sunset, cocktails | Couples, anniversaries, photo lovers |
| Kampoeng Bali | Balinese cuisine, traditional dance and fire dance | Those wanting to experience local culture |
| KISIK Seafood & Grill | Seafood barbecue, beachfront dining | Seafood lovers, those wanting to dine with an ocean view |

Each of the restaurants above has its own distinct positioning, so it's not fair to compare them solely based on meal prices or online ratings. In particular, breakfast offerings, teppanyaki set menus, performance schedules, and menu prices are all subject to change based on current arrangements.

## Breakfast Edition: How to Choose Between Padi, To’Ge, and Karang?

AYANA's breakfast restaurants each have their own character, but you don't necessarily need to visit every single one. First, check which breakfast entitlement is included in your room rate and whether you can choose restaurants in different resort sections.

### Padi: Leisurely Breakfast by the Lotus Pond

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793370283124-15429.jpg)

Padi is located at AYANA Resort, surrounded by a lotus pond, offering an international buffet breakfast, while dinner focuses on Asian flavors like Thai, Indonesian, and Indian cuisine.

If the focus of this trip is to enjoy the resort rather than rushing out to explore attractions every morning, Padi is a great choice for a relaxed start to the day. After breakfast, you can continue using the pool or other facilities without feeling the need to rush to the next activity.

However, loving the views doesn't mean you have to go out of your way to eat breakfast in a different area. If you're planning to hire a driver for the day, it's usually more practical to consider time and transportation first.

[Official website details & location map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/padi/)

### To'Ge: Staying at RIMBA, Convenience Is Also an Advantage

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793370514785-15430.jpg)

To'Ge is located at RIMBA, offering international cuisine and all-day dining in a setting that blends tropical gardens with poolside seating.

Its standout feature is a more varied menu—not just breakfast, but also other international dishes. For guests staying at RIMBA, the restaurant's location is convenient, saving you the need to take a shuttle just for breakfast.

If your travel companions have different dietary preferences or you don't want to eat the same type of food every day, To'Ge is worth considering.

[Official website details & location map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/toge/)

### Karang: Start Here If You're Staying at AYANA Segara

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793370851874-15431.jpg)

Karang is located at AYANA Segara, featuring a modern style with international cuisine and pool views.

For Segara guests, dining close by is a clear advantage. If you don't have a strong desire to try other restaurants, there's no need to venture to another area just for comparison.

**The rule for choosing breakfast is simple: first check your accommodation package, then consider the restaurant setting.** Don't assume your booking includes the same dining benefits just because other travelers have shared their breakfast experiences.

[Official website details & location map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/karang/)

## HONZEN: Worth Considering for Teppanyaki

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793371077922-15432.jpg)

If you've already tried plenty of Balinese local dishes and want a different dinner experience at the resort, HONZEN Japanese Restaurant is worth considering.

HONZEN is located in the Japanese garden area of AYANA Resort, offering Japanese cuisine with highlights including teppanyaki, Omakase sushi, and other Japanese dishes. The official introduction also mentions Chef Kimura and the philosophy of using fresh local ingredients to present Japanese cuisine.

https://youtu.be/-jj7i_7Cv98?si=GSbGZf9TxXgm1g0H

Unlike typical Japanese restaurants, teppanyaki offers not just food and flavor, but also the live cooking performance right in front of you. For those who enjoy watching the cooking process up close and want a more interactive dinner experience, this itself is the main attraction.

### What Do Online Reviews Say?

Based on public reviews on Tripadvisor, HONZEN generally receives positive feedback. Travelers often praise the food quality, service attitude, and dining atmosphere, with some specifically highlighting the teppanyaki experience.

However, HONZEN offers more than just teppanyaki. Public reviews also mention Japanese dishes like unagi rice and salmon sashimi, so you shouldn't assume all reviews apply directly to the teppanyaki set menu.

In my opinion, when choosing a restaurant like this, you should first clarify what you truly want to experience: if it's watching the chef cook live, confirm the teppanyaki serving style and seating; if you just want Japanese cuisine, compare the à la carte items and set menus.

### Is the Price Worth It?

HONZEN is one of the higher-priced dining options within the AYANA resort, especially when choosing ingredients like wagyu beef or seafood, so you'll need to budget accordingly.

Tripadvisor has previously listed some wagyu menu prices, such as Japanese wagyu 100g at IDR 2,150,000 and 200g at IDR 3,400,000; Australian wagyu 100g at IDR 1,900,000 and 200g at IDR 2,950,000. These are menu data from public pages and may not be current, nor should they be taken as fixed prices for teppanyaki sets.

Therefore, it's best to confirm the current menu, set prices, portion sizes, service charges, and taxes before making a reservation.

**My advice:** If you love Japanese cuisine or want to arrange a more ceremonial dinner at AYANA, HONZEN is worth considering; but if you just want a simple meal, there's no need to splurge on a high-priced set just for the photo op.

[Official website details & location map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/honzen/)

## Sami Sami: Choose This Spot for Pizza and Pasta

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793371709510-15434.webp)

Sami Sami is an open-air Italian restaurant located in the ocean-view area of AYANA Resort.

It focuses on Trattoria-style Italian cuisine, offering pizzas, pastas, and other classic dishes. The semi-outdoor setting with sea breezes makes it ideal for a relaxed lunch or dinner.

Based on public reviews from Tripadvisor, Sami Sami receives positive feedback overall, with guests highlighting the food, service, and ocean-view ambiance. Some travelers particularly recommend the pizza and pasta, though actual tastes may vary by personal preference.

If your travel companions enjoy Western cuisine, Sami Sami is an easy choice. Pizzas are great for sharing, and pairing them with pasta makes for a complete meal.

I'd position it as a spot that balances good food and atmosphere, rather than a purely Instagram-worthy restaurant.

[Official website details & location map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/sami-sami/)

## Damar Terrace: A Dinner Option for Families

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793371905249-15435.webp)

Damar Terrace serves Asian and Indonesian cuisine in an open-air setting with lotus pond views.

Compared to the bar vibe at Rock Bar or the Japanese teppanyaki experience at HONZEN, Damar Terrace is better suited for a simple dinner.

In public traveler reviews, some mention the beef rice, salad, and desserts, while others praise the overall dining atmosphere. Some dining notes also point out that the restaurant offers kid-friendly options like chicken and fries.

For families staying at the resort, whether the restaurant is convenient and the menu caters to different ages can be more important than whether it's a popular Instagram spot.

If you've spent the whole day at the pool and don't feel like going out to find dinner in the evening, Damar Terrace can be a practical choice.

[Official detailed info & location map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/damar-terrace/)

## Rock Bar: One of AYANA's Most Iconic Sunset Experiences

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1783681094664-02-story-body-04_ceioia.jpeg)

When it comes to AYANA, many people first think of Rock Bar.

This bar is located on a seaside cliff area, renowned for its Indian Ocean views, sunsets, cocktails, and unique arrival experience. Guests typically take a cable car to the bar, making the journey itself part of the resort experience.

Rock Bar's appeal lies not only in its drinks but also in the scenery and atmosphere it offers. For couples, honeymooners, or anyone looking to plan a special evening, it truly has its value.

### What Do Online Reviews Say?

In public traveler reviews, positive comments often highlight the sea views, sunsets, service, and overall atmosphere; differing opinions mention high prices, wait times during peak hours, and service speed that may not always meet expectations.

These reviews reflect Rock Bar's positioning: it's not a restaurant that wins on food portion size and price, but a venue centered on scenery and the bar experience.

If you're expecting a hearty, value-for-money dinner, you might find the prices steep; but if you're looking to create a special sunset memory in Bali, the evaluation criteria shouldn't focus solely on the food.

### How to Plan for the Best Value?

I recommend having dinner at another restaurant first, then heading to Rock Bar for drinks and sea views. This makes it easier to control your budget without relying on bar food to replace a full meal.

During popular sunset hours, it's best to confirm reservations and entry rules in advance, and allow time for check-in, shuttle, and cable car waits. Weather can also affect the actual view, so don't take a perfect sunset for granted.

[Official website details & location map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/rock-bar/)

## Kampoeng Bali: A Dinner Blending Balinese Traditional Performance

If you want to arrange a more complete evening within the resort, consider the Kampoeng Bali Dinner Show.

It combines a Balinese cuisine buffet with traditional performances, turning dining into more than just a meal—it's a way to experience local culture. For first-time visitors to Bali, those interested in traditional dance, or families traveling with children, this format is more likely to become a special travel memory.

In public guest reviews, some praise the buffet selection, while others particularly enjoy the dance and fire show performances, finding the overall event more engaging than a simple meal.

Of course, if you prioritize refined à la carte dishes and a quiet dining atmosphere, this kind of performance-based dinner may not be the best fit.

### Who Is This For?

- First-time visitors to Bali who want to experience traditional culture.
- Those who prefer not to arrange transportation and want to enjoy dinner and entertainment within the resort.
- Families traveling with children who are looking for evening activities.
- Anyone planning a more ceremonial family trip or anniversary dinner.

The cost of this dinner covers not just the meal but also the performances and overall event arrangement, so it's not fair to compare it directly with a standard buffet.

Performance dates, start times, and booking methods may change, so it's best to confirm the current schedule before your trip.

[Official website details & location map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/kampoeng-bali/)

## KISIK Seafood & Grill: Dining on the Beach with Fresh Seafood

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793373160564-15436.webp)

If you're in the Jimbaran area and planning a seafood dinner, consider KISIK Seafood & Grill.

Located on the beachfront of AYANA Resort, this restaurant specializes in grilled seafood and beachside dining. Compared to typical indoor restaurants, the main draw here is enjoying dinner by the sea with the sound of waves.

However, there's a practical issue with seafood restaurants: the items you want may not always be available.

Some guests have shared that they specifically visited KISIK for oysters, only to find they were sold out that day, eventually dining elsewhere. This doesn't reflect poorly on the seafood quality, but it's a reminder to check availability if a particular dish is a must-try.

If you're planning a seafood dinner, it's best to confirm the daily menu, specific seafood availability, and whether advance booking is needed when making a reservation.

## Is AYANA Dining Really Expensive?

Compared to typical street-side restaurants in Bali, dining within the AYANA resort complex tends to be on the higher end price-wise.

However, the cost isn't just about the food itself. The dining atmosphere, service, resort shuttle access, ocean views, and the convenience of not having to deal with traffic outside the resort are all factors that guests may be willing to pay for.

I would categorize the restaurants into three types:

**Type 1: Focused on the meal itself.**

For example, Sami Sami or Damar Terrace. These restaurants are best suited for a full dinner, where you should prioritize the dishes, portion sizes, and the preferences of your dining companions.

**Type 2: Focused on specialty cuisine and the dining experience.**

For instance, HONZEN Japanese Cuisine. The value of teppanyaki lies not only in the ingredients but also in the chef's live cooking performance. If you're interested in Japanese cuisine, this experience may matter more than simply comparing meal prices.

**Type 3: Focused on the view or activities.**

Examples include Rock Bar and Kampoeng Bali. The former highlights cliffside views and sunsets, while the latter combines a buffet with traditional performances. What you're paying for isn't just the food, but the entire experience.

When comparing prices, first decide whether you're buying a meal, a culinary experience, or an evening activity.

## How to plan your dining if staying at AYANA for a few nights?

### Staying only two or three nights

There's no need to rush from one restaurant to another just to try them all.

I'd suggest picking one or two restaurants that interest you most, then pairing them with simpler breakfasts and dinners near your accommodation.

If catching the sunset is your top priority, include Rock Bar; if you love Japanese cuisine, consider HONZEN; if you're interested in Balinese culture, go with Kampoeng Bali.

There's no need to dine at every spot—otherwise, your food budget can easily get out of hand.

### Staying Four to Six Nights

With a longer stay, you can take your time exploring different dining styles.

Let your breakfast depend on your accommodation and daily plans, while rotating dinners between Italian, Japanese teppanyaki, bar experiences, seafood, and traditional performances.

However, AYANA's grounds are vast—traveling between different lodging areas and restaurants still requires shuttle and walking time. Don't assume every restaurant within the resort is just minutes away.

### Do You Need to Eat Every Meal at AYANA?

Not at all.

If you want to explore cafes, local eateries, or specialty dishes elsewhere in Bali, you can easily combine dining out with visits to nearby attractions.

AYANA's strength lies in its variety and complete resort facilities, but that doesn't mean every meal has to be eaten on-site.

## AYANA Restaurant Reservations & Dining Tips

Before you go, here are a few things to confirm:

- **Breakfast entitlements:** Check which breakfast options are included in your room rate and whether you can dine at restaurants in other sections of the resort.
- **Special dinners:** For Rock Bar, HONZEN Teppanyaki, and Kampoeng Bali, it's best to confirm seating and service arrangements early.
- **Latest pricing:** Note whether menu prices already include tax and service charges.
- **Resort transportation:** Allow time for shuttle rides, walking, and check-in, especially on evenings with fixed reservations.
- **Special dietary needs:** If you require high chairs, have allergies, or other dietary requests, it's advisable to confirm with the restaurant in advance.
- **Operating hours:** Restaurant hours, entertainment schedules, and menus are subject to change—always check the latest official information.

## Final Thoughts: The Best Meal at AYANA Isn't Necessarily the Most Famous One

The biggest advantage of dining at AYANA is the variety—there's something for every taste. But not every restaurant suits every traveler.

If you love Italian cuisine, consider Sami Sami; for a Japanese teppanyaki dinner, HONZEN is worth adding to your list; if you're dining with kids and want a relaxed evening, check out Damar Terrace; to make the sunset part of your travel memories, Rock Bar has its unique charm; and if you want dinner to be more than just a meal, Kampoeng Bali is better suited as a full experience.

As for breakfast, it's usually more practical to choose a restaurant close to your accommodation that matches your taste, rather than traveling across the resort just for the view.

I wouldn't recommend that everyone put all of AYANA's restaurants on their must-try list. Even at a five-star resort, you might find dishes that don't suit your palate, longer wait times for service, or prices that exceed expectations. Public reviews praise the views and service, but also note varying opinions on pricing and wait times—factors worth considering before booking.

**If I had to pick just one or two, I'd first decide what travel memory I want to take home, then decide where to spend my money.** Wanting a great dinner, watching the sunset, experiencing Japanese teppanyaki, or learning about Balinese culture are all different needs—there's no need to judge them by the same standard.

After all, the most important part of a Bali vacation isn't how many restaurants you've checked off, but whether you truly enjoyed your time.
