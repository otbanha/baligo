When staying at AYANA Bali, planning your dining is just as important as enjoying the pools, ocean views, and resort facilities.

The AYANA estate includes AYANA Resort, RIMBA, and AYANA Segara, offering a wide range of restaurants—from international buffet breakfasts and Italian cuisine to Japanese teppanyaki, beachfront seafood dinners, and Balinese cultural feasts with traditional dance.

But not every restaurant is worth your time. If you're staying two or three nights, how do you choose without wasting your budget? Which ones are for a proper meal, and which are more about the views and atmosphere?

This guide highlights the restaurants worth knowing within the AYANA estate, combining public guest reviews, official restaurant info, and dining scenarios to help you find the best fit for your needs.

## AYANA Restaurants at a Glance

| Restaurant | Main Feature | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Padi | International breakfast, Asian dishes, lotus pond views | Those who enjoy a leisurely breakfast |
| To’Ge | International cuisine, all-day dining, poolside setting | Those who value convenience and a diverse menu |
| Karang | Modern style, pool views, international cuisine | AYANA Segara guests |
| HONZEN Japanese Restaurant | Japanese teppanyaki, sushi, Omakase | Japanese food lovers who value the dining experience |
| Damar Terrace | Asian and Indonesian cuisine, lotus pond setting | Family travelers, those wanting a formal dinner |

Each of the above restaurants has its own unique positioning, so it's not fair to compare them solely based on meal prices or online ratings. In particular, breakfast offerings, teppanyaki set menus, performance schedules, and menu prices are all subject to change based on current arrangements.

## Breakfast Guide: How to Choose Between Padi, To’Ge, and Karang?

AYANA’s breakfast restaurants each have their own character, but you don’t necessarily need to visit every single one. First, check which breakfast entitlement is included in your room rate and whether you can choose a restaurant in a different section of the resort.

### Padi: A Leisurely Breakfast by the Lotus Pond

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793370283124-15429.jpg)

Padi is located at AYANA Resort, surrounded by a lotus pond, and offers an international buffet breakfast, while dinner focuses on Asian flavors like Thai, Indonesian, and Indian cuisine.

If the highlight of your trip is enjoying the resort rather than heading out to explore sights early every day, Padi is a great choice for a relaxed morning. After breakfast, you can continue using the pool or other facilities without rushing to your next activity.

However, loving the views doesn’t mean you have to go out of your way to eat breakfast in a different area. If you’re planning to hire a driver for the day, it’s usually more practical to consider timing and transportation first.

### To’Ge: Staying at RIMBA, Convenience Is an Advantage

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793370514785-15430.jpg)

To’Ge is located at RIMBA, offering international cuisine and all-day dining in a setting that blends tropical gardens with poolside seating.

Its standout feature is a wider variety of menu options—not just breakfast, but also other international dishes. For guests staying at RIMBA, the restaurant’s location is convenient, saving you the need to take a shuttle just for breakfast.

If your travel companions have different dietary preferences or you don’t want to eat the same type of food every day, To’Ge is worth considering.

[Official Website & Location Map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/toge/)

### Karang: Start Here If You're Staying at AYANA Segara

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793370851874-15431.jpg)

Karang is located at AYANA Segara, featuring a modern style with international cuisine and pool views.

For Segara guests, dining nearby is a straightforward advantage. If you don't have a specific craving to try other restaurants, there's no need to venture to other areas just for comparison.

**The principle for choosing breakfast is simple: first check your accommodation package, then consider the restaurant setting.** Don't assume your booking includes the same dining benefits just because you've seen other travelers sharing their breakfast experiences.

[Official Website & Location Map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/karang/)

## HONZEN: Worth Considering for Teppanyaki

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793371077922-15432.jpg)

If you've already had plenty of local Balinese cuisine and want a different dinner experience at the resort, HONZEN Japanese Restaurant is worth considering.

HONZEN is located in the Japanese garden area of AYANA Resort, offering Japanese cuisine with highlights including teppanyaki, Omakase sushi, and other Japanese dishes. The official introduction also mentions Chef Kimura and the philosophy of using fresh local ingredients to present Japanese cuisine.

https://youtu.be/-jj7i_7Cv98?si=GSbGZf9TxXgm1g0H

Unlike typical Japanese restaurants, teppanyaki offers not just ingredients and flavor, but also the live cooking performance right in front of you. For those who enjoy watching the culinary process up close and want a more interactive dinner experience, this itself is the main draw.

### What Do Online Reviews Say?

Based on public reviews from Tripadvisor, HONZEN generally receives positive feedback. Travelers frequently praise the food quality, service attitude, and dining atmosphere, with some specifically highlighting the teppanyaki experience.

However, HONZEN offers more than just teppanyaki. Public reviews also mention Japanese dishes like unagi rice and salmon sashimi, so it's important not to apply all the feedback on every dish directly to the teppanyaki set menu.

In my view, when choosing a restaurant like this, you should first clarify what you truly want to experience: if it's watching the chef cook live, confirm the teppanyaki setup and seating; if you simply want Japanese cuisine, compare the à la carte options and set menus.

### Is the Price Worth It?

HONZEN is one of the higher-end dining options within the AYANA resort, especially when choosing ingredients like wagyu beef or seafood, so you'll need to budget accordingly.

Tripadvisor has previously listed some wagyu menu prices, such as Japanese wagyu 100g at IDR 2,150,000 and 200g at IDR 3,400,000; Australian wagyu 100g at IDR 1,900,000 and 200g at IDR 2,950,000. These are menu data from public pages and may not be current, nor should they be taken as fixed prices for teppanyaki sets.

Therefore, it's best to confirm the current menu, set prices, portion sizes, service charges, and taxes before making a reservation.

**My advice:** If you enjoy Japanese cuisine or want to arrange a more ceremonial dinner at AYANA, HONZEN is worth considering; but if you just want a simple meal, there's no need to splurge on a high-priced set just for the photo op.

[Official website details & location map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/honzen/)

## Sami Sami: A Great Pick for Pizza and Pasta

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793371709510-15434.webp)

Sami Sami is an open-air Italian restaurant located in the ocean-view area of AYANA Resort.

It serves Trattoria-style Italian cuisine, with options like pizza, pasta, and other classic dishes. The semi-outdoor space, paired with sea breezes, makes it ideal for a relaxed lunch or dinner.

Based on public reviews from Tripadvisor, Sami Sami receives positive feedback overall, with guests highlighting the food, service, and ocean-view setting. Some travelers particularly recommend the pizza and pasta, though actual tastes may vary by personal preference.

If your travel companions enjoy Western cuisine, Sami Sami is a relatively easy choice. Pizza is great for sharing, and pairing it with pasta makes for a complete meal.

I see it as a spot that balances good food and ambiance, rather than a restaurant purely for photo ops.

[Official website details & location map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/sami-sami/)

## Damar Terrace: A Dinner Option for Families

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793371905249-15435.webp)

Damar Terrace serves Asian and Indonesian cuisine in an open-air setting with lotus pond views.

Compared to the bar vibe at Rock Bar or the Japanese teppanyaki experience at HONZEN, Damar Terrace is better suited for a simple dinner.

In public traveler reviews, some mention dishes like beef rice, salads, and desserts, while others praise the overall dining atmosphere. Some dining notes also mention that the restaurant offers kid-friendly options like chicken and fries.

For families staying at the resort with children, whether the restaurant is convenient and the menu caters to different ages can sometimes matter more than whether it's a popular Instagram spot.

If you've spent the whole day at the pool and don't feel like going out to find a restaurant in the evening, Damar Terrace can be a practical choice.

[Official website details & location map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/damar-terrace/)

## Rock Bar: One of AYANA's Most Iconic Sunset Experiences

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1783681094664-02-story-body-04_ceioia.jpeg)

When mentioning AYANA, many people first think of Rock Bar.

Located on a seaside cliff, this bar is famous for its Indian Ocean views, sunsets, cocktails, and unique arrival experience. Guests typically take a cable car to the bar, making the journey itself part of the resort experience.

Rock Bar's appeal lies not only in its drinks but also in the scenery and atmosphere it offers. For couples, honeymooners, or anyone looking to plan a special evening, it truly has its value.

### What Do Online Reviews Say?

In public guest reviews, positive comments often highlight the sea views, sunsets, service, and overall atmosphere; differing opinions mention high prices, waiting times during peak hours, and service speed that may not always meet expectations.

These reviews reflect Rock Bar's positioning: it's not a restaurant that wins on food portion size and price, but a venue focused on scenery and the bar experience.

If you're expecting a hearty, value-for-money dinner, you might find the prices steep; but if you're looking to create a special sunset memory in Bali, the criteria for evaluation shouldn't be limited to the food alone.

### How to Plan It More Wisely?

I'd suggest having dinner at another restaurant first, then heading to Rock Bar for drinks and sea views. This makes it easier to control your budget without relying on bar food to replace a full meal.

During popular sunset hours, it's best to confirm reservations and entry rules in advance, and allow time for check-in, shuttle, and cable car waits. Weather can also affect the actual view, so don't take a perfect sunset for granted.

[Official website details & location map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/rock-bar/)

## Kampoeng Bali: A Dinner Blending Balinese Traditional Performance

If you want a more complete evening within the resort, consider the Kampoeng Bali Dinner Show.

It combines a Balinese cuisine buffet with traditional performances, making dining more than just eating—it's a way to experience local culture. For first-time visitors to Bali, those interested in traditional dance, or families traveling with kids, this format is more likely to become a special travel memory.

In public reviews, some guests praise the buffet selection, while others especially enjoy the dance and fire show performances, finding the overall experience more engaging than just a meal.

Of course, if you prefer refined à la carte dining and a quiet atmosphere, this performance-based dinner may not be the best fit.

### Who Is This For?

- First-time visitors to Bali looking to experience traditional culture.
- Travelers who want to avoid arranging transportation and prefer to enjoy dinner and entertainment within the resort.
- Families traveling with children who want an evening activity to participate in.
- Those planning a meaningful family trip or anniversary dinner with a sense of ceremony.

The cost of this dinner covers not just the meal but also the performances and overall event arrangement, so it's not fair to compare it directly with a standard buffet.

Performance dates, start times, and booking methods may change, so it's best to confirm the schedule before your trip.

[Official website details & location map](https://www.ayana.com/zh-hant/bali/dining/kampoeng-bali/)

## KISIK Seafood & Grill: Dining on the Beach with Fresh Seafood

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1793373160564-15436.webp)

If you're in the Jimbaran area and craving a seafood dinner, consider KISIK Seafood & Grill.

Located on the beachfront of AYANA Resort, this restaurant specializes in grilled seafood and beachside dining. Compared to typical indoor restaurants, the main draw here is enjoying dinner by the sea with the sound of waves.

However, there's a practical issue with seafood restaurants: the items you want may not always be available.

Some guests have shared that they specifically visited KISIK for oysters, only to find them sold out for the day, eventually dining elsewhere. This doesn't reflect poorly on the seafood quality, but it's a reminder to check availability if a particular dish is a must-try.

If you're planning a seafood dinner, it's best to confirm the daily menu, specific seafood availability, and whether advance booking is needed when making a reservation.

## Is Dining at AYANA Really Expensive?

Compared to typical street-side restaurants in Bali, dining within the AYANA complex generally falls into a higher price range.

However, the cost isn't just about the food itself. The dining environment, service, resort shuttle access, ocean views, and the convenience of not having to leave the resort and deal with traffic are all factors that may justify the price for guests.

I would categorize the restaurants into three types:

**Category 1: Focus on the meal itself.**

For example, Sami Sami and Damar Terrace. These restaurants are best suited for a full dinner, where you should prioritize the dishes, portion sizes, and your dining companions' preferences.

**Category 2: Focus on specialty cuisine and the dining experience.**

For example, HONZEN Japanese Cuisine. The value of teppanyaki lies not only in the ingredients but also in the chef's live cooking performance. If you're interested in Japanese cuisine, this experience may matter more than simply comparing meal prices.

**Category 3: Focus on the view or activity.**

For example, Rock Bar and Kampoeng Bali. The former highlights cliffside views and sunsets, while the latter combines a buffet with a traditional performance. What you're paying for isn't just the food, but the entire experience.

When comparing prices, first decide whether you're buying a meal, a culinary experience, or an evening activity.

## How many nights to stay at AYANA and how to plan your dining?

### Staying only two or three nights

There's no need to rush from one venue to another just to try every restaurant.

I’d recommend picking one or two specialty restaurants that interest you most, then pairing them with breakfast near your accommodation and simpler dinners.

If catching the sunset is your top priority, include Rock Bar; if you love Japanese cuisine, consider HONZEN; if Balinese culture appeals to you, go with Kampoeng Bali.

There’s no need to dine at every spot, or your food budget will quickly get out of hand.

### Staying Four to Six Nights

With a longer stay, you can take your time exploring different dining styles.

Let your breakfast plans follow your accommodation and daily itinerary, while dinners can rotate between Italian, Japanese teppanyaki, bar experiences, seafood, and traditional performances.

However, AYANA’s grounds are vast—traveling between different lodging areas and restaurants still requires shuttle and walking time. Don’t assume every restaurant within the resort is just minutes away.

### Do You Need to Eat Every Meal at AYANA?

Absolutely not.

If you want to explore cafés, local eateries, or specialty dishes elsewhere in Bali, you can easily combine dining out with visits to nearby attractions.

AYANA's strength lies in its variety and comprehensive resort facilities, but that doesn't mean every meal has to be had on-site.

## AYANA Restaurant Reservations & Dining Tips

Before you go, it's recommended to confirm the following:

- **Breakfast Entitlements:** Check which breakfast options are included in your room rate and whether you can dine at restaurants in other sections of the resort.
- **Special Dinners:** For Rock Bar, HONZEN Teppanyaki, and Kampoeng Bali, it's best to confirm seating and service arrangements early.
- **Latest Pricing:** Be aware of whether menu prices are subject to additional taxes and service charges.
- **Resort Transportation:** Allow time for shuttle rides, walking, and check-in, especially on evenings with fixed reservations.
- **Special Dietary Needs:** If you require high chairs, have allergies, or other dietary requests, it's advisable to confirm with the restaurant in advance.
- **Operating Hours:** Restaurant hours, entertainment schedules, and menus are subject to change, so always refer to the latest official information.

## Final Thoughts: The Best Meal at AYANA Isn't Necessarily the Most Famous One

The biggest advantage of dining at AYANA resort is the wide variety of options, catering to different dining needs. However, not every restaurant suits every traveler.

If you love Italian cuisine, consider Sami Sami; for a Japanese teppanyaki dinner, HONZEN is worth adding to your list; traveling with kids and want a relaxed dinner? Check out Damar Terrace; if you want to make the sunset part of your travel memories, Rock Bar has its unique charm; and if you want dinner to be more than just a meal, Kampoeng Bali is better suited as a full experience.

As for breakfast, it's usually more practical to choose a restaurant close to your accommodation that matches your taste, rather than traveling across the resort just for the view.

I wouldn't recommend that everyone put all of AYANA's restaurants on a must-try list. Even at a five-star resort, you might encounter dishes that don't suit your palate, longer wait times for service, or prices that exceed expectations. Public reviews include both praise for the views and service, as well as differing opinions on pricing and wait times—all factors worth considering before booking.

**If I had to pick just one or two, I'd first decide what kind of travel memory I want to take home, then decide where to spend my money.** Wanting a great dinner, watching the sunset, experiencing Japanese teppanyaki, or learning about Balinese culture are all different needs—there's no need to judge them by the same standard.

After all, the most important thing about a Bali vacation isn't how many restaurants you've checked off, but whether you truly enjoyed your time.
