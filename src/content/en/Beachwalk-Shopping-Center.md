# Kuta Beachwalk Shopping Center: Shopping, Dining, and Ocean Views All in One

When traveling in Bali, you don't have to chase tourist spots every day. On a scorching afternoon, during a sudden downpour, or after a long day at the beach when you crave air conditioning and a good meal, the **Beachwalk Shopping Center**, right across from Kuta Beach, is a perfect leisure destination.

It's not just home to international clothing brands like Zara and Uniqlo, but also Balinese-style beauty and lifestyle boutiques, Indonesian cuisine, Japanese restaurants, a cinema, and lush green spaces ideal for a stroll and photos. After shopping, you can head straight to Kuta Beach, making it easy to combine shopping, dining, and sunset-watching in one afternoon.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1791800230791-78.jpg)

## Where is Beachwalk Shopping Center? Location and Transportation

Beachwalk Shopping Center is located in Kuta, southern Bali, at Jl. Pantai Kuta No. 60, directly across from Kuta Beach.

The mall isn't far from Ngurah Rai International Airport, but traffic in Kuta is often heavy, especially during peak afternoon and evening hours, so the actual travel time may be longer than the map suggests. If you've just arrived in Bali, it's best to drop off your luggage first before heading over; if you're staying near Kuta, you can simply walk.

Beyond shopping, it pairs well with nearby attractions. A relaxed itinerary could be: shop in the afternoon, enjoy dinner, then cross the street to Kuta Beach for a sunset stroll.

## Architectural Highlights: Shopping in a Resort-Like Setting

One of Beachwalk's biggest draws is that it doesn't feel like a traditional, fully enclosed shopping mall.

The overall design embraces open and semi-outdoor spaces, inspired by Bali's iconic rice terraces. The architecture connects different levels with flowing, curved pathways, complemented by greenery, water features, and abundant natural light, letting you soak up a tropical island vacation vibe while you shop.

Spanning about 3.7 hectares, the complex stretches roughly 250 meters along the beach road. From the lush walkways inside to the outdoor seating areas, it's perfect for a leisurely stroll and snapping photos as you go.

If you're not keen on staying cooped up in air conditioning all day, Beachwalk's semi-outdoor design is a definite plus. However, some public areas are quite open, so keep an eye on the weather—especially during the afternoon downpours or when the sun is blazing.

## What to Buy at Beachwalk? International Brands and Balinese Curated Finds

Beachwalk's stores span clothing, beauty, sportswear, accessories, and lifestyle goods—whether you're looking for everyday outfits or travel essentials, you'll find plenty of options.

### Affordable Fashion and Everyday Wear

Fans of international fast fashion can browse stores like Zara, Mango, Stradivarius, Pull&Bear, Bershka, Uniqlo, and H&M.

If you suddenly need a new outfit, swimsuit, or a photo-ready look during your trip, there's no need to head to another mall.

### Boutiques, Sportswear & Accessories

The mall also features brands like Lacoste, Adidas, Guess, Kate Spade New York, Michael Kors, and Victoria's Secret, along with several stores for activewear, footwear, and accessories.

Don't miss the SKY shopping district on the third floor—it's worth a look for travelers eager to explore more unique brands.

### Beauty, Skincare & Balinese Brands

Beyond Sephora, Sociolla, The Body Shop, and L'Occitane, you'll find beauty and skincare brands like Sensatia Botanicals and Innisfree.

Among them, Sensatia Botanicals is a local Balinese skincare brand worth checking out. If you're already planning to buy natural-ingredient skincare, body care products, or travel souvenirs, it's a convenient stop.

## Where to Buy Bali Souvenirs? Candylicious & Cocomart

If you want to stock up on souvenirs one last time during your trip, Beachwalk is very convenient.

### Candylicious: A Must-Visit for Candy Lovers

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1791801064529-80.webp)

Candylicious is a specialty store themed around candies, chocolates, and various sweets, featuring a colorful array of packaged candies and chocolates.

Whether you're buying gifts for friends and family, sharing with colleagues, or bringing treats for the kids at home, you'll find fun options here. For families traveling with children, just browsing the candy store is a delight in itself.

### Cocomart: Stock Up on Snacks

Located on the B1 basement level, Cocomart is the mall's supermarket, perfect for buying snacks, drinks, and daily essentials.

If you plan to do some shopping before your trip ends, you can save Cocomart for last on your shopping list, making it easier to organize all your purchases at once.

However, if you're looking for Bali's specialty coffee, traditional pastries, or specific local souvenirs, it's still a good idea to check the product range first rather than relying on one mall for everything.

## What to Eat at Beachwalk? From Indonesian Cuisine to Japanese Restaurants

Beachwalk offers a diverse range of dining options, from Indonesian local dishes and Balinese cuisine to Japanese restaurants, cafes, and dessert shops. Even if your travel companions have different food preferences, it's easy to find something for everyone.

### Bebek Tepi Sawah: Give It a Try If You Like Roast Duck

Bebek Tepi Sawah is a restaurant known for its Indonesian-style cuisine and duck dishes, with the signature crispy duck being a popular choice among travelers.

If you're looking to enjoy Indonesian food again while shopping, this spot is worth considering. Compared to the usual fast food found in malls, restaurants like this can turn dining into part of the travel experience.

### Mr. Wayan by The Sea: Seaview and Indonesian Cuisine

For a dining spot with a better atmosphere, check out Mr. Wayan by The Sea.

It focuses on locally inspired dishes paired with coastal views. Whether you're craving familiar Indonesian fare like fried rice or noodles, or planning a relaxed dinner in the evening, this place is worth considering.

### Pasarame: Explore Indonesian Flavors in One Place

If you want to try a wider variety of Indonesian cuisine, Pasarame is another option.

Its dining concept covers traditional flavors from different regions of Indonesia, making it ideal for travelers who want to explore diverse local dishes in one spot. If your travel companions are interested in Indonesian food, this is also a great chance to compare culinary traditions from various areas.

### Padang Sederhana: Padang Cuisine

For those who enjoy Indonesian food, Padang Sederhana is worth a look.

Originating from the West Sumatra region, Padang cuisine features dishes like beef rendang, various curries, and spice-braised stews. Compared to local Balinese fare, these dishes use different spices and flavors, making them a great choice for travelers wanting to explore more of Indonesia's culinary culture.

### Not in the Mood for Indonesian Food? Here Are Other Options

If you're craving a change, Beachwalk also offers a variety of dining choices:

- **Japanese Cuisine**: Sushi Tei, Genki Sushi, Gyu-Kaku, Kimukatsu.
- **Other Asian Cuisine**: Zenbu, Goobne, Pepper Lunch.
- **Western Dining & Fast Food**: Cafe Sardinia, Burger King.
- **Coffee & Desserts**: %Arabica, Starbucks, Häagen-Dazs, Cold Stone Creamery, Venchi.

If you just want a quick bite or a cup of coffee, there's no need to plan a formal restaurant stop—you can decide based on your shopping pace that day.

## More Than Just Shopping: Movies, Public Spaces, and Beach Strolls

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1791800734019-79.jpg)

Beachwalk offers leisure options beyond shopping.

### Cinema XXI

The mall houses Cinema XXI on the second floor. If you encounter heavy rain, scorching heat, or want a relaxed activity after dinner, check the day's movie schedule.

Note that most films follow Indonesia's local release schedule. If you're looking for a specific movie or an English-language original version, it's best to confirm the showtime and language in advance.

### Sunset Deck and Mall Public Spaces

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-10/1791802872380-81.png)

The mall features various public spaces like Fountain Stage, Oasis Stage, Garden Pod, and Sunset Deck. Some areas host performances tied to events and festivals.

These spaces make Beachwalk more than just a shopping center—it's a place to stroll, rest, or catch live activities. Actual performances and schedules are subject to mall announcements.

### Walk to Kuta Beach for Sunset

This is what I consider Beachwalk's greatest advantage: the mall sits right across from Kuta Beach.

After finishing at the mall, follow the path leading to the beach to check the waves, take a stroll, or watch the sunset. If you have no other attractions planned for the day, you can easily combine shopping, dinner, and a beach walk into a half-day itinerary.

However, if you plan to watch the sunset at the beach, it's best to check the weather for that day and allow enough time to walk from the mall to the shore.

## Beachwalk Shopping Center Practical Info

**Address:** Jl. Pantai Kuta No. 60, Kuta, Badung, Bali 80361

**Opening Hours:**

- Monday to Thursday: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM
- Friday to Sunday: 10:00 AM – 11:00 PM

These are the current general opening hours; individual stores and special holidays or events may vary.

**Getting There:** You can take a taxi or use a ride-hailing service; the mall has a parking lot. If you're riding a scooter, it's a good idea to check the navigation route and entrance location in advance to avoid getting lost due to road layouts.

**Official Website:** [https://www.beachwalkbali.com/](https://www.beachwalkbali.com/)

**Instagram:** @beachwalk_bali

### When Is the Best Time to Visit?

If your main goal is shopping, I'd recommend going in the afternoon to avoid the hottest hours, combining indoor shopping with a break. If you also want to catch the sunset, head to Kuta Beach in the early evening.

The mall is usually liveliest from afternoon to early evening. If you prefer a relaxed shopping experience with time to browse, consider arriving a bit earlier.

Plan for about 2 to 3 hours for shopping and dining. If you also want to catch a movie, join an event, or spend time at the beach, set aside half a day.

## Final Thoughts: Spend Half a Day at Beachwalk on Your Kuta Trip

Beachwalk Shopping Center is a perfect fit for a more relaxed day during your Bali itinerary.

It's not just a shopping mall; it's a leisure spot that seamlessly blends international brands, local-style goods, diverse cuisine, movie entertainment, and a beach stroll.

Whether you've just arrived in Bali and want to ease in, need a break from the midday sun, or are looking for a place to eat and pick up snacks and souvenirs before heading home, this is a great option.

**My advice: spend the afternoon at Beachwalk, have dinner in the early evening, and finish off watching the sunset at Kuta Beach.** No need to rush between attractions—you can still make the day fulfilling. Next time you're in Kuta, consider adding this to your itinerary.
