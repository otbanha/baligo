**Bottom Line First**: Beginners can do it too—**Timbis Beach** in Uluwatu is Bali's most famous paragliding spot. **No running start needed**; the instructor waits for the wind on the cliff edge and takes off smoothly. The flight lasts about **10 minutes** in the air, with the instructor controlling the direction—you just enjoy the views and take photos. Hotel pickup is included (about a 1-hour drive), and a GoPro records the entire experience for you to take home.

I've always been the kind of person who's too scared to even ride a roller coaster. Every time I saw photos of others up in the sky, I felt a mix of envy and fear. This trip to Bali, I finally mustered the courage to try my first paragliding experience! Honestly, my hands were shaking when I booked it, and my mind kept replaying terrifying scenarios: What if I fall? What if the wind is too strong? But looking back now, all those worries were completely unnecessary~

Choosing Timbis Beach in Uluwatu was the right call! Not only is it Bali's most famous paragliding spot, but the professional instructor team and comprehensive safety measures put me completely at ease as a first-timer.

But getting ready to try my first paragliding, I couldn't help being super nervous before departure—after all, you take off from a cliff edge. But after actually experiencing it, I found **it's really not as scary as I imagined**, and the scenery is so stunning it makes you forget your fear!

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_214329c952f788b942561a41d700944d.jpg)

## **🚁 Paragliding Experience Process**

1. **Hotel Pickup & Check-in**
2. - The driver arrived on time in the morning, and it took about an hour to reach Timbis Beach.

- After arriving, I signed a safety consent form, and the instructor explained the process (mostly in English, but gestures worked too).

3. **Gear Up & Takeoff Instructions**
4. - The instructor helped me put on a safety harness and helmet that meet international safety standards, including a GoPro recording the whole time.
5. **Takeoff! The Thrilling Moment at the Cliff Edge**
6. - **No running needed!** The instructor led me to a slope on the cliff edge, adjusted the paraglider lines from behind, and **with a gentle push when the wind came**, we were instantly airborne!
7. **10 Minutes in the Air, Enjoying Unbeatable Ocean Views**
8. - The instructor controlled the direction, and I just had to enjoy the scenery and take photos!

- **The Indian Ocean is right beneath you**, the water is so blue it glows, and you can see the entire stretch of Uluwatu's cliffs—absolutely stunning!
- The instructor asked if I wanted '**a little thrill?**' then suddenly spun around, feeling like a roller coaster ride—super fun!

9. **Land on the beach, collect photos & videos**
10. - The landing was smoother than expected, and it was over as soon as my feet touched the ground.

    - The staff will transfer the GoPro videos and photos to your phone—**make sure to check they saved successfully!**

## **⚠️ Notes & Tips**

✅ **Before You Go**

- **Attire**: Wear **sneakers**, not flip-flops or sandals.
- **Sun Protection**: The UV rays are intense at high altitudes—be sure to apply sunscreen!
- **Weight Limit**: You must weigh between 40–110 kg (88–242 lbs) to fly—too light or too heavy won’t work.
- **Age Limit**: 5–66 years old

❌ **What Not to Bring**

- **Backpacks and handbags** (they can affect balance; small items can go in pockets).
- **Selfie sticks or anything that might easily fall off**.

💡 **Other Tips**

- **Weather-dependent**: Paragliding relies on wind. If the wind is too light or it rains, the flight may be canceled, so it’s best to **leave some buffer time**.
- **If you’re scared, tell your instructor**: They’re experienced and can keep the flight smoother.

### **🌟 Verdict: Safer and more fun than I imagined!**

![心得：比想像中安全又好玩！](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_967be453cf97a01700bb55bec2eb3271.jpg)

I thought it would be terrifying, but it was actually **super stable**—like floating slowly in the air. The instructor was really nice and even explained the scenery in English. **The most breathtaking part was seeing the Indian Ocean from above—that shade of blue is unforgettable!**

If you're into thrills, you can ask the instructor to do spins; if you're afraid of heights, a smooth flight is just as amazing. **Highly recommended for paragliding beginners**—it's really not that scary, and the views are totally worth it!

**Overall, I highly recommend it!** Next time I'm in Bali, I want to fly again~ 🪂💙
