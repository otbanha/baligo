## 1. Where is Bali and how do I get there?

Bali is an island belonging to Indonesia, geographically located in Southeast Asia. Surrounded by the sea, it borders Java to the west and Lombok to the east. With its unique character and diverse landscapes, Bali has become a popular Southeast Asian vacation destination, attracting travelers from around the world each year.

![1.峇里島在哪裡？怎麼去？](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e14497d695ce945e47d1b8738cd5faa6.gif)

ps. Taiwan's EVA Air and China Airlines both offer direct flights.

## 2. What language is spoken in Bali?

In Bali, locals primarily use Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia), the official language of Indonesia. However, due to Bali's unique culture, they also speak Balinese, a language widely used in daily life and considered the mother tongue for many residents. Balinese is mainly used at home, in religious ceremonies, and during traditional activities, playing a significant role in local religious and community life.

***

## 3. Do Balinese people speak English?

Generally speaking, Bali's tourism industry is highly developed, and many locals working in tourism-related jobs speak basic English. Whether at restaurants, hotels, shops, or attractions, most service staff can communicate in simple English, especially in southern areas like Kuta, Seminyak, and Jimbaran. However, you may still encounter language barriers when talking to elderly locals or residents in remote villages. Some older generations only speak Balinese.

For travelers, learning a few simple Indonesian phrases like "Halo" (Hello) and "Terima kasih" (Thank you) can make interactions more enjoyable and smoother.

## 4. What currency is used in Bali?

Bali uses the Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). Travelers can easily exchange currency at airports, banks, hotels, or money exchange offices in town. It's recommended to carry some cash for small shops, street stalls, or traditional markets, allowing for more flexible handling of small payments.

## 5. What is the climate like in Bali? What is the best time to visit?

Located near the equator, Bali has a **tropical monsoon climate**, warm and humid year-round. The climate is divided into two main seasons: the **dry season** and the **rainy season**. These distinct weather patterns make Bali suitable for travel all year, though the experience varies slightly by season.

![5.峇里島的氣候如何？什麼月份拜訪峇里島最好？](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_6e7fac42f701496457dff00feb523003.png)

**Dry Season (May to October)**

The dry season is Bali's peak tourist season, with moderate temperatures, lower humidity, plenty of sunshine, and clear skies. Daytime temperatures typically range from **27°C to 32°C**, cooling down slightly at night. This is the perfect time for outdoor activities like beach sunbathing, snorkeling, diving, and hiking, as well as water sports and capturing stunning sunsets.

**Rainy Season (November to April)**

The rainy season is Bali's low season. While rainfall is more frequent during this period, showers are typically brief and intermittent, rarely lasting for consecutive days. Daytime temperatures are similar to the dry season, but humidity is higher with more frequent rain. Temperatures still hover around **26°C to 30°C**. Rain mainly falls from afternoon to evening, leaving mornings and late evenings open for activities.

## 7. Is it better to travel to Bali independently or with a tour group?

**Advantages of independent travel:**

- High itinerary flexibility, allowing in-depth exploration based on personal interests
- Generally more affordable overall compared to group tours
- Ability to vacation at your own pace
- Greater variety of dining options

However, planning a free-and-easy trip requires considerable preparation:

- Transportation arrangements are complex, with limited public transit, requiring research into private drivers or car rentals
- Attractions are scattered and route planning is challenging, requiring study of distances and visiting order between sites
- You need to consider whether the character of each accommodation area (e.g., Kuta's lively vibe, Ubud's artistic atmosphere) suits your needs
- You must understand weather, food hygiene, and visiting etiquette

**Advantages of Group Tours:**

- No need to worry about transportation and accommodation arrangements
- Guided tours with explanations and support
- Well-planned itineraries, ideal for travelers with limited time
- No need to worry about language barriers

**Here are some factors to consider when choosing:**

1. Language skills - English is widely used in tourist areas, but if you don't speak any foreign language, joining a tour is recommended.
2. Trip duration - For short stays, a guided tour allows you to visit key spots quickly.
3. Budget planning - Independent travel lets you adjust expenses flexibly, while tours are mostly all-inclusive with no extra costs to worry about.
4. Travel style - Whether you prefer in-depth exploration or quick sightseeing.

Bali's cost of living is actually quite flexible, depending on your travel style and spending habits:

1. **Budget-Friendly Options:**

## 21. Is it convenient to use credit cards in Bali?

The credit card payment environment in Bali is quite mature, but there are a few things to keep in mind:

1. **Where cards are accepted:**
2. - Upscale restaurants, shopping malls, five-star hotels, and beach clubs usually accept international credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex).

- Many car rental companies and travel agencies also support credit card payments, but may charge an additional fee (around 1.5–3%).

3. **Where cards are not ideal:**
4. - Street stalls, small local eateries (Warung), traditional markets, and remote areas may only accept cash.

- Some restaurants have a minimum spending amount for card payments; you can't use a card below that threshold.

5. **Recommendations:**
6. - **Carry Indonesian Rupiah cash:** Cash is most convenient for small transactions or tips, especially in remote areas or when shopping at street stalls.

- **Bring a multi-currency credit card:** Visa and MasterCard are the most widely accepted.
- **Use digital payments:** GoPay, an e-wallet in Bali, is becoming increasingly popular; consider installing the relevant app. For reference: [Comprehensive Guide to Registering, Using, and Topping Up Gojek and GoPay in Indonesia/Bali](/en/blog/indonesia-gojek-gopay-guide/)

## 38. Are Bali's traditional dance performances worth watching?

![38.峇里島的傳統舞蹈表演值得看嗎？](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_82b8c46b8f153469d536f48be8633bf8.jpg)

When visiting Bali, experiencing the local traditional dances is an absolute must! Balinese dance is not just an art form but also carries deep religious and cultural significance. Through intricate costumes, graceful movements, and expressive gestures, these dances perfectly showcase Bali's unique cultural identity.

Here are several traditional Balinese dances worth watching:

1. **Kecak Dance**

This is one of Bali's most iconic traditional dances, also known as the "Monkey Dance." Inspired by the Hindu epic Ramayana, dancers sit in a circle and use rhythmic "chak-chak-chak" sounds to mimic a troop of monkeys, creating an electrifying atmosphere. The most famous venue for the Kecak Fire Dance is at Uluwatu Temple at sunset, where the golden sky and stunning ocean backdrop make for a mesmerizing visual and auditory experience.

[Top 6 Kecak Fire Dance Locations in Bali](/en/blog/bali-kecak-fire-dance-locations/)

2. **Barong Dance**

The Barong Dance tells the story of the eternal struggle between good and evil, featuring the benevolent mythical beast Barong battling the malevolent Rangda, symbolizing balance and harmony. Dancers wear elaborate costumes and showcase intricate movements, making this performance especially popular with families.

3. **Legong Dance**

Considered Bali's most graceful dance, the Legong is often performed by young girls. Through highly refined hand gestures and expressive eye movements, they bring enchanting mythological tales to life. Known for its tight rhythm and precise choreography, this dance is typically staged at Ubud Palace or local art villages, making it a favorite among art enthusiasts.

Barong and Legong dances can be enjoyed at the indoor Balerung Stage. For details, check [here](/en/blog/balerung-stage-cultural-show/).

**Why It's Worth Watching**

1. **Cultural Immersion**: These dances are deeply tied to Balinese religious rituals, offering a profound insight into local traditions.
2. **Visual Feast**: Accompanied by traditional gamelan music, the performances feature exquisite costumes and delicate movements that are a delight to behold.
3. **Family-Friendly**: Most dance shows are suitable for all ages, providing enjoyment for families, couples, and solo travelers alike.
