# Bali Tourist Hot Topics + News Roundup｜October 7, 2026

## 📚 Ubud Writers & Readers Festival Runs October 21–25, Featuring Over 150 Writers and Artists

If you happen to be in Bali in late October, this year's **Ubud Writers & Readers Festival (UWRF)** is worth checking out.

The 2026 event will take place **October 21–25** at Ubud Taman Baca and Kedewatan, marking its 23rd edition with the theme:

**Samarasa: Awareness. Empathy. Action.**

This year, over **150 writers, thinkers, journalists, artists, and poets** will participate, with more than 100 activities including author talks, book launches, workshops, literary lunches, spoken word performances, and art shows.

Ticket prices for general admission (Indonesian citizens):

- **4-Day Festival Pass: IDR 1,700,000**
- **1-Day Pass: IDR 600,000**
- Student 4-Day Pass: IDR 700,000
- Student 1-Day Pass: IDR 180,000

Tickets can be purchased through the official UWRF channels.

For the average traveler, I think the highlight of this event isn't just "listening to author talks"—it's the chance to **combine a Ubud trip with cultural activities**.

If you're already planning to stay in Ubud in late October, especially if you enjoy reading, art, film, and cultural events, it's worth setting aside a day for this.

## 🌊 German Tourist Drowned After Being Swept Away by Current at Tabanan Beach—Stay Cautious for Beach Activities

A 59-year-old German tourist, **Mikel Lanczek**, went with four friends to **Pantai Petangahan** in Tabanan on the morning of October 6.

Around 11:15 AM, he and two friends went swimming and gradually moved into deeper waters, when they were suddenly swept away by a current. Fellow travelers tried to rescue him with a bamboo pole, but failed.

Police, BPBD, and locals launched a search, finding his body about two hours later.

This incident serves as a special reminder for travelers heading to Bali soon:

**Don't decide to go into the water just by looking at how calm the sea surface appears.**

Especially along West Bali and the southern coast, some beaches are not safe for free swimming.

Today, BMKG also forecasts **wave heights of up to 3.5 meters in the southern Bali waters**, so anyone planning beach, surfing, snorkeling, or swimming activities in the near future should strictly follow on-site warnings.

## 😷 Bali Reports 9 Cases of Influenza A This Year; Health Officials Say No Need to Panic, But Take Precautions

Bali's Health Agency has released the latest monitoring data:

**From January to September 2026, a total of 113 influenza samples were tested, with 9 confirmed as Influenza A.**

These cases cannot fully represent the actual infection rate across all of Bali, as the sentinel surveillance is mainly concentrated at two medical facilities in Denpasar.

The Health Agency states that common symptoms of Influenza A include:

- Fever
- Cough
- Runny nose
- Headache

In general, most people recover on their own, but young children, the elderly, and those with weaker immune systems are at higher risk.

Therefore, there is **no need to cancel your trip to Bali just because of these 9 cases**.

Travelers can simply take basic precautions as usual, such as washing hands frequently, wearing a mask in crowded indoor spaces when appropriate, and avoiding large group activities if noticeable symptoms appear.

## 🚗 63 people have died in traffic accidents in Jembrana from January to September this year, with the Denpasar–Gilimanuk highway remaining a high-risk stretch

Traffic accident data from Jembrana this year is worth noting for travelers.

From January to September 2026, Jembrana recorded **465 traffic accidents**, resulting in:

**63 deaths**

Causing approximately **IDR 1.054 billion** in property damage.

One key reason is that Jembrana is home to the **Denpasar–Gilimanuk National Highway**, where large trucks, long-distance buses, cargo trucks, and regular vehicles mix, making certain sections of the road more hazardous.

Police also noted that **82 accidents this year involved animals suddenly entering the road**, along with other road safety issues such as low-hanging telecom or fiber optic cables.

If your Bali itinerary takes you all the way to:

**Tabanan → Negara → Gilimanuk**

Especially if you're catching a ferry to Java, I still recommend **not treating Google Maps' estimated time as a guaranteed time**.

## 🌬️ Karangasem Issues Strong Wind Warning, Southern Bali Waves Up to 3.5 Meters

Most of Bali's land areas are forecast to be sunny to partly cloudy today, but BMKG has issued a strong wind warning specifically for **Karangasem**.

From 08:00 on October 7 to 08:00 on October 8, Karangasem is the only regency currently under a strong wind advisory.

Meanwhile, **wave heights in southern Bali waters could reach up to 3.5 meters**, while northern waters are expected to see waves of about 0.5 to 2 meters.

So the most practical impact of today's weather information for travelers isn't 'will it rain in Bali,' but rather:

**Marine activities may require more caution than land-based ones.**

If you have ferry trips, snorkeling, diving, or surfing planned in Sanur, Padangbai, Amed, or other areas, it's best to reconfirm with the ferry company and check BMKG sea conditions on the day of departure.
