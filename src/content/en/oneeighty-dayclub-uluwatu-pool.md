## Oneeighty Dayclub

Guests at Oneeighty can unwind in the cliffside VIP area or stretch out on sun loungers in the relaxation zone. You can also sit comfortably indoors while enjoying the cool, natural breeze.

Beyond this one-of-a-kind pool, visitors can also capture stunning shots at the artificial river/lagoon beneath Oneeighty's glass bridge. Though man-made, its beauty rivals that of natural lakes—many of Bali's iconic Instagram photos are taken here!

![Oneeighty Dayclub](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_32615e318e839d897e9701fd01a67e9f.jpg)

Oneeighty is not just a popular attraction but also offers an unforgettable culinary journey. Popular appetizers include Baja Taco, Soft Shell Crab, and Indonesian Tuna Ceviche, while main dishes feature small snapper, local lobster, and a trio of fish with mango.

If you want to spend a leisurely day at Oneeighty snapping Instagram-worthy photos, you have two options:

Book a VIP ticket (for up to 3 people) with front-row cliffside ocean views, currently priced at Rp3,127,628 (sharing one large daybed), which converts to about USD$200. Rp1,000,000 of this can be used toward food and drinks. **Note:** Children under 12 are not allowed in this area.

The other option is a single general admission ticket, priced at Rp615,000, which converts to about USD$40. This includes approximately USD$32 (Rp500,000) in food and drink credit per person.
