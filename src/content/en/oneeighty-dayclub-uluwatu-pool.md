## Oneeighty Dayclub

Guests visiting Oneeighty can relax in the cliffside VIP lounge or stretch out on sun loungers in the rest area. Visitors can also sit comfortably indoors while enjoying the cool, natural breeze.

Beyond this one-of-a-kind pool, guests can capture photos at the artificial river/lagoon beneath Oneeighty's glass bridge. Though man-made, its beauty rivals that of natural lakes—many of Bali's iconic influencer shots are taken here!

![Oneeighty Dayclub](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_32615e318e839d897e9701fd01a67e9f.jpg)

Oneeighty is not only a celebrated attraction but also offers an unforgettable culinary journey. Popular appetizers include Baja Taco, Soft Shell Crab, and Indonesian Tuna Ceviche, while main dishes feature small snapper, local lobster, and a trio of fish with mango.

If you want to spend a leisurely day at Oneeighty snapping some influencer-worthy photos, you have two options:

Book a VIP ticket (up to 3 people) for a front-row cliffside sea view spot, currently priced at Rp3,127,628 (shared large daybed), roughly USD$200. Of this, 1,000,000 Indonesian Rupiah can be used toward food and drinks. **Note:** Children under 12 are not allowed in this area.

The other option is a single general admission ticket priced at 615,000 Indonesian Rupiah, roughly USD$40. This includes about USD$32 (500,000 Rupiah) per person in food and drink credit.

When visiting Oneeighty, a few rules must be followed: no outside food or drinks, no strollers or wheelchairs, no drones, and wedding photography is not permitted.
