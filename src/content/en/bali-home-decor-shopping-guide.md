## 📍 Three Treasure-Hunting Routes Recommended (Google Maps Navigable)

### 1️⃣ First Stop: Near Ubud Palace (about 14 minutes by car)

**Street: JI. Raya Andong**
The roadside is lined with various small shops selling:

- Woven lamps
- Chairs
- Bags
- Bamboo baskets
- Dream catchers
- **Shell lamps** and other handmade crafts

### 2️⃣ Second Stop: Near Seminyak (about 14 minutes by car from Seminyak Village shopping mall)

**Street: JI. Gn. Athena**
The shops here have a more upgraded look, but the prices are still **very affordable**!

### 3️⃣ Route 3: Canggu Main Road (Lots of home decor shops along this stretch)

Street name: Jl. Raya Canggu

## 🧳 Quick Tips

- **Bring an empty suitcase** to fill up
- Avoid budget airlines — the baggage weight and size limits will make you cry
- For three-dimensional items like woven baskets and hanging lamps, make sure to confirm **whether they can be checked in after packaging**
