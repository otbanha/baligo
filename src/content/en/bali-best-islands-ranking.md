## Bali Climbs to World's Top 3! Travel + Leisure's 2025 Best Islands Ranking Unveiled

![峇里島躍升世界前3！《Travel + Leisure》2025最佳島嶼排行揭曉](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_fd614936412bc49943234a0f0b484811.jpg)

In the authoritative travel magazine [Travel + Leisure's 2025 World's Best Islands](https://www.travelandleisure.com/worlds-best-awards-2025-islands-world-11749514) poll, Bali secured the 3rd spot, once again showcasing its immense appeal on the global travel stage. This marks Bali's eleventh consecutive year on the list, standing out from hundreds of thousands of reader votes, proving that its cultural charm and natural beauty continue to deeply move travelers from around the world.

## Why Does Bali Captivate Global Travelers?

Bali is not only one of Indonesia's most popular tourist destinations but also a sacred island that seamlessly blends natural landscapes with spiritual culture. From a religious tradition boasting over 10,000 temples to UNESCO-recognized rice terraces and volcanic vistas, every corner holds unique stories and energy.

![峇里島為何能吸引全球旅人？](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_8eeac6deb511c17075b54478c049814f.jpg)

Whether you're looking to stay in world-class luxury resorts or opt for charming local budget accommodations, Bali has it all. Many lodgings offer high-quality service and stunning views at prices far more affordable than other international hotspots—**incredible value for money**—making it a top pick for both budget-conscious and high-end travelers alike.

## Bali's Cultural Highlights

Bali's charm lies not only in its natural beauty but also in its profound cultural heritage:

- **Traditional dances like the Barong and Kecak fire dance**
- **Daily rituals and ceremonies woven into every aspect of life**
- **The locals' reverence for nature and the divine**, shaping a unique life philosophy

![峇里島文化特色](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2664ad6d94007006dec606128661dd90.jpg)

All of this blends into Bali's distinctive "Way of Harmony" (Tri Hita Karana), perfectly uniting God, people, and nature.

## Bali Also Reigns Supreme in Asia-Pacific

Beyond ranking among the world's top three, Bali last month beat Thailand's Koh Samui and Vietnam's Phu Quoc Island to be named the **Best Island in Asia-Pacific** at the *Travel + Leisure* Luxury Awards Asia Pacific. This award focuses on the luxury travel market, further proving that Bali offers both accessible and high-end appeal, catering to all types of travelers.

## Detailed World Ranking List

### 1. Paros, Greece

- Features classic Greek blue-and-white villages, especially the charming town of Naoussa.
- Known for local dishes featuring mushrooms, chickpeas, and seafood.
- High-end resorts abound, but the true luxury is the endless Aegean Sea.

**Score: 96.50**

---

### 2. Palawan, Philippines

- Untouched nature with dramatic limestone cliffs and coral reefs.
- Secluded white-sand beaches, a paradise for divers.
- The Underground River National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage site, with stunning caves and ancient forests.

**Score: 93.60**

---

### 3. Bali, Indonesia

- Bali boasts deep cultural roots, with over 10,000 temples and stunning rice terraces.
- The newly opened Anantara Ubud Bali Resort blends modern and traditional design.
- A single trip can span beaches, mountains, and cultural villages.

**Score: 93.25**

---

### 4. Ischia, Italy

- Italy's thermal spa destination, famous for over 100 natural hot springs.
- Charming coastal towns offer a blend of sea views and wellness travel.
- Luxury spa hotels like Mezzatorre attract global celebrities.

**Score: 93.25**

---

### 5. Fiji Islands

- Composed of over 300 islands, featuring stunning white sand beaches and tropical scenery.
- A blend of diverse cultures, with uniquely flavorful local cuisine.
- A cultural and natural paradise in the South Pacific.

**Score: 93.20**

---

### 6. Maldives

- Islands scattered across the Indian Ocean, the top choice for honeymoons and luxury getaways.
- Offers private islands and overwater villas, perfect for diving and snorkeling.
- Local culture is also worth exploring, such as learning Dhivehi or enjoying traditional afternoon tea.

**Score: 92.65**

---

### 7. Galápagos Islands (Ecuador)

- Rich in wildlife, including Galápagos penguins, giant tortoises, and marine iguanas.
- A paradise for ecotourism enthusiasts.
- Exploring multiple islands by cruise allows for in-depth wildlife viewing.

**Score: 91.23**

---

### 8. Hvar & Dalmatian Islands (Croatia)

- Hvar is known for luxury vacations and vibrant nightlife.
- The island features lavender fields, olive groves, and wineries.
- The Dalmatian coastal islands are breathtakingly beautiful and steeped in history.

**Score: 91.09**

---

### 9. Madeira, Portugal

- Famous for its wine, natural coastal pools, and stunning forests.
- Diverse terrain, perfect for hiking and relaxing with ocean views.
- Unique culture blending African and Portuguese influences.

**Score: 89.93**

---

### 10. Mallorca, Spain

- Combines nightlife, cuisine, and serene mountain landscapes.
- Explore towns and ports on a vintage tram.
- Ideal for family trips, romantic getaways, or solo escapes.

**Score: 89.54**

---

### 11. Crete, Greece

- Greece's largest island, with history dating back to the Minoan civilization.
- Features dramatic mountains and ancient fishing villages—a paradise for archaeology enthusiasts.
- Both the beaches and the city of Heraklion offer unique charms.

**Score: 89.49**

---

### 12. Phuket, Thailand

- A famous beach destination rich in history and culture.
- The old town features Sino-Portuguese architecture.
- Temples, night markets, and local cuisine are top attractions for visitors.

**Score: 89.47**

---

### 13. Vancouver Island, Canada

- Close to the city yet filled with pristine forests and rugged coastlines.
- Victoria offers British-style architecture and museums.
- Ideal for nature adventures and luxury eco-tourism.

**Score: 89.31**

---

### 14. Koh Samui, Thailand

- Filming location for 'The White Lotus,' featuring beautiful bays and forests.
- Stay in five-star hotels or explore inland waterfalls.
- Rich in temples and local food culture.

**Score: 89.31**

---

### 15. Anguilla

- Features 33 world-class beaches, such as Shoal Bay.
- A blend of exceptional cuisine and signature Caribbean hospitality.
- Resorts like Cap Juluca are top luxury choices.

**Score: 89.21**

---

### 16. Milos, Greece

- A lesser-known Greek island, serene and enchanting.
- Home to traditional fishing villages and unique white rock formations.
- Ideal for travelers seeking to avoid crowds.

**Score: 89.11**

---

### 17. Great Barrier Reef Islands, Australia

- Includes the famous Whitsunday Islands and rainforest-covered isles.
- Serves as the gateway to exploring the world's largest coral reef system.
- Perfect for low-carbon, eco-friendly travel.

**Score: 88.95**

---

### 18. (Tie) Azores, Portugal

- Located in the North Atlantic, comprising 9 volcanic islands.
- Each island offers hot springs, wineries, and forest trails.
- A hidden gem cherished by Europeans.

**Score: 88.89**

---

### 18. (Tie) Dominica

- Unlike typical Caribbean islands, it features mountains, waterfalls, and hot springs.
- Morne Trois Pitons National Park is a UNESCO World Heritage site.
- Ideal for travelers who love adventure and nature.

**Score: 88.89**

---

### 20. Cape Breton Island, Canada

- A rich blend of Indigenous, Scottish, and Acadian cultures.
- Stunning highland landscapes and trails like the Skyline Trail are worth exploring.
- Food and seafood are also highlights.

**Score: 88.79**

---

### 21. Maui, USA

- Combines the best of Hawaii's beaches, volcanoes, and tropical rainforests.
- The Road to Hana is one of the most famous scenic drives.
- Great for family and couple getaways.

**Score: 88.26**

---

### 22. Sardinia, Italy

- Rich in Mediterranean history, with cuisine influenced by diverse cultures.
- Best known for the Costa Smeralda (Emerald Coast) and the dunes of Costa Verde.
- Explore the ancient Nuragic civilization ruins at Su Nuraxi.

**Score: 88.22**

---

### 23. Bora-Bora, French Polynesia

- Famous worldwide for its crystal-clear lagoon and overwater bungalows.
- Volcanic terrain and coral reefs create a tropical paradise.
- A synonym for luxury and romance.

**Score: 88.11**

---

### 24. St. Lucia

- The twin volcanic peaks, the Pitons, are the island's iconic symbol.
- Romantic resorts and abundant natural landscapes attract couples.
- Cuisine and historical culture are also outstanding.

**Score: 87.56**

---

### 25. Island of Hawaii, USA

- The largest island in Hawaii, home to active volcanoes and black sand beaches.
- Hilo's local culture and Kona coffee are widely acclaimed.
- Volcanoes National Park offers stunning landscape experiences.

**Score: 87.37**
