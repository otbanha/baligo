Bali's five-star hotels are overwhelming. Ubud has [Capella](/en/blog/capella-ubud-luxury-resort/), Uluwatu has [Alila](/en/blog/alila-villas-uluwatu-bali/), Nusa Dua has [Apurva Kempinski](/en/blog/apurva-kempinski-bali-nusa-dua/)... But the biggest surprise of 2026 is a brand-new resort in Sanur—**The Meru Sanur**.

Just this month, it won the highest honor in the '2026 Best New Hotel in Indonesia' awards from one of Asia's most influential travel media outlets, **Tatler Asia**, and was also named to the 'Top 20 Best Hotels in Indonesia' list. For a newly opened hotel, this is like crashing the elite club and making a grand entrance.

***

## Built in Sanur, a Surprisingly Bold Location Choice

![蓋在沙努爾，這個選址有點出乎意料](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-04/1776479383196-stunning-beachfront-bali-resort-wins-best-new-hotel-in-2026-at-tourism-awards-768x512.jpg)

Sanur has long been positioned as the 'quiet side of Bali'—no Kuta chaos, no Seminyak buzz. Those who love it want a place with a bit of tradition and a slower pace.

But the arrival of The Meru Sanur suddenly offers a completely different option for Sanur.

It sits at the northern end of Sanur, within the 42-hectare 'Sanur Special Economic Zone,' which also houses the Bali International Hospital, the Bali Beach Convention Center, and an ethnomedicinal plant garden. That sounds official, but the real highlight is the 1-kilometer-long private white sand beach.

***

## What's It Like to Stay There?

The lobby soars like a temple, with flowing water, traditional Balinese stone carvings, and large wooden structures interwoven. The first glance makes it clear why this place won design awards.

Four buildings are connected by breezeways, with extensive use of local timber and natural stone, complemented by tropical plants and water features. The overall atmosphere is so serene that you'll forget you're traveling—it feels more like a retreat.

All guest rooms start at the suite level—the most basic Tropical Suite is 83 square meters, complete with a standalone bathtub, rain shower, and private balcony. The top-tier Presidential Suite spans 209 square meters, so spacious you might need a moment to find the bathroom. The design incorporates plenty of Indonesian local timber and traditional Balinese craftsmanship, offering not a cold modern luxury but a warm, refined elegance.

For dining, the main restaurant Arunika serves a buffet breakfast starting at 6:30 AM daily. Word has it the Sunday Brunch is quite the spectacle, featuring roast lamb, roast pork, grilled prawns, sashimi, and a dessert station—rivaling the signature offerings of Bali's long-established hotels. The Taru Pramana spa takes a healing approach, focusing on mind-body balance rather than just standard massage services.

## What Does Tatler Asia's Recognition Mean?

This year's awards ceremony was held at the Bali Beach Convention Center right next to The Meru Sanur, covering hotel, restaurant, and bar selections across Asia.

In the list of Indonesia's Top 20 Hotels, Bali claimed 11 spots, including perennial favorites like [Mandapa, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve](/en/blog/ubud-resorts-guide/#%E5%B3%87%E9%87%8C%E5%B3%B6%E7%9A%84%E5%AF%A7%E9%9D%9C%E8%81%96%E6%AE%BFmandapa-a-ritz-carlton-reserve), [Capella Ubud](/en/blog/capella-ubud-luxury-resort/), and [The Apurva Kempinski](/en/blog/apurva-kempinski-bali-nusa-dua/). The Meru Sanur's emergence as a "new hotel" among these established heavyweights is not just about hardware—it's because it nails Indonesian hospitality.

## If You're Planning to Go, Here's What to Know First

- **Address**: Jl. Hang Tuah, Sanur Kaja, Denpasar, Bali (🗺️[Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=12W9y9WtufmH2a430BAds6QtTa5TyHoE&usp=sharing))
- **Number of Rooms**: 184 all-suite
- **Distance from Airport**: About 13 km (roughly 30–40 minutes by car)
- **Special Perk**: Very close to the Sanur fast boat pier, making trips to Nusa Penida super convenient
- **Best For**: Travelers seeking a quiet beach, spacious rooms, and high-quality service; families with kids will appreciate the kids' club
- **Full Review**: See [here](/en/blog/the-meru-sanur/)

The northern end of Sanur has indeed been growing in popularity in recent years, shedding its old reputation as a place 'only for older travelers.' The Meru Sanur has accelerated this transformation.
