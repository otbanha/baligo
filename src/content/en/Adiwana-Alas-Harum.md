Finding a hotel in Ubud that offers profound jungle serenity without feeling completely cut off is rare, and Adiwana Alas Harum is hard to beat. This hotel, which just opened in February 2026, was designed by award-winning architect Popo Danes. It sits at the start of the Campuhan Ridge Walk, overlooking Ubud's most famous jungle valley—the sacred Wos River winding through, with the silhouette of Mount Agung in the distance. Its 45 suites and 10 private pool villas blend traditional Balinese craftsmanship into modern design, letting you stay in a space that truly respects local culture.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784125769170-1mc6512000rk9lc30f11e_w_1280_853_r5.webp)

## 📍 Location & Surroundings

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784125820296-1.webp)

The hotel is located in the village of Keliki, north of Ubud. It looks remote, but it's actually just a 5–10 minute drive from Ubud's center. Even better, you can access the Campuhan Ridge Walk directly from the hotel's doorstep—Ubud's most classic morning hiking trail is practically in your backyard. This 2-kilometer trail passes the sacred Gunung Lebah Temple, flanked by deep green valleys and rice paddies, with every turn looking like a movie still. The hotel itself is in the small village of Bangkiang Sidem at the trail's start, so you don't need to queue or drive—just wake up and go.

[https://youtube.com/shorts/3PmPpoHI_6c](https://youtube.com/shorts/3PmPpoHI_6c)

The surrounding area is quiet but has everything you need. Heading into Ubud center, the Ubud Monkey Forest is a 15-minute drive, Ubud Palace is 10 minutes away, and the Sacred Art Market is 15 minutes. For more adventure, Tegenungan Waterfall is also nearby. Restaurants and cafes are scattered along the Campuhan Ridge, with Nari Restaurant perched above Gunung Lebah Temple offering stunning views—the hotel staff will be happy to arrange it for you.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784125912524-1mc1j12000rk9rok1e992_w_1280_853_r5.webp)

## 🏨 Facilities Overview

Stepping into the lobby, you'll be amazed by the walls full of traditional Balinese carvings and natural materials—wood, stone, bamboo, and rattan—every detail showing care. From downstairs, you can see the infinity pool, its clear blue water seemingly flowing straight into the jungle.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784125969035-1mc6z12000rk9kvmz2d63_w_1280_853_r5.webp)

The two main restaurants each have their own character. Black Garlic Restaurant features ceilings adorned with ancient Kamasan paintings depicting traditional Balinese life, making you feel like you're dining in a cultural heritage site. Flame Bar & Grill is more relaxed, perfect for cocktail hour. Breakfast is cooked-to-order, not a buffet—the chef prepares it fresh based on your preferences, whether you want pancakes, an omelet, or traditional Indonesian dishes.

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784126009236-1mc3612000rk9knfa3d9a_w_1280_853_r5.webp)

Tejas Spa Alas Harum is a tranquil corner of the hotel, offering not only traditional massages but also signature Balinese therapies. The gym is open 24 hours, with yoga classes led by instructors (Wednesday and Sunday mornings). The hotel puts real effort into cultural activities—mandala making, boreh making (traditional herbal skincare), jamu making (traditional herbal medicine), and Balinese traditional dance classes on Tuesdays and Fridays. If you're bringing kids, there are daily painting classes. Even more special, the hotel leads guests on the Campuhan Ridge Walk every morning at 7 AM, saving you the trouble of Googling it yourself.

## 👥 Who It's For

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784126936671-unnamed.webp)

Honeymooners are the perfect fit for Adiwana—private pools, serene ambiance, and romantic dining. Those seeking spiritual growth will also love it, with yoga, meditation, and cultural activities galore. Digital nomads and long-stay travelers are well-suited too, thanks to fast WiFi and a quiet environment. If you're a family with kids, the children's painting activities and gentle atmosphere are welcoming, but be prepared for lots of stairs and walking up and down.

They offer a variety of massage treatments, including a **60-minute Balinese signature massage, aromatherapy massage, deep tissue massage, deep relaxation massage, healing candle massage**, as well as a 90-minute herbal compress therapy. If you've planned a jungle getaway in Ubud, consider adding a spa to your accommodation itinerary, allowing yourself to unwind with a relaxing healing session after exploring the rice terraces, waterfalls, and nearby attractions. Even if you're not solely visiting for a massage, it's a wonderful extra indulgence during your vacation.

## Honest Little Details

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-07/1784126968962-unnamed_2_.webp)

Let's also mention the hotel's downsides. Though not far from the town center, its location is somewhat remote—if you prefer walking to restaurants and shops, this isn't the place. There are many stairs, which can be tiring with large luggage. The restaurant serves delicious food, but prices are on the higher side (by Indonesian standards); for cheaper eats, head into town. Some room types (especially villas) are quite far from the main building, and walking at night can feel a bit isolated, though the hotel provides flashlights and 24-hour security, so safety isn't an issue.

Overall, Adiwana Alas Harum isn't a "cheap and high-value" hotel, but it truly justifies its price. If you're looking to deeply relax in Ubud, soak in the jungle serenity, and appreciate quality service, this hotel will make you want to return again and again.
