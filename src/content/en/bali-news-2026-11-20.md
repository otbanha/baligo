# **Bali Latest News | November 20: Rainy Season Heavy Rain Warning, Airport Flight Changes, and Attraction Updates**

Key updates for international travelers on November 19 focus on rainy season weather, air traffic, and attraction status. BMKG's latest warning indicates heavy rain in parts of Bali; Ngurah Rai International Airport also experienced a power outage the previous afternoon, affecting 4 international flights.

## **🌧️ BMKG Issues Heavy Rain Warning! Heavy Downpours Expected in 4 Regencies, Southern Sea Waves Over 2 Meters**

The Bali Region III Meteorological Center of the Indonesian Agency for Meteorology, Climatology and Geophysics (BMKG) issued a weather warning on November 18, alerting that from November 19 to 20, parts of Bali may experience moderate to heavy rainfall, with some areas reaching heavy rain warning levels.

According to the forecast, the warning areas from November 19 to 20 include:

Heavy Rain Warning

- Badung Regency
- Bangli
- Buleleng
- Tabanan

Other areas should watch for moderate rainfall, including Denpasar, Gianyar, Jembrana, Karangasem, and Klungkung.

**Overall Weather Forecast for November 20:**

| Item | Forecast |
| --- | --- |
| Weather | Moderate rain, possibly heavy in some areas |
| Temperature | 20–32°C |
| Relative Humidity | 65–95% |
| Wind Direction | East to south |
| Wind Speed | 4–37 km/h |
| Northern Sea Wave Height | 0.25–1 meter |
| Southern Sea Wave Height | 1–2 meters |

BMKG also warns that parts of the southern waters of Bali and the southern Lombok Strait may experience wave heights of 2 meters or more.

This wave warning is expected to last until 8:00 AM Bali time on November 22.

**Impact on Travelers:**

- In mountainous areas like Ubud, Kintamani, and Bedugul, watch for rain and slippery roads.
- For visits to waterfalls, rice terraces, and outdoor natural attractions, check if the sites are open before heading out.
- When traveling to Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, and other outlying islands, check with the ferry company for the latest sea conditions.
- If there's a thunderstorm, it's not recommended to continue with water activities or outdoor mountain excursions.

## **✈️ Power Outage at Ngurah Rai International Airport! 4 International Flights Delayed, Power Restored**

On the afternoon of November 18, a power supply anomaly occurred in the international terminal of Bali's Ngurah Rai International Airport, affecting some passenger services.

According to data released by airport management, the outage lasted approximately 75 minutes, from around 3:45 PM to 5:00 PM.

This incident impacted 4 international departure flights, with an average delay of about 30 minutes:

| Airline | Flight Number | Route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AirAsia | AK-377 | Bali - Kuala Lumpur |
| Malaysia Airlines | MH-850 | Bali - Kuala Lumpur |
| EVA Air | BR-256 | Bali - Taipei |
| Indonesia AirAsia | QZ-536 | Bali - Melbourne |

The airport stated that power in the international terminal was restored around 5:00 PM, the domestic terminal was unaffected, and overall flight operations were not completely disrupted.

As for the cause of the outage, PLN initially indicated that one of the power feeders supplying the international terminal experienced a load drop, which may have been the reason for the anomaly, though further confirmation is still needed.

The airport authority has provided beverages, snacks, and on-site assistance to affected passengers, and has apologized for the inconvenience caused by this incident.

**Reminders for Travelers:**

Power has been restored, but that doesn't necessarily mean subsequent flights will be unaffected. However, if you're flying out of Bali today or in the coming days, it's recommended to:

- Check with your airline for the latest flight status before heading to the airport.
- Don't rely solely on third-party flight tracking websites.
- Allow plenty of time for airport check-in.
- For airport service information, contact InJourney Airports customer service at 172.

## **🏛️ Klungkung Bali Cultural Center Closed from November 15! No General Visitors During Construction**

The Pusat Kebudayaan Bali (Bali Cultural Center) in Klungkung Regency has been closed to the public since November 15, 2026, until further notice from the operating authority.

According to the relevant announcement, this temporary closure is to facilitate ongoing construction work at the cultural center and ensure the safety of the public and visitors during the construction period.

This project is spearheaded by PT Pusat Kebudayaan Bali, with an overall budget of approximately IDR 1.3 trillion, implemented as a multi-year endeavor and expected to be completed by December 2027.

In the future, this cultural park is set to become a major venue for large-scale cultural and artistic events in Bali, potentially hosting some of the Pesta Kesenian Bali activities currently held at the Denpasar Art Centre.

Confirmed opening information so far:

- Temporary closure start date: November 15, 2026
- Reopening date: Not yet announced
- Reason for closure: Subsequent construction work and safety concerns
- Applicable area: Bali Cultural Center complex in Klungkung

It is important to note that this refers to the Pusat Kebudayaan Bali in Klungkung, not the Taman Budaya Art Centre in Denpasar—these are two distinct cultural facilities.
