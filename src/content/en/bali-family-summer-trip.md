**Key Takeaways**: Four tested tips for a summer family trip to Bali with kids—① Visa on arrival was processed **without long waits**, and paying in USD will get you change in Indonesian Rupiah; ② Traffic **jams all day** are the norm, so don't overpack your itinerary; ③ The weather is pleasant but **UV rays are strong**, so sun protection is a must; ④ Resort pools are **quite deep**, so be sure to bring floaties or other buoyancy aids for kids.

Taking from the group, giving back to the group. Just returned from Bali and want to share our travel experience. On this trip, we experienced everything from entry and transportation to weather, activities, and food. I hope these insights help with your travel planning.

## Smooth Entry

We opted for a visa on arrival and flew to Bali with China Airlines. After landing, the entry process went very smoothly with almost no waiting time. The immigration officers were efficient and friendly. We paid the visa fee in USD, and they gave us change in Indonesian Rupiah—simple and straightforward.

## Traffic in Bali

Traffic in Bali is terrible—it's congested almost all day. So, it's best not to pack your itinerary too tightly, or you'll end up exhausted. We prepared well before the trip, but we still had to adjust our schedule flexibly to avoid travel delays affecting the experience.

## Pleasant Weather

The weather in Bali is very comfortable. Although the sun is strong, it doesn't feel as harsh on the skin as Taiwan's heat. However, you can still get sunburned easily, so be sure to take sun protection measures, including applying sunscreen, wearing protective clothing, and a hat.

## Resort Pool Depth

Resort pools in Bali are typically deeper than those in Taiwan, which is a noticeable difference. If you're traveling with children, parents need to pay extra attention to their safety. It's recommended to bring arm floats or other flotation devices to ensure kids can play safely in the water.

## Hard Rock Hotel's Kids Club

Hard Rock Hotel is truly perfect for families with kids. One day, we let our children join the full-day Kids Club activities while we adults went shopping at the nearby department store and enjoyed a spa. Not only did we get a peaceful day to ourselves, but the kids also had a blast. We stayed at the Hard Rock Hotel in Kuta for the last three days. The Kids Club is very popular, so I recommend booking in advance. The hotel provides a WhatsApp contact, and you can also email to reserve ahead. If your itinerary is set, it's safer to book early since cancellations are allowed before 10 a.m. I booked upon arrival and was lucky to snag the last spots.

## Delicious Food

The food in Bali is absolutely delicious—we didn't encounter any duds this trip. However, since our kids are wary of spicy food, I suggest asking the staff if a dish is spicy when ordering to avoid ruining their dining experience. There are plenty of restaurants near Hard Rock Hotel, and we picked a random one without any disappointment. Within walking distance, there are two department stores and many dining options, with Jamie Oliver's restaurant, KFC, and McDonald's at the back entrance. I honestly think the KFC fries in Bali are especially tasty.

## Thrilling White-Water Rafting Experience

**Quick Reference:** [Bali Rafting Guide (FAQ)](/en/blog/bali-rafting-ayung-telaga-waja/)

This time, we also tried white-water rafting in Ubud, and it was a blast. With so many options available, we simply asked our driver to book it for us, and he found a well-reviewed operator. I recommend choosing the full 2-hour trip rather than the 1-hour one for a more complete experience. We had two younger kids with us—one just over five and the other seven—and they both had a great time. The rafting activity lasted about two hours; we set off from our villa in Ubud at 9 AM, enjoyed a simple lunch included afterward, and wrapped up around 1 PM. To reach the rafting starting point, you need to walk down a long flight of stairs, but our kids managed it slowly without any trouble—it wasn't too difficult.

## Fun Hair Braiding Experience

The hair braiding experience in Bali is a lot of fun and really adds to the vacation vibe. I saw many foreign kids with braids in various colors, so I took my children to try it too—it was truly unique.

Our family got our braids done on the street near Jamie Oliver's restaurant at Kuta Beach. This street is lined with many spa shops that offer braiding services, with sample braids displayed at their entrances. I thought they would use a lot of hairspray to secure the braids, but they didn't. When removing the braids, you just cut off the fake hair—pretty convenient.

## Convenient Car Charter Service

This time we opted for a private car service (driver + Chinese-speaking guide). Hiring a guide who speaks Chinese not only solved the language barrier but also made our trip more relaxed and enjoyable. For instance, we adults enjoyed three spa sessions—once the guide looked after the kids at our villa, another time he took them out for a meal, allowing us to fully unwind during the spa.
