## Anantara Uluwatu Bali Resort

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_6658304da6f8325b8c8840cf8cd0f829.jpg)

Anantara Uluwatu Bali Resort is a truly unique and worthwhile hotel in Bali, honored with the '2023 Travelers' Choice' award.

Perched on a cliff in southern Bali, this hotel offers direct access to spectacular ocean views—absolutely breathtaking! Its standout feature is that some ocean-view rooms come with private infinity pools overlooking the sea, allowing you to enjoy the stunning scenery from your own secluded pool. This design is extremely rare in Bali and a major selling point of the Anantara.

It's also one of Bali's premier wedding venues, with a sacred cliffside chapel overlooking the ocean, making for a romantic seaside ceremony. Anantara also offers luxury wedding planning services.

Whether you're planning a honeymoon, a relaxing getaway, a wedding, or a party, Anantara Uluwatu is an excellent choice. If you want to experience a one-of-a-kind cliffside hotel in Bali, you must stay here at least once!
