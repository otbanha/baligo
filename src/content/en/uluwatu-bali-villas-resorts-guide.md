Seascape sits on a cliff in Pecatu on the Bukit Peninsula, just a few minutes' drive from Bingin Beach, and not far from Uluwatu Temple, Padang Padang, and Dreamland. Guests describe it as a well-hidden treasure—quiet, relaxing, with breathtaking views.

One blogger spent a lazy afternoon at the public pool, swimming for a long time, feeling as if the water merged with the horizon. For dining, the Japanese restaurant Tsune Uluwatu is a highlight, with tuna tartare, grilled prawns, and wagyu receiving high praise. The spa is also well-regarded, with the signature treatment at Svaha Spa Bingin featuring slow, firm pressure, ending with a floral-scented massage bath and ginger tea.

**Rooms**｜The Grand Cabana comes with an infinity pool, about 120 square meters, spread over three levels: a living room on the first floor, a bathroom in the middle, and a bedroom on the top floor, with direct access to a private pool—spacious yet not over-the-top.

**Review** | Guests have called it one of the best hotels they've ever stayed at, with attentive and responsive staff and an outstanding breakfast. The overall atmosphere is serene, understated, and minimalist—ideal for couples or solo travelers seeking relaxation over excitement.

**Final Verdict** | If your Uluwatu itinerary prioritizes pool time, sunsets, and a great dinner over sightseeing, this place is worth considering.
