## **🌦️ Bali Weather Outlook for November 17: Rain in Many Areas, Southern Sea Waves Could Reach 2.5 Meters**

The latest forecast from the Bali Region III Meteorology Center of the Indonesian Agency for Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics (BMKG) indicates that on November 17, 2026, Bali will be mostly cloudy, with rain and thunderstorms possible in some areas.

According to BMKG's weather information:

| Item | November 17 Forecast |
| --- | --- |
| General Temperature Range | 19–34°C |
| Relative Humidity | 65–90% |
| Wind Direction | East to South |
| Wind Speed | Approximately 3–30 km/h |
| Northern Bali Sea Wave Height | 0.5–1.25 meters |
| Southern Bali Sea Wave Height | 0.5–2.5 meters |

Rainfall conditions vary by area:

- Denpasar: Light rain, around 25–32°C
- Badung Regency: Possible thunderstorms, around 24–31°C
- Gianyar Regency: Light rain, around 23–29°C
- Bangli: Light rain, around 19–27°C
- Tabanan: Light rain, around 22–27°C
- Buleleng: Light rain, around 24–30°C

BMKG warns that parts of Bali may experience moderate rainfall accompanied by lightning, and wave heights could exceed 2 meters in southern waters, particularly in the southern straits of Bali, Badung Strait, and the southern sea area.

For travelers planning outdoor attractions, waterfalls, or mountain trips tomorrow, it's advisable to keep your itinerary flexible. If heading to offshore islands like Nusa Penida or Nusa Lembongan, be sure to check the latest sea conditions with ferry companies and ports.

The rainy season doesn't mean it will rain all day, but localized downpours may affect road traffic and marine activities.

## **🏥 Sanur Medical Tourism Special Zone Attracts IDR 5.76 Trillion in Investment! Creates Nearly 6,000 Jobs**

The Sanur Special Economic Zone (KEK Sanur) has announced its latest investment results. As of the third quarter of 2026, the zone has achieved cumulative investments of IDR 5.76 trillion (approximately USD $186 million) since 2023.

According to data released by Indonesia's Coordinating Ministry for Economic Affairs on November 16:

- Cumulative investment: IDR 5.76 trillion
- Cumulative jobs created: 5,911
- Enterprises established: 15
- New investment in the first three quarters of 2026: 515.87 billion Indonesian rupiah
- Full-year investment target for 2026: 1.25 trillion Indonesian rupiah
- Cumulative patients in the third quarter: 36,798

Of these, foreign patients reached 22,046, while domestic Indonesian patients totaled 14,752.

The Sanur Medical Tourism Special Economic Zone is Indonesia's first special economic zone centered on health and medical tourism, primarily aiming to attract international patients and reduce the outflow of foreign exchange caused by Indonesians seeking medical treatment abroad.

Currently, the Bali International Hospital began operations on April 14, 2025, offering specialized services in cardiology, oncology, neurology, gastroenterology, obstetrics and gynecology, and orthopedics.

Additionally, Alster Lake Clinic and The Solitaire Clinic have also started operations, while other medical facilities are still under development.

For Bali, Sanur is not just a traditional beach resort area in the future—it may gradually evolve into a key hub for medical tourism and the health industry.

However, the growth in investment and patient numbers does not mean that all medical facilities have been fully completed or opened.

## **🚦 Bali Launches Operasi Zebra Agung 2026! Foreign Tourists Also Subject to Traffic Enforcement**

Bali police launched the Operasi Zebra Agung 2026 traffic safety enforcement campaign on November 16, aiming to reduce traffic violations, accidents, and accident fatalities.

Bali police stated that the operation deploys a total of 1,067 officers, including:

- Bali Provincial Police Headquarters: 263 officers
- Local police units across regencies and cities: 804 officers

Police noted that from January to September 2026, traffic fatalities in Bali increased to 415, compared to 404 during the same period in 2025; minor injuries rose from 7,572 to 9,581.

On the other hand, serious injuries decreased from 66 to 52.

Police said that in addition to enforcement, Operasi Zebra Agung will also strengthen traffic safety education and prepare for traffic management during the upcoming Christmas and New Year holidays (Nataru).

The operation does not distinguish between Indonesian residents and foreign tourists—all road users must comply with traffic rules.

For travelers in Bali, it's recommended to pay special attention when renting a scooter or driving yourself:

- Must hold a valid driver's license compliant with Indonesian regulations
- Wear a helmet when riding a scooter
- No driving against traffic or dangerous overtaking
- No drunk driving
- Obey road signs and traffic signals

Police also stated that electronic traffic law enforcement (ETLE) and manual ticketing have increased by 24% compared to 2025.

This campaign is about traffic safety enforcement, not an indication that all roads in Bali will have fixed checkpoints; actual enforcement locations will be adjusted based on daily police assessments.

## **🏛️ Gajah Mada Historic District: Partial Construction Halted! Secondary Skin Facade Design Under Review**

On November 16, the Denpasar City Government announced a suspension of construction on some building facade projects (Secondary Skin) in the historic Jalan Gajah Mada district in the city center.

This decision primarily responds to public feedback on the design, while also reviewing construction quality and the overall street style.

Denpasar's Public Works and Spatial Planning Agency (PUPR) stated that the construction suspension is expected to last several days, allowing relevant units to reassess whether the facade design aligns with the architectural character of the historic district.

The overall street planning involves:

- Jalan Gajah Mada
- Jalan Sumatera
- Jalan Hasanuddin
- Jalan Thamrin

These areas include not only commercial activities but also religious sites and community living spaces for different ethnic groups.

The city government stated that only part of the Secondary Skin facade construction has been suspended, while other projects will continue to maintain the original schedule.

For travelers, Jalan Gajah Mada is a historically and commercially significant area in downtown Denpasar. If you're planning a visit for sightseeing or shopping soon, be mindful of the ongoing construction conditions.

The authorities have not officially announced a full street closure, so this construction pause should not be interpreted as a complete road shutdown.

## **💰 Gianyar Completes Parliamentary Process for 2027 Draft Budget! Local Revenue Target Reaches 2.47 Trillion IDR**

The Gianyar Regency Government and the Regional House of Representatives completed the parliamentary resolution process for the 2027 Draft Regional Budget (APBD 2027) on November 16, prioritizing an increase in Local Own Revenue (PAD).

According to ANTARA News, Gianyar Regent Made Mahayastra stated that the 2027 regional finances will continue to support public services and local development despite limited revenue.

Key budget figures are as follows:

| Item | 2027 Budget Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Total Regional Revenue | 3.75 Trillion IDR |
| Local Own Revenue (PAD) | 2.47 Trillion IDR |
| Central and Other Transfer Revenue | 1.27 Trillion IDR |
| Total Regional Expenditure | 3.69 Trillion IDR |
| Estimated Surplus | 60.8 Billion IDR |

The Local Own Revenue target is an increase of 81.5 billion IDR compared to 2026, representing a growth of approximately 3.40%.

The Gianyar Regional House of Representatives also proposed digitizing local taxes and fees to enhance revenue management efficiency, and prioritizing infrastructure spending on projects that meet residents' needs.

Additionally, the meeting approved six draft local regulations covering population administration, local innovation, housing and residential areas, building construction, environmental protection, and disaster management.

It's worth noting that after completing the local parliamentary process, the 2027 Draft Budget still needs to be submitted to the Governor of Bali for evaluation before proceeding to the formal approval stage.

For travelers, this budget is not an immediate new tourism tax or accommodation fee adjustment, but rather a plan for Gianyar Regency's local finances and development direction for the upcoming fiscal year.
