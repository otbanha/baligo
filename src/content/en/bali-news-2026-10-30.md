## 🌧️ Heavy Rain Hits Bali! BMKG Warns of Potential Flooding in Multiple Areas, Southern Seas See Waves Up to 3 Meters

Bali is transitioning into the rainy season, with recent downpours across the island. The Indonesian Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) Bali Region III issued a new warning on October 28, alerting that moderate to heavy rain may affect parts of Bali from October 28 to 31.

According to weather analysis, areas likely to experience rainfall from October 28 to 29 include Badung, Bangli, Gianyar, Karangasem, and Tabanan.

From October 30 to 31, rainfall risk areas include Badung, Buleleng, Jembrana, Karangasem, and Tabanan.

In addition to rain, the agency warns that waves in southern Bali waters could reach up to 3 meters, with the Badung Strait, Lombok Strait, and southern Bali waters also seeing waves over 2 meters.

### What’s the Weather Like in Key Tourist Areas?

BMKG’s forecast for October 29 shows:

- **Ubud, Kintamani:** Mostly cloudy
- **Kuta, Seminyak, Nusa Dua:** Mostly cloudy
- **Sanur, Tanah Lot:** Mostly cloudy
- **Bedugul:** Rain expected around midday

It's worth noting that cloudy doesn't mean it won't rain all day—tropical weather can change quickly, so conditions may still vary.

### What Should Travelers Keep in Mind?

If you're planning a trip to Bali soon, here are some tips:

- Schedule outdoor activities with flexible timing and bring a raincoat or umbrella.
- When heading to Ubud, Kintamani, or mountain attractions, watch out for road conditions after rainfall.
- Before visiting Nusa Penida or Lembongan Island, check with the boat company about sea conditions and schedules.
- Avoid roads prone to flooding or falling rocks during heavy downpours.

Additionally, BMKG predicts that Bali's rainy season will gradually begin in November 2026, peaking in February 2027, and may last until April 2027.

This doesn't mean it will rain every day, but planning outdoor activities in Bali over the next few months will indeed require more flexibility than during the dry season.

## 📈 Bali Welcomes 19,000 to 24,000 Foreign Tourists Daily! Some Luxury Hotels Report Occupancy Rates Over 90%

Bali Governor Wayan Koster released the latest tourism market data on October 29, stating that in 2026, Bali receives approximately **19,000 to 24,000 foreign tourists daily**, with stable visitor numbers throughout the year.

He noted that some high-end star-rated hotels even boast occupancy rates exceeding 90%, while restaurants, local small and medium enterprises, and tourism service providers continue to benefit from tourist spending.

Additionally, as of October 22, local revenue from Bali's hotels and restaurants increased by approximately 600 billion Indonesian rupiah compared to the same period in 2025.

In 2025, Bali welcomed about 7.05 million foreign tourists and 9.3 million domestic Indonesian tourists. The governor believes that the overall tourism market performance in 2026 remains positive.

### What Does This Mean for Travelers?

This data shows that Bali's tourism demand remains strong, especially in the high-end accommodation market.

However, high hotel occupancy doesn't mean that all areas and price ranges are fully booked. If you're planning to visit Bali during the year-end peak season or around Christmas and New Year's, it's advisable to compare accommodation prices and booking conditions early.

## ✈️ China Eastern Airlines Adds Daily Shanghai–Bali Flights in December!

China Eastern Airlines has announced that it will **add flights from Shanghai Pudong to Bali starting December 11, 2026**.

Currently, there is one daily flight between Shanghai Pudong and Bali. With the new addition, service will increase to two daily flights, offering travelers more departure and arrival time options.

The new flight schedule is as follows:

- **MU5045:** Departs Shanghai Pudong at 01:00, arrives in Bali at 07:35.
- **MU5046:** Departs Bali at 09:35, arrives in Shanghai Pudong at 15:55.

The new flights are expected to use the Airbus A330-200, while the existing flights will continue with the A330-300.

### How Does This Benefit Travelers from Taiwan and Hong Kong?

This route primarily serves travelers between Shanghai and Bali, but it also offers an additional flight option for those considering a transfer through Shanghai to Bali.

If you plan to transit through Shanghai, be sure to confirm when booking whether it's a single connecting ticket, whether your luggage will be checked through, and whether the layover time is sufficient.

The new flights are not expected to begin operations until December 11, so you should still check the airline's latest schedule before booking.

## 👗 Bali's Sustainable Fashion Event Sets a World Record! 67 Eco-Certified Outfits Hit the Runway

On October 28, Sira Village on Bali's Turtle Island hosted Labdanation Fashion Week Season III, themed 'Threads of Legacy,' blending local design, the textile industry, and environmental concepts.

The event featured **67 sustainably certified outfits** on the runway, setting a Guinness World Record for the most certified sustainable textiles in a fashion show.

The event involved five local Balinese design brands and institutions, including:

- Morrato Bali
- Kimmyra
- IDB Bali
- Unice
- Novel Mide

From design and textile materials to printing and sewing, the clothing emphasizes sustainable production and the use of local resources.

The organizers hope to boost the international visibility of Indonesian textiles through global recognition, while also introducing more people to Bali's local designers and creative industries.

### What does this mean for travelers?

Events like this reflect that Bali is gradually developing a local design and sustainable fashion industry beyond tourism, hotels, and dining.

If you love Bali's local brands, beyond the familiar clothing, swimwear, and handicraft labels, keep an eye out for works by local designers.

This event ended on October 28 and is not an ongoing exhibition, so travelers should not treat it as a permanent attraction that can still be visited.

## 🍹 Bali's Arak Goes Global! Three Young Bartenders to Represent Bali at the World Championship in Poland

Bali's traditional spirit, Arak Bali, is gradually stepping onto the international stage.

On October 28, Bali Governor Wayan Koster met with three young bartenders preparing to compete abroad. They will represent Bali at the **Food Town Flair Challenge IV** in Poland from November 18 to 19.

The three contestants are:

- Gede Rico Hardiana Widnyana
- I Wayan Eka Darmawan
- I Kadek Suniantara

Among them, Gede Rico Hardiana Widnyana previously won the world runner-up title at The TDuB World Championship Grand Finals in Las Vegas, USA.

At that competition, 33 contestants from 18 countries participated, and Rico earned a silver belt and a prize of USD$4,000.

For this trip to Poland, the Bali government hopes that through the bartenders' professional skills, more international audiences will get to know Arak Bali, blending this traditional local spirit into modern cocktail culture.

### What is Arak Bali?

Arak Bali is a traditional Balinese distilled spirit, typically made from coconut palm flowers or other local plant ingredients. It is an integral part of Balinese cuisine and certain traditional cultural practices.

In recent years, local producers have begun introducing this traditional beverage to a wider international audience through modern cocktails, brand design, and international competitions.
