Most Sanur hotels are situated near the bustling town center or along the beautiful 8-kilometer beach. The quiet, laid-back town, affordable hotel rates, and excellent beach make Sanur one of the top accommodation options for families and budget-conscious travelers in Bali.

Complimentary WiFi and free parking add to the convenience.
