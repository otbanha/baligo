### 【2026 Bali Travel Outlook】From the End of the Rainy Season to Clear Blue Skies: A Complete Guide to Festivals and Climate from March to June

![【2026 峇里島旅遊展望】從雨季尾聲到蔚藍晴空：3～6 月節慶與氣候全攻略](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_faf6335054f12d89f60b16fd7a533b9d.jpg)

**Bottom line first**: The best window to visit in the first half of 2026 is the **golden transition period of April–May** (weather dries up, sunshine chances soar—perfect for water activities and photos); **March is still the tail end of the rainy season** (humidity can reach 80%, with afternoon showers being the norm); don't forget to note the special **Nyepi Day of Silence** when the entire island shuts down (shops closed, no going outside). Below is a full breakdown combining climate data and the festival calendar.

Planning a trip to **Bali** in the first half of 2026? This period is the island's most "dramatic" time. You might encounter magical moments when the entire island comes to a standstill, or accidentally fall into the traffic nightmare locals dread most.

To help you travel like a seasoned pro, we've combined climate data with local festivals to put together this "March–June Travel Outlook."

***

### 🌦️ Climate and Conditions: A "Survival Guide" for the Wet-Dry Season Transition

![氣候與賽道：乾雨季交替的「生存指南」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_07409a02cddf0c7953f3992184b40d23.png)

These four months mark the critical shift from the rainy season to the dry season:

- **March (End of Rainy Season):** Humidity can peak at 80%. While rainfall starts to decrease, brief afternoon showers remain common.
- **April–May (Golden Transition):** The weather begins to dry out, with sunshine becoming much more frequent—this is the most beautiful time for water activities and photography.
- **June (Peak Dry Season):** Influenced by cool air from the Southern Hemisphere, refreshing breezes blow during this month, making it one of the most comfortable times of the year in terms of climate.

***

## 📅 Month-by-Month Deep Dive: Festivals & Tips to Avoid Crowds

## **March: Between Extreme Festivity and Absolute Stillness**

{{video1}}

- **Climate Overview:** March is the **transition period** in **Bali** from the rainy season to the dry season. Humidity is high (around 80%), and while there are still occasional showers, they usually come and go quickly. This is when the island's vegetation is at its lushest and the rice terraces are most beautiful.
- **Packing Tips:** Light, breathable cotton clothing, and carry a foldable umbrella or lightweight raincoat with you at all times.

**Major Festivals:**

- **March 18: Ogoh-ogoh Parade (Pawai Ogoh-ogoh):** A island-wide exorcism ritual with a spectacular, dramatic atmosphere.
- **March 19-20: Nyepi (Day of Silence):** Bali's New Year, where the entire island comes to a standstill for 24 hours—a great opportunity to deeply experience the concept of 'emptiness.'
- **Note:** Late March coincides with Indonesia's **Eid al-Fitr** holiday period. This is the number one peak season we strongly advise you to **avoid**! Tourists from all over Indonesia flood into **Bali**, causing massive traffic jams and skyrocketing accommodation prices. If you're seeking a relaxing getaway, be sure to skip this week, or stay put in a remote resort.

**Recommended Places to Visit in March**

- **Denpasar:** On the evening of March 18, head to Puputan Square to witness the most spectacular and authentic **Ogoh-ogoh Monster Parade**. (Reference: [Nyepi Day Guide 2026: Everything You Need to Know About Bali's Day of Silence & Monster Parade](/en/blog/nyepi-day-bali-guide-2026/))
- **Uluwatu Cliff Resort:** Stay at a resort with stunning ocean views during **Nyepi Day** (March 19-20). With minimal light pollution, this is the best spot on the island to see the "Nyepi Milky Way." (Reference: [Uluwatu Bali Accommodation Guide: 13 Private Villa Resorts with Breathtaking Sea Views](/en/blog/uluwatu-bali-villas-resorts-guide/))

## **April: A Time for Mind, Body, and Soul to Awaken**

![4 月：身心靈的甦醒時刻](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_cc16394692a6e8dd69ea0fab7f62c062.png)

- **Weather Overview:** In April, rainfall decreases significantly, and sunny days become more frequent. Water visibility improves, marking the start of the ideal season for snorkeling and diving. Temperatures range from 25°C to 32°C.
- The Qingming Festival holiday in early April is a brilliantly timed window! Indonesia's busiest holiday, Eid al-Fitr, has just ended, with the massive homecoming crowds dissipating. As locals return to work and international tourists aren't as frantic as during peak season, Bali enjoys a brief, precious period of calm.
- **What to Wear:** Summer attire with a sun-protective jacket, and don't forget your swimsuit.

**Major Festivals:**

- **April 15-19: Bali Spirit Festival:** An annual gathering for yoga enthusiasts worldwide, featuring yoga, dance, and music.
- **April 4: Saraswati Day (Goddess of Knowledge):** Observe locals honoring and making offerings to books and knowledge.

**Recommended Places to Visit in April**

- **Ubud:** This is the home of the **Bali Spirit Festival**. We recommend staying near a yoga studio in the Monkey Forest area or joining a workshop at **The Yoga Barn**. (Reference: [Top 10 Ubud Yoga Retreats in Bali: Dreamy Stays with Yoga Classes and Relaxation](/en/blog/ubud-yoga-retreats-bali/))
- **Tegallalang Rice Terraces:** With less rain in April, the sunlight filtering through the emerald terraces creates the most enchanting scenery. It's perfect for an early morning stroll to avoid the heat. (Reference: [A Guide to Bali's Two Major Rice Terraces: Tegallalang and Jatiluwih — Entrance Fees, Best Time to Visit, and Season Tips](/en/blog/bali-rice-terraces-comparison/))
- **Campuhan Ridge Walk:** As the weather dries up, the trail is no longer slippery, making it ideal for an evening walk. (Reference: [A Paradise Just a Walk from Ubud Center? Introducing the 'Ubud Skywalk' Campuhan Ridge Walk](/en/blog/ubud-campuhan-ridge-walk/))

## **May: The Golden Month for Foodies and Outdoor Enthusiasts**

![5 月：吃貨與戶外控的黃金月](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b7405a76a96c0882e108c5117afe7a87.png)

- **Weather Overview:** May is the month most travel experts recommend. The dry season officially arrives, humidity drops significantly, and the air feels refreshing and non-sticky. The sky is at its bluest, making it the best time for Instagram-worthy shots.
- **Labor Day (May 1):** Take 4 days off (April 27-30) for a 9-day long weekend exclusive to workers!
- **Packing Tips:** Flaunt your summer outfits; sunglasses and high-SPF sunscreen are must-haves.

{{video2}}

**Major Festivals:**

- **May 29-31: Ubud Food Festival:** A must-attend Indonesian culinary feast for foodies.
- **May 31: Waisak Day:** Experience the solemnity and tranquility of Buddhist culture.

**Top Travel Destinations for May**

- **Ubud Center:** For the **Ubud Food Festival**, it's essential to stay near the center for easy access to long-table lunches and chef talks. (See [Ubud accommodation recommendations](/en/blog/ubud-resorts-guide/#%E5%B3%87%E9%87%8C%E5%B3%B6%E7%A7%98%E5%A2%83-padma-resort-ubud-%E6%93%81%E6%8A%B1%E5%AF%A7%E9%9D%9C%E8%88%87%E5%A5%A2%E8%8F%AF))
- **Water Activities:** This is the sweet spot—no peak-season crowds, yet the best weather! (See [The Complete Guide to Bali Water Activities: 15 Unexpected Ways to Play Beyond Snorkeling](/en/blog/2026-bali-water-activities/))
- **Island Hopping/Snorkeling:** Sea conditions stabilize in May, making it the perfect time to head to **Nusa Lembongan** or **Nusa Penida** for snorkeling with manta rays. (Check out [Nusa Lembongan Guide](/en/blog/nusa-lembongan-ceningan-guide/) / [Nusa Penida Guide](/en/blog/nusa-penida-guide/))
- **Tulamben:** With the dry season arriving, underwater visibility is excellent—ideal for exploring the shipwreck dive. (See [Complete Guide to Bali's Northeast Hidden Gems: Amed and Tulamben](/en/blog/amed-tulamben-diving-guide/))

## **June: The Peak of Culture and Beauty**

![6 月：文化與美感的巔峰](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_dd2c5771be5e78e79e23c43530cfe5c2.png)

- **Weather Overview:** June offers extremely stable weather with very low rainfall. Influenced by cool air from the Southern Hemisphere, refreshing breezes blow in, and mornings and evenings can feel a bit chilly—making it the most comfortable time for hiking (e.g., Mount Batur).
  **Quick Reference:**
- **Dragon Boat Festival Long Weekend:** Take 4 days off (June 15–18) for an epic 9-day early-summer getaway!
- **Packing Tips:** In addition to summer clothes, bring a light long-sleeve layer or windbreaker for the cool mornings and evenings.

**Major Festivals:**

- **Bali Arts Festival (PKB) – All of June:** Held in Denpasar, this is the ultimate venue for experiencing traditional dance, crafts, and parades.
- **Galungan & Kuningan:** Streets are lined with spectacular Penjor bamboo decorations, creating the most festive atmosphere.

**Recommended June Travel Destinations**

- **Werdhi Budaya Art Centre, Denpasar:** Throughout June, the **Bali Arts Festival (PKB)** features top-tier traditional dance and theater performances from afternoon to evening every day. (See the [Denpasar Culture Guide](/en/blog/denpasar-culture-guide/))
- **Mount Batur:** June is the driest month of the year, with the coolest mornings, making it ideal for a sunrise hike. The chance of seeing a sea of clouds is extremely high. (See the [Bali Kintamani Batur Volcano Lake Guide: Sunrise Hike × Royal Boat × Lakeview Café × Mountain Playground × Hot Springs](/en/blog/batur-lake-volcano-guide/))
- **Pandawa Beach:** Join the **Pandawa Beach Festival** to enjoy cultural parades and music performances by the azure sea. (Wondering how to explore the southern tip of Bali? Check out the [Bukit Peninsula Adventure Guide: From Jimbaran Sunsets to Uluwatu Cliff Temples – Bali's Hidden Paradise](/en/blog/bukit-peninsula-guide/))

## 💡 Editor's Smart Travel Tips

1. **What to Wear:** In March and April, pack a raincoat and moisture-wicking clothes; for May and June, bring a light jacket in addition to summer wear, as the evening breeze can sometimes give you goosebumps. Check out [Do You Need a Jacket in Bali? Bali Packing Guide: 8 Essential Items for Your Trip](/en/blog/bali-packing-essentials/)
2. **Avoid Peak Times:** Again, the Eid al-Fitr holiday in mid-to-late March is a **local peak season**; if possible, shift your vacation to after April.
3. **Booking Advice:** If you plan to attend the Ubud Food Festival or the Spiritual Festival, book your accommodation at least three months in advance, as Ubud city center gets very crowded during these events.
