# 2027 Bali Holiday Guide: Plan Ahead to Avoid Crowds and Snag Cheap Flights

Planning a trip to Bali? The 2027 holiday schedule just released by the Indonesian government is crucial for you!

Because these holidays don't just mean time off for locals—they also mean **Bali will be packed, hotel prices will skyrocket, and flights will be insanely expensive**. If you know how to use this holiday calendar, you can smartly choose your travel timing—either join the long holiday crowds for festive vibes, or grab bargains during the off-peak periods before and after the holidays.

## Key Highlights

2027 has a total of **18 national holidays** and **8 collective leave days**. The March Eid al-Fitr period is the longest stretch of consecutive holidays all year, while the May golden week features three major holidays in quick succession. This complete holiday schedule is your best tool for planning a 2027 Bali trip.

## 8-Day Public Holidays: What You Need to Know About Peak Season

Below are Indonesia's officially approved public holidays. Around these dates, Bali and major attractions will be packed, and flights and hotels will be sky-high:

https://youtu.be/15zXGMEE3Q8

1. **February 6 (Saturday)** — Lunar New Year
    - Massive influx of travelers from China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, and Singapore
    - Temples and traditional villages will be extremely crowded
2. **March 8–15** — Nyepi Day + Eid al-Fitr Holiday
    - **Most important!** This is Indonesia's longest consecutive holiday week of the year
    - Flights are hardest to book, hotels are most expensive, and attractions are most crowded
- But the local religious festival atmosphere is worth experiencing.
3. **March 26 (Friday)** — Good Friday
    - Connects with the weekend, forming a long weekend
    - More Western tourists arrive
4. **May 17 (Monday)** — Eid al-Adha
    - Tied to the weekend, Bali sees an influx of Indonesian domestic family travelers
    - Scooter rentals and restaurants tend to be fully booked
5. **May 20 (Thursday)** — Vesak Day
    - A Buddhist festival, with ceremonies held at Bali's temples
    - If you want to photograph temples, get up early
6. **Friday, December 24** — Christmas Holiday
    - Coincides with Christmas and New Year
    - The final peak period of the year, with all resorts fully booked

## Practical Tips for International Travelers

💡 **Why Is the Holy Night Commemorated Twice?** The Islamic calendar is a lunar calendar, with only 354–355 days per year. As a result, the same Islamic holiday may span two Gregorian years. In 2027, both January 5 and December 26 fall on the Holy Night—this is perfectly normal.

🎫 **Flight Booking Tips**

- Airfare spikes 2–3 months before the holiday
- Recommended timing: buy tickets 1–2 weeks before peak season (fewer people think of this)
- If you don't mind crowds: grab last-minute deals on the holiday itself
- Best savings: travel in the off-season to save 20–50% on airfare

🏨 **Hotel Booking Secrets**

- During peak seasons (Eid al-Fitr, Christmas), book at least 2–3 months in advance
- Avoid peak times: the same hotel can be over 30% cheaper

🕌 **Holiday Etiquette Tips**

- Some temples may be closed to the public during Eid al-Fitr
- Dress modestly and remove shoes when entering temples for religious ceremonies
- Always ask locals for permission before taking photos
- Avoid eating or drinking in front of Muslims during Ramadan

☀️ **Weather & Seasons**

- May to October is the **dry season**, with the best weather
- November to March is the **rainy season**, with frequent afternoon showers but beautiful mornings
- Traveling in the off-season can offer a different kind of scenery

📱 **SIM Cards & Internet**

- SIM cards at the airport sell out quickly during peak season, so it's best to buy one in advance
- No need to fight for spots during the low season
