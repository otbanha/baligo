**Bottom line first**: The top three must-buys at Ubud Market are **Endek traditional handwoven textiles** (vibrant colors, perfect as tablecloths or shawls), **Sarong** (affordable, versatile, and more collectible if made with Batik technique), and Balinese-style T-shirts. These are common stall items—remember to haggle. Below are details and tips for each.

When it comes to the soul of Bali, **Ubud** is an unmissable cultural hub. Beyond the rice terraces, yoga retreats, and artistic vibes, the Ubud Market is a place every traveler will linger. It's brimming with handmade crafts and local artworks that will have you marveling at the Balinese passion for detail and creativity. Today, let's explore what you should buy at Ubud Market! 🌿

![峇里島烏布市場必買攻略：從手工藝到藝術收藏，一次買個過癮！](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_4afef2016a21557b5908bae9c5c70f79.jpg)

***

## 🧶1. Handmade Textiles

![1. 手工織品（Handmade Textiles）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_218f0d194c432ade542611ace179bde4.jpg)

At Ubud Market, you'll be drawn to a traditional Balinese fabric called **Endek**. This handwoven textile is a source of pride for locals, with vibrant colors, diverse patterns, and ethnic flair. You can use it as a tablecloth, shawl, or even a decorative fabric at home. Each piece of Endek feels like a work of art, embodying the warmth and craftsmanship of Balinese culture.

***

## 🧣2. Sarong: A Versatile and Culturally Rich Fabric

![2. 沙龍布（Sarong）：多功能又富文化意義的布料](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_37f79c58c6862ad429cb1e686e6615f4.png)

The sarong is one of Bali's most classic souvenirs. This traditional fabric is not only affordable but also highly versatile, serving purposes such as:

- Beach towel
- Cloth bag or backpack
- Baby wrap
- Tablecloth or sofa cover
- DIY into skirts, tops, or shorts

Some sarongs are made using traditional **Batik** techniques, with colors and motifs that carry local stories and cultural symbolism. When shopping, ask vendors about the meaning behind different patterns to add depth to your collection and keepsake. This is not just a piece of cloth—it's an extension of Balinese culture.

***

## 👕3. Bali-themed T-shirts

![3. 峇里風T恤（T-shirt）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_4c17893fefca873287d8967a8fb63949.jpg)

At Ubud Market, you'll find T-shirts everywhere printed with **"Bali" lettering or Barong motifs**. The fabric is soft, the styles are casual, and they're perfect for personal use or as souvenirs. Prices are usually affordable, and the designs are full of island vibes—wearing one feels like bringing a piece of Bali's sunshine home ☀️.

***

## 🪵4. Wooden Carvings: Lifelike Traditional Art

![4. 木雕工藝（Wooden Carvings）：栩栩如生的傳統藝術](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2dbb0b57c27c67f42d3c1f9bd259fb9c.jpg)

Bali is world-famous for its wooden carvings, with Ubud and the Tegallalang region being key origins of this art form. From **symbols of peace, the tree of life, mythological characters, to elephant motifs**, each piece is crafted by skilled local artisans, delicate and full of vitality.

Beyond traditional art, there are also toy-like wooden carving collectibles that are both fun and stylish!

![4. 木雕工藝（Wooden Carvings）：栩栩如生的傳統藝術](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_d8efdab6446270abc68c86a25529abfc.png)

Visitors can even sign up for wood carving workshops to carve their own souvenirs by hand, experiencing the charm of this ancient craft.

## 🎨5. Local Paintings

![5. 本地畫作（Paintings）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_dbe855a474b5a5cf9786d05e24c820b6.png)

Ubud has long been a gathering place for artists. In the market, you can find many **paintings depicting Balinese life, natural landscapes, or abstract themes**. Most of these works are original creations by local painters, each with its own unique story. You don't have to buy large oil paintings; small canvases or hand-painted cards are also great for collecting or gifting.

***

## 💍6. Handmade Silver Jewelry

![6. 手工銀飾（Jewelry）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_72b61db47b0441a0b11437e94330ad1d.png)

Bali's silver jewelry craftsmanship is equally renowned, especially in the Ubud market, where the selection is dazzling. Whether it's **bracelets, rings, necklaces, or earrings**, each piece is meticulously crafted by artisans, featuring fine lines and diverse designs. If you prefer minimalist or ethnic styles, you're sure to find your perfect match here.

**Join a Silver Jewelry Workshop**: [Dive into Balinese Culture: A Must-Do Day Trip with Silver Craft Workshops](/en/blog/bali-silver-workshop/)

***

## 🧺7. Balinese Handicrafts

![7. 峇里島手工藝品（Handicrafts）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_45771c55bc31c1317e3fea935860e721.png)

Beyond fabrics and accessories, the Ubud market is also brimming with **bamboo and rattan woven goods**, such as bags, hats, and baskets. These items are not only stylish but also highly practical. Perfect for everyday use, they make for the most heartfelt souvenirs from your journey. It's recommended to choose pieces with sturdy construction and tight weaves for better durability and aesthetics.

***

## 🏡8. Exotic Home Decor

![8. 異國風情的居家飾品（Home Decor）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_aef6507b2d960d9de93bb987f44150e2.png)

The Ubud market is also a treasure trove for home decor finds. You'll discover many **tropical and exotic Balinese-style** home decorations, such as handcrafted rattan mirror frames, scented candle holders, bamboo lampshades, wooden trays, and hanging ornaments. These pieces not only showcase the texture of natural materials but also infuse your home with Bali's relaxed, laid-back vibe. Even a small trinket can evoke memories of a journey filled with sunshine and rice terraces. 🌴

***

## 🎁9. Lightweight Souvenirs: Classic Choices for Gifts and Keepsakes

![9. 輕便小物（Small Souvenirs）：適合送禮與收藏的經典選擇](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_1b64458b40cb2a01da5bf20f8d3fb7c8.png)

If you're looking to bring home something practical yet compact as a souvenir, consider the following options:

- Handmade keychains
- Woven bracelets
- Scented incense sticks

These small items are not only affordable but also easy to find in major art markets, making them perfect gifts for friends and family back home. Though compact, they are brimming with the essence of Bali.
