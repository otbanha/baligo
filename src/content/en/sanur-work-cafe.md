# Bali Sanur Coworking & Work Cafe Guide｜Cafes and Workspaces for Remote Work

Sanur has a different vibe compared to Seminyak and Canggu.

It lacks the intense party and nightlife atmosphere, offering a more relaxed pace instead. That's why if you're looking to travel and work in Bali, Sanur is actually a great area for a longer stay.

Beyond formal coworking spaces, Sanur also has plenty of cafes where you can work with your laptop. This roundup highlights some noteworthy options, from coworking spaces with stable internet for long work sessions to work cafes perfect for grabbing a coffee while handling tasks.

# Sanur Coworking Space

## [FLOW Workspace Bali](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=FLOW+Workspace+Bali+Sanur)｜Sanur

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DQBI_ZgEsjy/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

If you need a more formal coworking space, FLOW Workspace is a solid choice in Sanur. Nomadwise tested Wi-Fi at around **150 Mbps**, and it features air conditioning, comfortable seating, ample power outlets, low background music, and an environment suitable for calls, plus 24-hour access.

If you need to work more than 6 hours a day, a space with a clear work area, seating, and call equipment is more convenient than a regular café.

## [Livit Hub Bali](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Livit+Hub+Bali+Sanur)｜Sanur

https://www.instagram.com/reel/DRwXhTvjxll/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

Livit Hub is another solid coworking option in Sanur. Nomadwise tested the Wi-Fi at around **102.1 Mbps**, with air conditioning, comfortable seating, plenty of outlets, low background music, and it's marked as call-friendly, plus it offers office chairs and monitors.

If you need to handle lots of documents, join online meetings, or work a set number of hours daily, Livit Hub is a better fit than a typical work café.

## [Medin Bali Cafe & Coffee](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Medin+Bali+Cafe+%26+Coffee+Sanur)｜Sanur

https://www.instagram.com/p/DdJUgnCPag2/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

If you'd rather not pay for a coworking space, Medin Bali Cafe & Coffee is a standout café option. Nomadwise tested the Wi-Fi at around **189.3 Mbps**, with air conditioning, comfortable seating, plenty of outlets, and a relatively traffic-noise-free environment.

Looking purely at work conditions, Medin's internet speed is even faster than some formal coworking spaces. If you just need to work with your laptop for a few hours, it's a top pick.

## [Genius Cafe Sanur](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Genius+Cafe+Sanur+Bali)｜Sanur

https://www.instagram.com/p/DWxrYgRkZrH/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==

Genius Cafe is a beachfront café in Sanur with a strong vacation vibe, perfect for coffee, a bite to eat, or even getting some work done. Nomadwise tested the Wi-Fi at around **30.1 Mbps**, and it offers comfortable seating, low background music, minimal traffic noise, and a pleasant atmosphere.

If your workload isn't too heavy, working while gazing at the sea with a cup of coffee feels far more in tune with Sanur's travel rhythm than sitting in a formal coworking space.

# Working in Sanur Doesn't Mean You Have to Sit in a Coworking Space

If you need to work a solid 6–8 hours daily with online meetings, stable Wi-Fi, and power outlets, then **FLOW Workspace or Livit Hub** would be a better fit.

If you only need 2–4 hours a day to handle emails, articles, or website work, cafes like **Medin Bali Cafe & Coffee, Blend, Kafe Bloom, Farmors, and Over the Moon** are more than sufficient.

Meanwhile, **Genius Cafe and Byrd House** are ideal for blending 'work' and 'vacation' without locking yourself indoors all day.

Sanur's advantage is that you can freely switch based on your workload for the day.
