## ⛽ Bali Fuel Supply Crisis Update: Shorter Gas Station Lines, Government Says Tourist Numbers Unaffected

The ongoing fuel supply and distribution issues in Bali remain a key concern for both locals and travelers. Governor Wayan Koster stated on October 19 that the fuel problem has not significantly impacted tourist arrivals in October, noting continued growth in international and domestic flights as well as foreign visitor numbers.

According to data released at the time, Bali's fuel reserves include approximately 30 million liters of Pertalite and 10 million liters of Pertamax. The government emphasized that the crisis primarily stems from fuel transportation, distribution delays, and concentrated demand at gas stations, rather than a complete absence of fuel on the island.

To stabilize distribution and gas station services, the provincial government has extended the odd-even license plate fueling measure through **Friday, October 23**. However, whether all areas have fully returned to normal depends on on-the-ground supply conditions.

### What Does This Mean for Travelers?

Travelers renting motorbikes, driving themselves, or hiring cars in Bali should still confirm their vehicle's fuel level in advance, especially for long-distance trips across regions. Don't assume that just because some gas stations have shorter lines, the island's fuel supply has fully recovered.

While officials say tourist numbers haven't been significantly affected, that doesn't mean every traveler's transportation plans will go smoothly. It's advisable to allow extra travel time and check the latest fueling rules with your driver or rental provider.

## 🔥 16 Disasters in Bali Over One Week: Forest and Land Fires Dominate, Dry Season Water Shortages Raise Concerns

Bali's disaster response agency announced that from October 12 to 18, 2026, a total of 16 disaster events were recorded across the island, including 10 forest and land fires, 4 building or residential fires, and 2 incidents related to strong winds.

The prolonged dry season has increased fire risks and water stress in some areas. During this period, authorities also distributed approximately 177,500 liters of clean water to assist residents in need.

Additionally, on October 19, four more land fires were reported in Karangasem, Klungkung, Buleleng, and Badung.

### Impact on Travelers

While these figures do not indicate that Bali's main tourist areas are widely affected by fires, they serve as a reminder to be mindful of fire sources during the dry season.

When visiting mountainous, forested, or rural attractions, avoid discarding cigarette butts or burning trash. If your accommodation is in a remote area, keep an eye on the local water supply and avoid wasting water.

## 📚 Ubud Writers & Readers Festival Officially Kicks Off! Over 150 Writers Gather in Bali

The 2026 Ubud Writers & Readers Festival takes place from **October 21 to 25**, marking its 23rd edition with the theme "Samarasā: Awareness. Empathy. Action."

The main venue is Taman Baca Ubud in Ubud, with over 150 writers, thinkers, journalists, artists, and poets from Indonesia and around the world expected to participate.

The program includes author talks, panel discussions, book launches, workshops, art performances, and other cultural activities. This is not just a gathering for literature enthusiasts—it also offers travelers a chance to explore stories from Indonesia and beyond through creators from diverse cultural backgrounds.

How can travelers plan their visit?

If you happen to be staying in Ubud these days, consider adding the literary festival to your itinerary. However, some events may require advance ticket purchase or registration—not all sessions are open for walk-ins.

When heading to Taman Baca Ubud and the surrounding area during the event, it's best to check session times, entry requirements, and transportation arrangements in advance.

## 💻 Bali Tech Week runs through October 23, bringing international tech and startup professionals to Bali

Bali Tech Week 2026 takes place from October 19 to 23, connecting 13 major events covering artificial intelligence, digital economy, cybersecurity, venture capital, and game development.

The main events are expected to attract over 4,000 participants from 30 countries, with more than 200 speakers and VIP guests. The goal is to create opportunities for entrepreneurs, investors, tech companies, regulators, and digital creators to network and build partnerships.

Among them, the Indonesia Game Developer Exchange (IGDX) runs from October 21 to 23, offering game development studios a chance to showcase their work, connect with publishers, and find international partners. Other events focus on AI applications, startup growth, and digital infrastructure.

### How does this affect regular travelers?

Bali Tech Week is not a single large exhibition but a collection of multiple events, each with different venues, registration requirements, and entry methods.

If you work in tech, startups, or digital content, you may want to check out relevant events; regular travelers don't need to change their plans, but if your accommodation or workspace is near an event venue, allow extra travel time.

## 💰 Bali Governor Pledges to Reassess 2027 Minimum Wage, Aiming to Narrow Gap with Reasonable Living Costs

On October 21, Bali Governor Wayan Koster met with representatives from the Kamisan protest in Denpasar to discuss labor conditions and the 2027 Provincial Minimum Wage (UMP).

Koster stated that if calculated purely by the current formula, Bali's 2027 minimum wage would only increase by about 200,000 Indonesian rupiah from the current level, reaching roughly 3.4 million rupiah. He pledged to reexamine the adjustment rate, hoping to avoid a significant gap with the Reasonable Living Cost (KHL).

Protest representatives urged the government to more fully consider actual living expenses—including housing, food, transportation, and other basic needs—when setting the minimum wage.

For now, this remains at the stage of a government commitment to review, and does not mean the 2027 minimum wage has been officially set at 5.2 million rupiah. The final amount still awaits further policy discussions and official announcements.

### Why Should Travelers Care?

The minimum wage policy primarily affects local workers and business operating costs, and will not directly change current accommodation or dining prices for travelers. However, for long-term Bali residents, expatriates, and those running local businesses, the relationship between wage adjustments and living costs is worth monitoring closely.
