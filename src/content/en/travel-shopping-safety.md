**Key Takeaways**: 4 principles for preventing pickpocketing and loss while shopping in Bali—① Bring only enough cash, store the rest in the hotel safe; ② Keep cash and passport/documents **separately**; ③ Place valuables in a front or waist pouch, separate from shopping bags; ④ Nearly 90% of shops in tourist areas accept **GoPay** e-wallet (built into the Gojek app, can be linked to a credit card or topped up by your driver), so prioritize cashless payments and minimize wallet use for maximum safety.

【Shopping】 is often a key activity during travel. When shopping on a trip, convenience and safety are crucial considerations. While browsing for items you want, you might become less alert, which increases the risk of losing money due to dropped wallets or pickpocketing.

To make travel shopping more convenient and secure, try the following tips:

## 1. Carry Only a Modest Amount of Cash

Carrying a modest amount of cash is a great first step for travel shopping, making your vacation safer and more convenient. However, bringing too much cash adds unnecessary risk! Plus, carrying just enough cash can help prevent overspending, as people with too much cash on hand sometimes become more wasteful.

For a safer and more comfortable trip, it's recommended to keep cash and documents separate. This helps prevent the loss of both money and important papers.

## 2. Keep Cash and Tickets/Documents Separate

Cash is typically used for payments, while tickets or passports are only needed at specific times. It's best to store cash separately from tickets and documents to avoid losing everything at once due to carelessness.

## 3. Carrying a Bag with Collectible Items

Make sure the bag you bring is smaller than your main backpack so it doesn't feel cumbersome. Additionally, keep the bag on your chest or waist to store valuables—this makes it easy to keep an eye on your precious items at all times.
