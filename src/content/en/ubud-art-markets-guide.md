**Bottom Line First**: The Ubud Art Market is the top pick, famous for its appearance in the movie *Eat Pray Love*, and known for handmade rattan bags, traditional batik fabrics, and wooden carvings. This article also features four other Ubud art markets worth visiting, perfect for a full day of souvenir shopping.

Ubud, as the cultural and artistic heart of Bali, is home to several uniquely charming art markets, each with its own allure and hidden treasures. Today, let's explore these five must-visit art markets and soak in Bali's most authentic cultural vibe.

![推薦烏布五大藝術市集/藝術市場完整指南：購買紀念品/伴手禮，尋訪峇里島文化寶藏](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_bc06ce29e5089cfece271e264d77f120.jpg)

## 1. Ubud Art Market

Located in the heart of Ubud, this is one of the most famous traditional art markets. It gained worldwide fame after being featured in the movie *Eat Pray Love*.

![Julia Roberts主演的「Eat, Pray, Love」在烏布藝術市場取景](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_8b10b3f0782a8f55634de8bd99244af2.jpg)

The movie *Eat Pray Love*, starring Julia Roberts, was filmed at the Ubud Art Market

### Specialty Items:

- Handwoven rattan bags
- Traditional batik fabrics
- Wood carving artworks
- Handmade accessories
- Balinese traditional clothing

### Practical Information:

- Opening hours: 10 AM to 5 PM
- Best shopping time: 10-11 AM
- Tip: Remember to bargain; you can usually negotiate down to 40-50% of the asking price

## 2. Jalan Goutama (Goutama Street)

This art-filled street is renowned for its boutique shops and art galleries.

![2. Jalan Goutama（高塔瑪路）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_dc338e01ed09d3ce9c1251be69b6b1b0.jpg)

### Highlights to See:

- Exquisite art galleries
- Handcrafted silver jewelry shops
- Contemporary art pieces
- Boutique coffee shops
- Handicraft stores

### Shopping Tips:

- Prices here are relatively fixed, with guaranteed quality
- Take your time to appreciate the unique character of each shop
- Many stores accept credit card payments

## 3. Jalan Monkey Forest (Monkey Forest Road)

More than just the road to the Sacred Monkey Forest, it's a street lined with art shops.

![3. Jalan Monkey Forest（猴子森林路）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0b24218c03d8ce933d431b6311628adc.jpg)

### Specialty Items:

- Balinese wood carvings
- Hand-painted artworks
- Handwoven textiles
- Organic skincare products
- Local spices

### Shopping Tips:

- Avoid the midday heat
- Plenty of nearby restaurants to take a break
- Beware of wild monkeys

## 4. Sukawati Art Market

This is the art market most frequented by locals, offering relatively affordable prices.

![4. Sukawati Art Market（蘇卡瓦提藝術市場）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_92238406ed523649c94d45eeb0afe549.jpg)

### Must-Buy Items:

- Traditional dance costumes
- Handwoven textiles
- Home decor items
- Religious offerings
- Local handicrafts

### Shopping Tips:

- Opening hours: 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM (though art shops usually open later)
- Best time to shop: Morning hours
- Prices are 30-40% cheaper than Ubud Market
- Go early in the morning to avoid crowds
- Most vendors accept cash only

## 5. Guwang Art Market

Less crowded, this market is a great spot for finding unique artworks.

![5. Guwang Art Market（古旺藝術市場）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_34b5eb5728027dd48cb10e11bda274c3.jpg)

### Market Highlights:

- Wholesale prices
- Authentic local art
- Fewer tourists
- Authentic local market atmosphere

### Shopping Tips:

- Opening hours: 8 AM to 6 PM
- Best time to shop: Morning hours
- Ideal for bulk purchases
- Bring cash
- Consider hiring a local guide

## Practical Tips

![實用小貼士](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_aa743f0755f2c685561b29cc3268fda6.jpg)

### Bargaining Tips:

1. Compare multiple vendors before committing to a purchase
2. Start bargaining at 40-50% of the asking price
3. Keep a friendly smile and be patient
4. Bring cash—it makes bargaining easier
5. Being the first customer in the morning often gets you the best price

### Best Times to Visit:

- Early morning: Cool weather and fewer crowds
- Avoid midday: Hot and crowded
- Rainy season tip: Bring rain gear

Ubud's art markets each have their own character. Whether you're looking for traditional crafts, contemporary art, or a taste of local culture, you'll find treasures in these markets. Plan for 2-3 days to fully savor the unique charm of each market—you're sure to leave with plenty of finds!
