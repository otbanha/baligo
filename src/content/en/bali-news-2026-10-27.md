## 🌊 Waves Up to 4 Meters in Bali's Southern Waters! Island Transport to Watch from October 27 to 30

Bali's meteorological agency has issued a latest forecast indicating that from October 27 to 30, wave heights in Bali's southern waters and parts of the southern Lombok Strait could reach **4 meters**. The sea conditions are linked to wind patterns, which may increase risks for boat navigation.

For travelers planning to visit Nusa Penida, Lembongan Island, or arrange water activities, this is a key update worth noting in the near term.

The Badung Strait is a crucial waterway connecting Sanur and Nusa Penida. Even if the weather on Bali's mainland is clear, the sea may not be calm, so it's unwise to judge sailing conditions solely based on the departure point's weather.

### What Should Travelers Watch Out For?

- Check with your boat company before departure to confirm if services are running as scheduled.
- If the boat company adjusts or cancels trips due to sea conditions, follow their arrangements and avoid taking risks by going out to sea.
- When booking day trips to the islands, first confirm the cancellation, rescheduling, and refund policies.
- If you need to catch a flight after taking a ferry back to Bali, be sure to allow plenty of time.

This forecast does not mean all ferry services to outlying islands will be suspended; actual conditions depend on the route, the ferry company, and sea conditions at the time. Travelers should keep an eye on the latest weather and shipping announcements.

## 🛵 Bali Launches 14-Day Traffic Enforcement Operation! Canggu, Sanur, and Other Tourist Areas Under Scrutiny

Bali Police launched the **Operasi Patuh Agung 2026** traffic enforcement operation on October 26, which is expected to run until November 8, lasting 14 days.

The operation mobilizes 1,055 officers with the goal of improving road users' compliance with traffic rules and reducing violations, accidents, and casualties.

Police say busy traffic areas in Denpasar and Badung Regency will be targeted, with tourist zones like Canggu and Sanur also under surveillance. Authorities will use CCTV systems to monitor traffic conditions and dispatch personnel to address congestion when necessary.

### Special Note for Travelers Renting Scooters

Many travelers in Bali rent scooters to get around, but the risk of violations is higher when unfamiliar with local traffic rules.

Before heading out, it's recommended to check the following:

- Wear a helmet while riding and obey traffic signals.
- Ensure your driver's license and international driving permit comply with Indonesian regulations.
- Do not drive against traffic, park illegally, or use your phone for navigation.
- Before renting a car, confirm the insurance coverage and accident procedures.

This campaign does not mean all foreign tourists will be singled out for checks, but following traffic rules is especially important during enforcement. If you're unfamiliar with local roads, opting for an experienced driver is usually more hassle-free.

## 🌱 Bali Designates Six Low-Emission Zones! Ubud, Canggu, Sanur, and Nusa Penida Are on the List

The Bali government continues to push forward with low-emission zone policies, planning to gradually implement green transportation, energy transition, and environmental management in six areas.

The six areas include:

- Ubud
- Nusa Penida
- Nusa Dua
- Kuta
- Sanur
- Gajah Mada Heritage Area in Denpasar

This initiative is jointly promoted by the Bali provincial government and relevant regency/city governments, with implementation areas including green energy, low-emission transportation, environmental management, tourism industry transformation, and community participation. It is expected to be rolled out in phases through 2030.

Among these, Nusa Penida is planned as a green island demonstration zone, with long-term goals including increasing the proportion of renewable energy use and promoting electric transportation.

### What impact does this have on travelers?

Currently, this policy is a phased environmental development plan. It does not mean that gasoline vehicles are completely banned in the six areas, nor does it require travelers to switch transportation immediately.

However, when visiting these areas in the future, travelers may gradually see more eco-friendly transportation, waste sorting, and measures to reduce single-use plastics.

For travelers, the most practical approach is to follow waste sorting rules at accommodations and attractions, reduce the use of single-use plastics, and keep an eye on local transportation and environmental management measures announced in the future.

## 🏥 Bali Explores the Australian Medical Tourism Market! Future Possibilities Include Combining Healthcare, Accommodation, and Wellness Services

Bali's tourism industry is exploring new directions for development. On October 20, the Bali Convention Bureau exchanged ideas with representatives from businesses and related organizations in Indonesia and Australia, discussing the potential for deepening bilateral commercial cooperation, including health tourism, wellness management, and certain medical services.

The discussions covered not only Bali's existing offerings like spas, yoga, massages, and wellness retreats, but also dental services, medical tourism, and accommodation arrangements for travelers needing recovery after treatment.

Australia is one of Bali's key source markets for international visitors. If standardized medical service collaborations can be established in the future, Bali has the potential to attract more travelers seeking wellness management or specific medical needs.

### Has this collaboration already started?

It is still in the exploratory phase of commercial cooperation opportunities, with no official announcement of a new medical tourism program, nor confirmed partner hospitals, service prices, or launch dates.

If travelers are considering receiving medical or dental services in Bali, decisions should not be based solely on vacation experiences or online reviews. It's essential to verify the medical facility's legal credentials, the doctor's expertise, treatment costs, post-procedure care, and whether travel insurance covers related risks.

The key takeaway from this news is that Bali aims to explore more diverse tourism development opportunities beyond its traditional vacation market, rather than indicating that it has fully become a medical tourism hub.

## 🏡 Penglipuran Gains International Travel Media Attention Again! Traditional Village Shines as a Cultural Tourism Highlight in Bali

Penglipuran, a traditional village in Bali's Bangli Regency, has once again caught the eye of international travel media, being featured in Condé Nast Traveler's 2026 list of the world's most beautiful towns.

Penglipuran is renowned for its orderly traditional architecture, village pathways, courtyards, and well-preserved local culture. Unlike the southern beaches and resort areas, it offers a glimpse into the living environment and cultural heritage of a traditional Balinese village.

This international media recognition also gives travelers a chance to rediscover Bali's inland travel options. If you've already visited Ubud, Tanah Lot, or the southern beaches and are looking for a different kind of cultural experience, consider adding Penglipuran to your itinerary.

### What to Know Before You Go?

Penglipuran is not a newly opened attraction but a village with a long-standing cultural tradition. Visitors should respect the daily lives and traditional customs of the local residents.

- Avoid entering private homes without permission.
- Ask for consent before photographing residents or religious activities.
- Follow the village's visiting guidelines and on-site instructions.
- If traveling from southern Bali, allow extra time for transportation.

Being featured on an international media list does not mean the attraction has added new facilities or adjusted ticket prices. Actual entrance fees and opening arrangements should be confirmed on-site.
