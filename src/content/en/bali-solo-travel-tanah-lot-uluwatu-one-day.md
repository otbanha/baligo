# Solo Travel in Bali: How to Get Around? YouTuber Casper Tests Tanah Lot + Uluwatu in One Day

First time traveling solo in Bali and wondering how to handle transportation?

Bali's attractions are spread out, from Tanah Lot in the north to Uluwatu Temple at the southern tip. They may seem close on the map, but the actual travel distance is significant. If you want to visit both iconic spots in one day, is it better to hire a private driver or use Grab?

In this [Bali solo travel video](https://youtube.com/watch?v=G2P4uq-9sgU), YouTuber Casper takes on the challenge of visiting Tanah Lot and Uluwatu in a single day, testing the convenience and costs of different transport options, while sharing key tips for first-time visitors to Bali.

## First Bali Trip: Starting from Arrival

This is Casper's first time in Bali. Before departure, he arranged an eVOA (Electronic Visa on Arrival) and paid the Bali tourism tax, then used the automated immigration gates upon arrival.

Although there was a minor hiccup during the process, he eventually got through smoothly.

> [1-Minute Guide: Latest Process from Landing to Customs to Baggage Claim](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-all-indonesia-arrival-guide/)

I booked the airport transfer in advance through Klook, heading straight to my hotel in Seminyak. For a first-time solo traveler to Bali, arranging a pick-up ahead of time really saves a lot of hassle.

Casper chose to stay in Seminyak this time, planning to use it as a base to explore nearby Kuta and Canggu over the next few days.

## Grab + Private Car: How to Plan a Two-Temple Day Trip?

The most valuable part of this video is Casper's real-world test of Bali's transportation options.

When heading to Tanah Lot Temple, he opted to use Grab for a ride, finding it quite convenient—showing that solo travelers don't necessarily need a private car every day.

However, for the afternoon trip to Uluwatu Temple to watch the famous Kecak Fire Dance, he switched to a pre-booked private car.

The reason is simple: after a popular performance, the crowd dispersing is massive, making the return trip potentially more troublesome than expected.

This flexible approach of mixing Grab and private car based on the itinerary might suit some solo travelers better than hiring a car for the entire trip.

Beyond the transportation test, the video also captures Casper's first impressions of Tanah Lot.

Arriving in the morning, the temple grounds were not too crowded, and the surrounding shops hadn't fully opened yet. This shows how visiting Tanah Lot at different times can lead to completely different travel experiences.

The highlight of the entire trip was, of course, the fire dance at Uluwatu Temple.

The Kecak Fire Dance is a highly representative traditional performance in Bali, set against coastal cliffs and a sunset backdrop—truly an experience many travelers to Bali wouldn't want to miss.
