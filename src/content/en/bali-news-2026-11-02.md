## 🌧️ Days of Heavy Rain Cause Widespread Flooding in Bali! Some Tourists Evacuated by Rubber Rafts

Bali has been hit by heavy rainfall recently, leading to flooding in Denpasar and several surrounding areas, with some tourists even needing to be evacuated by rubber rafts.

According to disaster reports released on November 1, the heavy rain began on the evening of October 26, affecting multiple administrative regions across Bali. Local disaster management authorities confirmed that some stranded tourists have been assisted in evacuating.

Flood-prone areas of concern include:

- **Denpasar:** Sanur, Padangsambian, Renon
- **Badung Regency:** Seminyak, Kuta

The Bali government has installed 12 flood warning systems along rivers prone to flooding, with 6 of them located in Denpasar, aiming to alert residents early when water levels reach dangerous levels.

Previously, BMKG also issued warnings for heavy rain and high waves, with southern sea waves potentially reaching up to 3 meters.

What should you keep in mind when traveling to Bali soon?

This flooding incident serves as a reminder that during the rainy season in Bali, heavy rainfall can affect low-lying areas and roads with poor drainage.

If you're staying in Seminyak, Kuta, or Sanur, it's a good idea to check road conditions around your accommodation. When heavy rain hits, don't risk riding a scooter through flooded sections, and don't underestimate how much longer travel can take in wet weather.

It's important to note that the heavy rain and flooding reported mainly occurred after October 26—not that the entire island was still flooded on November 2. Travelers should base their plans on the latest weather and road updates.

## 🏄 Cliff Jumping in Bali Is Gaining Popularity! Blue Lagoon and Mahana Point Become Hotspots

Beyond surfing, snorkeling, and diving, Bali has recently seen a rise in another sea activity that adventurous travelers are flocking to: cliff jumping.

According to a travel report on November 1, this activity is increasingly popular among young travelers—not just for the thrill, but also because the footage of leaping off a cliff into the sea is perfect for short videos and social media content.

The most talked-about spots are mainly on the Nusa Islands:

**Blue Lagoon｜Nusa Ceningan**

This is a well-known cliff diving spot with platforms at different heights, including approximately 4, 6, 8, and 13 meters.

The striking contrast between the blue water and the cliffs makes it a favorite photo spot for adventurous travelers. However, waves may crash directly against the rock face, so you need to pay close attention to the actual currents and landing zones.

**Mahana Point｜Nusa Ceningan**

Mahana Point offers diving platforms at about 5 and 10 meters, along with stairs to help visitors get out of the water.

Compared to jumping directly from a natural cliff, this spot has more defined diving facilities, but you still need to check sea conditions and safety factors.

**Nusa Lembongan**

Some parts of Nusa Lembongan's coastline also have cliff diving spots, though certain locations have been closed in the past due to insufficient water depth or poor sea conditions.

### Cliff Diving Is Not Just Playing in the Water!

This activity carries certain risks, especially as water depth, tides, currents, and wave conditions can change at any time.

Things to note before you go:

- Never dive alone; it's best to have someone familiar with the area accompany you.
- Check that the water is deep enough and the landing area is free of rocks or floating debris.
- Avoid diving during big waves, strong currents, or bad weather.
- Make sure there is a safe route to get back to shore.
- Don't rely solely on old social media videos to judge whether a spot is safe.

If you're not a strong swimmer or there's no reliable safety management on site, it's best not to push yourself.

## 🙏 Bali's Banyu Pinaruh Traditional Ceremony! Hundreds Gather at Jasri Beach

November 1st marks the Balinese Hindu festival of Banyu Pinaruh, and hundreds of locals gathered at Jasri Beach in Karangasem, East Bali, early in the morning.

According to local media, families began arriving at the beach as early as 6 a.m. to take part in the traditional purification ritual.

Banyu Pinaruh is usually held the day after the Saraswati festival, making it one of the important traditional events in Balinese Hinduism.

Banyu means water, while Pinaruh is associated with knowledge. This ritual symbolizes cleansing the body and mind through water, washing away negative thoughts, and welcoming wisdom and knowledge.

Many locals choose Jasri Beach because it is relatively clean, and the waves are calmer on that day, making it suitable for families and the elderly to participate.

Beyond the religious ceremony, many locals also enjoy nearby local delicacies after the event concludes.

### Can foreign tourists participate?

Banyu Pinaruh is an important religious and cultural event for locals. Tourists can observe and appreciate it from a distance, but should respect the religious customs of the participants.

If you encounter similar traditional events during your trip to Bali, it's best to ask for permission before photographing people or rituals, and avoid disturbing ongoing prayers and purification activities.

Located in the Karangasem Regency of East Bali, Jasri Beach is also a coastal destination worth discovering for travelers planning to explore eastern Bali.

## 🚙 Tabanan Launches VW Vintage Car Tour Route! Tanah Lot Connects to Jatiluwih Rice Terraces

Tabanan Regency in Bali is experimenting with a new tourism product, aiming to let travelers visit more than just a single attraction by linking different types of sightseeing areas with a unique mode of transport.

Travel news released on October 31 announced that a local Volkswagen (VW) vintage car tour route has been launched, linking the iconic coastal landmark Tanah Lot Temple with the UNESCO World Heritage landscape of Jatiluwih Rice Terraces.

This tour product offers travelers the chance to experience two contrasting sides of Bali in a single journey:

**Tanah Lot Temple**

Located on the coast of Tabanan, this famous sea temple is renowned for its coastal rock formations, temple scenery, and stunning sunsets, making it a key tourist attraction in southwestern Bali.

Beyond sunset viewing, visitors can enjoy meals at the nearby Sunset Terrace and sample local snacks such as Rujak and Klepon.

**Jatiluwih Rice Terraces**

Jatiluwih is famous for its vast, layered rice terraces, a key representation of Bali's traditional Subak irrigation system and part of a UNESCO World Heritage site.

In contrast to the coastal scenery of Tanah Lot, Jatiluwih showcases Bali's inland agricultural culture and natural landscapes.

### Is this tour worth trying?

For travelers who love vintage cars, photography, and slow travel, the VW Tour offers a unique experience beyond standard private car tours.

However, the information released so far mainly introduces this travel product and the concept of connecting attractions, without fully disclosing fixed departure times, pricing, or daily schedules.

Interested travelers should first confirm the actual route, included attractions, travel time, and costs with local operators before deciding whether to book.
