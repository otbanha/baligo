Ubud is located in central Bali and serves as the island's tourism and cultural heart. With a rich history dating back to the 8th century AD, it has become one of Bali's most famous travel destinations.

The scenery here is strikingly different from other parts of the island, renowned for its unique blend of religion, art, and natural beauty. The cultural atmosphere of this mountain town is intoxicating, making Ubud a prime spot for capturing Instagram-worthy photos. Ancient temples, stunning rice terraces, and Balinese architecture everywhere showcase an irresistible visual charm. Every corner of Ubud feels like a unique work of art, letting you feel the city's profound soul and rich history.

### Ubud Water Palace (👉🏼 Map: [Ubud Center](/map/ubud/))

Ubud Water Palace (Pura Taman Saraswati), located just a three-minute walk from Ubud Palace, is a hidden corner behind Starbucks often overlooked by tourists, yet it boasts breathtaking beauty. Compared to Ubud Palace, this temple is more serene and mysterious, offering an excellent photography spot where you can capture unique and exquisite moments.

Ubud Water Palace is famous for its unique water features, situated at the end of a lotus pond walkway. The architecture of Ubud Water Palace is also rich in Bali's distinctive artistic style, with mysterious stone carvings and structures reflecting the deep cultural heritage of the region. Strolling here feels like traveling through time, immersed in ancient myths and religious beliefs.

### Monkey Forest (📍 Map: [Ubud Center](/map/ubud/))

Ubud Monkey Forest is a treasure trove where nature and culture blend seamlessly. Step into this lush sea of trees, where monkeys frolic around you, making you feel part of the natural world. Interact with these clever little creatures and experience the wonder of coexisting with nature.

However, since the monkeys here are quite mischievous, visitors should stay alert when entering the Monkey Forest. These curious little animals might climb all over you or even snatch your glasses or belongings. So, be cautious when interacting with them—avoid carrying food or obvious items that might attract their attention.

### Folk Pool & Gardens: A Serene Pool Club Hidden in the Heart of Ubud

![Folk Pool & Gardens 隱藏在烏布鬧區中的幽靜Pool Club](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2cc396ec6670466a36f49416c315f046.jpg)

### World Heritage Rice Terraces (👉🏼 Map: [Popular Attractions Around Ubud](/map/ubud/))

![世界遺產梯田景觀（地圖：烏布周邊熱門景點）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b32736a5ef98517bd7b4ebd87af9506f.jpg)

The rice terraces around Ubud are a highlight of Bali—a breathtaking landscape that feels like a painting and has earned UNESCO World Heritage status. This terraced area not only showcases nature's wonders but also highlights the beautiful harmony between nature and humans, making it an unmissable destination in Bali.

Tegalalang Rice Terrace is one of the easiest and most popular terraced areas to reach from Ubud. Its unique layout features winding paths through the terraces, weaving a green canvas that vividly brings nature's vitality to life. Here, rice paddies ripple like waves, creating a moving agricultural poem that blends natural beauty with farming culture.

Another less commercialized terrace area is Jatiluwih Rice Terrace, but it's farther from Ubud—about a 90-minute drive from Ubud Palace. However, it offers a more pristine experience of the terraces' beauty!

The best time to capture the beauty of the rice terraces is at dawn, just as the sun rises. At that moment, golden sunlight spills over the terraces, casting rich layers of shadow and creating a mesmerizing play of light. This is the favorite time for photographers and visitors, as the terraces seem immersed in a golden dream.

**Dalem Pingit Holy Spring** is a relatively secluded temple. Not only does it feature unique architecture, but its holy spring is also deeply revered by local devotees. In this tranquil setting, you can feel the sincerity and peace of Bali's original religious traditions. Dalem Pingit is especially known for its beautiful waterfall-fed holy spring.

This serene temple area is filled with a sense of mystery, making you feel as if you've traveled back in time to experience Bali's ancient sacredness.

Each of these two holy water temples has its own unique charm and cultural depth, while also offering a relatively quiet bathing environment where you can find peace and purification in Bali's sacred atmosphere.

### Wisata Kayu Putih Bayan Ancient Tree Scenic Area (👉🏼 Map: [Popular Attractions Around Ubud](/map/ubud/))

![Wisata Kayu Putih Bayan 神木風景區（地圖：烏布周邊熱門景點）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2c3bc45f0626ebf69733b0745a47a021.jpg)

The Wisata Kayu Putih Bayan Ancient Tree Scenic Area, located in Buleleng Regency in northern Bali, is famous for its spectacular thousand-year-old sacred tree. This massive white bark tree (Kayu Putih, meaning 'white wood' in Indonesian) is estimated to be over a thousand years old, with a trunk so thick it takes dozens of people to encircle it, and a towering canopy that covers a vast area. Local residents regard this ancient tree as sacred, believing it possesses spiritual power, and devotees often come here to pray and perform traditional ceremonies.

This ancient tree is not only a natural wonder but also a symbol of the harmonious coexistence of Balinese culture and nature. A small shrine at its base is adorned with traditional black-and-white checkered cloth (poleng), highlighting its religious significance. Visitors can feel the sacred and serene atmosphere and admire this ancient life that has witnessed centuries of change in Bali. The surrounding natural environment is tranquil, making it a unique spot to experience Bali's spiritual culture and natural beauty.

## [Around Pura Ulun Danu Bratan]

### Pura Ulun Danu Bratan (👉🏼 Map: [Popular Attractions Around Ubud](/map/ubud/))

![峇里島水神廟 （地圖：烏布周邊熱門景點）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0bcbb190a64dc4d8d05b92348d7fda9c.jpg)

Pura Ulun Danu Bratan, completed in 1633, sits on the shores of Lake Bratan in central Bali, and is also known as the "Lake Bratan Temple." Since Lake Bratan is the main source of irrigation for central Bali, Pura Ulun Danu Bratan was once used as a venue for ceremonies honoring Dewi Danu, the goddess of water, lakes, and rivers. Additionally, the 11-tiered temple is dedicated to Shiva and Parvati, and a statue of Buddha is also enshrined here, making it a fusion of Hindu and Buddhist traditions.

![峇里島水神廟 （地圖：烏布周邊熱門景點）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_bf3c1b5963a5c33d38b37b9e3375ccf4.jpg)

Interestingly, this Balinese water temple is also featured on Indonesia's national currency, specifically the second-largest denomination currently in circulation: 50,000 Indonesian rupiah, which is equivalent to about USD$100.

### Pura Besakih (👉🏼 Map: [Popular Attractions Around Ubud](/map/ubud/))

Pura Besakih is one of the largest and most sacred Hindu temples in Bali, perched on the slopes of Mount Agung. Considered the spiritual heart of the island, it is revered as the "Mother Temple." The complex features multiple shrines and altars dedicated to various deities.

This magnificent temple complex is set amidst stunning valleys and lush green rice terraces, offering breathtaking views. Visitors can immerse themselves in the ancient religious atmosphere, exploring the intricate temple architecture and sacred ambiance. Pura Besakih hosts many of Bali's religious and cultural events, such as festivals and ceremonies.

In 1963, Mount Agung erupted, killing 1,700 people, yet the lava flow stopped just a few meters from the temple, sparing Pura Besakih. This was seen as a miracle by the Balinese, a sign that the gods displayed their power without destroying faith. The temple holds profound religious significance and cultural value, drawing visitors from around the world. Guests must adhere to dress codes and respect local religious customs to honor this sacred site.
