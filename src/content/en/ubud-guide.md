### Ubud Water Palace (👉🏼 Map: [Ubud Center](/map/ubud/))

The Ubud Water Palace (Pura Taman Saraswati) is located just a three-minute walk from the Ubud Royal Palace, tucked away in a corner behind Starbucks, often overlooked by tourists yet boasting breathtaking beauty. Compared to the Ubud Royal Palace, this temple is more serene and mysterious, offering an excellent spot for photography where you can capture unique and exquisite moments.

The Ubud Water Palace is famous for its distinctive water features, situated at the end of a lotus pond walkway. The architecture of the Ubud Water Palace is also rich in Bali's unique artistic style, with mysterious stone carvings and structures reflecting the deep cultural heritage of the region. Strolling here feels like traveling through time, immersed in ancient myths and religious beliefs.

### Monkey Forest (👉🏼 Map: [Ubud Center](/map/ubud/))

Ubud Monkey Forest is a treasure where nature and culture blend seamlessly. Step into this lush sea of greenery, and monkeys frolic around you as if you've become part of the natural world. Interact with these clever little creatures and experience the wonder of coexisting with nature.

However, because the monkeys here are quite mischievous, visitors should stay alert when entering the forest. These curious little animals may climb all over you or even snatch your glasses or belongings. So, interact with them cautiously—avoid carrying food or obvious items that might attract their attention.

### Folk Pool & Gardens: A Serene Pool Club Hidden in Bustling Ubud

![Folk Pool & Gardens 隱藏在烏布鬧區中的幽靜Pool Club](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2cc396ec6670466a36f49416c315f046.jpg)

In the heart of Ubud’s lively center—amid endless shops, unique artisanal finds, delicious and healthy global cuisine, and rejuvenating yoga and spa experiences—there lies a secluded paradise: Folk Pool & Gardens.

This isn’t your typical restaurant where people just eat and leave. It’s different—spacious and unique. You can spend an entire day here, and it’s a fantastic spot for photos!

It’s the perfect final stop after a day of wandering and shopping through Ubud’s bustling streets—come here to relax, unwind, and snap some beautiful shots. At night, they even screen open-air movies!

To swim at Folk Pool & Gardens, a single sun lounger costs IDR 150,000 (about USD$5), and they provide a towel so you can swim and take photos. If you’d like to rent a bale (pavilion) that seats up to four people, it’s IDR 350,000 (about USD$11). It’s best to [book in advance](https://www.folkubud.com/).

### World Heritage Rice Terrace Views (👉🏼 Map: [Top Attractions Around Ubud](/map/ubud/))

![世界遺產梯田景觀（地圖：烏布周邊熱門景點）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b32736a5ef98517bd7b4ebd87af9506f.jpg)

The terraced rice fields around Ubud are a highlight of Bali, offering a breathtaking landscape that feels like a painting. This area has even earned UNESCO World Heritage status. These terraces not only showcase nature's wonders but also highlight the harmonious coexistence of nature and humanity, making it an unmissable destination in Bali.

Tegalalang Rice Terrace is one of the easiest and most popular terraced areas to reach from Ubud. Its unique layout features winding paths that weave through the green canvas of the fields, vividly displaying nature's vitality. Here, the rice paddies ripple like waves, creating a moving agricultural poem that blends natural beauty with farming culture.

Another less commercialized terraced area is Jatiluwih Rice Terrace, though it's farther from Ubud—about a 90-minute drive from the Ubud Palace. However, it offers a more authentic experience of the pristine beauty of the rice terraces.

The best time to capture the beauty of the terraces is at dawn, just as the sun rises. At this moment, golden sunlight spills over the fields, casting layered shadows and creating a mesmerizing play of light. This is a favorite time for photographers and travelers, as the terraces seem immersed in a golden dream.

The Wisata Kayu Putih Bayan Ancient Tree Scenic Area, located in Buleleng Regency in northern Bali, is famous for its spectacular thousand-year-old sacred tree. This massive Kayu Putih tree (meaning 'white wood' in Indonesian) is estimated to be over a thousand years old, with a trunk so thick it takes dozens of people to encircle it, and a towering canopy that covers a vast area. Local residents regard this ancient tree as sacred, believing it possesses spiritual power, and devotees often come here to pray and hold traditional ceremonies.

This ancient tree is not only a natural wonder but also a symbol of the harmonious coexistence of Balinese culture and nature. A small shrine at its base, adorned with traditional black-and-white checkered cloth (poleng), highlights its religious significance. Visitors can feel a sacred and serene atmosphere here, admiring this ancient life that has witnessed centuries of Bali's history. The surrounding natural environment is tranquil, making it a unique spot to experience Bali's spiritual culture and natural beauty.

![峇里島水神廟 （地圖：烏布周邊熱門景點）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0bcbb190a64dc4d8d05b92348d7fda9c.jpg)

Pura Ulun Danu Bratan, completed in 1633, sits on the shores of Lake Bratan in central Bali and is also known as the 'Bratan Water Temple.' Since Lake Bratan is the main irrigation source for central Bali, this temple once served as a venue for ceremonies honoring Dewi Danu, the goddess of water, lakes, and rivers. Additionally, the 11-tiered Pura Ulun Danu Bratan is a temple dedicated to Shiva and Parvati, and a statue of the Buddha is also enshrined here, making it a fusion of Hindu and Buddhist traditions.

![峇里島水神廟 （地圖：烏布周邊熱門景點）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_bf3c1b5963a5c33d38b37b9e3375ccf4.jpg)

Remarkably, this Balinese water temple is featured on Indonesia's national currency, appearing on the second-largest denomination in circulation: the 50,000 Indonesian rupiah note, which is equivalent to about USD$3.

As a volcanic island, Bali is home to one of its most famous hot springs, Toya Devasya. This hot spring water park, built on the shores of Lake Batur, is one of the island's top attractions. It's known for its natural hot springs, water park, and stunning lake views.

### Pura Besakih (Mother Temple) (👉🏼 Map: [Popular Attractions Around Ubud](/map/ubud/))

Pura Besakih, the largest and holiest Hindu temple in Bali, sits on the slopes of Mount Agung. This temple is considered the spiritual heart of Bali and is revered as the 'Mother Temple.' It comprises multiple shrines and altars dedicated to various deities.

This magnificent temple complex sits nestled between beautiful valleys and lush green rice terraces, offering breathtaking views. Visitors can immerse themselves in an ancient religious atmosphere, exploring intricate temple architecture and a sacred ambiance. Besakih Mother Temple hosts many of Bali's religious and cultural events, such as festivals and ceremonies.

In 1963, Mount Agung erupted, killing 1,700 people, yet the lava flowed just meters away from the temple, sparing Besakih Mother Temple. This was seen as a miracle by the Balinese, who believe it was a sign from the gods demonstrating their power without destroying faith. The temple holds profound religious significance and cultural value, attracting visitors from around the world. Guests must adhere to dress codes and respect local religious customs to show reverence for this sacred site.
