✈️ **Bali Six-Day, Five-Night Self-Guided Trip Journal (June 22–27)**

This trip was truly a long-awaited time for just the two of us! 👫

After leaving our child with grandma, my wife and I packed our bags with barely contained excitement, ready to board our flight to Bali. For parents who haven't had a break abroad in ages, this relaxing vacation felt especially precious—our anticipation was through the roof.

## 📌 **Practical Tips: Three Online Procedures to Complete Before Departure**

Before this trip, we followed advice from many travel experts and applied for Indonesia's **e-VOA (Electronic Visa on Arrival)** in advance. I honestly think this step is crucial—it can save you a lot of precious waiting time.

However, I also want to warn you about a little pitfall—my wife **accidentally entered the wrong birth date** when filling out the e-VOA 😓. As a result, she had to go through the manual visa-on-arrival counter and ended up waiting in line for **an hour and a half**. So regretful!

Besides the visa, there are two other online procedures that are also important. I recommend taking care of them before boarding to avoid any last-minute hassle upon arrival:

1. **All Indonesia Arrival Card (including e-CD Customs Declaration and SSHP Health Statement)**
2. **Tourist Levy Payment Receipt**

All four steps are **digital documents that can be completed online**, and they're actually quite straightforward—just have your passport copy, flight details, and payment method ready. I recommend saving the QR codes as screenshots in your phone album after completion, as presenting them for scanning at immigration will speed things up significantly. For a detailed walkthrough, check this out: [2026 Bali Entry Changes in One Minute: Indonesia/Bali Arrival Guide - From Landing, Customs, to Baggage Claim](/en/blog/bali-all-indonesia-arrival-guide/)

Next up, it's time to kick off our Bali duo adventure~ 🌴

## 🌅 **Day 1: Arriving in Bali, Checking into the Dreamy AYANA Resort**

We took EVA Air's direct 9:50 AM flight, scheduled to land in Bali at 3:30 PM. The flight was a bit long, but after a quick nap on the plane, my mind was already switching into vacation mode.

After arriving at Ngurah Rai International Airport, we queued for the manual visa process, so by the time we cleared customs, it was already getting dark. We hopped into our pre-booked transfer and arrived at **AYANA Resort and Spa Bali** in Jimbaran around 6 PM.

### 🏨 **AYANA Resort is Worth Staying for Two Nights!**

This hotel was hands-down the most anticipated stop of our trip! AYANA is one of Bali's most renowned resorts, boasting top-notch facilities, stunning ocean views, and a variety of signature restaurants. The absolute must-visit is the legendary **Rock Bar Bali**—one of the world's top ten sunset bars and a wildly popular Instagram-worthy spot.

https://youtu.be/lbDfKut2J7M

Although we arrived a bit late and missed the sunset, only catching the night view, standing at the cliffside bar area, listening to the waves crash and watching the lights sway, the atmosphere was incredibly romantic 💫. The food itself was decent but on the pricier side—but honestly, you're not here for the cuisine; you're here for that magical vibe of dancing with the sea and sky.

![(Johnny Wei提供)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_197ac68f3eeb4671e523304f84bab3d1.jpg)

We stayed in a sea-view room, which cost about USD$387 per night. While it was already quite nice, here's a little tip: **the AYANA resort is massive**. If you choose a more budget-friendly room, you might end up in a remote area, and walking to the main facilities could leave your legs exhausted. I'd recommend adding a bit to your budget and picking a room closer to the main facilities for a much better experience!

The crowd for photos was already gathering early in the morning—you have to queue, and each person gets just one minute for a quick snap. But the good news is—you can line up again! 📷

### 🍽 **Hearty Breakfast, Skip Lunch**

For breakfast, we chose the buffet restaurant near the AYANA lobby. Although it was a bit pricey at about USD$30 per person, the selection was incredibly rich—featuring Indonesian local dishes, Japanese cuisine, European cold cuts, and even made-to-order omelets along with bread and desserts. It was truly generous. After eating our fill, we couldn't even manage lunch, saving us a meal budget 😂

A little side note: we left a **$2 USD tip** while housekeeping was cleaning, and when we returned to the room in the afternoon, the housekeeping lady had quietly prepared a little surprise — thoughtful and heartwarming, truly capturing AYANA’s spirit of service. In contrast, last time in Chiang Mai, a friend left $10 USD and got nothing in return. I guess luck is a kind of skill too 😆

## ☕️ **Day 3: Heading to the Highland Café, Checking into a Romantic Jungle Villa in Ubud**

After a hearty breakfast and a leisurely morning at AYANA, we checked out at 11:00 AM, ready to begin the "mountain chapter" of our trip 🌿.

### 🚗 **A Long Drive, All for a Cup of Coffee and the View**

Our first stop of the day was a scenic café highly recommended by my cousin — **Montana Del Café**, located in the high-altitude Kintamani region of northern Bali. Driving from AYANA in Jimbaran, the one-way trip took a whopping **two and a half hours** — a real mountain-crossing journey.

![(Johnny Wei提供)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_5245fa7b61c80c4a4252d2e9876cc3e0.jpg)

(Photo by Johnny Wei)

But I have to say, it was totally worth it once we arrived! Montana Del Café boasts sweeping mountain views, with the majestic Mount Batur and its lake in the distance. Whether you sit indoors or on the terrace, it's like a postcard-perfect scene 📸. We ordered two coffees and two slices of cheesecake, totaling around USD$16 — quite reasonable for a scenic café. The real highlight? That priceless feeling of sipping coffee above the clouds ✨

If you love photography or crave fresh mountain air, I highly recommend adding this to your itinerary. Just keep in mind the long travel time — ask your driver if you can pair it with other Ubud attractions, like volcanic hot springs, to make the trip more efficient.

### 🌿 **Check-in: Abisena Wellness & Resort Ubud**

After wrapping up our highland adventure, we drove back to central **Ubud** and checked into **Abisena Wellness & Resort**, a hidden gem nestled in the jungle. This stay turned out to be one of the most delightful surprises of our trip, perfectly matching our desire for a "relaxing + romantic" getaway vibe!

![(Johnny Wei提供)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0bcb27348f2a4fd72f4e265beed93773.jpg)

First off, this is an adults-only resort. Without the noise of children playing, the atmosphere is especially serene. The staff are warm and attentive, from the welcome drink to luggage assistance—every detail shows their thoughtfulness.

We chose the **Jungle View Villa**. The room design blends modern and natural elements, with large floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking lush mountain forests. You can even hear birds chirping, insects humming, and the gentle flow of a stream—perfect for couples seeking a romantic and peaceful getaway. The villa comes with a private balcony and pool, so even if you don't feel like going out, you can soak in the tub surrounded by greenery—pure bliss 💚

## 🏝 **Day 4: Check into The Claremont Luxury Villas in Seminyak for a Convenient and Comfortable Stay**

Leaving the tranquil Ubud highlands behind, we headed to the lively beach area—**Seminyak**. This is Bali's trendy district, packed not only with restaurants and bars but also boutique shops and Instagram-worthy cafes. It's super convenient and perfect for travelers wanting a city-resort vibe.

That night we stayed at **The Claremont Luxury Villas**, right in the heart of Seminyak. The value for money here is incredible—around **USD$148 per night**—yet the space is spacious, amenities are complete, and it even has a private pool! Within a few minutes' walk, you'll find convenience stores, international restaurants, and massage shops—no need to call a ride.

## 🎉 **Day 5: Pilgrimage to Finns Beach Club — Worth Splurging Like a VIP for a Day!**

Having visited Bali many times, we finally made it to the much-anticipated **Finns Beach Club** on this trip! As one of Bali's most iconic beach clubs, located in the Canggu area, the venue stretches right onto the beach, featuring multiple pools, sun loungers, bars, DJ stages, and VIP cabanas. The vibe is lively and absolutely buzzing.

https://youtu.be/MpetWBEQ2Ts

However, online reviews are really mixed — some people complain about **cold service and high prices**, while many others rave about the **great atmosphere, excellent facilities, and say it's worth a visit**.

From our own experience — **if you just order the cheapest sunbed package, you really do get overlooked**; but this time we bit the bullet and upgraded to a VIP sofa cabana package. We spent a good chunk of money over the day, but the treatment we received was completely different! ✨

💡The key to getting good service really is **spending your money in the right places**! When you choose a high-end cabana, the security guards, waitstaff, and even dancers will proactively come over to greet and interact with you, instantly elevating the vibe to something like a private party.

The group next to us was also in the cabana area, but they probably didn't order much or engage much, and ended up being almost completely ignored — even the dancers walked right past without a glance 😅 So, **if you want to have a blast at Finns, you've got to splurge like a VIP for a day** (the happy kind), and only then can you truly experience its charm!

## 🛫 **Day 6: A Bittersweet Goodbye, Heading Back to Taiwan**

Our short six-day, five-night Bali trip came to an end. After checking out at noon, we took a car to Ngurah Rai Airport for our flight home. ## 📝 **Five Key Takeaways from This Bali Trip, Hope They Help:**

### ✅ **1. Get Your Online Visa on Arrival in Advance!**

I can't stress this enough — **the e-VOA online visa is a lifesaver!** It saves you tons of time waiting in line. Especially if you're traveling with kids or on a red-eye flight, applying ahead of time is a true act of self-care.

### 📱 **2. Always Use Gojek or Grab for Rides**

These two local ride-hailing apps let you pay by credit card, with transparent pricing and peace of mind. We once made the mistake of hailing a motorbike on the street for convenience, only to get ripped off — they quoted us in US dollars with a hefty markup, acting like it was totally normal 😓. Don't skimp on the small stuff and end up losing big.

{{block:包車}}

### 🚦 **3. Traffic Jams Are the Norm in Bali — Don't Overpack Your Itinerary!**

Especially on the routes from Jimbaran and Seminyak to Ubud, traffic jams are common. I strongly recommend planning just 1–2 key activities per day, otherwise you'll spend your trip constantly 'missing out' and it'll be exhausting.

### 💵 **4. Tipping is fine, but don't let guilt pressure you!**

Over 75% of Bali's population works in the service industry, so tipping moderately is a gesture of politeness and support. However, you may encounter people who use verbal manipulation or act entitled to tips. In such cases, **you really don't need to force yourself to tip**—maintaining boundaries and self-respect is more important.

Quick reference: [Do you need to tip in Bali? How to tip? How much to tip in various situations?](/en/blog/bali-tipping-guide-driver-spa/)

### 🐰 **5. Gold Rabbit Coffee Factory? Google it yourself before deciding whether to go!**

If you book a private driver or guided tour, you'll often be 'conveniently taken' to this coffee factory. These places mainly push expensive civet coffee, sometimes with high-pressure sales tactics or scripted spiels. Whether it's worth a visit is up to you to decide 🧐
