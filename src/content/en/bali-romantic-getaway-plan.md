✈️ **Bali Six-Day, Five-Night Independent Travel Journal (June 22–June 27)**

This trip was truly a long-awaited time for just the two of us! 👫

After leaving our child with grandma, my wife and I practically packed our bags in a hurry, eager to board the flight to Bali. For parents who haven't had a break abroad in a while, this relaxing vacation felt especially precious, and our excitement was through the roof.

## 📌 **Practical Tips: Three Online Procedures to Complete Before Departure**

Before this trip, we followed advice from many travel experts and applied for Indonesia's **e-VOA (Electronic Visa on Arrival)** in advance. I honestly think this step is crucial—it can save you a lot of precious waiting time.

However, here's a little trap to watch out for: my wife **accidentally entered the wrong birth date** when filling out the e-VOA 😓. As a result, she had to go through the manual visa-on-arrival channel and waited in line for **an hour and a half**—so regretful!

Besides the visa, there are two other online procedures that are also important. I recommend taking care of them before boarding to avoid any last-minute hassle upon arrival:

1. **All Indonesia Arrival Card (including e-CD Customs Declaration and SSHP Health Statement)**
2. **Tourist Levy Payment Receipt**

All four steps are **digital documents that can be completed online**, and they're actually quite straightforward—just make sure you have a copy of your passport, flight details, and a payment method ready. Once done, I recommend saving the QR codes as screenshots in your phone album—showing them at immigration will speed things up significantly. For a detailed walkthrough, check this out: [2026 Bali Entry Changes in One Minute: Indonesia/Bali Arrival Guide - From Landing, Customs, to Baggage Claim](/en/blog/bali-all-indonesia-arrival-guide/)

Next up, it's time to kick off our Bali duo adventure~ 🌴

## 🌅 **Day 1: Arriving in Bali, Checking into the Dreamy AYANA Resort**

We took EVA Air's direct 9:50 AM flight, scheduled to land in Bali at 3:30 PM. The flight was a bit long, but a quick nap on the plane, and my mind was already switching into vacation mode.

After arriving at Ngurah Rai International Airport, we queued for the manual visa process, so by the time we cleared customs, it was already getting dark. We hopped into our pre-booked transfer and reached **AYANA Resort and Spa Bali** in Jimbaran around 6 PM.

### 🏨 **AYANA Resort Is Worth Staying Two Nights!**

This hotel was hands-down the highlight we were most excited about! AYANA is one of Bali's most renowned resorts, boasting top-notch facilities, stunning ocean views, and a variety of signature restaurants. The absolute must-visit is the legendary **Rock Bar Bali**—one of the world's top ten sunset bars and a wildly popular Instagram spot.

https://youtu.be/lbDfKut2J7M

Although we arrived a bit late and missed the sunset, only catching the night view, standing at the cliffside bar area, listening to the crashing waves and watching the twinkling lights, the atmosphere was incredibly romantic 💫. The food was decent but a bit pricey—what you're really paying for here isn't the meal, but that magical vibe of dancing with the sea and sky.

We stayed in a sea-view room for about $387 USD per night. While it was already quite nice, here's a tip: **the AYANA resort complex is enormous**, so if you opt for a cheaper room category, you might end up in a more remote area and have to walk quite a distance to reach the main facilities. I'd recommend budgeting a bit more for a room closer to the main facilities for a better experience!

The photo crowd gathers early—there's a queue, and each person gets just one minute to snap their shot. But the good news is—you can queue up again! 📷

### 🍽 **Hearty Breakfast, Skip Lunch**

For breakfast, we chose the buffet restaurant near the AYANA lobby. Though it was a bit pricey at about $31 USD per person, the selection was incredibly rich—featuring Indonesian local dishes, Japanese cuisine, European cold cuts, and even made-to-order omelets, pastries, and desserts. It was well worth it. After eating our fill, we couldn't even manage lunch, saving us a meal budget 😂

A little side note: we left a **$2 tip** while housekeeping was cleaning, and when we returned to the room in the afternoon, the housekeeping lady had quietly prepared a little surprise—thoughtful and heartwarming, truly capturing AYANA’s spirit of service. In contrast, last time in Chiang Mai, a friend left a $10 tip and got nothing in return—guess luck is a kind of skill too 😆

## ☕️ **Day 3: Heading to the Highland Café, Checking into a Romantic Jungle Villa in Ubud**

After a hearty breakfast and a leisurely morning at AYANA, we checked out at 11:00 AM, ready to begin the "mountain chapter" of our trip 🌿.

### 🚗 **A Long Drive, All for a Cup of Coffee and Stunning Views**

Our first stop of the day was a scenic café highly recommended by my cousin — **Montana Del Café**, located in the high-altitude Kintamani region of northern Bali. Driving from AYANA in Jimbaran, the one-way trip took a whopping **two and a half hours** — a real mountain-crossing journey.

![(Johnny Wei提供)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_5245fa7b61c80c4a4252d2e9876cc3e0.jpg)

(Photo by Johnny Wei)

But I have to say, it was totally worth it once we arrived! Montana Del Café boasts sweeping mountain views, with the majestic Mount Batur and its lake in the distance. Whether sitting indoors or on the terrace, it felt like stepping into a postcard 📸. We ordered two coffees and two slices of cheesecake, costing around USD$16 in total — quite reasonable for a scenic café. The best part? That priceless feeling of sipping coffee above the clouds ✨

If you love photography or crave fresh mountain air, I highly recommend adding this to your itinerary. Just keep in mind the long travel time — ask your driver if you can pair it with other Ubud attractions, like volcanic hot springs, for a more efficient route.

### 🌿 **Check-in: Abisena Wellness & Resort Ubud**

After wrapping up our highland adventure, we drove back to central **Ubud** and checked into **Abisena Wellness & Resort**, a hidden gem nestled in the jungle. This accommodation turned out to be one of the most delightful surprises of our trip, perfectly matching our desire for a "relaxing + romantic" getaway vibe!

First of all, this is an 'adults-only' resort. Without the noise of children playing, the atmosphere is exceptionally peaceful. The staff are warm and attentive, from the welcome drink to luggage assistance—every detail shows their dedication.

We chose the **Jungle View Villa**. The room design blends modern and natural elements, with large floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking lush mountains. You can even hear birds chirping, insects humming, and a babbling stream—perfect for couples seeking a romantic and tranquil getaway. The villa comes with a private balcony and pool, so you can soak in the greenery without stepping out. Pure bliss 💚

## 🏝 **Day 4: Check into The Claremont Luxury Villas in Seminyak for a Convenient and Comfortable Stay**

Leaving the serene Ubud highlands behind, we headed to the lively coastal area—**Seminyak**. This is Bali's trendy district, packed not only with restaurants and bars but also boutique shops and Instagram-worthy cafes. It's super convenient and perfect for travelers who want a city-meets-resort vibe.

That night, we stayed at **The Claremont Luxury Villas**, right in the heart of Seminyak. The value for money here is incredible—around **USD$148 per night**—yet the space is spacious, fully equipped, and comes with a private pool! Within a few minutes' walk, you'll find convenience stores, international restaurants, and massage places, so you don't even need to call a ride.

## 🎉 **Day 5: Pilgrimage to Finns Beach Club—Worth Playing the Splurge Card for a Day!**

Having visited Bali many times, we finally made it to the long-awaited **Finns Beach Club** on this trip! As one of Bali's most iconic beach clubs, located in the Canggu area, the venue stretches out to the beach with multiple pools, sun loungers, bars, DJ stages, and VIP cabanas. The vibe is lively and absolutely buzzing.

https://youtu.be/MpetWBEQ2Ts

That said, online reviews are really mixed—some find the **service staff indifferent and prices steep**, while many others rave about the **great atmosphere, top-notch facilities, and think it's worth a visit**.

From our own experience—**if you just order the cheapest sunbed package, you really do get overlooked**; but this time, we splurged and upgraded to a VIP sofa cabana package. It cost a pretty penny for the whole day, but the treatment we received was a world apart! ✨

💡 The key to getting good service really is 'spending your money in the right places'! Once you opt for a higher-tier cabana, the security, servers, and even dancers proactively come over to greet and interact with you, instantly elevating the vibe to something like a private party.

The group next to us was also in the cabana area, but they probably didn't order much or engage much, and ended up being largely ignored the whole time—even the dancers walked right past without a glance 😅 So, **if you want to have a blast at Finns, you've got to play the splurge card for a day** (the happy kind), and only then can you truly soak in the charm here!

## 🛫 **Day 6: A Bittersweet Goodbye, Heading Back to Taiwan**

Our short six-day, five-night Bali trip came to an end. After checking out at noon, we took a car to Ngurah Rai Airport for our flight home. Although the flight was scheduled for 4:30 PM, a last-minute airline scheduling delay pushed things back, and we didn't land in Taiwan until **11:00 PM** — hours later than expected, leaving us utterly exhausted.

But hey, that's travel — full of unexpected twists. What matters is the memories we made, and it was all worth it! 🎒

## 📝 **Five Quick Takeaways from This Bali Trip (Hope They Help!):**

### ✅ **1. Get Your E-Visa on Arrival in Advance!**

Seriously, I can't stress this enough: **the e-VOA online visa is a lifesaver!** It saves you tons of time waiting in line. Especially if you're traveling with kids or arriving on a red-eye flight, applying ahead of time is a must for your own sanity.

### 📱 **2. Always Use Gojek or Grab for Rides**

These two local ride-hailing apps let you pay by credit card, with transparent pricing and zero hassle. We once took a street motorbike for convenience and got completely ripped off — they quoted us in US dollars like it was totally normal 😓. Don't lose big money trying to save a little.

{{block:包車}}

### 🚦 **3. Traffic Jams Are the Norm in Bali — Don't Overpack Your Itinerary!**

Routes like Jimbaran, Seminyak, and Ubud are notorious for traffic. I highly recommend planning just 1–2 key activities per day, or you'll spend the whole trip feeling like you're constantly missing out — and it'll be a real struggle.

### 💵 **4. Tipping Culture: It's Fine to Tip, but Don't Let It Turn into Emotional Blackmail!**

Over 75% of Bali's population works in the service industry, so tipping moderately is a polite gesture of support. However, you may encounter people who use verbal manipulation or act entitled to tips. In such cases, **you really don't need to force yourself to tip**—maintaining boundaries and self-respect is more important.

Quick reference: [Do You Need to Tip in Bali? How to Tip? How Much to Tip in Various Situations?](/en/blog/bali-tipping-guide-driver-spa/)

### 🐰 **5. Gold Rabbit Coffee Factory? Google It Yourself Before Deciding!**

If you book a private driver or guided tour, you'll often be 'conveniently taken' to this coffee factory. These places mainly push expensive civet coffee, sometimes with high-pressure sales tactics or scripted explanations. Whether it's worth a visit is entirely up to you to decide 🧐
