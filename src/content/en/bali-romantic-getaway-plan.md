✈️ **Bali Six-Day, Five-Night Independent Travel Journal (June 22–June 27)**

This trip was truly a long-awaited time for just the two of us! 👫

After leaving our child with grandma, my wife and I practically packed our bags in a hurry, ready to board the flight to Bali. For parents who haven't had a break abroad in a while, this relaxing vacation felt especially precious, and our expectations were through the roof.

## 📌 **Practical Tips: Three Online Procedures to Complete Before Departure**

Before this trip, we followed advice from many travel experts and applied for Indonesia's **e-VOA (Electronic Visa on Arrival)** in advance. I honestly think this step is super important—it can save you a lot of precious waiting time.

However, I also want to warn you about a little pitfall—my wife **accidentally entered the wrong birth date** when filling out the e-VOA 😓. As a result, she had to go through the manual visa-on-arrival channel and waited in line for **an hour and a half**. So regretful!

Besides the visa, there are two other online procedures that are also important. I recommend handling them before boarding to avoid any last-minute hassle upon arrival:

1. **All Indonesia Arrival Card (including e-CD Customs Declaration and SSHP Health Statement)**
2. **Tourist Levy Payment Receipt**

All four steps are **digital documents that can be completed online**, and they're actually quite straightforward—just make sure you have a copy of your passport, flight details, and a payment method ready. Once done, I recommend saving the QR codes as screenshots in your phone album; presenting them for scanning upon arrival will speed things up significantly. For a detailed walkthrough, check this out: [2026 Bali Entry Changes in One Minute: Indonesia/Bali Arrival Process - From Landing, Customs, to Baggage Claim](/en/blog/bali-all-indonesia-arrival-guide/)

Next up, it's time to kick off our Bali duo trip~ 🌴

## 🌅 **Day 1: Arriving in Bali, Checking into the Dreamy AYANA Resort**

We took EVA Air's direct 9:50 AM flight, scheduled to land in Bali at 3:30 PM. The flight was a bit long, but after a quick nap on the plane, my mind had already switched into vacation mode.

After arriving at Ngurah Rai International Airport, we queued for the manual visa process, and by the time we cleared customs, it was already getting dark. We hopped into our pre-booked transfer and reached **AYANA Resort and Spa Bali** in Jimbaran around 6 PM.

### 🏨 **AYANA Resort is Worth Staying for Two Nights!**

This hotel was hands-down the most anticipated stop on our trip! AYANA is one of Bali's most renowned resorts, boasting top-notch facilities, stunning ocean views, and a variety of signature restaurants. The absolute must-visit is the legendary **Rock Bar Bali**—one of the world's top ten sunset bars and a wildly popular Instagram spot.

https://youtu.be/lbDfKut2J7M

Although we arrived a bit late and missed the sunset, only catching the night view, standing at the cliffside bar area, listening to the waves and watching the lights sway, the atmosphere was incredibly romantic 💫. The food was decent but on the pricier side—but honestly, you're not here for the meal; you're here for that magical vibe of dancing with the sea and sky.

![(Johnny Wei提供)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_197ac68f3eeb4671e523304f84bab3d1.jpg)

We stayed in a sea-view room for about USD$387 per night. While it was already quite nice, here's a little tip: **the AYANA resort complex is enormous**. If you choose a more budget-friendly room type, you might end up in a remote area, and walking to the main facilities could leave your legs sore. I'd recommend adding a bit to your budget and picking a room closer to the main facilities for a much better experience!

The crowd for photos was already gathering early, with a queue and a one-minute time limit per person—but the good news is you can line up again and again! 📷

### 🍽 **Eat a Hearty Breakfast, Skip Lunch**

For breakfast, we chose the buffet restaurant near the AYANA lobby. Although it was a bit pricey at about USD$30 per person, the selection was incredibly rich—featuring Indonesian local dishes, Japanese cuisine, European cold cuts, and even made-to-order omelets, bread, and desserts. It was truly generous. After eating our fill, we couldn't even manage lunch, saving us a meal budget 😂

A little side note: we left a **$2 tip** while housekeeping was cleaning, and when we returned to the room in the afternoon, the housekeeping lady had quietly prepared a little surprise—thoughtful and heartwarming, truly capturing AYANA's service spirit. In contrast, last time in Chiang Mai, a friend left a $10 tip and got nothing in return—guess luck is also a kind of skill 😆

## ☕️ **Day 3: Heading to the Highland Café, Checking into a Romantic Jungle Villa in Ubud**

After a hearty breakfast and a leisurely morning enjoying AYANA, we checked out at 11:00 AM, ready to begin the 'mountain chapter' of our trip 🌿.

### 🚗 **A Long Drive for a Cup of Coffee and Stunning Views**

Our first stop of the day was a scenic café highly recommended by my cousin — **Montana Del Café**, located in the high-altitude Kintamani region of northern Bali. Driving from AYANA in Jimbaran, the one-way trip took a whopping **two and a half hours** — a real mountain-crossing journey.

![(Johnny Wei提供)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_5245fa7b61c80c4a4252d2e9876cc3e0.jpg)

(Photo by Johnny Wei)

But I have to say, it was totally worth it once we arrived! Montana Del Café boasts sweeping mountain views, with the majestic Mount Batur and its lake in the distance. Whether you sit indoors or on the terrace, it's a postcard-perfect scene 📸. We ordered two coffees and two slices of cheesecake, costing around USD$16 in total — quite reasonable for a scenic café. The real highlight was that feeling of 'sipping coffee above the clouds' — truly priceless ✨

If you love photography or crave fresh mountain air, I highly recommend adding this to your itinerary. Just be mindful of the long travel time — you can ask your driver if it's possible to pair it with other Ubud attractions, like volcanic hot springs, for a more efficient route.

### 🌿 **Check-in: Abisena Wellness & Resort Ubud**

After wrapping up our highland adventure, we drove back to central **Ubud** and checked into **Abisena Wellness & Resort**, a hideaway nestled in the jungle. This accommodation turned out to be one of the most delightful surprises of our trip, perfectly matching our desire for a 'relaxing + romantic' vacation vibe!

First off, this is an adults-only resort. Without the noise of children playing, the atmosphere is exceptionally serene. The staff are warm and attentive, from the welcome drink to luggage assistance—every detail shows their thoughtfulness.

We chose the **Jungle View Villa**. The room design blends modern and natural elements, with large floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking lush mountain forests. You can even hear birds chirping, insects humming, and a babbling stream—perfect for couples seeking a romantic and peaceful getaway. The villa comes with a private balcony and pool, so you can soak in the greenery without stepping out. It's pure bliss 💚

That night, we stayed at **The Claremont Luxury Villas**, right in the heart of Seminyak. This villa offers incredible value—around **USD$148 per night**—yet it's spacious, well-equipped, and comes with a private pool! Within a few minutes' walk, you'll find convenience stores, international restaurants, and massage places, so you don't even need to call a ride.

## 🎉 **Day 5: Pilgrimage to Finns Beach Club – Worth Splurging for a Day of Luxury!**

Having visited Bali many times, we finally made it to the long-awaited **Finns Beach Club** on this trip! As one of Bali's most iconic beach clubs, located in Canggu, the venue stretches out to the beach with multiple pools, sun loungers, bars, DJ stages, and VIP cabanas. The vibe is lively and absolutely buzzing.

https://youtu.be/MpetWBEQ2Ts

However, online reviews are really mixed—some find the **service staff indifferent and prices steep**, while many others think the **atmosphere is great, facilities are top-notch, and it's worth a visit**.

From our own experience—**if you just order the cheapest sun lounger package, you really do get overlooked**; but this time we bit the bullet and upgraded to a VIP sofa cabana package. While it cost a pretty penny for the whole day, the treatment we received was completely different! ✨

💡The key to getting good service really is 'spending your money in the right places'! When you choose a high-end cabana, the security guards, servers, and even dancers will proactively come over to greet and interact with you, instantly elevating the vibe to something like a private party.

The group next to us was also in the cabana area, but they probably didn't order much or engage much, so they were almost entirely ignored throughout—even the dancers walked right past them without a glance 😅 So, **to have a blast at Finns, you've got to splurge like a VIP for a day** (the happy kind), and that's how you truly experience its charm!

## 🛫 **Day 6: A Bittersweet Farewell, Heading Back to Taiwan**

Our short six-day, five-night Bali trip came to an end. After checking out at noon, we took a car to Ngurah Rai Airport for our flight back to Taiwan. ## 📝 **Five key takeaways from this Bali trip, hope they help:**

### ✅ **1. Get your e-VOA online visa in advance!**

I can't stress this enough — **the e-VOA online visa is super important!** It saves you a ton of time waiting in line. Especially if you're traveling with kids or on a red-eye flight, applying early is a sign of respect for your own energy.

### 📱 **2. Always use Gojek or Grab to book rides**

These two local ride-hailing apps in Indonesia let you pay by credit card, with transparent pricing and peace of mind. We once made the mistake of hailing a motorbike on the street for convenience, and got ripped off — they quoted us in US dollars with a markup, acting like it was totally normal 😓. Don't try to save a little and end up losing big.

{{block:包車}}

### 🚦 **3. Traffic jams are a daily reality in Bali — don't overpack your itinerary!**

Especially on the routes from Jimbaran and Seminyak to Ubud, traffic jams are common. It's highly recommended to plan just 1–2 key activities per day, or you'll spend most of your trip 'missing out' and struggling to keep up.

### 💵 **4. Tipping is optional, but don't let guilt pressure you!**

Over 75% of Bali's population works in the service industry, so tipping moderately is a polite gesture of support. However, you may encounter people who use verbal manipulation or act entitled to tips. In such cases, **there's no need to force yourself to tip**—it's more important to set boundaries and respect yourself.

Quick reference: [Do you need to tip in Bali? How to tip? How much to tip in various situations?](/en/blog/bali-tipping-guide-driver-spa/)

### 🐰 **5. Gold Rabbit Coffee Factory? Google it yourself before deciding!**

If you book a private driver or guided tour, you'll often be 'conveniently taken' to this coffee factory. These places mainly push expensive civet coffee, sometimes with high-pressure sales tactics or scripted pitches. Whether it's worth a visit is up to you to decide! 🧐
