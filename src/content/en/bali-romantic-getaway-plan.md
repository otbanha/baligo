✈️ **Bali Six-Day, Five-Night Self-Guided Trip Report (June 22–27)**

This trip was truly a long-awaited time for just the two of us! 👫

After leaving our child with grandma, my wife and I practically packed our bags in a hurry, eager to board our flight to Bali. For parents who haven't had a getaway in a while, this relaxing vacation felt especially precious, and our excitement was through the roof.

## 📌 **Practical Tips: Three Online Procedures to Complete Before Departure**

Before this trip, we followed advice from many travel experts and applied for Indonesia's **e-VOA (Electronic Visa on Arrival)** in advance. I honestly think this step is crucial—it can save you a lot of valuable waiting time.

However, I want to warn you about a little pitfall—my wife **accidentally entered the wrong birth date** when filling out the e-VOA 😓, so she had to go through the manual visa-on-arrival channel and ended up waiting in line for **an hour and a half**. So regretful!

Besides the visa, there are two other online procedures that are also important. I recommend taking care of them before boarding to avoid any last-minute hassle upon arrival:

1. **All Indonesia Arrival Card (including e-CD Customs Declaration & SSHP Health Statement)**
2. **Tourist Levy Payment Receipt**

All four steps are **digital documents that can be completed online**, and they're actually quite straightforward—just have your passport copy, flight details, and payment method ready. I recommend saving the QR codes as screenshots in your phone album after completing them, as presenting them for scanning at immigration will speed things up significantly. For a detailed walkthrough, check this out: [2026 Bali Entry Changes in One Minute: Indonesia/Bali Arrival Process - From Landing, Through Customs, to Baggage Claim](/en/blog/bali-all-indonesia-arrival-guide/)

Next up, it's time to kick off our Bali duo adventure~ 🌴

## 🌅 **Day 1: Arriving in Bali & Checking into the Dreamy AYANA Resort**

We took EVA Air's direct 9:50 AM flight, scheduled to land in Bali at 3:30 PM. The flight was a bit long, but a quick nap on the plane had us already switching into vacation mode.

After arriving at Ngurah Rai International Airport, we queued for the manual visa process, so by the time we cleared customs, it was already getting dark. Our pre-booked transfer picked us up, and we finally reached **AYANA Resort and Spa Bali** in Jimbaran around 6 PM.

### 🏨 **AYANA Resort Is Worth Staying for Two Nights!**

This hotel was hands-down the most anticipated stop of our trip! AYANA is one of Bali's most renowned resorts, boasting top-notch facilities, stunning ocean views, and a variety of signature restaurants. The absolute must-visit is the legendary **Rock Bar Bali**—one of the world's top ten sunset bars and a wildly popular Instagram-worthy spot.

https://youtu.be/lbDfKut2J7M

Although we arrived a bit late and missed the sunset, only catching the night view, standing at the cliffside bar area, listening to the crashing waves and watching the twinkling lights, the atmosphere was incredibly romantic 💫. The food itself was decent, though a bit pricey—but honestly, you're not here for the meal; you're here for that magical vibe of dancing with the sea and sky.

![(Johnny Wei提供)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_197ac68f3eeb4671e523304f84bab3d1.jpg)

We stayed in a sea-view room, costing about USD$387 per night. While it was already quite nice, here's a tip: **the AYANA resort is massive**. If you choose a more budget-friendly room, you might end up in a far-flung area, and walking to the main facilities will leave your legs sore. I'd suggest adding a bit to your budget and picking a room closer to the main facilities for a better experience!

The crowd for photos was already gathering early in the morning—you have to queue, and each person gets just one minute for a quick snap. But the good news is—you can queue up again! 📷

### 🍽 **Eat a Big Breakfast, Skip Lunch**

For breakfast, we chose the buffet restaurant near the AYANA lobby. Though the price was a bit steep—about USD$30 per person—the options were incredibly rich, with Indonesian local dishes, Japanese cuisine, European cold cuts, and even made-to-order omelets, bread, and desserts. It was well worth it. After eating our fill, we couldn't even manage lunch, saving us a meal's budget 😂

As a little side note, we left a **$2 tip** while housekeeping was cleaning, and when we returned to the room in the afternoon, the housekeeping lady had quietly prepared a small surprise for us—thoughtful and heartwarming, truly capturing the spirit of AYANA's service. In contrast, last time in Chiang Mai, a friend left a $10 tip and got nothing in return—guess luck is also a kind of skill 😆

## ☕️ **Day 3: Heading to the Highland Café, Checking into a Romantic Jungle Villa in Ubud**

After a hearty breakfast and a leisurely morning at AYANA, we checked out at 11:00 AM, ready to begin the 'mountain chapter' of our trip 🌿.

### 🚗 **A Long Drive, All for a Cup of Coffee and the View**

Our first stop of the day was a scenic café highly recommended by my cousin — **Montana Del Café**, located in the high-altitude Kintamani region of northern Bali. The drive from AYANA in Jimbaran took a whopping **two and a half hours** one way — a true journey over hills and mountains.

![(Johnny Wei提供)](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_5245fa7b61c80c4a4252d2e9876cc3e0.jpg)

(Photo by Johnny Wei)

But I have to say, it was totally worth it once we arrived! Montana Del Café boasts sweeping mountain views, with the majestic Mount Batur and its lake in the distance. Whether you sit indoors or on the terrace, it's like a postcard come to life 📸. We ordered two coffees and two slices of cheesecake, costing around USD$16 in total — quite reasonable for a scenic café. The real treasure, though, was that feeling of sipping coffee above the clouds — absolutely priceless ✨

If you love photography or just want to breathe in the crisp mountain air, I highly recommend adding this to your itinerary. Just keep in mind the long travel time — ask your driver if you can pair it with other Ubud attractions, like volcanic hot springs, to make the trip more efficient.

### 🌿 **Check-in: Abisena Wellness & Resort Ubud**

After our highland adventure, we headed back to central **Ubud** and checked into **Abisena Wellness & Resort**, a hidden gem nestled in the jungle. This stay turned out to be one of the most delightful surprises of our trip, perfectly matching the 'relaxation + romance' vibe we were after!

First off, this is an adults-only resort, so without the noise of children playing, the atmosphere is especially serene. The staff are warm and attentive, from the welcome drink to help with luggage—every detail shows their thoughtfulness.

We chose the **Jungle View Villa**. The room design blends modern and natural elements, with large floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking lush mountain forests. You can even hear birds chirping, insects humming, and a babbling stream—perfect for couples seeking a romantic and peaceful getaway. The villa comes with a private balcony and pool, so even if you don't feel like going out, you can soak in the tub surrounded by greenery—it's pure bliss 💚

## 🏝 **Day 4: Check into The Claremont Luxury Villas in Seminyak for a Convenient and Comfortable Stay**

Leaving the tranquil Ubud highlands behind, we headed to the lively beach area—**Seminyak**. This is Bali's trendy district, packed not only with restaurants and bars but also boutique shops and Instagram-worthy cafes. It's super convenient and perfect for travelers wanting a city-resort vibe.

That night we stayed at **The Claremont Luxury Villas**, right in the heart of Seminyak. The value for money here is incredible—around **USD$148 per night**—yet the space is spacious, fully equipped, and comes with a private pool! Within a few minutes' walk, you'll find convenience stores, international restaurants, and massage places, so you don't even need to call a ride.

## 🎉 **Day 5: Pilgrimage to Finns Beach Club — Worth Being a Splurger for a Day!**

Having visited Bali many times, we finally scheduled our long-awaited trip to **Finns Beach Club**! It's one of Bali's most iconic beach clubs, located in the Canggu area. The venue stretches out to the beach, featuring multiple pools, sun loungers, bars, DJ stages, and VIP cabanas — the vibe is lively and absolutely buzzing.

https://youtu.be/MpetWBEQ2Ts

However, online reviews are really mixed — some people find the **staff attitude cold and the prices high**, while many others think the **atmosphere is great, facilities are awesome, and it's worth a visit**.

Our own experience? **If you just order the cheapest sun lounger package, you really do get overlooked more easily**. But this time, we bit the bullet and upgraded to a VIP sofa cabana package. We spent a good chunk of money over the day, but the treatment we received was completely different! ✨

💡 The key to getting good service really is 'spending your money in the right place'! When you choose a high-end cabana, the security guards, waitstaff, and even dancers proactively come over to greet and interact with you — the vibe instantly feels like you're at a private party.

The group next to us was also in the cabana area, but they probably didn't order much or engage much, and they were almost completely ignored throughout — even the dancers walked right past without a glance 😅 So, **if you want to have a blast at Finns, you've got to be a splurger for a day** (the happy kind) to truly feel the magic here!

## 📝 **Five key takeaways from this Bali trip, hope they help you:**

### ✅ **1. Apply for your e-Visa on Arrival online in advance!**

I can't stress this enough — **the e-VOA online visa is super important!** It saves you a ton of time waiting in line. Especially if you're traveling with kids or on a red-eye flight, getting it beforehand is a true act of self-care.

### 📱 **2. Always use Gojek or Grab to book rides**

These two local ride-hailing apps let you pay by credit card, with transparent pricing and peace of mind. We once made the mistake of hailing a motorbike on the street for convenience, and got totally ripped off — they quoted us in US dollars with a markup, acting like it was perfectly normal 😓. Don't try to save a little and end up losing big.

{{block:包車}}

### 🚦 **3. Traffic jams are a daily reality in Bali — don't overpack your itinerary!**

Especially on the routes from Jimbaran and Seminyak to Ubud, traffic jams are common. I strongly recommend planning just 1–2 key activities per day, otherwise you'll spend your trip constantly 'missing out' and it'll be exhausting.

### 💵 **4. Tipping is fine, but don't let it become emotional blackmail!**

Over 75% of Bali's population works in the service industry, so tipping appropriately is a gesture of courtesy and support. However, you may encounter people who use verbal manipulation or act entitled to tips. In such cases, **you really don't need to force yourself to tip**—maintaining boundaries and self-respect is more important.

Quick reference: [Do you need to tip in Bali? How to tip? How much to tip in various situations in Bali?](/en/blog/bali-tipping-guide-driver-spa/)

### 🐰 **5. Gold Rabbit Coffee Factory? Google it yourself before deciding whether to go!**

If you book a private driver or guided tour, you'll often be 'conveniently taken' to this coffee factory. These places mainly sell overpriced civet coffee, sometimes with high-pressure sales tactics or scripted presentations. Whether it's worth visiting is up to you to decide 🧐
