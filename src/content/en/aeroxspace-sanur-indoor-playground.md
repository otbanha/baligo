## AeroXSpace Overview

**Bottom Line**: Located in South Denpasar, AeroXSpace covers **3,500 square meters** with **26 rides and attractions**, just **19 minutes** from Sanur Beach and about **30 minutes** from Kuta Beach by car. Its highlight is the AeroXCourt, where you can play basketball in simulated lunar gravity—perfect as a rainy-day backup or a family pit stop during your southern Bali itinerary.

[AeroXSpace Indoor Adventure Park](https://aeroxspace.id/) brings fresh entertainment options for families and visitors to Bali. Spanning 3,500 square meters, this adventure hub features 26 different attractions and game activities, ensuring fun for all ages whether it's sunny or rainy.

![AeroXSpace是佔地有1000多坪的室內遊樂場](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_d3a30e0fe3ea2ae0e3f2554f11a9875e.jpg)

AeroXSpace is an indoor playground covering over 1,000 ping (approx. 3,500 sqm).

### Location & Transportation

Located on Jalan Bypass in South Denpasar, AeroXSpace is just a 19-minute drive from Sanur Beach and about 30 minutes from Kuta Beach, making it a convenient stop on your travel itinerary. (Map location)

### Main Attractions

The park features a variety of family-friendly games and adventure activities, with the most popular being the AeroXCourt experience zone, where visitors can play basketball on a court simulating lunar gravity for an unprecedented sporting thrill.

### AeroXCourt Experience

The AeroXCourt is an inflatable multi-sport court with a soft, breathable surface that makes visitors feel like they're on the moon, experiencing low-gravity basketball or soccer. It's one of the most innovative and captivating attractions in the park.

### Space Roller Coaster

The Space Roller Coaster lets visitors soar along a 100-meter track, simulating the sensation of traveling through space. It's another must-try ride suitable for all ages.

### Toddler Zone

Beyond adventure facilities for adults and teens, AeroXSpace features a dedicated Toddler Space for preschoolers, allowing little ones to enjoy playtime safely.

### Learning Integration

AeroXSpace is more than just an entertainment venue—it combines learning with fun, sparking children's curiosity and imagination, and offering families an environment that educates while it entertains.

### Safety and Facilities

The park places a high priority on safety and offers a range of facilities to ensure every visitor's comfort and enjoyment, including professional medical staff, advanced safety equipment, and clean sanitary amenities.

### Future Plans & Development

AeroXSpace Adventure Park plans to add more attractions in the future to draw in more visitors, with the goal of becoming a long-term family travel hotspot in Bali.

### Expert Opinions & Visitor Feedback

Tourino Dilaga, General Manager of AeroXSpace, says the park is not just a place for adventure, but also a platform for learning and personal growth.

### Practical Tips

Visitors are advised to come in the morning or afternoon to avoid the midday heat, and to [book tickets online in advance](https://aeroxspace.instantlybooking.com/product/universal-pass) to guarantee entry.

### 🎉 Saturday Party Time: A 'Mini Nightclub' for Kids

AeroXSpace has a hidden gem that drives kids wild—every Saturday after 5 PM, the park transforms into a party paradise just for children. With a live DJ spinning tunes, energetic dancers, and trapeze artists showering candy and red envelopes from above, the atmosphere is electric.

![週六限定派對時段：小孩也能體驗的「迷你夜店」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2ebc3daabf903126c0b5d744f860c0b5.jpg)

The event runs from 5 PM to 9 PM. Admission includes a party pack with a shirt, socks, glow glasses, LED ring, and glow bracelet, so kids are fully decked out for the fun.

**Ticket prices** are a steal: kids' entry is 350,000 IDR, adult companion ticket is 50,000 IDR, plus 5,000 IDR for insurance—total just 405,000 IDR, roughly USD$13. Incredible value. Admission also comes with a voucher for a free bottle of water and a box of popcorn (sweet, salty, or mix), or 15% off any meal.

**Adults have plenty of flexibility.** Parents can relax on the first or second floor sofa area with free Wi-Fi, or stay and play with their kids for a while before heading out to run errands and returning later to pick them up—staff say this arrangement is perfectly fine.
