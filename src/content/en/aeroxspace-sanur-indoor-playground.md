## About AeroXSpace

**Bottom line**: Located in South Denpasar, AeroXSpace covers **3,500 square meters** with **26 rides and activities**, just **19 minutes** from Sanur Beach and about **30 minutes** from Kuta Beach by car. Its highlight is the AeroXCourt, where you can play basketball in simulated lunar gravity, making it a perfect rainy-day backup or a family-friendly stopover on a southern Bali itinerary.

[AeroXSpace Indoor Adventure Park](https://aeroxspace.id/) brings new entertainment options for families and visitors to Bali. This 3,500-square-meter adventure center features 26 different attractions and games, ensuring fun for all ages regardless of weather.

![AeroXSpace是佔地有1000多坪的室內遊樂場](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_d3a30e0fe3ea2ae0e3f2554f11a9875e.jpg)

AeroXSpace is an indoor playground spanning over 3,500 square meters.

### Location and Transportation

Located on Jalan Bypass in South Denpasar, AeroXSpace is just a 19-minute drive from Sanur Beach and about 30 minutes from Kuta Beach, making it a convenient stopover on any travel itinerary. (Map location)

### Main Attractions

The park's facilities include a variety of family-friendly games and adventure activities, with the most popular being the AeroXCourt experience zone, where visitors can play basketball on a simulated lunar gravity court, enjoying an unprecedented sporting thrill.

### AeroXCourt Experience

AeroXCourt is an inflatable multi-sport court with a soft, air-cushioned surface that makes visitors feel like they're on the moon, experiencing low-gravity basketball or soccer. It's one of the park's most innovative and captivating attractions.

### Space Roller Coaster

The Space Roller Coaster lets visitors soar along a 100-meter track, simulating the sensation of zipping through outer space. This is another must-ride attraction suitable for all ages.

### Toddler Zone

Beyond the adventure rides for adults and teens, AeroXSpace features a dedicated Toddler Space for little ones (preschool age), allowing young children to enjoy the fun safely.

### Learning Through Play

AeroXSpace is more than just an entertainment venue—it blends learning with play, sparking children's curiosity and imagination, and offering families an environment that's both educational and fun.

### Safety & Facilities

The park places a strong emphasis on safety and provides a range of facilities to ensure every visitor's comfort and enjoyment, including professional medical staff, advanced safety equipment, and clean sanitary facilities.

### Tickets & Opening Hours

### Practical Tips

It's recommended to visit in the morning or late afternoon to avoid the midday heat, and to [book tickets online in advance](https://aeroxspace.instantlybooking.com/product/universal-pass) to guarantee entry.

### 🎉 Saturday-Only Party Time: A 'Mini Nightclub' Experience for Kids

AeroXSpace has a hidden gem that drives kids wild—every Saturday after 5 PM, the park transforms into a children's party paradise. With a DJ spinning tunes, energetic dancers, and trapeze artists showering candy and red envelopes from above, the atmosphere is electric.

![週六限定派對時段：小孩也能體驗的「迷你夜店」](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_2ebc3daabf903126c0b5d744f860c0b5.jpg)

The event runs from 5 PM to 9 PM, and your ticket includes a party pack with a shirt, socks, glow glasses, LED rings, and glow bracelets—so kids can fully immerse in the vibe from head to toe.

**Pricing is a steal:** Kids' tickets are 350,000 IDR, adult companion tickets are 50,000 IDR, plus 5,000 IDR for insurance—totaling just 405,000 IDR, roughly USD$13. You also get a voucher for a free bottle of water and a box of popcorn (sweet, salty, or mixed) or 15% off meals.

**Adults have plenty of flexibility too.** Parents can chill on the first or second-floor sofa areas with free Wi-Fi, or play with their kids for a while, then leave to run errands and come back later—staff say it's totally fine.
