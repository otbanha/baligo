**Bottom line**: In Ubud, focus on three experiences—for **Instagram-worthy vibes**, head to **Taman Dedari** (Google 4.6⭐, giant fairy statue + afternoon tea); for **waterfall views**, go to **D'Tukad River Club** (treehouse day club atop Tegenungan Waterfall, 360-degree waterfall views); for **local classics**, seek out famous dirty duck rice and pork rib joints. Below is a full list sorted by ratings and settings.

When you visit Ubud, Bali, besides admiring the terraced rice fields and exploring ancient temples, you absolutely can't miss the food experience! From traditional Indonesian dishes to creative Western restaurants, from cafés hidden among rice paddies to street food in lively markets, Ubud gathers the most exciting culinary culture of Bali. This guide curates must-eat restaurants and local foods in Ubud, ensuring your taste buds also enjoy an unforgettable Bali trip!

## Boni Bali Restaurant (Google Rating 4.3⭐) 👉🏼 Map: [Immerse in Scenic Beauty](/map/ubud/)

![Boni Bali Restaurant (谷歌評價4.3) 地圖：沈浸山水美景](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_5046f26f284c95d89a998876cafa9852.jpg)

Enjoying Indonesian traditional cuisine while overlooking the breathtaking Tegallalang Rice Terraces is one of the great pleasures of traveling to Bali.

Located in northeastern Bali, the Tegallalang Rice Terraces are a UNESCO World Heritage site. Spanning 2,500 hectares, the terraces consist of thousands of stepped rice paddies that cascade layer upon layer to the horizon. Under the sunlight, they shimmer in a vibrant palette of gold, green, and brown—a truly stunning sight.

Boni Bali Restaurant sits on the edge of the Tegallalang Rice Terraces, serving Indonesian traditional dishes. Guests can savor classics like Nasi Goreng, Sate Ayam, and Rendang here.
