# Bali News｜September 7, 2026 Published Tomorrow Morning

## ✈️ Anak Krakatau Volcano Erupts! 17 Flights Affected at Bali Airport

Today's biggest travel news in Indonesia is the eruption of **Anak Krakatau (Child of Krakatau) Volcano**.

As volcanic ash spread, multiple airports across Indonesia were affected, including Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta International Airport; Bali's **I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport** also saw disruptions today.

According to NusaBali, **a total of 17 flights at Bali's airport were affected by the Anak Krakatau eruption**.

https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dc7ogbhS2P6/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&stkn=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==

More notably, international flights have also been impacted in a chain reaction. Reuters reports that volcanic ash has caused several Indonesian airports to suspend flights, affecting at least 301 flights, including 58 international ones.

Currently, **there is no evidence that Bali itself faces a direct threat from the volcanic eruption**.

What you really need to pay attention to is:

**If your flight is between Bali and Jakarta/other Indonesian cities, or requires a transit through Jakarta, be sure to reconfirm your flight status before departure tomorrow.**

Especially since airlines may experience delays or cancellations due to the impact on previous flights.

## 🌋 Mount Ijen Remains Closed! Suspended Until Further Notice After Forest Fire

Mount Ijen (Kawah Ijen) is still closed.

The East Java Natural Resources Conservation Agency (BBKSDA Jawa Timur) has suspended all tourism activities at **Ijen Crater Nature Tourism Park** due to forest and land fires in the Ijen area.

And it's not just a matter of 'certain hours being off-limits'—it's:

**Suspended until further notice.**

Mount Ijen is famous for its blue fire and turquoise volcanic lake, and it's a popular cross-island destination for many Taiwanese travelers heading from Bali to Banyuwangi.

So if you have plans coming up:

**Bali → Gilimanuk → Ketapang → Banyuwangi → Ijen Crater**

You must cancel or change your Ijen itinerary for now.

## 🏔️ Mount Agong Hiking Accident! Teenager Rescued After Falling into Ravine

Bali's highest peak, **Mount Agung (Gunung Agung)**, saw a hiking accident today.

A young hiker slipped and fell into a ravine while climbing Gunung Agung, prompting a rescue operation by the search and rescue team.

Basarnas Karangasem dispatched rescue personnel to the scene today and successfully evacuated the injured hiker.

This incident serves as a reminder for travelers planning to hike:

**Gunung Agung is not a typical tourist attraction.**

The mountainous terrain, weather conditions, and certain sections of the trail are quite challenging. If you have no hiking experience, it's not recommended to attempt it on your own.

Especially since Bali is still in the dry season, mountain weather and ground conditions can change quickly.

If you want to climb Agung, it's best to arrange it through a licensed local guide who knows the route well.

## 🚧 Kamasan Tourism Village Road Temporarily Closed! Be Aware of Construction When Heading to Klungkung

If you're planning to visit **Klungkung** soon, here's a traffic update worth noting.

A section of road in the **Kamasan Tourism Village** has been temporarily closed due to drainage culvert (gorong-gorong) construction.

Kamasan itself is an important traditional art village in Bali, famous for its Kamasan paintings (Lukisan Kamasan).

If your itinerary includes:

**Klungkung → Kamasan → Semarapura**

It's advisable to check road conditions in advance and allow extra time for detours.

This kind of roadwork may not affect most independent travelers, but it's especially worth noting for those **hiring a private driver, self-driving, or planning a day trip covering multiple Klungkung attractions**.
