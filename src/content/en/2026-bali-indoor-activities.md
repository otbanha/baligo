**Bottom Line**: When it rains or you need to escape the sun, the top pick in the Kuta area is **Trans Studio Bali** (a large indoor theme park on the top floor of a major shopping mall, with 7 themed zones and 23 rides—enough to keep you busy all day). In other areas, options include the newly opened Icon Bali mall, five-star hotel afternoon teas, and hands-on workshops in Ubud. This article organizes 60 indoor activities by region: Ubud, Canggu, Sanur, and Nusa Dua.

On a vacation in **Bali**, nothing is more frustrating than an unexpected afternoon thunderstorm or the scorching midday sun that’s hard to bear. But don’t let the weather ruin your holiday spirit! Beyond the stunning ocean views, this island hides countless high-quality indoor spaces. Whether you want to shop leisurely in air-conditioned comfort, let the kids burn off energy at a five-star indoor playground, or enjoy a serene silver jewelry-making workshop amidst the misty Ubud jungle, these 60 carefully selected indoor backup plans will reveal a refined and cozy side of **Bali**.

Before heading out, check the [Bali real-time weather](/en/weather/) to see if you really need a backup plan or if it’s just a brief afternoon shower.

### Upside Down World Bali

![在峇里島顛倒屋拍出令人驚異的照片](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_80ed988eca4472540b1a905c96e4468a.jpg)

Capture mind-bending photos at Bali's Upside Down World

### Ice Skating Rink

![冰刀溜冰場](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_ed02bd7b74729a4efdb65c31a67a5cbf.png)

Looking for a unique way to cool off in Bali's hot weather or on a rainy day? Head to the west wing, 3rd floor of MAL BALI GALERIA in Kuta, to **Bali Ice Skating Arena** and enjoy the fun of skating! The ice rink here makes you feel like a pro, showcasing your gliding skills as you drift freely across the ice.

Each ice skating session is limited to 45 skaters, and each ticket grants 1.5 hours of skating fun. Whether you're with friends or family, you can enjoy an exhilarating time gliding across the ice! Bali Ice Skating Arena is a perfect spot to cool off and have fun on rainy or scorching hot days.

## Canggu

On a rainy day in Canggu, here's how to pass the time:

### Bounce Bali

![Bounce Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0f7c35106c3dbcfbe5b4b4f11acfd0a6.jpg)

If you encounter a rainy day in Bali, why not drop by Bounce Bali? As Bali's first trampoline park, located inside the Canggu Club, this air-conditioned facility is designed purely for the joy of bouncing! Unlike home trampolines, Bounce features specially designed spring frames that let you jump higher. With 360-degree trampoline walls surrounding you, there's no other place like this in Bali. All frames and springs are covered with two-inch-thick safety padding. If you love to bounce, this is your paradise.

At Bounce Bali, you can freely jump and practice all kinds of tricks, or join organized activities like dodgeball or basketball on trampolines. The Strike+ Bar offers a full view of the entire bounce center and serves delicious meals that satisfy both kids and adults—whether you're hungry from bouncing or just here to soak up the vibrant atmosphere. They also offer customized party packages for any type of celebration. Come to Bounce Bali for an hour or two and enjoy endless fun!

## Ubud

If you're near Ubud when it rains, here are some activities to consider:

### Join a Workshop

![參加手作坊體驗](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_d6581c6040ade12c3dcdb42b1a7bc04c.jpg)

Ubud is not only a treasure trove of natural beauty but also a cradle of art and craftsmanship.
