**Bottom Line**: When it rains or you need to dodge the sun, the top pick in the Kuta area is **Trans Studio Bali** (a large indoor theme park on the top floor of a shopping mall, with 7 themed zones and 23 rides—enough to keep you busy all day); other areas feature the newly opened Icon Bali Mall, five-star hotel afternoon teas, and Ubud craft workshops. This article organizes 60 indoor itineraries by region: Ubud, Canggu, Sanur, and Nusa Dua.

On a vacation in **Bali**, nothing is more frustrating than a sudden afternoon thunderstorm or the scorching midday sun. But don't let the weather ruin your holiday spirit! Beyond the stunning ocean views, this island hides countless stylish indoor spaces. Whether you want to leisurely shop in air-conditioned comfort, let the kids burn off energy in a five-star indoor playground, or enjoy a serene silver jewelry-making class in the misty Ubud highlands, these 60 carefully selected indoor backups will reveal a refined and cozy side of **Bali**.

Before heading out, check the [Bali live weather](/en/weather/) to see if you really need a backup plan or if it's just a brief afternoon shower.

If you're in the Kuta area and encounter rain or strong sun, consider these backups:

Trans Studio Bali is a popular attraction near Seminyak in Kuta. It's suitable for all ages, making it a great spot for families and friends to hang out.

### Upside Down World Bali

![在峇里島顛倒屋拍出令人驚異的照片](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_80ed988eca4472540b1a905c96e4468a.jpg)

Snap mind-bending photos at Bali's Upside Down World

Upside Down World Bali is a quirky attraction on the island. It features seven specially designed rooms where the decor and furniture are all flipped upside down. Spend a delightful day here with family and friends, capturing loads of fun photos. Once you flip the images, you'll see that despite feeling awkward as staff guide your poses, the results make it look like you're doing the impossible!

### Ice Skating Rink

![冰刀溜冰場](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_ed02bd7b74729a4efdb65c31a67a5cbf.png)

Looking for a unique way to cool off in Bali's heat or on a rainy day? Head to the **Bali Ice Skating Arena** on the 3rd floor of the West Wing at MAL BALI GALERIA in Kuta. This ice rink will make you feel like a pro skater, showing off your gliding skills as you drift freely across the ice.

Each session is limited to 45 skaters, and every ticket grants a 1.5-hour ice skating experience. Whether you're with friends or family, you can enjoy an exhilarating time gliding across the ice! Bali Ice Skating Arena is a perfect spot to cool off and have fun on rainy or scorching hot days.

## Canggu

On a rainy day in Canggu, here's how to pass the time:

### Bounce Bali

![Bounce Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0f7c35106c3dbcfbe5b4b4f11acfd0a6.jpg)

If you encounter a rainy day in Bali, why not head to Bounce Bali? It's the first trampoline park on the island, located within the Canggu Club. This air-conditioned facility is uniquely designed for the pure joy of bouncing! Unlike home trampolines, Bounce features specially engineered spring frames that let you jump higher. Surrounded by 360 degrees of trampolines, there's nothing else like it in Bali. All frames and springs are covered with two-inch-thick safety padding—if you love to bounce, this is your paradise.

At Bounce Bali, you can free-jump and practice tricks, or join organized activities like dodgeball or basketball. The Strike+ Bar offers a full panoramic view of the trampoline center and serves delicious meals that satisfy both kids and adults—whether you're hungry after bouncing or just soaking up the vibrant atmosphere. They also offer customized party packages for any type of celebration. Come spend an hour or two at Bounce Bali for endless fun!

### Sports & Fitness

![運動健身](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_321421d5b6c376f44537b4ea57e6f539.jpg)

If you're in Canggu and don't know where to go on a rainy day, this area is a fitness lover's paradise with 20 to 30 gyms. You can use the rainy time to build a strong physique and stay active. Notable gyms include **CrossFit Wanderlust, Body Factory Bali**, and **Avenue Fitness**. Whether you're into strength training or cardio, you'll find something that suits you, making rainy days fulfilling and healthy!

### Rock Climbing Fun

![攀岩樂趣](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_38f97362bae8b91ae23e97debe1fb670.jpg)

Located in Canggu, **Bali Climbing** is a semi-outdoor climbing gym just steps from the beach. Here, you can not only get fit but also have fun and make new friends. We offer a variety of climbing routes, from beginner to expert, so everyone finds a challenge. **Bali Climbing** also provides climbing courses for kids and adults, retreats, climbing trips, and event spaces. Their goal is to make everyone feel part of this passionate community—even on a rainy day, you can spend an energetic day here.

### Bowling Fun

![玩保齡球](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_92693933716c5496982c2f770ef67351.jpg)

Head to **Strike Bowling Bali** for a laughter-filled bowling session on a rainy day! Whether it's a family outing or a gathering with friends and colleagues, bowling is a fun way to bond. Strike offers Bali's most modern ten-pin bowling experience with six automatic lanes and a computerized scoring system. To help little ones (or anyone else!), they have guardrails to prevent balls from rolling into the gutters.

Strike also features an air-conditioned restaurant and bar area serving food and drinks, so you can satisfy your hunger and thirst before, during, or after your game.

Whether you're 3 or 80, **Strike Bowling Bali** is a fantastic choice for having fun while staying active. Let the good times roll!
