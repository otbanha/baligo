**Bottom Line**: When it rains or you need to escape the sun, the top pick in the Kuta area is **Trans Studio Bali** (a large indoor theme park on the top floor of a major shopping mall, with 7 themed zones and 23 rides—enough to keep you busy all day). Other options include the newly opened Icon Bali mall, five-star hotel afternoon teas, and hands-on workshops in Ubud. This article organizes 60 indoor activities by area: Ubud, Canggu, Sanur, and Nusa Dua.

On a vacation in **Bali**, nothing is more frustrating than a sudden afternoon thunderstorm or the intense midday sun that's hard to bear. But don't let the weather ruin your holiday spirit! Beyond its stunning beaches, this island hides countless sophisticated indoor spaces. Whether you want to leisurely shop in air-conditioned comfort, let your kids burn off energy at a five-star indoor playground, or enjoy a serene silver jewelry-making workshop amidst the misty Ubud jungle, these 60 carefully selected indoor alternatives will reveal a refined and cozy side of **Bali**.

If you're in the Kuta area and encounter rain or strong sun, consider these alternatives:

Trans Studio Bali is a popular attraction near Seminyak in Kuta. It's suitable for all ages, making it a great destination for families and groups of friends.

If you're traveling in Bali and it rains, throwing your original plans off course, don't worry! Trans Studio Bali indoor theme park will provide you with a day full of fun and excitement. ### Upside Down World Bali

![在峇里島顛倒屋拍出令人驚異的照片](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_80ed988eca4472540b1a905c96e4468a.jpg)

Capture mind-bending photos at Bali's Upside Down World

Upside Down World Bali is a fun attraction on the island. It features seven specially designed rooms where the decor and furniture are all flipped upside down. You can spend a delightful day here with family and friends, snapping loads of amusing photos. ### Ice Skating Rink

![冰刀溜冰場](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_ed02bd7b74729a4efdb65c31a67a5cbf.png)

Looking for a unique way to cool off in Bali's heat or on a rainy day? Head to MAL BALI GALERIA in Kuta, to the **Bali Ice Skating Arena** on the 3rd floor of the West Wing, and enjoy the fun of ice skating! The rink here makes you feel like a skating pro, letting you show off your gliding skills and drift freely across the ice.

Each session is limited to 45 skaters, and every ticket gives you 1.5 hours of skating time. Whether with friends or family, you can have an exhilarating time gliding on the icy surface! Bali Ice Skating Arena is a perfect spot for cooling off and having fun on rainy or scorching hot days.

## Canggu

On a rainy day in Canggu, here's how to pass the time:

### Bounce Bali

![Bounce Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0f7c35106c3dbcfbe5b4b4f11acfd0a6.jpg)

If you encounter a rainy day in Bali, head to Bounce Bali! It's the first trampoline park on the island, located inside the Canggu Club. This air-conditioned facility is designed purely for the joy of 'bouncing'! Unlike home trampolines, Bounce features specially engineered spring frames that let you jump higher! Surrounded by 360 degrees of trampolines, there's nothing else like it in Bali. All frames and springs are covered with two-inch-thick safety padding—if you love to bounce, this is your paradise.

At Bounce Bali, you can free-jump and practice tricks, or join organized games like dodgeball or basketball on trampolines. The Strike+ Bar offers a full view of the entire bounce center and serves delicious food to satisfy both kids and adults, whether you're hungry after jumping or just soaking up the vibrant atmosphere. They also offer customized party packages for any type of celebration. Come to Bounce Bali for an hour or two of nonstop fun!

### Fitness and Workouts

![運動健身](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_321421d5b6c376f44537b4ea57e6f539.jpg)

If you're stuck on a rainy day in Canggu and don't know where to go, this area is a fitness lover's paradise with 20 to 30 gyms. You can use the rainy time to build a strong physique and stay active. Notable gyms include **CrossFit Wanderlust, Body Factory Bali**, and **Avenue Fitness**—whether you're into strength training or cardio, you'll find something to keep your rainy day fulfilling and healthy!

### Rock Climbing Fun

![攀岩樂趣](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_38f97362bae8b91ae23e97debe1fb670.jpg)

Located in Canggu, **Bali Climbing** is a semi-open-air rock climbing gym just steps from the beach. Here, you can not only get fit but also have fun and make new friends. We offer a variety of climbing routes suitable for everyone from beginners to pros. **Bali Climbing** also provides climbing classes for kids and adults, retreats, climbing trips, and a venue for events. Their goal is to make everyone feel part of this passionate family! Even on rainy days, you can spend an energetic day here.

### Go Bowling

![玩保齡球](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_92693933716c5496982c2f770ef67351.jpg)

Head to **Strike Bowling Bali** for a fun-filled bowling session even on rainy days! Whether it's a family outing or a gathering with friends and colleagues, bowling is a great way to have fun and bond. Strike offers Bali's most modern ten-pin bowling experience with six automatic lanes and a computerized scoring system. To help little ones (or anyone else!), they also have bumpers to prevent balls from rolling into the gutters.

Strike features an air-conditioned restaurant and bar area serving delicious food and drinks, so you can satisfy your hunger and thirst before, during, or after your game.

Whether you're 3 or 80 years old, **Strike Bowling Bali** is a fantastic choice for having fun while staying active. Let the good times roll!

### Ubud Cooking Classes

![烏布美食烹飪班](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a06a0bd308129f244507a2130d8ea06e.jpg)

Ubud's cooking classes offer a fantastic opportunity to dive deep into the local food culture. The experience typically includes a market visit, learning cooking techniques, hands-on cooking, and tasting the results. Participants will join an instructor at the market to pick fresh ingredients and learn unique Balinese cooking methods.
