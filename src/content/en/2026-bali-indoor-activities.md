**Bottom Line First**: When it rains or you need to escape the sun, the top pick in the Kuta area is **Trans Studio Bali** (a large indoor theme park on the top floor of a major shopping mall, with 7 themed zones and 23 rides—enough to keep you busy all day); other areas offer alternatives like the newly opened Icon Bali mall, five-star hotel afternoon teas, and hands-on workshops in Ubud. This article organizes 60 indoor activities by region: Ubud, Canggu, Sanur, and Nusa Dua.

On a vacation in **Bali**, nothing is more dreaded than a sudden afternoon thunderstorm or the scorching midday sun that's hard to bear. But don't let the weather ruin your holiday spirit! Beyond its stunning ocean views, this island hides countless high-quality indoor spaces. Whether you want to shop leisurely in air-conditioned comfort, let the kids burn off energy at a five-star indoor playground, or enjoy a serene silver jewelry-making workshop amidst the misty Ubud jungle, these 60 carefully selected indoor alternatives will introduce you to another refined and comfortable side of **Bali**.

Before heading out, check [Bali's real-time weather](/en/weather/) to see if you really need a backup plan or if it's just a brief afternoon shower.

## Kuta Area

If you're in the Kuta area, consider these alternatives for rainy or sunny days:

### Beachwalk Shopping Center｜Shopping, Movies, and Dining All in One Across from the Beach

Located across from Kuta Beach, Beachwalk can easily fill half a day. On the second floor, there's Cinema XXI for catching a movie on a rainy day. Shopping options include Zara, Uniqlo, H&M, Sephora, The Body Shop, as well as local Balinese brands like Bamboo Blonde and Bali Tees. There's a wide range of dining choices: Indonesian cuisine at Bebek Tepi Sawah and Mr. Wayan by The Sea, Japanese options at Sushi Tei, Genki Sushi, Gyu-Kaku, and Kimukatsu. On the basement level B1, you'll find Cocomart supermarket, and Candylicious candy store is great for kids.

- Address: Jl. Pantai Kuta No. 60, Kuta
- Opening Hours: Monday to Thursday 10:00–22:00, Friday to Sunday 10:00–23:00
- Suggested Stay: Allow about 2–3 hours for shopping and dining; add a movie or a trip to the beach for a half-day outing
- Tip: The building features an open, semi-outdoor design with greenery, water features, and natural light. During heavy afternoon rain or intense sun, it's best to stick to the cinema and indoor shops. In the evening, you can walk directly to the beach to watch the sunset.

### Upside Down World Bali

![在峇里島顛倒屋拍出令人驚異的照片](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_80ed988eca4472540b1a905c96e4468a.jpg)

Snap mind-bending photos at Bali's Upside Down World

Upside Down World Bali is a quirky attraction on the island. It features seven specially designed rooms where the decor and furniture are all flipped upside down. Spend a fun-filled day here with family and friends, snapping loads of playful photos. Once you flip the images, you'll see yourself—awkwardly guided by staff into poses—doing the impossible!

### Ice Skating Rink

![冰刀溜冰場](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_ed02bd7b74729a4efdb65c31a67a5cbf.png)

Looking for a unique way to beat the heat or escape the rain in Bali? Head to **Bali Ice Skating Arena** on the 3rd floor of the West Wing at MAL BALI GALERIA in Kuta. Glide across the ice and feel like a pro, showing off your moves and drifting freely on the frozen surface.

Each session is limited to 45 skaters, and every ticket gives you 1.5 hours of ice time. Whether you're with friends or family, you'll have an exhilarating time on the cold ice. Bali Ice Skating Arena is the perfect spot for cooling off and having fun on rainy or scorching days.

## Canggu

On a rainy day in Canggu, here's how to pass the time:

### Bounce Bali

![Bounce Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0f7c35106c3dbcfbe5b4b4f11acfd0a6.jpg)

If you encounter a rainy day in Bali, head over to Bounce Bali! As Bali's first trampoline park, located inside the Canggu Club, this air-conditioned facility is uniquely designed for the pure joy of bouncing! Unlike home trampolines, Bounce features specially engineered spring frames that let you jump higher. Surrounded by 360 degrees of trampolines, there's truly no other place like this in Bali. All frames and springs are covered with two-inch thick safety padding—if you love to bounce, this is your paradise.

At Bounce Bali, you can free-jump and practice all kinds of tricks, or join organized activities like dodgeball or basketball on trampolines. The Strike+ Bar offers a full panoramic view of the entire trampoline center and serves delicious meals that satisfy both kids and adults—whether you're hungry from bouncing or just soaking up the vibrant atmosphere. They also offer customized party packages, perfect for any type of celebration! Come to Bounce Bali for an hour or two and enjoy endless fun!

### Puppy Yoga

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-06/1781160568678-index1.jpg)

On rainy days or when the sun is too strong, Puppy Yoga is a great relaxing choice. It's a play-focused, yoga-enhanced experience lasting about 60 minutes, with no yoga experience required. Classes are held in Uluwatu, Canggu, and Ubud. Yoga mats and puppies are provided by the organizer, and you can take photos and videos on-site. Most of the puppies involved are rescued strays, and part of the class fee goes toward their vaccines, food, and medical care.

Wear comfortable, stain-resistant sportswear. Those allergic to dog hair should proceed with caution, and be sure to check the age restrictions for each session before booking.
