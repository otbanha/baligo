**Bottom Line**: When it rains or you need to dodge the sun, the top pick in Kuta is **Trans Studio Bali** (a massive indoor theme park on the top floor of a shopping mall, with 7 themed zones and 23 rides—enough to keep you busy all day). In other areas, there are new options like the recently opened Icon Bali Mall, five-star hotel afternoon teas, and hands-on workshops in Ubud. This article organizes 60 indoor activities by region: Ubud, Canggu, Sanur, and Nusa Dua.

On a **Bali** vacation, nothing is more dreaded than a sudden afternoon downpour or the relentless midday sun. But don’t let the weather ruin your holiday spirit! Beyond its stunning beaches, this island hides countless stylish indoor spaces. Whether you want to shop leisurely in air-conditioned comfort, let the kids burn off energy in a five-star indoor playground, or enjoy a serene silver jewelry-making class amidst the misty Ubud jungle, these 60 carefully curated indoor backup plans will reveal a refined and cozy side of **Bali**.

### Upside Down World Bali

![在峇里島顛倒屋拍出令人驚異的照片](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_80ed988eca4472540b1a905c96e4468a.jpg)

Capture mind-bending photos at Bali's Upside Down World

Upside Down World Bali is a quirky attraction on the island. It features seven specially designed rooms where the decor and furniture are all flipped upside down. You can spend a fun day here with family and friends, snapping loads of amusing photos. After flipping the images, you'll see that despite feeling awkward while staff guided your poses, the photos make it look like you're doing the impossible!

### Ice Skating Rink

![冰刀溜冰場](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_ed02bd7b74729a4efdb65c31a67a5cbf.png)

Looking for a unique way to cool off in Bali's heat or on a rainy day? Head to MAL BALI GALERIA in Kuta, on the 3rd floor of the West Wing, to **Bali Ice Skating Arena** and enjoy some ice skating fun! The rink here makes you feel like a pro, showing off your gliding skills as you drift freely across the ice.

Each session is limited to 45 skaters, and each ticket gives you 1.5 hours of skating time. Whether with friends or family, you can share an exhilarating time on the icy surface! Bali Ice Skating Arena is a perfect spot for cooling down and having fun on rainy or scorching days.

## Canggu

On a rainy day in Canggu, here's how to pass the time:

### Bounce Bali

![Bounce Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0f7c35106c3dbcfbe5b4b4f11acfd0a6.jpg)

If you encounter a rainy day in Bali, head over to Bounce Bali! As Bali's first trampoline park, located inside the Canggu Club, this air-conditioned facility is designed purely for the joy of bouncing. Unlike home trampolines, Bounce features specially engineered spring frames that let you jump higher. Surrounded by 360 degrees of trampolines, there's no other place like this in Bali. All frames and springs are covered with two-inch-thick safety padding, making it a paradise for anyone who loves to bounce.

At Bounce Bali, you can freely jump and practice all kinds of tricks, or join organized activities like trampoline dodgeball or basketball. The Strike+ Bar offers a full panoramic view of the entire bounce center and serves delicious meals that satisfy both kids and adults—whether you're hungry after bouncing or just here to soak up the vibrant atmosphere. They also offer customized party packages for any type of celebration. Come to Bounce Bali for an hour or two and enjoy endless fun!

### Sports & Fitness

![運動健身](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_321421d5b6c376f44537b4ea57e6f539.jpg)

If you're stuck in Canggu on a rainy day and don't know where to go, this area is a paradise for fitness enthusiasts, with 20 to 30 gyms. You can make use of the rainy time to build a strong physique and stay active. Notable gyms include **CrossFit Wanderlust, Body Factory Bali**, and **Avenue Fitness**. Whether you're into strength training or cardio, you'll find something that suits you, making rainy days both fulfilling and healthy.

### Rock Climbing Fun

![攀岩樂趣](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_38f97362bae8b91ae23e97debe1fb670.jpg)

Located in Canggu, **Bali Climbing** is a semi-outdoor climbing gym just a few steps from the beach. Here, you can not only get fit but also have fun and make new friends. We offer a variety of climbing routes suitable for both beginners and professional climbers. **Bali Climbing** also provides climbing courses, retreats, climbing trips for kids and adults, and a venue for events. Their goal is to make everyone feel part of this passionate community! Even on a rainy day, you can spend an energetic day here.

### Bowling Fun

![玩保齡球](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_92693933716c5496982c2f770ef67351.jpg)

Head to **Strike Bowling Bali** for a laughter-filled bowling session on a rainy day! Whether it's a family outing or a gathering with friends and colleagues, bowling is a fun way to bond and have a great time. Strike offers Bali's most modern ten-pin bowling experience with six automatic lanes and a computerized scoring system. To accommodate kids (or anyone else!), they also have bumpers to prevent balls from rolling into the gutters.

Strike features an air-conditioned restaurant and bar area serving delicious food and drinks, so you can satisfy your hunger and thirst before, during, or after the game.

Whether you're 3 or 80 years old, **Strike Bowling Bali** is a fantastic choice for staying active while having fun. Let the good times roll!
