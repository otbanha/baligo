**Bottom Line**: When it rains or you need to dodge the sun, the top pick in the Kuta area is **Trans Studio Bali** (a massive indoor theme park on the top floor of a shopping mall, with 7 themed zones and 23 rides—enough to keep you busy all day). In other areas, options include the newly opened Icon Bali Mall, five-star hotel afternoon teas, and hands-on workshops in Ubud. This article organizes 60 indoor activities by region: Ubud, Canggu, Sanur, and Nusa Dua.

On a **Bali** vacation, nothing dampens the mood quite like an unexpected afternoon thunderstorm or the relentless midday sun. But don’t let the weather ruin your holiday spirit! Beyond its stunning beaches, this island hides countless stylish indoor spaces. Whether you want to shop in air-conditioned comfort, let the kids burn off energy at a five-star indoor playground, or enjoy a serene silver jewelry-making class in the misty Ubud jungle, these 60 carefully selected indoor backup plans will reveal a refined and cozy side of **Bali**.

Before heading out, check the [Bali live weather](/en/weather/) to see if you really need a backup plan or if it’s just a brief afternoon shower.

## Kuta Area

If you’re in the Kuta area and encounter rain or strong sun, consider these backup plans:

### Trans Studio Bali

Trans Studio Bali is a popular attraction near Seminyak in Kuta. It’s suitable for all ages, making it a great spot for families and groups of friends.

https://youtube.com/shorts/POSTtRPKK5c

Trans Studio Bali is itself a large shopping mall, and the massive indoor amusement park on the top floor features seven themed zones with 23 rides and attractions—you could spend a whole day and still not get through them all! If you encounter rain during your Bali trip and your original plans fall through, don't worry! Trans Studio Bali Indoor Theme Park will provide you with a day full of fun and excitement. ### Upside Down World Bali

![在峇里島顛倒屋拍出令人驚異的照片](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_80ed988eca4472540b1a905c96e4468a.jpg)

Snap mind-bending photos at Bali's Upside Down World

Upside Down World Bali is a fun attraction on the island. It features seven specially designed rooms where the decor and furniture are all flipped upside down. You can spend a delightful day here with family and friends, taking loads of amusing photos. After flipping the images, you'll find that despite feeling awkward while being guided into poses by the staff, the final shots make it look like you're doing the impossible!

### Ice Skating Rink

![冰刀溜冰場](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_ed02bd7b74729a4efdb65c31a67a5cbf.png)

Looking for a unique way to cool off in Bali's hot weather or on a rainy day? Head to MAL BALI GALERIA in Kuta, on the 3rd floor of the West Wing, to **Bali Ice Skating Arena** and enjoy the fun of ice skating! The rink here makes you feel like a pro, showcasing your gliding skills as you freely drift across the ice.

Each ice session is limited to 45 skaters, and every ticket grants 1.5 hours of skating fun. Whether you're with friends or family, you can enjoy an exhilarating time gliding across the ice! Bali Ice Skating Arena is a perfect spot to cool off and have fun on rainy or scorching hot days.

## Canggu

On a rainy day in Canggu, here's how to pass the time:

### Bounce Bali

![Bounce Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0f7c35106c3dbcfbe5b4b4f11acfd0a6.jpg)

If you encounter a rainy day in Bali, why not head to Bounce Bali? This is Bali's first trampoline park, located within the Canggu Club. Featuring air-conditioned, unique facilities designed purely for the joy of 'bouncing'! Unlike home trampolines, Bounce has specially designed spring frames that let you jump higher! Surrounded by 360 degrees of trampolines, there's no other place like this in Bali. All frames and springs are covered with two-inch-thick safety pads. If you love bouncing, this is your paradise.

At Bounce Bali, you can freely jump and practice various tricks, or join organized activities like trampoline dodgeball or basketball. The Strike+ Bar offers a full view overlooking the entire bounce center and serves delicious meals that satisfy both kids and adults, whether you're hungry after bouncing or just stopping by to soak in the vibrant atmosphere. They also offer customized party packages, so any type of celebration can be hosted here! Come to Bounce Bali for an hour or two and enjoy endless fun!

### Sports & Fitness

![運動健身](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_321421d5b6c376f44537b4ea57e6f539.jpg)

If you're in Canggu and don't know where to go on a rainy day, this area is a haven for fitness enthusiasts, with 20 to 30 gyms. You can use the rainy time to build a strong physique and keep yourself energized. Notable gyms include **CrossFit Wanderlust, Body Factory Bali**, and **Avenue Fitness**. Whether you're into strength training or cardio, you'll find a suitable workout to make your rainy day fulfilling and healthy!

### Rock Climbing Fun

![攀岩樂趣](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_38f97362bae8b91ae23e97debe1fb670.jpg)

Located in Canggu, **Bali Climbing** is a semi-open-air climbing gym just a few steps from the beach. Here, you can not only get fit but also have fun and make new friends. We offer a variety of climbing routes, with challenges suitable for both beginners and professional climbers. **Bali Climbing** also provides climbing courses for kids and adults, retreats, climbing trips, and a venue for events. Their goal is to make everyone feel part of this passionate community! Even on a rainy day, you can spend an energetic day here.

### Bowling Fun

![玩保齡球](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_92693933716c5496982c2f770ef67351.jpg)

Head to **Strike Bowling Bali** to enjoy a laughter-filled bowling session even on a rainy day! Whether it's a family outing or a gathering with friends and colleagues, bowling is a fun way to bond. Strike offers Bali's most modern ten-pin bowling experience, with 6 automatic lanes and a computerized scoring system. To accommodate kids (or anyone else!), they also have bumpers to prevent balls from rolling into the gutters.

Strike features an air-conditioned restaurant and bar area serving food and drinks, so you can satisfy your hunger and thirst before, during, or after your game.

Whether you're 3 or 80, **Strike Bowling Bali** is a fantastic way to have fun while staying active. Let the good times roll!
