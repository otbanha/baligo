**Bottom Line**: When it rains or you need to dodge the sun, the top pick in the Kuta area is **Trans Studio Bali** (a massive indoor theme park on the top floor of a shopping mall, with 7 themed zones and 23 rides—enough to keep you busy all day); in other areas, there are options like the newly opened Icon Bali mall, five-star hotel afternoon teas, and Ubud craft workshops. This article organizes 60 indoor activities by region: Ubud, Canggu, Sanur, and Nusa Dua.

On a **Bali** vacation, nothing is more frustrating than sudden afternoon thunderstorms or the relentless midday sun. But don’t let the weather ruin your holiday spirit! Beyond its stunning beaches, this island hides countless stylish indoor spaces. Whether you want to shop leisurely in air-conditioned comfort, let the kids burn off energy at a five-star indoor playground, or enjoy a serene silver jewelry-making class amidst the misty Ubud jungle, these 60 carefully selected indoor backup plans will reveal a refined and cozy side of **Bali**.

Before heading out, check the [Bali live weather](/en/weather/) to see if you really need a backup plan or if it’s just a brief afternoon shower.

If you’re in the Kuta area and encounter rain or strong sun, consider these backup plans:

Trans Studio Bali is a popular attraction near Seminyak in Kuta. It’s suitable for all ages, making it a great destination for families and groups of friends.

If you encounter rain during your Bali trip and your original plans fall through, don't worry! Trans Studio Bali Indoor Theme Park will provide you with a day full of fun and excitement. ### Upside Down World Bali

![在峇里島顛倒屋拍出令人驚異的照片](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_80ed988eca4472540b1a905c96e4468a.jpg)

Capture mind-bending photos at Bali's Upside Down World

### Ice Skating Rink

![冰刀溜冰場](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_ed02bd7b74729a4efdb65c31a67a5cbf.png)

Looking for a unique way to cool off in Bali's hot weather or on a rainy day? Head to MAL BALI GALERIA in Kuta, on the 3rd floor of the West Wing, to **Bali Ice Skating Arena** and enjoy the fun of ice skating! The rink here makes you feel like a pro, showcasing your gliding skills as you freely drift across the ice.

Each session is limited to 45 skaters, and every ticket grants 1.5 hours of ice skating fun. Whether you're with friends or family, you can enjoy an exhilarating time gliding across the ice! Bali Ice Skating Arena is a perfect spot to cool off and have fun on rainy or hot days.

## Canggu

On a rainy day in Canggu, here's how to pass the time:

### Bounce Bali

![Bounce Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0f7c35106c3dbcfbe5b4b4f11acfd0a6.jpg)

If you encounter a rainy day in Bali, head over to Bounce Bali! As Bali's first trampoline park, located within the Canggu Club, this air-conditioned facility is designed purely for the joy of bouncing! Unlike home trampolines, Bounce features specially designed spring frames that let you jump higher! Surrounded by 360 degrees of trampolines, there's no other place like this in Bali. All frames and springs are covered with two-inch-thick safety padding—if you love to bounce, this is your paradise.

At Bounce Bali, you can freely bounce and practice various tricks, or join organized activities like trampoline dodgeball or basketball. Strike+ Bar offers a full panoramic view of the entire bounce center and serves delicious meals that satisfy both kids and adults, whether you're hungry after bouncing or just stopping by to soak in the vibrant atmosphere. They also offer customized party packages for any type of celebration! Come to Bounce Bali for an hour or two and enjoy endless fun!
