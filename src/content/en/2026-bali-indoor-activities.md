**Bottom Line**: When it rains or you need to dodge the sun, the top pick in the Kuta area is **Trans Studio Bali** (a large indoor theme park on the top floor of a major shopping mall, with 7 themed zones and 23 rides—enough to keep you busy all day); other areas offer backups like the newly opened Icon Bali mall, five-star hotel afternoon teas, and handcraft workshops in Ubud. This article organizes 60 indoor activities by region: Ubud, Canggu, Sanur, and Nusa Dua.

On a vacation in **Bali**, nothing is more dreaded than an unexpected afternoon thunderstorm or the scorching midday sun that's hard to bear. But don't let the weather ruin your holiday spirit! Beyond its stunning beaches, this island hides countless high-quality indoor spaces. Whether you want to shop leisurely in air-conditioned comfort, let the kids burn off energy at a five-star indoor playground, or enjoy a serene handcrafted silver jewelry workshop amidst the misty Ubud jungle, these 60 carefully selected indoor backups will reveal another refined and comfortable side of **Bali**.

Before heading out, check the [Bali real-time weather](/en/weather/) to see if you really need a backup plan or if it's just a brief afternoon shower.

## Kuta Area

If you're in the Kuta area, consider these backups for rainy days or scorching sun:

### Beachwalk Shopping Center｜Shopping, Movies, and Dining All in One Across from the Beach

Located across from Kuta Beach, Beachwalk can easily fill half a day. The second floor has a Cinema XXI movie theater—perfect for watching a film on a rainy day. Shopping options include Zara, Uniqlo, H&M, Sephora, The Body Shop, as well as local Balinese brands like Bamboo Blonde and Bali Tees. There's a wide variety of dining choices: Indonesian cuisine at Bebek Tepi Sawah and Mr. Wayan by The Sea, Japanese at Sushi Tei, Genki Sushi, Gyu-Kaku, and Kimukatsu. On the basement level B1, you'll find a Cocomart supermarket, and Candylicious candy store is great for kids.

- Address: Jl. Pantai Kuta No. 60, Kuta
- Hours: Monday–Thursday 10:00–22:00, Friday–Sunday 10:00–23:00
- Suggested stay: 2–3 hours for shopping and dining; add a movie or beach visit for a half-day trip
- Tip: The building features an open, semi-outdoor design with greenery, water features, and natural light. During heavy afternoon rain or strong sun, it's best to stick to the cinema and indoor shops. In the evening, you can walk directly to the beach to watch the sunset.

## Canggu

On a rainy day in Canggu, here's how to pass the time:

### Bounce Bali

![Bounce Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0f7c35106c3dbcfbe5b4b4f11acfd0a6.jpg)

If you encounter a rainy day in Bali, head to Bounce Bali! It's Bali's first trampoline park, located inside the Canggu Club. This air-conditioned facility is designed for pure bouncing fun! Unlike home trampolines, Bounce features specially engineered spring frames that let you jump higher! With 360-degree trampoline walls, there's no other place like this in Bali. All frames and springs are covered with two-inch-thick safety padding. If you love to bounce, this is your paradise.

At Bounce Bali, you can free-jump and practice tricks, or join organized activities like dodgeball or basketball on trampolines. The Strike+ Bar offers a full view of the entire bounce center and serves delicious meals that satisfy both kids and adults—whether you're hungry after jumping or just soaking up the vibrant atmosphere. They also offer customized party packages for any type of celebration! Come to Bounce Bali for an hour or two of non-stop fun!

### Puppy Yoga

![](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-06/1781160568678-index1.jpg)

On rainy or scorching days, Puppy Yoga is a perfect way to unwind. This playful, yoga-light experience lasts about 60 minutes and requires no prior yoga experience. Sessions are held in Uluwatu, Canggu, and Ubud, with yoga mats and puppies provided by the organizer—feel free to snap photos and videos. Most puppies are rescued strays, and part of the fee goes toward their vaccines, food, and medical care.

Wear comfortable, stain-resistant activewear. Those allergic to dog hair should proceed with caution, and check age restrictions for each session before booking.
