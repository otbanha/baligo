In other areas, you’ll find the newly opened Icon Bali Mall, five-star hotel afternoon teas, and hands-on workshops in Ubud. Beyond the stunning ocean views, this island hides countless stylish indoor spaces. Whether you want to shop leisurely in air-conditioned comfort, let the kids burn off energy at a five-star indoor playground, or enjoy a serene silver jewelry-making workshop in the misty Ubud highlands, these 60 carefully curated indoor backup plans will reveal a refined and cozy side of **Bali**.

### Upside Down World Bali

![在峇里島顛倒屋拍出令人驚異的照片](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_80ed988eca4472540b1a905c96e4468a.jpg)

Each session is limited to 45 skaters, and each ticket grants 1.5 hours of skating experience. Whether with friends or family, you can enjoy an exhilarating time on the icy surface! Bali Ice Skating Arena is a perfect spot for cooling off and having fun on rainy or scorching days.

## Canggu

On a rainy day in Canggu, here's how to pass the time:

### Bounce Bali

![Bounce Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_0f7c35106c3dbcfbe5b4b4f11acfd0a6.jpg)

If you encounter a rainy day in Bali, head to Bounce Bali! It's the first trampoline park on the island, located inside the Canggu Club. This air-conditioned facility is designed for pure bouncing fun. Unlike home trampolines, Bounce features specially designed spring frames that let you jump higher! With 360-degree trampoline walls, there's nothing else like it in Bali. All frames and springs are covered with two-inch-thick safety padding—if you love to bounce, this is your paradise.

At Bounce Bali, you can bounce freely and practice all kinds of tricks, or join their organized activities like dodgeball or basketball on trampolines. Strike+ Bar offers a full panoramic view of the entire bouncing center and serves delicious meals that satisfy both kids and adults—whether you're hungry after jumping or just want to soak up the vibrant atmosphere. They also offer customized party packages, so no matter what kind of celebration you're planning, it can happen here! Come to Bounce Bali for an hour or two and enjoy endless fun!

### Fitness & Workouts

![運動健身](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_321421d5b6c376f44537b4ea57e6f539.jpg)

If you're stuck in Canggu on a rainy day and don't know where to go, this area is a fitness lover's paradise with 20 to 30 gyms. You can use the rainy time to build a strong body and stay active. Well-known gyms include **CrossFit Wanderlust, Body Factory Bali**, and **Avenue Fitness**—whether you're into strength training or cardio, you'll find a workout that suits you, making rainy days both fulfilling and healthy!

### Rock Climbing Fun

![攀岩樂趣](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_38f97362bae8b91ae23e97debe1fb670.jpg)

Located in Canggu, **Bali Climbing** is a semi-outdoor climbing gym just a few steps from the beach. Here, you can not only get fit but also have fun and make new friends. They offer a variety of climbing routes suitable for everyone from beginners to pros. **Bali Climbing** also provides climbing classes for kids and adults, retreats, climbing trips, and event spaces. Their goal is to make everyone feel part of this passionate community! Even on rainy days, you can spend an energetic day here.

### Bowling Fun

![玩保齡球](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_92693933716c5496982c2f770ef67351.jpg)

Head to **Strike Bowling Bali** for a laughter-filled bowling session on rainy days! Whether it's a family outing or a gathering with friends and colleagues, bowling is a fun way to bond. Strike offers Bali's most modern ten-pin bowling experience with six automatic lanes and a computerized scoring system. To help little ones (or anyone else!), they also have bumpers to prevent balls from rolling into the gutters.

Strike features an air-conditioned restaurant and bar area serving food and drinks, so you can satisfy your hunger and thirst before, during, or after your game.

Whether you're 3 or 80 years old, **Strike Bowling Bali** is a fantastic choice for having fun while staying active. Let the good times roll!

### Ubud Cooking Classes

![烏布美食烹飪班](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_a06a0bd308129f244507a2130d8ea06e.jpg)

Ubud's cooking classes offer a fantastic opportunity to dive deep into the local food culture. The experience typically includes a market visit, learning cooking techniques, hands-on preparation, and tasting the final dishes. Participants head to the market with their instructor to pick fresh ingredients and learn about Bali's unique cooking methods.
