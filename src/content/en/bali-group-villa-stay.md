**Bottom line**: For groups of 6+ or extended family trips, booking a **private pool villa with three or more bedrooms** is more cost-effective and convenient than reserving multiple hotel rooms—spacious enough for kids to play and adults to enjoy privacy. Bali is a renowned vacation destination, famous for its beautiful beaches, warm hospitality, and rich culture. In recent years, it has also become a top choice for family travel, with more and more families opting to vacation there.

### **Why Choose a Family Villa**

- **Spaciousness**: Villas are typically much roomier than hotel rooms, which is key for families. Kids have plenty of space to play and relax, while parents can enjoy some privacy.
- **Privacy**: Villas offer greater seclusion, which is important for families. You can enjoy your own pool and garden without worrying about other guests.
- **Convenient Amenities**: Many villas come equipped with kitchens, washing machines, and other handy facilities, making it easy to cook meals during your stay and save both time and money.
- **Flexibility**: Villas provide more flexibility, allowing you to choose the size, location, and amenities that best suit your family's needs.

### **How to Choose a Family Villa**

When choosing a family villa, there are several factors to consider:

- **Family Size**: Select a villa of the right size to accommodate your entire family.
- **Location**: Choose a villa close to the attractions and activities you're interested in.
- **Amenities**: Pick a villa equipped with the facilities you need, such as a kitchen, washing machine, and swimming pool.
- **Budget**: Opt for a villa that fits your budget.

Looking for a place where parents can unwind and kids can have a blast? **Family Nest Villas** in Uluwatu is a must-book! This family-focused resort features multiple private pool villas, a kids' club, a treehouse play area, a café, and a poolside restaurant—making your vacation both relaxing and fun.

Villas range from one to four bedrooms, with kids' rooms stocked with toys and optional cribs. Each villa comes with a private pool, balcony, refrigerator, and premium bedding for thoughtful comfort. Even better, **children aged 0 to 17 stay free**, making family travel worry-free.

The location is super convenient—just a 10-minute drive from Uluwatu and Bingin beaches, surrounded by trendy cafés and local restaurants. At the heart of the property, a large outdoor pool is nestled within tropical gardens, offering the perfect spot for kids to splash and adults to chill. Free Wi-Fi and parking are available throughout, and airport transfers can be arranged upon request.
