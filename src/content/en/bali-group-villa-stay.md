**Bottom line first**: For groups of 6 or more or extended families, choosing a **private pool villa with 3+ bedrooms** is more cost-effective and convenient than booking multiple hotel rooms—spacious enough for kids to play and adults to have privacy. Bali is a renowned vacation destination, famous for its beautiful beaches, warm hospitality, and rich culture. In recent years, it has also become a top choice for family travel, with more families opting to holiday there.

### **Reasons to Choose a Family Villa**

- **Spaciousness**: Villas are typically more spacious than hotel rooms, which is important for families with children. Kids have plenty of room to play and relax, while parents enjoy some privacy.
- **Privacy**: Villas offer greater privacy, a key benefit for families. You can enjoy your own pool and garden without worrying about other guests.
- **Convenient Amenities**: Many villas come with kitchens, washing machines, and other amenities that make family travel easier. You can cook your own meals during the trip, saving both time and money.
- **Flexibility**: Villas provide more flexibility, allowing you to choose the size, location, and features that best suit your family's needs.

### **How to Choose a Family Villa**

When choosing a family villa, there are several factors to consider:

- **Family Size**: Select a villa of the right size to accommodate your entire family.
- **Location**: Choose a villa close to the attractions and activities you're interested in.
- **Amenities**: Pick a villa with the facilities you need, such as a kitchen, washing machine, and swimming pool.
- **Budget**: Opt for a villa that fits your budget.
