# Bali Travel Hot Topics & News Roundup｜August 24, 2026

## 🎵 Taiwan Beats 2026 Lands in Bali! Taiwan's Music Industry Connects with Indonesia

Supported by Taiwan's Ministry of Culture's Bureau of Audiovisual and Music Industry Development (BAMID), **Taiwan Beats 2026** is currently underway in Bali and Jakarta, hosting the Taiwan–Indonesia Music Exchange & Business Matching event.

On August 22, the event took place at **Biji World Ubud** in Gianyar, featuring Taiwanese musicians **LINION** (Taiwanese R&B/Neo-Soul artist, singer, and producer) and **MANAGONA** (herbal pharmacist) performing alongside Bali's Rhythm Rebels, Robokids, and Adam Alydrus.

But this event is more than just 'coming to Bali to perform.'

Taiwan Beats also organized **Business Matching, industry forums, music industry visits, and Showcases**, enabling direct exchanges between Taiwan's music industry and Bali and Indonesia's musicians, record labels, festival curators, venues, and creative industry players. The entire Taiwan–Indonesia Music Exchange & Business Matching Event runs through **August 25**.

For Bali, this marks an intriguing shift: the island is not only a favorite travel destination for Taiwanese visitors but is also emerging as **a key platform for Taiwan's cultural and creative industries to enter the Indonesian market**.

## 🏖️ Savaya Team Expands into Kuta! New Zumana Beach Club Begins Soft Launch

The Kuta coastline has recently gained a new name: **Zumana Beach Club**.

Zumana is created by the renowned Savaya team, though it's a different brand and venue from Savaya on the Uluwatu cliffs. This new beach club is located along the **Kuta coastline**, focusing on ocean views, dining, and nightlife experiences.

Zumana has already **started welcoming its first guests on August 19**, but it's currently in a preview run / soft launch phase.

The official grand opening event is scheduled for:

**📅 September 25–26, 2026**

In other words, if you're staying in Kuta soon, you don't need to wait until the end of September to check it out. But if you're aiming to join the official opening party, you'll need to wait until September.

This marks a notable shift for Kuta.

In the past, most of Bali's newest beach clubs were concentrated in Canggu, Seminyak, or Uluwatu. Now, Kuta's coastline is also starting to see high-end beach entertainment brands emerge.

## 🏨 The Nusa Dua attracted 1.798 million visitors in six months, with tourist numbers increasing by 8.16% compared to last year

The Nusa Dua area in southeastern Bali has maintained strong tourist traffic in the first half of this year.

According to the latest data from InJourney Tourism Development Corporation (ITDC), the Nusa Dua area recorded **1,798,004 visitors** in the first and second quarters of 2026, an increase of **8.16%** compared to the same period last year.

This figure not only includes hotel guests but also reflects the total visitor volume across the entire resort area.

Nusa Dua still maintains a different positioning from Kuta, Seminyak, and Canggu—it's a hub for large international resorts, beaches, conferences, and high-end tourism facilities.

If you're looking for a **quieter, more resort-like area** in Bali, Nusa Dua remains a solid choice.

## 🦠 Bali Official Responds to Zika Virus Concerns: Travel Still Safe for Now

Bali's tourism authority today issued a statement addressing recent travel concerns over the Zika virus, confirming that they have coordinated with the Bali Provincial Health Office and relevant agencies, and emphasized that travel to Bali remains safe.

The discussion was mainly sparked by recent health travel advisories from the United States.

Bali officials hope to prevent tourists from overreacting to related news while continuing to monitor the health situation.

For travelers, there's no need to cancel a Bali trip just because of this news—but when visiting tropical regions, **taking mosquito precautions** is still a very practical move.

## 🛂 Canggu Beauty Salon Controversy Escalates, Norwegian Woman Deported

A few days ago, a Norwegian woman identified as NF sparked online discussion over a payment dispute at a Canggu beauty salon. The latest update is that she has been **deported** by Indonesian immigration authorities.

According to NusaBali, she was deported from the Denpasar Immigration Detention Center (Rudenim Denpasar) on August 21. An immigration investigation found that she had an **overstay** issue.

This is a key point for travelers to note amid the recent string of foreigner incidents in Bali:

**A social media dispute over a purchase is one thing; visa legality is another.**

Even as an ordinary tourist, you must double-check your permitted stay and avoid accidentally overstaying due to last-minute itinerary changes.
