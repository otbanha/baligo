# Bali Travel Hot Topics & News Roundup｜August 24, 2026

## 🎵 Taiwan Beats 2026 Lands in Bali! Taiwan's Music Industry Connects with Indonesia

Supported by Taiwan's Ministry of Culture's Bureau of Audiovisual and Music Industry Development (BAMID), **Taiwan Beats 2026** is currently underway in Bali and Jakarta, hosting the Taiwan–Indonesia Music Exchange & Business Matching event.

On August 22, the event took place at **Biji World Ubud** in Gianyar, featuring Taiwanese musicians **LINION** (Taiwanese R&B/Neo-Soul artist, singer, and producer) and **MANAGONA** (herbal pharmacist) performing alongside Bali's Rhythm Rebels, Robokids, and Adam Alydrus.

But this event is more than just 'coming to Bali to perform.'

Taiwan Beats also arranged **Business Matching sessions, industry forums, music industry visits, and Showcases**, enabling direct exchanges between Taiwan's music scene and Bali's and Indonesia's musicians, record labels, festival curators, venues, and creative industry players. The entire Taiwan–Indonesia Music Exchange & Business Matching Event runs through **August 25**.

For Bali, this marks an intriguing shift: the island is not only a favorite travel destination for Taiwanese visitors but is also emerging as **a key platform for Taiwan's cultural and creative industries to enter the Indonesian market**.

## 🏖️ Savaya Team Expands to Kuta! New Zumana Beach Club Begins Soft Opening

The Kuta coastline has recently gained a new name: **Zumana Beach Club**.

Zumana is created by the renowned Savaya team, though it's a different brand and venue from Savaya on the Uluwatu cliffs. This new beach club sits along the **Kuta coastline**, focusing on ocean views, dining, and nightlife experiences.

Zumana has already **welcomed its first guests since August 19**, but it's currently in a preview run / soft opening phase.

The official grand opening is scheduled for:

**📅 September 25–26, 2026**

In other words, if you're staying in Kuta soon, you don't need to wait until late September to check it out; but if you're aiming for the official opening party, you'll have to wait until September.

This marks a notable shift for Kuta.

In the past, most of Bali's newest beach clubs were concentrated in Canggu, Seminyak, or Uluwatu. Now, Kuta's coastline is also seeing the emergence of upscale beach entertainment brands.

## 🏨 The Nusa Dua attracted 1.798 million visitors in six months, with tourist numbers increasing by 8.16% compared to last year.

The Nusa Dua area in southeastern Bali has maintained strong tourist traffic in the first half of this year.

According to the latest data from InJourney Tourism Development Corporation (ITDC), the Nusa Dua area recorded **1,798,004 visitors** in the first and second quarters of 2026, an increase of **8.16%** compared to the same period last year.

This figure not only includes hotel guests but also reflects the total visitor count for the entire resort area.

Nusa Dua still maintains a different positioning from Kuta, Seminyak, and Canggu—it's a hub for large international resorts, beaches, conferences, and high-end tourism facilities.

If you're looking for a **quieter, more resort-oriented** area in Bali, Nusa Dua remains a solid choice.

## 🦠 Bali Officially Responds to Zika Virus Concerns: Travel Remains Safe for Now

Bali's Tourism Office today issued a statement addressing recent travel concerns over the Zika virus, confirming coordination with the Bali Provincial Health Office and relevant agencies, and emphasizing that travel to Bali remains safe.

The discussion primarily stems from recent health travel advisories from the United States.

Bali's authorities aim to prevent excessive panic among tourists due to related news while continuing to monitor health conditions.

For travelers, there is no need to cancel a trip to Bali just because of this news. However, when visiting tropical regions, **taking mosquito prevention measures** is still a very practical approach.

## 🛂 Canggu Beauty Salon Controversy Escalates, Norwegian Woman Deported

A few days ago, a Norwegian woman, identified as NF, sparked discussion on social media over a payment dispute at a Canggu beauty salon. The latest update is that she has been **deported** by Indonesian immigration authorities.

According to NusaBali, she was deported from the Denpasar Immigration Detention Center (Rudenim Denpasar) on August 21. An immigration investigation revealed that she had overstayed her visa.

This is a key point for travelers to note amid the recent string of incidents involving foreigners in Bali:

**A social media dispute over a purchase is one thing; visa legality is another.**

Even as an ordinary tourist, it's essential to double-check your visa validity and avoid accidentally overstaying due to last-minute itinerary changes.
