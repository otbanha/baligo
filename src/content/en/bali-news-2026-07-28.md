# Bali Tourist Hot Topics & News Roundup (July 28, 2026)

The peak summer travel season continues, with cultural events popping up alongside weather, safety, and transportation updates worth noting. Here are the 5 most noteworthy travel news stories today.

## 🌦️ 35 Disaster Incidents in Bali in One Week: Officials Warn of Weather Risks Even in Dry Season

The Bali Disaster Management Agency (BPBD) reported **35 disaster incidents** across the island from July 19 to 26, including strong winds, fallen trees, and localized landslides. Officials noted that while the dry season has arrived, atmospheric circulation may still bring brief heavy rain and strong winds to certain areas, urging residents and travelers to stay vigilant.

**Traveler Tips:** Before heading to waterfalls, mountainous areas, or outdoor attractions, check the latest weather updates. If strong winds or thunderstorms occur, avoid staying near large trees or temporary structures.

## 🍽️ Tanah Lot Art & Food Festival VIII Returns at the End of August

Tanah Lot, the iconic sea temple in Tabanan, has announced that the **Tanah Lot Art & Food Festival VIII** will take place at the end of August. The event will feature traditional Balinese art performances, local cuisine, a market, and cultural showcases, aiming to attract more domestic and international travelers to immerse themselves in Balinese culture.

**Traveler Tip:** If you're heading to Bali at the end of August, plan your visit to Tanah Lot during the festival. You'll not only catch the sunset but also enjoy exclusive cultural activities.

## 🌅 Komodo's Golo Mori Sunset Run Debuts in August, Perfect for Pairing with a Bali Trip

The **Golo Mori Sunset Run 2026**, set in Labuan Bajo, will take place in August, combining a coastal run, sunset views, and live music. It's become one of Indonesia's hottest sports tourism events in recent years. Since many travelers plan a dual-destination itinerary of Bali and Komodo, the event is expected to boost tourism demand for Komodo.

**Traveler Tip:** If you're planning to fly from Bali to Labuan Bajo, book your domestic flights and accommodation early to avoid sellouts during the event.

***

## 🚫 Canggu Running Club Discrimination Incident Update: Two Foreign Organizers Banned from Indonesia for 10 Years

The recent controversy over the Canggu running group restricting Indonesian locals from participating has taken a new turn. Indonesia's Directorate General of Immigration has announced that the two foreign organizers involved have been blacklisted and **banned from entering Indonesia for 10 years**. Officials stated that foreigners organizing events in Indonesia must still comply with local laws and regulations.

**Traveler Advice:** When joining community activities like running, yoga, or surfing, it's recommended to choose public, legal events open to all travelers to protect your own interests.

***

## ⛴️ Ferries Between Sanur and Nusa Penida Continue Normal Operations, Authorities Monitor Sea Conditions

Despite recent localized rainfall and increased waves, port authorities confirm that speedboat services between **Sanur, Nusa Penida, and Nusa Lembongan** are still operating normally, with no suspension or extreme weather warnings issued. However, officials will continue to monitor sea conditions and may adjust schedules if necessary.

**Traveler Advice:** On the day of your trip to Nusa Penida or Nusa Lembongan, it's recommended to reconfirm ferry schedules with the boat company and allow flexibility in your itinerary to accommodate weather changes.
