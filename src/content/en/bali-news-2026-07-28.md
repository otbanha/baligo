# Topics of Interest for Bali Tourists + News Roundup (July 28, 2026)

As the summer travel peak continues, cultural events are rolling out, while weather, safety, and transportation updates are worth noting for travelers. Here are the top 5 travel news stories worth following today.

***

## 🌦️ 35 Disaster Incidents in Bali in One Week, Officials Remind Travelers to Stay Vigilant During Dry Season

The Bali Provincial Disaster Management Agency (BPBD) reported **35 disaster incidents** across the island from July 19 to 26, including strong winds, fallen trees, and localized landslides. Officials noted that while the dry season has begun, atmospheric circulation may still bring brief heavy rain and strong winds to certain areas, urging residents and tourists to remain alert.

**Traveler Tips:** Before heading to waterfalls, mountainous areas, or outdoor attractions, check the latest weather updates; if strong winds or thunderstorms occur, avoid staying near large trees or temporary structures.

## 🍽️ Tanah Lot Art & Food Festival VIII Set for Late August

The iconic Tanah Lot temple in Tabanan has announced that the **Tanah Lot Art & Food Festival VIII** will take place at the end of August. The event will feature traditional Balinese art performances, local cuisine, a market, and cultural showcases, aiming to attract more domestic and international travelers to experience Balinese culture in depth.

**Traveler's Tip:** If you're visiting Bali in late August, consider timing your trip to Tanah Lot Temple during its special events—you'll not only catch a stunning sunset but also enjoy exclusive cultural activities.

## 🌅 Komodo's Golo Mori Sunset Run Debuts in August, Perfect for Pairing with a Bali Itinerary

The **Golo Mori Sunset Run 2026**, held in Labuan Bajo this August, combines a coastal run, sunset views, and live music, making it one of Indonesia's hottest sports tourism events. Since many travelers plan a 'Bali + Komodo' dual-destination trip, this event is expected to boost travel demand to Komodo.

**Traveler's Tip:** If you're flying from Bali to Labuan Bajo, book your domestic flights and accommodation early to avoid sellouts during the event.

***

## 🚫 Canggu Running Club Discrimination Incident Update: Two Foreign Organizers Banned from Indonesia for 10 Years

The recent controversy over a Canggu running club restricting Indonesian participants has taken a new turn. Indonesia's Directorate General of Immigration has announced that the two foreign organizers involved have been blacklisted and **banned from entering Indonesia for 10 years**. Officials emphasize that foreigners hosting events in Indonesia must still comply with local laws and regulations.

**Traveler's Tip:** When joining community activities like running, yoga, or surfing, choose public, legal events open to all travelers to protect your rights.

***

## ⛴️ Sanur to Nusa Penida Ferry Services Remain Normal, Authorities Monitor Sea Conditions

Despite recent localized rain and increased waves, port authorities confirm that speedboat services between **Sanur, Nusa Penida, and Nusa Lembongan** are still operating normally, with no suspension or extreme weather warnings issued. However, officials will continue to monitor sea conditions and may adjust schedules if necessary.

**Traveler's Tip:** On the day you head to Nusa Penida or Nusa Lembongan, it's recommended to reconfirm the ferry schedule with the boat company and keep your itinerary flexible to accommodate weather changes.
