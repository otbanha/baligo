If you're looking for an indoor activity that the whole family can get lost in, don't miss **ZONE OUT Bali**, located in the Legian area of Kuta! As Bali's first **VR virtual reality arcade**, it blends technology and entertainment to instantly transport you into another world 🚀.

Whether you're a speed-loving racer, a challenge-seeking shooter, or want to explore space, ride roller coasters, or survive a zombie apocalypse with your kids, this place has it all. Each game features **highly immersive visuals and dynamic equipment** that will keep you coming back for more.

***

### 🌟 Why You Must Visit ZONE OUT Bali

![為什麼一定要來 ZONE OUT Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_cfedd8137f0c6d17f1b48a138e4dfd1d.jpg)

![為什麼一定要來 ZONE OUT Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_72aa078824a91036c852becb7b2c0677.jpg)

![為什麼一定要來 ZONE OUT Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_835bc3a5864dbf77112a0383b60f6459.jpg)

- **Thrilling Game Selection**: From racing and horror to adventure and shooting, there's something for everyone, guaranteed to get your adrenaline pumping.
- **Highly Immersive Design**: ZONE OUT offers wireless VR headsets and a free-roaming 'VR Arena' area, letting players not just sit and play but step into a virtual world.
- **Fun for All Ages**: Whether you're a child, teen, or adult, you'll find a game that suits you.
- **Perfect for Rainy Days**: Bali's occasional afternoon showers are no problem—come here to stay dry and have a blast.
- **Perfect for couples, families, and friends**: Tackle VR missions, competitions, or adventures together—it's especially easy to create unforgettable memories.
- **Super convenient location**: Situated on the main street of Legian, just a short distance from Kuta Beach and popular attractions.
