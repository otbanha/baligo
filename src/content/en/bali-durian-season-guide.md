**Bottom line**: Bali’s main durian season runs from **November to February or March** (December to January is the peak for both quality and quantity, with trucks piled high on the roadside and prices at their lowest). The extended season from March to August offers sporadic supply, while September to October sees almost none. For the best deals and abundance, plan your trip for December to January.

If you’re a durian lover, timing your visit to Bali is key—otherwise, you might only catch a whiff without the “overflowing bounty” 😂

## 📅 Bali Durian Season Timeline

👉 **Main season: November to February/March (peak)**
👉 **Extended season: March to August (sporadic)**
👉 **Off-season: September to October (nearly none)

According to local farming rhythms, durian ripens most during the rainy season, so **December to January** typically marks the dual peak for quality and quantity.

***

## 🥇 When’s the Best Time to Eat?

Here’s my recommendation 👇:

### ✔ For “cheap and plentiful”

👉 **December to January**

- Trucks piled high with durian along the roadside 🚚
- Lowest prices (sometimes ridiculously cheap)
- Hard to go wrong with any purchase

### ✔ For those who want 'consistent quality with fewer crowds'

👉 **February to March**

- Still in stock, but fewer tourists
- Easier to take your time picking

### ✔ For the go-with-the-flow traveler

👉 **Around June**

- Occasionally there is a 'second mini-season'
- But quality and quantity are inconsistent

## ⚠️ A Few Things You Might Not Know

### 1️⃣ Durian in Bali Is Not Available Year-Round

Although many parts of Indonesia have durian all year, Bali still treats it more as a 'seasonal fruit'

### 2️⃣ Weather Affects the Harvest

- Too much rain 👉 flowers drop → lower yield
- Normal weather 👉 abundant harvest (prices can be cut in half)

### 3️⃣ Location Also Matters

- The north (such as the Kintamani area) is more common
- You can occasionally find street vendors around Ubud

***

## 🍴 Travel Tips (Important)

- Many hotels 🚫 **forbid bringing durian into the room**
- Private drivers, taxis, and Gojek/Grab drivers may also refuse to take you
- Best way to eat it: 👉 **buy it on the street and open it right there**

***

## ✍️ Bali Durian Cheat Sheet

👉 For all-you-can-eat durian → **go straight in December–January**
👉 To avoid the crowds → **February–March works too**
👉 Other times → it's a gamble 😆
