## 🛬 Day 1: Arrive in Bali – Time to Unwind!

After landing at Ngurah Rai Airport, we took our pre-booked airport transfer to a hotel in Sanur on Bali's east coast. Sanur is a tranquil seaside town in southeastern Bali, known for its laid-back vibe and slow pace of life—a favorite among families and long-stay travelers. It boasts a long beachfront promenade and sunrise-facing beaches, perfect for morning walks, cycling, and swimming. Unlike bustling Kuta, Sanur exudes a relaxing, elegant charm.

https://youtu.be/SpcgZ4iK7qs

We stayed at Griya Santrian, a resort right on the beachfront—just a few steps from the pool to the sand, ideal for water lovers or beach bums. The architecture retains traditional Balinese style, with lush, serene garden views. The hotel also offers SPA, yoga classes, and a sea-view restaurant, perfect for travelers wanting to embrace the 'slow living' charm of Sanur.

## Day Tour FAQ

### 1. Q: How many people are needed for a private tour on Klook?

A: Yes! Most private tours on Klook require **just 2 people to start, no group sharing, and even solo travel is fine**—no need to worry about gathering a group. Whether you're a couple, friends, or a family, you can easily enjoy a private driver-guide with more flexible itineraries.

### 2. Q: Can I choose a Chinese-speaking guide?

A: **Some tours offer a Chinese-speaking guide option—please check the 'Language' field when booking.** If a Chinese guide isn't available, English-speaking guides or local drivers are usually provided. You can also contact the local driver in advance via WhatsApp to discuss your needs.

### 3. Q: Are entrance fees, lunch, or parking fees included in the itinerary?

A: Each itinerary is slightly different. Some include entrance fees and lunch, but most will note 'meals at your own expense' or 'entrance fees not included.' For example, our Ubud day trip requires no extra parking fees at any of the stops, but lunch is at your own cost. We recommend reading the details carefully before departure or contacting customer service.

### 4. Q: Will the driver accompany us into the attractions?

A: Yes! Most itineraries are 'guided private car tours,' where the driver will accompany you into the attractions to explain, take photos, and help with ticket purchases. However, if you choose the English-speaking driver option, you may explore some attractions on your own.

### 5. Q: Can the itinerary be customized? Can we change the route midway?

A: Generally, no, but some private itineraries offer more flexibility. You can ask customer service or the driver in advance if minor adjustments are possible (e.g., swapping a stop or extending time at a location), but this depends on traffic and time availability on the day.

### 6. Q: If we're staying in different areas, like Ubud or Jimbaran, can we still be picked up?

A: Yes, but the pickup range and fees may vary by area. Most itineraries offer 'free pickup' in areas like Kuta, Seminyak, Sanur, and Ubud. Other areas may require an additional fee or confirmation of availability.

### 7. Q: Will the day trip feel rushed?

A: Klook itineraries are generally 'moderately paced,' lasting about 8–10 hours. Although many stops are planned, private tours don't rush, and the driver will adjust the route to avoid crowds. For example, we arrived early before the Kecak fire dance performance and didn't have to queue—a huge plus for the experience!

### 8. Q: Do I need to bring cash?

A: It's highly recommended to bring some Indonesian Rupiah in cash. While some attractions and restaurants accept cards, places like Ubud Traditional Market, fire dance tickets, and temple donations still require cash.
