## 🛬 Day 1: Arrive in Bali, Switch to Chill Mode!

After landing at Ngurah Rai Airport, we took our pre-booked airport transfer to a hotel in Sanur on Bali's east coast. Sanur is a tranquil seaside town in southeastern Bali, with a laid-back vibe and slow pace of life—a favorite among families and long-stay travelers. It boasts a long beachfront promenade and sunrise-facing beaches, perfect for morning walks, cycling, and swimming. Unlike bustling Kuta, Sanur exudes a relaxing, elegant charm.

https://youtu.be/SpcgZ4iK7qs

We stayed at Griya Santrian, a resort right on the beachfront—just a few steps from the pool to the sand, ideal for water lovers and beach bums. The architecture retains traditional Balinese style, with lush, serene garden views. The hotel also offers SPA, yoga classes, and a sea-view restaurant, perfect for travelers wanting to embrace the 'slow living' charm of Sanur.

## 🌿 Day 3: Half-Day Free Time in Ubud + Spa Time

Here, you can join yoga classes, visit artisan villages, immerse yourself in Balinese temple culture, and unwind at one of the many high-quality villas.

Soaking in the pool under the stars at night and waking up to birdsong in the morning made it the most relaxing and romantic night of our Ubud trip ❤️. Highly recommended for honeymoons or couples celebrating a special occasion!

### ⛱️ 09:00 Depart → 11:00 Padang Padang Beach

Our first stop is a hidden gem of a beach—**Padang Padang Beach**. Surrounded by lush rock walls, you have to squeeze through a narrow crevice to reach it, giving it a real sense of adventure! The beach is small but boasts fine white sand and crystal-clear blue water, making it a perfect spot for swimming and snapping photos 🌊📸

https://youtu.be/isVxI2UEq6U

### 🍴 12:30 Lunch Time

We chose our own lunch spot, and Tawan kindly recommended a restaurant with ocean views. Enjoying grilled fish with coconut water while watching surfers ride the waves was the ultimate chill vibe.

### 🏝️ 14:00 Blue Point Beach (Suluban Beach)

Next, we headed to **Suluban Beach**, also known as "Blue Point Beach," a secret paradise for many surfers. This beach is surrounded by massive sea caves, and at low tide, you can walk into the caves and explore small tidal pools—mysterious and full of adventure! It truly felt like discovering an "adult-sized secret hideout" 🏄‍♂️

https://youtu.be/hQbBTktqEqY

### 🛕 16:30 Uluwatu Temple

Perched on a cliff, this ancient temple is not only one of Bali's six holiest temples but also offers a stunning vantage point over the Indian Ocean. The cheeky monkeys here are playful but mischievous—keep your belongings secure (especially sunglasses and hats!). With our guide, we learned about the history and spiritual significance of Uluwatu Temple, gaining a deeper appreciation for the locals' reverence for nature and the divine.

![16:30 烏魯瓦圖廟（Uluwatu Temple）](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_f221761e8c64cd4b7a6f2dcc5e6f1859.jpg)

### 🔥 18:00 Kecak Dance Performance (Highlight: **Skip-the-line entry!** )

The show begins at sunset, ending with bursts of flames and roaring cheers from the crowd—an absolutely electrifying vibe 🔥🔥🔥

> Entrance fees for these attractions may fluctuate with the peak season, so it's a good idea to check the latest reference prices on the [**Bali Entrance Fee Price List**](/en/tickets/) before you go.

### 4. Q: Will the driver accompany us into the attractions?

A: Yes! Most itineraries are "**guided private car tours**," where the driver will accompany you into the attractions to explain, take photos, and help with ticket purchases. However, if you choose the English-speaking driver option, you may explore some attractions on your own.

### 5. Q: Can the itinerary be customized? Can we change the route midway?

A: In principle, no, but some private itineraries offer more flexibility. You can ask customer service or the driver in advance if minor adjustments are possible (such as swapping a specific attraction or extending your stay), but this depends on the day's traffic and time availability.

### 6. Q: If we're staying in different areas, like Ubud or Jimbaran, can we still be picked up?

A: Yes, but the pickup range and fees may vary depending on the area. Most itineraries offer "free pickup" in areas like Kuta, Seminyak, Sanur, and Ubud, while other areas may require an additional fee or need confirmation on whether pickup is possible.

### 7. Q: Will the day trip feel very rushed?

A: Most Klook tours have a 'moderate pace,' lasting about 8–10 hours a day. Although there are many stops, private tours don't rush you, and the driver adjusts the route to avoid crowds. For example, we arrived early before the Kecak fire dance and got in without queuing—a huge plus!

### 8. Q: Do I need to bring cash?

A: It's highly recommended to bring some Indonesian rupiah in cash. While some attractions and restaurants accept cards, places like Ubud's traditional market, fire dance tickets, and temple donations still require cash.

### 9. Q: Will there be traffic jams? Is time allocated for that?

A: Bali's peak traffic can be heavy, but Klook drivers usually avoid busy hours or adjust the route order to bypass congestion.

### 10. Q: How do I contact the driver or guide?

A: Klook provides the driver's contact info before the trip. I suggest downloading WhatsApp—it's the most common communication tool in Bali. You can confirm pickup times, guide language, etc., in advance.
