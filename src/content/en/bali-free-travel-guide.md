This minimizes time stuck in traffic. Our 8-day, 7-night guide follows this logic, covering transportation, accommodation, attractions, food, and SPA — feel free to adjust based on your trip length.

Bali is a popular Indonesian destination known for its stunning beaches, rich culture, and history. However, traffic can be a major headache for travelers, potentially disrupting plans or even causing you to miss flights.

To make your Bali trip more enjoyable, we've crafted this travel guide. You can tailor it to your own schedule. We've especially factored in traffic when designing the itinerary, so you can explore Bali at your leisure.

## Day 3 to Day 4: Sanur or Nusa Penida

From Seminyak or Canggu, it takes about an hour by car to reach the east coast of southern Bali. Depending on your preferences, you can choose [Option 1] to explore the beach vibes of Sanur, or [Option 2] to head to Nusa Penida for its stunning natural landscapes and underwater world.

### Option 1: Sanur

- Sanur is a laid-back beach town on Bali.
- It boasts serene, beautiful beaches with a beachfront path stretching for kilometers, perfect for sunbathing and leisurely strolls.
- The beach path is lined with eclectic seaside restaurants, shops, and markets blending exotic and Balinese styles, where you can buy local handicrafts and souvenirs.
- Sanur offers spectacular sunrise views—wake up early to soak in the tranquility and beauty of Bali.
- It's also a hotspot for water sports like kayaking, stand-up paddleboarding, and windsurfing.
- The ferry terminal to the nearby islands is right here, making it easy to catch an early boat to Nusa Penida or Lembongan.

## **Day 7 to Day 8: Kuta or Jimbaran**

To tackle Bali's traffic challenges, we recommend spending your last one or two nights in Kuta or Jimbaran. Both areas are close to the airport, and as long as you don't stay too far north or south, you can usually reach the airport in about 30 minutes on your final day. This arrangement ensures you won't arrive at the airport too early and get bored, nor worry about missing your flight due to traffic jams. Whether you choose Kuta or Jimbaran, there are plenty of amazing spots to explore, perfect for wrapping up your trip on a high note.

**Option One: Kuta**

- Kuta is one of Bali's busiest commercial districts, packed with shops, markets, and malls. Here you can find exquisite Balinese crafts, clothing, jewelry, and all sorts of souvenirs.
- Kuta Beach is one of Bali's most famous beaches, known for its golden sand and impressive waves. It's a surfer's paradise and a perfect spot for sunbathing and watching the sunset.
- Kuta offers a wide range of dining options, from [traditional local dishes](/en/blog/bali-top-10-foods/) to international cuisine. You can savor flavors from around the world to satisfy your taste buds.
- Kuta's nightlife is vibrant, with numerous bars, clubs, and entertainment venues. After dark, you can dive into Bali's lively party scene.
- At Kuta Beach, you can try surfing—it's world-famous as a great spot for beginners. [Check out Jay's first surfing video](https://youtu.be/gECdNvG16ww).

## What NOT to Do vs. What TO Do

### How NOT to Plan Your Bali Itinerary

1. **Overpacked and Rushed Schedule**: Avoid filling every single day with attractions and activities. Instead, give yourself time to relax and soak in Bali's stunning scenery and laid-back vibe. A good rule of thumb is to schedule no more than four attractions/activities plus lunch/dinner restaurants per day.
2. **Bouncing Between Regions**: While Bali isn't huge, traffic jams are a serious issue. Moving between regions can eat up a lot of time and energy, leading to fatigue and inconvenience. When planning your itinerary, try to focus your exploration and activities within one area. This saves time and effort, and allows you to dive deeper into the local culture and sights.
