## The Perfect Blend of Bali's Volcanic Majesty and Tranquil Coastlines

**Bottom line**: Amed and Tulamben, located on Bali's northeast coast, are about a **2.5-3.5 hour drive** from the airport. These hidden gems are famous for the Liberty wreck dive and black sand beaches, far from the tourist crowds. On clear days, you can see three volcanoes—Mount Agung, Mount Abang, and Mount Batur—all at once, making it ideal for a 1-2 day deep diving or peaceful seaside getaway.

Nestled on Bali's northeast coast, Amed and Tulamben offer a world apart from the bustling tourist hubs. There's no Kuta's lively chaos or Ubud's artistic vibe here; instead, you'll find a serene fishing village atmosphere and breathtaking natural landscapes.

![阿貢火山（Gunung Agung）  峇里島最高、最具宗教與文化意義的活火山，海拔約 3,031 公尺；上古火山殘留的 Gunung Abang，海拔約 2,151 公尺；巴杜爾火山（Gunung Batur）  是一座活火山錐，海拔約 1,717 公尺](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_38b4f9c2c400c72b9f94875f5af8aeaa.jpg)

Mount Agung (Gunung Agung) – Bali's highest and most culturally significant active volcano, standing at around 3,031 meters; the ancient volcanic remnant of Mount Abang, at about 2,151 meters; and Mount Batur (Gunung Batur) – an active volcanic cone rising to roughly 1,717 meters.

From this tranquil black sand coastline, you can take in three of Bali's most famous volcanoes at once: Mount Agung, Mount Abang, and Mount Batur. When the clouds part, all three volcanoes appear before you, as if stepping into a mythical world.

The drive from Ngurah Rai International Airport to this area takes about 2.5 to 3.5 hours, winding through villages, forests, and rice terraces—a scenic road trip in itself.

## Dive into the Underwater World

What makes Amed and Tulamben most captivating is their crystal-clear waters and diverse marine life. This is a paradise for diving enthusiasts—whether you're a seasoned diver or a complete beginner, you'll find waters that suit your level.

Tulamben's USAT Liberty Wreck is one of the world's most famous dive sites. With moderate depth and excellent visibility, you can dance among colorful tropical fish while getting up close to this historic wreck, now embraced by the ocean.

## Dancing with Sunrise and Sunset

### Chasing Sunrise / Sunset / Sea Fishing on a Jukung

https://youtube.com/shorts/0f-eAQ78no4

Beyond diving and snorkeling, there's one unique experience you absolutely can't miss—heading out to sea on a traditional local fishing boat called a **Jukung**. These small wooden boats with outriggers have been used by local fishermen for generations and offer the most authentic way to explore Amed's coastline.

In the early morning, many visitors choose to take a Jukung out to sea to **chase the sunrise**. As the boat gently glides away from the shore, you'll watch the sun slowly rise above the horizon, casting golden light across the water and painting the world in soft, warm shades of orange. The tranquility and grandeur of that moment are truly unforgettable.

In the evening, you can experience a **Jukung sunset cruise**. Unlike the crisp morning air, the sea breeze at sunset is gentle and slightly cool. As the sun slowly sinks behind the volcano, the sky and sea are dyed in shades of pink, purple, gold, and orange—an incredibly dreamy sight.

If you're interested in fishing village culture, you can also opt for a **sea fishing experience**. Many local fishermen will take visitors out to fish, letting you experience their daily rhythm firsthand. After catching fish, a local restaurant can prepare them for you, giving you a true 'catch-and-cook' seafood dinner.

### Sunset Point (📍[Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1IrHQ5679D68HUAXlXWyqhgnvITAONfE&usp=sharing))

![由於地形的關係，很神奇的是Sunset Point這個角度面向西邊](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_5b312da0651b1112f4c52963c74fa9ee.jpg)

Due to the terrain, it's fascinating how Sunset Point faces directly west.

In Amed, the most popular spot to watch the sunset is undoubtedly **Sunset Point Amed Bali**. Perched on a hillside, this viewpoint offers an unbeatable panorama of the entire coastline, with distant volcanoes and a small fishing village in view—making it a true 'sunset sanctuary' for many travelers.

As the sun slowly sinks behind the volcano, the sky transforms from deep blue to shades of orange, pink, and purple, mirrored by the sea in a dreamlike scene. The serene surroundings, free from commercial hustle, make it a place where you can truly unwind and connect with nature.

Near the viewpoint, a few small cafes and bars let you sip a cold drink while quietly taking in the sunset, enjoying a peaceful and leisurely time. Last time, Xiao Jie visited [this spot](https://www.sunsetpointamedbali.com/)—the food was average, but the views were priceless!

If you're in Amed, be sure to save an evening for Sunset Point—the sunset here is absolutely worth the wait.

### A Must-Visit for Food Lovers: Amed Traditional Salt Workshop
![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1789050589867-amed_salt_center2.webp)

Beyond diving, Amed also offers a glimpse into traditional salt-making. Along the eastern coast, salt farmers spread seawater over black volcanic sand to concentrate it under the sun, then filter and sun-dry it again to produce mineral-rich sea salt—a perfect embodiment of 'from sea to table.'

#### Amed Salt Center (📍[Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1IrHQ5679D68HUAXlXWyqhgnvITAONfE&usp=sharing))
![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1789050519786-amed_salt_center.jpg)

Located in town, **Amed Salt Center** sells these locally made salts, each carrying the unique flavor of Amed's coast. Besides shopping, if you make a donation, you can tour the salt fields behind and witness the traditional salt-making process firsthand—a rare cultural experience. These handcrafted sea salts are also an essential seasoning for Balinese chefs, adding a distinctive layer to any dish.

## Fishing Village Flavors

Amed and Tulamben were originally traditional fishing villages, so fresh seafood naturally takes center stage on the dining table here.

### 9 Notable Restaurants in the Area (📍[Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1IrHQ5679D68HUAXlXWyqhgnvITAONfE&usp=sharing))

**1. Warung Agung Amed**

A cozy family-run restaurant serving a fusion of Chinese, Indonesian, and seafood dishes. Popular recommendations include the daily catch, corn fritters, and fish curry, plus must-try desserts like caramelized pineapple ice cream and black rice pudding. The owner sometimes puts on live music performances!

**2. Warung Enak Amed**

A budget-friendly eatery loved by both locals and travelers, offering delicious Balinese pancakes, BBQ, and freshly grilled fish. The lava cake is a hidden dessert gem—best enjoyed all to yourself!

**3. Restaurant Bukit Segara**

An oceanfront restaurant specializing in daily fresh catches and a diverse mix of Asian and European cuisine. Prices are on the higher side, but the service, quality, and stunning views make it a meal worth investing in.

**4. Galanga Amed**

A small, cozy Asian fusion restaurant serving Balinese, Indonesian, and healthy smoothie bowls. With a relaxed vibe, it's a great spot for lunch, dinner, and desserts.

**5. di Lipah Warung**

A traditional seafood eatery, their signature dish is Pepe Ikan (grilled fish in coconut leaves), and the desserts are also highly praised. Though there's no view, the food and warm service leave a lasting impression.

**6. Gusto Resto Amed**

A comfortable home-style restaurant specializing in Mediterranean and Indonesian fusion dishes. Recommended are the fresh salads, rendang chicken, and pan-seared tuna steak—a great choice for group dining.

**7. Green Melon Warung**

Known for healthy cooking with fresh ingredients at affordable prices. Try the BBQ tuna and squid dishes paired with Balinese salad, plus the adorable banana leaf cone rice. Cash only.

**8. Trattoria Amed**

An authentic Italian restaurant with a romantic atmosphere and excellent service. The pizzas are incredibly popular, and there's a professional wine cellar and well-equipped restrooms—a paradise for pizza lovers.

**9. Sails Restaurant**

With stunning ocean views reminiscent of a Greek island, this spot is a must. Try the banana leaf-wrapped fish, lamb patties, and frozen margaritas. The vibe is fresh, the staff are friendly, and they even offer a free shuttle service.

## Transportation

## Important Tips Before You Go

https://youtube.com/shorts/XS1j72uEtTY

Since Amed and Tulamben are relatively remote with less developed infrastructure than the southern tourist areas, travelers must prepare thoroughly before heading out:

- **Cash is king**: ATMs are scarce in the area and may not accept foreign cards, so it's best to withdraw enough cash before your trek or dive.
- **Bring basic medications**: Local medical facilities are limited—pack simple items like stomach medicine, pain relievers, and mosquito repellent.
- **Book transport in advance**: If you need a private car or driver, make reservations ahead of time.
- **Limited internet**: Signal can be unstable in some areas, so consider getting a local SIM card or portable WiFi.
- **Protect the marine ecosystem**: Avoid touching coral and use reef-safe sunscreen to treat this precious ocean with care.

## FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions

**Are Amed and Tulamben suitable for beginner divers?**

Yes, the sea conditions here are stable with gentle currents, making them especially suitable for diving beginners. Local dive schools also offer introductory courses.

**Can I go directly from Ubud or Kuta?**

Yes, but it's recommended to hire a private car for the journey, which takes about 2.5 to 3 hours. You can also arrange stops at other attractions along the way.

**What is the best time to visit?**

The dry season (May to October) is the best time to visit, with clear skies and crystal-clear waters perfect for water activities.

**Do local restaurants offer vegetarian options?**

Most restaurants can provide vegetarian options, though the choices are limited. It's advisable to inform them in advance and confirm.

**What other attractions are nearby?**

If you have a bit more time, I recommend visiting three stunning spots in the area:

![FAQ 常見問題解答](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_ed589a98d59d6d3d1de01faf3eb0b822.jpg)

- **Lempuyang Temple (Heaven's Gate)**: Perched on a mountainside, this temple is famous for its dramatic volcanic backdrop and sacred stone gate, making it a paradise for photographers.
- **Tirta Gangga (Holy Water Garden)**: A former royal water palace where stone carvings and koi fish create a mesmerizing scene in the pools—perfect for a leisurely stroll and photos.
- **Ujung Water Palace**: An ancient palace blending European and Balinese styles, surrounded by ponds and lush greenery, offering a serene and historically rich atmosphere.
