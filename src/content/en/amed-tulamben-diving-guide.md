## The Perfect Blend of Bali's Volcanic Majesty and Tranquil Coastlines

**Bottom line**: Amed and Tulamben, located on Bali's northeast coast, are about a **2.5–3.5 hour drive** from the airport. These hidden gems are famous for their wreck diving and black sand beaches, far from the tourist crowds. On clear days, you can see three volcanoes—Mount Agung, Mount Abang, and Mount Batur—all at once. Ideal for a 1–2 day deep diving or peaceful seaside escape.

Nestled on Bali's northeast coast, Amed and Tulamben offer a world apart from the bustling tourist hubs. There's no Kuta's lively chaos or Ubud's artistic vibe here—just a serene fishing village atmosphere and breathtaking natural scenery.

![阿貢火山（Gunung Agung）  峇里島最高、最具宗教與文化意義的活火山，海拔約 3,031 公尺；上古火山殘留的 Gunung Abang，海拔約 2,151 公尺；巴杜爾火山（Gunung Batur）  是一座活火山錐，海拔約 1,717 公尺](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_38b4f9c2c400c72b9f94875f5af8aeaa.jpg)

Mount Agung (Gunung Agung): Bali's highest and most culturally significant active volcano, standing at about 3,031 meters; the ancient volcanic remnant of Mount Abang, at roughly 2,151 meters; and Mount Batur (Gunung Batur), an active volcanic cone at about 1,717 meters.

From this tranquil black sand coastline, you can take in three of Bali's most famous volcanoes at once: Mount Agung, Mount Abang, and Mount Batur. When the clouds clear, all three appear before you, as if stepping into a mythical realm.

The drive from Ngurah Rai International Airport to this area takes about 2.5 to 3.5 hours, winding through villages, forests, and rice terraces—a scenic road trip in itself.

What makes Amed and Tulamben most captivating is their crystal-clear waters and diverse marine life. This is a paradise for diving enthusiasts—whether you're a seasoned diver or a complete beginner, you'll find waters suited to your level.

## Dancing with Sunrise and Sunset

### Chasing Sunrise/Sunset/Sea Fishing by Jukung

https://youtube.com/shorts/0f-eAQ78no4

Beyond diving and snorkeling, there's one unique experience you absolutely can't miss: heading out to sea on a traditional fishing boat called a **Jukung**. These small wooden boats with outriggers have been used by local fishermen for generations and offer the most authentic way to explore Amed's coastline.

In the early morning, many visitors choose to take a Jukung out to **chase the sunrise**. As the boat gently glides away from shore, you'll watch the sun rise slowly over the horizon, casting golden light across the sea and painting the world in soft warm orange. The peace and grandeur of that moment are unforgettable.

In the evening, you can experience a **Jukung sunset cruise**. Unlike the crisp morning air, the sea breeze at sunset is gentle and slightly cool. As the sun slowly sinks behind the volcanoes, the sky and sea are dyed in shades of pink, purple, gold, and orange—absolutely dreamy.

If you're curious about fishing village culture, you can also opt for a **sea fishing experience**. Many local fishermen will take you out to fish, letting you experience their daily rhythm firsthand. After catching fish, a local restaurant can cook them for you, giving you a true 'catch-and-cook' seafood dinner.

### Sunset Point (📍[Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1IrHQ5679D68HUAXlXWyqhgnvITAONfE&usp=sharing))

![由於地形的關係，很神奇的是Sunset Point這個角度面向西邊](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_5b312da0651b1112f4c52963c74fa9ee.jpg)

Due to the terrain, it's fascinating how Sunset Point faces west.

In Amed, the most popular spot to watch the sunset is undoubtedly **Sunset Point Amed Bali**. Perched on a hillside, this viewpoint offers an unbeatable panorama of the entire coastline, with distant volcanoes and a small fishing village in view—making it a true 'sunset sanctuary' for many travelers.

As the sun slowly sinks behind the volcano, the sky transforms from deep blue to shades of orange-red and violet-pink, mirrored by the sea in a dreamlike scene. The peaceful surroundings, free from commercial hustle, make it a place where you can truly quiet your mind and connect with nature.

A few small cafes and bars near the viewpoint let you sip a cold drink while quietly taking in the sunset, enjoying a calm and leisurely moment. The last time I visited, I went [here](https://www.sunsetpointamedbali.com/); the food was just okay, but the scenery was priceless!

If you're in Amed, be sure to save an evening for Sunset Point—the sunset here is absolutely worth the wait.

### A Must-Visit for Food Lovers: Amed Traditional Salt Workshop

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1789050589867-amed_salt_center2.webp)

In Amed, beyond diving, there's a traditional salt-making technique worth exploring. Along the eastern coast, salt farmers spread seawater over black volcanic sand to concentrate it under the sun, then filter and sun-dry it again to produce mineral-rich sea salt—a perfect 'from sea to table' story.

#### Amed Salt Center (📍[Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1IrHQ5679D68HUAXlXWyqhgnvITAONfE&usp=sharing))

![raw-image](https://images.gobaligo.id/images/2026-09/1789050519786-amed_salt_center.jpg)

Located in the town, the **Amed Salt Center** sells these locally made salts, each carrying the unique flavor of Amed's coast. Besides shopping, you can also visit the salt fields behind with a donation, witnessing the traditional salt-making process firsthand—a rare cultural experience. These handcrafted sea salts are essential seasonings for Balinese chefs, adding unique depth to any dish.

## Fishing Village Flavors

Amed and Tulamben were originally traditional fishing villages, so fresh seafood naturally takes center stage on the dining table here.

### 9 Notable Restaurants in the Area (📍[Map Location](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/edit?mid=1IrHQ5679D68HUAXlXWyqhgnvITAONfE&usp=sharing))

**1. Warung Agung Amed**

A cozy family-style restaurant serving a fusion of Chinese, Indonesian, and seafood dishes. Popular recommendations include the daily catch, corn fritters, and fish curry, plus must-try desserts like caramelized pineapple ice cream and black rice pudding. Sometimes the owner even puts on a live music performance!

**2. Warung Enak Amed**

A budget-friendly eatery loved by both locals and travelers, offering delicious Balinese pancakes, BBQ, and freshly grilled fish. The lava cake is a hidden dessert gem—best enjoyed all to yourself!

**3. Restaurant Bukit Segara**

An oceanfront restaurant specializing in daily fresh catches and a diverse mix of Asian and European cuisine. Prices are mid-to-high, but the service, quality, and stunning views make it a meal worth investing in.

**4. Galanga Amed**

A small, cozy Asian fusion restaurant serving Balinese, Indonesian, and healthy smoothie bowls. With a relaxed atmosphere, it's a great spot for lunch, dinner, and desserts.

**5. di Lipah Warung**

A traditional seafood eatery, with the signature dish being Pepe Ikan (grilled fish in coconut leaves). The desserts are also highly praised. Though it lacks a view, the food and warm service leave a lasting impression.

**6. Gusto Resto Amed**

A comfortable home-style restaurant specializing in Mediterranean and Indonesian fusion dishes. Recommended items include fresh salads, rendang chicken, and pan-seared tuna steak—a great choice for group dining.

**7. Green Melon Warung**

Known for healthy cooking, fresh ingredients, and affordable prices. Recommended are the BBQ tuna and squid dishes, paired with Balinese salad, plus the adorable banana leaf cone rice. Cash only.

**8. Trattoria Amed**

An authentic Italian restaurant with a romantic atmosphere and excellent service. The pizzas are incredibly popular, and it features a professional wine cellar and well-equipped restrooms—a paradise for pizza lovers.

**9. Sails Restaurant**

With stunning ocean views and a style reminiscent of a Greek island, this spot is a must. Try the banana leaf-wrapped fish, lamb patties, and frozen margaritas. The atmosphere is fresh, the staff friendly, and they even offer a free shuttle service.

### **Café Recommendation: Rimba Café**

Rimba Café is a rare find in Amed, blending a natural vibe with creative light bites. Surrounded by greenery, the atmosphere is fresh and relaxing. It specializes in handmade desserts, smoothie bowls, coffee, and healthy light meals, making it a favorite among travelers and yoga enthusiasts.

The coffee here rivals city-level quality, and paired with homemade baked goods like banana cake or coconut milk pudding, it's easy to become a regular. They also offer vegetarian and plant-based options, making it an ideal spot for brunch or an afternoon unwind.

If you're in Amed looking for a place to slow down and enjoy a great cup of coffee, **Rimba Café** is definitely a solid choice.

## Important Things to Know Before You Go

https://youtube.com/shorts/XS1j72uEtTY

Since Amed and Tulamben are relatively remote and not as well-equipped as the southern tourist areas, travelers should be well-prepared before heading out:

- **Cash is king**: ATMs are scarce in the area and may not accept foreign cards, so it's best to withdraw enough cash before your trek or dive.
- **Bring basic medications**: Local medical facilities are limited—pack simple items like stomach medicine, pain relievers, and mosquito repellent.
- **Book transportation in advance**: If you need a private car or driver, it's wise to arrange it ahead of time.
- **Limited internet**: Signal can be spotty in some areas, so consider getting a local SIM card or portable WiFi.
- **Protect the marine ecosystem**: Avoid touching coral and use reef-safe sunscreen to help preserve this precious ocean.

## FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

**Are Amed and Tulamben suitable for beginner divers?**

Yes, the sea conditions here are stable with gentle currents, making it especially suitable for diving beginners. Local dive schools also offer introductory courses.

**Can I go directly from Ubud or Kuta?**

Yes, but it's recommended to hire a private driver. The drive takes about 2.5 to 3 hours, and you can arrange stops at other attractions along the way.

**What is the best time to visit?**

The dry season (May to October) is the best time to visit, with clear skies and crystal-clear waters perfect for water activities.

**Do local restaurants offer vegetarian options?**

Most restaurants offer vegetarian options, but the selection is limited, so it's best to inform them in advance and confirm. 

**What other attractions are nearby?**

If you have extra time, I recommend visiting three stunning spots in the area:

![FAQ 常見問題解答](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_ed589a98d59d6d3d1de01faf3eb0b822.jpg)

- **Lempuyang Temple (Sky Gate)**: Perched on a mountainside, this temple is famous for its dramatic volcanic backdrop and sacred stone gate, making it a paradise for photographers.
- **Tirta Gangga**: A former royal water garden where stone carvings and koi fish create a mesmerizing scene, perfect for a leisurely stroll and photo opportunities.
- **Ujung Water Palace**: An ancient palace blending European and Balinese styles, surrounded by pools and greenery, offering a serene and historically rich atmosphere.
