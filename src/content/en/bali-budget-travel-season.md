**Bottom line**: The cheapest months to visit Bali are the **low season from January to April and early October to mid-December** (excluding holidays like Lunar New Year). During these times, flights and accommodations are discounted, and attractions are less crowded. Three money-saving tips: **rent a scooter or share a private car** instead of taking taxis, eat at **local eateries (Warung)** instead of tourist restaurants, and **book water activities and spa packages online** for discounted rates.

Dreaming of Bali but worried about the cost? By timing your trip right and using smart saving strategies, you can enjoy the 'Island of the Gods' on a small budget. This guide breaks down the cheapest travel months, accommodation deals, transportation options, and food choices, so you can still have an immersive Bali experience during the low season.

## **Key Expenses for a Bali Trip**

When planning a low-budget Bali trip, keep these major costs in mind:

- **Flights**: Book early and be flexible with dates to save the most.
- **Accommodation**: Rates are lowest during the low season from January to May and September to early December.
  (Excluding consecutive holidays like Lunar New Year; see [here](/en/blog/bali-peak-season-travel-tips/))
- **Transportation**: Renting a scooter is the cheapest option, or consider hiring a car with a driver, which works well for groups.
- **Food**: Local eateries (Warung) and street stalls offer the best value.
- **Activities**: Book water sports or spa treatments online for package deals.
- **Miscellaneous**: Don't forget to budget for souvenirs, tips, and parking fees.

## **Bali's Low Season: The Most Affordable Months**

![峇里島淡季：最划算的月份](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_7d538c747ad9deaaf455df6252953555.png)

### **Benefits of the Low Season**

- Low season: **January–April, October–early December**
- **Fewer crowds**: Enjoy quieter attractions and a slower travel pace.
- **Lower prices**: Discounts on hotels, flights, and activities.

### **Tips for Traveling During the Rainy Season**

- Rain is **usually brief showers** that won't disrupt most plans.
- The scenery is greener and the air is fresher.
- It's recommended to bring a lightweight raincoat or waterproof jacket.

***

## **Accommodation Deals and Booking Tips**

- **Off-Peak Discounts**: Many hotels and villas offer discounts during the low season.
- **Booking Platforms**: Lock in deals early on platforms like Agoda.
- **Best Timing**: February to May and September to early December see significantly lower rates.

## **Money-Saving Tips for Flight Tickets**

- **Book Early**: Booking at least 6 months in advance can save you a lot.
- **Snag deals at the online travel fair:** [China Airlines ITF Online Travel Fair](https://2025ttektf.china-airlines.com) runs until November 20, 2025, with Taipei–Bali tickets starting from USD$382.
- **Avoid peak seasons:** Prices are highest during June–August summer break, late December Christmas–New Year holidays, and Lunar New Year.
- **Fly on weekdays:** Tuesdays and Wednesdays are usually cheaper than weekends.
- **Consider connecting flights:** Transiting via Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok, or Vietnam can be more affordable than direct flights.

## **Bali Budget Eats & Daily Expenses**

- **Local Warung**: Authentic Nasi Goreng and satay skewers at half the price of tourist restaurants.
- **Street Stalls**: Affordable and flavorful snacks with local character.
- **Drink Savings**: Bring a reusable water bottle and refill for free at your accommodation or cafes.

## **Money-Saving Transport Tips in Bali**

- **Scooter Rental**: Around IDR 100,000–250,000 per day—flexible and cheap.
- **Public Transport**: Buses and Bemo minibuses are the cheapest option, though less frequent.
- **Private Car Hire**: Most cost-effective for groups of 2 or more, including a driver.

### **FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions**

## **1. What is the cheapest time to visit Bali?**

The low season runs from January to May and September to early December, offering the cheapest accommodation and flights. However, avoid certain long weekends.

## **2. Is it inconvenient to visit Bali during the rainy season?**

Rain usually comes in short bursts and doesn't affect most itineraries; in fact, the scenery is even more beautiful.

## **3. What are the affordable transportation options in Bali?**

Renting a scooter is the most budget-friendly option; if traveling in a group, consider hiring a private car with a driver.

## **4. Is eating cheap in Bali?**

Dining at a local Warung is very affordable, with meals costing around USD$1–2 each.

## **5. How to find cheap flights to Bali?**

Book tickets 6 months in advance, avoid peak season, and make good use of price comparison sites and connecting flights. **Don't miss out on online travel fair deals**—such great offers are not to be missed!
