Want to soak in a pool, bask in the sun, and snap photos on a daybed in Bali without breaking the bank on a day pass?

Actually, Bali has plenty of budget-friendly **Day Clubs, Pool Clubs, and poolside restaurants** where you don't have to spend over IDR 1,000,000. Some are even **free to enter with no minimum spend**—just order a meal or drink and use the pool.

At some spots, hitting a certain spend gets you access to sunbeds, daybeds, or pool towels.

This setup is perfect for regular travelers: no need to pay hefty entry fees just to lounge by the pool for a few hours, and you can combine **dining, swimming, photo ops, and chilling** all in one go.

Here's a roundup of the most notable affordable Day Clubs in Bali, stretching from Uluwatu and Melasti to Canggu, Seminyak, and Ubud.

## Mrs Sippy｜Seminyak

https://youtube.com/shorts/6CLaiuAloo8

If you're after a pool club with vibe in Seminyak, **Mrs Sippy** is worth considering, but it's not a free-entry Day Club. General admission requires a ticket, recently around **IDR 100,000–150,000/person**, and the entry fee may not be redeemable for food and drinks; for a Day Bed, there's usually a higher minimum spend.

Therefore, Mrs Sippy is better suited for travelers who want to enjoy a combination of pool, music, dining, and Beach Club atmosphere all at once, rather than those simply looking for a cheap place to swim. If you're on a budget, it's better to choose general admission with pool access, which makes it easier to control spending than booking a high-consumption Day Bed.

**Best for:** Couples, friends, small groups, and travelers looking to soak up the lively Beach Club vibe in Seminyak.

# When Choosing a Day Club, **Don't Just Look at 'Free Entry'**

"Free entry" doesn't mean sunbeds are free; "minimum spend" doesn't always mean an extra charge—in some places, the minimum spend can be fully used for food and drinks.

What you really need to compare is:

**What's the minimum spend? Can it be offset against food and drinks? Does it include pool access? Does it include a daybed? Are towels provided?**

Once you clarify these points, it becomes much easier to find a Bali Day Club that truly suits you.

A final reminder: prices, seating rules, and minimum spend at Bali's Day Clubs may vary depending on peak season, weekends, events, or venue policies. **Be sure to double-check the latest prices and booking conditions before you go**, especially the rules for daybed and pool use.
