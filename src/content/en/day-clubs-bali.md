Dreaming of lounging by the pool, soaking up the sun, and snapping photos on a daybed in Bali without breaking the bank on a day pass?

The truth is, Bali is home to plenty of budget-friendly **Day Clubs, Pool Clubs, and poolside restaurants** where you don't have to spend over IDR 1,000,000. Some are even **free to enter with no minimum spend** — just order a meal or a drink and you can use the pool.

At others, spending a certain amount gets you access to sunbeds, daybeds, or pool towels.

This setup is perfect for independent travelers: skip the hefty entrance fee just to swim for a few hours, and combine 'dining + pool + photos + relaxation' all in one go.

Here's a roundup of the most noteworthy affordable Day Clubs in Bali, stretching from Uluwatu and Melasti to Canggu, Seminyak, and all the way to Ubud.

## Mrs Sippy｜Seminyak

https://youtube.com/shorts/6CLaiuAloo8

If you're after a pool club with some vibe in Seminyak, **Mrs Sippy** is worth considering — but it's not a free-entry Day Club. General admission requires a ticket, recently around **IDR 100,000–150,000 per person**, and the entrance fee may not be redeemable for food and drinks; for a Day Bed, expect a higher minimum spend.

Therefore, Mrs Sippy is better suited for travelers who want to enjoy a combination of pool, music, dining, and Beach Club atmosphere all in one go, rather than those simply looking for a cheap place to swim. If you're on a budget, it's advisable to choose general admission with pool access, which makes it easier to control spending compared to directly booking a high-consumption Day Bed.

**Best for:** Couples, friends, small groups, and travelers who want to soak up the vibrant Beach Club vibe in Seminyak.

# When choosing a Day Club, **don’t just look at “Free Entry”**

“Free entry” doesn’t mean loungers are free; “minimum spend” doesn’t necessarily mean an extra fee—in some places, the minimum can be fully used for food and drinks.

What you really need to compare is:

**What’s the minimum spend? Can it be used for food and drinks? Is the pool included? Is the Daybed included? Are towels provided?**

Once you clarify these points, it’s much easier to find a Bali Day Club that truly suits you.

A final note: Prices, seating rules, and minimum spend at Bali’s Day Clubs may vary due to peak season, weekends, events, or policy changes. **Be sure to double-check the latest rates and booking conditions before you go**, especially regarding Daybed and pool usage rules.
