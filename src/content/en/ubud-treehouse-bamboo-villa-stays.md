As Bali's most beloved hub of art and culture, Ubud has long been known for its traditional crafts and dance performances. In recent years, however, a new travel trend has taken root—tree houses and bamboo villas. Crafted from the region's abundant bamboo resources, these villas seamlessly blend the raw, rustic beauty of nature with modern design, offering travelers a truly unique stay. Let's dive into 10+ of Ubud's most distinctive tree houses and bamboo villas!

## Ulaman Eco Luxury Resort

![Ulaman Eco Luxury Resort](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b9a20c09368eebae86d748190a51e8c4.jpg)

This villa is built with 100% renewable bamboo, harmonizing beautifully with the sweeping views of the surrounding tropical rainforest. True to its eco-friendly ethos, the villa also uses solar power and eco-friendly shower fixtures, letting you experience the luxury of living in harmony with nature.

Ulaman Eco Luxury Resort is arguably the most premium bamboo resort in all of Bali. This 5-star luxury property sets a new benchmark for bamboo hotels on the island with its unparalleled design. Nestled in the tranquil village of Kaba Kaba, it's 7 km from the iconic Tanah Lot Temple and about a 30-minute drive from Canggu.

The resort features a swimming pool, fruit orchard, terrace, and a restaurant serving American cuisine. It also offers spa treatments, airport transfers, yoga classes, and complimentary bicycle rentals, with breakfast included in the room rate.

The bamboo villa boasts a lofty bedroom design with interwoven wood and bamboo, furnished with a king-size bed, minibar, coffee machine, and TV. The bathroom features dual stone sinks, a rain showerhead, and a wooden bathtub. Sliding glass doors open onto an outdoor terrace with a private pool.

Though the decor is understated, it exudes luxury—curved archways and sheer bed drapes add a touch of romance. The lake-view villas are a delight, but the sky villas offer the most breathtaking panorama, overlooking lush, untouched jungle. Note that children under 12 are not allowed, allowing adults to fully unwind in this serene haven.

## Veluvana Bali

![Veluvana Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_c1aa4c350cffaa9a132c81ec05e5d13a.jpg)

Veluvana Bali is an adults-only bamboo hotel, where each villa boasts a unique design, promising an unprecedented stay. Located in the village of Sidemen, it's 8 km from Labuhan Amuk Beach and a 100-meter walk from three restaurants.

Paid airport transfers, in-room dining, and bicycle rentals are available. The hotel features a snack bar, daily yoga classes, and car rental services. Breakfast is included in the accommodation rate.

The bamboo villas come with a king-size bed and canopy, plus a semi-open mini-bar, kettle, and stove. The bathroom is open-plan with an uncovered shower. Each villa has a balcony, seating, or a hammock overlooking stunning, pristine forest views.

The main difference between villas lies in the private pool design. Scorpio House and Owl House have pools on the balcony or beneath the structure; Manta House features a long infinity pool. If you're not afraid of heights, the mini pool on Octopus House's rooftop will captivate you. Unique designs deliver a one-of-a-kind living experience.

## Samanvaya Luxury Resort & Spa

![Samanvaya Luxury Resort & Spa](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_16bdb24e7ad4c1f9cd4adc6ffc9d04ce.jpg)

Samanvaya Luxury Resort & Spa is a 4-star luxury resort offering a choice of wooden, bamboo, and concrete villas—a great option for those who prefer not to stay in bamboo accommodations. It's undoubtedly one of the most comfortable and luxurious bamboo resorts in Bali.

Nestled among the rice paddies of the Sidemen Valley, this resort is about a one-hour drive from Ubud and a five-minute walk to three restaurants. As a four-star resort, it features a swimming pool, spa, and restaurant. Paid airport transfers and in-room dining are available, and the accommodation rate includes a continental breakfast.

The bamboo villas come in one-bedroom and two-bedroom options, accommodating 2–4 guests. The double beds are fully draped with canopies, and the living area includes a sofa and terrace. Some villas even have a private pool. The decor is elegant and stylish, with bamboo furniture and crisp white linens.

The bathroom features dual stone vanities, a shower, and a semi-open copper or stone bathtub. All villas also provide a smartphone for guests to use during their stay. The rooms are spacious and comfortable, offering the ultimate luxury experience in Bali.

## Stone House

![Stone House](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_8d08e839a451e12c70b2f613359cdd1b.jpg)

Stone House is a highly-rated four-star hotel with six villas crafted from stone, wood, or bamboo. Despite being in the heart of Ubud, it offers a serene, secluded feel—making it a great choice for couples and families with elderly members. If you want to stay in a bamboo villa without worrying about bugs, Stone House is the perfect pick.

The hotel is 2 kilometers from Ubud Palace and 4 kilometers from the Monkey Forest, with two restaurants just 150 meters away. Paid airport transfers, bicycle and car rentals, a garden, and 24-hour security are available. A restaurant and café are on-site, and breakfast is included in the rate.

The bamboo villa is enclosed with a ceiling woven from bamboo strips. Inside, you'll find a king-size bed with a canopy, a desk, safe, minibar, and electric kettle. The semi-open bathroom has an uncovered shower.

The villa's front terrace has a sofa and chairs overlooking the garden. The interior decor is antique-style, giving a nostalgic, vintage feel. Children aged 5 and above are welcome, and extra beds are available for an additional fee.

If you're looking for a safe, comfortable, and tranquil bamboo villa in Ubud away from the hustle and bustle, Stone House is the best choice.

## Bambu Indah

![Bambu Indah](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_50b375439cef2dfbcf57bf8b2ba3646d.jpg)

In Ubud, if sustainability and design are your criteria, Bambu Indah is undoubtedly the top choice for a bamboo villa retreat. Its stunning design and meticulous attention to detail—especially the water filtration system that lets you drink tap water directly and avoid plastic waste—are truly impressive.

This 4-star boutique hotel is just 2 kilometers from the Monkey Forest, with three restaurants within 650 meters. It features a swimming pool filtered by natural volcanic rock and a restaurant serving produce grown on the property. Paid airport transfers, spa services, and in-room dining are also available, with breakfast included in the room rate.

Accommodations range from tents set on bamboo platforms to one- and two-bedroom bamboo villas. The bamboo villas are furnished with king-size beds and mosquito nets, a safe, minibar, and electric kettle, along with a sofa, small dining table, and a bathroom with a copper bathtub. The elevated, open-plan villas have no windows or doors, offering stunning views of the tropical jungle.

For a unique stay, book the pointed bamboo treehouse that looks straight out of a fairy tale—one of Bali's most remarkable treehouses. If you crave a private pool, the luxurious and spacious two-bedroom river-view room is your perfect choice. Either way, Bambu Indah promises an unforgettable bamboo villa experience in Ubud.

## Le Sabot Ubud

![Le Sabot Ubud](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_55c9f85a37df8575094a1c1df9698a82.jpg)

Finding an affordable, well-designed, and spacious bamboo stay in a popular destination like Ubud isn't easy, but Le Sabot Ubud is undoubtedly one of the few options that ticks all the boxes.

This hotel is just a 10-minute walk from Ubud Market, 2 km from Ubud Palace, and 4 km from the Monkey Forest, with three restaurants within a 300-meter walk. It features a swimming pool and garden, and rooms are cleaned daily.

Standard thatched-roof villas come with a double bed, table, and fan, while the enclosed bathroom has a shower. Each villa's front balcony offers views of the garden. For a more spacious and unique bamboo experience, consider booking a one-bedroom bamboo villa with a stove.

The best choice for families is the massive two-story, two-bedroom villa, which includes a fan, electric kettle, and fridge. The decor is very simple (just a few chairs and a wardrobe), and there's no TV, but if you're looking for the best value bamboo stay for a night, Le Sabot is a solid pick.

Whether you're a couple or a family, Le Sabot offers great value bamboo experiences, letting you enjoy your Ubud getaway without breaking the bank.
