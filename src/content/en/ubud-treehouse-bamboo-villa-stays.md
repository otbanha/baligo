## Ulaman Eco Luxury Resort

![Ulaman Eco Luxury Resort](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b9a20c09368eebae86d748190a51e8c4.jpg)

Recently awarded the 2026 World Travel Awards (WTA) title of 'Asia's Best Green Resort'!

This villa is built with 100% renewable bamboo, harmonizing with the vast tropical rainforest surroundings. It also follows eco-friendly principles, using solar power and eco-friendly shower facilities, allowing you to experience luxury living in harmony with nature.

Ulaman Eco Luxury Resort is arguably Bali's most premium bamboo resort. This 5-star luxury resort sets a new benchmark for other bamboo hotels on the island with its unparalleled design. Located in the tranquil Kaba Kaba village, it's 7 km from the iconic Tanah Lot Temple and a 30-minute drive from Canggu.

The resort features a pool, fruit orchard, terrace, and a restaurant serving American cuisine. It also offers spa treatments, airport transfers, yoga classes, and complimentary bicycle rentals, with breakfast included in the accommodation rate.

The bamboo villa features a high-ceilinged bedroom design with interwoven wood and bamboo, furnished with a king-size bed, minibar, coffee machine, and TV. The bathroom includes dual stone countertop sinks, a rain showerhead, and a wooden bathtub. Sliding glass doors open onto an outdoor terrace with a private pool.

Though the decor is understated, it exudes understated luxury, with arched doorways and sheer bed drapes adding a touch of charm. The lake-view villas are delightful, but the sky villas offer the most breathtaking views, overlooking the lush, pristine jungle. Note that children under 12 are not accepted, allowing adults to fully unwind in this serene haven.

## Veluvana Bali

![Veluvana Bali](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_c1aa4c350cffaa9a132c81ec05e5d13a.jpg)

Veluvana Bali is an adults-only bamboo hotel where each villa boasts a unique design, promising an unprecedented stay. Located in the village of Sidemen, it's 8 km from Labuhan Amuk Beach and a 100-meter walk from three restaurants.

Paid airport transfers, in-room dining, and bicycle rentals are available. The hotel features a snack bar, daily yoga classes, and car rental services. Breakfast is included in the accommodation fee.

The bamboo villas come with a king-size bed and canopy, along with a semi-open minibar, kettle, and stove. The bathroom is open-plan with an uncovered shower. Each villa has a balcony, seating, or a hammock overlooking the stunning pristine forest.

The main difference between villas lies in the private pool design. Scorpio House and Owl House have pools on the balcony or beneath the structure; Manta House features a long infinity pool. If you're not afraid of heights, the mini pool on the top floor of Octopus House will captivate you. Unique designs offer a one-of-a-kind living experience.

## Samanvaya Luxury Resort & Spa

![Samanvaya Luxury Resort & Spa](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_16bdb24e7ad4c1f9cd4adc6ffc9d04ce.jpg)

Samanvaya Luxury Resort & Spa is a 4-star luxury resort offering a variety of wooden villas, bamboo villas, and concrete villas, making it a great choice for travelers who prefer not to stay in bamboo accommodations. It is undoubtedly one of the most comfortable and luxurious bamboo villa resorts in Bali.

Nestled among the rice paddies of the Sidemen Valley, the resort is about a 1-hour drive from Ubud and a 5-minute walk from three restaurants. As a 4-star resort, it features a swimming pool, spa, and restaurant. Paid airport transfers and in-room dining are available, and the room rate includes a continental breakfast.

The bamboo villas come in one-bedroom and two-bedroom options, accommodating 2-4 guests. The double beds are fully draped with mosquito nets, and the living room includes a sofa and terrace. Some villas even have a private pool. The decor is elegant and stylish, featuring bamboo furniture and crisp white linens.

The bathroom features dual stone sinks, a shower, and a semi-open copper or stone bathtub. All villas also come with a smartphone for guests to use during their stay. The rooms are spacious and comfortable, offering the ultimate luxury resort experience in Bali.

## Stone House

![Stone House](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_8d08e839a451e12c70b2f613359cdd1b.jpg)

Stone House is a highly acclaimed 4-star hotel with 6 villas crafted from stone, wood, or bamboo. Despite being located in the heart of Ubud, it offers a serene escape from the hustle and bustle, making it a great choice for couples and families with elderly members. If you want to stay in a bamboo villa while avoiding bugs and bites, Stone House is the perfect pick.

The hotel is 2 km from Ubud Palace and 4 km from the Monkey Forest, with two restaurants just a 150-meter walk away. It offers paid airport transfers, bicycle and car rentals, a garden, and 24-hour security. A restaurant and café are on-site, and breakfast is included in the room rate.

The bamboo villas are enclosed with a ceiling woven from bamboo strips. Inside, you'll find a king-size bed with mosquito netting, a desk, safe, minibar, and electric kettle. The semi-open bathroom has an uncovered shower area.

The front terrace of each villa is furnished with a sofa and chairs, offering garden views. The interior decor is antique and nostalgic, giving off a vintage charm. Children aged 5 and above are welcome, and an extra bed is available for an additional fee.

If you're looking for a peaceful, safe, and comfortable bamboo villa away from the hustle and bustle in Ubud, Stone House is definitely the top choice.

## Bambu Indah

![Bambu Indah](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_50b375439cef2dfbcf57bf8b2ba3646d.jpg)

In Ubud, if sustainability and design are your criteria, Bambu Indah is unquestionably the best bamboo villa resort. Its stunning design and attention to detail, especially the water filtration system that lets you drink tap water directly and avoid plastic waste, are truly impressive.

This 4-star boutique hotel is just 2 km from the Monkey Forest, with three restaurants within 650 meters. It features a pool filtered by natural volcanic rock and a restaurant serving produce grown on-site. Paid airport transfers, spa services, and in-room dining are available, and breakfast is included in the rate.

Rooms range from tents set on bamboo platforms to one- and two-bedroom bamboo villas. The bamboo villas are furnished with king-size beds and mosquito nets, a safe, minibar, and electric kettle, along with a sofa, small dining table, and a copper bathtub in the bathroom. These elevated, open-plan villas have no windows or doors, offering stunning views of the tropical jungle.

For a unique stay, book the cone-shaped bamboo treehouse that feels straight out of a fairy tale—one of Bali's most remarkable treehouses. If you crave a private pool, the spacious two-bedroom river-view room is your perfect choice. Either way, Bambu Indah promises an unforgettable bamboo villa experience in Ubud.

## Beehouse Dijiwa Ubud

Nestled in a tranquil countryside, the 4-star Beehouse Dijiwa Villa Resort is adults-only, offering a luxurious escape for those seeking a romantic getaway or wellness retreat.

The resort is just a 10-minute walk from Ubud's city center, with plenty of dining options nearby. It features a year-round swimming pool (offering unobstructed views across the rice paddies), a garden, and a balcony. The bar and restaurant serve à la carte dishes or an American breakfast, with breakfast included in the room rate.

There is 24-hour front desk service, round-the-clock security, paid airport transfers, and bicycle rentals available.

The bamboo villas feature wooden flooring, air conditioning, and cylindrical bamboo frames, with elevated foundations designed to maximize the scenic surroundings. Each room is equipped with a king-size bed, seating area, safe, TV, and refrigerator. The bathroom includes a stone sink, shower, and separate bathtub.

Slide open the floor-to-ceiling glass doors to step onto a balcony with seating, offering views of the rice fields and pool. The Deluxe Villa provides more spacious accommodations, a separate living room, and a stone bathtub. For those wanting to stay in a bamboo room with a private pool in Ubud, consider booking the one-bedroom Dijiwa villa.

Whether you're seeking romance, relaxation, or simply admiring the unique bamboo design, Beehouse Dijiwa is sure to leave you with unforgettable memories.

## Le Sabot Ubud

![Le Sabot Ubud](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_55c9f85a37df8575094a1c1df9698a82.jpg)

Finding affordable, well-designed, and spacious bamboo accommodation in a popular destination like Ubud isn't easy, but Le Sabot Ubud is undoubtedly one of the few great options that ticks all the boxes.

This hotel is just a 10-minute walk from Ubud Market, 2 km from Ubud Palace, and 4 km from the Monkey Forest. Three restaurants are within a 300-meter walk. The property features a swimming pool and garden, with daily housekeeping.

The standard thatched-roof villas come with a double bed, table, and fan, while the enclosed bathroom includes a shower. Each villa's front balcony offers views of the garden. For a more spacious and distinctive bamboo stay, consider booking a one-bedroom bamboo villa with a stove.

The best choice for families is the huge two-story, two-bedroom villa, equipped with a fan, electric kettle, and refrigerator. The decor is very simple (just a few chairs and a wardrobe), and there's no TV, but if you're simply looking for the most affordable bamboo cottage for a night, Le Sabot is a solid pick.

Whether you're a couple or a family, Le Sabot offers great value bamboo experiences, letting you enjoy your Ubud getaway without breaking the bank.
