## 🗞️ Today's Highlights: Strong Recovery of International Events and Low-Carbon Tourism

- **'Oceanman Bali 2026' World Open Water Swim Officially Returns**: After a two-year hiatus, the highly anticipated **Oceanman Bali 2026** international open water swim event is set to take place from **June 19 to 21** at Kedonganan Beach in Jimbaran. With the theme 'Waves of Change,' the event has drawn over 900 top athletes and enthusiasts from 27 countries and regions, including Indonesia, Malaysia, Australia, Europe, and the Americas—a 30% surge in participants compared to previous years. The competition spans six categories, from a 500-meter children's race to the grueling 10-kilometer main event, promising a massive boost in sports tourism along the Jimbaran coast.
- **From 'Consumer Tourism' to 'Contributive Tourism': A Green Transformation**: Amid growing reflection on overtourism and environmental carrying capacity across the island, Bali's tourism industry is undergoing a fundamental shift toward an 'Eco-Tourism Future.' Leading hospitality players and local environmental groups are pivoting from a focus on maximizing visitor numbers and pure consumption to driving long-term environmental contributions. An increasing number of upscale villas and resorts are adopting rainwater harvesting, zero-plastic commitments, and community co-prosperity initiatives, attracting eco-conscious high-net-worth travelers to participate in local tree planting and coral restoration, turning tourism into a tangible force for protecting local ecosystems.
## 📄 Topics of Interest to Travelers: Slow Travel Picks, Art Exhibitions, and Community Initiatives
- **Nusa Dua Promotes Premium 'Wellcation' Long-Stay Retreats**: To cater to international high-end professionals and slow travelers, five-star luxury resorts in the Nusa Dua enclave, such as Grand Hyatt Bali, have recently launched customized 'Long Stay Wellcation' experiences. These combine high-privacy ocean-view accommodations, local wellness and meditation therapies, healthy meal management, and cultural craft workshops, offering a refined sanctuary for long-stay guests seeking balance, rejuvenation, and deep relaxation away from the crowds.

- **Deep Art and Culture Trend: Slow Exploration of 'Timeless Elegance' Craft Villages in Ubud**: Beyond sun and surf, Bali's vibrant traditions of **wood carving, painting, and weaving** are becoming core exploration goals for deep travelers this May. The Ubud Open Studios, located along the Wos River and in surrounding traditional villages, have recently launched immersive workshops led by local artisans, allowing international visitors to step into craftsmen's homes and experience the centuries-old island soul and philosophy behind 'every chisel stroke and every brush of color,' making it a popular intellectual slow-travel route.

- **努沙杜瓦（Nusa Dua）推動頂級「長住療癒度假（Wellcation）」**：
為了迎合國際高階白領與慢旅族群的需求，位於努沙杜瓦特區的國際五星級高檔度假村（如 Grand Hyatt Bali）於近日正式推出專為現代人設計的「長住療癒假期（Long Stay Wellcation）」客製化體驗。該模式結合了高隱私度的海景長住空間、在地身心靈冥想療癒、健康膳食管理及文化工藝體驗，旨在為尋求「平衡、充電與深度放鬆」的長住遊客提供免受喧囂干擾的精緻避風港。
- **深度藝文風潮：烏布（Ubud）「永恆優雅」手工藝村落慢遊**：
除了陽光與衝浪，峇里島極具生命力的**傳統木雕、繪畫與編織工藝**正成為今年 5 月深度遊客的核心探索目標。位於烏布沃斯河（Wos River）畔及周邊傳統村落的開放工作室（Ubud Open Studios），近期推出了由在地工匠親自帶領的沉浸式工作坊，讓外國旅客得以走進工匠家中，親身感受「每一刀雕刻、每一筆色彩」背後傳承數百年的島嶼靈魂與哲學，成為熱門的知性慢旅動線。
- **觀光收益回饋地方：南灣與薩努爾區啟動年度 CSR 關懷行動**：
島內知名指標性跨國酒店集團（如 Prime Plaza Hotel Sanur 與 Marriott 商業委員會）於本週陸續啟動了 2026 年度的大型**企業社會責任（CSR）與公益籌款計畫**。透過舉辦慈善高爾夫球賽、社區公益晚宴等形式，將觀光收益實質投入支持在地孤兒院、弱勢家庭與基層社福基金會。這項行動不僅溫暖了在地社群，也讓國際旅客在享受島嶼之美的同時，見證了觀光產業對當地社會的正面回饋。
