### Kids' Animal Friends: Meet the Unique Alpacas

At Pacha Alpaca, children can observe and interact up close with these gentle and adorable alpacas. Each alpaca has its own unique personality and story, and the on-site staff explain things in a lively, engaging way, helping kids learn about alpacas through interaction. It's not just a fun experience—it's a vivid nature lesson!

### Heartwarming Family Moments

The activities here are designed for parent-child participation. Parents can feed the alpacas with their kids, pet their soft fur, and even join in simple interactive games. This close bonding not only strengthens family ties but also helps children develop compassion and responsibility through animal contact. For kids, it's a memory filled with laughter and warmth.

### Relax in Nature

Pacha Alpaca is set in the Nuanu area, with vast green spaces and fresh air—a perfect spot for the whole family to unwind. Kids can run and explore on the grass while parents enjoy a moment of peace. The surrounding art sculptures and natural landscapes add a unique charm, making the entire experience richer and more colorful.

## Stay with the Alpacas

### Alpaca Lodge

Accommodating up to two guests, the cozy 'Alpaca Bamboo Cabin' is nestled within Nuanu's tranquil alpaca park. This 27-square-meter sustainable bamboo hut offers a unique stay in harmony with nature.

The cabin features a simple yet warm design, with comfortable bedding and basic amenities, and windows overlooking lush garden views. Guests can enjoy the adorable sight of alpacas strolling across the grass in the morning, and the stay includes a complimentary breakfast and an 'Alpaca Interaction Experience'—where you can hand-feed and pet these gentle animals, and capture unforgettable photos in a natural setting.
