Now there's a great new option for traveling to Bali. STARLUX Airlines officially launched the Taipei–Bali route on October 1. The inaugural flight JX765 departed from Taoyuan International Airport at 10:10 AM, with 5 weekly flights thereafter.

Bali is STARLUX's second destination in Indonesia, and with this new route, the airline's network expands to 34 cities.

## **Lounge Transforms into Yoga Studio**

![](https://imgcdn.cna.com.tw/www/WebPhotos/1024/20261001/1152x768_wmky_0_C20261001000070.jpg)

(Source: Central News Agency)

The inaugural event was held in the Taoyuan Airport lounge. STARLUX partnered with Canadian sportswear brand lululemon, inviting brand ambassadors to lead passengers in a yoga session. Before even boarding, travelers could slip into Bali's relaxed rhythm.

After the flight arrived at Ngurah Rai International Airport, a ribbon-cutting ceremony was held to officially celebrate the new route.

## **Why Bali?**

Beyond its island resort appeal, Bali draws travelers with yoga, wellness retreats, and slow living experiences. STARLUX CEO Chien-Hua Chai noted that this route strengthens the airline's Asian network and aims to connect European and American travelers via Taoyuan Airport's transit services, linking Southeast Asia with Western markets.

## Direct Flights from Taiwan to Bali (Departing from Taoyuan)

Currently, three airlines—China Airlines, EVA Air, and STARLUX—offer direct flights from Taoyuan to Bali. The flight time is around 5 hours, and since Bali is in the same time zone as Taiwan, there's no jet lag to worry about.

| Airline | Outbound | Return | Flight Days |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| China Airlines | CI771 ~09:10 departure, ~14:35–14:40 arrival | CI772 ~15:40–15:55 departure, ~21:00–21:20 arrival | Daily |
| EVA Air | BR255 09:50 departure, 15:15 arrival | BR256 16:30 departure, 22:00 arrival | Daily |
| STARLUX (New route from Oct 1) | JX765 10:10 departure, 15:40 arrival | JX766 17:20 departure, 22:50 arrival | Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun |

### **How to Choose the Best Option?**

- For daily availability: China Airlines and EVA Air fly every day, making it easy to pick your travel dates.
- Want more morning time: China Airlines departs earliest at around 09:10.
- Want to get home later: STARLUX's return flight departs at 17:20 and arrives at 22:50, giving you an extra afternoon on the island. STARLUX operates the A321neo and even offers a limited-time yoga activity at the lounge before boarding.

### **Practical Tips**

- All times above are local times.
- There are slight discrepancies in China Airlines' data sources (e.g., 14:35 vs. 14:40), and winter flight schedules may be adjusted by airlines. Please confirm with each airline's official website before booking.
