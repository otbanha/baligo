Want to visit Bali? There's now a fantastic new option. STARLUX Airlines officially launched its Taipei–Bali route on October 1, with the inaugural flight JX765 departing from Taoyuan International Airport at 10:10 AM, followed by 5 weekly services.

Bali is STARLUX's second destination in Indonesia, and with this new route, the airline's network expands to 34 cities.

## **Boarding Lounge Transforms into a Yoga Studio**

![](https://imgcdn.cna.com.tw/www/WebPhotos/1024/20261001/1152x768_wmky_0_C20261001000070.jpg)

(Source: Central News Agency)

The inaugural event was held in the Taoyuan Airport boarding lounge. STARLUX partnered with Canadian sportswear brand lululemon, inviting brand ambassadors to lead passengers in a yoga session. Before even boarding, travelers could slip into Bali's relaxed rhythm.

After the flight arrived at Ngurah Rai International Airport, a ribbon-cutting ceremony was held to celebrate the new route.

## **Why Bali?**

Beyond its island resort appeal, Bali draws travelers with yoga, wellness retreats, and slow living experiences. STARLUX CEO Chien-Hua Chai noted that this route strengthens the airline's Asian network and aims to connect European and American travelers via Taiwan's Taoyuan Airport transit, linking Southeast Asia with Western markets.

## Direct Flights from Taiwan to Bali (Departing from Taoyuan)

Currently, three airlines—China Airlines, EVA Air, and STARLUX—offer direct flights from Taoyuan to Bali, with a flight time of just over 5 hours. Since Bali shares the same time zone as Taiwan, there's no jet lag to worry about.

| Airline | Outbound | Return | Flight Days |
| China Airlines | CI771: Departs ~09:10, arrives ~14:35–14:40 | CI772: Departs ~15:40–15:55, arrives ~21:00–21:20 | Daily |
| EVA Air | BR255: Departs 09:50, arrives 15:15 | BR256: Departs 16:30, arrives 22:00 | Daily |
| STARLUX (New route from Oct 1) | JX765: Departs 10:10, arrives 15:40 | JX766: Departs 17:20, arrives 22:50 | Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun |

### **How to Choose the Best Option?**

- For daily departures: China Airlines and EVA Air fly every day, making scheduling easy.
- Want more morning time: China Airlines departs earliest at around 09:10.
- Want to come home later: STARLUX's return flight departs at 17:20 and arrives at 22:50, giving you an extra afternoon on the island. STARLUX operates the A321neo, and you can even experience a limited-edition yoga activity at the lounge before boarding.

### **Practical Tips**

- All times above are local times.
- There are slight discrepancies in the China Airlines data (e.g., 14:35 vs. 14:40), and airlines may adjust winter flight schedules, so please confirm with each airline's official website before booking.
