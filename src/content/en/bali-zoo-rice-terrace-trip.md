## Travel Dates: Feb 6–9, 2025

This was my first-ever trip to Bali, and though it was just four short days, it was a journey full of surprises and emotions. A big thank you to the seasoned travelers who offered invaluable advice when I asked online last September—their tips made this itinerary so much smoother. To give back, I’m sharing my detailed experiences and recommendations, hoping they’ll help others planning a trip to Bali.

## 1. **Mara River Safari Lodge: A Unique Sleepover with Animals**

![1. Mara River Safari Lodge：與動物共眠的獨特體驗](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b29252ee62cf7e762ff1f447761b8fa7.jpg)

The highlight of this trip for my 7-year-old was the **Mara River Safari Lodge**. This resort, located inside the safari park, offers not only convenience but also up-close encounters with various animals. Every morning, we enjoyed breakfast while watching lions through the glass windows—a truly special experience. The lions seemed accustomed to the visitors’ gaze, striking poses as if they were posing for us. My child was mesmerized, watching them with wide-eyed excitement.

Plus, the park’s shuttle made it easy to see all the animals. The kids kept gasping with delight on the ride, fully immersed in this vibrant environment. If you’re traveling with children, this is an absolute must.

## 2. **Ulun Danu Beratan Temple: A Serene Sanctuary**

![2. 水神廟：寧靜致遠的靈性之地](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_bbe079ae357378ab17d4b8d0b2fcadd3.jpg)

For our 70-year-old elders, **Ulun Danu Beratan Temple** was the most relaxing spot. The atmosphere here is incredibly tranquil, surrounded by mountains with a mirror-like lake and gentle breezes that soothe the soul. We took a slow stroll, sipped coffee, and soaked in the peace and beauty of nature. The misty mountain scenery felt like stepping into a fairyland—the elders especially loved this escape from the hustle and bustle, calling it a place to truly unwind.

## 3. **Tanah Lot: A Majestic Sea Erosion Wonder**

![3. 海神廟：壯麗的海蝕奇觀](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_e939c789a7f931dc80650833c7e66e7c.jpg)

**Tanah Lot** is one of Bali's iconic landmarks, with its dramatic sea-eroded archways and platforms that perfectly matched our vision of the island. Though the sun was intense by the shore, watching the waves crash against the rocks was utterly breathtaking. I'd recommend bringing a hat or umbrella and visiting in the late afternoon to avoid the heat and catch a stunning sunset.

## 5. **Tirta Empul: The Dual Surprise of Purification Rituals and a Presidential Retreat**

![5. 聖泉寺：沐浴儀式與總統行宮的雙重驚喜](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_4f849d9c235d59a137fbef5de65d6f96.jpg)

**Tirta Empul** is a place steeped in religious atmosphere. We saw many locals performing purification rituals here—a scene both solemn and fascinating. However, compared to the rituals, I was personally more intrigued by the presidential retreat up on the hill. This building is rich with history, and its surroundings are beautifully scenic, well worth taking time to explore.

## 6. **Cliff Road: Breathtaking Views and Travel Tips**

![6. 斷崖公路：壯麗景色與交通建議](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b589b4f1505f339f21868993d57b2ec4.jpg)

The views along the **Cliff Road** are spectacular. However, since a landslide occurred there recently, although repairs are now complete, it's still recommended to take a shuttle bus. Also, the sun here is extremely strong, so remember to bring a hat or sun protection to avoid getting sunburned.

## 7. **Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park: Majestic Yet Somewhat Sparse**

![7. 神鷹文化公園：雄偉卻略顯空曠](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_9ff7c54eabc3f3383b36804884d88bd9.jpg)

**Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park** covers a vast area, and its most striking feature is the enormous Garuda statue—its height and scale are truly awe-inspiring. You can even spot this statue from the airport, which speaks to its grandeur. That said, the park's facilities still feel somewhat incomplete, leaving the space a bit empty. I'd recommend visitors plan a short stop here to admire the statue before heading to other attractions.

## 8. **Private Driver: The Unsung Hero of the Trip**

A big part of this trip's success goes to our private driver **Xiao Wu** and his wife **Betty Pingky**. Since there were five of us with a lot of luggage, Xiao Wu specially arranged a large car to ensure we could move comfortably throughout the journey. His driving was incredibly steady—no sudden braking or jerky movements—allowing our sick child and elderly parents to rest well in the car.

![8. 包車司機：旅行的幕後功臣](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_3143a1eb899bf827d37ad8851ac6b8d4.jpg)

Moreover, Xiao Wu knew Bali's traffic conditions inside out, perfectly avoiding rush hours and congested routes, which made our itinerary much smoother. Whether the elderly needed a bathroom break or the kids kept asking 'Are we there yet?', he always managed to arrive at our destinations just in time, keeping the whole family in a harmonious mood. His Mandarin was very fluent with standard pronunciation, making it easy to chat with both the elderly and the kids, adding a warm touch to the entire trip.

It's worth mentioning that Betty Pingky was very meticulous before departure, helping us confirm the hotel package deals and adjusting the itinerary based on our needs. Even during the trip, when we casually mentioned a certain attraction, Xiao Wu would go out of his way to take us there. This thoughtful service truly touched us.

## 9. **Why Didn't We Hit Any Traffic?**

Throughout this trip, we barely encountered any traffic jams, which pleasantly surprised me. Reflecting on it later, I think there were a few reasons:

1. **Smart timing**: We visited **Tanah Lot** and **Ulun Danu Beratan Temple** during off-peak hours, choosing to go around lunchtime when traffic was lighter.
2. **Driver's expert route choices**: Xiao Wu knew Bali's roads well—when heading to **Tirta Empul** and **Jimbaran**, he took some back roads to avoid congestion on the main arteries.
3. **Personal tolerance for traffic**: Having lived in Jakarta for a long time, I'm used to endless gridlock. Compared to that, Bali's traffic felt like paradise.

These factors combined made our journey much smoother and greatly boosted my impression of Bali.

## 10. **Why So Few Photos: Busy Taking Care of the Young and Old**

We ended up with far fewer photos than expected on this trip, mainly because once we got out of the car, I was almost always busy looking after our 7-year-old child and 70-year-old parents. The kid was energetic and needed constant supervision, while the elderly required timely rest and assistance. Although we missed out on some great shots, being able to keep the whole family happy and safe felt more important than taking pictures. After all, the essence of travel is creating memories, not just checking in.

## 11. **A Small Regret About the Villa Pool**

![示意圖](https://images.gobaligo.id/vocus/vocus_b77f1b401df4485747072419ea8c5e64.jpg)

Illustration

The villa we stayed in this time had a beautiful setting, but the only downside was that the pool was 1.5 meters deep—too deep for the kids to enjoy playing in the water safely. This also meant the adults couldn't fully relax, as we had to keep a constant eye on them. My advice for future villa bookings is to always check the pool depth, especially for families with children. Opting for a shallow area or a kids' pool would be much more suitable.

## Summary and Rating

This Bali trip left our whole family very satisfied. The professional service of our private driver and the natural beauty of Bali made a deep impression on us. I had expected traffic in Bali to be as congested as in Jakarta, but we barely encountered any jams, which greatly enhanced our travel experience. On a scale of 100, I'd give this trip **95 points**—the only deduction being the villa pool depth issue.

Although the trip was busy—we didn't even have time for a massage (I went for one as soon as I got back to Jakarta)—at least the whole family agreed that Bali was fun. The kids and the elders all had a great time, which made all the effort worthwhile. The meaning of travel isn't about perfection, but about the time spent together and the memories created as a family.

---

## Recommendations and Tips

- **Private Driver**: Highly recommend **Betty Pingky** and her husband **Xiao Wu**. Their service is attentive and thoughtful, and they speak fluent Chinese, making them perfect for family trips.
- **Itinerary Planning**: Tailor your schedule to your family members' ages and interests. For families with kids, include more activities like the zoo or rice terraces; for elders, opt for serene spots like Ulun Danu Beratan Temple.
- **Accommodation**: When choosing a villa, be sure to check the pool depth, especially if traveling with children.
- **Transportation**: Avoid peak hours and choose a driver familiar with local roads to greatly improve your travel experience.

This Bali trip created wonderful memories for our whole family. We look forward to visiting this enchanting island again in the future!
